### Bedford
* Internal flooding of a commercial property located near Priory Lake caused by the River Great Ouse overtopping its banks; property lies immediately south of the riverbank in Environment Agency Flood Zone 2. (p1)
* Wider area experienced flooding on 25/12/2020; Bedford Borough Council declared a major incident, the Lead Local Flood Authority visited to provide assistance, the fire service provided flooding advice and responded to requests for sandbags, and council teams and volunteers carried out property impact assessments to support recovery and clean up. (p2)
* Quant: one commercial property (p1)

### East Anglia
* December 2020 had unusually high rainfall across East Anglia, producing saturated ground that contributed to rivers (including the Great Ouse) overtopping their banks and regional flood impacts; Main River asset inspections across East Anglia were carried out after the event and remedial needs were identified. (p2) [TAB]
