

# St Ives flooding 13 Aug 2020 - 16 Aug 2020

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: St Ives<br>021\_Cambridgeshire County/021\_2020\_Cambridgeshire County/St-Ives-August-2020-Flood-Investigation.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### St Ives
* {1947, Easter 1998, January 2003}: Town experienced fluvial (river) flooding from the River Great Ouse (historic floods) and is also at risk from surface water flooding; development has occurred on the natural floodplain on both banks. The River Great Ouse at St Ives flooded in 1947, and some parts suffered seriously at Easter 1998 and in January 2003. (p4) [MUL]
* {general}: Parts of St Ives lie within Flood Zone 3 (highest fluvial flood risk) concentrated along the River Great Ouse and extending into southern and eastern parts of the town; Flood Zone 2 covers additional areas including parts of the town centre and residential zones. Flood defences are present near the river and benefit some areas. (p5) [IMG]
* {general}: Surface-water flood risk present across the town: high-risk areas concentrated along the main river and tributaries and in low-lying urban zones; medium and low risk zones extend into residential and adjacent fields. Key infrastructure and residential areas (e.g. near the river, Thorndown School, Recreational Ground, Sewage Works, Cornwallis Place, Bridge Court) are shown within or adjacent to flood risk zones, indicating vulnerability to surface water flooding. (p6) [IMG]
* Heavy, short-duration rainfall on 13 and 16 August 2020 overwhelmed local drainage, resulting in flooding within the town and pressure on drainage infrastructure. (p7) [IMG, MUL]
* Intense rainfall on 13 and 16 August 2020 overwhelmed surface water drainage and road drainage networks. Surface water accumulated on roads and from highways gullies, sewers surcharged causing flooding, and adjacent land and premises (including commercial premises on Harding Way) experienced internal flooding. (p8) [PAG]
* Areas and properties within the town were affected by surface water flooding as drainage systems were unable to convey intense rainfall, causing excess surface water to flow over ground following local topography. (p9)
* Internal flooding of commercial premises and associated land at Harding Way; external flooding reported affecting properties across multiple streets (Orwell Close; Kings Hedges; Nursery Gardens; Pig Lane; St. Audrey's Lane; Sandwich Close). Three key risk areas in the town experienced property/internal flooding or significant external premises flooding. (p10) [PAG, IMG]
* Multiple streets and properties across St Ives experienced flooding (highways, footways, car parks and properties) during the August 2020 event. (p11)
* Commercial premises adjacent to Marley Gap Brook at Harding Way were identified in the investigation as being affected by flooding; the brook and surface water interaction at this location contributed to flood impacts on premises. (p12) [TAB]
* Community and infrastructure in St Ives were affected by flooding; the investigation identified capacity, operational and riparian maintenance issues and prompted maintenance actions and further investigation by the LLFA. (p16)
* Quant: 15 properties; three key risk areas (p10) [PAG, IMG]

### Marley Gap Brook
* {general}: An ordinary watercourse in St Ives that contributes to fluvial flood risk; flooding from this watercourse can exacerbate town flooding when the River Great Ouse and/or Parson's Drove remain high and contributory watercourses and connected drainage (highway gullies and surface water sewers) cannot discharge efficiently. (p4) [TAB]
* {general}: Identified as local watercourses that contribute to fluvial flood risk in the northeast of St Ives; flood risk zones (Flood Zone 3 and 2) follow their paths, indicating areas adjacent to these watercourses are at risk of river (fluvial) flooding. (p5) [IMG]
* Sections of the brook flooded during the extreme rainfall, overflowing onto adjacent land and premises. (p8) [PAG]
* A section of Marley Gap Brook adjacent to commercial premises at Harding Way was identified as a relevant contributor to flooding at that location; the brook's state of maintenance and capacity is being investigated by the district council and LLFA. (p12) [TAB]
* Identified as having capacity and RMA operational issues contributing to flooding during the investigated event. (p16) [TAB]

## Topics without coordinates

### Anglian Water
* Surface water and road drainage networks were inundated, resulting in surcharging of Anglian Water sewers which contributed to local flooding. (p8) [PAG]
* Anglian Water investigated the local drainage system following the August 2020 flooding, conducting inspection and maintenance of drainage assets as part of the response. (p12) [TAB]
* Acted as the response point for flooding from surface water drains and foul sewers during the event; handled reports via its 24-hour helpline where sewer or drainage failures affected properties or services. (p16)

### Parsons Drove
* {general}: When Parsons Drove remains high for long periods it can impede discharge from contributory watercourses, highways gullies and surface water sewers, exacerbating flooding in St Ives. (p4) [TAB]
* {general}: Identified as local watercourses that contribute to fluvial flood risk in the northeast of St Ives; flood risk zones (Flood Zone 3 and 2) follow their paths, indicating areas adjacent to these watercourses are at risk of river (fluvial) flooding. (p5) [IMG]
* Parson's Drove was recorded as having capacity and RMA operational issues contributing to flooding during the investigated event. (p16) [TAB]

### Houghton Field Drain
* {general}: An ordinary watercourse within St Ives that contributes to the town's fluvial flood risk; flooding can be exacerbated when the River Great Ouse and/or Parson's Drove remain high and contributory watercourses and connected drainage cannot discharge efficiently, increasing local flood risk. (p4) [TAB]
* Flooding occurred at the confluence with the Houghton Field Drain, causing inundation of adjacent land and premises. (p8) [PAG]

### Kings Brook Drain
* {general}: An ordinary watercourse in St Ives identified as contributing to fluvial flood risk; when main rivers (River Great Ouse and Parsons Drove) remain high and interconnected drainage cannot discharge, this watercourse can contribute to exacerbated flooding in the town. (p4) [TAB]
* Sections of the drain flooded during the intense rainfall, contributing to inundation of adjacent land and premises. (p8) [PAG]

### Huntingdonshire District Council
* The district council identified a section of Marley Gap Brook adjacent to commercial premises at Harding Way as relevant to flooding at that location; they are collaborating with the LLFA and drainage consultants who visited audited sections and will prepare a report and identify flood alleviation measures. (p12) [TAB]

### Environment Agency
* Received reports and managed incidents of main-river flooding via the incident hotline during the investigated event; acted as the reporting/contact point for main river flood impacts in the St Ives area. (p16)
