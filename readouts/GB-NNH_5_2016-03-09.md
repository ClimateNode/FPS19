## Place names with coordinates

### River Ise
* The River Ise, the main drainage conduit flowing adjacent to the community building, was associated with fluvial flooding that affected the nearby community building (the building is located in Flood Zone 3). Surface water public sewers from neighbouring residential estates discharge into the River Ise at five outfall locations to the north of the flooded building, forming part of the flood pathway. (p10) [PAG]
* River Ise flood water flowed around the front and rear of the community building at Ise Lodge, ponding at the property (external sports facilities were completely submerged) and entering the rear store where water reached the rear walls; deposited arisings were evident on adjacent banks. Partially fallen trees in the channel likely produced a damming effect and contributed to raised upstream levels. (p12)
* The River Ise received high volumes of water from the saturated catchment, overwhelmed its channel in the vicinity of Ise Lodge and flooded onto the adjacent floodplain. Fallen trees downstream and restrictions at Barton Road bridge were noted as possible contributors to raised upstream water levels. Large amounts of deposited arisings were evident on the adjacent banks. (p13) [PAG]
* Floodwater in the River Ise carried and deposited debris along the channel and banks; flow interacted with in‑channel obstructions (trees) leading to accumulation of debris downstream. (p51) [PAG]
* Downstream reach of the River Ise experienced flooding that deposited debris on banks and in drainage features. Flood flows were impeded by a second tree obstruction on the main channel, exacerbating local high water levels. Floodwater also accumulated into a downstream storm water outlet/pond located below a flooded building and collected around a concrete culvert fitted with a metal grate, indicating overstressing of local drainage infrastructure. (p53)
* Obstructions (fallen trees / debris) were present on the downstream reaches of the River Ise and contributed to blocked flow that led to flooding of nearby property; the river downstream is explicitly cited in the report pages describing flood impacts. (p54) [PAG]
* Quant: 5 outfall locations (p10) [PAG]
* Quant: up to 300mm ponded depth (p12)

### Ise Lodge
* The community building at Ise Lodge is the subject of the Flood Investigation Report, indicating it was impacted by flooding and investigated for flood damage/impacts. (p1) [PAG]
* Northamptonshire County Council Section 19 flood investigation concerns the community building at Ise Lodge, indicating the building was affected by flooding and is the subject of the report. (p2)
* Internal flooding of a community building: water entered the building fabric. Building lies within the floodplain and flooded due to heavy rainfall on a saturated catchment, overwhelmed drainage and river overtopping. (p7)
* The community building at Ise Lodge was flooded when the River Ise, receiving high volumes of water from a saturated catchment, overwhelmed the channel and flooded onto the adjacent floodplain around the building. Surface water runoff from nearby residential closes and car park areas also contributed to the volume of water present around the building. The building lies within the floodplain and was identified as being at risk of flooding without resistance measures. (p13)
* Quant: 1 property (p7)

### Environment Agency

### Barton Road Bridge
* Restriction of the A6003 Barton Road bridge (noted alongside fallen trees downstream) may have contributed to raised water levels upstream around Ise Lodge during the flood event. (p13)

### Kettering Borough Council
* Following the flood incident the council was engaged in post‑flood activity: consulting with the EA and LLFA on planning applications, reviewing planning policies, assessing drainage designs for nearby developments, using enforcement powers where riparian owners fail to maintain ordinary watercourses, and assisting with preparing detailed drainage/asset plans to share with the LLFA and community. (p21)
