

# Rotherham flooding 16 Aug 2022

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Flood Investigation Report for Delves Lane, Wales: 16 August 2022<br>104\_Rotherham/Flood\_Investigation\_Report\_for\_Delves\_Lane\_Wales.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Rotherham
* High-intensity rainfall caused overwhelmed highway drainage across the area, producing surface water/urban flooding. At least 5 properties on Delves Lane and 1 property at Barn House, School Road were recorded as flooded internally. (p6) [TAB]
* The August 2022 event in Rotherham generated internal and external flooding reports from residents (16 August), five houses reported basement flooding (17 August); multi-agency response and investigations followed, and Yorkshire Water cleared sewer blockages on 2 November 2022. (p8) [IMG, MUL]

### Wales
* The locality experienced surface water/urban flooding on 16 August 2022 that affected Delves Lane and School Road, resulting in internal flooding of properties and deposition of loose debris, refuse and waste. (p4)
* Quant: 6 properties flooded internally (5 on Delves Lane and 1 on School Road) (p4)

### School Road, Wales
* Surface water running down from the village entered the property's driveway at substantial flow, causing rapid inundation of the barn house and internal flooding of the property. (p4) [PAG]
* Surface water ran down from the village and entered a property's driveway on School Road at a substantial rate of flow, causing the property to quickly flood (internal flooding). (p5) [PAG]
* Barn House on School Road was flooded internally; internal water entry affected sanitary/toilet areas. Recorded as 1 property flooded internally. (p6) [TAB]
* Quant: 1 property (the barn house on School Road) (p4) [PAG]

### Delves Lane, Wales
* Surface water from playing fields ran down onto Delves Lane, causing highway surface water to rise over the kerb, run onto private property and backflow into sewers. The floodwater entered terraced houses, bringing loose debris, refuse and waste and causing internal flooding and property damage. (p4)
* Addresses 104, 105, 106, 107, 108, 109 on Delves Lane experienced internal flooding; the report records 5 properties flooded internally. Flooding was associated with overwhelmed highway drainage and runoff from the highway toward properties. (p6) [TAB]
* Quant: 5 properties (the last five properties on Delves Lane); all 5 properties flooded internally (p4)

### Church Street, Wales
* Surface water overflowed from the highway into a private driveway, blocking both the entrance and exit. (p9) [TAB]


## Topics without coordinates

### Rotherham Council
* An RMBC-owned manhole formed part of the drainage connection for private properties; the clay 4-inch pipe connecting gullies to that manhole was blocked leading to high gully water levels. This drainage failure contributed to surface water rising over the kerb, runoff onto private property and backflow into the sewer system that was associated with internal flooding of nearby properties. (p4) [PAG]
* Received reports from residents of internal and external flooding (residents unable to flush toilets or use amenities), coordinated emergency response (site visits, provision of emergency supplies such as bottled water and sandbags), instigated a Section 19 investigation and liaised with Yorkshire Water over responsibility and solutions. (p8) [IMG, MUL]
* Quant: one RMBC-owned manhole; 4-inch clay pipe between houses 103 and 104 (p4) [PAG]

### Rotherham Catchment
* {15 Aug 2022, 16 Aug 2022}: The catchment received Met Office warnings and heavy rainfall that led to flooding within the Rotherham catchment area, contributing to internal and external flooding reported by residents during the event. (p8) [IMG, MUL]

### Yorkshire Water
* Liaised with RMBC regarding responsibility for the flooding (22 August 2022) and cleared blockages in connected sewers (2 November 2022), after which there were no further resident reports and the case was closed. (p8) [IMG, MUL]
