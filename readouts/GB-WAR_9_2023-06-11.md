

# Piccadilly flooding 11 Jun 2023

Reference document:<br>Addendum to Flooding 2020 Piccadilly Crescent, Kingsbury<br>140\_Warwickshire County/140\_2023\_Warwickshire County/WCCC-453486374-186.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Piccadilly Crescent, Piccadilly
* {2020}: Experienced flooding during the 2020 flood event. (p1)
* Two properties on Piccadilly Crescent experienced internal flooding during an intense, localized thunderstorm on 11 June 2023; the heavy downpour caused internal property flooding. (p3) [PAG]
* Internal flooding of two properties occurred when high‑intensity rainfall exceeded the capacity of the piped drainage network or prevented flows entering the network quickly enough. Flooding was compounded because the two properties sit at the low spot of Piccadilly Crescent and front landscaping created an accessible route for surface water flows. Flooding was reported to the LLFA and verified with photographs; a site visit found no apparent asset failure or restriction within the drainage network. (p4) [IMG]
* Properties on Piccadilly Crescent were subject to flooding caused by an under-performing local drainage network and surface water run‑off; actions include gully cleansing, maintenance of the 150mm VC drain, installation of a continuous liner to improve connectivity to Trinity Road, and property flood resilience consideration. (p6) [TAB]
* Quant: two properties (p3) [PAG]
* Quant: two properties (p4) [IMG]

### Kingsbury
* {2020}: Impacted by the 2020 flood event (the addendum references flooding at Piccadilly Crescent in Kingsbury). (p1)
* Properties on Piccadilly Crescent in Kingsbury were affected by flooding, triggering actions including gully cleansing, lining of the 150mm VC drain (continuous liner installed between Piccadilly Crescent and Trinity Road funded by NWBC), and installation of a new chamber with a grated cover; PFR recommendations survey undertaken and funding identified for mitigation. (p6)

### Piccadilly
* Internal property flooding occurred within the settlement (two properties on Piccadilly Crescent) during a high‑intensity rainfall event; properties are located in a low spot and on the surface water flow path, contributing to inundation. (p4) [IMG]
* Quant: two properties (p4) [IMG]

### Wood End
* The nearby settlement experienced flooding during the same high‑intensity rainfall event on 11 June 2023 (noted as being affected during the same event that produced internal flooding in Piccadilly Crescent). (p4)

### Trinity Road, Wood End
* The 150mm VC drainage system between Piccadilly Crescent and Trinity Road had works (continuous liner installation) to address drainage connectivity and reduce flood risk; this links Trinity Road into the drainage interventions for affected properties. (p6) [TAB]

### Thistlewood Brook
* {general}: The brook was identified for cleansing to allow free flow and reduce the risk of backup to nearby properties; the action was listed as ongoing but is noted as 'On hold' with the comment that property flooding is not linked to fluvial flood risk. (p6) [TAB]


## Topics without coordinates

### North Warwickshire Borough Council
* Reported and verified internal flooding of two properties on Piccadilly Crescent. Photographic evidence was provided to the LLFA and a site visit concluded flooding arose from a high-intensity rainfall event that exceeded the capacity of the piped drainage network and/or prevented flows entering the piped network quickly enough; the two properties are located at the low spot of Piccadilly Crescent and were in the surface water flow path, with front landscaping creating an accessible route for flows onto the properties. (p4)
* Quant: two properties (p4)

### Warwickshire County Council
* Responsible authorities (WCC LLFA and NWBC) are implementing measures addressing drainage and flooding affecting Piccadilly Crescent, including establishing ownership of the 150mm VC drainage network and ensuring the system is maintained to reduce surface-water/drainage-related flooding. (p6) [TAB]
