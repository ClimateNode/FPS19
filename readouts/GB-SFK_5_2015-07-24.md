

# Carlton Colville flooding 24 Jul 2015

Reference document:<br>Kirkley Stream Flood Report – December 2015<br>124\_Suffolk County/124\_2015\_Suffolk County/2015-December-Kirkley-flood-update.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Carlton Colville
* Drainage systems in the Carlton Colville area were inspected and, where necessary, cleared (Suffolk Highways and Anglian Water) as part of responses to flood risk; local residents are being supported (JEPU) following the flooding issues. (p1)

### Aldwyck Way, Lowestoft
* Aldwyck Way (spelled Adwyck/Aldwyck in the text) formed part of the area that was the subject of the Section 19 investigation following the one-night flood; residents were affected by that event. (p1)

### Velda Close, Lowestoft
* Properties in Velda Close were flooded during the one-night event; several private residents remained unable to return to their properties for many months while some Housing Association residents have returned home. This location was the subject of the Section 19 investigation and is a focus for clearance and resilience actions. (p1)

### Tom Crisp Way, Lowestoft
* {general}: The junction of Tom Crisp Way and Southwell Road contains an Anglian Water trash screen that catches debris flowing down Kirkley Stream; blockages at this location can cause flooding. Anglian Water is reviewing/redesigning the screen and installing a level monitor on the grate (with a radio link to the Fen Park pumping station) to give warnings and enable rapid removal of debris to reduce chances of flooding caused by stream blockages. (p1)

### Southwell Road, Lowestoft
* {general}: The junction of Southwell Road and Tom Crisp Way has an Anglian Water trash screen that catches debris from Kirkley Stream; blockages here can cause flooding. It is being reviewed for redesign and a level monitor will be installed to warn of rising stream levels to allow rapid action to prevent/block flooding. (p1)

### River Kirkley Stream
* Overflowing/blocked Kirkley Stream caused flooding along its course including the area around Velda Close/Adwyck Way; triggered surveys, clearance works (vegetation/silt removal and channel clearance), and other measures to reduce future flood risk. (p1)

### Fen Park
* {general}: The Anglian Water pumping station at Fen Park will receive radio-linked alerts from a level monitor installed on the trash screen at Tom Crisp Way/Southwell Road; these alerts will notify operators if water levels rise in Kirkley Stream so local action can be taken to keep the stream free of blockages and reduce flooding risk. (p1)


## Topics without coordinates

### Anglian Water
* {general}: Anglian Water is directly involved in flood-response and risk reduction: jointly funding clearance of vegetation/silt in the stream, inspecting/clearing drainage in Carlton Colville, reviewing and potentially redesigning the trash screen at Tom Crisp Way/Southwell Road, and installing telemetry/level monitoring to notify the Fen Park pumping station/Operational Management Centre when stream levels rise to enable local action to prevent blockages and flooding. (p1)

### Suffolk County Council
* Suffolk County Council jointly funded and will directly carry out clearance of vegetation and silt in the stream channel and banks, inspected and cleared drainage in the Carlton Colville area, and is agreeing ongoing maintenance procedures to reduce future flood risk after the July 2015 event. (p1)

### Environment Agency
* The Environment Agency undertook clearance work upstream of Bloodmoor Roundabout (main river section) to increase flows and reduce flood risk; removal of a mature oak tree that may increase flood risk was considered but delayed due to protected bats, with a by-pass channel as a potential alternative. (p1)

### Cotman Housing Association
* Properties managed by Cotman Housing Association were flooded during the one-night flood in July 2015; residents who were displaced are reported to be back in their homes. (p1)
