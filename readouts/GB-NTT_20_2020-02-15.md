### Nottinghamshire
* Widespread flooding during Storm Dennis with numerous flood warnings and alerts across the county; local transport disruption and emergency response actions were implemented. (p1)
* Sustained intense rainfall on 16 February 2020, on already saturated ground, resulted in extensive flooding across the Nottinghamshire area; a major incident was declared. (p2)
* Quant: 38 flood warnings; 16 flood alerts; 51 road closures; over 70 flood signs; over 4000 sandbags (p1)
* Quant: 12 properties (p2)

### Rushcliffe
* Communities in Rushcliffe were affected by the flooding and received emergency support, sandbags, recovery management and grants from Rushcliffe Borough Council and partner authorities. (p5) [PAG]

### Cotgrave
* Part of the extensive flooding on 16 February 2020; internal flooding occurred to properties on local streets (Hickling Way, Fosse Walk, Marlwood and Owthorpe Road). (p2) [IMG]
* Extended rainfall saturated adjacent open fields causing surface water runoff to flow down a steep embankment into rear gardens of properties on Hickling Way, Fosse Walk and Marlwood; water entered properties at the rear before dispersing into the highway drainage at the front, resulting in internal flooding of residential properties. (p4)
* Quant: 12 properties (p2) [IMG]

### Hickling Way, Cotgrave

## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Coordinated/oversaw local flood response during Storm Dennis: response actions facilitated (via contractor) including road closures, deployment of flood signage and distribution of sandbags across the county. (p1)
* {general}: Coordinated emergency planning and response; administered the Flooding Hardship Fund; initiated and led the Section 19 Flood Investigation; liaised with landowners on riparian ownership advice; supports Flood Warden and Community Flood Signage schemes to assist communities affected by the flood. (p5)
* Quant: 51 road closures; over 70 flood signs; over 4000 sandbags; 38 flood warnings; 16 flood alerts (p1)

### Environment Agency
* {general}: Issued a record-breaking number of flood warnings and alerts across England in response to the Storm Dennis rainfall. (p2)
* Partners with Nottinghamshire County Council to administer Flood Warden and Community Flood Signage schemes to support communities at risk and assist in flood response and resilience activities following the flood. (p5)

### Rushcliffe Borough Council
* Provided emergency response support; delivered sandbags to affected residents; managed and continues to manage recovery in affected Rushcliffe communities; administered the flood hardship fund and community recovery grant and a council tax/business rate relief grant scheme; engaged in the Section 19 Flood Investigation. (p5) [PAG]
