

# Prudhoe flooding 06 Aug 2011

## Place names with coordinates

### Northumberland
* Portion of the county (Prudhoe) experienced flooding on 06/08/2012 caused by ordinary watercourses, surface water and sewer sources; impacts recorded in Prudhoe included business and road flooding. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 6 business properties; 3 roads (p1) [IMG]

### Prudhoe
* Flooding in Prudhoe on 06/08/2012 was caused by ordinary watercourses, surface water and sewer sources. Flood extent and flow-paths on the map show impacts clustered near watercourses and surface-water flow directions. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* {01 Apr 2011 - 30 Apr 2011, 06 Aug 2011, 26 Aug 2011, 24 Jun 2012, 28 Jun 2012, 06 Jul 2012, 25 Sep 2012}: Multiple documented flood events in recent years. Surface water and drainage problems recorded; a collapsed surface water drainage sewer near the town centre on 6 August 2011 caused localised flooding and increased surface water runoff into Low Prudhoe via highway and natural flow routes. The extent of internal property flooding is not known from the report; reported incidents relate to surface water and drainage issues. (p2) [MUL]
* Quant: 6 business properties; 3 roads (p1) [IMG, PAG]

### Low Prudhoe
* {01 Apr 2011 - 30 Apr 2011, 06 Aug 2011, 26 Aug 2011, 24 Jun 2012, 28 Jun 2012, 06 Jul 2012, 25 Sep 2012}: Majority of historic flooding problems occur in Low Prudhoe. Flood sources reported as surface water sewer outflows, ordinary watercourses (culverted and open) and overland flow. Industrial premises toward the toe of the wooded bank and land adjacent to the A695 frequently experience flooding or an imminent threat of flooding from moderate to heavy rainfall. (p2) [MUL]

### Station Road, Wylam
* During the 6 August 2011 event a collapsed surface water drainage sewer near Prudhoe town centre caused localised flooding and sent flows down the highway (Station Road), contributing to surface water runoff towards Low Prudhoe. (p2)
