

# Parbold flooding 01 Nov 2021 - 30 Nov 2021

Reference document:<br>November 2021 Flooding In Lancashire: Brandreth Drive, Parbold, West Lancashire<br>069\_Lancashire County/section-19-flood-investigation-report-brandreth-drive-parbold-november-2021.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Parbold
* Parbold was affected by the November 2021 flooding event. (p1)
* Residents on Brandreth Drive in Parbold reported internal flooding of a house on 01 Nov 2021 (and a more significant internal flood on 05 Oct 2021). Nearby external areas and gardens were extremely boggy as a result of the local culverted watercourse being capacity-constrained and obstructed on private land. (p3)
* An historic culverted watercourse in disrepair prevented proper drainage; under heavy rainfall a local house flooded (water entered from beneath the property and from a natural water source). The culvert was subsequently repaired and there have been no repeat reports of flooding to homes at this location since the repairs. (p6)
* Quant: 1 property flooded internally on 01 Nov 2021; significant internal flooding on 05 Oct 2021. (p3)
* Quant: 1 house (p6)

### West Lancashire
* The West Lancashire area was affected by the November 2021 flooding event. (p1)
* A very localised flooding incident in the West Lancashire area (Parbold, Brandreth Drive) on 01 Nov 2021 was caused by capacity constraints and obstructions in a private culverted watercourse. The issue was addressed by private owners repairing/replacing culvert sections; the report found no outstanding works required by the local highway authority or lead local flood authority. (p3)
* Quant: 1 property flooded internally on 01 Nov 2021. (p3)

### Brandreth Drive, Parbold
* Brandreth Drive was impacted by the November 2021 flooding event. (p1)
* Internal flooding of a house on the street on 01 Nov 2021 (and a more significant internal flood on 05 Oct 2021). Historic internal floods at the same property occurred in Sept 2019 and Feb 2020. Flooding was caused by a capacity-constrained and obstructed culverted watercourse passing under the house and garden and through neighbouring gardens; nearby gardens and external areas were extremely boggy. The issue was resolved when damaged/obstructed lengths of the culvert within private property boundaries were replaced or repaired. (p3)
* Repeated surface water flooding incidents overwhelmed the local highway drainage. The culverted watercourse leaving the public highway and entering private land had become damaged/dysfunctional, leading to out-of-hours emergency responses where homes appeared to be at risk. Landowners later carried out coordinated repairs and, after those works, no further reports of homes flooding in this location were recorded. (p5)
* Quant: 1 property flooded internally on 01 Nov 2021; additional significant internal flooding on 05 Oct 2021; previous incidents in Sept 2019 and Feb 2020. (p3)

### The Common, Parbold
* The culverted watercourse outfalls to a property at The Common where it reaches the local surface water pumping station and discharges into the public sewer network. This outfall and pumping station formed part of the flow path associated with the flooding of Brandreth Drive. (p3)


## Topics without coordinates

### Lancashire County Council
* {05 Oct 2021, 01 Nov 2021 - 30 Nov 2021}: The council's local Highways team responded to repeated reports of flooding on Brandreth Drive and investigated. Investigations identified the point where an historic culverted local watercourse left the public highway and entered private land; the reported flooding was found to be very localised and caused entirely by capacity constraints and obstructions on private land to that culvert. The report documents flooding of a home on 05 Oct 2021 and 01 Nov 2021. (p4) [MUL]
* {05 Oct 2021, 01 Nov 2021 - 30 Nov 2021}: Quant: 1 home (p4) [MUL]
