

# Kingsbury flooding 18 Feb 2020, Kingsbury flooding 14 Jun 2020 and Kingsbury flooding 17 Jun 2020 - 18 Jun 2020

## Place names with coordinates

### Kingsbury

* {14 Jun 2020, 17 Jun 2020, 18 Jun 2020}: Internal property flooding occurred in the Pit Hill area of Kingsbury as a result of intense thunderstorms. (p3) [MUL]

* {general}: Flooding occurred within Kingsbury limited to Piccadilly Crescent where a single property was flooded. (p7)

(p11) [MUL]

### Piccadilly Crescent, Piccadilly
* {2020}: Flooding occurred at Piccadilly Crescent during the 2020 flood event. (p1)
* {2020, 18 Feb 2020, 14 Jun 2020, 17 Jun 2020 - 18 Jun 2020}: Flooding affected residents at Piccadilly Crescent in 2020. Authorities (WCC, the Borough Council and WCC Highways) identified affected residents, provided advice and guidance, and have carried out, and continue to carry out, remedial works to the drainage infrastructure. (p4)
* {general}: A property on Piccadilly Crescent experienced flooding; the investigation and map identify a single flooded property at this location. (p7)
* {01 Jun 2020 - 30 Jun 2020}: The drainage system serving Piccadilly Crescent (surveyed jointly by Warwickshire County Council Highways and Flood Risk Management) was subject to jetting, camera surveys and sonde surveys as part of the investigation following flooding. Restrictions in culverts/drains are linked to internal flooding of nearby residential properties. (p8) [PAG]
* {general}: Surface water runoff entered the local piped drainage (SWS) network which became restricted. CCTV found a significant hole, cracking and displaced joints in the 150mm vitrified clay pipe draining Piccadilly Crescent to Thistlewood Brook. The damaged pipe caused blockages and surcharge from a chamber in a garden, leading to highway build-up and overtopping of the kerb. Flow was funnelled through lowered front gardens and entered properties via front doors and open joints, causing internal property flooding. (p10) [PAG]
* {general}: No known reports of property flooding on Piccadilly Crescent prior to 2020. Drainage-related interventions have been carried out to reduce flood risk (establish ownership of the 150mm VC network, gully cleansing schedule, continuous liner installed between Piccadilly Crescent and Trinity Road, installation of a new chamber to mitigate backup). These works indicate risk of surface water / drainage-related flooding to the street and properties. (p11) [TAB, PAG]
* {18 Feb 2020, 14 Jun 2020, 17 Jun 2020 - 18 Jun 2020}: Quant: 2 properties (p10) [PAG]

### Warwickshire
* {18 Feb 2020, 14 Jun 2020, 17 Jun 2020, 18 Jun 2020}: Parts of the county experienced heavy rainfall (18 February 2020) and isolated intense thunderstorms (14, 17 and 18 June 2020) leading to internal property flooding in locations including Pit Hill; internal property flooding was also experienced elsewhere in the county though not meeting the investigation threshold. (p3) [MUL]
* {2020}: Flooding occurred within the county area during 2020 leading to investigations and recovery activity (identification of affected residents, provision of advice/guidance and remedial works to drainage infrastructure) including work at Piccadilly Crescent, Kingsbury. (p4)
* {18 Feb 2020, 14 Jun 2020, 17 Jun 2020, 18 Jun 2020}: Quant: 2 residential bungalows (p3) [MUL]

### North Warwickshire
* {general}: Two properties within the borough flooded due to surcharge and collapse/holes in a surface-water-collecting piped drainage system. North Warwickshire Borough Council (NWBC) funded relining works to the SWS pipe as a remediation action. (p10)
* {general}: Quant: 2 properties (p10)

### Piccadilly
* {general}: Properties in the Piccadilly settlement (specifically on Piccadilly Crescent) experienced internal flooding after surface water entered and surcharged the piped drainage system due to a collapsed/holed pipe and restricted outfall. The nearby Thistlewood Brook is heavily silted and may have exacerbated the situation but is not identified as the primary cause. (p10) [PAG]
* {general}: Quant: 2 properties (p10) [PAG]

### Trinity Road, Piccadilly
* {01 Jun 2020 - 30 Jun 2020}: The drainage system at Trinity Road was surveyed and subject to jetting, camera surveys and sonde surveys as part of the response. Restrictions in the artificial drainage infrastructure contributed to internal flooding of nearby residential dwellings. (p8)
* {general}: Historic issues of highway flooding and surcharge from gullies and manholes have occurred on Trinity Road; additional survey works were procured to investigate these highway drainage issues. (p10)
* {general}: Historic issues of highway flooding and surcharge from gullies and manholes have been reported at this location. Additional survey works were procured on Trinity Road as part of the investigation and are being continued by WCC Highways. (p11) [TAB, PAG]

### Thistlewood Brook
* {2020}: The brook is described as having poor conveyance and heavy siltation that restricts the outlet for the Piccadilly Crescent drainage system; this restriction may have contributed to or exacerbated the property flooding at Piccadilly Crescent, though it is not considered the primary mechanism. (p10)
* {general}: The watercourse (Thistlewood Brook) is being cleansed to allow free flow of water. Actions also include installation of a new chamber with a grated cover to mitigate against backup from the ordinary watercourse, indicating the brook (or associated ordinary watercourse) can cause backup/flooding. (p11) [TAB]
* {2020}: Quant: two properties (p10)


## Topics without coordinates

### Pit Hill
* {14 Jun 2020, 17 Jun 2020, 18 Jun 2020}: Internal property flooding of residential properties in the Pit Hill area. (p3) [MUL]
* {01 Jun 2020 - 30 Jun 2020}: Short-duration, high-intensity rainfall produced surface water flooding of gardens and low-lying land. Internal flooding to residential dwellings occurred due to restrictions in the artificial drainage infrastructure (culverts/drains). (p8) [PAG]
* {14 Jun 2020, 17 Jun 2020, 18 Jun 2020}: Quant: 2 residential bungalows (p3) [MUL]

### Highways
* {general}: Procured and are continuing survey works on Trinity Road to investigate historic highway flooding and surcharge from gullies and manholes as part of the Section 19 investigation. (p10)
* {general}: Directly responsible for operational flood-reduction actions: scheduling highway cyclic gully cleansing on Piccadilly Crescent and installing a new chamber with a grated cover to mitigate backup from the adjacent ordinary watercourse; these works address highway/highway-drainage flooding and surcharge risks. (p11) [TAB, PAG]

### North Warwickshire Borough Council
* {2020}: North Warwickshire Borough Council funded relining works to repair the damaged surface water sewer asset after the pipe collapse/blockage that led to internal property flooding, and has been involved in investigations/repairs. (p10)

### Warwickshire County Council
* {general}: Established ownership of the 150mm VC drainage network serving Piccadilly Crescent and facilitated installation of a continuous liner between Piccadilly Crescent and Trinity Road (funded by NWBC) as part of measures to reduce surface-water flooding and surcharge issues. (p11) [TAB]
