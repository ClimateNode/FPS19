

# Towcester flooding 22 Nov 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Burcote Fields, Towcester, 22nd November 2012<br>090\_West Northamptonshire/Burcote Fields, Towcester - November 2012.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Burcote Fields, Towcester
* Intense rainfall onto a saturated catchment caused excess surface water to flow to low points on Burcote Fields. Surface water was drained via highway drainage and flowed north beyond Burcote Fields toward Burcote Road. At least one property experienced internal flooding. (p5) [PAG]
* Intense short-duration rainfall on a saturated catchment caused excess surface water to flow over ground to low points on Burcote Fields. This led to internal flooding of property(ies); flows were drained partly by highway drainage and also flowed north beyond Burcote Fields toward Burcote Road. (p6) [PAG]
* At least one residential property experienced internal flooding (water crossed the building threshold). Several other properties on the estate were identified as being at high risk of flooding. (p8) [PAG]
* A large volume and high-velocity surface water flow from the headwall of a culverted watercourse overtopped the headwall (the grille at the headwall was blocked by debris) and flowed over the footpath and into the carriageway. Flow followed natural contours to low points causing ponding immediately outside two residential properties; water entered at least one property and flooded the ground floor. (p11)
* Localised ponding reached a depth of approximately 150mm and entered at least one residential property, flooding the ground floor of that property to a depth of approximately 25mm. (p12) [PAG]
* Intense rainfall on a saturated catchment caused existing drainage systems and watercourses to be unable to convey flows; systems overtopped and water flowed to the low point at Burcote Fields. Drainage at this location was not designed to drain the quantity of water experienced to the public sewerage system. Inlets and structures were affected by silt and debris, reducing flow capacity. (p15)
* Flows of land drainage and surface water from higher ground overtopped drainage systems and collected at the low point at Burcote Fields. The existing drainage at this low point was not designed to cope with the volume experienced during the flood, resulting in overspill of land drainage onto the public highway and into private property. (p16) [PAG]
* Location of the culvert entrance where debris accumulated at the grille and downstream scour was observed; evidence indicates the field's drainage infrastructure experienced obstructive debris and erosive flows that affected the immediate area. (p35) [IMG]
* Downstream of a culvert the stream channel and a metal fence had accumulated debris (branches, leaves and litter) caught on and around the fence and culvert outlet, restricting flow. Scour marks and exposed soil on the banks indicate erosion and sediment deposition in the channel. Photographs show the low point in the Burcote Fields residential area adjacent to the channel. (p36) [IMG, PAG]
* Surface water collected at the low point in Burcote Fields and flowed toward the start of a culvert; surface water flows were reported moving through the field boundary (see gap at east boundary). (p37) [IMG, PAG]

### Towcester
* The Burcote Fields area of Towcester was affected by surface water flooding during the event; at least one property in Burcote Fields (within Towcester) flooded internally. (p5) [PAG]
* The flood incident at Burcote Fields (within Towcester) resulted in at least one property flooding internally on 22nd November 2012; the occurrence had been experienced previously within the preceding five years. (p6) [PAG]
* The culverted watercourse flowing south to north through Towcester was identified for full investigation (route, condition and all connections) as part of the response to the flood incident, indicating the culvert and its connections were implicated in the flooding. (p16)
* Quant: at least one property (p5) [PAG]
* Quant: at least one property (p6) [PAG]

## Topics without coordinates

### Towcester Catchment
* {22 Nov 2012, 24 Nov 2012, 20 Dec 2012}: Significant rainfall on 21–22 November 2012 produced flooding that affected people and property in the catchment. Recorded impacts include flooding to at least one property and one garage on 22/11/2012; subsequent incidents where flooding to two properties was prevented by provision of sandbags on 24/11/2012 and further flooding prevented by sandbags on 20/12/2012. (p10) [MUL]
* {22 Nov 2012, 24 Nov 2012, 20 Dec 2012}: Quant: one property and one garage; two properties (p10) [MUL]

### South Northamptonshire Council
* Engaged post‑flood to consult with the Environment Agency on planning for new developments to reduce flood risk and to liaise with other bodies over ownership and maintenance of the culverted ordinary watercourse through Burcote Fields following flooding that threatened properties. (p17)

### Northamptonshire County Council
* NCC (LLFA and Emergency Planning) were required to provide advice to residents, instate/liaise with a Flood Warden, produce/update a community flood plan and advise on property‑level protection. NCC Highways were tasked with regular highway drainage cleansing and assessing highway drainage asset capacity because insufficient capacity and blockage contributed to flooding of properties. (p17)

### Environment Agency
* Was asked to carry out a review of the maintenance regime of the Main River Tove downstream of Towcester because river maintenance issues were identified as relevant to the flooding and risk to downstream properties. (p17)

### Anglian Water
* Public sewerage assets (notably the headwall and grille at the start of the culverted ordinary watercourse) were implicated in flood risk; Anglian Water was asked to carry out frequent inspections and clearing and to assess sewer capacity because insufficient capacity/congestion contributed to property flood risk. (p17)
