

# Warkworth flooding 05 Dec 2013

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Warkworth<br>091\_Northumberland/Warkworth\_Flood\_Investigation\_Report\_05Dec13.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Northumberland
* A tidal surge affecting the county (east coast) resulted in overtopping at locations including Warkworth, producing localised flooding (road inundation and one internally flooded property). (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 1 property; 1 road (p1) [IMG]

### Warkworth
* Overtopping of river banks adjacent to The Butts caused the road along The Butts to become flooded and one property to suffer internal flood damage. Floodwaters retreated within about an hour as the tide ebbed. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 1 property; 1 road (p1) [IMG, PAG]


## Topics without coordinates

### East Coast Northumberland
* Sea levels along the east coast reached their highest in about 60 years during the tidal surge, which contributed to overtopping of the River Coquet's banks at Warkworth. (p1)

### Environment Agency
* {general}: Completed the Warkworth flood alleviation scheme which reduces the risk of flooding to the Butts; added further detail to flood alert texts (improvements to warning communication). (p2) [TAB]
