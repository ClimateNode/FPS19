## Place names with coordinates

### Sycamore Way, Brantham
* Internal flooding affected a property on more than one occasion at this location during the reported flood events. (p2)
* {01 Jun 2019 - 30 Jun 2019, 01 Nov 2020 - 30 Nov 2020, 21 Dec 2020 - 27 Dec 2020}: Surface-water flooding following significant rainfall caused internal flooding to a property (hallway and an outbuilding) and flooding of the public highway. Investigation found a section of surface-water pipe removed by UK Power Network; possible exceedance or blockages of the highway surface-water drainage system are noted. The area is identified on Environment Agency surface-water flood mapping. (p3) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
* Location affected by surface water flooding during significant rainfall; highway surface water drainage capacity may have been exceeded and blockages/breakages were present (a section of surface water pipe was found removed). Actions taken included investigation and remediation of drainage blockages, review of inspection/maintenance schedule, and consideration of additional gullies to capture surface water. Property-level resilience advice to be provided for affected properties. (p4) [TAB]

## Topics without coordinates

### Suffolk County Council
* Public highway (Sycamore Way) was flooded; council is named as the highway authority responsible for maintenance and is recommended to investigate and remediate blockages/breakages, review inspection/maintenance schedules and consider additional gullies. (p3) [PAG]
* Responsible highway authority for an area affected by surface water flooding following significant rainfall. Investigated and remedied blockages/breakages in the highway surface water drainage system, reviewed inspection and maintenance schedule for Sycamore Way assets, and confirmed outfall was clear (action status recorded as Complete). Recommended further works (investigate highway drainage capacity, consider additional gullies) and property-level resilience advice for affected properties. (p4) [TAB, PAG]

### UK Power Network
* A section of surface water pipe was found to have been removed by UK Power Network; this removal is noted in the investigation as a possible factor contributing to drainage failure and the surface water flooding observed. (p3)

### Highway Authority
* Authority responsible for investigating and remediating blockages/breakages to the surface water drainage system linked to surface water flooding after significant rainfall. Actions include reviewing inspection and maintenance schedules, investigating drainage capacity and considering additional gullies, advising riparian owners, and promoting property-level resilience measures. (p4) [TAB, PAG]
