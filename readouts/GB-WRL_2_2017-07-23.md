### Wirral
* Eastern Wirral (from Birkenhead through to Bromborough) experienced an intense short‑duration rainfall event that caused rapid surface water runoff, localised flooding and property damage and disruption to transport routes. (p4)
* A band of intense, localised rainfall on 23 July 2017 caused internal property flooding and property damage across parts of the Wirral. Transport disruption occurred: floodwater affected Woodchurch Road (remained passable) and the Wallasey Tunnel approach roads (remained passable). Press reports identified flooding in Birkenhead Park and adjacent roads. (p10)
* Parts of the Wirral experienced intense, short-duration rainfall on 23rd July 2017 that produced localised flash and surface-water flooding. Radar analysis was used to identify areas of heavy, localised rainfall that caused flooding within the borough. (p12)
* Properties across the borough experienced internal and external flooding. Internally flooded properties were concentrated in central Birkenhead while external flooding was more widely distributed across the peninsula. Flooding incidents were mapped in GIS and verified incidents entered into the LLFA flooding register. (p15) [IMG]
* Widespread flooding caused by excessive/intense rainfall that exceeded the capacity of highway gullies and United Utilities combined sewers, plus an operational problem on the combined sewer. Impacts across the borough included internal flooding of properties in Oxton, structural damage to highways at Townfield Lane and Shrewsbury Road, and overland flow through Birkenhead Park that flooded adjacent roads and businesses. (p17)
* Intense short-duration rainfall across parts of Wirral exceeded highway gully and sewer capacity and soil infiltration, causing rapid onset surface water and sewer flooding that led to properties being flooded, especially in eastern Wirral. (p23) [PAG]
