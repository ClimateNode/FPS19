### Newark and Sherwood
* Areas within the district including Newark-on-Trent experienced flooding on 20 October 2023 due to sustained heavy rainfall during Storm Babet; roads and properties were flooded as the ground was saturated. (p1)
* District council delivered intensive response to flooding: formed large multi-department staff team working through the event; visited impacted communities and vulnerable individuals; assisted evacuations and provided emergency accommodation; delivered aqua sacs and prepared/cleaned roads, paths and removed bulky waste from flooded homes as event transitioned from surface water flooding to ongoing river flood risk; coordinated in-person visits where extreme River Trent levels were predicted; commenced recovery and grant funding to eligible affected residents. (p9)

### Nottinghamshire
* County-level emergency response during the flood: provided 24-hour Emergency Planning support; liaised with community flood signage schemes; delivered sandbags to affected areas and communities; closed roads to promote safety and avoid additional flood damage; shared resources with district/borough partners; communicated key messages to public, partners and elected members; met with communities after the event to inform the Section 19 report and next steps; LLFA to consider Property Flood Resilience Programme for communities affected by Storm Babet. (p9) [PAG]
* Quant: 77 roads (p9) [PAG]

### River Trent
* Predicted extreme river levels from the River Trent created an increased risk of river flooding, prompting coordinated in-person visits by the council to high-risk communities to reinforce actions to prepare for potential flooding. (p9)

### River Devon

## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* As Lead Local Flood Authority, the council witnessed flood devastation to communities, prioritised the most vulnerable, is liaising with the Environment Agency over flooding from the River Devon, and will review its Property Flood Resilience Programme for communities affected by Storm Babet and take recommendations through the Cabinet. (p10)

### Severn Trent
* Severn Trent reported it had received reports of flooding in the area and is completing ongoing investigations into the source of that flooding; the company actively engaged with the Section 19 flood investigation. (p9)

### Environment Agency
* Will be liaised with by the LLFA in relation to flooding from the River Devon; involved as a Risk Management Authority in investigating the incident. (p10)
