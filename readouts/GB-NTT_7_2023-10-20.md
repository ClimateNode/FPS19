## Place names with coordinates

### Arnold
* Areas of Arnold suffered internal flooding from sustained heavy rainfall during Storm Babet. Five residential properties and one business experienced internal flooding; additional gardens, outbuildings and roads were flooded. Flooding was exacerbated by previously saturated ground. (p1)
* Flooding affected areas within the town, specifically the streets Mansfield Road, Bonington Drive and Georgia Drive; residential properties on these streets were impacted. (p2) [PAG]
* Flooding affected multiple streets in Arnold (Mansfield Road, Bonington Drive, Georgia Drive), with floodwater entering properties and roads being submerged, causing disruption to traffic and property flooding. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Two residential properties on Bonington Drive experienced internal (internal) flooding; significant damage to gardens and outbuildings. Flooding was caused by water flowing from the Coppice Road flood storage basin after the attenuation basin overtopped, with flows travelling through back gardens on Bentwell Avenue before entering properties on Bonington Drive. Separately, one residential property on Georgia Drive was flooded when overland flows from the agricultural field to the north entered the property. (p5) [IMG, MUL]

### Gedling
* Parts of the borough (notably Arnold) experienced flooding during Storm Babet: internal flooding affected residential properties and a business, and gardens, outbuildings and roads were flooded. (p1)
* Garden flooding observed downstream of the overtopped Coppice Road Attenuation Basin; limited impact on properties such that evacuation was not required. (p7) [PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* Major flood event affected the county: internal flooding of properties was verified; council staff attended sites to assess internal flooding, fed verified flood impact data to partners, delivered sandbags and closed roads to reduce additional flood damage. (p7) [PAG]
* Quant: 77 roads (p7) [PAG]

### Coppice Road, Arnold
* The flood storage/attenuation basin at Coppice Road overtopped during the event, releasing water that flowed through back gardens on Bentwell Avenue and into properties on Bonington Drive. (p5)

### Bentwell Avenue, Arnold
* Back gardens of properties on Bentwell Avenue were flooded as water from the Coppice Road basin flowed through them en route to Bonington Drive. (p5)


## Topics without coordinates

### Gedling Borough Council
* Observed overtopping of the Coppice Road storage area and monitored downstream water levels; garden flooding was observed downstream though property impacts were limited; coordinated sandbag distribution and clean-up. (p7) [PAG]
* {general}: Gedling Borough Council established and coordinated a multi-agency group to investigate the flooding impacts arising from the Coppice Road Flood Storage Area and is working with partners on investigation and action planning. (p8)
* Quant: 1200 sandbags (p7) [PAG]

### Coppice Road Attenuation Basin
* Overtopping of the storage area on 20 October; flooding recorded at the flood storage area and contributed to raised downstream water levels. (p7)
* {general}: Flooding occurred at the Coppice Road Flood Storage area; this asset is the subject of multi-agency investigation and action planning by Severn Trent, Gedling Borough Council and Nottinghamshire County Council. (p8)

### Severn Trent
* {general}: Severn Trent Water are aware of flooding at the Coppice Road Flood Storage area and are working with Gedling Borough Council and Nottinghamshire County Council to investigate the flooding and develop an action plan; Severn Trent actively engaged with the Section 19 flood investigation. (p8)

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* As Lead Local Flood Authority, Nottinghamshire County Council is leading a feasibility study to assess flood risk management options in Arnold, working with GBC and STW, and will consider property flood resilience measures for communities affected by the flood event referenced (Storm Babet). (p8)
