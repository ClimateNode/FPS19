

# Gunthorpe flooding 01 Jan 2024 - 31 Jan 2024

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Gunthorpe – Storm Henk, January 2024<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/gunthorpe-s19-storm-henk-jan-2024.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### River Trent
* The River Trent forms the southern boundary of Gunthorpe and was implicated in the January 2024 flood event affecting the village. (p1) [PAG]
* Surcharging of the River Trent during the event causes backwater effects that prevent tributaries from draining, contributing to tributary and surface-water flooding in nearby settlements (including Gunthorpe). (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* The River Trent surcharged and flooded out of bank (during Storm Henk), preventing its tributaries from discharging and directly causing the village to be cut off; one property reported internal flooding from the river. (p3)
* Experienced predicted extreme levels that created a continued risk of river flooding, prompting coordinated in-person visits to high-risk communities and influencing preparedness and evacuation actions. (p6)
* Quant: 1 property (p3)

### Gunthorpe
* The village was affected by flooding during Storm Henk (January 2024). The River Trent forms the southern boundary of Gunthorpe and tributaries including the Cocker Beck run through the village; the flooding in the village prompted a Section 19 investigation into which RMAs had relevant functions and responses. (p1) [PAG]
* When the River Trent surges it prevents tributaries from discharging, causing those tributaries to surcharge and other surface-water drainage assets in the village to be unable to function effectively; Gunthorpe is described as having a significant history of flooding and is at high flood risk during the event. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Village was cut off when the River Trent flooded out of bank; Trent surcharge prevented tributaries discharging so other surface water assets in the village could not function. One property experienced internal flooding from the river; ten properties experienced internal flooding attributed to rising groundwater (residents reported water coming up through floors). Affected areas lie within Flood Zone 3. (p3) [IMG]
* Community experienced flooding leading to public meetings and a community drop-in; residents were helped to evacuate homes, aqua sacs were delivered, emergency accommodation provided where necessary; cleanup actions (roads/paths and bulky waste removal) and visits to vulnerable individuals were carried out as part of the response. (p6) [PAG]
* Residents experienced sewer-related issues and local flooding associated with the event; community required support leading to a drop-in session and direct contact from authorities. (p7) [PAG]
* Quant: 1 property; 10 properties (p3) [IMG]

### Nottinghamshire
* Experienced widespread flooding on 02/01/2024 as a result of heavy rainfall from Storm Henk. (p2)

### Newark and Sherwood
* District council conducted multi-department flood response across the district: stood up manager teams, visited impacted communities and vulnerable individuals, supported evacuations and provided emergency accommodation, coordinated cleanup of roads/paths and removal of bulky waste from flooded homes, and delivered recovery support and grants to affected residents. (p6)

### Cocker Beck
* When the River Trent surcharged on 2 January 2024 it impeded tributaries' ability to discharge; tributaries such as the Cocker experienced backwater/surcharging that contributed to local surface-water flooding in Gunthorpe. (p2) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* As Lead Local Flood Authority, the council has responsibilities to investigate the significant local flooding incident, publish the investigation, and lead emergency planning and recovery after the event; determine which risk management authorities have relevant powers. (p3)
* Verified and recorded internal flooding at properties; visited flood-affected residents and businesses to establish sources and severity; delivered sandbags; closed roads to promote safety and avoid additional flood damage; provided 24-hour emergency support and led recovery coordination including data collation and publication. (p5)
* As Lead Local Flood Authority, the council responded to the flood event: contacted affected residents about sewer-related issues, attended and supported the Gunthorpe community drop-in, is continuing investigations into causes, and will support community flood resilience measures (e.g. signage) and strengthen riparian enforcement/asset inspection. (p7)
* Quant: 69 roads closed (p5)

### Environment Agency
* Operated flood patrols and managed hydraulic structures during the flood event; cleared blockages in the area before and after peak flows; coordinated community information officers to visit and gather information from affected communities. (p6) [PAG]

### Severn Trent
* Received reports of sewer flooding in the area, undertook extensive investigations into causes, contacted affected residents about sewer-related issues, and attended/support community drop-in sessions in Gunthorpe following the storm. (p6)
