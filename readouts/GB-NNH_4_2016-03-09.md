### St Catherine's Road, Ise Lodge
* Surface water from heavy rainfall on a saturated catchment flowed over ground following natural contours into low points around private property, causing internal flooding of a property on St Catherine's Road. (p7)
* Internal flooding entered a property on St Catherine's Road (water crossed the building threshold). (p9) [PAG, MUL]
* A property on St Catherine's Road was flooded. Surface water runoff accumulated to the rear/adjacent to the property (localised area shown at high risk), with roofs and paved areas draining to the surface water sewer that connects to public sewers on Deeble Road. A surface water public sewer manhole and highway gully are located in front of the flooded property; runoff from nearby agricultural fields flowed toward the rear of the property. (p10)
* {04 Nov 2012, 21 Dec 2012, 24 Dec 2012, 09 Mar 2016}: Surface water runoff from adjacent arable land flowed into rear gardens, ponded to approximately 300mm depth at the rear of a garden, entered air bricks and flooded a garage; properties were affected by the inundation of rear gardens. (p11) [TAB, PAG, MUL]
* Surface water runoff from arable fields ponded to the rear of a garden and entered private property. Water collected (~300 mm) at the rear boundary, flowed into a neighbouring garden then down the west side of the garden to the patio and around the north and west elevations of the house, entering air bricks and the garage. Reported water depth at the house was 100–150 mm. Floodwater then flowed along both sides of the driveway onto St Catherine's Road, overwhelming the adjacent highway gully and flooding across and down the road. (p12) [PAG]
* Heavy rainfall over a saturated catchment caused drainage systems to be ineffective, resulting in excess surface water flowing over ground following natural contours into low points around private properties on St Catherine's Road. Some properties experienced surface water intrusion to gardens and private areas; one property further west has a raised rear garden wall that provided a defence. (p13)
* {04 Nov 2012}: Residents' photographs document flooding at properties on St Catherine's Road (images labelled 'Property front right', 'Rear patio', 'Property front left'), showing the road's properties were inundated during the event. (p36)
* {24 Dec 2012}: Residential properties on the street were flooded; photographs from the incident show water affecting the front and side of a property on St Catherine's Road. (p41)
* External areas of residential properties (front, side and rear patio) were inundated; residents' photographs from the incident show floodwater affecting property curtilage and entrances. (p43)

### Kettering
* The flood investigation covers an incident in Ise Lodge (St Catherine's Road), which is located within Kettering. (p2)
* Surface water runoff following heavy rainfall on a saturated catchment overwhelmed land drainage systems and flowed to low points around private property in Kettering; this led to internal flooding of one property on St Catherine's Road. (p7)
* An internal flooding incident occurred on St Catherine's Road within Kettering; one property was flooded internally. (p9) [MUL]
* Localised surface water flooding occurred within the Kettering area (specifically at St Catherine's Road) due to heavy rainfall over a saturated catchment and absence/alteration of drainage routes, directing runoff towards private properties. (p13)
* Overland surface-water flooding in a residential area converging at a location adjacent to football pitches. The map shows a blue-shaded flood area and flow paths from surrounding residential catchments and the football pitches that converge at the site. Significant ponding was witnessed at a junction (marked '01') and an approximate drainage manhole associated with the football pitch development is marked ('02'), indicating localised ponding and drainage issues at those points.
