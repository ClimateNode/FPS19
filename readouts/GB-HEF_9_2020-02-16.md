

# Cross Keys flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Cross Keys<br>055\_Herefordshire County/08\_Cross\_Keys.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Cross Keys
* The settlement is the primary investigation area and is shown within the red-circled focus on the Environment Agency Flood Map for Planning and adjacent to water features and flood-prone land, indicating exposure to river flooding risk. (p1) [IMG]
* Internal flooding of residential properties and external flooding of outbuildings and gardens. Properties and gardens were inundated by fluvial flows (from the Lugg/Kymin Brook) and surface water; some respondents reported blocked bridges, overflowing ditches and blocked gullies contributing to flood mechanisms. (p2)
* Cross Keys experienced flooding primarily caused by fluvial flows from the Little Lugg and its tributary. (p3)
* Quant: 8 properties; depths ranging between less than 6 inches and 2 ft (internal); outbuildings and gardens up to 3 ft; flood durations up to 3 days; five properties with previous flooding (three of which recorded flooding in 2007) (p2)

### River Lugg
* Identified as the source of fluvial flooding affecting Cross Keys; high water levels in the Lugg (and its tributary the Kymin Brook) contributed to inundation of properties and to flows bypassing culverts onto the A465. (p2)
* Fluvial flows from the Little Lugg (and its tributary) are identified as the primary source of flooding affecting Cross Keys. (p3)


## Topics without coordinates

### A465
* Fluvial flooding of the carriageway where Kymin Brook is conveyed beneath the road; flows bypassed a culvert and spilled onto the carriageway, resulting in a road closure during the flood. (p2) [TAB]
* The A465 culvert is reported as a possible constriction to flow and may have exacerbated fluvial flooding in the area (Cross Keys). (p3)
* Quant: Road closure implemented on 16th February 2020 (p2) [TAB]

### Kymin Brook
* Kymin Brook (a tributary of the Little Lugg) was identified as a primary source of fluvial flooding at Cross Keys, contributing to internal flooding of properties, flooding of outbuildings and gardens, and causing fluvial flows to bypass a culvert under the A465 and spill onto the carriageway. (p2)
* Identified by Herefordshire Council LLFA as a focus for a feasibility study (alongside Little Lugg Brook) because of existing fluvial flood risk; included in actions arising from the recent flooding response. (p3)
* Quant: 8 properties; internal depths between less than 6 inches and 2 ft; outbuildings and gardens flooded up to 3 ft; flood durations up to 3 days; five properties with previous flooding (three recording 2007). (p2)

### Lugg Brook
* Fluvial flows from the Little Lugg and its tributary were identified as the primary source of flooding affecting Cross Keys. (p3)
