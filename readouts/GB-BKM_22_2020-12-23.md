### Thornton
* {1917, 2011, 23 Dec 2020}: During the 23 December 2020 storm the catchment was very sensitive due to saturated soils, and flows in the watercourses through the village were higher than normal (event rarity approx. 10–50 years). The report also notes few recorded historic flooding events affecting Thornton in 1917 and 2011. (p5) [MUL]
* Extreme rainfall and overtopping of local watercourses caused overland flow and channel overflow through the village, resulting in confirmed internal flooding of residential properties, resident displacement, loss of possessions and negative mental and physical health impacts. Channel edges showed slumping and erosion around culvert headwalls; lack of safe relief flow routes forced water onto roads. A culvert on Nash Road was later cleared on 5 February 2021. (p6)
* During the 23 December 2020 event the local watercourse overtopped its channel and forced water onto roads. Site inspection (June–July 2021) showed channel-edge slumping and erosion around culvert headwalls consistent with turbulent high flows and overtopping; lack of safe relief flow routes resulted in road inundation and properties being identified as at-risk of internal/external flooding. (p7) [TAB]

The report focused on understanding flood sources, pathways and receptors and the impacts on the community and local drainage infrastructure, using RoFSW/Flood Map for Planning, LiDAR, photographs, rainfall data, residents' questionnaires and information on drainage infrastructure. (p13)
