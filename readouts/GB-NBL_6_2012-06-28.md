

# Alnwick flooding 28 Jun 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Alnwick (West)<br>091\_Northumberland/Alnwick-west-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Northumberland
* Heavy rain on 28/06/2012 caused surface water flooding and flooding associated with an ordinary watercourse (the Wash Burn) in west Alnwick. Properties, roads and businesses in the west of Alnwick were flooded when water backed up in a blocked culverted watercourse and emerged from gardens. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 4 residential properties; 1 business; 1 other building; 3 roads (p1) [IMG]

### Alnwick
* Heavy rain on 28 June 2012 caused surface water flooding and flooding associated with an ordinary watercourse (the Wash Burn) in west Alnwick, resulting in inundation of properties, roads and businesses. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 4 residential properties; 1 business; 1 other building; 3 roads (p1) [IMG]

### Alwynside, Alnwick
* Residential properties in Alwynside were flooded when the Wash Burn (an ordinary watercourse) runs in a culvert beneath southern properties; water emerged from gardens and entered properties. Blockages in the culvert caused water to back up and flood properties. (p1) [MUL]
* The Wash Burn (including a culverted section) experienced blockages that caused water to back up and subsequently flood residential properties; excavations in a garden and the culverted stone-to-concrete transition are documented in photos. Remedial works (clearing blockages, installing manholes) were carried out. (p2) [TAB]
* Quant: 4 residential properties (p1) [MUL]

### St Thomas Crescent, Alnwick
* Surface water flow routes and affected roads on the map indicate flooding of residential streets forming a loop through St Thomas Close (sic). Residential areas around St Thomas Close were primarily impacted by the surface water flooding on 28/06/2012. (p1) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Northumberland County Council
* Investigated the route of the Wash Burn through Alwynside, removed blockages within the culvert and installed manholes on two sections of the watercourse as remedial actions after the Wash Burn backed up and flooded residential properties. Worked with Johnnie Johnson Housing Association on a culverted section passing through their land. (p2) [TAB]

### Johnnie Johnson Housing Association
* A section of culvert that passes through land for which the association has riparian responsibilities was implicated in the Wash Burn backing up and flooding properties; the association worked with NCC while blockages were removed and manholes installed. (p2) [TAB]
