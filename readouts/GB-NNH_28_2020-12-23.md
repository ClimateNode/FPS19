

# Geddington flooding 23 Dec 2020

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Geddington, 23 December 2020<br>090\_North Northamptonshire/Flood investigation report - Stamford Road and Newton Road, Geddington.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Newton Road, Geddington
* Subject of a North Northamptonshire Council (LLFA) Section 19 flood investigation into flooding at Stamford Road/Newton Road, Geddington. (p2)
* Internal flooding occurred at properties on Newton Road; water entered buildings during the event. (p6)
* Flooding at Newton Road on 23rd December 2020 led to internal flooding of properties in the area and contributed to a formal S19 investigation being undertaken. (p8)
* {1980s to 1990s}: External flooding affected a property on Newton Road (recorded as an external flooding event). (p9) [TAB]
* Overland / surface water flooding inundated rear gardens and caused internal flooding in several properties (ground floor and an integral garage). One resident moved out for a period after internal flooding and has since installed their own flood barriers. (p15)
* Overland sheet flow and two distinct flow routes ran north–south through rear gardens leading to internal flooding of properties. Road gullies were reported lacking capacity, and a recently constructed property's bund on the northern boundary likely diverted and concentrated flows toward two other Newton Road properties. (p16) [PAG]
* {1980s to 1990s}: Quant: 1 property (p9) [TAB]
* Quant: Property 1 (north): external ~460mm; internal ~50–75mm. Property 2 (north): external ~150–200mm; internal ~25–45mm. Property 3 (north): external flooding (no depth given). Property 4 (north): external ~250mm (close to causing internal flooding). Property 5 (north): external ~25mm. Property 1 (south): internal flooding of integral garage ~200mm. Total: 3 properties internally flooded on Newton Road. (p15)
* Quant: 3 properties (internally flooded) (p16) [PAG]

### Stamford Road, Geddington
* Internal flooding occurred at properties on Stamford Road; water entered buildings during the event. (p6)
* Flooding at Stamford Road on 23rd December 2020 led to internal flooding of properties in the area and contributed to a formal S19 investigation being undertaken. (p8)
* {1980s to 1990s}: External flooding affected a property on Stamford Road (recorded as an external flooding event). (p9) [TAB]
* External flooding affected rear gardens and the frontage from the carriageway; no internal flooding reported for the properties listed. Other dwellings on Stamford Road also reported external rear-garden flooding. (p15)
* A ditch running parallel to Stamford Road breached at a culvert, causing overland flows that affected footways and front gardens. One property on Stamford Road was reported to have flooded internally. (p16) [PAG]
* {1980s to 1990s}: Quant: 1 property (p9) [TAB]
* Quant: Property 1: external flooding approx. 75mm (from carriageway); Property 4: external flooding approx. 200mm. (p15)
* Quant: 1 property (internally flooded) (p16) [PAG]

### Geddington
* Flooding at the Stamford Road / Newton Road area of Geddington caused internal flooding of several properties. (p6)
* Internal flooding occurred at several properties in Geddington during the event on 23rd December 2020, prompting a formal S19 flood investigation. (p8)
* {general}: Flooding on the northern edge of the village (north of the watercourse) resulted in external flooding of properties on Newton Road and Stamford Road. (p10)
* Intense, short-duration heavy rainfall produced overland flows from agricultural land that converged and caused internal flooding of dwellings. Lack of ditches immediately upstream of the affected homes allowed uncontrolled flows to reach properties; there were no community or property-level resilience measures in place. (p18)
* The village was the subject of a Section 19 flood investigation following flooding on 23rd December 2020. (p21)
* {general}: Quant: 1 property in Newton Road; 1 property in Stamford Road (p10)
* Quant: 4 properties (p18)

### New Road, Geddington
* Surface water flooding caused internal flooding to a conservatory in one property and external flooding to other properties. (p15)
* Converging overland flow routes crossed Newton Road and affected properties on the southern side of Newton Road and New Road; properties experienced overland/external flooding and there were localised areas of standing water. (p16)
* Quant: Property 1: internal (conservatory) ~200mm and external ~300mm. Property 2: external flooding estimated ~200–300mm. Total: 1 property internally flooded on New Road. (p15)
