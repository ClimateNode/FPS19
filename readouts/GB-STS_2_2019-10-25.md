### Sandyford Brook
* Sandyford Brook (a Main River) contributed to flooding of Sandon Road / Peel Terrace / Freeman Street in Stafford. (p21) [TAB, PAG]
* Designated as Main River and listed in the post-event investigations; identified as the Environment Agency's responsibility and implicated in local flooding risk following the October 2019 event. (p22)
* Water levels in the Sandyford Brook rose significantly during 25–26 October 2019 and the brook did not convey flow effectively (insufficient gradient / backing up). This back-up, combined with compromised highway drainage and sewerage, led to inundation of properties and multiple internal flooding incidents. (p24) [IMG]
* The brook failed to convey flows effectively (insufficient fall/gradient), causing water to back up and contribute to inundation of properties in the adjacent Sandon Road area. (p25) [TAB, PAG, MUL]
* Observed high flow during the October 2019 storm: an observed peak total flow of about 2.11 m3/s (ReFH2 hydrograph). The event’s magnitude places its return period between a 1 in 5 and 1 in 10 year event (approximately 10–20PCSYM annual probability), indicating a notable river flow event on the Sandyford Brook during the storm. (p32) [PAG]
* Observed an elevated flow event during the October 2019 storm: the measured peak flow at the Sandyford Brook location reached ~2.11 m3/s, exceeding the 1 in 5 year design flow (2.08 m3/s) and below the 1 in 10 year design flow (~2.44 m3/s), indicating a hydrological event with an estimated return period between 1 in 5 and 1 in 10 years. (p33) [PAG, IMG]
* Quant: 37 properties (p21) [TAB, PAG]
* Quant: observed peak flow 2.113 m3/s; 1 in 5 year design flow 2.08 m3/s; 1 in 10 year design flow ~2.44 m3/s; estimated event probability 10PCSYM–20PCSYM in that year (p32) [PAG]
* Quant: peak flow 2.11 m3/s; 1 in 5 year design flow 2.08 m3/s; 1 in 10 year design flow ~2.44 m3/s (p33) [PAG, IMG]
