

# Newark-On-Trent flooding 02 Jan 2024

## Place names with coordinates

### River Trent
* Flood zone 3 (highest risk) is concentrated along the River Trent; the river is the primary feature associated with the predicted river-flood extents that affect adjacent floodplain and settlements around Newark-on-Trent. (p3) [IMG]
* Rivers and tributaries (including the Trent) became surcharged during the storm, preventing drainage systems from discharging effectively and contributing to internal flooding in Newark. (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* High flows on the River Trent triggered multiple flood warnings, EA flood patrols and clearance of blockages, and deployment of pumps (over‑pumping at Brewers Wharf) to manage inundation. (p7)
* Predicted extreme River Trent levels and river floodwater entered urban areas in Newark and entered/overwhelmed the sewer network in parts of the town, contributing to local flooding. (p8)
* River flooding along the Trent (specifically referenced along Kelham Road) contributed to impacts on infrastructure, prompting resilience investigations. (p9)
* Quant: 3 flood warnings issued for locations on the River Trent (08:05; 11:42; 17:13) (p7)

### Newark-On-Trent
* Newark on Trent is the subject of this Section 19 investigation and was within the area affected by the heavy rain from Storm Henk that caused flooding on 2 January 2024. (p1) [PAG]
* Areas of Newark-on-Trent close to the River Trent lie within Flood Zone 3 (high risk) and nearby urban/peri‑urban areas are shown as being at medium to high surface-water flood risk. Figure 5 is noted to show areas affected by internal flooding within the mapped area. (p3) [IMG]
* Internal flooding affected multiple parts of the town when drainage assets were overwhelmed following heavy rainfall from Storm Henk; properties, open spaces and public highways were flooded. (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* Flood warnings were issued for River Trent locations affecting Newark; multi‑agency response and community engagement (public meeting, drop‑in event) took place to gather impacts and support affected residents. (p7)
* Experienced surface water flooding that transitioned to river flooding from the River Trent on 2 January 2024; floodwater entered and overwhelmed the sewer network in some locations of the town, causing local flooding impacts and prompting ongoing investigations. (p8)
* The Environment Agency has planned asset data improvements across Newark and commissioned an updated flood risk model to test scenarios and inform future works following flooding in the area. (p9)
* Quant: three discrete areas (p4) [IMG, PAG]

### Newark and Sherwood
* The district containing Newark on Trent was within the area affected by heavy rain from Storm Henk leading to flooding on 2 January 2024. (p1)
* Internal flooding affected properties, open spaces and public highways in Newark on Trent. Three discrete areas were internally flooded: Farndon Road area, Kelham Road area and Lime Grove. Kelham Road flooding was attributed to interaction between the floodplain catchment and the public sewerage system; Lime Grove appears to have been flooded due to intense local rainfall. Rivers and tributaries were surcharged and drainage assets were overwhelmed, preventing effective discharge. (p4)
* Newark and Sherwood District Council played an intensive role in flood response: formed a large cross-departmental team, staff worked beyond normal hours to prioritise and deliver flood support. (p7)
* Quant: 3 discrete areas (p4)

### Kelham Road, Newark-On-Trent
* Kelham Road area suffered internal flooding; the flooding there has been attributed to interaction between the flood-plain catchment and the public sewerage system, contributing to properties and public areas being flooded. (p4) [IMG]
* Environment Agency issued a Flood Warning for Kelham (River Trent) on 2nd January 2024 and engaged with residents of Kelham Road through a public meeting; EA community information officers visited affected communities to gather information and validate impacts. Flood patrols and clearance activities were carried out in the area as part of the response. (p7)
* Flooding from the river affected the area along Kelham Road, impacting infrastructure associated with the water company's network and prompting resilience investigations. (p9)

### Brewers Wharf, Newark-On-Trent
* Environment Agency deployed a team with pumps at Brewers Wharf and over-pumped flood water throughout the event to manage flooding. (p7)
* Assets at Brewers Wharf were damaged by flooding; repairs have been carried out by the Environment Agency. (p8)

## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Responded to a major flood incident across the county: initiated a Community Flood Recovery Grant to support individuals affected by internal flooding; attended sites to verify internal flooding and to offer support, guidance and advice to affected residents and businesses; collated and published verified flood impact data on Resilience Direct; chaired and attended Strategic, Tactical and Recovery Coordination Group meetings. (p6)
* Led and coordinated emergency flood response across the county: provided 24-hour support, delivered sandbags, carried out actions requested by Strategic/Tactical/Recovery groups, initiated the Section 19 flood investigation and supported partners. (p7)
* Coordinated and delivered the local authority flood response: stood up a multi-department team, staff worked beyond normal hours, visited impacted communities and vulnerable individuals, delivered aqua sacs, helped evacuate people, provided emergency accommodation, communicated warnings and briefings, cleaned up roads and paths and removed bulky waste from flooded homes, contacted affected individuals to identify humanitarian needs, coordinated in-person visits to high-risk communities due to predicted extreme River Trent levels, and began recovery actions including distributing grant funding. (p8)
* Quant: 69 roads (p7)

### Environment Agency
* Issued timely flood warnings for River Trent locations, operated flood patrols, cleared blockages, deployed a pump team at Brewers Wharf for continuous over‑pumping, organised community engagement events and coordinated community information officers to collect flood impact data. (p7)
* Repaired damage caused by flooding to assets at Brewers Wharf and undertook access improvement works at a debris screen (Orchard School) as part of the post-flood response. (p8) [PAG]
* Assets at Brewers Wharf were damaged by flooding and have been repaired by the Environment Agency. (p9) [PAG]
* Quant: 3 flood warnings issued (for named locations on 2nd January 2024) (p7)

### Severn Trent
* Attended multi-agency community drop-in event and engaged in multi-agency response and community outreach during the flood event. (p7)
* Responded to reports of flooding in Newark, provided emergency response crews where accessible, are investigating causes, contacted affected residents about sewer-related issues; investigations indicate floodwater from the River Trent entered and overwhelmed the sewer network in some Newark locations. (p8)
* Severn Trent's network was affected by river flooding along Kelham Road; the company is investigating options to increase flood resilience on that part of its network and will report findings to the county council. (p9)
