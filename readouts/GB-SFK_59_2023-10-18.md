### Charsfield
* Charsfield was severely impacted when intense rainfall from Storm Babet overwhelmed natural flow routes, watercourses and drainage infrastructure; converging overland flow paths led to internal flooding of properties and disruption to infrastructure and services. (p4)
* Major flooding on 20 October 2023 from both fluvial (ordinary watercourse / river) and pluvial (surface water run‑off) sources that produced flow paths through the community and caused internal flooding to residential and commercial properties. (p8) [IMG]
* Major flooding on 20 October 2023 from both fluvial (Potsford Brook overtopping its banks) and pluvial (surface water runoff) sources. Floodwater entered properties on multiple streets: Church Road and The Street flooding included a fluvial element from Potsford Brook; Monewden Road, Chapel Lane and St Peters Close were mainly affected by surface water runoff. (p9) [IMG, PAG]
* Flooding occurred across five investigation zones within the village (Monewden Road; St. Peters Close; Church Road and Hall Road; The Street; Wickham Market Road), affecting roads and the village centre and prompting an investigation of transport and residential nodes. (p10) [IMG]
* {01 Jan 2016 - 31 Jan 2016, 01 Jan 2021 - 31 Jan 2021, 01 Apr 2022 - 30 Apr 2022, 01 Aug 2022 - 31 Aug 2022, 01 Nov 2022 - 30 Nov 2022, 09 May 2023, 18 Oct 2023 - 21 Oct 2023}: Multiple roads, pavements and properties in the village were flooded; blocked or overflowing highway drains were reported and two clusters of property flooding were recorded (around the junction of Chapel Lane & The Street, and on St Peters Close). Several streets (Chapel Lane, St Peters Close, Church Road, Hall Road and The Street) are identified as at-risk and were affected during the October 2023 storm. (p11) [IMG, MUL]
* {01 Apr 2022 - 30 Apr 2022, 01 Aug 2022 - 31 Aug 2022, 01 Nov 2022 - 30 Nov 2022, 09 May 2023, 18 Oct 2023 - 21 Oct 2023}: Parts of the village experienced both surface-water (pluvial) and fluvial flooding during Storm Babet (October 2023). Properties surrounding the junction of Chapel Lane and The Street, and properties on St Peters Close, were reported flooded on 9 May 2023. Flooded road/pavement was also reported on three occasions in 2022 (April, August and November). The village's valley topography concentrates overland flows into the settlement, contributing to these impacts. (p12) [PAG, IMG, MUL]
* Low-lying parts of the village experienced converging overland pluvial flow and fluvial flooding. The valley setting retained excess runoff which slowly discharged to Potsford Brook, leading to inundation of streets and overwhelmed drainage/watercourse capacity. Church Road, The Street and Wickham Market Road were identified as being at risk and were among the worst affected during the event. (p13) [PAG, IMG]
* Charsfield sits in the Potsford Brook valley where surface water from the wider catchment drains; during Storm Babet this led to increased surface water runoff into the valley and contributed to flooding of low-lying areas and roads. (p14) [PAG, IMG]
* Localised surface water and flash flooding on the village's lowest-lying roads. The Street and Wickham Market Road were identified as some of the worst-affected locations; high runoff from low-permeability soils and existing flow-paths, together with saturated ground, increased inundation during the event. (p15) [PAG, IMG]
* Intense rainfall and saturated ground produced large surface-water runoff from adjacent fields and flow down Davey Lane onto Monewden Road, overwhelming limited highway drainage. Properties with downward-sloping drives were internally flooded before midday; floodwater had receded by early evening. (p16) [PAG]
* {09 May 2023, 18 Oct 2023 - 21 Oct 2023}: Localized surface water flooding across the village driven by intense, localised rainfall and overland run-off from higher ground to the north and northeast funnelled through ditches and across roads (notably Chapel Lane and St Peters Close). Some residential streets experienced internal flooding; flow paths were consistent with predicted Medium–High surface water flood risk in the area. (p18) [IMG, MUL]
* {09 May 2023, 18 Oct 2023 - 21 Oct 2023}: High rainfall produced large amounts of surface water run-off from fields north of Chapel Lane and St Peters Close. Field ditches and a piped watercourse were overwhelmed, allowing water to follow predicted downhill flowpaths and impact properties from the front (Chapel Lane) and rear (St Peters Close) and to overwhelm highway drainage on The Street. (p19) [PAG, IMG, MUL]
* Large areas of the village experienced internal and highway flooding due to a combination of Potsford Brook overtopping and large volumes of surface water run‑off from surrounding fields. Roadside drainage was overwhelmed and low points (notably The Street) accumulated floodwater. (p20) [PAG]
* The Street (one of the lowest points in Charsfield) acted as the central basin where floodwater accumulated. Roadside highway drainage assets were completely overwhelmed and outfalls submerged, leading to accumulation of floodwater in low-lying parts of the village. (p21) [PAG]
* The village experienced combined fluvial and pluvial flooding: early accumulation at St Peters Close and widespread inundation along The Street, internal flooding of homes and businesses, and properties surrounded by floodwater where surface runoff and brook overtopping converged. (p23) [PAG, IMG]
* Potsford Brook was overwhelmed by runoff from fields west of the village and came out of bank at Church Road. Floodwater flowed down The Street (across the full width of the road), surface water from St Peters Close and Chapel Lane added to accumulations, drainage assets on the highway were overwhelmed, properties and rear gardens were overtopped and a number of houses were surrounded by floodwater. (p24)

Highway drainage assets were overwhelmed and floodwater flowed along the road, impacting properties by flooding their frontages from the road. (p25) [IMG]

### Deben Catchment
* Acts as the downstream fluvial system receiving discharge from Potsford Brook; contributed to fluvial flood risk for Charsfield during the event (Potsford Brook is identified as the main fluvial influence). (p12) [PAG]
* Storm Babet delivered significant rainfall across the catchment (18–21 Oct), producing rapid rises in water levels and increased runoff that contributed to local flooding and limited forewarning. (p16)
* Identified as the focus of a long-term EA programme to better map flooding extents of the River Deben and its tributaries following flooding associated with Storm Babet; hydraulic modelling planned to inform future maintenance and flood extent mapping. (p33) [TAB, PAG]
* Flooding in the Deben catchment contributed river flows and surface-water/pluvial/overland flows affecting Charsfield; Environment Agency engaged with catchment landowners, assessed main river maintenance needs and prepared a funding bid and future hydraulic modelling to address these issues.
