### Mill Road, Haverhill

(p3) [MUL]
* {01 Jul 2021 - 31 Jul 2021, 01 Aug 2021 - 31 Aug 2021, July - August 2020}: Surface water runoff travelling down Mill Road within the highway jumped the kerb, entered rear gardens and then flooded the rear of properties (water entered through rear doors). Floodwater was reported to 'smell foul', indicating possible mixing of surface water with surcharging foul sewer overflow. (p4) [MUL]
* {01 Aug 2021 - 31 Aug 2021}: A route of surface water overland flow down Mill Road was identified; this flow route travels past the properties on Chauntry Road and contributed to the flooding reported at those properties. Mapping also shows rear gardens of the affected properties at risk from surface water. (p5) [PAG]

### Chauntry Road, Haverhill
* This investigation report is titled for Chauntry Road, indicating a flooding event at this street; the map in the report places Chauntry Road within the central Haverhill area that is shown to lie in mapped flood risk zones (the highest mapped risk following the central watercourse is concentrated nearby). Suffolk County Council (the LLFA) determined it necessary to investigate the flood event affecting Chauntry Road. (p1) [IMG]
* {25 Jul 2021, July - August 2020}: Two properties at the junction of Mill Road and Chauntry Road flooded during the intense localized rainfall event; the same properties also experienced flooding on a further two occasions (July/August 2020 and one other occasion within the six months prior to the report). (p3) [IMG, MUL]
* {01 Aug 2021 - 31 Aug 2021}: Floodwater entered the ground floors of properties on Chauntry Road to a depth of 10–20 mm. The water was reported to 'smell foul', indicating it originated from the foul sewer or surface water mixed with surcharging foul sewer overflow. This caused repeated damage to internal flooring materials, furniture and personal property and resulted in an insurance claim. (p5) [PAG]
* Properties on Chauntry Road reported internal/external flooding caused by surface-water runoff entering their rear gardens from Mill Road and adjacent areas; residents reported floodwaters smelled foul, consistent with sewer surcharge being involved. (p6)
* A foul sewer manhole in rear gardens had recently surcharged. Surface water travelling down Mill Road missed gullies and jumped the kerb into adjacent properties via rear gardens, resulting in internal flooding of at least one property (floodwater accumulating inside a living room). (p8) [TAB]
* Action requested to investigate a foul sewer to the rear of properties to establish whether it has received surface water flows and whether its capacity and condition are sufficient—indicating surface water affected rear properties/sewer. (p9)
* {25 Jul 2021, July - August 2020}: Quant: 2 properties; 3 flood occasions (25.07.2021; July/August 2020; one additional occasion within 6 months prior to report) (p3) [IMG, MUL]
* {01 Aug 2021 - 31 Aug 2021}: Quant: 10–20 mm flood depth (p5) [PAG]

### Haverhill
* The map shows flood risk zones through central Haverhill following a watercourse; the highest flood risk is concentrated along this corridor and affects areas in the town centre. (p1) [IMG]
* An intense, localised rainfall event on 25.07.2021 caused widespread surface water/urban flooding across much of Haverhill; multiple streets reported flooding to Suffolk County Council (Sturmer Road, Hazel Close, High Street, Harewood Terrace, Greenwood Close, Elm Close, Chauntry Road and Withersfield Road). (p3) [IMG]
* {01 Jul 2021 - 31 Jul 2021, July - August 2020}: Residential properties experienced pluvial/surface water flooding. Anecdotal evidence reports floodwaters entered properties via the rear doors after surface water runoff travelled down Mill Road, jumped the kerb and entered rear gardens before flooding the properties. Floodwater was reported to 'smell foul', indicating it either originated from the foul sewer or surface water mixed with overflow from a surcharging foul sewer. Historical records and a map extract held by Suffolk County Council show multiple recorded pluvial flood incidents in the Haverhill area (events dated between 2015–2020) and the July 2021 event formally reported as affecting properties. (p4) [IMG, MUL]
* Central Haverhill experienced surface-water flow routes with areas of faster flow concentrated along main roads and through the town centre; mapping and observations indicate urban surface-water flooding affected streets and low-lying areas. (p6) [IMG]
* {01 Jul 2021 - 31 Jul 2021, July - August 2020}: Quant: 2 properties (p4) [IMG, MUL]
