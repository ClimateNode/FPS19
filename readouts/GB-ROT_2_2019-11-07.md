### River Don
* River Don flooded through Rotherham town centre as flows from the upper catchment reached the town; water levels in the Don peaked just after midnight on 7th/8th November. The town-centre river flooding contributed to widespread flooding of businesses and directly caused closure/disruption of rail infrastructure (Rotherham Central station closed due to flooding from the River Don). (p5)
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019, 07 Nov 2019 - 08 Nov 2019}: River Don reached its highest recorded peak through the town centre; extensive out-of-bank flow with uncertainty in flow estimates. Flooding resulted from very high river levels following prolonged saturated catchment conditions. (p6) [MUL]
* Quant: Rotherham Central railway station closed for four days; 277 Businesses directly impacted; 92 Businesses indirectly impacted (p5)
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019, 07 Nov 2019 - 08 Nov 2019}: Quant: peak level 3.322 m; calculated flow 593 m3/s; return period in excess of 150 years (p6) [MUL]

### South Yorkshire
