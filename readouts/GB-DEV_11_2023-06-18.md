

# Crediton flooding 18 Jun 2023

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report Crediton Surface Water Flooding 18 June 2023<br>035\_Devon County/035\_2023\_Devon County/download (8).pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Crediton
* Local drainage systems were overwhelmed by short-duration heavy rainfall, causing internal flooding to multiple properties in Crediton. (p1) [TAB, PAG]
* The event produced concentrated surface water flowpaths and areas of pooling across parts of Crediton (notably Market St, North St, Blagdon and Glen Creedy Terrace), resulting in localized urban surface-water flooding and impacts to properties and streets within the town. (p2) [IMG]
* Short‑duration, high‑intensity surface water flooding affected the town's highway network and properties; highway drainage feeding South West Water's surface water sewer was overwhelmed and surface water entered properties in areas off the A377. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Surface water flooding affected the urban extent of Crediton, mainly affecting roads off the A377 (including North Street and Mill Street) and causing property flooding. (p4) [PAG]
* Surface water flooding affected Crediton town centre; investigation and recommendations include considering a town-centre surface water management scheme and feeding information into DCC's ongoing surface water study. (p5) [TAB]
* Quant: 9 properties; 6 residential; 3 commercial (p1) [TAB, PAG]
* Quant: 9 properties (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: at least 9 properties (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: at least nine properties (p4) [PAG]

### North Street, Crediton
* Mapped surface water flowpaths ran along North Street with identified areas of pooling at low-lying intersections, causing localized surface water flooding on the street and exposing nearby properties to floodwater. (p2)
* Surface water flows entered properties on North Street; internal flooding occurred and is part of a recurring pattern of surface water flooding in the street. (p3) [PAG]
* {August 2022, 18 Jun 2023}: Surface water flowed onto properties causing internal flooding. At least four properties were internally flooded; internal flood depths primarily 1–10 mm. Properties on North Street are affected regularly by surface water flows. (p4) [PAG, MUL]
* Quant: 4 properties internally flooded (p3) [PAG]
* {August 2022, 18 Jun 2023}: Quant: at least four properties (p4) [PAG, MUL]

## Topics without coordinates

### Devon County Council
* Devon County Council (highways) was the responsible highways authority where significant surface water flooding affected the highway network in Crediton; DCC highways are identified to review highway gullies as part of follow-up because highway drainage was overwhelmed during the event and contributed to surface water reaching properties. (p3)

### Highways
* Significant surface water flooding affected the highway network in Crediton; highway drainage systems that feed into the public surface water sewer were overwhelmed, causing pooled surface water and contributing to property flooding on roads off the A377. (p3)

### South West Water
* Highway drainage systems that feed into South West Water's public surface water sewer were overwhelmed during the event, indicating the public surface water sewer network was unable to contain the inflow; South West Water is recommended to review the condition of its network as part of the investigation. (p3)
