

# Chatburn flooding 01 Jul 2021 - 31 Jul 2021

Reference document:<br>July 2021 Flooding In Lancashire: Clitheroe Road, Chatburn, Ribble Valley<br>069\_Lancashire County/section-19-flood-investigation-report-clitheroe-road-chatburn-july-2021.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Shackletons Garden Centre
* Internal flooding of the premises and flooding around the car park and outdoor spaces; this internal and external flooding also impacted the adjacent public highway. (p3)
* Surface water from overwhelmed local drainage accumulated in the Garden Centre car park and entered the business premises through the main doors, causing flooding of the commercial premises. (p4) [PAG]
* {2008-2009}: Surface water draining from nearby highways and private land has repeatedly overwhelmed local drainage routes, allowing water to accumulate at the garden centre site and to enter the business through the main doors; one documented internal flood occurred in 2008/2009 causing extensive damage. (p5)
* {general}: Surface water was directed onto and under land occupied by industrial businesses (Shackleton's land); the drainage on Shackleton's land could not safely accommodate the water, contributing to lost/compromised culvert routing and local ponding. (p6)
* {2008-2009}: Quant: 1 internal flooding event (2008-2009) (p5)

### Lancashire
* County area affected by the July 2021 flood event and subject of a Section 19 (Flood and Water Management Act 2010) investigation. (p1)

### Ribble Valley
* Ribble Valley (local authority area) was affected by the July 2021 flood event; locations within it (e.g. Chatburn, Clitheroe Road) are the subject of the Section 19 investigation. (p1)

### Chatburn
* The village of Chatburn was affected by the July 2021 flood event and is included in the Section 19 investigation. (p1)
* Flooding of business premises (Shackleton's Garden Centre) occurred when local drainage was overwhelmed during the July 2021 weather, causing surface water to accumulate in the Garden Centre car park and enter the premises through the main doors. (p4)

### Clitheroe Road, Chatburn
* Clitheroe Road was impacted by the July 2021 flooding and is named in the Section 19 investigation. (p1)
* Surface water flooding affected the public highway adjacent to the Garden Centre. (p3)
* Local drainage on Clitheroe Road was overwhelmed during the event; drainage speed was severely compromised contributing to surface water accumulation that affected the adjacent Garden Centre and the public highway. (p4)
* {general}: Experienced ponding of surface water near the Garden Centre; new lengths of highway drain were installed in Clitheroe Road to bypass the lost length of watercourse and resolve local ponding. (p6)

### Worston Road, Chatburn
* Surface water flooding affected the public highway adjacent to the Garden Centre. (p3)
* Local drainage capacity and flow from the public highway were severely compromised by constraints in the local watercourse network, causing surface water to accumulate on the highway and contributing to surface water runoff that entered the adjacent Shackleton's Garden Centre car park and business premises. (p4)


## Topics without coordinates

### Lancashire County Council
* Acted as lead local flood authority and Flood Risk Management team investigated the surface water flooding event at Shackleton's Garden Centre (July 2021) and historical repeated incidents; recorded investigations and reviews of the cause (overwhelmed local drainage and constrained watercourse network). (p4)

### LCC Highways
* {2015}: Responded to ponding and surface water routed to the highway by collaborating to investigate culvert routes (2015) and by installing new lengths of highway drain to bypass the lost watercourse, enabling the highway drainage system to accommodate surface water. (p6)

### Network Rail
* {2015}: Collaborated with LCC Highways in 2015 to explore the route of the watercourse and identify/clear any compromised culverts on Network Rail land to enable free flow of water; a dashed route of a lost culvert through multiple industrial premises was recorded but not proven and may no longer exist. (p6)
