

# Haverhill flooding 27 Aug 2020

## Place names with coordinates

### Haverhill
* The Glebe area of Haverhill experienced internal flooding to one property on multiple occasions (causing property damage) and flooding to gardens and driveways of multiple properties. (p2)
* Quant: one property (p2)

### The Glebe, Haverhill
* Internal flooding of a single property on multiple occasions resulting in damage to property; multiple properties experienced flooding of gardens and driveways. (p2)
* {after July 2020}: Surface water sewer (AWS) became blocked or overwhelmed, causing surcharge of connected highway and private drainage systems and overland flow from the carriageway onto driveways and the front of properties; resulted in flooding entering through brickwork and flooring of properties on The Glebe. (p4)
* Quant: one property (p2)


## Topics without coordinates

### Anglian Water
* {01 Jul 2018 - 31 Jul 2018, 01 Aug 2018 - 31 Aug 2018, after July 2020}: AWS surface water sewer became blocked or was overwhelmed, causing the connected highway and private drainage systems to surcharge. Surcharging led to overland flow from the carriageway that flooded the fronts of properties and permitted water to enter through brickwork and flooring. (p4) [MUL]
* Identified as the responsible authority for the surface water sewer asset linked to flooding; tasked with investigating, maintaining and modelling the SW sewer to assess capacity and mitigate sewer/surface water flooding. (p5) [TAB]

### SCC Highway
* {01 Jul 2018 - 31 Jul 2018, 01 Jul 2020 - 31 Jul 2020, after July 2020}: The highway drainage network (which connects to the AWS surface water sewer) experienced surcharge contributing to overland flow from the carriageway that flooded property fronts. The Highway Authority carried out CCTV investigation and jetting of gullies as part of the response; gullies were reported jetted and clear in July 2020, though flooding was experienced after that date. (p4) [MUL]

### Suffolk County Council
* {01 Jul 2018 - 31 Jul 2018, 01 Jul 2020 - 31 Jul 2020}: Conducted highway drainage investigation (CCTV and jetting) which confirmed connection of highway and private drainage to an AWS surface water sewer and identified a blockage in the AWS surface water sewer; gullies were jetted and cleared. Actions relate to response and investigation of surface water/sewer surcharging that caused overland flow and property flooding. (p4) [MUL]
