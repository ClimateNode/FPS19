### Worksop
* Sustained heavy rainfall during Storm Babet caused flooding of roads and residential properties in Worksop; flooding occurred faster than normal because the ground was already saturated. (p1)
* Multiple streets across Worksop experienced surface water and urban flooding during the intense rainfall event; numerous properties were internally flooded across the town. (p2) [PAG]

### River Ryton
* The River Ryton rose rapidly on 20 October 2023 due to sustained heavy rainfall and contributed to flooding in Worksop. The Ryton peaked at 2.11 m at the Worksop gauge on Stubbing Lane, coincident with flooding of adjacent streets and properties. (p3)
* Due to sustained heavy rainfall the River Ryton rose rapidly on 20 October, peaking at the Worksop gauge and contributing to flooding in adjacent areas of Worksop. The river rise is cited as a primary cause contributing to floodwater affecting streets and properties on the river’s south side. (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* Overtopping of banks during sustained heavy rainfall (Storm Babet) caused floodwater to flow over a bridge at Watson Road and contributed to pooling and internal flooding of a business property. (p6)
* Flooding from the River Ryton (including through the town centre and downstream) required proposed desilting and conveyance works; the report also proposes exploring Natural Flood Management in the upper Ryton catchment to reduce peak flows into the river. (p13)

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Officers from across the County Council played a key role in the response to the Major Incident caused by the flooding, supporting the immediate response to significant impacts across the county. (p10)
* Provided immediate response and short- and long-term recovery actions across the county in response to the flood event: verified internal flooding at affected properties, offered support, guidance and advice to residents and businesses; collated and published verified flood impact data on Resilience Direct; visited affected properties to establish flood sources and severity; delivered sandbags to affected areas; closed roads to promote safety and avoid further flood damage; chaired and attended Strategic/Tactical/Recovery coordination groups; provided 24-hour Emergency Planning support; initiated and led the Section 19 Flood Investigation and led procurement and delivery of the DEFRA Property Flood Resilience Repair Grant Scheme. (p11)
* As Lead Local Flood Authority responded to flooding in Worksop on 20/10/2023: cleansed significant detritus from roads, car parks and paths; rehoused affected tenants from Council properties; undertook repairs to affected Council housing properties; promoted and administered Business Recovery Grant and Business Rates Relief; actively engaged in the Section 19 Flood Investigation and will liaise with Risk Management Authorities regarding multi‑source flooding in the Central Avenue and Newcastle Avenue area. (p12)
* As Lead Local Flood Authority, Nottinghamshire County Council recorded and responded to multi‑source flooding in Worksop during the event on 20th October 2023. (p13)
