

# Slaggyford flooding 28 Jun 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Slaggyford<br>091\_Northumberland/Slaggyford-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Slaggyford
* Three residential properties flooded due to surface water runoff from the access road pooling and an ordinary watercourse coming out of bank and flowing overland, which combined to produce deeper floodwater; one road was also affected. (p1) [IMG]
* Annotated map shows multiple surface-water flow routes entering Slaggyford from the northwest, west and southwest, converging in the village centre and then dispersing to the southeast/east. This indicates surface-water flooding flowed through the settlement (the map also shows The Croft and Lamb House Caravan Park on or near these flow paths). (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 3 properties; 1 road (p1) [IMG]
