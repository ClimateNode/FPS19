

# Sheffield Metropolitan District flooding 10 Jun 2012

Reference document:<br>Investigation of Flooding Event on 10 June 2012<br>109\_Sheffield/Flood Investigation Report for 10 June 2012.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Sheffield Metropolitan District
* Parts of North West Sheffield were flooded. (p1)
* Parts of North West Sheffield experienced flooding; local residents were affected by the flood. (p3)
* Flooding affected parts of the area, resulting in inundation of properties and principal roads during the intense late-evening rainfall event. (p5)
* Fast-flowing surface water generated by intense rainfall was channelled down roads in steep sided slopes west of Middlewood Road causing flooding in the valley bottom; flood waters dissipated within hours and roads were re-opened by 08:00 the following morning. High surface water flows overwhelmed local drainage and washed silt and debris causing blockages. (p6)
* Sewer-related flooding on Wadsley Lane and overland flooding at the Wadsley Lane / Marlcliffe Road junction caused highways to flood and properties to be inundated. (p9) [IMG]
* Quant: 2 number properties (Wadsley Lane sewer flooding); 2 number properties (Wadsley Lane / Marlcliffe Road flooding) (p9) [IMG]

### Middlewood Road, Sheffield Metropolitan District
* Fast-flowing water channelled down roads in steep slopes to the west of Middlewood Road caused flooding in the valley bottom; vehicles driving through flooded sections produced a wave effect increasing flow depth and velocity. Residents reported that highway drainage in flooded sections requires improvement. (p6)
* Overland/surface water flooding affected the highway and one property on the northern section (noted as Middlewood Road North). (p9) [IMG]
* Quant: 1 property (p9) [IMG]

### Wadsley Lane, Sheffield Metropolitan District
* Reports of internal sewer flooding to properties on Wadsley Lane. (p6)
* Overland/surface-water flooding affected the highway at Wadsley Lane (including the junction with Marlcliffe Road) and nearby properties. (p9) [IMG]
* Quant: 2 properties (p9) [IMG]

### Middlewood
* Flooding affected properties and principal roads in parts of Middlewood. (p5)
* Surface water flows from intense rainfall overwhelmed local drainage in parts of Middlewood, washing down silt and debris and causing blockages; residents experienced distress and the Council recognises a risk of surface water flooding in this area. (p7)
* Overland flow/flooding affected Middlewood Road locations: highways were flooded and nearby residential properties were inundated. (p9) [IMG]
* Quant: 1 number property (Middlewood Road North); 3 number properties (Middlewood Road) (p9) [IMG]

### Hillsborough
* Flooding affected properties and principal roads in parts of Hillsborough. (p5)
* Surface water flows from intense rainfall overwhelmed local drainage in parts of Hillsborough, washing down silt and debris and causing blockages; the Council recognises a risk of surface water flooding in this area. (p7)
* Leppings Lane experienced overland/roadway flooding that affected the highway and adjacent residential properties. (p9) [IMG]
* Quant: 4 number properties (p9) [IMG]

### Leppings Lane, Sheffield Metropolitan District
* Surface water flows washed silt and debris down onto the highway; Street Force removed silt and debris and commenced investigations into defects in the highway drainage on Leppings Lane. Yorkshire Water checked the sewer system in this area and found it operating correctly. (p6)
* The Leppings Lane highway drainage system is the subject of a completed/ongoing investigation as part of addressing surface water flooding risk, implying the highway drainage was implicated in flooding/blockage issues. (p7)
* Overland/surface water flooding affected the highway and flooded four properties on Leppings Lane. (p9) [IMG]
* Quant: 4 properties (p9) [IMG]

### Marlcliffe Road, Middlewood
* Overland exceedance / surface water flooding affected the highway at Marlcliffe Road, with flooding reaching nearby properties at the Wadsley Lane/Marlcliffe Road location. (p9) [IMG]
* Quant: 2 properties (p9) [IMG]

### Catch Bar Lane, Sheffield Metropolitan District
* Catch Bar Lane sewer system was implicated in the surface water flooding risk; further investigation details were requested from Yorkshire Water, indicating the sewer system may have experienced issues related to surface water flows, silt and debris causing blockages. (p7)


## Topics without coordinates

### Sheffield City Council
* {10 Jun 2012, 11 Jun 2012}: As highway authority the Council's Street Force attended affected areas, assisted emergency services, issued sandbags, reinstated blown manhole covers and re-opened sections of roads (sections closed during the event were re-opened by 08:00 on 11 June). The Council also undertook a formal investigation of the 10 June 2012 flooding. (p5) [MUL]
* {10 Jun 2012, 12 Jun 2012}: Land Drainage officers and Street Force inspected affected areas and interviewed residents to establish cause/extent (inspection on 12 June). The Council concluded flooding was primarily due to high surface water flows overwhelming local drainage and washing down silt/debris causing blockages. Hotspots were not checked prior to the event because no medium risk warning was issued. (p6) [PAG, MUL]

### Yorkshire Water
* {10 Jun 2012, 30 Jun 2012}: Attended affected areas during and after the flood event; inspected sewer system and removed a blockage on 30 June; checked the remainder of the sewer system in the Middlewood Road and Leppings Lane areas and found them operating correctly; continuing checks on the sewer system at Catch Bar Lane. (p6) [MUL]
* Yorkshire Water is being asked to provide further investigation details relating to the Catch Bar Lane sewer system, indicating the sewer system at Catch Bar Lane was implicated in the flooding and/or blockages. (p7)
