

# Worksop flooding 07 Nov 2019

## Place names with coordinates

### Worksop
* Significant internal flooding across the town caused by intense rainfall and fluvial (river) flooding. Flooding affected residential properties, businesses, public highways, gardens and curtilages. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Significant flood event beginning morning 07/11/2019: 308 reported incidents of internal flooding across the town. Floodwater also affected public highways and many gardens/curtilages. (p3)
* Overtopping of the River Ryton led to initial flooding at the Caravan Park and sports pavilion, later spilling over the right bank and flooding highways and properties in central Worksop (Central Avenue area and listed streets); tributaries backed up and overtopped, roads became impassable and some were closed for safety. (p5)
* Central area affected: evacuations carried out; large parts of the town lost power; several roads in and out of the town were closed; properties and businesses experienced internal flooding. (p6)
* {07 Nov 2019 - 08 Nov 2019}: Severe flooding in Worksop during the event: flood levels remained high until the early morning of 8 November; emergency services evacuated people from properties (Newcastle Avenue area) and significant overnight response from emergency services, volunteers and charities was required. (p9) [PAG]
* Affected by flooding associated with the River Ryton; town centre and surrounding areas were identified for vegetation removal (Canch area and town centre) and for consideration of storage and hydraulic-modelling-based alleviation options. (p10) [PAG]
* Quant: 308 reported incidents of internal flooding; 128 properties; 180 businesses (p2) [IMG, PAG]

## Topics without coordinates

### River Ryton
* The River Ryton (a main river flowing through the centre of Worksop) contributed to fluvial (river) flooding during the severe rainfall event that affected Worksop. (p2)
* {07 Nov 2019, 14 Nov 2019}: River levels rose to near-record peaks and the river overtopped its banks, driving flooding through the heavily urbanised centre of Worksop and contributing to the November 2019 inundation. (p3) [IMG, MUL]
* {general}: On 7th November the River Ryton overtopped its left bank adjacent to the Riverside Caravan Park and the sports pavilion. The river backed up at the point where it is culverted under the access to the sports pavilion and town centre buildings downstream. Overtopping led to the Caravan Park being the first area of Worksop to flood; residents moved caravans and valuables out of rising flood water. The sports pavilion and cricket pitches also flooded, with flood waters confined to those locations for a short while. (p4) [IMG]
* The River Ryton overtopped its left bank then later its right bank; it became surcharged, preventing drainage discharge, causing tributaries to back up and overtop and driving floodwater into town streets and properties. (p5) [IMG, PAG]
* River Ryton is served by the control structures at The Canch which are being investigated for their hydraulic interaction during the flood event (i.e. the river/system contributed to the flooding dynamics being investigated). (p6)
* {07 Nov 2026 - 08 Nov 2026}: The River Ryton is implicated in the flooding affecting Worksop; the incident prompted proposals for detailed hydraulic modelling of the River Ryton and consideration of storage options to reduce future flood risk. (p9)
* {07 Nov 2019, 14 Nov 2019}: Quant: 2.16 m peak level (second highest on record) (p3) [IMG, MUL]
