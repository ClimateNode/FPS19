### Oldham Metropolitan District
* Widespread flooding from intense/prolonged rainfall affected local communities: homes, businesses, local infrastructure and the environment. Floodwater entered habitable and non-habitable parts of properties across the borough. (p4)
* The borough experienced rapid runoff and flash flooding risk exacerbated by steep catchments, narrow valleys and limited drainage capacity; high-intensity rainfall and dry ground increases runoff from the moors and debris build-up can block drainage. (p10)

### Arthurs Lane, Saddleworth
* During intense rainfall on 21 November 2016, flood water collected when a trash screen on the culverted Golborne Clough became blocked. Water overspilled onto a nearby bridle path, ran along drainage channels, cascaded down onto Arthurs Lane and entered residential properties; a manhole on the bridle path surcharged and contributed to the flooding. A photograph documents water emerging from a stone wall near a garage and flowing onto the street. (p21) [IMG]
* On 21 November 2016 flood water cascaded down from a bridle path (after Golborne Clough's trash screen became blocked and the culvert was hydraulically overwhelmed) and flooded numerous properties on Arthurs Lane; a surcharging manhole on the bridle path also contributed to the flooding at Arthurs Lane. (p22) [IMG]
