

# Bingham flooding 14 Nov 2019

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Bingham – November 2019<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/section19reportbinghamnovember2019.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Nottinghamshire
* {07 Nov 2019, 14 Nov 2019}: Experienced extensive flooding across the county (including Bingham); response actions across the county included road closures, deployment of flood signage and distribution of sandbags; a major incident was declared during the event. (p1) [PAG, MUL]
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Severe weather across the county led the Nottinghamshire Local Resilience Forum to declare a major incident; prolonged/intense rainfall caused additional surface-water and fluvial (river) flooding and wide-area flood response activity. (p2)
* {07 Nov 2019, 14 Nov 2019}: Quant: 66 road closures; over 750 flood signs; over 5000 sandbags (p1) [PAG, MUL]
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Quant: 38 flood warnings; 16 flood alerts (p2)

### East Midlands
* {07 Nov 2019, 14 Nov 2019}: Parts of the region experienced extreme rainfall (a month's worth in 24 hours) that contributed to flooding across the area, including impacts recorded in Nottinghamshire. (p1) [MUL]

### Rushcliffe
* Communities in Rushcliffe were affected by the flooding; local authority (Rushcliffe Borough Council) delivered sandbags, provided emergency response and continue to manage recovery and administer hardship and recovery grants. (p5)

### Bingham
* Internal flooding of residential properties on Western Avenue following prolonged heavy rainfall. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Residential properties in Bingham (specifically on Western Avenue) experienced internal flooding after a nearby watercourse was overwhelmed by prolonged rainfall. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 6 properties (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 6 properties (p3) [IMG]

### Western Avenue, Bingham
* Internal flooding of residential properties on Western Avenue caused by an adjacent watercourse being overwhelmed after prolonged rainfall. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 6 properties (p3) [IMG]

### Newton Avenue, Bingham
* Internal flooding of residential properties on Western Avenue caused by an adjacent watercourse being overwhelmed after prolonged heavy rainfall. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 6 properties (p3) [IMG]

### Bishops Road, Bingham
* Residential properties on Western Avenue in Bingham suffered internal flooding after a nearby Network Rail watercourse was overwhelmed by prolonged rainfall. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 6 properties (p3) [IMG]

### Hill Drive, Bingham

## Topics without coordinates

### Network Rail
* A watercourse located on land owned by Network Rail was overwhelmed by prolonged rainfall and resulted in flooding of adjacent properties on Western Avenue. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* An on-site watercourse within land owned by Network Rail was overwhelmed by prolonged rainfall and resulted in flooding of adjacent properties. Network Rail has identified the need for maintenance on that section of watercourse and plans initial tree/vegetation clearance to enable access and further works to reduce future flood risk. (p4) [PAG]
* Responded to issues arising from the flooding and are pursuing maintenance works on a watercourse to reduce the risk of future flooding; will be monitored and detailed proposals are to be finalised in liaison with Nottinghamshire County Council and Rushcliffe Borough Council. (p5) [PAG]
* Quant: 6 properties (p3) [IMG, PAG]
