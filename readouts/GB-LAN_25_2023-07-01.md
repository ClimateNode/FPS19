## Place names with coordinates

### Tincklers Lane, Eccleston
* Tincklers Lane was affected by flooding associated with the July 2023 event (subject of the Section 19 investigation). (p1)
* {01 Jun 2023 - 30 Jun 2023, 01 Jul 2023 - 31 Jul 2023}: A residential property on Tincklers Lane flooded on two occasions during heavy rainfall when local surface water drainage was overwhelmed. (p3) [MUL]
* {18 Jun 2023, 01 Jul 2023 - 31 Jul 2023}: A residential property on Tincklers Lane flooded during heavy rainfall; the same home was reported flooded on 18 June 2023 and again during the heavy rain on 8 July 2023. (p4) [MUL]
* A house on Tincklers Lane was flooded on two occasions; floodwater included foul contamination. (p5) [PAG]
* Residents experienced two flooding incidents (June 2023 and July 2023) when local drainage systems were temporarily unable to disperse a high volume of rainfall; at least one house on Tincklers Lane was affected by foul contamination in the flood water. (p6)
* {01 Jun 2023 - 30 Jun 2023, 01 Jul 2023 - 31 Jul 2023}: Quant: 1 property; 2 flooding incidents (p3) [MUL]
* Quant: 2 occasions (p5) [PAG]
* Quant: 2 flood incidents; 1 property affected by foul contamination (p6)

### Lancashire
* Lancashire experienced flooding during the July 2023 flood event. (p1)
* Flooding incidents occurred within the county (Tincklers Lane incidents in June and July 2023). Lancashire County Council (local highways team) received reports and inspected nearby highway drainage assets, finding no obstructions but identifying the need to confirm ownership and maintenance responsibilities for recently-installed gullies. (p6) [PAG]
* Quant: 2 flood incidents; 1 property affected by foul contamination (p6) [PAG]

### Chorley District
* The Chorley area (which contains Eccleston and Tincklers Lane) was affected by the July 2023 flooding. (p1)

### Eccleston
* Eccleston (Chorley) was impacted by the July 2023 flood event. (p1)
* {18 Jun 2023, 01 Jul 2023 - 31 Jul 2023}: A house in Eccleston was flooded on two occasions when local surface water drainage networks were overwhelmed. Local highway drainage assets in the vicinity showed anomalies (new, unregistered gullies). Floodwater at the property contained foul contamination, though sewer monitoring indicated the local sewer did not surcharge and United Utilities found no sewer bursts or reports of sewer flooding attributable to the network. (p5) [MUL]
* {18 Jun 2023, 01 Jul 2023 - 31 Jul 2023}: Quant: 1 home flooded on 2 occasions; 2 anomalies identified with local surface water drainage assets; 1 new gully installed opposite the driveway; 3 new gullies installed adjacent to the highway (p5) [MUL]


## Topics without coordinates

### LCC Highways
* The county council's local Highways team responded to reports of blocked gullies and flooding in or from the nearby public highway; inspections found no obstructions in the adopted drainage network and identified new gullies not recorded in the highway asset register. (p5)
* Quant: 1 gully; 3 gullies (p5)

### Lancashire County Council
* The county council investigated the flooding at the property, identified anomalies in local surface water drainage assets (new gullies not on the asset register) and set out actions to determine gully ownership and register/schedule maintenance or notify landowners. (p5)
* Quant: 1 gully; 3 gullies (p5)

### United Utilities
* United Utilities investigated a report that floodwater contained sewage; their checks showed no reported sewer bursts or sewer surcharging in the area and sewer flow monitors indicated the sewer network reacted to rainfall but did not surcharge. The foul contamination of the floodwater at the property cannot be attributed to a systemic defect in the local sewer network. (p5)
