### Lancashire
* Significant flooding hit Lancashire in December 2015, affecting local communities and exposing shortcomings in communication and coordination; this prompted the development of district-level reports to inform affected communities and identify opportunities for future flood risk management. (p3) [PAG]
* {December 2015}: Lancashire experienced extreme flooding during December 2015 with the highest river flow levels on record and the wettest December (and wettest calendar month) since records began in 1910; this resulted in widespread impacts on people, homes and businesses. (p8) [TAB]
* Extreme storms and rainfall in November/December 2015 caused flooding throughout December 2015 affecting the county: over 2,500 properties flooded and damage to private gardens, garages and out‑buildings, business premises, agricultural property and critical public infrastructure including roads, bridges and retaining walls, water treatment plants, power and communications installations, and community buildings such as schools, village and town halls. (p9)
* Two major storm events in December 2015 caused flooding across Lancashire. The countywide event affected numerous communities; the Lead Local Flood Authority recorded and verified data about the December 2015 floods and used that to establish flood timelines and identify affected communities for a countywide Section 19 investigation. (p10) [PAG]
* Relevant risk management authorities met to discuss primary flood mechanisms and community impacts. The Lead Local Flood Authority promoted property-level flood resilience; an affected resident used available grants to install property-level flood protection measures. (p38)
* {December 2015}: Quant: over 2,500 properties flooded in Lancashire (p8) [TAB]
