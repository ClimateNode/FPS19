

# Elmbridge flooding December 2013 - February 2014

## Place names with coordinates

### Elmbridge
* Internal property flooding at multiple addresses across the borough; road closures reported. (p2) [TAB]
* Predominantly fluvial flooding across the borough during winter 2013/14, resulting in internal property flooding incidents. (p7)
* Sites across the borough experienced flooding during the winter 2013/14 event; internal property flooding occurred and 47 investigation sites were identified within the borough. (p9) [IMG]
* Elmbridge (within the lower River Mole catchment) experienced fluvial inundation from the River Mole across flood plains; saturated soils produced local pluvial (surface water) flooding; elevated groundwater levels across the period contributed to groundwater flooding impacts in the borough. (p10) [PAG]
* Quant: approximately 100 incidents of internal property flooding (p7)
* Quant: 47 sites; approximately 100 incidents of internal property flooding (p9) [IMG]

### River Thames
* Internal property flooding at multiple addresses and road closures reported in Cobham during the winter 2013/14 event. (p2) [TAB]
* Identified as a main river source for the fluvial flooding in winter 2013/14 that contributed to internal property flooding across Elmbridge. (p7)
* River Thames levels rose in the February 2014 event to the highest recorded since 1947, causing back-up into the River Wey and contributing to flooding that affected properties in the Weybridge/Walton sub area. (p24)
* Quant: approximately 100 incidents of internal property flooding in Elmbridge (p7)
* Quant: 33 properties (affected by flooding from either river or a combination) (p24)

### Surrey
* {December 2013 - February 2014, winter 2013/14}: Operated the Flood Alert and Flood Warning service for the affected area; deployed flood ambassadors and flood data recorders to areas affected by flooding; carried out maintenance along the River Thames in locations including parts of Spelthorne as part of the response to the winter 2013/14 floods. (p8) [PAG]
* Widespread flooding across the county during winter 2013/14 from a range of sources; groundwater levels rose very high and caused groundwater flooding in parts of the county. (p9) [IMG, PAG]
* Widespread flooding across Surrey during winter 2013–14 from multiple sources (high groundwater, fluvial flooding and local pluvial/surface water). In the Elmbridge area this led to inundation across flood plains from the River Mole and internal property flooding reported to the council. (p10) [IMG]
* County‑wide impacts from the winter 2013/14 storms and flooding: roads and carriageways were damaged and assessed for repair (Project 400), roads were cleansed and re-opened, trees and debris were cleared and ditching carried out to restore drainage, Surrey Fire and Rescue Service pumped flood waters to protect residents and infrastructure, and coordination groups were convened to manage the response. (p16) [PAG]
* Historic flooding occurred across Surrey during the winter 2013/14 event; a large amount of information on property and historic flooding was gathered as a result of the winter 2013/14 floods to help identify areas most at risk. (p28) [PAG]
* Quant: 47 sites; approximately 100 incidents of internal property flooding (p10) [IMG]

### Weybridge
* Internal property flooding at multiple addresses; road closures reported in the area. (p2) [TAB]
* Part of the 'Weybridge & Walton‑on‑Thames' sub‑area investigated in this report; S19 investigation sites and property flooding occurred in this sub‑area during winter 2013/14. (p9) [IMG]
* During winter 2013/14 river levels rose causing road closures and internal property flooding in the sub-area. Levels in the River Wey rose on 25 December 2013, peaking on 26 December 2013. Property flooding started on the afternoon of 25 December 2013; three properties in the vicinity of Wey Road were worst affected. A later February 2014 Thames high-flow event caused backing up in the River Wey and contributed to wider property flooding in the sub-area. (p24) [MUL]

## Topics without coordinates

### Surrey County Council
* Responded to flooding during winter 2013/14 by assisting residents on the ground (working with EBC and the Army), deploying signs and sandbags, inspecting and repairing affected highways and drainage assets, and administering post‑event Repair and Renew grants to help protect properties. (p7)
* As the Lead Local Flood Authority and Highways Authority, SCC inspected flood‑affected roads, arranged defect repairs carried out by contractor Kier (including assessment of extensively damaged carriageways for inclusion in the Project 400 resurfacing programme), cleansed and re-opened roads, provided and recycled sandbags, cleared trees/debris and carried out ditching to restore drainage, operated a call centre for residents during the floods, administered the Repair and Renew Grant (up to £5000) for flooded properties, and convened strategic/tactical coordination meetings in advance of the February 2014 event. (p16)
* {winter 2013/14, December 2013 - February 2014}: Quant: approximately 100 incidents of internal property flooding in Elmbridge (p7)
* Quant: up to £5000 (p16)

### South East England
* Parts of South East England received substantially above-normal rainfall in winter 2013–14, causing high groundwater levels and associated groundwater flooding and persistent surface-water flooding; monitoring at Wisley recorded the wettest 60‑day period in its record, reflecting the prolonged elevated flood risk. (p10)

### Surrey Fire and Rescue Service
* Pumped water away from properties during the flooding to reduce damage to those properties. (p7)
* Pumped flood waters away to protect residents, property and infrastructure during the Winter 2013/14 flooding and assisted with clearing/reopening affected roads. (p16)

### Mole Catchment
* {December 2013 - February 2014, winter 2013-14}: The upper Mole catchment produced a rapid ('flash') river response to the major storm events due to catchment characteristics, contributing to sudden rises in river levels and downstream fluvial flooding during the winter storms. (p10) [MUL]

### Internal Drainage Board
* Internal property flooding at multiple addresses across the borough and road closures reported during the winter 2013/14 flood events. (p2) [TAB]

### Elmbridge Borough Council
* Undertook a Section 19 investigation into the flooding within its area and met with the Flooding Task Group to review resilience and response actions following the winter 2013/14 fluvial flooding. (p7)

### Environment Agency
* Operated the Flood Alert and Flood Warning service, deployed flood ambassadors and flood data recorders to affected areas, and carried out maintenance along the River Thames (including areas in Spelthorne) during the winter 2013/14 fluvial flooding. (p7)

### Thames Water
* Responded during the winter 2013/14 flooding by prioritising maintenance of customer services, protecting critical assets needed to deliver service, and restoring service where disruption occurred. (p7)

### A224
* The A224 (Leatherhead Road) is noted in the LFRMS with a culvert identified and added to the Flooding Asset Register after the winter 2013/14 flooding. (p16)
