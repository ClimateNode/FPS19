

# Kirkwhelpington flooding 28 Jun 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Kirkwhelpington<br>091\_Northumberland/Kirkwhelpington-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Kirkharle
* Dwellings and a road in the village were inundated, with flood extent concentrated around Bolt House and the Ford. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 2 residential properties; 1 road (p1) [IMG]

### Kirkwhelpington
* Heavy rain on 28 June caused flooding of two properties at the north-eastern end of Kirkwhelpington. One property flooded from excess surface water discharged from non-cultivated farmland down a public bridleway/vehicle access track (flooding at the upper end of the track). The second property was flooded due to overtopping of the Whitridge Sike running through the garden and adjacent to the property. (p2)
* Quant: two properties (one at upper end of access track; one at lower end adjacent to Whitridge Sike) (p2)


## Topics without coordinates

### Whitridge Sike
* Whitridge Sike (an ordinary watercourse) overflowed on 28/06/2012, contributing to flooding concentrated around the Ford and Bolt House in Kirkharle and inundating nearby dwellings and a road. (p1) [IMG]
* Overtopping of the Whitridge Sike's banks during the event resulted in flooding of an adjacent property; the burn runs through the property's garden and passed adjacent to the property that flooded. (p2)
* Quant: 2 residential properties; 1 road (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: one property (p2)
