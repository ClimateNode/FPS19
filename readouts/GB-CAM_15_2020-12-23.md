### Buckden
* {1998, August 2020}: Ordinary watercourses pass through the town (via culverts beneath the highway and through the curtilage of some properties), creating surface water flood risk to significant areas of the village; Buckden has previously experienced flooding in 1998 and August 2020. (p4) [IMG, MUL]
* {1998, August 2020}: Significant areas of central Buckden are at risk from surface water flooding, with the highest risk concentrated in central parts and along the main local watercourses. Ordinary watercourses convey water toward Buckden Marina and the River Great Ouse; some run in culverts beneath the highway and through the curtilage of properties, contributing to the flood risk. The village has previously experienced flooding in 1998 and August 2020. (p5) [PAG, IMG, MUL]
* LLFA received 15 flood reports for Buckden; community reported eight properties flooded internally and additional external flooding to gardens/garages/outbuildings; Parish and District Council communications indicate more widespread flooding across the parish. (p8)
* Town-wide impacts from surface water when watercourses exceeded capacity and from overwhelmed sewer infrastructure (foul water backup); road and transport disruption including a flooded northern underpass beneath the A1 and the A1 being impassable for several hours; loss of access/egress for some flood-resilient properties (e.g., near Buckden Marina); flooding of amenity land and high water at an attenuation pond. (p10) [PAG, IMG]
* Surface water flooding occurred when watercourses exceeded channel capacity; Anglian Water assets were overwhelmed with floodwater entering sewers causing foul backups; the northern underpass beneath the A1 was flooded and the road between Buckden Marina and the level crossing was flooded, cutting off an exit from the village; the A1 flooded and was impassable for some hours. (p11) [TAB, PAG]
* Buckden experienced flooding driven by surface-water runoff, including from an underpass and contributions from the A1 carriageway. This prompted community engagement and interventions (gully cleansing, highway drainage improvements at High Street, riparian and watercourse maintenance) aimed at alleviation. The report notes no reports of residents needing to leave their properties. (p12)
* On 23 December 2020 a prolonged period of higher-than-average rainfall across Autumn 2020 followed by intense rainfall produced rapid runoff that caused significant overland surface water flooding and flooding from drainage systems which exceeded capacity. Flooding overwhelmed highway drainage and prompted actions including highway surface water drainage improvements (installation commenced February 2022) and measures to uphold riparian maintenance; further upstream flood-alleviation options near Taylor's Lane are being considered. (p16)
* Quant: 15 flood reports; 8 properties (internal) (p8)
* Quant: c.23 properties (total reported in parish table) (p10) [PAG, IMG]
* Quant: Properties internally up to a depth of 60cm (Taylors Lane and High Street); Gardens up to a depth of 91cm (Taylors Lane); 1 northern underpass flooded (p11) [TAB, PAG]

### East Anglia

### National Highways
* Their A1 carriageway and highway drainage contributed surface water to flooding at Buckden (including an underpass). National Highways undertook walkovers, arranged cleansing of accessible surface-water drainage gullies at flooded locations, surveyed and maintained highway drainage systems, and installed improvements to highway surface-water drainage at High Street, Buckden as part of flood-alleviation activity. (p12)

### Huntingdonshire District Council
* {January–February 2021, April 2022}: Surface water flooding at High Street and flooding from the underpass in Buckden (including contributions from the A1) that required actions including cleansing of surface water drainage gullies, installation of improvements to highway surface water drainage at High Street, surveying and maintaining highway drainage systems, and collaboration with National Highways, Anglian Water and the LLFA toward flood alleviation. (p12) [MUL]

### Cambridgeshire County Council
* {general}: Ongoing surface water flood risk at Buckden (underpass and A1 contributions). The County Council, as LLFA, is working with the community and Huntingdonshire District Council to raise resident awareness of riparian maintenance obligations and flood preparedness and to identify and progress flood-alleviation measures. (p12)
