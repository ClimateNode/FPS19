## Place names with coordinates

### Horsmonden
* Multiple streets within Horsmonden reported internal and external property flooding and carriageway flooding during the event (see listed roads in Table 1). (p2)
* Properties in the village experienced internal and external flooding and local carriageway flooding; KFRS pumped floodwater from four properties and made one property safe. (p3)
* Areas of Horsmonden are indicated to be at high risk of surface-water flooding, mainly associated with the unnamed tributaries of the River Teise. Local loamy/clayey soils with impeded drainage promote overland runoff, causing accumulation in depressions that is consistent with the locations of reported properties affected during the flood event. (p4)
* The village and surrounding areas have flooded on a number of historic occasions associated with the River Teise, ordinary watercourses, surface water runoff and blocked drains. (p5) [IMG, TAB]
* Affected properties in Horsmonden were associated with the flood event on 25 July 2021; rainfall recorded at the nearby Horsmonden gauge and radar across the local catchment were used in the analysis of the event. (p8)
* A flood event in the village affected properties (recorded flooded properties are referenced). The radar-derived rainfall distribution and catchment analysis were used specifically because the radar grid squares covered the recorded flooded properties in Horsmonden; the most intense hourly rainfall occurred between 11:00–12:00 during the event. (p9)
* Residents reported flooding between 13:00 and 14:00 when intense rainfall and surface runoff overwhelmed drainage and sewer systems, causing carriageway flooding and flooding of properties within Horsmonden. (p10) [IMG]
* On 25 July 2021 Horsmonden experienced multiple flooding incidents where watercourse blockages and culvert overtopping affected residential streets (Brenchley Road, Furnace Lane, Marle Place Road and Olivers Court). (p11)
* The report states it is possible that the Hormonden area was impacted by a more intensive, localised rainfall event (on 25 July 2021) that was not accurately recorded by gauges/radar and which may have contributed to the level of flooding experienced. (p12)
* Flooding occurred at multiple locations within the village (red-dotted points), concentrated along main roads and at entry/junction points, creating vulnerability to transport access and local movement. (p13) [IMG]
* {general}: Areas around Horsmonden are shown to be at risk of surface water flooding, driven by unnamed tributaries of the River Teise. High-risk zones are concentrated along tributaries west and south of the village, while most of the built-up centre shows lower risk. (p14) [IMG]
* Quant: 4 properties flooded internally (Furnace Lane); 2 properties flooded externally (Furnace Lane); 2 properties internal (Spout Lane); 1 property internal (Olivers Court); KFRS pumped floodwater from 10 properties (Olivers Court) and from 4 properties (Furnace Lane); 1 property internal (Marle Place Road); carriageway flooding reported at Lamberhurst Road, Lamberts Place and Oast View; depths reported: 30 cm - 100 cm (Furnace Lane); up to 25 cm (Spout Lane); 8 - 10 cm (Marle Place Road) (p2)
* Quant: 1 property (Brenchley Road internal); 4 properties (pumped by KFRS); 1 property (Green Road internal); 1 property (Fromandez Drive external); 1 property (Orchard Close internal); 1 property (Kirkins Close flooding, internal unknown); 1 property (Back Lane flooding, internal unknown) (p3)
