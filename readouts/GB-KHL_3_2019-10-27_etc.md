### East Hull Pumping Station

* {general}: The Environment Agency side of East Hull Pumping Station lifts Holderness Drain into the estuary to allow discharge. Because the EA pumps were declared unfit for purpose in 2018 the EA has had to rely on Yorkshire Water to operate pumps to maintain flows and reduce flood risk. The Holderness Drain ran high and required frequent pumping to prevent/treat flood risk to the city. (p25) [MUL]

(p29)
* {27 Oct 2019}: Quant: one business premises (p13)

### Old Fleet Drain

* {01 Sep 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Old Fleet drain experienced high flows that risked backing up (influenced by tidal levels). (p29) [PAG]
* {27 Oct 2019 - 30 Oct 2019}: High volumes of water flowing down Old Fleet Drain, combined with high tides, meant drain levels were kept high and it was unable to effectively discharge; flows overtopped banks and contributed to flooding through gardens to properties. (p30) [MUL]
