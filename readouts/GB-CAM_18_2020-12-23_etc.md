

# Huntingdonshire flooding 23 Dec 2020 and Huntingdonshire flooding 14 Jan 2021

## Place names with coordinates

### Offord Cluny
* {general}: The settlement is liable to fluvial (river) flooding from the River Great Ouse and is also at significant risk from surface water flooding; flooding is exacerbated when the River Great Ouse remains high and the contributory ordinary watercourse cannot discharge efficiently into it. Land to the west of the Offords is specifically at risk from fluvial flooding. (p5)
* {general}: The villages (Offord Cluny and Offord D'arcy forming 'The Offords') lie in the River Great Ouse valley and are liable to fluvial flooding; land to the west is at fluvial risk and large areas are at significant risk from surface water flooding. Some properties are within or adjacent to Flood Zone 3 along the river edge. (p6) [PAG, IMG]
* {23 Dec 2020, 14 Jan 2021}: As part of The Offords, Offord Cluny was within the area reported to have experienced significant flooding on 23 December 2020 and on 14 January 2021. The report and accompanying surface-water risk map show built-up areas (including parts of Offord Cluny) overlap medium/high surface-water risk zones, but the report does not specify which particular streets or properties in Offord Cluny were flooded. (p7) [PAG, IMG, MUL]
* {23 Dec 2020}: Widespread flooding across the settlement with internal and external property flooding in multiple areas. Three key risk areas with internal property flooding were identified (High Street North; Station Lane; Parkway/Elm Close/Latin Close/New Road/High Street South). Ingress into properties did not recede for a number of days; some properties lost foul sewerage provision for about three weeks. Significant and prolonged flooding occurred along the B1043 High Street to the north, causing a full road closure. At least eight properties were rendered uninhabitable for between one and eight months. (p11)
* {23 Dec 2020}: Extensive surface-water flooding at High Street (North) low spot and adjacent wetland overtopping, causing inundation of the B1043 carriageway, gardens and properties to the east and low rear spots. Flood depths reached approximately 45 cm; highway was closed for approximately 3 weeks; Anglian Water sewer system was impacted leaving some residents without foul sewerage for about 3 weeks. Ingress into properties did not recede for days and at least some properties were made uninhabitable for extended periods (see combined figures). (p12) [PAG, IMG]
* {23 Dec 2020}: Flooding associated with the 23rd December 2020 event inundated properties in The Offords; residents were reported to have to leave their homes for around 6–8 months. Investigations identified a substantially blocked Network Rail railway culvert on High Street (North) as potentially critical to local flood risk and maintenance actions were confirmed. (p15)
* {23 Dec 2020, January 2021}: Rural road inundated with standing floodwater (photo Jan 2021); road surface damaged and scheduled for replacement after prolonged flooding and high groundwater; a Network Rail culvert in the vicinity was found to be substantially blocked and identified as potentially critical to local flood risk; some properties experienced loss of foul sewerage service associated with ingress to the sewer system. (p16) [IMG, MUL]
* {23 Dec 2020}: Reported flooding prompted LLFA investigations and downstream inspections; highway response included cleansing of highway gullies across The Offords and increasing gully maintenance frequency (response to local flooding and drainage issues). (p17) [TAB]
* {23 Dec 2020, February 2021}: Experienced flooding and sewer-system impacts from excessive rainfall: highway gullies across The Offords required cleansing and gully maintenance frequency was increased; numerous properties were left without foul sewerage for an extended period, prompting response actions. (p19) [MUL]
* {23 Dec 2020}: The village experienced flooding associated with the ordinary watercourse and blocked/undersized culverts on 23 December 2020. Post-event actions recorded include significant riparian maintenance and clearance of the ordinary watercourse and culverts under the railway to reduce future risk, and Parish Council work to prepare a Local Flood Plan. (p25)
* {23 Dec 2020}: Quant: 22 flood reports, 29 properties flooded internally, 31 properties flooded externally, 8 properties uninhabitable, road closure lasting approximately 3 weeks (Offord Cluny and Offord d'Arcy) (p11)
* {23 Dec 2020}: Quant: 45 cm; 3 weeks; 3 weeks; 8 properties (uninhabitable, at least) (p12) [PAG, IMG]

### High Street, Offord Cluny
* {23 Dec 2020}: North section experienced significant and prolonged highway flooding that resulted in a full road closure. Properties on the High Street (reported as a key risk area) suffered internal flooding with ingress that did not recede for a number of days; some affected properties subsequently experienced loss of access to foul sewerage provision for an extended period. (p11) [PAG]
* {23 Dec 2020}: Extensive surface water flooding of the highway (B1043), adjacent gardens and properties. Wetland to the east exceeded capacity and flooded the road, field edges and properties. Flood water reached significant depth and persisted; the Anglian Water sewer system was impacted and some residents were without foul sewerage provision. (p12) [PAG, IMG]
* {23 Dec 2020}: Extensive surface water flooding from an overwhelmed wetland and high groundwater flooded the B1043 (High Street) and adjacent field edge, inundating gardens, outbuildings and properties to the east and causing some rear garden flooding. Floodwater reached significant depth and persisted, causing the road to be closed and the Anglian Water sewer system to be impacted, leaving some residents without foul sewerage provision. (p13) [TAB]
* {23 Dec 2020}: Flows were reported to come out of bank from the High Street and flow downstream into Millennium Green and nearby industrial/business curtilage, contributing to property and open-space inundation; impeded culvert and vegetation downstream affected flow routes. (p14) [TAB]
* {23 Dec 2020}: Reports of flooding at High Street (North) were investigated by RMAs and the LLFA; site visits identified that a substantially blocked small-diameter Network Rail culvert in the vicinity likely contributed to local flooding at this location, prompting confirmation of maintenance actions. (p15) [TAB]
* {23 Dec 2020}: Localised flooding caused by reduced flow in the adjacent watercourse due to vegetation and silt build-up and an unconsented metal security fence obstructing the channel. Investigation and riparian works were carried out: fence removed (actioned under S23), vegetation and silt clearance advised and undertaken, resulting in improved flow by May 2021. (p18) [IMG]
* {23 Dec 2020}: Sections of High Street (North and South) were affected during the 23 December 2020 event: surface water flooding occurred (both North and South), and groundwater flooding was recorded in the North. Contributing issues identified for High Street included sewer capacity problems, mis‑connections to foul sewers, RMA operational issues and riparian maintenance shortcomings. (p26) [TAB]
* {23 Dec 2020}: Quant: full road closure lasting approximately 3 weeks (p11) [PAG]
* {23 Dec 2020}: Quant: approximately 45 cm; full road closure lasting approximately 3 weeks; loss of foul sewerage provision to properties for approximately 3 weeks (p12) [PAG, IMG]
* {23 Dec 2020}: Quant: approximately 45 cm depth; road closure lasting approximately 3 weeks; approximately 3 weeks without foul sewerage provision (p13) [TAB]
* {23 Dec 2020}: Quant: flood depths approximately 20-45cm across the area; residents displaced for 6-8 months (p14) [TAB]

## Topics without coordinates

### Network Rail
* {23 Dec 2020}: Silt build-up within the railway culvert and vegetation within the watercourse on a Network Rail controlled section impeded flows through the culvert, contributing to reduced conveyance and impacting downstream flooding. (p14) [PAG]
* {23 Dec 2020}: A small-diameter railway culvert owned/managed by Network Rail was identified as substantially blocked and not previously included on Network Rail mapping; this culvert was highlighted as potentially critical to local flood risk at High Street (North) and required maintenance actions to reduce future flooding. (p15) [TAB, PAG]
* {23 Dec 2020}: A Network Rail railway culvert was identified as substantially blocked and potentially critical to local flood risk (it had not previously been mapped due to small diameter). Network Rail confirmed required maintenance actions and met on site with LLFA, Parish Council and landowners to determine and schedule remedial works. (p16) [PAG]
* {23 Dec 2020}: Network Rail's under-track culverts (including Culvert 137E and 137F) were investigated after flooding: jetting and CCTV surveys commissioned, culverts de-watered and de-silted, a manhole was raised, structural inspections found only minor defects and minimal flow; follow-up monitoring and possible capacity works were progressed to address culvert-related drainage issues. (p17) [TAB, PAG]
* {23 Dec 2020}: Network Rail undertook inspection and maintenance actions in response to the flooding: jetting and cleansing of culvert ECM/137A (removing ~1 m of silt over a 31 m length), downstream vegetation clearance, and established an inspection/maintenance regime for the culvert and downstream ditch. (p20) [IMG]
* {23 Dec 2020}: The culvert conveying the ordinary watercourse under the railway and the downstream ditch for which Network Rail is riparian owner were implicated in the village flooding. Network Rail has confirmed an inspection and maintenance regime and will commence annual visual inspection and clearance of the ditch and culvert in response to the flooding. (p25)

### Anglian Water
* {23 Dec 2020}: The Anglian Water sewer system was impacted by the flooding; residents lost foul sewerage provision for about three weeks. A nearby Anglian Water pumping station overflowed and its overflow was routed into a piped watercourse during the event. (p13)
* {23 Dec 2020}: Responded to multiple properties being without foul sewerage for about three weeks; investigating resilience of sewerage infrastructure to surface- and groundwater ingress (infiltration reduction plan awaiting Environment Agency approval); CCTV survey planned for Autumn/Winter; pumping station overflow surveyed and found free-flowing; land drain connected to foul sewer capped. (p16)
* {23 Dec 2020, February 2021}: Sewer system surcharge and ingress following excessive rainfall led to properties losing foul sewerage service; Anglian Water obtained and used a temporary Local Environmental Permit from the Environment Agency to pump wastewater into the ordinary watercourse to protect properties while the sewer system recovered (permit required until February 2021). Increased highway gully maintenance frequency and planned measures to reduce infiltration (Infiltration Reduction Plan awaiting EA approval). CCTV survey of sewers scheduled for Autumn/Winter to investigate infiltration. (p19) [MUL]

### Culvert 137F
* {23 Dec 2020}: Subject of investigation and maintenance after flooding reports: monitored for service condition; jetting, CCTV survey and cleansing of silt/debris carried out (works including CCTV and cleansing on 23/6/21); manhole raised for visibility; assessed as structurally intact with only minor defects; options being explored to increase drainage capacity. (p17) [TAB]

### Highway Authority
* {23 Dec 2020}: Highway gullies across The Offords were cleansed and gully maintenance frequency increased to reduce highway flooding and address drainage failures associated with the flood event. (p17)

### Network Rail Culvert
* {23 Dec 2020}: Culvert ECM/137A was heavily silted and vegetated, reducing conveyance capacity; cleansing and jetting were carried out and CCTV after cleansing showed the culvert free from debris.
