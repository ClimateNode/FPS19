### Cossall
* Sustained heavy rainfall during Storm Babet on 20 October 2023, on already saturated ground, caused surface water flooding in the village; roads and properties in Cossall were flooded. (p1)
* Localised internal property flooding occurred in the village. Flooding was caused by surcharging manholes that filled the highway and flowed into adjacent land and properties, and by an ordinary watercourse at capacity which prevented highway gulleys from operating, producing localised highway flooding. (p2) [IMG]
* Localised internal property flooding within the village (specifically on Awsworth Lane and Church Lane) and localised highway flooding where gullies were overwhelmed; manhole surcharging contributed to flow into properties and adjacent land. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 2 properties; 3 properties (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 5 properties (2 on Awsworth Lane, 3 on Church Lane) (p3) [IMG, PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* Significant flooding impacts occurred across the county: roads were closed to promote safety and avoid further flood damage, sandbags were delivered to affected areas, and internal flooding of properties was verified and assessed as part of the response and short‑term recovery. (p5) [PAG]
* Quant: 77 roads (p5) [PAG]

### Broxtowe
* Communities in Broxtowe were affected by flooding; local recovery is being managed by the borough council and support (sandbags, hardship funds and relief grants) has been provided to affected residents and businesses. (p5)

## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Responded to major flooding across the county: officers attended sites after flooding to verify the scale of internal flooding, offered short-term internal flooding recovery aid and support. (p4)
* Nottinghamshire County Council attended flooded sites to verify internal flooding of properties, offered guidance to residents and businesses, collated and published verified flood impact data, chaired response and recovery coordination meetings, provided 24‑hour support, initiated the Section 19 Flood Investigation, led procurement/delivery of the DEFRA Property Flood Resilience Repair Grant Scheme, liaised on community flood signage, delivered sandbags and closed roads to reduce flood risk. (p5)
* Quant: 77 roads (p5)

### Broxtowe Borough Council
* Provided emergency response and recovery support for flooding in Broxtowe: delivered and distributed sandbags to affected residents, managed ongoing recovery in affected communities, administered a flood hardship fund and relief grant schemes, and engaged in the Section 19 Flood Investigation. (p5)
