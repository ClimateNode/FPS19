

# Lowestoft flooding 24 Jul 2015 - 25 Jul 2015

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Velda Close and Aldwyck Way, Lowestoft<br>124\_Suffolk County/124\_2019\_Suffolk County/section-19-report-velda-close-reformatted.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Aldwyck Way, Lowestoft
* Site of a flood event that was the subject of a Section 19 (Flood and Water Management Act 2010) investigation by Suffolk County Council as Lead Local Flood Authority. (p1)
* Internal flooding of domestic properties affecting main living areas; flooding of outbuildings and gardens; public highway flooded for approx. 50 m to an approximate depth of 450 mm; local Anglian Water surface water drainage system was surcharged. (p2)
* The audible early warning system on Aldwyck Way (owned by Cotman Housing Association) failed to activate during the flood event. (p4)
* Highway surface water drainage capacity at this location was identified for evaluation and reporting following the flood investigation (i.e. the highway drainage was a concern in the flood event). (p6) [TAB]
* Quant: 33 domestic properties; approx. 50 m of public highway; approx. 450 mm depth (p2)

### Lowestoft
* The town containing Velda Close and Aldwyck Way, which were the subject locations of a Section 19 flood investigation by Suffolk County Council (LLFA). (p1)
* Parts of Lowestoft (specifically Velda Close and Aldwyck Way) experienced internal flooding of domestic properties (main living areas), flooding to outbuildings and gardens, and highway flooding; the Anglian Water surface water drainage system was surcharged during the event. (p2)
* Quant: 33 domestic properties; approx. 50 m of public highway; approx. 450 mm depth (p2)

### Kirkley
* {01 Sep 2002 - 30 Sep 2002, 2013, 24 Jul 2015 - 25 Jul 2015, September - October 2006}: Residential area experienced internal flooding of domestic properties (main living areas) and flooding of outbuildings and gardens. The public highway flooded over a stretch of approximately 50 m to around 450 mm depth. The Anglian Water surface water drainage system and highway drainage were exceeded/surcharged. There is a history of repeat internal flooding reported in September 2002, September/October 2006 and 2013. (p3) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
* {01 Sep 2002 - 30 Sep 2002, 2013, 24 Jul 2015 - 25 Jul 2015, September - October 2006}: Quant: 33 domestic properties; 50 m of public highway; 450 mm depth (public highway); 300 mm internal depth (p3) [IMG, PAG, MUL]

### Velda Close, Lowestoft
* Site of a flood event that was the subject of a Section 19 (Flood and Water Management Act 2010) investigation by Suffolk County Council as Lead Local Flood Authority. (p1)
* Internal flooding of domestic properties affecting main living areas; flooding of outbuildings and gardens; public highway flooded for approx. 50 m to an approximate depth of 450 mm; local Anglian Water surface water drainage system was surcharged. (p2)
* Identified in the action plan for Suffolk County Council Highways to evaluate the highway surface-water drainage system and its design capacity. (p6) [TAB]

### Tom Crisp Way, Lowestoft

### Kirkley Stream
* {01 Sep 2002 - 30 Sep 2002, 2013, 24 Jul 2015 - 25 Jul 2015, September - October 2006}: Blockages, debris and excessive vegetation within the channel at several locations contributed to the drainage system being exceeded and to surcharge of surface water systems, which in turn caused internal flooding of properties and highway flooding in the area. (p3) [PAG, MUL]
* Blockages at several locations along Kirkley Stream — caused by structures, debris, excessive vegetation and illegally dumped/fly-tipped material washed downstream — contributed to channel blockages, impeding drainage and exacerbating flooding. Some surface water outlets were at bed level and could not drain into the stream. (p4) [PAG]
* Subject of a commissioned full topographical survey and of actions to consider limiting new discharges related to development following the flood investigation. (p6) [TAB]
* {01 Sep 2002 - 30 Sep 2002, 2013, 24 Jul 2015 - 25 Jul 2015, September - October 2006}: Quant: 33 domestic properties (p3) [PAG, MUL]


## Topics without coordinates

### Anglian Water
* Anglian Water's surface water drainage systems were exceeded by the rainfall event. Their pumps (three screw pumps discharging to Lake Lothing) were operational and were reported to be working within capacity during the event. (p4)
* Quant: three screw pumps (p4)

### Cotman Housing Association
* Some properties owned by Cotman Housing Association were flooded despite having property-level mitigation measures fitted; those measures failed to prevent water entering the properties. An audible early warning system owned by Cotman Housing Association (on Aldwyck Way) failed to activate during the event. (p4)

### SCC Highway
* The county highway drainage system was exceeded by the event (surface water drainage capacity exceeded), contributing to surface water / urban flooding. The investigation found no evidence that highway attenuation basins overtopped. (p4)

### Highways
* Suffolk County Council highway surface water drainage was exceeded/overwhelmed during the event; highway drainage systems exceeded their design capacity. The report notes no evidence that highway attenuation basins overtopped. (p4)
