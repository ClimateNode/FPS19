

# Lancashire flooding 01 Jun 2018

Reference document:<br>1 June 2018 Floods In Lancashire: Section 19 Investigation Report; Appendix A<br>069\_Lancashire County/appendix-a-flood-investigation-details-june-2018-flooding.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Pendle
* Extreme rainfall overwhelmed the local drainage in Earby; capacity had been reduced by blockages/tree roots. A local Yorkshire Water pumping station operated as designed but the time-lag to trigger the pump allowed surface water to build up. Pendle Borough Council acquired demountable pumps and flood barriers afterwards. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: 2 properties internally flooded (p1) [TAB]

### Wyre
* Surface water flooding from neighbouring land (private drainage issue) affected the village of Pilling in Wyre during the event. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: 1 property (0 internally flooded) (p1) [TAB]

### Hyndburn
* Blocked highway gully led to surface water flooding affecting a property in Accrington; gullies and outfall were cleaned as remedial action. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: 1 property (p1) [TAB]

### Ribble Valley
* Multiple localities in the district were affected on 1 June 2018. Surface water flooding occurred from highways and neighbouring land (Billington, Hurst Green). In Longridge the drainage network was overloaded by intense rainfall and a local sewer was obstructed by tree roots causing foul (sewer) flooding on one side of a road; other properties suffered surface water flooding. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: 43 properties affected (16 internally flooded) (p1) [TAB]

### Rossendale
* Within Rossendale (Bacup) cellar flooding from an unknown source affected properties; surface water flooding from neighbouring land affected properties. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: 2 properties (cellar flooding); 1 property (surface water flooding) (p1) [TAB]

### Lancashire
* Very heavy, isolated rainfall across the central belt of Lancashire on 1 June 2018 produced runoff faster than urban drainage could collect and disperse. Local drainage networks were overwhelmed in multiple locations; post-event investigations revealed defects in highway drainage systems and public sewers and an overall lack of capacity to accommodate runoff from such intense rainfall. (p1) [TAB]

### Accrington

### Fulwood

### Billington

### Earby
* Extreme, intense rainfall on 1 June 2018 overwhelmed the village drainage; capacity was reduced by blockages/tree roots. A local Yorkshire Water pumping station operated as designed but the time-lag to pump triggering allowed surface water to build up. Pendle BC acquired demountable pump and flood barriers after the event. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: 3 properties affected (0 internally flooded); 2 properties affected (2 internally flooded) (p1) [TAB]

### Longridge
* Multiple separate flooding incidents on 1 June 2018: blocked gullies and outfalls were attended; an intense rainfall event overloaded the drainage network putting homes at risk due to local gradients; a sewer obstructed by tree roots caused foul flooding on one side of a road while the opposite side suffered surface water flooding. All drainage obstructions were later inspected and cleared. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: 21 properties (0 internally flooded); 8 properties (8 internally flooded); 1 property (0 internally flooded); 9 properties (5 internally flooded) (p1) [TAB]

### Barnoldswick
* Multiple incidents in Barnoldswick involved surface water flooding from the highway and neighbouring land and blocked gullies; local drainage/culvert issues contributed and gullies/outfalls were cleaned as remediation. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: 5 properties total impacted, 1 property internally flooded (p1) [TAB]

### Hurst Green

### Bacup

## Topics without coordinates

### Yorkshire Water
* Local Yorkshire Water pumping station in Earby operated as designed during the event, but the time‑lag to triggering the pump allowed surface water to build up under extreme rainfall; Yorkshire Water undertook significant post‑event work with LCC Highways and was involved in clearing drainage obstructions. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: 2 properties internally flooded in Earby (report associates Yorkshire Water with response to this incident). (p1) [TAB]

### LCC Highways
* Attended multiple blocked-gully reports and highway surface-water flooding across the affected towns; cleaned gullies and outfalls; advised re‑profiling footways in future schemes to reduce run‑off risk; carried out significant post‑event works in conjunction with Yorkshire Water in some locations. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: Properties impacted in multiple towns (examples from report): Accrington 1; Barnoldswick 2; Earby 3; Fulwood 1; Hurst Green 3; Longridge entries: 21, 8, 1, 9. (p1) [TAB]

### Pendle Borough Council
* Pendle Borough Council invested grant funds to acquire a demountable pump and flood barriers for use in similar future events and was involved in the post‑event response in Earby. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: 2 properties internally flooded in Earby (incident where Pendle BC provided mitigation funding and response). (p1) [TAB]

### United Utilities
* Foul flooding in Longridge was caused by a local sewer obstructed by tree roots; UU plc (with LCC Highways) inspected and cleared the obstructions and inspected drainage systems following the event. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: 9 properties affected in Longridge, of which 5 were internally flooded. (p1) [TAB]
