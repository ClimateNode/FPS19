### Devon
* Short, intense rainfall between 3–5 September 2016 caused flooding of numerous roads and properties across the county; highway drainage systems struggled or overflowed, producing highway and external property flooding and prompting investigation and recommended maintenance by Devon County Council Highways. (p8) [PAG]
* Across Devon (3–5 Sept 2016) short-duration, high-intensity rainfall caused highway drainage and sewer systems in several areas to struggle and sometimes overflow. Roads were closed in parts of South Hams and surface water was reported on roads in South Hams, West Devon, Torridge and Teignbridge; drains became overwhelmed by mud and silt. (p10) [IMG, PAG]

## Topics without coordinates

### Devon County Council
* Drains were overwhelmed during the intense rainfall, resulting in surface water running down roads; a number of enquiries were sent to Devon County Council Highways about overwhelmed drains during the 3 September 2016 event. Recommended actions were made for DCC to develop Surface Water Management Plan options for Braunton. (p11)
* As Lead Local Flood Authority (LLFA), DCC responded to the surface water flooding (03 Sep 2016 - 05 Sep 2016) by monitoring and recording incidents, incorporating them into its action plan and future investment programme, and leading delivery and review of the Braunton surface water management scheme. (p14) [TAB, PAG]

### North Devon District Council
* The North Devon district experienced heavy, intense rainfall on 3 September 2016 that caused surface water and highways flooding in Braunton and elsewhere in the district; drains were reported as overwhelmed leading to roads and properties flooding. (p11)

### South West Water
* Worked with the Fire Service to pump away flood water from affected properties in Charles Street, Dartmouth. (p12)

### Devon Fire and Rescue Service
* Responded to flooding in Charles Street, assisting South West Water to pump water away from affected properties; incident recorded as external flooding in their log. (p12)

### Devon County Council Highways
* Highways were affected by surface water flooding during 03 Sep 2016 - 05 Sep 2016: drains and culverts could not cope, roads were impassable in some places with ponding lasting over 24 hours. DCC Highways were tasked with reviewing and carrying out maintenance of drains/culverts and reviewing movement on Caen Street to reduce bow-wave effects. (p14) [TAB]
