## Place names with coordinates

### Spondon
* Other areas of Spondon experienced surface water flooding during the same event, including isolated cases of internal property flooding affecting nearby localities. (p3)
* A significant cluster of internally flooded residential properties occurred within Spondon — specifically on Willowcroft Road — triggering the formal investigation. Internal flooding affected nearby homes. (p5) [IMG]
* The urban area of Spondon forms the large upstream catchment whose impermeable surfaces and paved areas generate concentrated surface water runoff that drains by gravity towards the investigation area (Willowcroft Road). This runoff contributed to surface water flow along the public surface water sewer system and the natural flow path during intense rainfall (e.g. 19 July 2014), increasing surface water flood risk in the downstream study area. (p6) [IMG]
* {1981, 1986, May 1997, July 2002, July 2007, 06 Jul 2012, October 2013}: Multiple historical surface water and foul (sewer) flooding events affected properties and gardens across the Spondon area, including internal property flooding and highway/garden flooding during recorded events. (p7) [PAG, MUL]
* Around 7 homes along Willowcroft Road (north of the A52) experienced flood water in ground-floor living areas. An undetermined number of nearby properties suffered severe external property flooding, in some cases close to entering ground-floor living areas. (p10) [IMG, PAG]
* On 19th July 2014 an extremely isolated, high‑intensity storm produced rapid surface runoff from largely impermeable paved areas. The local highway gullies and the public surface water sewer network were inundated and exceeded capacity, directing rapid downslope flows into lower elevations. This led to internal flooding of properties and significant damage to ground‑floor living areas; flow routes were defined by local topography toward the River Derwent. (p11) [PAG]
* Spondon was particularly affected by surface water flooding during the intense storm; the investigation focused on a cluster of properties that were internally flooded as drainage was overwhelmed and runoff followed natural flow paths. (p12)
* An intense, isolated thunderstorm on the afternoon of 19th July 2014 caused surface water drainage (highway drains and surface water sewers) to be exceeded, resulting in surface water flooding across many areas of Spondon. (p13)

### Willowcroft Road, Spondon
* Intense short-duration rainfall caused surface water flooding on Willowcroft Road resulting in internal flood damage to properties, severe highway flooding and external property flooding. (p3)
* Internal flooding of residential properties occurred on Willowcroft Road during the flood event. (p4)
* A significant cluster of internal property flooding occurred with multiple residential properties flooded internally on Willowcroft Road, triggering a formal Section 19 investigation. (p5) [PAG]
* Roofs, driveways and highway gullies in the catchment drain to the surface water sewer along Willowcroft Road. Willowcroft Road lies on a natural flow path and conveys collected surface water down the slope towards Nottingham Road, concentrating urban runoff from a large catchment during intense rainfall. (p6) [PAG]
* Ground-floor living areas of properties on Willowcroft Road were inundated; external property flooding also occurred in the area and in some cases was close to entering ground-floor living areas. (p10)
* On 19 July 2014 intense, highly localised convective rainfall produced rapid surface runoff that inundated gullies and the public surface water sewer network. Excess runoff flowed downslope along localised routes into lower-elevation areas and entered properties on Willowcroft Road, causing internal flooding of ground-floor living areas and significant damage to those areas. (p11)
* Surface water from an intense, localised summer storm overwhelmed the local drainage; rapid runoff down steep local topography affected properties on Willowcroft Road as surface water travelled from highways, roofs and driveways. (p12)
* Residents reported floodwater on Willowcroft Road to Derby City Council following the intense, isolated storm; the street experienced surface water flooding during the event. (p14)
* Quant: 7 properties (p3)
* Quant: 7 residential properties (p4)
* Quant: 7 residential properties (p5) [PAG]
* Quant: 7 homes (p10)
* Quant: 7 properties (p11)
