

# Caldecote flooding 08 Aug 2014 - 09 Aug 2014

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Caldecote<br>021\_Cambridgeshire County/021\_2014\_Cambridgeshire County/Flood\_Investigation\_Report\_Caldecote.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Caldecote
* {2001, 08 Aug 2014 - 09 Aug 2014}: Various locations in Caldecote experienced flooding from multiple sources, specifically drains, local ditches and surface water runoff during the incident. (p2) [TAB, MUL]
* An extreme short-duration rainfall event overwhelmed drainage and watercourses causing flooding across the village: 12 properties were reported flooded internally; the road network was inundated; local drains surcharged and watercourses overtopped; 17 residents were evacuated from a residential care home. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 12 properties; 17 residents (p3) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### South Cambridgeshire District Council
* Assessed the ditches ('awarded watercourses') after the flood event and determined no additional maintenance was required; relevant maintenance work was completed by December 2014. (p4) [TAB]

### Anglian Water
* Installed a storm tank at the pumping station at Highfields, Caldecote to provide greater retention during extreme rainfall; pumps were uprated and flow meters installed to increase flow capacity and provide telemetry. (p4) [TAB]

### Cambridgeshire County Council
* Following the extreme rainfall event, highways' surface water drains for which the council is responsible required cleansing and surveying. (p4) [TAB]

### Cambridgeshire County Council Highways
* Following an extreme rainfall event, surface water drains for which Cambridgeshire County Council Highways are responsible were cleaned and surveyed to address drainage issues after flooding. (p4) [TAB]
