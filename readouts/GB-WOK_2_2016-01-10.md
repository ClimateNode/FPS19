

# Wokingham Unitary Authority flooding 10 Jan 2016 - 13 Jan 2016

## Place names with coordinates

### Sandford Lane, Wokingham Unitary Authority
* Floodwater from the Emm Brook and River Loddon spilled into the local network of lakes and roadside ditches and flowed onto the highway. The section immediately west of Emm Brook Bridge was flooded first (water spilled out of the roadside ditch), reaching approximately 50 mm depth on 10 January. As the event progressed, the central section and the area adjacent to the Black Bridge were also flooded (water flowing out of the roadside ditch and directly from Sandford Lake). Back-up in the Emm Brook (observed above culvert soffit) caused flow upstream into the roadside ditch; overtopping of Sandford Lake contributed to highway flooding. (p6)
* Sections of the highway flooded from water flowing out of roadside ditches and from overland flow fed by the River Loddon and Emm Brook. Floodwater remained on the highway for multiple days, causing the highway to be closed for the duration of the event and then re-opened after drainage. (p7) [PAG]
* {11 Jan 2016 - 14 Jan 2016, early January 2016}: Experienced overland flow/flooding during the early part of January 2016; was closed by Wokingham Borough Council from 11 January 2016 until reopening on 14 January 2016. Flood water carried debris that could have blocked drainage and required clearance; the highway was swept to remove flood debris and silt once water had drained. (p8) [PAG, MUL]
* Portion of Sandford Lane was inundated during the January 2016 flood event. The flooded area of Sandford Lane was not within an Environment Agency Flood Warning Area and did not receive a flood warning from the Environment Agency. (p9) [PAG]
* Floodwater from the River Loddon and Emm Brook flowed onto and inundated the highway (Sandford Lane). Measures considered include raising the carriageway or constructing a bund to prevent floodwater entering the road; roadside ditches and drainage require maintenance to reduce recurrence. (p10) [PAG]
* {general}: Subject to flood risk from the River Loddon and Emm Brook. Bridge structures associated with these Main Rivers in the vicinity need regular maintenance to ensure they do not increase flood risk. Alleviation would require Environment Agency capital works, but no such scheme is likely to be funded because there are no properties affected. (p11) [PAG]
* {01 Jan 2016 - 31 Jan 2016, 10 Jan 2016 - 13 Jan 2016}: Sections of Sandford Lane were inundated during the January 2016 event. The map annotations identify a 'first section of the road to flood' and a 'final section of the road to flood.' Floodwater moved from Sandford Lake, Lavell's Lake and associated roadside ditches toward the road and bridges. Channels were cut into the roadside verge to try to drain water into the ditches, but these measures were insufficient to prevent flooding of the road. (p18) [IMG]
* Quant: approximately 50 mm (p6)
* Quant: flood water depths on the highway ranged from approximately 50 mm to 300 mm; a section between River Loddon Bridge and Black Bridge reached approximately 600 mm; flooded 4 times since the beginning of 2016; typically floods between 6 and 8 times each year; highway remained flooded on 11, 12 and 13 January and was re-opened on 14 January (road closed for 3 days); longest previous closure 1 week. (p7) [PAG]
* Quant: 0 properties affected (p10) [PAG]
* {general}: Quant: 0 properties affected by flooding on Sandford Lane (p11) [PAG]

### Sandford Lake
* Water in Sandford Lake overtopped the lake banks and flowed into the roadside ditch, contributing to flood water on Sandford Lane (highway) during the January 2016 event. (p6) [PAG]
* Water flowed directly out of Sandford Lake onto Sandford Lane, causing overland flooding of the highway adjacent to the lake. (p7)
* Flood water from Sandford Lake could flow onto Sandford Lane; a bund between the road and Sandford Lake was proposed to prevent flood water reaching the highway. (p10)
* {01 Jan 2016 - 31 Jan 2016}: Sandford Lake is shown within flood-affected shading on the map for the January 2016 event; the lake and its adjacent parkland are indicated as inundated or prone to inundation, contributing to local flood extent within Dinton Pastures Country Park. (p16) [IMG]
* {10 Jan 2016 - 13 Jan 2016, January 2016 flood event}: Included within the confirmed flood extent on the map and acted as a source of floodwater that flowed toward Sandford Lane; contributed to inundation of adjacent road sections. (p18) [IMG]

### Wokingham Unitary Authority
* Significant rainfall in Wokingham and the surrounding area led to the River Loddon and the Emm Brook bursting their banks in the vicinity of Dinton Pastures Country Park, which in turn caused flooding within the borough (notably Sandford Lane). (p6) [PAG]

### Emm Brook
* Emm Brook burst its banks / had water levels above the soffit height of the bridge culverts, causing back-up of water into the roadside drainage ditch; the backed-up water (and water entering the ditch from the Country Park) flowed onto the highway and contributed to flooding of Sandford Lane. (p6)
* {01 Jan 2016 - 31 Jan 2016}: Increases in the volume of water in Emm Brook within the wider catchment contributed to the flooding experienced in the vicinity of Sandford Lane. (p7)
* Flooding from Emm Brook (a Main River) contributed to the flood risk to Sandford Lane; associated bridge structures need maintenance to avoid increasing flood risk. (p10) [PAG]
* {01 Jan 2016 - 31 Jan 2016}: Map for the January 2016 event shows flood-affected areas concentrated around Emm Brook; flooding from the brook inundated adjacent parkland and contributed to elevated flood risk at its confluence with the River Loddon within Dinton Pastures Country Park. (p16) [IMG]

### Dinton Pastures Country Park
* Water from the River Loddon and the Emm Brook burst their banks within the vicinity of the Country Park; this caused river water to spill into the park's system of lakes and drainage ditches, which in turn contributed to flooding of the adjacent highway (Sandford Lane). Sandford Lake overtopped its banks and discharged into roadside ditches, adding to the highway flooding. (p6)
* {early January 2016}: Overland flow routes and approximate flood extents passed through the park during the early part of January 2016 (the park was within the mapped flood extents and overland flow routes). (p7)
* {01 Jan 2016 - 31 Jan 2016}: The park is shown with multiple lakes, marshes and rivers (River Loddon, Emm Brook) and large areas are shaded as flood-affected. The map indicates that parkland, lakes (Sandford Lake, Lavell's Lake, Heron's Water, Black Swan Lake, etc.), marshes and access routes within the country park were inundated during the January 2016 flood event. (p16) [IMG]

### River Loddon
* Flooding of the River Loddon caused inundation of the Sandford Lane area during the January 2016 flood event. (p9)
* The River Loddon is shown as a main river with flood-affected areas shaded along its course. The map indicates the river caused inundation of adjacent lakes, marshes and parkland (including areas around its confluence with Emm Brook) during the January 2016 event. (p16) [IMG]

### Wokingham Borough Council
* {01 Jan 2016 - 31 Jan 2016}: Highway Maintenance officers inspected and maintained highway infrastructure during the emergency; they visited Sandford Lane several times in January 2016, collected observational information and identified key problem areas and potential solutions related to flooding impacts on the highway. (p4)
* {11 Jan 2016 - 14 Jan 2016}: As the Lead Local Flood Authority and Highway Authority, officers closed Sandford Lane (11–14 January 2016), monitored the closure twice daily, cleared debris from flood water that could have blocked drainage, and swept the highway to remove flood debris and silt after the water drained. (p8)
