

# Romsey flooding 23 Dec 2013 - 06 Feb 2014

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report On Flooding Incident Investigation Romsey<br>050\_Hampshire/050\_2014\_Hampshire/romsey-phase-one-executive-summary.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Romsey
* Multiple flood mechanisms affected the town: fluvial, surface water, foul/sewer and groundwater flooding. Flooding directly affected properties across the town through a variety of mechanisms. (p1)
* {general}: The Report investigated flooding incidents in Romsey and identifies priority areas (Mainstone and Causeway) where flood risk needs to be addressed; the report is intended to form the basis for future options to manage flood risk in Romsey. (p2)
* Quant: up to 96 properties; 69 properties reported as flooded (26 internally, 37 externally, 8 with cellar flooding) (p1)

### Mainstone
* Surface water flooding from the A3090/A27 affected properties at Mainstone; separate incidents from high groundwater levels combined with excess flows in an ordinary watercourse caused flooding to the rear of properties on Mainstone and properties on the Causeway. (p1)

### Abbotswood
* The approved surface water drainage strategy and attenuation basins at Abbotswood were judged not to have increased flood risk to Cupernham Lane and in fact slightly alleviated the Cupernham Lane flooding witnessed between December 2013 and February 2014; a revised drainage strategy is being implemented by the development. (p2)

### The Causeway, Mainstone
* Surface water from the A3090/A27 flooded properties at Mainstone. Separately, high groundwater combined with excess flows in an ordinary watercourse in the field to the rear of Mainstone caused flooding of properties on the Causeway and the rear of properties on Mainstone. (p1)
* {general}: Identified as a priority area where flood risk needs to be addressed. (p2)

### Cupernham Lane, Cupernham
* Surface runoff, exacerbated by terrain and high groundwater levels, led to flooding of Cupernham Lane. (p1)
* Flooding was witnessed on Cupernham Lane during incidents between December 2013 and February 2014; this flooding was described as being slightly alleviated by attenuation provided by the Abbotswood development. (p2)

### Middlebridge Street, Mainstone
* Properties in Middlebridge Street were affected by surface water and foul/sewer flooding from sewer networks; some properties also reported groundwater flooding. (p1) [PAG]
* {general}: Properties experienced surface water flooding and foul flooding from sewer networks; some properties also reported groundwater flooding. (p2)

### Winchester Road, Romsey
* Groundwater issues led to flooding of properties' cellars on Winchester Road; inundated sewers contributed to surface water flooding of properties close to the Plaza Roundabout. (p1)

### Riverside Gardens, Mainstone
* Properties in Riverside Gardens were affected by surface water flooding originating from sewer networks; some properties also suffered direct fluvial flooding from the River Test. (p1)

### Mainstone, Romsey
* Surface water from the A3090/A27 flooded properties at Mainstone. Separately, high groundwater combined with excess flows in an ordinary watercourse in the field to the rear of Mainstone caused flooding of properties on the Causeway and the rear of properties on Mainstone. (p1)

### Plaza Roundabout
* Inundated sewers near the Plaza Roundabout led to surface water flooding of properties in that location. (p1)

### River Test
* Fluvial flooding from the River Test directly affected some properties (notably in Riverside Gardens). (p1)
