### Cocker Beck
* {1999, 2007, 2012, 2013, 2019, 2020, 2023}: Repeated flooding of the Lowdham catchment caused by Cocker Beck and its tributaries. Historic significant flooding included 1999 when 300 properties were flooded. The regular flood history led to flood storage works on the Cricket Pitch in 2000 and prompted the Environment Agency to fund a £25m flood alleviation project for the catchment (preliminary construction started September 2023). (p2) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
* {1999, 2007, 2012, 2013, 2019, 2020, 2023, 02 Jan 2024}: The main watercourse through Lowdham is shown as the area of highest predicted surface water flood risk on the map and is implicated in catchment flood risk affecting local infrastructure and properties; flooding associated with heavy rainfall (Storm Henk) caused internal flooding in Lowdham. (p3) [PAG, MUL]
* The Cocker Beck was overwhelmed by surface water from the saturated upper catchment, overtopped at points along its route and spilled out, contributing to flooding of nearby roads and properties. Pumps placed in the Beck (six pumps, only two operational) reduced through‑flow capacity and were reported as contributing to earlier overtopping and backup upstream (e.g. at Station Road). (p5) [PAG, IMG]
* {01 Oct 2023 - 31 Oct 2023, 02 Jan 2024}: A partial collapse of a structure into the Cocker Beck (October 2023) led to pumps being placed inside the Beck at Lime Tree Gardens to route water around the collapse; the pumps limited the Beck's capacity, causing the water to back up and contributing to earlier and significant overtopping of the wall on Station Road and associated local flooding. (p6) [MUL]
* {01 Jan 2024}: The Cocker Beck at Lowdham caused flooding that prompted a Flood Warning and required clearance of blockages and temporary pumping to mitigate risk of channel collapse and property flooding. (p8)
* {1999, 2007, 2012, 2013, 2019, 2020, 2023}: Quant: 300 properties; £25m (p2) [IMG, PAG, MUL]

### FLAG
* Lowdham Flood Action Group (FLAG) reported that during Storm Henk floodwaters from the overtopping Cocker Beck, surface water runoff and surcharging drainage led to widespread flooding; they observed that some defences (e.g. a barrier opposite the Magna Charta) were not erected, that the recently repaired left bank defence was overtopped flooding The Corner and properties on Main Street, and that floodwater proceeded down Southwell Road into The Cottages, Willow Holt and Merevale contributing to further property flooding. FLAG also reported pumps placed in the Beck and reduced pump availability which they considered contributed to earlier and significant overtopping upstream at Station Road, causing flooding of Victoria Avenue, Longmoor Avenue, Station Road and The Orchards. (p5)
* Quant: 30 properties; properties at 7-11 Main Street (addresses 7, 8, 9, 10, 11); six pumps (only two operational) (p5)

### Environment Agency
* Placed pumps inside the Cocker Beck at Lime Tree Gardens to pump water around a collapsed structure to Blenheim Bridge; the pumps (only two of six operational) reduced the effective capacity of the Beck to convey floodwater out of Lowdham, and may have contributed to early and significant overtopping of the wall on Station Road which led to flooding of properties in Victoria Avenue, Longmoor Avenue, Station Road and The Orchards. (p6)
* {01 Jan 2024}: Issued a Flood Warning for the Cocker Beck at Lowdham; instigated flood patrols and cleared blockages before and after peak flows; organised and attended a multi-agency community drop-in; coordinated community information officers to visit affected communities; deployed temporary pumps to mitigate potential collapse of a riparian channel side and to protect property (pumps removed once water levels receded). (p8)
