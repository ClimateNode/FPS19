

# Lowick flooding 09 Mar 2016

## Place names with coordinates

### Main Street, Lowick
* {03 Mar 2016}: Surface water and ordinary watercourse flows overwhelmed drainage and flowed to low points around private property, causing internal flooding of a property on Main Street (recorded on more than one occasion). (p7)
* Internal flooding of a residential property occurred at Main Street; this property has experienced internal flooding on more than one occasion in the five years prior to the investigation. (p9)
* Main Street lies in the valley and localised catchment of Harpers Brook and was affected by surface water runoff and ordinary watercourse flooding associated with the event; properties in the Main Street area were among those flooded. (p10)
* {Easter 1998, Late 2012/early 2013, 09 Mar 2016}: Surface-water overland flows travelled through the access tunnel beneath the A6116 to a cut-off ditch behind a stable; the ditch filled and spilled. Internal flooding of one residential property occurred on 09/03/2016 (all ground-floor rooms flooded) and there was minor ingress of surface water to the rear of a second residential property. Historical incidents include internal flooding of one property (kitchen and another room) at Easter 1998 and internal flooding of one property in late 2012/early 2013 (whole floor replaced). (p11) [TAB, PAG, MUL]
* Surface water flowed down an access road to the rear gardens of two properties on Main Street. A second flow route crossed the rear garden of a third property, submerging the patio and then entering the house, causing internal flooding. The flow through the access tunnel beneath the A6116 was reported as very strong. (p12) [PAG]
* Heavy rainfall on a saturated catchment caused drainage systems to be overwhelmed so excess surface water flowed over ground and followed natural contours to low points around private properties on Main Street; properties at a low point in the valley were vulnerable to overland flow, exacerbated by A6116 embankments which channelled a concentrated flow through the access tunnel. (p13)
* Properties and recent developments on Main Street experienced surface water flooding and/or had ineffective surface water drainage, prompting a recommendation to review planning policies, flood risk assessments and drainage designs and to contact developers to address the flooding and drainage failures. (p22)
* Main Street lies within the mapped geographic extent of the 9 March 2016 incident and was subject to overland flow routed from surrounding fields via the mapped drainage network (pipes/culverts and a cut-off ditch). (p30) [IMG]
* Surface water ran along the front of a property on Main Street and flowed through to the rear gardens of neighbouring properties, causing flooding of those rear gardens. (p44) [IMG, PAG]
* Surface water flowed across the paved driveway/access route in front of a property on Main Street, travelling along depressions and slope toward the rear gardens of neighbouring properties. (p45) [IMG, PAG]
* {03 Mar 2016}: Quant: 1 property (p7)
* Quant: 1 property (p9)
* {Easter 1998, Late 2012/early 2013, 09 Mar 2016}: Quant: 1 residential property (Easter 1998); 1 residential property (late 2012/early 2013); 1 residential property (09/03/2016 - all ground floor rooms flooded); minor ingress to rear of a second residential property (09/03/2016) (p11) [TAB, PAG, MUL]
* Quant: two properties; one property (patio submerged and water entered house) (p12) [PAG]
* Quant: two properties (p44) [IMG, PAG]

### Lowick
* {03 Mar 2016}: Flooding from heavy rainfall on a saturated catchment caused excess surface water to flow to low points around properties in Lowick, resulting in internal flooding of at least one property (experienced on more than one occasion). (p7)
* A flood incident on 9th March 2016 affected Main Street in Lowick, resulting in internal flooding of one property. (p9) [PAG]
* Internal flooding of residential properties and widespread local flooding from a combination of agricultural runoff, surface water runoff and ordinary watercourse (Harpers Brook) flooding. Main Street is within the affected valley and properties there and elsewhere in the village were impacted. (p10)
* Heavy rainfall on a saturated catchment caused local drainage systems to be unable to collect and convey rainwater, producing excess surface water that flowed overland to low points around private properties in Lowick; concentrated flows were formed by the A6116 embankments. (p13) [PAG]
* Overland flow from surrounding fields was routed toward and into the village during the incident (map inset shows the flood extent). Flow was channelled along mapped main routes and controlled/modified by three key drainage features (Q1, Q2, Q3), pipe/culvert sections and a cut-off ditch. (p30) [IMG]
* {03 Mar 2016}: Quant: 1 property (p7)
* Quant: 1 property (p9) [PAG]
* Quant: Over 70 properties flooded internally; over 200 reports of flooding (total reported incidents) (p10)
* Quant: three critical drainage points (Q1, Q2, Q3) (p30) [IMG]

### Northamptonshire
* Heavy rainfall (approx. 30mm in 12 hours) on saturated ground led to agricultural runoff, surface water runoff and ordinary watercourse flooding across the county, resulting in multiple internal flood reports and widespread surface water impacts. (p7)
* On 9th March 2016 heavy rainfall produced runoff and ordinary watercourse flooding across the county, resulting in widespread reports of flooding and internal flooding of properties. (p9) [PAG]
* Quant: over 70 properties; over 200 reports of flooding (p7)
* Quant: over 70 properties internally flooded; over 200 reports of flooding (p9) [PAG]

### Drayton Road, Lowick
* {2000}: Fluvial flooding affected the recreation ground and agricultural land to the west of Harpers Brook, occurring north and south of Drayton Road. (p11) [TAB]

### Harper's Brook
* Ordinary watercourse flooding from Harpers Brook contributed to the local flood event that affected Lowick, combining with agricultural and surface water runoff to cause internal flooding of properties in the village. (p10)
* {2000}: Fluvial (river) flooding of the recreation ground and agricultural land west of Harpers Brook, affecting areas north and south of Drayton Road (recorded in 2000). (p11) [TAB]
* Quant: Over 70 properties flooded internally; over 200 reports of flooding (impacts in Lowick linked to Harpers Brook's flooding) (p10)


## Topics without coordinates

### Anglian Water
* Public sewerage assets were identified as taking water and having insufficient capacity, contributing to flood risk to properties in Lowick; recommendation to assess sources entering the public sewer and to identify and improve any assets where capacity is insufficient to reduce property flooding. (p22)

### Highways
* Surface water runoff (including channelling from the A6116 embankments) resulted in water on carriageways; highway drainage capacity and cleansing were identified as necessary to reduce flood risk to adjacent properties. (p22)
