### River Wandle
* River water levels rose during the heavy rainfall, contributing to the combined fluvial/surface water/sewer flood event and placing adjacent Watermead Lane at risk of fluvial-influenced flooding; primary surface water flow path outflows into the River Wandle. (p3)
* River Wandle levels rose following heavy/prolonged rainfall and were implicated in flooding of adjacent roads and farmland and in the internal flooding of properties on Watermead Lane on 10th June 2019. The Environment Agency mobilised staff to check and clear blockages and debris on the river. (p12)
* Rising River Wandle levels, driven by surface water and overland flow from the defined catchment, contributed to flooding risk adjacent to Watermead Lane; the report identifies the river as a source of fluvial-influenced flooding and notes two faulty flap valves may have worsened the effect. (p16)
* {general}: During a modeled 1 in 1000 year event the River Wandle is predicted to spill out of its left bank, causing flooding along Watermead Lane. (p18)
* {general}: Once flood levels exceed the River Wandle channel capacity, water spills out of the river (west bank) and flows onto the adjacent highway (Watermead Lane), causing fluvial-influenced flooding of the highway corridor and threatening nearby properties. (p21) [PAG]
* {general}: Described as the source of fluvial flooding in the area: once river levels exceed channel capacity water spills out of the west bank and creates modelled fluvial flood extents that threaten adjacent roads and properties. (p22) [PAG, IMG]
* Contributed to flooding on 10 June 2019 via outfalls into which two defective flap valves were reported; this likely reduced discharge capacity and significantly increased flood levels along Watermead Lane. (p30)
* The River Wandle was implicated in the event; incident response staff were checking and clearing blockages on the River Wandle which contributed to flooding at nearby locations. (p32)
* The June 2019 hydrological review covered the event across the River Wandle catchment, indicating the river/catchment was implicated in the June 10, 2019 flooding event; the report concluded the rainfall was significant and that flooding from watercourses in locations within the catchment was possible. (p33)
* Fluvial influence from the River Wandle contributed to flooding at Watermead Lane on 10 June 2019; proximity to the river and lack/failure of drainage backflow protection allowed river water to flow into the road and is linked to internal flooding of properties on Watermead Lane. (p37)

### London Fire Brigade

### South East Water
* SES Water's private sewer network along Watermead Lane was identified as a contributing/exacerbating factor in the flood event; poor maintenance and limited capacity of the private sewer network exacerbated flooding which contributed to internal flooding of properties on Watermead Lane. (p3)
* SES Water owned drainage assets on Watermead Lane where two broken flap valves at the outfall by the River Wandle were identified as faulty; they replaced the two broken flap valves after the flood and will carry out routine maintenance and an investigation of the outfall alignment. (p35) [PAG]
* Carried out repairs and maintenance to drainage assets on Watermead Lane following the June 2019 flooding; confirmed replacement of two defective flap valves and committed to routine maintenance. (p38)
* Quant: 8 properties (p3)

### Thames Water
* Defective flap valves and connections in the Thames Water sewer network are identified as allowing river water to flow through sewer pipes and onto the highway, contributing to the flooding that occurred on 10th June 2019; sewer outfalls and manholes are mapped in the area (Figure 3.6). (p25) [IMG, PAG]
* Defective flap valves in the sewer network allowed water from the river to flow back through sewer pipes onto the highway, contributing to flooding of the highway (Watermead Lane area) during the event. (p26) [PAG, IMG]

### Environment Agency
* {10 Jun 2019, 12 Jun 2019}: EA incident response staff checked and cleared blockages on the River Wandle during the event and the EA visited Watermead Lane in the two days following the incident to speak to affected parties. (p32) [TAB, PAG, MUL]
* Collaborated with Sutton to analyse River Wandle gauged water level data to better determine the flood event that led to the June 2019 flooding affecting Watermead Lane and to align roles and responsibilities in the follow-up. (p38)

### Watermead Lane Catchment
* Experienced extreme flooding on 10 June 2019 along Watermead Lane; flooding was greater than expected for a 1-in-5-year rainfall and was likely worsened by two defective flap valves on outfalls into the River Wandle, contributing significantly to the severe inundation of the lane. (p30)

### Highway Authority
* Flooding affected residents on Watermead Lane; drainage assets on or serving the highway were implicated by residents as not being properly maintained, contributing to local surface water inundation. (p33)

### Sutton Council
* Visited the site, published a hydrological review of the June 2019 event, and liaised with stakeholders (including EA and SES Water) to determine causes and coordinate follow-up actions (surveys, mapping of surface water sewers, property-level protection and investigation of flood-defence and SuDS options). (p38) [PAG]
