

# Chalfont St Peter flooding 24 Sep 2019

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: High Street, Chalfont St Peter 2019<br>018\_Buckinghamshire County/018\_2019\_Buckinghamshire County/s19-invesitgation-report\_chalfont\_2019\_PE6AWeg.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### High Street, Chalfont St Peter
* Internal flooding of business premises and carriageway closure: five business properties on High Street experienced internal flooding; the road was closed by the local highway authority for the duration of the event. (p1) [PAG]
* Road experienced surface water/urban flooding that caused the carriageway to flood and be closed by the local Highway Authority for the duration of the event. The location was the only road affected due to a natural low point in the carriageway profile. (p2)
* A foul manhole in the middle of the carriageway surcharged and discharged a large amount of water onto the highway, overwhelming highway drainage, causing ponding in the road and rising water levels that encroached into and flooded shops along the High Street. (p4) [PAG]
* Heavy rainfall and surface water runoff entered the highway drainage and foul sewer system, overwhelming the foul sewer. Sewage discharged from a manhole in the middle of the carriageway and flowed into the highway drainage, causing backup, ponding of water in the road and rising water levels that encroached into shops along the High Street. (p5) [PAG]
* Foul sewer became overwhelmed during heavy rainfall and discharged sewage from a manhole in the middle of the carriageway into the highway drainage system. This caused drainage backup, ponding in the road and rising water levels which encroached into shops along High Street; Thames Water attended and pumped flood water away. (p6) [PAG, IMG]
* Quant: 5 business properties; 18 inches (maximum flood depth) (p1) [PAG]
* Quant: 5 business properties; 18 inches (p2)

### Buckinghamshire
* Internal flooding of business properties on High Street, Chalfont St Peter caused by a Thames Water foul sewer network issue; High Street was closed by the highway authority for the duration of the event. (p1) [IMG]
* Surface-water flooding occurred in the area shown on Figure 11 on the day of the event; the surface water drainage system was overwhelmed by water and debris discharging from the sewer system and could not drain away the excess. The drainage system was cleaned after the flooding. (p7) [PAG]
* Quant: 5 business properties; 18 inches (maximum depth); 1 road closed (p1) [IMG]

### Chalfont St Peter
* Heavy rainfall led to surface runoff entering the highway drainage and foul sewer, which became overwhelmed; sewage discharged from a manhole and floodwater ponded on the road and encroached into shops/properties on the High Street in Chalfont St Peter. (p4) [IMG, PAG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Thames Water
* A fault in Thames Water's foul sewer network caused the flood event on High Street, resulting in internal flooding to businesses and road closure. (p1)
* Attended on the morning of 24 September 2019 and pumped flood water away from the carriageway/highway drainage after a foul sewer manhole surcharged and discharged water onto the road. (p4)
* A sewer issue discharged water and debris that enhanced the surface-water flooding; Thames Water investigated a potential Flooding Local Improvement Project (which failed the initial engineering assessment) and will continue to monitor and carry out routine maintenance and respond if severe weather causes further flooding. (p7)
* Quant: 5 business properties (p1)

### Transport for Buckinghamshire
* The highway (surface water) drainage became overwhelmed by the volume of water and debris discharging from the sewer system and could not drain away the excess; Transport for Buckinghamshire is responsible for the highway drainage and the system was cleaned after the flooding event. (p7)
