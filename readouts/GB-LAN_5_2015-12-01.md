

# Fylde flooding December 2015

Reference document:<br>Fylde District Flood Report Recommended Actions<br>069\_Lancashire County/fylde-final-report.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Lancashire
* {01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015}: Significant flooding in December 2015 affected communities across Lancashire, exposing deficiencies in communication and coordination and prompting the Lead Local Flood Authority to develop district-level reports and improve RMA communication. (p3) [PAG]
* {01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015}: The December 2015 flood event produced the highest river flows on record and caused widespread impacts across the county — people, homes and businesses were heavily affected and recovery actions were required. (p8) [TAB]
* {01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015}: Extreme storms and rainfall in November–December 2015 caused flooding throughout December across Lancashire, damaging private gardens, garages and out-buildings, business premises, agricultural property and critical public infrastructure including roads, bridges and retaining walls, water treatment plants, power and communications installations, and community buildings such as schools and village/town halls. (p9)
* {01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015}: Multiple communities across the county experienced flooding as a result of two major storm events in December 2015; these communities were identified and included within the countywide investigation. (p10) [PAG]
* {01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015}: Quant: over 2,500 properties flooded in Lancashire (p8) [TAB]
* {01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015}: Quant: over 2,500 properties (p9)

## Topics without coordinates

### Lancashire County Council
* {01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015}: As the Lead Local Flood Authority and county council, it recorded and verified data from the two major storm events in December 2015, established a flood timeline, initiated and published a countywide Section 19 investigation (stage one) covering communities affected by the events, and is managing stage two follow-up actions and adaptations to multi‑agency flood response and communications. (p10) [PAG]
* {December 2015, 01 Dec 2015 - 31 Jan 2016}: LCC records indicate external flooding occurred (six occasions between December 2015 and January 2016) and LCC/its highways team undertook response works (gully cleansing and highway maintenance) following the December 2015 flood event. (p13) [TAB, MUL]
* LCC Highways carried out gully cleansing and repaired/reinstated a highway culvert on Ribby Road following the flood. Further gullies and connections were to be inspected and cleaned; carriageway resurfacing planned once works complete (planned March 2017). (p14) [TAB]
* {December 2015, 01 Dec 2015 - 31 Jan 2016}: Quant: 6 external flooding incidents; 1 commercial property (p13) [TAB, MUL]

### Lancashire Resilience Forum
* {01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015}: Acted as the multi-agency coordinator for the December 2015 flood response, directing risk management authorities to provide practical support to emergency services and communities (helping contact vulnerable people, assisting evacuations, closing roads/bridges, setting diversions, clearing blocked drains). (p9)

### National Health Service
* {01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015}: Was a recipient of multi-agency support during the December 2015 floods (assisted by risk management authorities under LRF direction to help contact vulnerable people and ensure safety, including evacuations). (p9)

### Environment Agency
* {01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015}: Carried out pumping activities in critical areas to manage floodwaters (explicitly in St Michaels, Rufford and Croston) as part of the December 2015 response. (p9)

### LCC Highways
* {general}: Carried out gully cleansing and highway maintenance to address drainage/highway issues following the December 2015 flooding. (p13) [TAB]
