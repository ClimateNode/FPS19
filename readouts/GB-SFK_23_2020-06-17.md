## Topics without coordinates

### Hollow Road Farm
* An intense, localised storm on 17 June 2020 caused ponding on the private front porch and adjacent pavement; water ponded against the property and seeped through porous/old brickwork and paths into lower living areas, causing internal flooding of the cellar and kitchen and external flooding to the porch/pavement. The footpath and porch are cambered towards the property and lack concrete grips found at neighbouring properties, promoting ponding and seepage into the building fabric. A previous flooding event at the same property occurred in October 2019. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: internal flooding in 1 property; flood depth <0.3m (p3) [IMG]

### Anglian Water
* Inspected the property after an intense localised storm on 17th June 2020 that caused ponding on the porch and seepage into lower living areas; checked the stop tap and private drainage for leaks and reported none. (p4)

### Suffolk County Council
* Responded to the surface water flooding on 17th June 2020 by advising affected residents of their rights and recommending property-level resilience measures (e.g. lining the front porch, raising the porch). Identified and recommended highway/footway interventions including investigating footway camber and installing footpath drainage such as a dished channel or ACO MultiDrain. (p4) [TAB]
