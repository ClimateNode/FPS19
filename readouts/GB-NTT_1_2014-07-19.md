

# Arnold flooding 19 Jul 2014

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Arnold (Front Street) 19 July 2014<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/arnold-section-19-report.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Nottinghamshire
* Parts of the county were subjected to intense rainfall on 19 July 2014 and experienced major flooding; this included Arnold where Front Street saw internal flooding of business premises. (p1)
* Quant: at least 5 properties (p1)

### Arnold
* Flooding in Arnold centred on Front Street; a number of business premises experienced internal flooding during the event. (p1) [PAG]
* Front Street, a main commercial street in Arnold, experienced surface water flooding when intense rainfall produced runoff from the surrounding catchment that overwhelmed drainage. (p2)
* Quant: at least 5 properties (p1) [PAG]

### Front Street, Arnold
* Front Street, a main commercial street at the bottom of a large urbanised catchment, experienced surface water flooding that caused internal flooding of business premises. (p1) [PAG]
* Surface water runoff from the surrounding urban catchment flowed onto Front Street; the existing drainage system was overwhelmed by intense rainfall, causing flooding of the main commercial street at the bottom of the catchment. (p2) [IMG]
* Front Street was the focus of flooding-related activity: additional clearance of highway gullies was carried out, a localised study of surface water assets was commissioned, and the public sewerage network serving Front Street has previously had flood-alleviation works, indicating the street was impacted by floodwater. (p3)
* Quant: at least 5 properties (p1) [PAG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Severn Trent
* STW (Severn Trent) actively engaged in the S19 investigation and led work on investigating surface water assets; the company has historically invested in flood alleviation for the public sewer network serving Front Street, indicating involvement in managing sewer-related flooding impacts. (p3)

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Surface-water flooding affected Front Street (highway gullies), prompting NCC to facilitate additional clearance of highway gullies and to secure funding to investigate the wider Daybrook catchment surface-water assets to understand asset performance and interactions. (p3)

### Highway Authority
* Highway gullies on Front Street required additional clearance in response to flooding; highway maintenance activity was facilitated to help manage floodwater on the public highway. (p3)

### Day Brook Catchment
* The wider Daybrook catchment's surface water assets were investigated because of their performance and interaction during the flood; this catchment is implicated in the surface water flooding that affected Front Street and surrounding areas. (p3) [PAG]

### Gedling Borough Council
* Surface-water flooding on Front Street led Gedling Borough Council to coordinate emergency planning, provide the centre point of contact for customer liaison, instigate a Flood Forum group and commission a localised study of surface-water assets on Front Street. (p3)
