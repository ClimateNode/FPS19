### Ide
* The village of Ide was the worst affected location in the county during the event, with internal flooding of properties. (p1)
* Surface water flooding affected the settlement, resulting in flooded properties. (p3) [TAB]
* Intense rainfall and surface water runoff caused flood water to travel from surrounding fields, overtopping garden walls and travelling through gardens and houses. Six properties were internally flooded (three residential and three commercial), including a local pub and the village hall; water levels reported at about 2 feet high in parts. (p5) [PAG]
* {07 Dec 2000, 30 May 2008, 30 Dec 2013, 16 Oct 2014, 02 Nov 2023 - 03 Nov 2023}: Known flow paths and approximate flooded locations were recorded in Ide during the main event; Ide has a history of repeated flooding from surface water and from the Alphin Brook watercourse, with properties flooded on multiple past occasions. (p6) [MUL]
* Inefficient conveyance of highway surface water and existing drainage arrangements contributed to large overland flows across fields around Ide; recommended actions target improving highway-to-watercourse conveyance and assessing drainage to reduce exacerbation of overland flows onto nearby land. (p10) [TAB]

### Devon
* Significant flooding occurred in isolated parts of the county: watercourses overtopped and drainage systems were overwhelmed by surface water runoff, resulting in internal flooding of properties across Devon. (p1) [PAG]
* County-wide event from Storm Ciarán (2–3 Nov 2023). Watercourses were overtopped and drainage systems overwhelmed by surface water run-off. Across Devon, 20 properties were internally flooded (13 residential and 7 commercial); the village of Ide (Teignbridge) was the worst affected location with 6 properties internally flooded. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* County-wide heavy rainfall and flooding on 2 November caused flooded roads and resulted in a total of 20 properties flooded across the listed towns and villages. (p3) [TAB, PAG]
* Quant: 20 properties (13 residential and 7 commercial) (p1) [PAG]
* Quant: 20 properties; 13 residential; 7 commercial; 6 properties (Ide) (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 20 properties (p3) [TAB, PAG]
