

# Trowbridge flooding 18 Sep 2014 - 19 Sep 2014

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Hill Street/Kitchener’s Place Trowbridge"<br>145\_Wiltshire/145\_2014\_Wiltshire/Section-19-hill-street-trowbridge.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Hill Street Court
* Flows collected in a sunken walkway from the carpark and access way, overwhelming soakaways; water collected to a depth of approximately 250 mm and worked through a wall into the bedroom/living room of the adjacent property. (p1)
* The courtyard of Hill St Court is listed within the extent of flooding. The management company cleared channels feeding the soakaways (which residents reported were in poor condition before the flood), indicating soakaway failure/contribution to local flooding of the courtyard. (p3) [PAG]
* Courtyard area at Hill St Court was flooded during the extreme event; channels feeding the soakaways were reported in poor condition prior to the flooding and have since been cleared by the building's management company. Highway drainage may have been surcharged due to silt build-up, contributing to local flooding; affected householders have been offered property-level protection. (p4)
* Quant: approx. 250 mm; 1 property (p1)
* Quant: 80+ properties; around 20+ businesses; 2+ schools (West Wilts event) (p3) [PAG]

### Trowbridge
* Pluvial runoff from an intense rainfall event caused the drainage system to surcharge in the Hill Street area of Trowbridge, producing overland flows into courtyards (Kitchener's Place, Hill Street Court) and into properties; at Hill Street Court water collected to about 250 mm and penetrated an adjacent property. (p1)
* Quant: approx. 250 mm; 1 property (p1)

### Hill Street, Trowbridge
* Intense rainfall caused the drainage system to surcharge, producing overland flow in the Hill Street area that entered local courtyards and flowed into properties. (p1)
* Hill Street and its highway drainage were implicated in the event: gullies on Hill St were connected into the foul (Wessex Water) culvert and were proven with dye; the highway courtyard area around Hill St was within the extent of flooding. Possible silt build-up in drains and blocked soakaway channels contributed to surface water exceeding highway/drainage capacity. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Highway drainage in the Hill St area surcharged during the event (silt build-up may have contributed), causing surface water/urban flooding in the local area including courtyards and properties associated with Hill St Court; jetting and CCTV surveying were carried out on the highway drainage afterwards. (p4) [PAG]
* Quant: 80+ properties; around 20+ businesses; 2+ schools (West Wilts event) (p3) [IMG, PAG]

### Kitcheners Court
* The courtyard of Kitchener's Place / Kitchener's Ct is explicitly listed in the 'Extent of flooding' and therefore was inundated by the event. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 80+ properties; around 20+ businesses; 2+ schools (West Wilts event) (p3) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### West Wiltshire
* The 18-19 September 2014 extreme flood event affected the West Wilts area, flooding properties, businesses and schools and impacting many communities across the district. (p4)
* Quant: 80+ properties; around 20+ businesses; 2+ schools (p4)

### Kitchener's Place
* Overland flow from intense rainfall and a surcharged drainage system entered the courtyards of Kitchener's Place, causing floodwater to enter properties. (p1)
* Courtyard of Kitchener's Place is listed in the extent of flooding heading, indicating it was flooded during the event; soakaway feed channels nearby were reported in poor condition prior to the flood and have been cleared by the management company. (p4)

### Town Bridge
* High water flows nearer Town Bridge have caused flooding in this general area. The extreme event on 18–19 September 2014 affected this area as part of a wider West Wilts flood that inundated properties and businesses. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* High water flows near the Town Bridge have previously caused flooding in the general area; the extreme event on 18-19 September 2014 produced greater flooding than seen before in this locality. (p4)
* Quant: 80+ properties; 20+ businesses; 2+ schools (across the West Wilts area) (p3) [IMG, PAG]
