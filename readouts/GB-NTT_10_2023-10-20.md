

# Bilsthorpe flooding 20 Oct 2023

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Bilsthorpe – Storm Babet, October 2023<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/bilsthorpe-s19-storm-babet-oct-2023.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Bilsthorpe
* Sustained heavy rainfall during Storm Babet caused surface water/urban flooding in the village—roads flooded and properties inundated internally. (p1)
* Localized residential flooding within the town: internal flooding of 13 residential properties on Mickledale Lane and 8 residential properties on Mickledale Close; 2 properties affected on Kirklington Road. (p2) [IMG]
* Rainworth Water (ordinary watercourse) has a large catchment at this location and, during heavy rainfall associated with Storm Babet, high flows caused the watercourse to burst its banks and flow into nearby properties adjacent to Mickledale Lane and Mickledale Close. The area is also shown as being at risk of surface water flooding on the Environment Agency surface water map. (p4) [IMG]
* Community was affected by flooding during Storm Babet; prompted development of a community flood signage scheme and support to develop a local flood action group, and consideration of property flood resilience measures. (p7)
* Quant: 23 residential properties (p1)
* Quant: 13 residential properties; 8 residential properties; 2 properties (p2) [IMG]

### Mickledale Lane, Bilsthorpe
* Internal flooding of residential properties along the street. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Rainworth Water (an ordinary watercourse) experienced high flows during Storm Babet, burst its banks and flowed into properties on Mickledale Lane causing internal flooding of residential properties. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Rainworth Water (ordinary watercourse) has a large catchment at this location; heavy rainfall during Storm Babet generated high flows, the watercourse burst its banks and floodwater (and surface water) flowed adjacent to Mickledale Lane and into nearby properties. (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 13 residential properties (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 13 residential properties (p3) [IMG, PAG]

### Mickledale Close, Bilsthorpe
* Internal flooding of residential properties along the street. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* High flows in Rainworth Water during Storm Babet caused the watercourse to burst its banks and flow into properties on Mickledale Close, resulting in internal flooding. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Area is shown at medium–high surface water flood risk; heavy rainfall during Storm Babet produced high flows in Rainworth Water which burst its banks and resulted in floodwater entering properties in the Mickledale Close area. (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 8 residential properties (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 8 residential properties (p3) [IMG, PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* County-wide flooding response: delivered sandbags, supported community flood signage schemes, published collated flood impact data, provided 24-hour emergency planning support, initiated and led the Section 19 flood investigation, and closed roads to reduce flood risk. (p6) [PAG]
* Quant: 77 roads (p6) [PAG]

### Newark and Sherwood
* District experienced surface water flooding which then carried a continued risk of river flooding (River Trent); staff visited impacted communities and vulnerable individuals, helped evacuations, delivered aqua sacs, provided emergency accommodation, coordinated in-person visits to high-risk communities, and commenced cleanup and bulky-waste removal as recovery actions. (p6)

### Kirklington Road, Kirklington
* Properties on the road were impacted by flooding (map annotation indicates affected properties). (p2) [IMG]
* Intense rainfall during Storm Babet exceeded highway drainage capacity; water pooled in a natural low spot on the road and then flowed into and flooded adjacent residential properties. (p4) [IMG]
* Quant: 2 properties (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 2 residential properties (p4) [IMG]

### River Trent
* The River Trent reached predicted extreme/high levels and presented a continued risk of river flooding, prompting the council to coordinate in-person visits to high-risk communities and take preparatory/response actions. (p6)
