### Devonshire Drive, Eastwood
* Large flows were observed running down Devanshire Drive from a combination of the brook overtopping and adjacent agricultural land runoff; these flows contributed to downstream impacts on nearby streets. (p4)

### Lower Beauvale, Eastwood
* {general}: The area of Lower Beauvale is mapped with concentrated surface-water flood risk along Beauvale Brook and tributaries; the highest risk shading follows the brook route indicating the settlement is within zones susceptible to surface-water flooding and to impacts from brook overtopping (which the report states caused flooding to rear of properties). (p4) [IMG]

### Beauvale Brook
* Beauvale Brook (ordinary watercourse) runs behind properties on Robey Drive, Fryar Road and Nether Close; its proximity to those streets is noted in the report and it is identified as a potential contributing factor to the internal flooding of adjacent properties during the event. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* The brook overtopped its banks causing flooding to the rear of several properties; flows from the brook (combined with runoff and upstream discharges from Moorgreen Reservoir/adjacent pond) exacerbated flooding on nearby streets, notably Coach Drive and Robey Drive. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* The brook overtopped its banks causing flooding to the rear of multiple properties; it received discharges from the upstream pond/reservoir and was affected by agricultural runoff. Localised minor blockages, vegetation and protruding debris in sections exacerbated the rise in water at pinch points. (p4) [IMG]
* Quant: Robey Drive (41 properties), Fryar Road (14 properties), Nether Close (6 properties); total internal flooding in Eastwood 76 properties (p2) [IMG, PAG]

### Moorgreen Reservoir
* Large volumes of water were discharged from the reservoir into the adjacent pond, which then discharged into Beauvale Brook; video evidence shows overflow weirs discharging large amounts, contributing to downstream flooding. (p4)


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* As Lead Local Flood Authority, responded to flooding across Nottinghamshire: provided 24-hour emergency planning support; delivered sandbags to affected areas and residents; closed roads to promote road safety and avoid additional flood damage; initiated and led the Section 19 Flood Investigation and liaison with RMAs, parish councils, community groups, affected residents and businesses; led procurement and delivery of the DEFRA Property Flood Resilience Repair Grant Scheme; shared resources with district and borough partners and managed recovery actions. (p6)

### Broxtowe Borough Council
* Provided emergency response and recovery in Broxtowe: delivered and distributed sandbags to affected residents, managed recovery in affected communities, administered the flood hardship fund and relief grant schemes, and actively engaged in the Section 19 Flood Investigation. (p6)
