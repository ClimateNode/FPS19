* High flows on the River Wey prompted a flood warning for the River Wey at Byfleet; the EA issued a flood warning and deployed ambassadors during the event and subsequently adjusted the flood warning trigger level and began an assessment for larger flood defences. (p23)

### Broad Ditch

* Blocked channel, low spots and build-up of debris reduced conveyance capacity; WBC and the EA are investigating clearance, bank repairs and maintenance (EA maintenance plan 2015/16) to improve conveyance and reduce future flooding. (p26)
* The Broad Ditch (classified as a Main River) was identified as a feature requiring management after the flood incident: EA and WBC are investigating coordinated actions with riparian owners to address low bank spots and debris build-up in the channel; the EA completed an initial assessment of a larger flood defence scheme for the Byfleet/Weybridge residential area.
