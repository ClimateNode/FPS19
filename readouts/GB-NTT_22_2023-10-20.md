

# East Markham flooding 20 Oct 2023

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – East Markham – Storm Babet, October 2023<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/east-markham-s19-storm-babet-oct-2023.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### East Markham
* Sustained heavy rainfall during Storm Babet (20 October 2023) on saturated ground resulted in flooding of roads and properties in the village. (p1)
* Surface water from an overwhelmed culvert ran overland across fields and onto roads, causing internal flooding to properties on York Street and York Paddock and flooding to gardens and outbuildings within the village. (p3) [IMG]
* An unnamed ordinary watercourse and its culvert (approximately 90 m west of York Street) was overwhelmed causing overland flow across a field onto York Street and York Paddock. Highway flooding exacerbated the flow. Properties lower than the road had water run down their gardens and into buildings. Four residential properties were internally flooded on York Street and two residential properties were flooded on York Paddock; many additional gardens and outbuildings were also flooded. (p4) [IMG]
* Quant: 4 residential properties (York Street); 2 residential properties (York Paddock) (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 4 residential properties (York Street); 2 residential properties (York Paddock) (p4) [IMG]

### Nottinghamshire
* Significant flood impacts across the county: internal flooding of properties verified on site, county-wide emergency response actions (delivery of sandbags, 24-hour emergency planning support) and closure of roads to reduce risk and avoid additional flood damage. (p5)
* Quant: 77 roads (p5)

### York Street, East Markham
* An upstream culvert was overwhelmed, allowing water to run overland across a field onto York Street; existing highway flooding exacerbated flows which ran down gardens and into properties, causing internal flooding. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 4 residential properties internally flooded (p3) [IMG]

### York Paddock, East Markham
* Topography meant properties lower than the road had water running down their gardens from overland flows following a culvert being overwhelmed, resulting in internal flooding to some properties and flooding of gardens/outbuildings. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 2 residential properties internally flooded (p3) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Verified and recorded internal flooding at properties; visited flood-affected residents and businesses to establish sources and severity; delivered sandbags to affected areas and communities; closed roads to promote road safety and avoid additional flood damage. (p5)
* As Lead Local Flood Authority, Nottinghamshire County Council experienced/observed the impacts of flooding from Storm Babet on communities; it is undertaking investigations, will review the Property Flood Resilience programme for affected communities, and will work with partners on riparian responsibilities and asset inspection processes. (p6)
* Quant: 77 roads (p5)

### Bassetlaw District Council
* Managed local flooding response in the district: deployed sandbags and aquasacs to vulnerable premises and residents, provided community resilience stores for access to sandbags/aquasacs and supplied temporary accommodation to affected residents. (p5) [PAG]
* Bassetlaw District Council's area was impacted by flooding from Storm Babet; the council supported affected residents (temporary accommodation, skips/caged vans/staffing for disposal of flood-damaged property), cleared detritus from highways and car parks, rehoused affected council tenants and undertook repairs to council housing, and participated in incident review and recovery activity. (p6)
