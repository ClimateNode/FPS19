### Low Street, Hoxne
* Internal flooding of residential and commercial properties on Low Street during Storm Babet; flooding included a fluvial element caused by local watercourses (Gold Brook/Chickering Beck) exceeding capacity and overtopping banks, combined with surface water run‑off. (p7)
* Listed as one of the eight investigation locations identified as being affected by flooding during the 18 Oct 2023–21 Oct 2023 event. (p9)
* {general}: Previous reports of full manholes with some external flooding on Low Street (indicative of sewer surcharge/blockage-related flooding). (p10)
* Low Street was identified as an area at high risk of fluvial flooding and was affected by flooding during Storm Babet. (p11)
* {18 Oct 2023 - 21 Oct 2023, 01 Nov 2023 - 02 Nov 2023}: Excess surface water flowed down the gradient from the north end of Low Street towards the Swan bridge area. Surface water drainage was inhibited by clogged gullies; a manhole in front of the Swan pub was surcharging from 09:30 on 20 October. This surcharging merged with surface water, pooling across the highway to the pub, Swan Bridge and along Sandpit Lane. Floodwater from the overtopping Gold Brook and Chickering Beck backed up at Swan Bridge, flowed across gardens and into properties. Multiple properties suffered internal flooding (depths reported), the area was submerged by mid-afternoon and highway drainage could not discharge to the overfull watercourse. Floodwater began to recede overnight and Low Street was clear by the morning of 21 October. Some properties flooded again during Storm Ciaran (1 - 2 November 2023). (p16) [PAG, MUL]
* {18 Oct 2023 - 21 Oct 2023, 01 Nov 2023 - 02 Nov 2023}: Intense, prolonged rainfall caused excess surface water to flow down Low Street from the north, merging with surcharging water from a manhole (surcharging observed from 09:30 on 20 Oct). Water pooled around the Swan pub and bridge area, flowed across gardens and into properties causing internal flooding (depths reported), and the highway drainage was overwhelmed and could not discharge into the overfull watercourse. Flood levels on Low Street peaked around 20:30 on 20 Oct and electricity failed on Low Street at 21:15. Floodwater receded by the morning of 21 Oct. Some properties on Low Street were reported to have flooded internally again during Storm Ciaran (1–2 Nov 2023). (p17) [PAG, IMG, MUL]
* Identified in LLFA recommendations for investigation of highway drainage capacity and Anglian Water assets near the Swan pub; residents and officers asked to report observed blockages below Swan bridge, indicating drainage failure or blockages were associated with the flooding response in the area. (p18) [IMG, PAG]
* The residential street was inundated with muddy floodwater extending along its length, reaching the entrances of multiple houses and covering public street furniture, affecting access and properties. (p23) [IMG]
