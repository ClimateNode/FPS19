

# Selston flooding 01 Oct 2023 - 31 Oct 2023

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Selston – Storm Babet, October 2023<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/selston-s19-storm-babet-oct-2023.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Nottinghamshire
* A Major Incident with significant impacts occurred across the county associated with the flooding response; surface water (overland) flows are described as the localised flood mechanism in the area. (p3) [PAG]
* Significant flooding across the county that caused internal flooding of properties and widespread disruption; roads were closed to promote safety and avoid additional flood damage. (p4) [PAG]
* Flooding during Storm Babet affected communities in Nottinghamshire; the Lead Local Flood Authority (Nottinghamshire County Council) is prioritising the most vulnerable, reviewing Property Flood Resilience for affected communities, and planning enforcement/inspection improvements for riparian assets. (p5)
* Quant: 77 roads (p4) [PAG]

### Ashfield
* Flooding of domestic properties within the district required deployment of sandbags and the opening of a rest centre for evacuees. (p4)
* Ashfield District undertook an internal Storm Babet debrief and proposed flood-related actions in response to the event, including a new Flood Advice webpage, Rest Centre review, production of a Flood Action Card for the Major Emergency Plan, business continuity exercises, reviewing internal sandbag stocks, and working with LLFA partners on flood attenuation/prevention. (p5)
* Quant: 1 rest centre (p4)

### Selston
* During Storm Babet Selston experienced flooding that resulted in internal flooding of 4 residential properties and 1 business; additional flooding affected gardens and outbuildings and roads. (p1)
* Localised flooding caused by overland surface water flows: surface water falling on an already saturated catchment flowed overland resulting in localised flooding in Selston. (p3)
* Quant: 4 residential properties; 1 business (p1)

### Hucknall
* Domestic properties in Hucknall were severely flooded, resulting in evacuees being accommodated at a rest centre. (p4)


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Coordinated and delivered response and short-term recovery actions for flooding across the county: verified internal flooding at properties and businesses, delivered sandbags to affected areas, collated and published verified flood impact data, provided 24-hour emergency planning support, initiated a Community Flood Recovery Grant and led the Section 19 Flood Investigation and DEFRA Property Flood Resilience Repair Grant procurement/delivery. Closed roads to promote safety during the flood response. (p4)
* As the Lead Local Flood Authority, responded to communities affected by Storm Babet and committed to continue investigations; Flood Risk Management team will review suitability of the Property Flood Resilience Programme for communities affected by Storm Babet, take recommendations to the Cabinet Member, and work with partners to strengthen enforcement of riparian responsibilities and develop asset inspection processes. (p5)
* Quant: 77 roads closed (p4)

### Ashfield District Council
* Operational emergency response: stood up an internal incident management team; prioritised attendance to flooded domestic properties; deployed sandbags to prevent internal flooding; opened a rest centre for evacuees; provided flood advice communications; held debriefs. (p4)
* Held an internal Storm Babet debrief and identified learning outcomes and actions in response to flooding; proposed actions include a new Flood Advice web page, Rest Centre review with NCC, production of a Flood Action Card for the ADC Major Emergency Plan, business continuity test/exercise(s), roll‑out of LRF 'Concept of Operations' training for ADC executives, review of internal sandbag stocks, and working with LLFA partners on future flood attenuation/prevention works. (p5)
* Quant: 1 rest centre (p4)
