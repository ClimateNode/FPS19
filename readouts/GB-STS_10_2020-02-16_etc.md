### Staffordshire
* {15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020}: Widespread flooding across Staffordshire during the Storm Dennis event on the weekend of 15–16 February 2020: waterlogged gardens, roads rendered impassable, highways inundated, and multiple watercourses breaching their banks causing internal property flooding. (p7)
* {16 Jun 2020}: Widespread flooding on 16 June 2020: waterlogged gardens; roads made impassable; water outflowing from highway gullies to watercourses which breached banks; manholes and sewers surcharged; internal property flooding. (p10)
* {16 Aug 2020 - 17 Aug 2020}: Widespread flooding on 16–17 August 2020 causing waterlogged gardens, impassable roads, water outflowing from highway gullies, watercourses breaching banks, manholes and sewers surcharging, and internal property flooding. (p11)

### Staffordshire
* {16 Feb 2020, 16 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020, 17 Aug 2020}: Storms on 16 Feb, 16 Jun and 16–17 Aug 2020 caused widespread flooding to highways and properties across Staffordshire. (p5) [MUL]

### Lichfield
* {16 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020 - 17 Aug 2020}: Local rain gauges recorded short, intense, highly localised rainfall on 16 June 2020 and during 16–17 August 2020; these intense local storms contributed to localised surface water/flash flooding in the Lichfield area and across Lichfield District. (p11) [PAG, MUL]
* {16 Feb 2020}: At least one internally flooded property in central Lichfield is identified on the Section 19 map for 2020 (the map shows a central star), associated with the February 2020 incidents. (p20) [IMG, TAB, PAG]
* {16 Feb 2020}: Parts of Lichfield (area south/east of Watery Lane) experienced inundation of roads and land due to overtopping of Stych Brook and overwhelmed drainage/culvert capacity; surface water and excess brook flow were driven toward a housing estate but did not penetrate properties internally. (p26)
* {16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 0 properties flooded internally (p26)

### Longdon
* {16 Feb 2020}: Portion of Longdon (specifically Brook End) experienced property internal flooding during February 2020; flow analysis for Brook End indicates ~1-in-2 to 1-in-5-year return period. (p20) [TAB]
* {16 Feb 2020}: The Brook End area of Longdon experienced internal property flooding caused by surcharging of the Shropshire Brook and subsequent breach/topple of drainage features upstream. (p23) [IMG]

### Longdon Green
* {16 Feb 2020}: Areas of Longdon Green (including Church Hill) experienced internal property flooding in February 2020; rainfall-based return period noted as >1-in-1-year. (p20) [TAB]
* {14 Jun 2020}: Multiple properties experienced flooding (some internal) and surrounding roads, driveways and gardens were flooded. Flooding was attributed to surface water runoff and a possible breach of the local brook. (p24) [IMG]

### Watery Lane, Curborough
* {16 Feb 2020}: Internally flooded property/locations on Watery Lane identified in the February 2020 Section 19 investigation. (p20) [TAB, PAG]
* {16 Feb 2020}: Roads to the south and east of Watery Lane and adjacent land were inundated when Stych Brook breached its banks and the culvert under the road overtopped. Existing drainage and the culvert were unable to convey excess flow, so surface water together with excess brook flow inundated surrounding highways and was forced toward nearby properties; no properties were flooded internally. (p26)
* {16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 0 properties flooded internally (p26)

### Old Road, Lichfield
* {16 Feb 2020}: Internal flooding of properties at Old Road (internally flooded properties recorded). Rainfall-based analysis indicates a return period >1-in-1-year (frequent occurrence). (p20) [TAB]
* {16 Feb 2020}: Surface water from the highway could not reach the watercourse because obstructions and blockages caused runoff to pond and enter properties. Urban debris caused a blockage under a bridge during the storm; when the blockage was removed water began to recede. (p22)

### Brook End, Lichfield
* {16 Feb 2020}: Flooding at Brook End (Longdon) resulting in internally flooded properties; watercourse flow analysis indicates an approximate return period between 1-in-2 and 1-in-5 years. (p20) [TAB]
* {16 Feb 2020}: Multiple properties at Brook End were flooded internally after surcharging of the Shropshire Brook upstream caused a weir and drainage features to be breached and toppled; excess flows across the highway entered properties. (p23) [IMG]

### Eastern Avenue, Lichfield
* {16 Feb 2020}: Road carriageway inundated when Stych Brook breached its banks and the culvert under the road overtopped; surface water runoff from adjacent fields added to flows. Existing drainage was unable to convey the storm flows and water was forced toward nearby properties, though no properties were flooded internally. (p26) [IMG]
* {16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 0 properties flooded internally (p26) [IMG]

### Kilby Avenue, Lichfield
* {16 Feb 2020}: Highway inundation from combined surface water runoff and excess flow from Stych Brook after the culvert overtopped; drainage systems could not cope and water was driven toward the housing estate adjacent to Kilby Avenue. No internal property flooding was reported. (p26) [IMG]
* {16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 0 properties flooded internally (p26) [IMG]

### Church Hill, Longdon
* {16 Feb 2020}: Internal property flooding at Church Hill (Longdon Green) recorded in February 2020; rainfall-based analysis gives a return period >1-in-1-year. (p20) [TAB]

### Chorley Road, Burntwood
* {16 Jun 2020}: Flow paths from multiple directions converged at a central point on Chorley Road so surface water flowed along the road; this contributed to flooding of roads, driveways, gardens and to internal flooding of nearby properties in Longdon Green. (p24) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Shropshire Brook
* {16 Feb 2020}: Surcharging of the Shropshire Brook upstream of the affected properties propagated downstream, causing a weir and other drainage features to be breached/toppled and leading to internal flooding of properties. (p23)
* {16 Feb 2020}: The brook experienced a significant high-flow event during Storm Dennis: an observed peak total flow of 1.37 m3/s was recorded, exceeding the 1-in-2 year design flow (1.17 m3/s) and lying between the 1-in-2 and 1-in-5 year design flows (approx. 1.53 m3/s). (p32) [PAG]
* {16 Feb 2020}: A significant flow event was recorded on the Shropshire Brook during Storm Dennis: an observed peak flow of 1.37 m3/s, exceeding the 1-in-2-year design flow (1.17 m3/s) but below the 1-in-5-year design flow (1.53 m3/s). The event was therefore assessed as having a return period between 1 in 2 and 1 in 5 years. (p33) [PAG, IMG]
* {16 Feb 2020}: Quant: peak flow 1.37 m3/s; 1-in-2 year design flow 1.17 m3/s; 1-in-5 year design flow ~1.53 m3/s; event probability 20PCSYM–50PCSYM (p32) [PAG]
* {16 Feb 2020}: Quant: peak flow of 1.37 m3/s; 1 in 2 year design flow 1.17 m3/s; 1 in 5 year design flow 1.53 m3/s; estimated probability 20PCSYM–50PCSYM (p33) [PAG, IMG]

### Staffordshire County Council
* {16 Feb 2020, 16 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020 - 17 Aug 2020}: As Lead Local Flood Authority, the council was made aware of multiple flooding incidents, distributed flood questionnaires to residents, and undertook investigations to determine causes of internal property flooding and appropriate actions. (p4) [MUL]
* {16 Feb 2020, 16 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020, 17 Aug 2020}: The widespread flooding from the 2020 storms prompted Staffordshire County Council, as Lead Local Flood Authority, to undertake Section 19 investigations into the areas where flooding occurred and to coordinate flood risk management responsibilities. (p5) [MUL]
* {16 Feb 2020, 16 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020 - 17 Aug 2020}: Flooding affected highways in Lichfield following the storm events, causing flooding of roads and transport routes managed by the highways authority. (p30) [MUL]
* {16 Feb 2020, 16 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020 - 17 Aug 2020}: Highways (SCC Highways responsibility) channelled intense surface water runoff which ponded at low points in the road. When highway capacity was exceeded the water spilled from the highway into adjacent properties, causing internal property flooding, garden flooding and highway flooding in Lichfield. (p31) [MUL]

### Lichfield District Council
* {16 Feb 2020, 16 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020 - 17 Aug 2020}: The council assisted Staffordshire County Council and Severn Trent Water by distributing flood questionnaires to residents in or near areas where flooding was reported and supported investigations following the storm events. (p4) [MUL]
* {16 Feb 2020}: Carried out site visit during the storm on 16 February 2020; worked with residents and councillors to determine that urban debris caused a blockage under a bridge which prevented highway surface water reaching the watercourse. Removal of the blockage allowed water to start to recede. Provided riparian duty guidance to residents and recorded recommended actions. (p22) [TAB]

### Severn Trent
* {general}: Named as the sewerage company responsible for operation and maintenance of public sewers in Staffordshire. The text describes sewer blockages or sewer capacity being exceeded by heavy/prolonged rainfall can cause sewage/back-up through the network and emerge at the surface (typically via manholes) and result in surface flooding, which would require Severn Trent Water involvement. (p16)
* {16 Feb 2020, 16 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020 - 17 Aug 2020}: Severn Trent Water's sewer infrastructure capacity was overwhelmed during the storms, causing water to be discharged from sewer manholes and highway gullies. This discharge exacerbated surface water flooding and contributed to internal property flooding, external garden flooding and flooding of highways in Lichfield. (p31) [MUL]

### Sill Green
* {16 Feb 2020}: Sill Green (Wigginton) is listed as a location of internal property flooding during February 2020; no return-period or specific flow/rainfall classification is provided in the table. (p20) [TAB]
* {16 Feb 2020}: Surrounding roads and a nearby property were flooded internally. Water was reported as originating from runoff from fields to the rear of the property. Floodwater caused damage to the inside of the property and to the garage, including damage to an electric garage door. Local field ditch conveyance was reported to no longer function correctly following nearby housing development, contributing to runoff. (p25)

### Stych Brook
* {16 Feb 2020}: The brook breached its banks and overtopped the culvert under the road, producing excess flow that inundated surrounding highways and contributed to surface water flooding of land south and east of Watery Lane. (p26)
