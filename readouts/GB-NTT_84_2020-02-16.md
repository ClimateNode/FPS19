

# Trowell flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Trowell – February 2020<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/trowellsection19reportfebruary2020.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Nottinghamshire
* {15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020}: During Storm Dennis Nottinghamshire experienced widespread flooding signalled by flood warnings and alerts; the event led to road closures and deployment of signage and sandbags to manage flood impacts across the county. (p1)
* Sustained intense rainfall on saturated ground on 16 February resulted in extensive flooding across the Nottinghamshire area, including Trowell. (p2)
* {15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 38 flood warnings; 16 flood alerts; 51 road closures; over 70 flood signs; over 4,000 sandbags (p1)
* Quant: 6 properties (p2)

### Broxtowe
* Communities in the Broxtowe area were affected by the flood event; Broxtowe Borough Council provided emergency response support, delivered sandbags, managed recovery, and administered hardship and recovery grants and relief schemes. (p5)

### Trowell
* Following prolonged heavy rainfall on 16 February, internal flooding occurred to properties on Cranbourne Close; six properties suffered internal flooding. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Village experienced extensive flooding during the intense rainfall event; properties in Cranbourne Close (within Trowell) were internally flooded when a nearby watercourse was overwhelmed. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 6 properties (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 6 properties (p3) [IMG, PAG]

### Cranbourne Close, Trowell
* Internal flooding of residential properties caused by a nearby watercourse being overwhelmed after prolonged/intense rainfall. Flooding resulted from large volumes of water entering the watercourse from saturated ground (not from backflow at the downstream sluice). (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 6 properties (p3) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* {15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020}: During Storm Dennis the authority (via contractor Via East Midlands Ltd) facilitated emergency responses across Nottinghamshire: arranging 51 road closures, placing flood warning/signage on the network and distributing sandbags to support communities. The county also experienced 38 flood warnings and 16 flood alerts during the event. (p1)
* Initiated and coordinated emergency planning procedures, administered the Flooding Hardship Fund for affected residents, and initiated and led the Section 19 flood investigation in response to the flooding event. (p5) [PAG]
* {15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 51 road closures; over 70 flood signs; over 4,000 sandbags delivered; 38 flood warnings; 16 flood alerts (p1)

### Via East Midlands
* {15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020}: Acted on behalf of Nottinghamshire County Council during Storm Dennis to facilitate flood response on the road network: implemented road closures, deployed flood signage and distributed sandbags to manage flooding impacts. (p1)
* {15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 51 road closures; over 70 flood signs; over 4,000 sandbags (p1)

### Broxtowe Borough Council
* Provided emergency response support, delivered sandbags to affected residents, managed and continue to manage recovery in affected Broxtowe communities, and administered the flood hardship fund and community recovery grants. (p5)

### Via East Midlands Ltd
* As the contractor acting on behalf of the highway authority, maintained public highways and highway drainage assets and provided a site-based presence and investigations immediately following the flooding event. (p5)

### Highway Authority
* Responsible for maintenance of public highways including highway drainage assets and provided site-based presence and investigations immediately following the flood event. (p5)
