

# Ivington flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Ivington<br>055\_Herefordshire County/22\_Ivington.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Ivington
* Village area adjacent to the river corridor is shown as being within or adjacent to Flood Zone 3 on the Environment Agency planning map, indicating exposure to river (fluvial) flooding. The mapping and report context link the settlement to the key flood event for the area. (p1) [IMG]
* Internal flooding recorded in the village during February 2020: four properties flooded internally; one respondent reported internal depths of 6 inches to 1 ft lasting less than one day. Ivington C of E Primary School was recorded as impacted during the event. (p2) [TAB]
* Quant: 4 properties; 1 property also flooded in 2019 (p2) [TAB]

### Honeylake Brook
* Identified as a likely primary fluvial source for properties in/adjacent to Flood Zone 3 in Ivington that experienced internal flooding during the February 2020 event. (p2)
* Fluvial flooding occurred in Honeylake Brook due to backwater effects from the River Lugg; this produced flood risk to nearby properties (report recommends Property Level Resilience measures). (p3)
* Quant: 4 properties; 1 property also flooded in 2019 (p2)

### Ivington Primary School
* Identified as critical infrastructure and recorded as being impacted during the February 2020 flooding event. (p2) [TAB]

### River Lugg
* Backwater effects from the River Lugg caused fluvial flooding in the Honeylake Brook. (p3)
