

# Burnley District flooding 01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015

### Burnley District
* Part of Lancashire which experienced the extreme November–December 2015 storms and flooding; community infrastructure (including schools) in the county were damaged during the December 2015 flood event. (p9)
* Internal flooding to residential properties: Briercliffe (Lane Bottom Area) — 4 properties suffered internal flooding; Brunshaw Area — 3 properties suffered internal flooding. The LLFA concluded the magnitude and intensity of the rainfall event was likely the primary cause for the Briercliffe flooding. (p14) [PAG]

### Lancashire
* Significant flooding occurred across Lancashire in December 2015, affecting communities and prompting review of communication and coordinated actions by risk management authorities. (p3)
* {December 2015, 01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015}: The December 2015 floods caused widespread flooding across the county, affecting homes and businesses; the event resulted in major impacts on people, homes and businesses and led to recommendations for property-level flood resilience and preparatory/response actions by risk management authorities. (p8) [TAB]
* Widespread flooding during the extreme storms and rainfall of November–December 2015 that caused damage across the county, including damage to private gardens, garages and out‑buildings, business premises, agricultural property, roads, bridges, retaining walls, water treatment plants, power and communications installations, and community buildings such as schools, village and town halls. (p9)
* Two major storm events in December 2015 caused flooding across Lancashire, affecting multiple communities; a countywide Section 19-style investigation was initiated to identify affected communities and priorities. (p10) [TAB, PAG]
* {December 2015, 01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015}: Quant: over 2,500 properties (p8) [TAB]
* Quant: over 2,500 properties (p9)

### Manchester Road, Burnley
* Overland flow from the blocked culverted watercourse reached Manchester Road and likely overwhelmed the existing highway drainage system, causing water to accumulate at the fronts of nearby properties until it drained away. Lancashire Highways carried out a CCTV survey of the highway drainage outside affected properties, identified two drainage faults which have been repaired. An appraisal of the highway drainage on Manchester Road has been recommended. (p16) [TAB, PAG]
* Volume of water from a blocked culverted watercourse was routed overland and reached Manchester Road, overwhelming the highway drainage and causing water to accumulate at the front of nearby properties until it drained away. Highway drainage faults on Manchester Road were subsequently repaired. (p17) [PAG]
* A blockage was identified within a culverted watercourse located approximately 6 m north east of Manchester Road; the blockage prevented the Lead Local Flood Authority from completing its investigation at that point so any impact of the blockage during the flood event is unknown. Local residents confirmed no further flooding has occurred at this point since the December 2015 flood event. Lancashire Highways have appraised the highway drainage on Manchester Road and programmed major drainage improvement works. (p18) [TAB]
* Quant: two drainage faults (p16) [TAB, PAG]
* Quant: two drainage faults (p17) [PAG]

## Topics without coordinates

### Lancashire County Council
* Following significant flooding in December 2015 the Lead Local Flood Authority (Lancashire County Council) coordinated flood-risk activities, identified shortcomings in community communication and developed district-level reports to inform affected communities and identify opportunities for future flood-risk management. (p3)
* Recorded and verified a significant amount of data relating to the December 2015 floods in Lancashire; data were used to establish a reliable flood timeline and to assess/commence Section 19 investigations; initiated a countywide investigation covering communities affected by the two major storm events in December 2015. (p10) [TAB, PAG]
* As the Lead Local Flood Authority, the organisation investigated the culverted watercourse blockage ~6 m NE of Manchester Road, notified the riparian landowner of findings, recommended further investigations and committed to ongoing monitoring; the blockage prevented completion of the investigation so any direct impact during the flood event is unknown. (p18) [TAB]

### LCC Highways
* Responsible for appraisal and delivery of highway drainage improvements on Manchester Road in response to the December 2015 flood event; completed appraisal has identified a preferred option and major improvement works (drainage kerbs, higher kerb faces, drainage footway channels and footway resurfacing) were programmed for 2016/17; cyclic and reactive gully maintenance to continue. (p18) [TAB]
* Flooding from the December 2015 event affected highway drainage and street surfaces in Padiham. Lancashire Highways carried out cyclic and reactive gully maintenance across the flood-hit area, repaired a collapsed drain and repaired a damaged footway. (p24) [TAB]
* Quant: 1 collapsed drain; 1 damaged footway (p24) [TAB]
