

# Blidworth flooding 20 Oct 2023

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Blidworth – Storm Babet, October 2023<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/blidworth-s19-storm-babet-oct-2023.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Nottinghamshire
* A major flood event caused significant impacts across the county, including internal flooding of properties. The county authority verified internal flooding on site, delivered sandbags, provided 24-hour emergency planning support, and closed roads to promote safety during the event. (p5) [PAG]
* Quant: 77 roads closed (p5) [PAG]

### Newark and Sherwood
* The district was affected by the flooding; the council played a full role in response, preparing managers ahead of the storm, supporting evacuated residents and providing emergency support to affected homes. (p5)

### Blidworth
* Sustained heavy rainfall during Storm Babet caused flooding across the village, leading to flooding of roads and internal flooding of residential properties. (p1)
* Surface water (overland) flowpath through the village became active during intense rainfall with water flowing west→east and entering properties. Internal flooding of residential properties occurred on Meadow Road, Mansfield Road and New Road as a result of this overland flow. (p4) [IMG]
* Quant: 6 residential properties (p1)
* Quant: 2 residential properties on Meadow Road; 3 properties on Mansfield Road; 1 property on New Road (6 properties total) (p4) [IMG]

### Meadow Road, Blidworth
* Internal flooding of residential properties caused by intense rainfall and overland (surface water) flowpath flowing west-to-east through the area, with water entering properties on Meadow Road. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Overland surface water flow (from an active west-to-east surface water flowpath during intense rainfall) entered residential properties, causing internal flooding. (p4) [PAG]
* Quant: 2 residential properties (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 2 residential properties (p4) [PAG]

### Mansfield Road, Blidworth
* Internal flooding of residential properties caused by intense rainfall and overland (surface water) flowpath flowing through the area, with water entering properties on Mansfield Road. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Overland surface water flow from the active flowpath entered properties, causing internal flooding. (p4) [PAG]
* Quant: 3 properties (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 3 residential properties (p4) [PAG]

### New Road, Blidworth
* Internal flooding of a residential property caused by intense rainfall and an active surface water flowpath flowing through the area, with water entering property on New Road. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Overland surface water flow from the active flowpath entered a property, causing internal flooding. (p4) [PAG]
* Quant: 1 property (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 1 residential property (p4) [PAG]

### River Trent
* Predicted extreme River Trent levels created a continued risk of river flooding and formed part of the flood event that followed surface water flooding; this risk prompted council actions including coordinated community visits and later clean-up of roads and paths and removal of bulky waste from flooded homes. (p6)
