### Northamptonshire
* Widespread flooding across the county on 9 March 2016 from heavy rainfall (approx. 30mm in 12 hours) caused surface water runoff, agricultural runoff and ordinary watercourse flooding, resulting in internal flooding across the county and numerous reports of flooding. (p7)
* During the event on 9 March 2016 parts of Northamptonshire experienced flooding from a combination of agricultural runoff, surface water runoff and ordinary watercourse flooding; this resulted in internal flooding and multiple reports across the county. (p9) [PAG]

Water pooled to around 300 mm depth at the Braybrooke Road / Watery Lane junction; surface water flowed down Watery Lane. One property on Watery Lane suffered internal flooding. A second property on Watery Lane had its garage protected by closed garage doors, but its driveway and garden were flooded. (p13)

Waters rose up to around 3 ft in places; river monitoring recorded a rise of 1.8 m at nearby gauges; waters had receded by 5pm on 10 March 2016. (p15) [MUL]
