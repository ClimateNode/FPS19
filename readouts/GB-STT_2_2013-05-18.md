

# Hardwick flooding 18 May 2013

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Ilkeston Walk, Hardwick, Stockton On Tees<br>122\_Stockton-on-Tees/122\_2013\_Stockton-on-Tees/Flood\_Investigation\_Report\_-\_Hardwick\_(FINAL)\_PDF.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Ilkeston Walk, Hardwick
* Pluvial (surface-water) flash flooding on 18 May 2013 caused internal flooding to properties on Ilkeston Walk; flooding was rapid (flash) and took several hours to subside, and anecdotal evidence indicates repeated past occurrences. (p9) [PAG]
* Pluvial/flash flooding affected properties on Ilkeston Walk; up to 9 properties experienced internal flooding and a further 3 properties were at risk, forcing residents to leave their homes. Floodwaters rose quickly and took several hours to subside; anecdotal evidence indicates repeated past flooding. (p10) [IMG, PAG]
* Runoff from an adjacent, higher field inundated the adopted footpath, overwhelmed the footpath gully/drain, flowed over the footpath and entered front gardens and properties via very low-level air bricks and then front doors, causing internal flooding of the dwellings. Properties had no flood protection in place. (p11) [PAG]
* {general}: The pedestrian footpath and adjacent verge are discussed in the context of drainage: the highway drainage is described as fit for footpath drainage but the gully is quite small and system capacity is limited, so replacement would have only limited benefit in reducing surface-water ponding/runoff from the adjacent field. (p12) [IMG]
* Flood water reached properties such that internal property flooding was possible; property-level protection (waterproof flood doors, one-way brick covers, external waterproofing) would prevent water entering and so avoid internal flooding. Without such measures residents experienced occasional short-term inconvenience rather than devastating internal flooding. (p13)
* Quant: 9 properties affected by internal flooding; 3 properties at risk (p9) [PAG]
* Quant: up to 9 properties affected by internal flooding; 3 properties at risk (p10) [IMG, PAG]

### Stockton-On-Tees Unitary Authority
* The flood event that is the subject of this Section 19 investigation occurred within the Stockton on Tees area (Ilkeston Walk, Hardwick). (p7)
* Very localised intense rainfall on 18 May 2013 caused flash surface-water (pluvial) flooding in parts of Stockton-on-Tees; rivers and ordinary watercourses were unaffected. (p9)

### Hardwick
* Flooding occurred in the Ilkeston Walk area of Hardwick, prompting a Section 19 investigation by Stockton on Tees Borough Council. (p7)
* Area-wide intense, very localised rainfall produced pluvial flash surface-water flooding in the Hardwick area (notably Ilkeston Walk), resulting in internal flooding of residential properties and some residents having to leave their homes. (p9)
* Quant: 9 properties affected by internal flooding; 3 properties at risk (at Ilkeston Walk) (p9)


## Topics without coordinates

### Arqiva Site
* Runoff from the Arqiva site was identified as the source of runoff collecting at the low point and causing surface water/flash flooding that inundated properties on Ilkeston Walk. (p10)
* {general}: Residents report that flooding on/around the field with the transmitter equipment has become worse following groundwork carried out in the field; the field within the site boundary is identified as benefiting from a drainage scheme to reduce runoff/ponding. Arqiva state no works in the last three years. (p12) [IMG]
* Quant: up to 9 properties affected by internal flooding; 3 properties at risk (as caused by runoff) (p10)
