## Place names with coordinates

### Lancashire
* Heavy rainfall on 8 June 2016 caused surface water flooding across the county, overwhelming urban drainage and resulting in flooding of homes and roads. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: 64 properties flooded; 50 homes internally flooded (p1) [TAB]

### Lancaster District

* Quant: 1 property flooded (0 internally) (p1) [TAB]

### Pendle

### Ribble Valley

### Lowerhouse Lane, Lowerhouse
* Surface water flooding (initially attributed to highway surface water) inundated the lane and adjacent properties; investigation found a failed culverted surface water drain from nearby industrial units contributing to the flooding which outfalls to Green Brook. (p1) [TAB]

### Farrington Close, Padiham

### Enterprise Way, Burnley

(p1) [TAB]

### Lancashire Fire and Rescue Service
* Officers attended the Lowerhouse Lane area in response to reports of surface water flooding; residents reported water 30–60 mm deep across ground floors and standing water in gardens. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: Residents reported water 30-60 mm deep across ground floors (p1) [TAB]

### Burnley Borough Council
* Council officers attended Lowerhouse Lane in response to surface water flooding reports affecting ground floors, gardens and local roads. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: Residents reported water 30-60 mm deep across ground floors (p1) [TAB]

### Lancashire County Council
* Lancashire County Council (via its Highways and Flood Risk Management teams) attended inundated roads and footways in Burnley, cleared gullies and outfalls, carried out repairs and monitored affected areas; they investigated drainage/culvert defects and progressed capacity improvement works. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: 64 properties flooded; 50 homes internally flooded; Lowerhouse Lane: 18 properties (17 internally flooded); Thornhill Street: 19 properties (19 internally flooded) (p1) [TAB]

### LCC Highways
* LCC Highways teams attended inundated roads and footways across multiple streets in Burnley, cleared gullies and outfalls, carried out repairs and monitored the areas; they also investigated and identified culvert/culverted watercourse defects requiring capacity improvements. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: Gullies and outfalls cleaned at multiple locations; 64 properties flooded; 50 homes internally flooded; Lowerhouse Lane: 18 properties (17 internally flooded); Thornhill Street: 19 properties (19 internally flooded) (p1) [TAB]
