### Burton Joyce
* Village experienced flooding caused by sustained rainfall over a saturated catchment. Ten residential properties suffered internal flooding; additional properties experienced flooding to gardens and outbuildings. (p1) [PAG]
* Sustained rainfall over a saturated catchment (Storm Henk) caused roads and properties to flood more rapidly than after dry conditions; 10 residential properties experienced internal flooding and additional properties suffered garden and outbuilding flooding. (p2) [PAG]
* Surface water and sewer-related flooding affected multiple streets across the village. Culverts in upper catchments exceeded capacity causing flows onto roads and gardens and a separate unauthorised connection caused the combined sewer to surcharge; these processes contributed to internal and external flooding across the village. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* The village experienced flooding during Storm Henk: bow-wave flooding threatened properties (volunteer road closures were implemented to limit bow wave impacts) and sewer-related flooding was reported at multiple locations; community drop-in and investigation support were provided following the flooding. (p8)

### Nottingham Road, Burton Joyce
* Properties on Nottingham Road experienced internal flooding during the event. (p2) [PAG]
* Upper-catchment runoff caused downstream culverts to exceed capacity leading to flow along the highway and adjacent gardens; an unauthorised connection into the combined sewer caused surcharge onto Woodside Road and Nottingham Road and likely contributed to internal flooding. Highway flooding affected vehicles; residential properties experienced internal flooding. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Highway flooding along Nottingham Road; flow from an upstream watercourse (via exceedance of downstream culverts) caused highway flooding and issues for vehicles. The STW combined sewer system also surcharged (partly due to an unauthorised connection identified from the Woodside Road watercourse), which may have contributed to internal flooding of properties on Nottingham Road. (p4) [IMG, PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* Flooding during Storm Henk prompted county-wide emergency response: 24-hour support through the Emergency Planning team; delivery of sandbags to affected areas and communities; closure of roads to promote safety and avoid additional flood damage; continued monitoring and intervention on drainage assets; delivery of a natural flood management (NFM) project upstream to provide temporary attenuation during flood events. (p8)

### Woodside Road, Burton Joyce
* A watercourse originating north of Woodside Road had an unauthorised connection into the STW combined sewer; this caused the combined system to surcharge onto Woodside Road and Nottingham Road and may have contributed to flooding at Nottingham Road and Cragmoor Road. (p3) [PAG]
* An unauthorised connection from the watercourse north of Woodside Road into the STW combined sewer caused the combined system to surcharge onto Woodside Road and Nottingham Road; this surcharge may have contributed to downstream property flooding. STW have since stopped flow via the unauthorised connection and are monitoring the area. (p4) [PAG]

### Whitworth Drive, Burton Joyce
* A watercourse flowing along Whitworth Drive carried water eastwards onto Nottingham Road, causing highway flooding and problems for vehicles; investigations found this flow did not directly cause internal property flooding. (p3)

### Crow Park Drive, Burton Joyce
* The watercourse originating north of Crow Park Drive caused some local external flooding in the area. (p3)

### Crock Dumble
* The Crock Dumble (a watercourse) exceeded its channel capacity after persistent rainfall over a saturated catchment, causing floodwater to overtop/escape its channel, flood Main Street and inundate a property; the Main Street culvert was identified as a conveyance pinch point. (p5)
* Quant: 1 residential property (p5)


## Topics without coordinates

### Severn Trent
* STW (the sewerage operator) found an unauthorised connection allowing water into the combined sewer which caused system surcharge onto Woodside Road and Nottingham Road; STW have stopped the unauthorised inflow and are monitoring the area for further flooding occurrences. (p3) [PAG]
* The STW combined sewer system surcharged as a result of excessive upstream runoff and an unauthorised connection from a watercourse; this surcharge contributed to flooding on local roads and properties. STW investigated, stopped water entering the combined system via the unauthorised connection, and are monitoring and investigating sewer-related flooding in the area (including at Chestnut Grove). (p4) [PAG]
* An unauthorised connection from a watercourse into the STW combined sewer caused the combined system to surcharge onto Woodside Road and Nottingham Road and may have contributed to flooding on Nottingham Road and further along the combined sewer at Cragmoor Road. STW have stopped water entering via the unauthorised connection and are monitoring the area for further flooding. (p5) [PAG]
* STW manage the impact of flooding on their water supply and sewerage infrastructure (surface water, foul and combined sewers) and provided emergency response/support as a Category 2 responder during the flood event. (p7)
* Received reports of sewer-related flooding in Burton Joyce during Storm Henk, sent emergency response crews, contacted affected residents, and investigated causes (investigations indicate ingress of land drainage into the sewer network exacerbated the flooding). (p8)

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* NCC investigated local flooding and worked with STW to identify that an unauthorised connection from a watercourse into the combined sewer contributed to surcharging and local flood impacts; NCC and STW are monitoring the area for further flooding occurrences. (p4)
* Responded to a major flood incident across the county: initiated a Community Flood Recovery Grant for internally flooded properties; attended sites to verify internal flooding and support residents/businesses; collated and published verified flood impact data; visited flood‑affected residents and businesses to establish sources and severity; chaired strategic/tactical/recovery coordination meetings and provided 24‑hour emergency planning support; led procurement/delivery of DEFRA Property Flood Resilience repair. (p7)
* As Lead Local Flood Authority and emergency responder, NCC provided 24-hour Emergency Planning support, delivered sandbags, closed roads (including closing 69 roads across Nottinghamshire) to protect at-risk areas, supported partners and communities, ran/continued flood signage and road closure training, led a natural flood management (NFM) project completed Spring 2024 to create upper-catchment storage to attenuate flood flows, and monitored/undertook interventions on drainage assets in flood-affected areas. (p8)

### Gedling Borough Council
* During the flood event the council deployed sandbags to at-risk properties, cleared trash screens and drainage grids to improve conveyance, and cleansed roads following the storm to restore safety and drainage. (p8)

### Burton Joyce Community Flood Signage Scheme
* The scheme activated during Storm Henk; volunteers closed several roads within Burton Joyce to limit bow-wave flooding impacts on properties. (p8)
