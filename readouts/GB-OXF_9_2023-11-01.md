

# East Challow flooding 01 Nov 2023 - 31 May 2024

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Flood Investigation Report: A417, East Challow, Wantage, OX12 9TE<br>095\_Oxfordshire County/S19-Flood-Investigation-A417-FINAL-2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Childrey Brook
* Unmaintained and fully silted sections of Childrey Brook caused flooding of the adjacent A417 between November 2023 and May 2024. (p2) [PAG]
* Sections of the channel were reported as fully silted, reducing conveyance. Landowners were contacted in October 2023 and a topographical survey demonstrating the levels of siltation was circulated in March 2024; the sections remained fully silted as of May 2024. (p3) [PAG]
* Obstruction and siltation of riparian-owned sections of the brook contributed to flooding that affected the A417 and surrounding drainage assets; report recommends full de-siltation and annual maintenance/inspection. (p4) [TAB, PAG]
* A heavily obstructed section of Childrey Brook prevented water following its natural course, causing flooding that led to the A417 becoming impassable during the reporting period; upstream increased water volumes (after canal works and prolonged rainfall) contributed to the severity. (p7) [IMG, PAG]
* A heavily obstructed section of Childrey Brook prevented water following its natural course, causing runoff and silt to back up and flood the nearby A417. Obstructions and returning silt after clearance contributed to ongoing flooding and required repeated silt and vegetation clearance downstream. (p8) [PAG]
* Silt accumulation in the open section of Childrey Brook downstream of the A417 caused the channel to act as a siphon. As the siphon effect failed, water backed up through the 0.6 m pipe beneath the A417 and spilled out onto the A417, i.e. the brook's siltation directly caused flooding onto the road. (p9) [PAG]
* Silt accumulation in the open section of Childrey Brook downstream of the A417 obstructed flow. The blocked channel caused the drainage system to act like a siphon; when the siphon broke, water backed up through a 0.6 m pipe beneath the A417 and spilled onto the A417, producing the worst flooding on available records. More frequent discharge from the restored canal has prolonged the duration of flooding, although the restoration works did not factually cause the blockage. (p10)
* Silting and blockages in riparian-owned sections contributed to the flood event; report recommends full de-siltation of riparian sections, annual monitoring and inspections, and follow-on camera surveys and remedial works on associated drainage assets. (p16) [TAB]
* Quant: 0.4 m (peak flood depth on the A417 in places) (p8) [PAG]

### East Challow
* Flooding of the A417 in East Challow led to the A road being closed, causing local transport disruption. (p2)
* Flooding in the local area (within the report area around East Challow) that rendered the A417 impassable after water was unable to follow its natural course due to an obstructed section of Childrey Brook; increased volumes of water in the area following canal restoration works and prolonged winter rainfall exacerbated conditions. (p7)

### A417 at East Challow
* Flooding of the A417 resulted in the A road being closed, disrupting traffic; the closure lasted for approximately two consecutive weeks. (p2) [PAG]
* The A417 lies adjacent to the affected section of the Childrey Brook and is described in the report as having experienced flooding impacts; the report includes recommendations to mitigate flooding now and in the future to the A417. The brook is conveyed beneath the A417 via a 0.6 m pipe, linking the channel issues to the road. (p3) [PAG]
* Floodwater spilled from the backed-up Childrey Brook through the 0.6 m pipe beneath the A417 and onto the carriageway, causing significant damage to the road surface with holes exposing the carriageway sub-base; the damage may require full road reconstruction. (p9) [PAG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Oxfordshire County Council
* Flooding affected highway infrastructure (A417) and drainage assets under OCC remit; OCC is tasked to investigate (camera surveys), carry out remedial works on drainage assets, review and repair the A417, and inspect the Childrey Brook annually as part of the response and recovery. (p4) [TAB]
* As the highways authority, the council issued a Temporary Traffic Regulation Order to close the A417 and undertook maintenance response actions including vegetation removal (outside their ownership), silt removal from Childrey Brook sections and roadside ditches, and temporary pumping to alleviate flooding. (p8)
* Acted as the Lead Local Flood Authority for the investigated flood incident: carried out a Section 19 investigation, maintains a register of assets affecting flooding (ordinary watercourses, surface runoff and groundwater), records flooding hotspots, notifies and advises third‑party asset owners about condition and maintenance responsibilities, and seeks support from other risk management authorities and communities to record/report assets and incidents. (p11)
* Acted as a lead stakeholder for responses and remedial actions: tasked to conduct/coordinate de-siltation monitoring, perform camera surveys of drainage assets after brook clearance, carry out necessary remedial works, review and repair A417 road construction, and inspect the brook annually. (p16) [TAB]

### Highway Authority
* Obstructed and silted sections of Childrey Brook caused it to overflow and inundate the A417 and adjacent drainage assets. The flooding affected the road construction and drainage infrastructure, prompting recommendations for de-siltation of the brook, camera surveys of drainage assets, remedial drainage works and repair of the A417. (p4) [TAB]
* The flooding caused significant damage to the A417 carriageway (holes exposing the sub-base that may require full reconstruction). The Highways Authority carried out extensive maintenance including vegetation and silt removal outside their ownership and months of temporary pumping to try to keep the road passable. (p8)
* Quant: 0.4 m (peak flood depth in places) (p8)

### Highways
* Highways assets (A417 carriageway and drainage infrastructure) were impacted by flooding; the Highways Team is to perform camera surveys after brook clearance, undertake remedial works on drainage assets and repair the A417 carriageway as required. (p4) [TAB]
