

# Ponteland flooding 25 Sep 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Ponteland<br>091\_Northumberland/Ponteland-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Ponteland
* Persistent heavy rainfall on 25/09/2012 produced overland surface water runoff that overwhelmed a drain at 1 Callerton Court; excess runoff flowed north along Callerton Road and spilled into Middle Drive and Dunsgreen, causing flooding to properties. Surface water runoff from Birney Hill Farm also contributed to flooding of rear gardens in nearby residential areas. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 9 residential properties; 4 roads (p1) [IMG]

### Callerton Lane, Ponteland
* Drainage problems reported around Callerton Lane associated with surface water runoff which overwhelmed a drain at 1 Callerton Court; excess runoff then flowed northwards along Callerton Road into downstream streets causing flooding. (p1)
* Floodwater covered the entire carriageway and extended onto the verges; road rendered impassable in the photographed scene, disrupting transportation and residential access. (p2) [IMG]

### Middle Drive, Ponteland
* Surface water spilling from Callerton Road entered Middle Drive, causing flooding to properties on the street. (p1)
* Quant: Contributed to the overall count of 9 residential properties flooded (p1)

### Dunsgreen, Ponteland
* Surface water spilling from Callerton Road reached Dunsgreen and caused flooding to several properties. (p1)
* Quant: Contributed to the overall count of 9 residential properties flooded (p1)

### Edge Hill, Ponteland
* Surface water runoff from Birney Hill Farm flows into the residential area off Edge Hill, causing flooding to back gardens of properties (overland flow not intercepted due to culverted former watercourse). On this occasion no internal property flooding was reported. (p1)

### Greenacres, Ponteland
* Surface water runoff from Birney Hill Farm contributed to flooding in the back gardens of properties within Greenacres (overland flows not intercepted by former watercourse culverted during development); no internal property flooding was reported on this occasion. (p1)

### Mayfair Gardens, Ponteland
* Flooding on Callerton Lane occurred adjacent to Mayfair Gardens; water extended onto verges near this location, affecting residential access and local transport. (p2) [IMG]

### Rotary Way Roundabout
* Drainage around the Rotary Way/Callerton Lane roundabout was overwhelmed by surface water runoff originating from land to the south-west, contributing to downstream flooding along Callerton Road and into Middle Drive and Dunsgreen. (p1)

### Callerton Court
* Surface water runoff originating from land to the south‑west overwhelmed a drain at 1 Callerton Court; the overwhelmed drain allowed excess runoff to continue northwards, contributing to downstream flooding on Callerton Road, Middle Drive and Dunsgreen. (p1)


## Topics without coordinates

### Callerton Road
* Excess surface water runoff flowed northwards along Callerton Road, transporting floodwater which then spilled into adjacent streets (Middle Drive and Dunsgreen) and caused property flooding. (p1)
* Quant: Part of the overall report total of 4 affected roads and 9 residential properties (p1)

### Birney Hill Farm
* Surface water runoff from Birney Hill Farm contributes overland flows into the residential area below (Birney Edge/Greenacres); historically an open watercourse would have intercepted these flows but a culvert installed during development means excessive overland flows now enter the residential area. (p1)

### Birney Edge
* Surface water runoff from Birney Hill Farm caused flooding in the back gardens of properties within Birney Edge (and Greenacres and other roads off Edge Hill). The report notes that on this occasion there was no awareness of water entering buildings, i.e. impacts were to gardens rather than interiors. (p1)

### Middle Drive Roundabout
* Localized pooling of water on the roundabout and adjacent grass; photographic evidence of localized flooding and an improvised sandbag barrier at a nearby driveway, indicating access disruption and drainage issues. (p2) [IMG]
