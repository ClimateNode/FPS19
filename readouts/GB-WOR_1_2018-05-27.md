

# Hollywood flooding 27 May 2018

Reference document:<br>Hollywood Flood Event May 2018 Investigation Report<br>150\_Worcestershire County/Hollywood Flood Event Report 2018.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Hollywood
* Surface water and watercourse flooding inundated residential areas and local businesses; infrastructure suffered relatively minor impacts while homes and businesses experienced severe damage and disruption. (p3) [PAG]
* An extremely intense rainfall event on 27th May 2018 produced surface water/flash flooding across Hollywood (part of the wider Wythall parish), triggering a multi‑agency response and recovery effort. (p6) [PAG]
* On 27th May 2018 extremely intense rainfall overwhelmed highway drains and sewer infrastructure; roads became surface-water flow paths with deep pooling in low spots. Local watercourses were quickly overwhelmed, overtopped and backed up where culverts, fences or garages restricted flow. In most locations water levels fell quickly but in a small number of locations water remained high for an extended period, indicating drainage issues. (p7) [IMG]
* The settlement experienced flooding that required establishment of an emergency response phone line, on-site advice/support (including at the Wythall Hub), assistance with removal of damaged goods, repair of damaged highways and sweeping of footways, and extensive inspection and surveying of drainage, sewers and local watercourses as part of the recovery and investigation. (p10)
* Settlement affected by the flood which prompted watercourse inspections and clearance, sewer and highway drainage responses, removal of a collapsed wall from Hollywood Brook, and subsequent drainage/highway remedial works. (p11)
* Surface water flooding affected the village on 27 May 2018, impacting local drainage and communities and prompting the investigation and recommendations (including investigation of upstream attenuation and installation of monitors). (p13) [TAB]
* Flooding associated with the Hollywood Brook and surface water runoff affected the community in Hollywood on 27th May 2018, causing property flooding, community disruption and resident health/welfare concerns that prompted recommendations for attenuation areas, monitoring, culvert/trash‑screen improvements and property flood resilience measures. (p14) [PAG]
* An unprecedented short-duration rain storm on 27 May 2018 overwhelmed drainage and local watercourses, causing severe surface water / flash flooding that was described as extremely significant and devastating to the community. Residential drainage and watercourse infrastructure, although generally in good order, were overwhelmed; the lack of adequate space for water in period residential developments contributed to the scale of impact. Emergency response and ongoing recovery were reported as good and speedy. (p16) [PAG]
* Flood flow routes concentrated through the central and eastern residential core of Hollywood. Multiple overland flow entry and exit points were identified at the settlement periphery; water routed from higher ground in the north and east toward lower-lying south and west, causing surface water to flow through residential streets and along major/minor roads. (p19) [IMG]
* Quant: more than 300 residential properties and businesses (combined across Hollywood and parts of Wythall) (p3) [PAG]
* Quant: over 1,000 topographic levels surveyed; two advice and support drop-in sessions organised (p10)
* Quant: in excess of fifteen million pounds (p16) [PAG]

### Wythall
* Parts of the wider Wythall parish experienced surface water and watercourse flooding causing severe impacts to residential properties and businesses and disruption to the community; some infrastructure experienced relatively minor impacts. (p3) [PAG]
* {2012}: The parish has a history of relatively minor flooding. Historic incident records within the parish include multiple events (1977; many years between 1981–2009) and in 2012 flooding affected properties and roads. (p5)
* Parts of the wider Wythall parish were affected on 27th May 2018 when intense rainfall overwhelmed drains and sewers, producing surface-water flow along roads and causing local watercourses to come out of bank and back up; some locations experienced prolonged high water indicating local drainage constraints. (p7) [IMG]
* The wider Wythall parish was struck by the same unprecedented short-duration rain storm on 27 May 2018; drainage and watercourse networks were overwhelmed causing surface water / flash flooding across the parish. The absence of adequate space for water in residential developments in the area contributed to the extent of flooding. Recovery efforts and agency response were reported positively. (p16)
* {27 May 2018, 31 May 2018}: Local community support and recovery activities were activated (hub staffed, sandbags deployed, properties/households visited by officers and councillors) in response to flooding impacts. (p20) [TAB, MUL]
* A local response hub in Wythall was established and staffed (hub staffed until Saturday 2 June) to support residents affected by the flooding from the 27 May 2018 event. (p22)
* Quant: more than 300 residential properties and businesses (combined across Hollywood and parts of Wythall) (p3) [PAG]
* {2012}: Quant: 4 properties; 2 roads (p5)
* Quant: in excess of fifteen million pounds (p16)

### Birmingham Metropolitan District

### Solihull Metropolitan District
* Parts of Solihull were also badly impacted on 27th May 2018 by the extremely intense rainfall that overwhelmed drainage and produced surface-water flows along roads. (p7) [IMG]

### Hinton On the Green

### Alcester Road, Hollywood
* {general}: Historic foul system improvement works were recorded for Alcester Road to address flooding, indicating the road experienced sewer/foul-water related flooding issues in the past. (p5)
* A drainage improvement scheme was completed on Alcester Road to reduce the risk of the road and adjacent properties flooding following the event. (p11)

### Lynbrook Close, Hollywood
* The culvert draining from Lynbrook Close (running beneath Hollywood Lane) could not cope with the significantly increased volume of rainwater, restricting flow and contributing to local surface water flooding. (p12)
* Surface water was conveyed from/through Lynbrook Close during the event, with insufficient conveyance underneath Hollywood Lane identified as a cause of local flooding; recommendations call for investigating and improving conveyance in Lynbrook Close. (p13) [TAB]

### Hollywood Lane, Hollywood
* The culvert beneath Hollywood Lane (serving flow from Lynbrook Close) was unable to cope with the increased runoff, contributing to localised surface water flow and flooding issues. (p12)
* Insufficient conveyance beneath Hollywood Lane (culvert/underpass) contributed to surface water flooding during the event; recommendation KR9 calls for investigating conveyance under Hollywood Lane to reduce future flooding. (p13) [TAB]

### Corbett Road, Hollywood
* {general}: Historic foul system improvement works were recorded for Corbett Road to address flooding, indicating the road experienced sewer/foul-water related flooding issues in the past. (p5)

### Windrush Road, Hollywood
* {general}: Historic storm water sewer upgrades were undertaken at Windrush Road to address flooding, indicating past storm-water/sewer flooding impacts at this location. (p5)

### Houndsfield Lane, Hollywood
* The Houndsfield Lane ford was temporarily closed due to inundation during the deluge; flooding along the road network overwhelmed drainage and caused temporary road closures at the ford. (p8)

### Silver Street, Hollywood
* Silver Street is shown on the flood water flow path map within the purple shaded floodplain/watercourse and was inundated during the 27 May 2018 flooding event, causing flooding of the street and adjacent residential properties. (p18) [IMG]

(p11)
* {27 May 2018, 29 May 2018, 30 May 2018}: Provided central coordination and multi-agency command (Silver meetings, deployment of Flood Risk and Highway Drainage Manager), established central communications and control arrangements and deployed staff and resources to respond to widespread surface water flooding affecting roads and properties. ### Hollywood Brook

* The watercourse responded to the intense rainfall on 27 May 2018 and contributed to flooding in the Hollywood area; investigation recommendations reference improving conveyance (under Hollywood Lane and in Lynbrook Close), provision of upstream attenuation within Hollywood greenspace, and installation of monitoring to better understand the brook's response and provide potential early warning. (p13) [TAB]
* Flooding of the Hollywood Brook contributed to flooding in the village of Hollywood, prompting a Section 19 investigation and recommendations including upstream attenuation within greenspace, installation of trash screens on culverts, and installation of water level monitors to better understand brook response and provide early warning.
