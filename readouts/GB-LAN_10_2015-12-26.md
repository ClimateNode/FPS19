

# Preston flooding 26 Dec 2015

Reference document:<br>Preston District Flood Report Recommended Actions<br>069\_Lancashire County/preston-final-report.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Lancashire
* {01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015}: Significant flooding affected communities across Lancashire in December 2015, prompting a review of communications and the development of district‑level reports to inform affected communities and identify opportunities for future flood risk management. (p3) [PAG]
* {December 2015}: Lancashire experienced exceptionally high river flows and widespread flooding during December 2015, resulting in extensive impacts across the county; over 2,500 properties were flooded and emergency flood response measures (e.g. pumps, barriers, flood basin activations) were carried out. (p8) [TAB]
* {01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015}: Extreme storms and rainfall in November–December 2015 caused flooding throughout December that affected communities and infrastructure across the county: over 2,500 properties were flooded; private gardens, garages and outbuildings, business premises and agricultural property were damaged; critical public infrastructure damaged included roads, bridges and retaining walls, water treatment plants, power and communications installations, and community buildings such as schools, village and town halls. (p9)
* {December 2015}: Quant: over 2,500 properties flooded (p8) [TAB]
* {01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015}: Quant: over 2,500 properties (p9)

### Rossendale

### Lancashire County Council
* {01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015}: As Lead Local Flood Authority, recorded and verified significant data from the December 2015 floods, established a reliable flood timeline, assessed whether incidents triggered Section 19 investigations, initiated a countywide Section 19 investigation covering all communities affected in December 2015, adopted a two-stage investigation approach (stage one investigating the two major storm events and identifying affected communities; stage two to report progress on recommended actions), and published findings to discharge its Section 19 responsibilities.
