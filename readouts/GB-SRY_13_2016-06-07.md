

# Caterham On the Hill flooding 07 Jun 2016

## Place names with coordinates

### Caterham On the Hill
* Town experienced a flood incident that is the subject of the Section 19 flood investigation report. (p1)
* Surface-water flooding occurred in the Caterham Valley area during the intense storm on 7 June 2016. (p6) [IMG, PAG]
* Localised surface-water flooding occurred in the Caterham on the Hill area as a result of the intense storm on 7th June 2016; flooding was caused by very high short-duration rainfall and rapid runoff in the town/catchment. (p7) [PAG]
* {general}: The Caterham on the Hill area experienced a short, intense rainfall event on 7th June 2016: the catchment-average accumulated rainfall was 77.62 mm between 12:30 and 15:00, with the peak intensity around 13:30–14:00. This concentrated rainfall produced rapid accumulation and runoff across the local area (catchment), consistent with conditions that produce surface-water/flash flooding in the settlement. (p8) [IMG, PAG]
* The upper settlement (Caterham on the Hill area) was subject to an intense convective rainfall event on 7th June 2016 that produced rapid surface water runoff within the Caterham on the Hill catchment. The report identifies the area as being on the flow route for surface water draining northwards (via piped routes) toward Croydon and highlights a high modelled risk of surface water flooding for this locality. (p9) [IMG, PAG]
* {general}: The catchment in Caterham has a high modelled risk of surface water flooding and contains historic flooding locations. The surface water flow route through the catchment is clearly delineated in model outputs; historic highway flooding (wetspots) and internal property flooding have occurred within the catchment. (p10)
* The town experienced flooding during the event on 7 June 2016. (p12)
* Experienced flash surface-water flooding on 7 June 2016; members of the public phoned emergency services about the severity of the flooding and blue light responders attended the flooded area. (p14)
* Intense rainfall produced widespread surface water flooding across Caterham on the Hill which hydraulically overloaded the sewer network, causing internal sewer flooding and requiring Thames Water attendance and pumping. Some locations experienced very deep standing water; highways were disrupted (including an emergency road closure of Ninehams Road) and responders carried out emergency actions to manage traffic and drainage. (p15) [PAG]
* Residential and local highway impacts from flooding: properties were flooded or displaced residents required assistance; flood‑damaged furniture and rubbish was removed from homes; debris was present on roads and alleyways requiring additional street cleaning; recovery and multi‑agency investigations were initiated across the catchment. (p19) [PAG]
* Flash flooding along the piped watercourse/surface water sewer affected the town: road network disruption, evacuations of vulnerable people and school closures were part of the incident response. (p20) [PAG]
* Surface water runoff and surcharging of the sewer system caused flooding in the Caterham on the Hill area, resulting in internal sewer flooding of properties and prompting multi‑agency investigations and resident engagement. (p21) [PAG]
* Properties in the Caterham on the Hill area were identified as being at risk of flooding; a Flood Action Group was established to coordinate RMAs and residents and to develop resilience measures for affected properties. (p22) [PAG]
* Surface water flooding and surface-water flow along the Caterham on the Hill piped watercourse / storm route affected the town. Historic highway flooding (wetspots) are recorded in the Caterham on the Hill area and there are recorded instances of internal property flooding in streets within the town; the Money Pit structure on the piped watercourse (adjacent to St Michaels Road) was inspected and being cleaned as part of the response. (p26) [IMG]
* The Caterham on the Hill catchment (upper reach in Caterham) experienced intense convective rainfall on 7 June 2016 that produced rapid surface water runoff along the piped watercourse/surface water sewer. This resulted in surface water flooding with recorded historic highway flooding (wetspots) on local roads and reports of internal flooding on streets within the catchment. (p27) [IMG, PAG]
* Intense, localized rainfall on 7 June 2016 (radar-observed catchment totals of ~77.6 mm over ~2.5 hours; local station 72.6 mm in 2 hours) produced surface-water/urban flooding in the Caterham on the Hill catchment. The flow route is a piped watercourse/surface water sewer draining northwards; the catchment and its highway drainage assets were the subject of a Section 19 investigation. The catchment has known historic highway wetspots at Queen's Park Road, Money Road, Banstead Road and Ninehams Road / Stites Hill Road, and previous reports of internal property flooding in Park Road and Court Road (historic locations referenced by the investigation). (p28)
* Surface-water flooding on 7 June 2016 caused property impacts across the town. Local authority response included two Flood Forum surgeries (13 and 28 June) with multiple agencies, a council meeting for residents of Hillcroft Court to assist 18 impacted properties, provision of council tax discounts for displaced households, approximately 70 residents contacting the council for assistance (requests for sandbags, bulky waste collection, council tax enquiries, flood advice) with about 27 follow-ups, an Emergency Parking Order in Park Road to enable contractor parking, additional street cleaning to remove flood debris, and press communications. (p31)

### Tandridge
* The area affected by the incident covered the north‑west of Tandridge, including the Caterham on the Hill catchment. Intense storms on 7 June 2016 produced flooding across this area resulting in numerous property and road impacts and localised traffic gridlock. (p6) [IMG, PAG]
* The Caterham on the Hill catchment (in north‑west Tandridge) experienced extensive flooding on 7th June 2016: internal property and external flooding, impassable roads and road closures. Many internal property floods included sewage; roads were impassable causing traffic gridlock; Caterham Drive (just north of the Croydon/Tandridge boundary) was badly flooded. (p7)

(p26) [PAG, IMG]

### Caterham On the Hill Catchment
* The Caterham on the Hill catchment was the focus of the investigation; the intense storm on 7 June 2016 caused widespread flooding across the catchment including internal property flooding (many with sewage contamination), external flooding and roads made impassable. (p6) [PAG]
* The Caterham on the Hill catchment experienced localized surface-water flooding during an intense storm on 7th June 2016; the catchment received an accumulated average rainfall of 77.62 mm over ~2.5 hours, producing rapid runoff and localised flooding in the catchment. (p7) [PAG]
* {general}: The Caterham on the Hill catchment (5.5 km²) received an average of 77.62 mm of radar-observed rainfall between 12:30 and 15:00 on 7th June 2016; the majority fell in a short intense burst around 13:30–14:00. The rapid, concentrated rainfall produced rapid accumulation across the catchment and would have driven fast runoff and surface-water / flash flooding behaviour in the watercourse and piped drainage within the catchment. (p8) [IMG]
* The catchment experienced flooding prompting a multi‑agency investigation into drainage and infrastructure; investigation and surveying have been carried out and maintenance works (including surveying/maintenance of the Money‑Pit) are underway to address flood pathways and drainage issues. (p19)
* Flash flooding along the piped watercourse/surface water sewer in the Caterham on the Hill catchment caused internal and external property flooding; the Money-Pit structure has been surveyed and maintenance works are being carried out as part of the response. (p20) [PAG]
* Surface water / urban flooding affected the Caterham on the Hill catchment, prompting a multi‑agency review of drainage and infrastructure and the recommendation to appraise and implement mitigation options. (p21)
* The Caterham on the Hill catchment received intense convective rainfall on 7 June producing high accumulated rainfall across the catchment and driving surface-water flow along the piped watercourse/surface water sewer northwards; the catchment is identified in modelling as being at high risk of surface water flooding and the mapped surface-water flow route coincides with observed/drainage asset locations. (p26) [PAG, IMG]
