

# Halesworth flooding 09 Feb 2018

## Place names with coordinates

### Norwich Road, Halesworth
* Shallow surface water (approximately 0.15 m) entered one property at the roadside edge of Norwich Road. The road experienced deeper flooding with depths of approximately 0.15–0.3 m. (p2)
* {01 Apr 2016 - 30 Apr 2016, 01 Jul 2016 - 31 Jul 2016, 01 Dec 2017 - 31 Dec 2017, 09 Feb 2018}: Runoff accumulates at the lowest elevation of Norwich Rd (A144) causing ponding that overtops the highway drop kerb and enters adjacent residential buildings, producing localised internal and external flooding. Highway interception measures can be overwhelmed during high-intensity storms and the system is sensitive to maintenance levels. (p3) [MUL]
* Blocked local watercourses and heavily silted highway drainage caused overloading of road drainage on Norwich Road; persistent rainfall and high-intensity events inundated local depressions, flood depths topped the footway kerb and ran into property; highway drainage outfalls were unable to discharge and car movements forced water out of the highway. (p4)
* Quant: 1 property; road flooding depth 0.15-0.3 m; shallow internal flood depth 0.15 m (p2)
* {01 Apr 2016 - 30 Apr 2016, 01 Jul 2016 - 31 Jul 2016, 01 Dec 2017 - 31 Dec 2017, 09 Feb 2018}: Quant: 1 property internally affected (p3) [MUL]

### Halesworth
* {01 Apr 2016 - 30 Apr 2016, 01 Jul 2016 - 31 Jul 2016, 01 Dec 2017 - 31 Dec 2017, 09 Feb 2018}: Localized surface water ponding and internal/external flooding occurred on Norwich Rd within Halesworth, driven by runoff accumulation at the low point of the road and overtopping into adjacent properties. (p3) [IMG, MUL]
* {01 Apr 2016 - 30 Apr 2016, 01 Jul 2016 - 31 Jul 2016, 01 Dec 2017 - 31 Dec 2017, 09 Feb 2018}: Quant: 1 property internally affected (p3) [IMG, MUL]


## Topics without coordinates

### A144
* {01 Apr 2016 - 30 Apr 2016, 01 Jul 2016 - 31 Jul 2016, 01 Dec 2017 - 31 Dec 2017, 09 Feb 2018}: Sections of the A144 (Norwich Rd) experience surface water ponding and overtopping at a low spot, with highway runoff and hardstanding contributing to flows that enter adjacent properties and cause internal/external flooding. Existing highway intercept measures can be overwhelmed during intense storms. (p3) [MUL]
* {01 Apr 2016 - 30 Apr 2016, 01 Jul 2016 - 31 Jul 2016, 01 Dec 2017 - 31 Dec 2017, 09 Feb 2018}: Quant: 1 property internally affected (p3) [MUL]

### Suffolk County Council
* As the highway maintenance authority and Lead Local Flood Authority, SCC inspected affected local drainage, found blockages and silted choked watercourses, and carried out/commissioned remedial works (system jetted and cleared by August 2018; S.25 letters issued June 2018; works completed July 2018) in response to the flooding. (p4)
