

# Woodborough flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Woodborough – February 2020<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/woodboroughsection19reportfebruary2020.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Nottinghamshire
* {15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020}: Widespread flooding during Storm Dennis: flood warnings and alerts issued across the county; a major incident was declared in the county on 07:00 16 February 2020. (p1)
* Extensive flooding occurred across the Nottinghamshire area following intense rainfall on 16 February 2020, with ground already saturated from an exceptionally wet autumn and winter. (p2)

### Gedling
* Gedling communities were affected by flooding; local recovery has been managed and continues to be managed there. Gedling responders provided emergency support, delivered sandbags to affected residents, and administered hardship and recovery grants. (p4)

### Woodborough
* Village experienced flooding on 16 February 2020 following intense rainfall; the Woodborough Brook (a Main River, partly culverted) runs through the village and is implicated in the flooding. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Residential properties on Main Street within the village experienced internal flooding when the Woodborough Brook overtopped and surface water exceeded the drainage network capacity. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 9 properties on Main Street (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 9 properties (p3) [IMG]

### Main Street, Woodborough
* On 16 February 2020 nine properties on Main Street suffered internal flooding, with reports that water entered both the rear and front of properties. (p2) [PAG]
* Internal flooding of properties on Main Street caused by overtopping of the Woodborough Brook where it is culverted (creating pinch points) combined with large volumes of surface water; floodwater entered properties from both the rear and the front. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 9 properties (p2) [PAG]
* Quant: 9 properties (p3) [IMG, PAG]

### Buckland Dr, Woodborough
* Internal flooding entered properties on Main Street from both the rear and the front after the Woodborough Brook overtopped at culverted pinch points and surface water ran into properties; the drainage network capacity was exceeded during prolonged heavy rainfall. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 9 properties (p3) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Woodborough Brook
* The Woodborough Brook (a Main River) overtopped where it is culverted, creating pinch points in the drainage network that led to water flowing onto Main Street and into properties. (p3)

### Via East Midlands
* Provided a site-based presence and carried out investigations immediately following the flood event; responsible for maintenance of the public highway network including highway drainage assets. (p4)

### Gedling Borough Council
* Provided emergency response support during the flood event; delivered sandbags to affected residents; managed recovery in affected Gedling communities; administered the flood hardship fund and community recovery / relief grants. (p4)

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Initiated and coordinated emergency planning procedures in direct response to the flood; administered the Flooding Hardship Fund to support those affected; initiated and led the Section 19 Flood Investigation into the event. (p4)

### Environment Agency
* Provided response support in managing the flooding event and actively engaged in the Section 19 Flood Investigation. (p4)
