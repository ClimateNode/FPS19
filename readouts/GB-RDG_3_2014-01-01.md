

# Reading Unitary Authority flooding 01 Jan 2014 - 28 Feb 2014

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Flood Report: River Thames, Kennet and Holybrook - Winter Flooding 2013 /2014<br>099\_Reading/faayadgamga0adqafab8afqacgb1aguafab8adaafaa1.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Reading Unitary Authority
* The Reading area was impacted by flooding attributed to the River Thames, Kennet and Holybrook. (p1) [TAB, PAG]

### Pingewood
* Flooding from the Kennet and its tributaries (including the Holybrook) caused internal flooding of homes in Pingewood over a period of days. (p2)
* Quant: 4 homes (p2)

### Burghfield
* Flooding from the Kennet and its tributaries caused internal flooding of homes in Burghfield. (p2)
* Quant: 10 homes (p2)

### Rose Kiln Lane, Reading Unitary Authority
* Properties on Rose Kiln Lane were internally flooded as part of the Kennet/tributary flooding incident. (p2)
* Quant: 7 properties (p2)

### River Thames
* Flooding from the River Thames was identified as a source that contributed to flooding in the Reading area. (p1) [PAG]
* River levels in Reading reached their highest peak on 10 January 2014. The River Thames was a main source of flooding in the Reading area and was responsible for direct internal flooding of properties; the Environment Agency investigated as the responsible Risk Management Authority. (p2) [MUL]
* Quant: 10 properties (p2) [MUL]

### River Kennet
* Flooding from the River Kennet was identified as a source that contributed to flooding in the Reading area. (p1) [PAG]
* Flood warnings were issued for the Lower Kennet (Theale to Reading). Flooding from the Kennet and its tributaries caused internal flooding of properties over a period of several days; a property in the Kirtons Farm area was reported flooding following the Lower Kennet warning. (p2) [MUL]
* Quant: 1 property (Kirtons Farm area); 26 properties (from the Kennet and its tributaries: 4 homes in Pingewood; 10 homes in Burghfield; 5 homes in Southcote; 7 homes on Rose Kiln Lane) (p2) [MUL]

### Holy Brook
* The Holybrook, as a tributary of the Kennet, was included among the watercourses causing flooding. Flooding from the Kennet and its tributaries (including the Holybrook) led to internal flooding of properties in the area. (p2) [MUL]
* Quant: 26 properties (total reported from the Kennet and its tributaries, including the Holybrook; detailed as 4 homes in Pingewood; 10 homes in Burghfield; 5 homes in Southcote; 7 homes on Rose Kiln Lane) (p2) [MUL]


## Topics without coordinates

### Kirtons Farm
* One property in the Kirtons Farm area was reported as flooding following high river levels and an EA flood warning for the Lower Kennet. (p2)
* Quant: 1 property (p2)

### Environment Agency
* The Environment Agency (EA), as the responsible Risk Management Authority for main rivers, investigated the winter 2013/14 flooding of the River Thames, Kennet and Holybrook; issued flood warnings (for example a Lower Kennet warning on 4 January 2014 and further warnings around 1 February 2014); and recorded flooded properties (EA records list 27 properties). The EA recorded one property flooding in the Kirtons Farm area on 4 January 2014, and a separate reporting of 26 properties flooding over several days from the Kennet and its tributaries (details include 4 homes in Pingewood, 10 homes in Burghfield, 5 in Southcote and 7 on Rose Kiln Lane). (p2) [MUL]
* Quant: 27 properties; 1 property in Kirtons Farm; 26 properties (4 Pingewood; 10 Burghfield; 5 Southcote; 7 Rose Kiln Lane) (p2) [MUL]
