

# Alnmouth flooding 05 Dec 2013

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Alnmouth<br>091\_Northumberland/Alnmouth\_Flood\_Investigation\_Report\_05Dec13.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Alnmouth
* A tidal surge moved inland from the Aln Estuary and inundated low-lying parts of Alnmouth, affecting properties and a main road adjacent to the water. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 6 residential properties; 1 road (p1) [IMG, PAG]

### Argyle Street, Alnmouth
* The western edge of Argyle Street and adjacent properties were directly impacted by the tidal surge; the main road adjacent to the affected area was affected and several properties closest to the water were inundated. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 6 residential properties; 1 road (p1) [IMG]

### Victoria Place, Alnmouth
* Impacted by a tidal surge from the Aln Estuary that moved inland and inundated properties along the western edge of Argyle Street; Victoria Place is shown among the properties in the shaded affected area and is adjacent to an affected main road. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: Part of the total of 6 residential properties affected in Alnmouth; 1 road affected (p1) [IMG]

### Riverside Road, Alnmouth
* During the tidal surge on 5 December 2013, water flooded Riverside Road from the boat slipway, overtopped the earth embankment on the western verge, flowed south along the road, entered driveways and internally flooded properties. Floodwaters retreated within an hour as the tide ebbed. Surcharging drains also affected properties, producing internal sewage-related flooding (surcharging baths/toilets/showers). (p2)
* Quant: 6 properties; 2 properties (p2)


## Topics without coordinates

### East Coast Northumberland
* A tidal surge coinciding with a spring tide caused sea levels along the east coast to reach their highest in 60 years, producing extreme coastal/tidal water levels associated with the flooding event. (p2)
* Quant: highest in 60 years (p2)
