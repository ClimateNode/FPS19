

# Coney Weston flooding 07 Jan 2016 - 11 Jan 2021

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: The Street, Coney Weston, Suffolk<br>124\_Suffolk County/124\_2021\_Suffolk County/section-19-report-the-street-coney-weston-first-issue.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Coney Weston
* Coney Weston (the village containing The Street) was the locality of the flood event and was the subject of a Section 19 investigation by Suffolk County Council (LLFA). (p1)
* Internal flooding of at least one property in Coney Weston (occurring on The Street) that has been experienced on more than one occasion. (p2)
* Properties in the centre of Coney Weston were flooded by surface water. The majority of the flooded properties are shown to be at risk from surface water on the GOV.UK indicative flood map; roadside ditches culverted downstream of 'Three Ways' and flowing towards the centre are identified as likely factors in the flooding investigated. (p5)
* Quant: one property (p2)

### The Street, Coney Weston
* Internal flooding of a property (domestic or business) on The Street, experienced on more than one occasion. (p2)
* Highway gullies along The Street were reported to have been surcharging during the storm event, resulting in water flowing down the highway and contributing to overland flow from adjacent greenfield areas. (p5) [TAB]
* Surface water from a nearby ordinary watercourse/blocked highway drainage affected The Street, prompting a capital scheme to improve highways drainage, regrade and improve roadside ditches, and to cleanse and survey the highways drainage system; property-level resilience options and funding are to be explored. (p6)
* Quant: one property (p2)


## Topics without coordinates

### Anglian Water
* The Anglian Water foul sewer and pumping station were reported to be surcharging during the storm event; ingress of surface water into the foul sewer may have contributed to the surcharging. Anglian Water are investigating and attended in Dec 2020. (p5) [TAB, MUL]

### SCC Highway
* {general}: SCC Highways concluded the watercourse is their responsibility and are to carry out/plan works (capital scheme to improve highways drainage, regrade/cleanse roadside ditches, and survey highways drainage) in response to the flooding. (p6)

### Suffolk County Council
* {general}: SCC (via its LLFA) supported property owners and riparian owners with actions relating to an ordinary watercourse and property-level resilience measures following flooding; SCC also concluded responsibility for the watercourse. (p6)
