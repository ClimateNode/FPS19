## Topics without coordinates

### Waltham Forest Council
* Borough-wide flooding occurred following an intense period of torrential rainfall; the event affected the London Borough of Waltham Forest on 17 August 2022 and is recorded as an addendum to the 2021 Section 19 report. (p1)
* The borough experienced intense rainfall on 17 August 2022 that overwhelmed the surface water drainage network in many places, leading to localised highway and property flooding across the borough. The widespread surface water flooding was a primary factor in the impacts recorded on that date. (p2)
* Widespread surface water flooding overwhelmed the borough's drainage on 17 August 2022; LBWF Highways suspended routine gully cleaning and diverted resources to attend flooded roads, and council teams cleared waste following property flooding. (p3)
* LBWF (as the Lead Local Flood Authority) received reports of flooded roads reported to them on 17 August 2022 and has produced a Section 19 investigation (2021) with recommended actions. The authority is undertaking hydraulic flood modelling (four new flood study areas) to cover the whole borough, is monitoring recommended actions, and is coordinating with partner RMAs to identify and pursue strategic capital drainage and mitigation schemes to reduce future flood impacts. (p5) [PAG]
* Quant: 55 roads flooded; property flooding recorded within 15 roads; 17 schools affected (p3)
