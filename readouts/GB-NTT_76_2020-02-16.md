

# Sutton Bonington flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Sutton Bonington – February 2020<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/suttonboningtonsection19reportfebruary2020.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Sutton Bonington
* Surface water collected at a topographical low in the village (south‑west area), with drainage ditches overwhelmed by prolonged rainfall causing internal flooding of properties. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Prolonged heavy rainfall and a significant surface water flow from agricultural fields accumulated at a topographic low in the south‑west of the village. The village surface water drainage and ditch network became overwhelmed, contributing to internally flooded properties in the village (notably on Pasture Lane). (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 5 properties (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 5 properties (on Pasture Lane) (p3) [IMG, PAG]

### Pasture Lane, Sutton Bonington
* Internal flooding of properties on Pasture Lane due to overwhelmed surface water drainage and ditch network following prolonged heavy rainfall. (p2)
* Internal flooding of properties on Pasture Lane due to surface water routed from fields north of the railway and an overwhelmed ditch drainage network, resulting in internal flood damage to properties. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Residents on Pasture Lane were affected by the flood; an Environment Agency pump was deployed to support resident safety; an EA asset on Pasture Lane is scheduled for maintenance; local ditch conveyance investigations relate to flows affecting the area. (p5)
* Quant: 5 properties (p2)
* Quant: 5 properties (p3) [IMG, PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* {15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020}: Widespread flooding during Storm Dennis with multiple flood warnings/alerts and a declared major incident; local transport disruption and flood response actions (road closures, signage, sandbag distribution). (p1)
* Extensive flooding across the area on 16 February 2020 driven by saturated ground and prolonged rainfall; surface water route flows from agricultural fields and an overwhelmed network of ditches led to internal flooding of properties in parts of the county. (p2)
* {15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 38 flood warnings; 16 flood alerts; 51 road closures; over 70 flood signs; over 4000 sandbags (p1)
* Quant: 5 properties (p2)

### Pasture Lane Stores
* Internal flooding of properties on Pasture Lane caused by surface water runoff overwhelming the local ditch network; prolonged rainfall and a surface water flow path from agricultural fields contributed to accumulation at the village low spot. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 5 properties (p3) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* {15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020, 16 Feb 2020}: Responded to widespread flooding during Storm Dennis (February 2020) across Nottinghamshire. Actions carried out on the Council's behalf included facilitation of road closures, placement of flood warning signs and distribution of sandbags; a major incident was declared on 16 February 2020. (p1) [MUL]
* Initiated and coordinated emergency planning and recovery actions in response to the flood; administered the Flooding Hardship Fund; initiated and led the Section 19 flood investigation. (p4)
* Provided emergency response support in managing the flood event; delivered sandbags to affected residents; administered the flood hardship fund, community recovery grant, council tax and business rate relief grant schemes; engaged in the Section 19 Flood Investigation; continues to administer the property flood resilience grant scheme; investigating the ditch system surrounding the village; offers Flood Warden and Community Flood Signage schemes. (p5)
* {15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 51 road closures; over 70 flood signs; over 4,000 sandbags; 38 flood warnings and 16 flood alerts across Nottinghamshire (p1) [MUL]

### Environment Agency
* Responded to issues and pursued maintenance on critical assets after the event; carried out post‑event inspections and confirmed maintenance; deployed a pump which materially contributed to the safety of residents on Pasture Lane; EA-owned assets performed as expected and maintenance on an asset on Pasture Lane is to be completed. (p5)

### Rushcliffe Borough Council
* Responded to the flood event and provided recovery support: delivered sandbags to affected residents; administered the flood hardship fund and Community Recovery Grant; administered council tax and business rate relief; continues to administer the property flood resilience grant scheme; arranged additional emotional support for residents via British Red Cross; runs a flood resilience store grant scheme and administers Government property flood resilience grants to affected properties. (p5)
