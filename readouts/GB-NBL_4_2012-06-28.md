

# Alnwick flooding 28 Jun 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Alnwick (East)<br>091\_Northumberland/Alnwick-east-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Royal Oak Gardens, Alnwick
* Royal Oak Gardens was flooded by surface water runoff when intensive rainfall exceeded drainage capacity. Runoff arrived from fields to the north, adjoining higher streets, and—most significantly—by overtopping at a depression in South Road which allowed water to flow through back gardens into properties on Royal Oak Gardens. (p1) [PAG]
* {1994, 28 Jun 2012}: Surface water from higher ground (Alnmouth Road) was channelled via a footpath into Royal Oak Gardens. The volume of water overwhelmed the sewer network, causing flooding of properties and a sewage backup affecting one property. Reports note gullies not connecting properly to the main sewer network and reduced network efficiency. This area has experienced similar flooding previously (third time in circa 20 years; similar event in 1994). (p2) [MUL]
* Surface water overtopped garden boundaries and flowed through rear gardens of properties on Royal Oak Gardens, with visible pooling, submerged lawns and displaced garden furniture. Flow paths shown on the aerial map direct runoff along the main road into these residential gardens. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 8 residential properties (p1) [PAG]
* {1994, 28 Jun 2012}: Quant: one property; three incidents (including a similar event in 1994) (p2) [MUL]

### Northumberland
* Surface water runoff on 28 June 2012 caused flooding in the east of Alnwick (notably Royal Oak Gardens). Runoff from fields north of Royal Oak Gardens, from higher adjoining streets and from a depression in South Road flowed into and through back gardens into Royal Oak Gardens; the local drainage system lacked capacity and water entered properties and roadways. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 8 residential properties flooded; 3 roads affected (p1) [IMG]

### Alnwick
* Heavy rainfall on 28 June 2012 caused surface water flooding across the east of Alnwick; surface water runoff and an overwhelmed drainage system led to floodwater affecting central and south-eastern parts of the area, inundating properties and roads. (p1) [IMG]
* Mapped flow paths in and around Royal Oak Gardens (within Alnwick) indicate concentrated surface water movement along a primary road with branching flows into adjacent residential areas, identifying locations at risk of surface water accumulation during heavy rainfall. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 8 residential properties; 3 roads (p1) [IMG]

### South Road, Alnwick
* A prominent dip in South Road collected surface water during the intense rainfall; water levels exceeded kerb heights and overtopped the road, flowing east through back gardens of some properties and contributing to flooding on Royal Oak Gardens. (p1)
* {general}: Gullies on South Road were cleaned post 28th June as part of response actions following the flood investigation. (p2) [TAB]
* Surface water runoff travelled from South Road into the rear gardens of properties on Royal Oak Gardens, acting as a flow source/pathway. Post-event maintenance: gullies on South Road were cleaned after 28 June (intended to improve drainage). (p3) [IMG, PAG]

### Alnmouth Road, Alnwick
* Surface water runoff from higher ground at Alnmouth Road/A1068 was channelled down toward Royal Oak Gardens; the map and report place the A1068 within the mapped flood-affected area and show roads affected by surface water flow. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* Surface water accumulated on higher ground at Alnmouth Road and was channelled down a footpath into Royal Oak Gardens, contributing to the flooding there. Gullies on Alnmouth Road were cleaned (post 28th June) as part of the response. (p2) [TAB]
