### Meadowgate Regulator

* The Meadowgate regulator was not operational and its river level was not recorded during the 19–21 Oct 2023 event (it was under refurbishment). Its non-operation meant no flows were held back at Meadowgate during the event, which contributed to lower river levels recorded at Killamarsh compared with 2007 and 2019. (p36)
* The Meadowgate Regulator was not operational during Storm Babet; modelling comparing operational vs non‑operational scenarios shows that when not operational there were slight increases in flood depths and extents in Catcliffe and Treeton for some AEPs (but no additional properties affected in those scenarios). During the event river flows lay between the 1PCSYM and 0.5PCSYM AEP. (p50) [PAG]
