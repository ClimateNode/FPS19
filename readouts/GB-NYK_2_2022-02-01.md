### River Wharfe
* {01 Feb 2022 - 28 Feb 2022}: Identified as the most significant mechanism causing flooding within Tadcaster (i.e. river overflow leading to town flooding). (p2)
* Very high river levels in the River Wharfe (reported as the second-highest in 30 years at Tadcaster) contributed to widespread flooding locally — most notably the major flood impact at Tadcaster — and to internally flooded properties elsewhere in the catchment. (p8)
* The lower River Wharfe experienced very high river levels (recorded as the second highest in 30 years at Tadcaster) and was a cause of flooding to communities in the Selby District (notably Tadcaster). (p10)
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: The River Wharfe overtopped and breached low points in the embankment/flood defences at Tadcaster, causing flooding of the town. (p11)
* {01 Feb 2022 - 28 Feb 2022}: River Wharfe overtopped and/or breached its banks at multiple specific locations through Tadcaster: near the Old Mill property adjacent to the weir (left bank); adjacent to Sainsbury's (left bank); a small site upstream where the defence takes a 90° turn and the footpath crosses (right bank); and downstream of the bridge near the plinth for the temporary footbridge (right bank). (p12) [IMG, PAG]
* {01 Feb 2022 - 28 Feb 2022}: High flows during the February 2022 flood event prompted additional EA surveys and changes to flood warning/alert triggers at Tadcaster; the river's high water levels contributed to the breach/circumvention of the defence embankment at Ulleskelf and to flooding/surcharging of Ulleskelf Ings. (p13)
* High water levels on the River Wharfe caused the breach/circumvention of the flood defence embankment behind West End properties in Ulleskelf, contributed to surcharging of the Ulleskelf Ings and triggered widespread flood warnings across the catchment. (p14) [PAG]
* Properties within the Wharfe catchment were internally flooded; at Kirkby Wharfe (on the Wharfe) one property was internally flooded and both approach roads were cut off by flooding. (p15) [TAB, PAG]
* {2015, 01 Feb 2022 - 28 Feb 2022}: EA reprofiling of low spots within the River Wharfe to reduce the risk of the river breaching; the river is linked in the report to flooding in Tadcaster (including the 2015 event) and the February 2022 event. (p16) [TAB, MUL]
* {01 Feb 2022 - 28 Feb 2022}: Experienced significant flooding as part of the February 2022 catchment flood events; subject of a formal flood investigation by NYCC. (p18)
* Quant: 63 properties internally flooded in Tadcaster; 6 properties internally flooded in Ulleskelf; an additional 15 internally flooded properties across the Nidd, Wharfe and Aire catchments. (p8)

### Tadcaster
* {01 Feb 2022 - 28 Feb 2022}: The town experienced flooding caused principally by the River Wharfe; this flood event prompted Section 19 investigations and local meetings with affected communities. (p2) [PAG]
* Community experienced flooding requiring a Flood Alleviation Scheme; surface water systems serving the location were implicated and must be accounted for in the FAS design. (p3) [PAG]
* Widespread internal flooding of residential properties in the town caused by very high river levels (River Wharfe). (p8)
* Flooding from the River Wharfe inundated the town, with the majority of internal flooding concentrated around Bridge Street and Commercial Street; many flooded properties were businesses. (p10)
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: Flooding occurred due to overtopping of the River Wharfe at low points within embankment/flood defences, followed by overtopping and breach of the flood wall; this produced significant flooding in the town (mechanism similar to 2015 event). (p11)

(p12) [IMG, PAG]
* {01 Feb 2022 - 28 Feb 2022}: The February 2022 flood event led to EA action on warnings at Tadcaster; properties (for example on Bridge Street) experienced internal flooding despite some property flood resilience measures, and some residents/businesses used warnings to reduce losses by moving furniture/equipment enabling quicker re-opening of a number of businesses. (p13)
* River levels breached defences in Tadcaster causing internal flooding of properties; overtopping of flood defences on the west bank contributed to flooding in the town. (p15) [TAB, PAG]
* {2015, 01 Feb 2022 - 28 Feb 2022}: Tadcaster experienced major flooding (notably the 2015 event and flooding in February 2022) that led to internal property flooding, installation of property flood resilience measures, and programme-level responses including development of the Tadcaster Flood Alleviation Scheme and re-profiling of low spots on the River Wharfe to reduce breach risk. (p16) [TAB, MUL]
