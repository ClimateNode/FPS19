### Helmdon
* Intense short-duration rainfall on a saturated catchment caused excess surface water to flow following natural contours to a low point at Station Road, resulting in internal flooding of properties (at least three) and closure of the highway; historic land drainage and drainage infrastructure were ineffective. (p5) [PAG]
* At least three properties in the village were flooded internally during the event; local highway(s) were closed as a result of flooding. Flooding resulted from intense rainfall on a saturated catchment causing excess surface water to flow to low points in the village. (p6) [PAG]
* Areas of the village (predominantly south of Helmdon Brook) were impacted by flooding on 21 November 2012; at least three properties flooded internally and several other properties were at high risk or had sanitary appliance issues; the highway was closed in the affected area. (p8)
* Upstream between the GCR viaduct and Helmdon village there was evidence of the brook overtopping its banks, with water flattening grass up to a distance from the channel. (p11)
* The village experienced flooding impacts to residential properties: multiple properties on Station Road had internal flooding (see Station Road details) and properties on The Green had sanitary appliances affected. (p12)
* Intense short-duration rainfall on a saturated catchment caused existing drainage systems and watercourses to be unable to cope, producing run-off that flowed into other areas and into highway drainage and public sewers. Ditches and watercourses were largely non-functional (full of silt and debris), causing overtopping from privately owned land into the public highway and contributing to flooding across the village. (p15)
* Land drainage in the village has overspilled onto the public highway and into the public sewerage system. The report recommends a village-wide reinstatement of historic drainage (excavating headwalls and piped ditches, investigating outfalls and culverted sections) and owner maintenance to restore hydraulic capacity and reduce future overspill and local flooding. (p16) [PAG]
* {2012, reported end of August 2012}: Surface water flooding occurred in the village on four separate occasions in 2012; these incidents were reported to Anglian Water by Helmdon Parish Council at the end of August 2012. (p37) [PAG, MUL]
* {29 Apr 2012, 04 Jul 2012, 18 Jul 2012, 15 Aug 2012}: Experienced extensive surface water flooding in 2012; photographic evidence shows extensive surface-water inundation and surcharge of the surface-water network during heavy rainfall. Foul flooding was also reported at the Parish Council meeting on 26 September, though photos primarily show surface-water flooding. (p38) [PAG, MUL]
* The document states that diverting or disconnecting ditches and land drainage from the surface water sewer would stop surcharge to the surface water sewers during storm events but would make the flooding worse in Helmdon, indicating Helmdon experiences surface water flooding and that local drainage connections influence flood severity in the village. (p42)
