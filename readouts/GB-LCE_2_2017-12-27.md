## Place names with coordinates

### Thornborough Road, Coalville
* Prolonged rainfall and an obstructed connecting pipe in a trench alongside the property prevented capture of runoff, allowing excess surface water to flow overland into a residential property and cause internal damage. (p3)
* Internal property flooding occurred on Thornborough Road from unknown or multiple sources; this triggered the mandatory investigation criterion for internal property flooding. (p5)
* {15 Jun 2016, 27 Dec 2017}: Repeated surface-water/road flooding at a low point in the highway. A historical incident (15 June 2016) flooded the highway due to surface water accumulation; residents and an investigation map for the 27 December 2017 incident indicate a blocked drainage outfall and a drainage trench from a property connecting to that outfall, implicating local drainage blockage in the December 2017 flooding. (p7) [IMG, MUL]
* Surface-water runoff concentrated along the road corridor on 27 December 2017; accumulations were shown adjacent to the road and adjacent properties and contributed to floodwater reaching the frontage, rear and sides of the affected property, enabling water to enter buildings through openings. (p11) [IMG]
* Storm (surface) water entered a property on Thornborough Road when saturated ground, a blocked outfall in a trench alongside the property and a narrow gap at the side of the building allowed ingress, causing internal flooding of the property. (p13)
* Quant: 1 residential property (p3)
* {15 Jun 2016, 27 Dec 2017}: Quant: 1 report (15 June 2016) (p7) [IMG, MUL]
* Quant: 1 residential property (p13)

### Coalville
* Surface water flooding on 27 December 2017 resulted in overland flow into at least one residential property on Thornborough Road causing internal damage. (p3)
* The flood incident on 27 December 2017 at Thornborough Road occurred within Coalville and resulted in internal property flooding at that location. (p5)
* {15 Jun 2016, 27 Dec 2017}: Localised surface-water/road flooding affecting the Thornborough Road area within Coalville. The county records include a highway flooding report for Thornborough Road (15 June 2016), and the investigation into the 27 December 2017 event involved residents from the site in Coalville. (p7) [MUL]
* Surface water flooding occurred on 27 December 2017 in Coalville (Thornborough Road area), with runoff from higher land causing accumulations immediately in front of, behind and to either side of the affected property and enabling water ingress into the property. (p11) [IMG]
* Quant: 1 residential property (p3)


## Topics without coordinates

### Leicestershire County Council
* Flooding occurred at Thornborough Road, Coalville on 27 December 2017 and triggered a formal Section 19 flood investigation carried out by the County Council as the Lead Local Flood Authority. (p4)

### Severn Trent
* A connecting pipe from a private trench into the STW combined sewer was observed blocked (site visit 5 Jan 2018), so the sewer could not convey excess surface runoff. This inability to accept flow contributed to surface water accumulating adjacent to the property and entering the ground floor. (p9)
