

# Wellington Marsh flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Wellington Marsh<br>055\_Herefordshire County/07\_Wellington\_Marsh.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Wellington Marsh
* Wellington Marsh was the location of the key flood event on 16 February 2020. Environment Agency flood mapping shows flood zones 2 and 3 to the east and southeast of the settlement, with some areas benefiting from flood defences; the main settlement is adjacent to, but not entirely within, the highest-risk flood zone. (p1) [IMG]
* Internal flooding of residential properties caused by runoff and overflowing minor watercourses; depths reported from under 6 inches up to 2 ft. One resident reported garden flooding that lasted 1–2 days. (p2)
* Internal flooding of properties in the settlement. (p3)
* Quant: 9 properties; depths <6 inches to 2 ft; 1 garden flooded for 1-2 days (p2)
* Quant: 9 properties (p3)

### River Lugg
* River Lugg may have influenced the event by raising downstream water levels and reducing the capacity of small tributaries to drain, contributing to flooding in the village. (p2)


## Topics without coordinates

### A49
* Surface water runoff from the A49 flowed through the village towards Wellington Marsh and contributed to overland flooding that led to internal flooding of properties in the Marsh. This flow may have been exacerbated by a culvert below the A49 that was not large enough to convey high flows. (p2)
* Quant: 9 properties; depths reported from less than 6 inches up to 2 ft; 1 garden flooded for 1-2 days (p2)
