### Torbay
* {19 Nov 2012 - 26 Nov 2012, 21 Nov 2012 - 26 Nov 2012}: Prolonged, sometimes torrential rainfall across Torbay between 19 and 26 November 2012 triggered multiple flood alerts and a coastal flood alert at the high tide on 24 November 2012; Torbay Council carried out pre-emptive inspections/cleaning of trash screens on main rivers and ordinary watercourses and readied sandbags. (p5) [PAG, MUL]
* Subject to Met Office severe weather warnings and Environment Agency flood alerts; experienced flooding of properties and roads during the storm, requiring sandbagging, road diversions, clearance of gullies and attendance by emergency services. (p6) [PAG, MUL]
* During the storm event across Torbay there were multiple incidents of internal and external flooding: buildings experienced internal flooding due to combined sewer system surcharging and groundwater ingress; external flooding affected buildings; highways were flooded (including a playing field) due to blocked road gullies and hydraulic overload of the Torre Valley watercourse. (p17) [PAG]
* Torbay experienced a flooding event between 21 and 26 November 2012. The Section 19 investigation identified vulnerable areas within Torbay and recommended actions to reduce flood risk. (p22)
* Quant: 3 properties; five flooding locations (p17) [PAG]
