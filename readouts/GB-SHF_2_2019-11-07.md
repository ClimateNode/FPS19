

# Sheffield Metropolitan District flooding 07 Nov 2019 - 08 Nov 2019

Reference document:<br>Investigation of Flooding Event in Sheffield on 7th and 8th November 2019<br>109\_Sheffield/Flood Investigation Report Sheffield - November 2019.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Chapeltown
* Environment Agency flood warnings issued from 8:00 to 20:00 on 7 November 2019 for Blackburn Brook, which was listed with Chapeltown as an affected area during the heavy rainfall event. (p1)
* Residential and commercial properties were flooded internally. (p3)
* Chapeltown (at Falding Street) experienced an overwhelmed combined sewer system that surcharged and caused internal flooding to houses; the sewer overflow could not discharge to the Blackburn Brook due to high water levels. (p4)
* {general}: Yorkshire Water confirmed they are assessing sewerage system improvement works to reduce the risk of sewer flooding at Falding Street in Chapeltown. (p5)
* Quant: 41 residential properties and 36 commercial properties (across Ecclesfield, Chapeltown, Deepcar, Woodhouse Mill, Hillsborough, Owlerton and Brightside) (p3)

### River Don
* Moveable flood gates, penstock and doors on the River Don were closed as part of the emergency response; all River Don flood gates and doors had been closed at 4:30 am to manage river flood risk. (p2)
* Rising levels in the River Don led to precautionary evacuations in the lower Don Valley; river levels were largely contained by defences but there was localised overtopping at Meadowhall and Brightside and some properties flooded internally at Owlerton, Ecclesfield and Woodhouse Mill. (p3)
* Rising River Don levels during the event. Levels were largely contained within the channel by newly built flood defences, but there was some localised overtopping of flood defences and river walls leading to flooding at locations including Meadowhall, Brightside, Owlerton, Ecclesfield and Woodhouse Mill. (p4) [PAG]
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: The River Don caused flooding during the November 2019 flood event affecting Sheffield; Lower Don Valley defences reduced the resulting impacts. (p5)
* Quant: 41 residential properties; 36 commercial properties flooded internally (city-wide incident; affected districts include Ecclesfield, Chapeltown, Deepcar, Woodhouse Mill, Hillsborough, Owlerton and Brightside). (p3)

### Meadowhall Centre
* Flood gates on the River Don were closed from Kelham Island to Meadowhall as part of the emergency response to the rainfall event. (p2)
* The shopping centre provided overnight accommodation for stranded customers because the surrounding area was flooded; Meadowhall experienced localised overtopping of flood defences/river walls. (p3)
* Localised overtopping of flood defences and river walls by flood water. (p4)
* Quant: Stranded customers accommodated overnight (p3) [PAG]

### Sheffield Metropolitan District
* Flooding occurred within the city during the heavy rainfall event on 7th–8th November 2019; the event caused urban/surface water and river-related flooding across Sheffield (Environment Agency reported river depths and rainfall second only to 2007). (p1)
* City-wide flooding impacted properties, transport and services: 41 residential and 36 commercial properties were flooded internally across multiple districts; transport disruption included hundreds of roads flooded, road closures, tram suspensions and rail line disruption; localised overtopping of river defences occurred at Meadowhall and Brightside and internal property flooding at Owlerton, Ecclesfield and Woodhouse Mill. Some businesses and hotels in the lower Don Valley were evacuated and Meadowhall Shopping Centre provided overnight accommodation for stranded customers. (p3)
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Sheffield was affected by flooding from the River Don during the November 2019 flood event; the completed Lower Don Valley capital defences made a significant contribution to reducing the impact of that flooding on the city's residential and business communities. (p5)
* Quant: 41 residential properties; 36 commercial properties; 129 roads flooded; 12 roads closed; tram services suspended for two days (p3)

### Blackburn Brook
* Environment Agency issued a flood warning for Blackburn Brook on 7 November 2019 during the heavy rainfall event; river depths were reported as very high (second only to 2007) as part of the city‑wide flood event. (p1)

### River Sheaf
* Very heavy rainfall on the Sheaf catchment contributed to overwhelmed watercourses and local drainage, resulting in flooding of properties and roads in the city. (p3)

### Whitewell Brook
* Elevated flows and debris build-up at culvert entrance grillages contributed to localised flooding; some properties in Ecclesfield were flooded internally. Amey carried out debris clearance on 8 November to restore free flow. (p4)

### Clough Dike
* Debris/culvert blockage on the Clough Dyke caused a build-up of floodwater in adjacent parkland; clearance work in November removed the blockage and released the floodwater. (p4)

### Don Valley

### Sheffield City Council
* The Council led citywide flood response: prioritised closing impassable roads, clearing road gullies and culvert entrances, and delivering sandbags to properties and locations being flooded or at imminent risk. The Council carried out investigations into flooded properties and reported totals for the event. (p3)
* Quant: 41 residential properties; 36 commercial properties; 129 roads flooded; 12 roads closed (p3)

### Yorkshire Water

### Upper Don Valley
