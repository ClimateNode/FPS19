### Ruddington

### High Street, Ruddington
* The main High Street sits in a natural low spot; the volume of rain overwhelmed drainage and water within the highway rose to threshold level, causing flooding of lower-lying properties and contributing to internal flooding of businesses in the centre. (p2) [IMG]
* High Street (natural low spot) was inundated when an intense afternoon storm produced more runoff than the local drainage could convey. Highway water levels rose to threshold level and some lower-lying properties experienced internal flooding. Eyewitnesses reported bow waves from vehicles driving through flooded highway sections that caused or worsened internal flooding in some premises. An isolated blocked private drain on private land caused rear flooding to some properties but was not a key contributor to the wider highway flooding. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 22 businesses (p2) [IMG]
