## Topics without coordinates

### TWA
* Ditches at TWA outfalls were blocked, contributing to surface water entering properties. (p7)
* Required to check their surface water outfalls to ditches/watercourses are clear and to notify the LLFA if obstructions were found, as part of responding to the surface water flooding issue. (p12)

### Environment Agency
* Worked with the Lead Local Flood Authority and Oxfordshire County Council to support the community response and advise on preparation and property-level protection following surface water flooding affecting Bridge Close and Edinburgh Drive. (p12) [PAG]
* Worked with the Lead Local Flood Authority and Emergency Planning Team to support the community (Flood Wardens) and to advise on community flood planning and property-level protection following the flood incident. (p13) [PAG]

### Highway Authority
* Tasked to place highway gullies on a one-year cleansing programme and to construct additional drainage infrastructure in response to the surface water flooding from the incident. (p12)

### Vale of the White Horse Council
* Acting as the Land Drainage Authority, to arrange for local ditch owners to cleanse the ditch outfall from Bridge Close in response to the surface water flooding identified by the investigation. (p12)

### Oxfordshire County Council
* Following the Didcot flood investigation, district councils were instructed to review planning policies and flood risk/drainage designs for developments in the vicinity of the flooding incident and to contact developers where surface water drainage or flood-risk mitigation appears ineffective. (p13)
