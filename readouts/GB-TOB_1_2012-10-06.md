

# Torbay flooding 06 Oct 2012

## Place names with coordinates

### Paignton
* Properties and streets in Paignton were flooded (examples include Dartmouth Road and Station Lane); emergency services and Torbay staff undertook pumping, sandbagging and road diversion/clearance activities. (p5)
* Low-lying areas of Paignton (including Dartmouth Road/Station Lane) experienced internal flooding of basement flats during the storm event when the combined sewer surcharged and a pumping station was overloaded; one property experienced groundwater ingress. The town also has a history of surface water and combined sewer flooding in these low-lying areas. (p7) [PAG]
* The Totnes Road / Saxon Meadow area of Paignton experienced flooding when the Yalberton Watercourse overtopped, resulting in floodwater across roads and external flooding of properties. (p8)
* Multiple locations in Paignton experienced internal and external flooding: properties suffered internal flooding and highways/footpaths were externally flooded due to hydraulic overload of the main river, hydraulic overload or blockage of the combined sewer, blocked private drainage and surface water run-off. (p12) [TAB, PAG]
* Multiple locations in Paignton experienced external flooding of highways and footpaths due to blocked road gullies and hydraulic overload of the combined sewer and of a watercourse; internal flooding to basements/properties from surface water/run-off is also listed in the same set of impacts. (p13)
* Quant: 7 basement flats (total across Station Lane/Dartmouth Road); 5 properties flooded due to combined sewer surcharge; 1 property flooded due to groundwater (p7) [PAG]
* Quant: six properties (p8)

### Torbay
* {04 Oct 2012 - 06 Oct 2012}: Prolonged and intense rainfall across the bay (4–6 Oct 2012) caused flooding across Torbay: flooded roads and properties, numerous calls to the control room, diversion of flooded roads, placement of warning signs, sandbagging, replacing blown manhole covers, clearing blocked gullies, clearing trash screens and general clean-up after the flooding. (p5) [PAG]
* Storm-related flooding across the Torbay area affecting highways, properties, footpaths and a school playing field due to blocked gullies, sewer hydraulic overload and watercourse/main river overload. (p12) [TAB]

### Brixham
* Brixham experienced intense rainfall during the event (raingauge at Laywell Reservoir recorded 65.0 mm). Devon and Somerset Fire and Rescue attended flooded properties at St Mary's Hill to assist with pumping out. (p5) [TAB]
* Reports of internal flooding were received from four properties in the St Mary's Hill area of Brixham during the 6 October 2012 storm; investigations attributed the flooding to an inadequately designed new drainage system at those properties and remedial works are being carried out. (p11)
* External flooding of highway in the town (notably Fore Street) due to blocked road gullies / surface water run‑off. (p13)
* Quant: 4 properties (p11)

### Torquay
* Torquay recorded intense rainfall (raingauge at Torre Abbey). Devon and Somerset Fire and Rescue attended flooded properties/locations in Torquay (Fleet Street) to assist with pumping out; highways and drainage teams were also deployed to manage flooded roads. (p5) [TAB]
* {prior to early 1990s, post early 1990s}: Torquay town centre has a history of flooding from the combined sewer system, surface water run-off and groundwater. Attenuation tanks were later built upstream which now provide protection for storm events up to a 1 in 25 year return period. (p10) [MUL]
* {prior to early 1990s, post early 1990s}: Quant: 1 in 25 years (p10) [MUL]

### Collaton St Mary

* {general}: Area has flood risk related to the condition and capacity of the combined sewer; investigations are recommended to identify repairs or improvements to reduce flood risk and to encourage sustainable drainage for new development; property-level protection should be considered for affected properties. ## Topics without coordinates

### Torbay Council
* {04 Oct 2012 - 06 Oct 2012}: Torbay experienced prolonged heavy rainfall from the evening of 4 October 2012 until the morning of 6 October 2012 (most severe in the early hours of 6 October), causing flooding across the bay to varying degrees. As Lead Local Flood Authority Torbay Council is required to ensure a flood investigation is carried out and that a flood investigation report is published following this flooding event. (p4)
* Torbay Council's 24-hour control room received flood calls (first at ~02:00 on 6 Oct). Highways, drainage and TOR2 staff were mobilised to divert flooded roads, install warning signs, sandbag locations to protect properties from highway-related flooding, replace blown manhole covers, clear blocked road gullies, clear trash screens and carry out cleanup after the event. (p5) [PAG]
* Torbay Council control room received incident reports of flooding across the bay; council carried out a detailed analysis of reported incidents and of causes (sewer surcharge, highway drainage, surface water, groundwater, watercourse flooding). (p6) [PAG]
* Collaton St Mary experienced flooding during the storm of 6 October 2012 that caused external flooding to properties, road and garden flooding; Torbay Council is recorded as investigating the culvert under Totnes Road, considering a flood alleviation scheme and potentially enforcing maintenance of watercourses to reduce future risk. (p8) [TAB, PAG]
* Flooding on 6 October 2012 caused surface water and highway-related flooding in parts of Torbay leading to required actions by the council: inspect and clear highway drainage and road gullies, investigate surface water mitigation measures and the condition/capacity of combined sewers, prioritise recommended actions and monitor their delivery in partnership with other Risk Management Authorities. (p14) [PAG]
* Quant: over 30 properties (p6) [PAG]
* Quant: 6 properties (p8) [TAB, PAG]

### Environment Agency

* Where flooding is from an ordinary watercourse or main river the Environment Agency is to investigate the condition and capacity of the watercourse/main river, identify maintenance or improvement works to reduce flood risk, and may consider enforcement if flows are restricted or blocked as a consequence of the 6 October 2012 flooding.
