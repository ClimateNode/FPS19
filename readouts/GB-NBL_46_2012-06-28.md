## Place names with coordinates

### Northumberland
* Contains the West Woodburn flood event (28/06/2012) where flooding from the main river and surface water affected properties and a road in the village. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 2 residential properties; 1 business; 1 road (affects West Woodburn within the county) (p1) [IMG]

### West Woodburn
* Flooding concentrated near the River Rede and river crossing; inundation of residential and business premises and an adjacent road. Two dwellings and one business were flooded and one road was affected, with surface water flow converging toward the river-adjacent area. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* {01 Jan 2005 - 31 Jan 2005, 01 Sep 2008 - 30 Sep 2008, 01 Jun 2012 - 30 Jun 2012, 01 Sep 2012 - 30 Sep 2012, 01 Jan 2013 - 31 Jan 2013}: An unnamed road between West Woodburn and East Woodburn becomes inundated after heavy rain, producing a significant flooding threat to two nearby residential properties (one of which also operates as a business). Floodwater arises from overspill of the River Rede (occurring ~250 m east of the affected properties) and runoff from fields to the south, following a westward flow path along the road. Highway drainage is reported not to function effectively; when river levels are very high discharge is forced up through drains, flowing opposite to intended direction. (p2) [MUL]
* Quant: 2 residential properties; 1 business; 1 road (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* {01 Jan 2005 - 31 Jan 2005, 01 Sep 2008 - 30 Sep 2008, 01 Jun 2012 - 30 Jun 2012, 01 Sep 2012 - 30 Sep 2012, 01 Jan 2013 - 31 Jan 2013}: Quant: 2 properties (p2) [MUL]

### River Rede
* Identified as a main source of flooding; the River Rede (together with surface water) caused inundation in the West Woodburn area, affecting residential properties, a business and a road. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 2 residential properties; 1 business; 1 road (p1) [IMG]

### West Woodburn Bridge
* The road/river crossing was within the main flooded zone; the affected road runs parallel to the river and crosses the flooded area, indicating the bridge/road crossing was inundated. (p1) [IMG]
