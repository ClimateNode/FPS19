### Tameside Metropolitan Borough Council
* River Tame overflowed its banks alongside Park Road at Stalybridge. Photographic evidence shows very high, fast-moving, muddy river water nearly reaching the top of a low stone wall that separates the river from the adjacent road. The high water level placed the road, parked vehicles, pedestrians and nearby red-brick buildings at risk of flooding if levels rose further. (p1)
* Responded to flooding at Demesne Drive (Stocks Brook/culvert inlet overflow). Actions during and after the event included emergency call‑out and diverting flows into the highway drainage when the culvert inlet was too dangerous to access; clearing the inlet structure grating; cleaning the highway drainage system and road sweeping; enlarging drainage openings along Demesne Drive to intercept and channel flows to the sewer; meeting riparian owners and removing a large volume of sediment and debris from the brook between Demesne Close and Illingworth Avenue; commissioning an S19 report; and commencing GiA/future works discussions with other RMAs. (p18) [PAG]
* Delivered immediate flood-response and mitigation actions on 21st November 2016: carried out clean-up including highway drainage cleaning and road sweeping; cleared inlet structure grating to allow greater flow through the main culvert and reduce overflow into the secondary system; commissioned a Section 19 investigation; arranged community engagement (drop-in) with EA and UU; commenced liaison with risk management authorities about future works. (p23) [PAG]
* During the 21 November 2016 flood TMBC officers attended site, diverted flows into the highway drainage system (culvert inlet assessed as too dangerous to clear during the event). After the event TMBC carried out clean-up (cleaning highway drainage and road sweeping), cleared the inlet structure grating to increase main culvert flow and reduce overflow, commissioned a Section 19 investigation and participated in multi-agency engagement with EA/UU (including organising a drop-in for affected residents and commencing future works discussions). (p25) [PAG]
* Responded to the surface water flooding at Tame Close: not made aware until the day after the event; carried out clean-up including cleaning the highway drainage system and road sweeping; commissioned a Section 19 report; liaised with the landowner of the adjoining field; organised and participated in a drop-in session for affected residents with EA and UU; ongoing liaison with EA and UU regarding this and other sites.
