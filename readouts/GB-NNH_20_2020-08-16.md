

# Wellingborough flooding 16 Aug 2020

## Place names with coordinates

### Sheep Street, Wellingborough
* Internal flooding affected properties on/around Sheep Street as part of the single flood incident at Sheep Street / Silver Street. (p5)
* Internal flooding of properties on Sheep Street as part of the single flood incident; water entered buildings (crossed thresholds) causing internal flooding. (p8)
* {Pre-2011}: Surface water run-off from surrounding roads funnelled to Sheep Street causing flooding of road gullies and regular cellar/internal flooding; a large flood was recorded in 1981. (p11) [TAB, MUL]
* Surface-water runoff and rapid flows entered properties from the street (front and rear). Reported internal flooding included ground-floor ingress under front doors and basements/cellars filling; one cellar and ground-floor areas flooded requiring recovery and refurbishment of premises. (p17) [TAB, PAG]
* Rapid runoff and ponding on Sheep Street; water rushed down the street and collected at the low point around the junction with Doddington Road forming a lake. Water entered properties from the back door and via underground pipework from a neighbouring property, possibly through an air brick. Whole cellar flooded; shallow flooding in rear hall and ground floor entrance hall, rear annexe and basement. Occupant remained in property and later returned on 18 August 2020. Residents and businesses tried to stop vehicles from driving through the flooded street. (p18)
* Multiple properties on Sheep Street experienced internal flooding; one instance was minor and managed by diverting the flow. (p19)
* Flow from Silver Street continued southeast down Sheep Street, affecting properties on both sides. At the low point of Sheep Street, runoff from rear roofs and the rear garden flowed towards the rear doors of a residential property. The cellar flooded due to surcharging and overflow of a drainage system that receives water from neighbouring properties and external areas. (p20)
* Quant: more than five buildings (p5)

* Quant: 50mm across ground floor; 400mm depth in cellar; external depths 1200mm to 1800mm at one property (p17) [TAB, PAG]
* Quant: 400mm depth in cellar; external depth between 1200mm and 1800mm (p18)

### Silver Street, Wellingborough
* Internal flooding affected properties on/around Silver Street as part of the single flood incident at Sheep Street / Silver Street. (p5)
* Internal flooding of properties on Silver Street as part of the single flood incident; water entered buildings (crossed thresholds) causing internal flooding. (p8)
* External flooding affecting property on Silver Street caused by blocked drainage. (p11) [TAB, MUL]
* Surface water rushed down Silver Street like a river, moving wheelie bins, temporary pedestrian barriers and areas of temporary asphalt and making the street dangerous for pedestrians. Floodwater entered ground-floor shop premises (under front doors and via letterboxes), contaminating floors with litter and debris and washing rats out of sewers. Businesses cleared stock/water and used a VAX-type floor cleaner; at least one premises lost electricity for approximately 24 hours due to wiring at floor level. (p17) [TAB]
* The strong flow continued southwest to Silver Street, affecting properties on both sides. Properties experienced internal flooding from flows and from water pooling to the rear where there was no drainage path; large debris (wheelie bins, barriers) was carried by the water, posing danger to pedestrians and affecting vehicles. (p19)
* Surface water flowed to the rear of properties on the east side of Silver Street with no means to drain away. Water increased in depth behind buildings and flowed under doors and through other voids, causing internal flooding of rear areas and properties. (p20) [PAG]
* Floodwater flowed along Silver Street, leaving the road wet and displacing street infrastructure (temporary pedestrian barriers and sections of asphalt), disrupting pedestrian access and affecting shopfront frontage. (p36) [IMG, PAG]
* Temporary pedestrian barriers and sections of asphalt were moved/displaced by flood water on Silver Street. (p37) [IMG, PAG]

### Wellingborough
* A flood incident at Sheep Street / Silver Street in Wellingborough caused internal flooding of more than five buildings in close proximity. (p5)
* Town experienced a flood incident on 16 August 2020 that caused internal flooding of properties in the Sheep Street / Silver Street area. (p8)
* Overland surface water flows from exceeded drainage in the catchment contributed to flooding within the lower parts of the town (town centre area) during the incident. (p14)
* The town experienced a surface water flooding incident on 16th August 2020. The report records that highway drainage in the area relies on road gullies connecting to the public surface water sewer; gullies at Silver Street and Sheep Street were last cleansed in September 2019 and those at High Street in June 2017. The investigation notes two StreetDoctor reports of blocked drains on Cambridge Street in the streets surrounding the incident. (p15)
* The town centre area experienced intense surface water flows from northwest to southeast; drainage systems were exceeded, producing powerful flows along roads (notably Broad Green, Church Street and High Street) that caused destructive impacts to buildings and internal flooding of properties. In total, 19 properties in this area are understood to have flooded internally. (p19)
* Intense short-duration rainfall on 16/08/2020 overwhelmed surface water drainage across the area. Deep, fast-moving surface water occupied the full width of roads and footways in affected parts of the town; properties and external areas were flooded and emergency crews pumped deep floodwater to safe areas. Town centre surfaces required clearing in follow-up operations and sandbags were delivered to some properties. (p21)
* Quant: more than five buildings (p5)
* Quant: more than five buildings (p8)
* Quant: 19 properties (p19)

### High Street, Wellingborough
* Reported as conveying surface-water runoff down into adjoining streets (including Silver Street and Sheep Street), contributing to flooding of properties in the town centre. (p17) [TAB]
* Surface water ran down High Street into Sheep Street, contributing to flows and localised flooding along the route. (p18)
* Flows from High Street combined with Church Street to form a powerful, damaging flow at the junction; at least one property had a flooded basement, low wall to a sunken frontage was overtopped, and multiple properties sustained external damage and possible internal flooding from the destructive flow. (p19)
* Powerful flow of surface water caused damage to brick mortar on the street's buildings (reported for High Street). (p36) [IMG, PAG]
* Powerful surface water flow eroded the mortar between bricks at the base/corner of a building, washing out mortar joints. (p37) [IMG]
* Quant: 3 properties (p19)

### Doddington Road, Wellingborough

### Swanspool Brook

### Highway Authority
* Highway drainage assets within the catchment experienced flooding during the event, creating flood risk to the highway and contributing to flood risk to properties; the authority was tasked to undertake cleansing, inspect/maintain drainage more regularly, assess capacity and consider improvement works and overland flow routes.
