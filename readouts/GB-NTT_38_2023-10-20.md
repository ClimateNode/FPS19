

# Laneham flooding 20 Oct 2023

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Laneham – Storm Babet, October 2023<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/laneham-s19-storm-babet-oct-2023.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Laneham
* Sustained heavy rainfall during Storm Babet on 20 October 2023 caused internal flooding to residential properties in the village; gardens and outbuildings were also flooded. (p1)
* Parts of the village experienced flooding driven by North Beck bursting its banks and runoff from surrounding fields; roads (Laneham Road and Dunham Road) and residential properties were flood-affected, including internal flooding of properties on Dunham Road. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* North Beck rose and overwhelmed the culvert under Dunham Road, causing water to pass over the road and then run down Dunham Road (pushed by the bridge hump). Additional runoff from fields west of Laneham Road flowed onto Dunham Road and into adjacent fields. As a result five residential properties on Dunham Road suffered internal flooding. (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 7 residential properties (p1)
* Quant: five residential properties (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: five residential properties (p4) [IMG, PAG]

### North Beck
* Prolonged heavy rainfall caused North Beck water levels to rise and ultimately overwhelm the culvert under Dunham Road; the beck burst its banks, producing flows that overtopped and then ran down Dunham Road and that contributed to internal flooding of nearby properties. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Prolonged heavy rainfall caused North Beck to rise and overwhelm the culvert under Dunham Road; the beck overtopped its banks, sending water over and then down Dunham Road and along the road approaches (pushed by the bridge hump) and contributing to flow from fields onto the road and into adjacent fields. The breach/overflow directly caused internal flooding to nearby residential properties. (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: five residential properties (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: five residential properties (p4) [IMG, PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* Major Incident with significant impacts across the county including internal flooding of properties. County Council officers attended sites to verify internal flooding, offered support, guidance and advice to affected residents and businesses; collated and published verified flood impact data on Resilience Direct; visited affected residents and businesses to establish sources/mechanisms and severity; delivered sandbags; closed roads to promote safety; provided 24-hour emergency planning support; chaired Strategic/Tactical/Recovery coordination groups; led the Section 19 Flood Investigation; and led procurement and delivery of the DEFRA Property Flood Resilience Repair Grant Scheme. (p6)
* Quant: 77 roads (p6)

### Laneham Road, Stokeham
* Laneham Road is highlighted as a flood-affected road; the report indicates properties on Laneham Road were internally flooded as a direct result of North Beck bursting its banks (specific property count not provided in the excerpt). (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Residential properties on Laneham Road suffered internal flooding as a direct result of North Beck breaching its banks; floodwater ran down the road following overtopping of the culvert/bridge hump. (p4) [PAG]

### Dunham Road, Laneham
* Culvert under Dunham Road was overwhelmed by North Beck levels; floodwater initially passed over the road and into the beck and fields, then ran down the road. Flood flows from fields west of Laneham Road also reached Dunham Road. Five residential properties on Dunham Road suffered internal flooding. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: five residential properties (p3) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Responded to a major flood event and to verified incidents of internal flooding: attended sites to verify internal flooding and offer support, gathered and published verified flood impact data, visited affected residents and businesses to establish sources/mechanisms and severity, chaired Strategic/Tactical/Recovery coordination groups, provided 24-hour emergency planning support, led the Section 19 flood investigation, initiated and led delivery of DEFRA Property Flood Resilience Repair Grant activities, delivered sandbags to affected areas, and closed roads to reduce risk and avoid further flood damage. (p6)
* Council properties and residents were affected by flooding: the council provided a rest centre, temporary accommodation, skips, caged vans and staffing to support disposal of flood‑damaged property, cleansed detritus from roads/car parks/paths, rehoused affected tenants from council properties, and undertook repairs to affected council housing. The council also promoted and administered recovery/grant schemes and engaged in the Section 19 Flood Investigation. (p7) [PAG]
* Quant: 77 roads (p6)

### Bassetlaw District Council
* Provided emergency response support for management of the flooding event, including use/distribution of aquasacs, issuing warnings and information, and supporting recovery actions. (p6)
* Responded to flooding caused by Storm Babet by providing immediate support and recovery measures: provision of a rest centre at Retford Leisure Centre; provision of temporary accommodation to affected residents; provision of skips, caged vans and staffing to help residents dispose of flood-damaged property; cleansing of detritus from roads, car parks and paths; rehousing affected council tenants and undertaking repairs to affected council housing properties; promoting and administering Business Recovery Grant, Business Rates Relief, Community Recovery Grant and Council Tax Discount schemes; establishing a working group reporting into Cabinet to focus on flooding; undertaking an internal review of the response to Storm Babet. (p7)
* The council's area was impacted by flooding associated with Storm Babet. The council undertook an internal review of its response which identified future actions, established a working group reporting to Cabinet to focus on flooding, and is taking steps (with partners) to lobby for changes to the Environment Agency funding framework and to assess Property Flood Resilience measures for affected communities. (p8) [PAG]

### Severn Trent
* There were reported foul pumping station issues associated with the event; the LLFA (Nottinghamshire County Council) will liaise with Severn Trent Water regarding these issues. (p7)
