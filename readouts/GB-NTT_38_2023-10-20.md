

# Laneham flooding 20 Oct 2023

## Place names with coordinates

### North Beck
* Prolonged heavy rainfall caused water levels in the North Beck to rise and overwhelm the culvert beneath Dunham Road. Water flowed out of bank and initially passed over the road and back into the beck and fields, then rose further and ran down Dunham Road (following the road profile caused by the bridge hump), sending flows north and south of the bridge and into adjacent fields. These flows, together with overland flow from fields west of Laneham Road, led to internal flooding of residential properties on Dunham Road. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Prolonged heavy rainfall caused water levels in the North Beck to rise and the beck to overtop/overwhelm the culvert under Dunham Road. Floodwater initially passed over the road and back into the beck and fields, then rose further and ran down the road (guided by the bridge hump), with additional significant flow from fields west of Laneham Road onto Dunham/Dunham Road and into adjacent fields. These flows directly caused internal flooding of nearby properties. (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 5 residential properties (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: five residential properties (p4) [IMG, PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* Widespread flooding across the county caused internal flooding to residents and businesses; county council coordinated response and recovery actions including site visits to verify internal flooding, provision of sandbags, collating and publishing verified flood impact data, 24-hour emergency support and chairing strategic/tactical/recovery coordination groups. (p6)
* Quant: 77 roads (p6)

### Laneham
* Sustained heavy rainfall during Storm Babet caused flooding in the village resulting in internal flooding to residential properties and flooding of gardens and outbuildings. (p1)
* Flows from the North Beck overtopped the culvert under Dunham Road and combined with overland flow from fields west of Laneham Road, causing floodwater to run down Dunham Road and enter properties; five residential properties on Dunham Road suffered internal flooding. (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 7 residential properties (p1)
* Quant: 5 residential properties (p4) [IMG, PAG]

### Laneham Road, Stokeham
* Properties on Laneham Road were internally flooded as a direct result of North Beck bursting its banks; flood mapping broadly shows the fluvial flood extent affecting this road. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Properties on Laneham Road suffered internal flooding reported as a direct result of the North Beck overtopping/bursting its banks; fluvial flood extent mapping for the area is referenced to show the flood trend. (p4) [PAG]

### Dunham Road, Laneham
* North Beck rose and overwhelmed the culvert under Dunham Road causing water to come out of bank, pass over and then run down the road following the bridge topography; additional overland flow from fields west of Laneham Road also ran onto Dunham Road and into adjacent fields, contributing to road inundation and property flooding. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 5 residential properties (p3) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Provided county-level emergency response and recovery during the flood on 20th October 2023: attended sites to verify internal flooding and offer support, gathered and published verified flood impact data, visited affected residents and businesses to establish sources and severity of flooding, chaired and attended strategic/tactical/recovery coordination meetings, provided 24-hour Emergency Planning support, delivered sandbags, closed roads to protect public safety and avoid further damage, initiated Community Flood Recovery Grant and led the Section 19 Flood Investigation and procurement/delivery for the DEFRA Property Flood Resilience Repair Grant Scheme. (p6)
* As Lead Local Flood Authority, witnessed communities devastated by flooding from the event and committed to continued investigation and response actions; will liaise with Severn Trent Water over reported foul (pumping station) issues, develop Flood Warden / Road Closure training, review Property Flood Resilience support for affected communities and pursue strengthened riparian enforcement and asset inspection. (p7)
* Quant: 77 roads (p6)

### Bassetlaw District Council
* Provided emergency response support during the flooding event, including deployment of AquaSacs (sandbag alternatives), warning and informing the public and recovery support. (p6)
* Coordinated and delivered multi-agency response and recovery actions for properties and residents affected by the flood on 20th October 2023: provided aquasacs and delivered warn/inform/recovery communications; established a rest centre at Retford Leisure Centre and arranged temporary accommodation for affected residents; supplied skips, caged vans and staffing support for disposal of flood-damaged property; cleaned significant detritus from roads, car parks and paths; rehoused tenants from Council properties and undertook repairs to affected Council housing; promoted and administered Business Recovery Grant, Business Rates Relief, Community Recovery Grant and Council Tax Discount schemes; worked in partnership with Nottinghamshire County Council on verification and administration of the Property Flood Resilience Grant Scheme; engaged in the section 19 Flood Investigation; undertook an internal review of the response and established a Council working group to focus on flooding. (p7)
* The council's area was impacted by flooding associated with Storm Babet. The council undertook an internal review of its response which identified future actions, established a working group reporting to Cabinet to focus on flooding, and is taking steps (with partners) to lobby for changes to the Environment Agency funding framework and to assess Property Flood Resilience measures for affected communities. (p8) [PAG]

### Council
* Responded to flooding by providing temporary accommodation and a rest centre, rehousing affected tenants from council properties, undertaking repairs to affected council housing, providing skips/caged vans and staffing to help residents dispose of flood-damaged property, cleansing detritus from roads/car parks/paths, and promoting/administering Business Recovery Grant, Business Rates Relief, Community Recovery Grant and Council Tax Discount schemes; engaged in the Section 19 flood investigation and coordination with partners. ### IDB
