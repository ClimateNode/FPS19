

# Sefton Metropolitan District flooding 24 Sep 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Fouracres (Maghull), Sefton Lane (Maghull), Hawksworth Drive (Formby), Water, Street (Thornton) and Moss Lane (Lydiate)<br>108\_Sefton/flood-investigation-report-2012.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Fouracres, Maghull
* Drainage outfalls into Whinny Brook and Dover's Brook backfilled when watercourse levels were high; drainage systems were overwhelmed and low-lying land flooded. Properties in the Fouracres area were affected or at risk. United Utilities proposed fitting non-return (flap) valves to reduce backflow. (p5) [TAB]
* Flooding occurred due to backfilling from nearby watercourses (Whinny Brook and Dover's Brook) into drainage outfalls during high river levels/excessive rainfall; recommendations were made specifically for Fouracres (Maghull) to investigate flap valves/non-return systems and for improved coordination of RMAs. (p6)
* {general}: Area identified where low outfalls combined with high river levels might create a flooding issue; United Utilities reviewing locations in the area and considering mitigation (e.g. flap valves/non-return systems) to prevent backflow and flooding. (p9)
* Flooding affected properties in the northwest section of the Fouracres cul-de-sac adjacent to Dovers Brook. A contiguous block of homes/properties are shown as inundated on the council map; affected properties are outlined and shaded blue. (p13) [PAG, IMG]
* The location plan is titled as showing the extent of a flooding event at Fouracres; the map indicates surface water flooding affecting the Fouracres residential street and adjacent properties, with drainage and surface water sewers shown discharging to outfalls at local watercourses (Dover's Brook / Whitney/Whinny Brook). The plan implies inundation of the residential area but gives no property-level counts or detailed damage descriptions. (p14) [IMG]
* Flood water rose in gardens of multiple properties and in a number of instances exceeded property thresholds causing internal flooding. Water in at least one instance appeared to come up through drains and through the floor. Fire Service pumps reduced levels but water remained; additional pumping over 24–26 Sept was required and at least one further property flooded on the evening of 25 Sept. (p16) [PAG]
* Approximately 40 properties on Fouracres were affected by flood water, with 24 properties internally flooded. Foul flooding was reported but not confirmed by debris; clean (surface) water flooded back gardens of properties adjacent to The Crescent. Additional small pumps were brought in on the 26th to help clear garden flooding around Fouracres. (p17) [PAG, IMG]
* Floodwater was present around houses in Fouracres (illustrated in Figure 6). United Utilities concluded flooding in the area occurred due to surface water entering the foul sewer system (via cross-connections, directly through foul drains, or flood water being discharged into it). (p18) [PAG, IMG]
* Properties and gardens to the rear of 11, 15 and 13 Fouracres were flooded when high levels in the nearby brooks forced water back up pipework and into gullies; pumping reduced levels in Fouracres only to a limited extent and the area began to drain only once brook levels dropped. (p19) [IMG]
* The drainage system allowed water to flow from the main river (when Brooks levels were high) into Fouracres, causing flooding of this low-lying area during the event. (p20)
* Whinney Brook burst its banks and caused flooding at Fouracres. Emergency services attended, manhole covers were opened and pumping commenced overnight. Power to the road was cut then later restored. Sandbags were delivered and pumps were used to remove floodwater (hoses directed into Whinney Brook). Council teams checked householders for vulnerability. (p51)

### Dover's Brook
* High levels in Dover's watercourse (Dover's Brook) prevented drainage discharge, flowed back into drainage systems or overflowed; low point in the defence at Dover's Bridge allowed overtopping/entry of water into Sefton Meadows. Environment Agency actions recommended to raise embankment and consider flood warnings. (p5) [TAB]
* The area of Fouracres (Maghull) experienced local flooding associated with backfilling of drainage outfalls from nearby watercourses (Whinny Brook and Dover's Brook); the report recommends investigating flap valves/non-return systems on the outfalls into those brooks to reduce backfilling. (p6)
* Dovers Brook is implicated as the source/adjacent watercourse for the event, with properties closest to its western boundary being flooded; proximity to the brook is identified as a risk factor for the inundation. (p13) [PAG, IMG]
* High water levels in Dover's Brook prevented outflow at outfalls and caused reverse flow up drainage pipework; water emerged from gullies in residents' gardens and later from manholes, contributing to local flooding. Pumping back into Dover's Brook provided only limited reduction in levels within the nearby residential area until brook levels fell. (p19)
* Outfalls into Dover's Brook were identified as a mechanism for flooding; the report recommends investigation and installation of flap valves/non-return systems on these outfalls to reduce backflow and future flooding during the 2012 event. (p21) [PAG, TAB]
* Bridges Lane (area B) experienced partial highway flooding with water pooling on the road and extending into adjacent fields; photographic evidence (25/09/2012) shows vehicles and agricultural machinery travelling through standing water. Highway flooding depth in the affected stretch was approximately 15 cm. (p27) [PAG]
* High levels on the River Alt contributed to tide-locking of the highway drainage system, preventing discharge and contributing to surface water flooding of the highway and gullies overflowing. (p28) [PAG, IMG]

### Water Street, Thornton

* {general}: The Council will continue working towards a solution that remedies defects and increases drainage capacity for this area, indicating Water Street has experienced capacity-related surface water flooding issues. (p8)
* {general}: Area identified as at risk of surface water flooding; funding has been secured for improvements to the piped watercourse to increase capacity and remedy defects. Works are awaiting landowner permission and require further investigation. (p11)

### Thornton

### Maghull
* Parts of Maghull (specifically the Fouracres and Sefton Meadows areas) experienced flooding or were at risk due to exceptional rainfall on 24 September 2012, overwhelmed drainage, very high groundwater and high watercourse levels causing backflow and overflow. (p5) [TAB]
* The flood event affected the Fouracres area within Maghull; properties in the northwest section of Fouracres were inundated, indicating a localized flood impact within the town. (p13) [IMG]

### Lydiate
* {general}: Flood-related issues in Lydiate (specifically at Moss Lane) prompting action to discuss riparian landowner responsibilities and seek a negotiated solution. (p8)

### Sefton
* Rainwater from a field on the north side ran into a dip and pooled, inundating an approximately 60m stretch of road between Sefton village and the bridge over the River Alt to around 15cm depth, causing road inundation and obstruction on 2012-09-24. (p25)

### Formby
* Surface water drainage could not discharge into Eight Acre Brook because of high water levels, causing flooding of properties (surface water flooding). Eight Acre Brook then overtopped its embankment close to the A565; water pooled behind the defence and flowed along the footpath. There was minor damage to the highway around a gully. (p34) [IMG]

### Moss Lane, Lydiate
* Moss Lane (Lydiate) was identified as one of the areas affected by the 24 September 2012 flooding event. Flooding was associated with overwhelmed drainage / watercourse flow; riparian owners subsequently cleared the watercourse (site meetings held, December 2012) allowing water to divert along its old course. (p5) [TAB]
* {general}: Flooding issues affecting Moss Lane that require discussion with riparian landowners to negotiate a solution (recommendation to engage riparian owners). (p8)
* Prolonged rainfall on 2012-09-24 overwhelmed the local drainage and culvert capacity causing stormwater to back up and overflow along natural ground contours. The event caused internal flooding to at least 1 property on Moss Lane and flooded gardens and outbuildings. The area also has a recorded significant flood in 2008 that caused internal flooding to 8 properties and led to highway collapse due to wash-out. (p44)

### Hawksworth Drive, Formby
* Surface water from the adjacent field was flowing into the highway via a low spot in the Environment Agency embankment; recommendation to raise the low spot to the same level as the rest of the embankment to stop flow into the highway. (p8)
* Surface water from tide-locked highway drainage and overflowing gullies pooled on the highway, found a low point in the pavement and flowed into properties on Hawksworth Drive; 4 properties were flooded and there was minor damage to the highway around a gully. Photos of the flooded area were taken on 2012-09-25 and the council received a sandbag request for this location. (p35)

### The Crescent, Maghull
* Foul (sewage) water flooded at least 3 properties on The Crescent; some foul water escaped via a manhole in the road but the majority emanated from manholes in residence gardens and from a side drain taking water from a kitchen. Clean (surface) water flooded rear gardens of properties on The Crescent. Additional small pumps were brought in on the 26th to clear garden flooding around The Crescent. (p17)
* Rear gardens at house numbers 39, 37 and 35 flooded when water pooled in low points and/or backflow occurred; mechanism remains unclear but flooding coincided with high levels in the nearby brooks. (p19)

### Sefton Lane, Maghull
* Surface-water flooding occurred on Sefton Lane between Dover's Brook bridge and the Cheshire Lines Bridge; the highway drain on the opposite side of the road was tide-locked by Dover's Brook, contributing to standing water on the carriageway. (p27)
* High river levels tide-locked highway drainage and gullies, causing surface water to flow onto Sefton Lane between Dover's Brook bridge and the Cheshire Lines Bridge. Flooding formed a pool across the road (~12 cm deep), pooled at the waste recycling centre entrance, flowed around rear of properties damaging outbuildings and a caravan, and caused ingress into at least 1 property. The Council closed the section of Sefton Lane at Dover's Bridge to Bridges Lane. (p28) [PAG, IMG]

### Bridges Lane, Maghull
* Highway flooding on Bridges Lane (area B) between the bridges over the River Alt and Dover's Brook to an approximate depth of 15 cm. Floodwater covered the road and extended into adjacent fields; large vehicles (truck/tractor) were able to pass though the flooded section. (p27) [PAG, IMG]

### Hartdale Road, Thornton
* Manholes surcharged on the highway following surface water drains failing to discharge into the piped watercourse, producing road flooding that followed the surcharging at Water Street. (p41)

### Brook Road, Thornton
* A section of the culvert near Brook Road was found to have completely collapsed; the collapse reduced capacity of the piped system and is identified as the mechanism that led to the flooding events. (p42)

### River Alt
* High River Alt levels restricted discharge from the surface water system and contributed to flooding that affected the adjacent road; the report records a roughly 60m stretch of road between Sefton village and the bridge being inundated to approximately 15cm depth on 2012-09-24. (p25)
* Highway flooding occurred on the stretch of highway between the bridges over the River Alt and Dover's Brook; floodwater reached approximately 15 cm depth along the carriageway. (p27)
* High River Alt levels on 2012-09-24 tide-locked the highway drainage system so drains could not discharge. Once gullies and pipes reached capacity water flowed onto the highway, causing highway flooding between the bridges over the River Alt and Dover's Brook to a depth of approximately 15 cm and contributing to surface water pooling on Sefton Lane (pooling reported at approximately 12 cm in places). (p28) [PAG]

### Sefton Meadows
* A low point in the embankment at Dover's Bridge allowed floodwater to inundate the area; surface water flowing from an adjacent field entered the highway. The Environment Agency recommended investigating raising the embankment and expanding flood warning coverage. (p5) [TAB]
* Surface water from an adjacent field flowed into the highway; a low point in an embankment/defence at Dover's Bridge was identified as a contributory weakness to local flooding risk; recommendation made to expand the flood warning service for the area. (p7)

### Molyneux
* Fouracres lies within the mapped wards (Park, Molyneux). The drainage description and the site plan show the Fouracres area experienced a surface-water/urban flooding event with sewer connections discharging to Dover’s Brook or Whinny Brook at the boundary of the ward. (p14)
