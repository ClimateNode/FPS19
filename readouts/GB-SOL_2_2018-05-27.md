### Stratford Upon Avon Canal

* Flooding of the Stratford‑Upon‑Avon Canal at Lady Lane is reported as a possible cause of additional flows into the River Blythe that contributed to a wave of water reaching properties in the Coppice Walk area. This linkage is reported as possible rather than confirmed. (p24) [IMG, PAG]
* Canal embankment/dam was damaged by water flowing down Malt House Lane and spilling down the slope to Valley Road; two land slips occurred on the embankment and were covered with tarpaulin to protect the integrity of the dam. The Canal & River Trust confirmed the lakes did not overtop/flood, but the embankment damage posed risk to the structure. (p26)

* Intense rainfall in the Blythe catchment overwhelmed a feeder to Earlswood Lakes; this overflow entered the Stratford-Upon-Avon Canal, causing a surge through the canal system. A culvert under the canal was identified as blocked/insufficient capacity and a section of bank adjacent to Waterside Heights had reduced freeboard, contributing to the canal overtopping risk. (p31) [TAB]
* Canal water level was reported higher than normal on 27th May 2018 and is understood to have restricted the outfall from the local sewer/highway drainage network. * Overtopping of the canal at a low point in the southern canal bank (south of Lady Lane) adjacent to the River Blythe Culvert allowed water into the River Blythe and contributed to flooding in the local area; further overtopping along the canal within Solihull was also reported. (p45) [TAB]
* Flood water from surface water/overland flow and overloaded sewers reached the Stratford Upon Avon Canal as the flood progressed through Pear Tree Crescent. (p49)
