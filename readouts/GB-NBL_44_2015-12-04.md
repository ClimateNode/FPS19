

# Warden flooding 04 Dec 2015 - 05 Dec 2015

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Warden<br>091\_Northumberland/Warden\_FIR\_2015.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### North England
* Severe gales and heavy persistent rainfall during Storm Desmond affected northern England, causing rivers (including the Tyne and its tributaries) to swell and leading to local overtopping and flooding such as that experienced in Warden. (p1)

### Low Prudhoe
* Flood defences at Low Prudhoe overtopped as a result of the swollen River Tyne during the event. (p1)

### River South Tyne
* River South Tyne swelled during Storm Desmond and caused flooding of properties on both the north and south banks in Warden; floodwaters flowed from the river towards the railway bridge and along the track, contributing to property inundation. (p1)
* Quant: 9 residential properties; 7 businesses; 2 properties flooded before 12pm; 3 properties flooded around Bridge End (overtopping); 3 properties flooded down the track (p1)

### Kielder Water
* Discussed as part of flood attenuation measures (whether maximum water level could be reduced to provide greater flood water attenuation); subject to modelling and operational decisions to manage flood risk. (p2) [TAB]

### River Tyne
* The River Tyne and its tributaries swelled during the event; this swelling contributed to overtopping at Low Prudhoe and to flooding downstream affecting Warden (via the River South Tyne). (p1)


## Topics without coordinates

### Bridge End
* Floodwater overtopped the floodwall at Bridge End, flooding three properties in the immediate area. (p1)
* Quant: 3 properties (p1)

### Northumberland County Council
* Provided property-level resilience grants to enable residents to install flood defences and increase property resilience following the flood event. (p2) [TAB]

### Environment Agency
* {general}: Reviewed and implemented improvements to the Flood Warning Service; inspected flood defence assets to determine if repairs were required following the floods; supported the Warden Flood Action Group updating its Community Flood Action Plan; completed an initial economic assessment to evaluate cost-benefit of future flood risk management works. (p2) [TAB]

### Northumbrian Water
* Following the documented flood event NW carried out some localised CCTV sewer surveys and engaged with the Environment Agency to assess whether reducing the maximum water level at Kielder Reservoir could provide greater flood water attenuation. NW also transferred the Kielder Reservoir model to new water-resources software (Aquator) to determine an acceptable maximum water level as part of the flood response and management actions. (p2) [TAB]
