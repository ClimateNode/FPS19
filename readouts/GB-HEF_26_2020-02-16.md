

# Hoarwithy flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Hoarwithy<br>055\_Herefordshire County/25\_Hoarwithy.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Hoarwithy
* Village centre and nearby buildings are shown within Flood Zone 3 on the flood map; the proximity of the River Wye places the central parts of Hoarwithy at high risk of river flooding during the event. (p1) [IMG]
* Seven properties experienced internal flooding arising from an overflowing watercourse (River Wye) with river water passing over the floodplain towards properties. One property (with areas raised above external ground level) flooded to between 2 ft and 3 ft. One basement flooding incident was reported. Flooding persisted for between 1 and 4 days. (p2)
* {16 Feb 2020, 01 Apr 2021 - 30 Apr 2021}: Wriggle Brook presents fluvial flood risk to properties in the village and causes nuisance/actual flooding of the highway. The brook's bed level may be impeding flow through the highway culvert (identified at site inspection), contributing to highway flooding. One listed (cultural heritage) property lies within the Flood Zone. (p3) [MUL]
* Quant: 7 properties; 1 property with raised-floor areas flooded (2–3 ft); 1 basement flooded; depths 1 ft to 5 ft; durations 1 to 4 days (p2)
* {16 Feb 2020, 01 Apr 2021 - 30 Apr 2021}: Quant: 3 properties; 1 listed property (p3) [MUL]

### Wriggle Brook
* {general}: The brook, aligned across the River Wye floodplain, can slow and is governed by Wye water levels; when the Wye is in flood the brook is more likely to surcharge, contributing to local fluvial flooding and spill onto the floodplain and highway. (p2)
* {general}: Anecdotal reports indicate Wriggle Brook presents a flood risk to properties and causes nuisance flooding of the highway. (p3)
* {general}: Quant: three properties (p3)


## Topics without coordinates

### River Wye
* The River Wye is the main river through Hoarwithy and is identified as the source of high flood risk in the village; flood zoning places highest risk closest to the river, indicating the river contributed to flooding of adjacent areas. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* Identified as the primary source of flood risk in Hoarwithy. Floodwater passed over the river floodplain towards properties causing internal flooding. This overtopping/overflow led to multiple properties being internally flooded with significant depths and multi-day durations. (p2) [PAG]
* {general}: Floodwater from the River Wye could inundate a proposed swale installed across the flood plain, which would reduce the swale's effectiveness. (p3)
* Quant: 7 properties; 1 property with internal flooding in areas raised above external ground level; 1 instance of basement flooding; depths varying from 1 ft to 5 ft; raised-area flooding depth 2 ft to 3 ft; flooding duration 1 to 4 days (p2) [PAG]
