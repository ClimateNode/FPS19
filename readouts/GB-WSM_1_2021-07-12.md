## Place names with coordinates

### London Borough of Westminster
* On 12 July 2021 intense rainfall and thunderstorms caused flooding to areas of Westminster (specifically Paddington and the West Kilburn / Maida Vale areas). Highway drains and the local sewer network were unable to cope with the high volume of rainfall, contributing to the flooding. (p1) [PAG]
* Widespread flooding across the City of Westminster: properties, roads and community facilities inundated; residents evacuated from several streets; transport services disrupted (London Underground delays/closures). Thames Water reported internal flooding in a subset of properties. (p2) [PAG]
* Flooding occurred across the borough as a result of a combination of surface water and sewer flooding; affected areas included Maida Vale and West Kilburn. (p3) [PAG]
* Parts of Westminster experienced surface water flooding during the extreme storm on 12 July 2021. WCC's Surface Water Flood Map shows the locations indicated as high risk included areas that flooded during the 12 July event. (p5) [PAG]
* {01 Jul 2007 - 31 Jul 2007, 01 Jun 2009 - 30 Jun 2009, 26 Jun 2016, 29 May 2018, 12 Jul 2021}: Historic and recent floods affected transport and properties in Westminster: closure of Victoria Station and flooding of Westminster Station entrance (historic incidents cited in PFRA); basement property flooding on Formosa Street due to sewer flooding; property flooding along Dorset Street from surface water; Maida Vale recorded flooding in June 2009 and July 2007 linked to sewer network overload; parts of Westminster contained areas that experienced flooding during the 12 July 2021 surface-water flood event (per WCC mapping). (p6) [MUL]
* Intense convective rainfall on 12 July 2021 produced surface water runoff that overwhelmed urban drainage and sewer systems, causing sewer flooding and property flooding within Westminster; the report references historic sewer-flooding records for Westminster postcodes and ongoing review with Thames Water about the event. (p7) [PAG]
* Central Westminster was at the core of intense, localized heavy rainfall on 12 July 2021 which produced surface-water/urban flooding in the area (radar shows rainfall cores up to and exceeding 16–32 mm/hour). The flooding formed part of the wider London event that generated large volumes of emergency and utility calls. (p9) [IMG, PAG]
* Intense afternoon rainfall on 12th July 2021 overwhelmed highway drainage and sewerage systems within Westminster causing sewer surcharge and resulting in flooding to property basements. (p16)
* {general}: Areas of the City of Westminster experienced flooding on 12th July 2021 due to intense rainfall and thunderstorms; highway drains and the local sewer network were unable to cope, leading to localised flooding in parts of the borough. (p18)
* Surface-water/urban flooding impacted the road network across the Westminster administrative area: impacted roads are concentrated in north‑west Westminster (Maida Vale and West Kilburn), a secondary cluster in Paddington (central‑west), with additional scattered affected routes in central and southern parts of the borough. (p21) [IMG]
* Quant: 230 properties; 64 roads; 80 properties flooded internally; 1 primary school; 3 libraries; 3 community centres; 7 London Underground lines (p2) [PAG]
* {01 Jul 2007 - 31 Jul 2007, 01 Jun 2009 - 30 Jun 2009, 26 Jun 2016, 29 May 2018, 12 Jul 2021}: Quant: 39 properties (flooded in Pimlico on 29 May 2018) (p6) [MUL]
* Quant: 1,362 internal incidents in the last 20 years; 14 occurrences of at least 2 internal incidents in the last 10 years; 28 occurrences of at least 1 internal incident in the last 10 years; 4 external incidents (last 10 years); 2 external incidents (last 10 years) (values from the table of historic sewer-flooding records). (p7) [PAG]

### London Region
* Intense rainfall on 12 July 2021 caused surface water/urban flooding in Westminster (London). Across Westminster this event impacted properties, roads, public buildings and transport: approximately 230 properties and 64 roads were reported impacted, 80 of those properties were reported by Thames Water as having flooded internally, and seven London Underground lines were delayed or closed due to flooding or signal failures. (p2) [PAG]
* Flooding occurred within the City of Westminster borough (part of Greater London) as a result of a combination of surface water and sewer flooding. (p3)
* Widespread surface-water/urban flooding across London on 12 July 2021 caused a major demand on emergency and utility services and local authority call handling, indicating widespread impacts from the intense, localized rainfall. (p9) [IMG, PAG]
* Intense rainfall and thunderstorms on 12th July 2021 caused flooding in parts of Westminster (including Paddington and the West Kilburn / Maida Vale areas). Highway drains and the local sewer network were unable to cope with the high volume of rainfall, resulting in urban/surface water and sewer-related flooding across the affected areas. (p18) [PAG]
* Quant: approximately 230 properties; 64 roads; 80 properties; seven London Underground lines (p2) [PAG]
