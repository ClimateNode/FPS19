

# Haydon Bridge flooding 04 Dec 2015 - 05 Dec 2015

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Haydon Bridge<br>091\_Northumberland/Haydon\_Bridge\_FIR\_2015.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Haydon Bridge
* Residential areas on both banks of the River Tyne were flooded when the river and tributaries swelled and overtopped defences. A culverted watercourse north of the village overtopped its inlet in the high school grounds and manholes surcharged along the culvert, affecting a number of properties. Significant surface-water highway flooding occurred at Belmont and Church Street. The caravan park on the south bank was also affected. (p1) [IMG, TAB]
* Centre of the village experienced higher water levels (several inches higher on the December 2015 event) with defences overtopping for longer, resulting in properties having more water during the event. In total 50 residential buildings and 8 businesses in the area were affected by the flooding. (p2) [TAB, PAG]
* Surface water flooding associated with poor culvert performance at Belmont prompted an investigation (in partnership with the EA), development of a Community Flood Action Plan, and improvements to the local Flood Warning service. (p3)
* Quant: 50 residential properties; 8 businesses; 1 road (p1) [IMG, TAB]
* Quant: 50 residential buildings; 8 businesses (p2) [TAB, PAG]

### Belmont, Haydon Bridge
* Significant surface-water highway flooding caused by a culverted watercourse overtopping and manhole surcharge; contributed to local highway inundation in the Belmont area. (p1)
* {Post-December 2015}: CCTV inspections of the culvert to the north of the railway and highway drainage were carried out; proposals call for further investigations and feasibility work to address performance of the culvert at Belmont to better manage surface water flooding. (p2) [TAB]

### Church Street, Haydon Bridge
* Surface-water highway flooding from surcharged sewers/culvert and surface water contribution affected Church Street, causing inundation of the highway. (p1)

### Temple Houses, Haydon Bridge
* {Post-December 2015}: Erosion repair works were carried out beneath the flood wall at Temple Houses using gabions following the flood event. (p2) [TAB]

### Martins Close, Haydon Bridge
* {Post-December 2015}: Repairing the flood bank at Martins Close (and Rockspring Crescent) and raising low spots behind Rockspring Crescent were carried out as post-flood works. (p2) [TAB]

### Rocksprings Crescent, Haydon Bridge
* Flooding affected the flood bank at Rockspring Crescent; repair works were carried out and low spots behind Rockspring Crescent were raised to restore and improve the bank. (p2) [TAB]

### Brigwood, Haydon Bridge
* Brigwood flood wall was repointed and outfalls along the wall were altered as part of repairs following the flood. (p2) [TAB]

### River South Tyne
* {Post-December 2015}: Erosion repair works were carried out on the north and south banks of the River South Tyne using some gravel from within the river channel; annual monitoring of gravel deposits in the South Tyne was proposed. (p2) [TAB]

### River Tyne
* The River Tyne and its tributaries swelled and overtopped defences, causing flooding of residential areas along both banks at Haydon Bridge. (p1)

### Poplars Caravan Park
* Located on the south bank of the Tyne and affected by flooding when river levels increased and overtopped defences. (p1)

### The Anchor
* Repair works to the sewer network near the Anchor Pub were carried out/investigated following the floods; work being investigated in partnership with the EA. (p2) [TAB]


## Topics without coordinates

### Northumbrian Water
* {Post-December 2015}: Northumbrian Water checked outfalls, CCTV'd sewer network in Haydon Bridge, cleansed sections of sewer, carried out sewer lining at three locations and is undertaking repair works to the sewer network near the Anchor Pub in partnership with the EA following the flood event. (p2) [TAB]
* {Post-December 2015}: Quant: 3,000m of sewer network CCTV'd; 1,200m cleansed; sewer lining at three separate locations (p2) [TAB]
