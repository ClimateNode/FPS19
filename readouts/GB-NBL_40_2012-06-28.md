

# Seaton Delaval flooding 28 Jun 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Seaton Delaval<br>091\_Northumberland/Seaton-Delaval-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Seaton Delaval
* Surface-water flooding concentrated in central and eastern parts of the town, moving generally southeast; transport disruption where roads were affected; residential, business and other buildings flooded. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* Heavy rainfall on 28 June 2012 overwhelmed the drainage network in Seaton Delaval, causing localised surface-water flooding of highways, access roads and some properties within the urban area. (p2)
* Urban and residential streets were inundated; floodwater covered roads, reached up to doorsteps of houses, impeded pedestrian movement and accessibility, and disrupted transport within the settlement. (p3)
* Quant: 18 residential properties; 6 businesses; 1 other building; 8 roads (p1) [IMG, PAG]

### Avenue Road, Seaton Delaval
* Photographs show surface-water flooding on Avenue Road during the 28 June 2012 event; a number of business premises became flooded. (p2)
* Deep floodwater covered the carriageway and sidewalks in a commercial area; water reached at least halfway up vehicle wheels in imagery, impeding traffic and risking damage to properties and businesses (shopfronts visible with metal shutters). (p3)

### Westbourne Terrace, Seaton Delaval
* Photographs show surface-water flooding on Westbourne Terrace (A192) on 28 June 2012; the report notes flooding on the road but states flooding to property had not been reported. (p2)

### Elsdon Avenue, Seaton Delaval
* Roadway flooding that affected nearby assets: Astley Park, Astley Community High School and some residential properties adjacent to the park. (p2)

### Fontburn Road, Seaton Delaval
* Surface water flooding of the road/residential area was reported (photographs show flooding); no flooding to property had been reported in the text. (p2)

### Hallington Drive, Seaton Delaval
* Surface water flooding of the road/residential area was reported (photographs show flooding); no flooding to property had been reported in the text. (p2)

### Mindrum Way, Seaton Delaval
* Surface water flooding of the road/residential area was reported (photographs show flooding); no flooding to property had been reported in the text. (p2)

### Linden Road, Seaton Delaval
* Residential properties in the Linden Road area were flooded when heavy rain on 28 June 2012 overwhelmed the drainage network and surface water accumulated, causing properties to flood. (p2)

### Delaval Avenue, Seaton Delaval
* Residential properties in the Delaval Avenue area were flooded when heavy rain on 28 June 2012 overwhelmed the drainage network and surface water accumulated, causing properties to flood. (p2)

### Astley Park
* Park was affected by surface water flooding associated with the heavy rainfall event. (p2)

### Astley Community High School
* School was affected by surface water flooding associated with the heavy rainfall event. (p2)
