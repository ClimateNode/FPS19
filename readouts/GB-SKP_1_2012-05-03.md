

# Mellor flooding 03 May 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report Cataract Bridge Mill, Mellor, Stockport<br>121\_Stockport/121\_2012\_Stockport/Cataract\_Bridge\_Mill\_Flood\_investigation\_Report.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Cataract Bridge Mill
* Property was the subject of a flood investigation following a recorded flood incident; the report addresses drainage, culvert condition and inlet trash screen as possible causes. (p1) [IMG]
* The mill and immediate surrounding site were inundated when an obstruction in the culverted section of Cataract Brook caused water to back up and flood the mill site. Removal of the culvert blockage and improvement of the inlet trash screen were identified as required actions to reduce repeat flooding. (p3)
* The mill site experienced a flood incident on 3 May 2012. Flooding at the site was exacerbated by a blockage and an inadequate trash screen; the report concluded that removing the blockage and improving the trash screen (with a maintenance clearance schedule) would significantly reduce flood risk to the site. (p4)
* The Cataract Bridge Mill site was inundated during the flood incident of 3 May 2012 and was the subject of a Section 19 investigation. The site contains a culvert and a manhole through which Cataract Brook passes (trash screen on the eastern culvert entrance is noted). (p5) [IMG]
* A blockage in the culvert/manhole (including a barrel and other debris) restricted flows and caused water to pond and flood the adjacent land at the Cataract Bridge Mill site; the inlet trash screen was partially blocked which would have increased ponding and contributed to the flooding. CCTV found barrels, tree branches and logs and siltation in the culvert was increased. (p9)
* Quant: 1 barrel (p9)

### Stockport Metropolitan District
* Stockport Metropolitan Borough Council, acting as the Lead Local Flood Authority, carried out a Section 19 investigation into the flood incident at Cataract Bridge Mill dated 3 May 2012. (p5) [PAG, IMG]

### Mellor
* Location of Cataract Bridge Mill where the flood incident occurred and was investigated; the investigation report is dated May 2012 and documents the 3 May 2012 incident. (p1) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Cataract Brook
* An obstruction within the culverted section of Cataract Brook caused the culvert to block, resulting in river-flooding that led to inundation of the nearby Cataract Bridge Mill site. (p3)
* Cataract Brook (a classified Main River) flows through the mill site via a culvert and is implicated in the flood incident that affected the Cataract Bridge Mill on 3 May 2012. (p5) [PAG]
* An obstruction within the culvert on Cataract Brook was identified as the main cause of flooding; removal of the obstruction and clearance/survey of the culvert were recommended to resolve the flooding problem. (p11) [PAG]
* Cataract Brook caused local flooding at the site due to culvert inlet obstruction and debris blocking flow; removal of the obstruction and improvement of the trash screen were recommended to significantly reduce flood risk. (p12)

### Stockport Metropolitan Borough Council
* A highway drainage inlet/trash screen (culvert inlet) associated with SMBC was observed partially blocked by mud, vegetation and debris, restricting flow and contributing to drainage failure and flooding at the Cataract Bridge Mill site. (p8) [IMG]
* Recorded details from affected residents and acted as the Lead Local Flood Authority (LLFA) to facilitate information sharing between risk management authorities and the community following the flooding. (p11)
