

# Lyonshall flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Lyonshall<br>055\_Herefordshire County/19\_Lyonshall.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Lyonshall
* Properties adjacent to Curl Brook lie within Flood Zone 3 (highest risk); areas benefiting from flood defences are limited and closely follow the river, so properties near the brook are at significant risk of river flooding. (p1) [IMG]
* Internal flooding occurred to properties in Lyonshall during the key event; flooding sources reported include river overflow from Curl Brook, blocked gullies/overland flows, and overflowing sewers/toilets (foul flooding). (p3)
* The village experienced internal flooding to residential properties (five properties internally flooded during the key flood event). Surface water runoff affected the highway with water draining onto the road and into the village; foul sewers surcharged during storms and there are reports of foul flooding on highways. No injuries or loss of life were reported. (p4)
* Quant: 3 properties recorded internal flooding (depths all less than 6 inches); 2 additional properties in the parish flooded from Sour Brook (p3)
* Quant: 5 properties (p4)

### Burgage Close, Lyonshall
* {general}: Identified on the EA Flood Map as being close to Curl Brook where Flood Zone 3 is concentrated, indicating significant risk of river flooding to properties on/near the street; flood defences in the area are limited and follow the river course. (p1) [IMG]
* {general}: Burgage Close is shown on the Surface Water Flood Map (June 2021) overlapping blue-shaded surface water flood risk zones; this indicates properties and infrastructure on Burgage Close are potentially vulnerable to surface water flooding. (p2) [IMG]

### Curl Brook
* Curl Brook is the main watercourse shown as producing river flooding; flood zones 3 and 2 follow the course of the brook, placing adjacent land and properties at the highest flood risk; areas benefiting from flood defences are limited and closely follow the brook. (p1) [IMG]
* River overflow from the Curl Brook caused internal flooding to properties in Lyonshall; site visit (June 2021) also confirmed previous episodes of flooding and foul (sewer) flooding associated with the brook. (p3) [IMG, MUL]
* Identified as a source of fluvial flood risk close to properties in the village. An upstream tributary of the Curl Brook was diverted (railway construction) and the channel gradient and routing are reported to allow water to drain onto the road and into the village; Herefordshire Council LLFA has proposed a feasibility study into the Curl Brook to address the fluvial flood risk. (p4)
* Quant: 2 properties reported river overflow from Curl Brook; internal flood depths reported as less than 6 inches (p3) [IMG, MUL]

### Sour Brook
* Flooding from the Sour Brook caused property flooding in the parish. (p3)
* Two properties in the parish flooded from the Sour Brook during the February 16, 2020 flood event (internal flooding reported). (p4) [PAG]
* Quant: 2 properties flooded from Sour Brook (p3)
* Quant: 2 properties (p4) [PAG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Herefordshire Council
* Herefordshire Council (LLFA) identified existing fluvial flood risk in the village linked to the Curl Brook and Sour Brook after an episode on 16th February 2020 that caused internal flooding of properties. The Council proposes to exercise its flood risk management function, promote and seek DEFRA funding for a feasibility study on the Curl Brook, and recognise a surface water runoff problem affecting the highway. Site visit evidence also identified foul sewer surcharge during storms with some foul flooding on highways. (p4)
* Quant: 5 properties (p4)
