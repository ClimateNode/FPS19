

# Haverhill flooding 25 Jul 2021

## Place names with coordinates

### Sturmer Road, Haverhill
* Map and analysis show flood extents and the highest flood risk concentrated along the river and its immediate floodplain; Sturmer Road is shown within or immediately adjacent to those flood risk zones, indicating it was vulnerable to river flooding and likely at risk of inundation and impacts to adjacent buildings/transport access. (p1) [IMG]
* Surface water accumulated in the low spot on/adjacent to Sturmer Road in front of the affected properties after highway drainage was overwhelmed; the inundation contributed to water entering properties and increased flow velocities when disturbed by passing vehicles. (p4) [PAG]
* Surface water (pluvial) flows follow main flow paths that cross Sturmer Road into the urban area; these flows (dark blue areas) can exceed 0.25 m/s. Rear gardens behind properties were inundated by fluvial floodwater, which may have reduced local storage capacity and contributed to internal flooding experienced within properties (internal flooding occurred but was not directly attributable to fluvial flooding). (p6) [IMG]
* Large volumes of surface water accumulated in the highway on Sturmer Road as drainage/gullies were overwhelmed; these highway flows contributed to inundation of nearby low‑lying properties. (p8)
* {September – October 2021}: Surface water overwhelmed highway drainage / suspected insufficient capacity or blocked drains leading to surface water flooding affecting the highway; LLFA recommended a reactive cleanse of highway drainage and an investigation of the highway drainage network. (p10) [TAB]
* Quant: Surface water depths on the highway approximately 400-500mm in places; internal flooding in properties reached 15-20mm. (p4) [PAG]
* Quant: Medium to High pluvial risk: 1 - 3.3PCSYM annual chance; properties located in Flood Zone 1 (<0.1PCSYM annual chance) while adjacent playing field/rear gardens lie largely in Flood Zone 2 (between 1PCSYM and 0.1PCSYM annual chance); flow velocities shown >0.25 m/s in darker blue areas. (p6) [IMG]

### River Stour Brook
* Received overland surface water flows from the road and properties as flows travelled down the side of a property in a northerly direction towards the embankment and Stour Brook beyond. (p5)
* Fluvial flooding from Stour Brook led to accumulations of floodwater in the recreation ground to the west that flowed through rear gardens, adding to flood volumes impacting nearby properties and contributing flows toward Sturmer Road. (p8)
* Fluvial flooding from Stour Brook contributed floodwater that accumulated in the adjacent recreation ground and then travelled through rear gardens, increasing the flood volumes affecting properties. (p9) [IMG, PAG]
* Stour Brook, culverted under an embankment at Motts Field, produced excess flows that combined with surface water overland flows and travelled eastwards through rear gardens. (p10) [TAB, PAG]
* {general}: The watercourse is cited as needing maintenance because blockages or poor flow can lead to accumulations/overflow and through-flow of floodwater into rear gardens. Riparian owners are instructed to ensure the channel is kept clear to reduce this flood risk. (p11)

### Haverhill
* An intense localised rainfall event on 25.07.2021 overwhelmed drainage across much of the town. Flooding was reported in multiple streets and properties were inundated north of Sturmer Road (postcode CO9 4BB). Runoff from a nearby industrial estate contributed to flows along Falconer Road once highway drainage was overwhelmed; an embankment prevented Stour Brook overtopping but also impeded flows into the watercourse. (p3)
* Rear gardens and the playing field to the west were inundated. Surface water accumulated in Sturmer Road (highway) and flowed through rear gardens, adding to volumes from the Stour Brook; this reduced local overland storage capacity and contributed to internal flooding of properties (internal flooding not directly attributable to fluvial flooding). Floodwaters mixed with sewage from private septic tanks on at least one occasion, increasing contamination risk. Highway gullies were overwhelmed and potential surcharge from a surface water sewer serving the industrial estate contributed to flows. (p7) [PAG]

### Falconer Road, Haverhill
* Excess runoff from the industrial estate flowed down Falconer Road once highway drainage was overwhelmed, acting as a conveyance path for surface water toward the low-lying properties north of Sturmer Road. (p3)
* Surface water runoff from a nearby industrial estate travelled down Falconer Road once highway drainage was overwhelmed, inundating the carriageway and contributing to flooding of the low spot immediately in front of properties north of Sturmer Road; vehicle movements through the flood increased depths and velocities, creating wash that further affected adjacent properties. (p4) [PAG]
* {September – October 2021}: Potential failure or insufficient capacity of the surface water sewer network leading to flooding impacts; Anglian Water is to investigate the surface water sewer network and instigate mitigation if appropriate. (p10) [TAB]
* Quant: Road surface water depths approximately 400-500mm in places; contributed to at least three properties being internally flooded (see properties) with internal depths of 15-20mm. (p4) [PAG]

### Hazel Close, Haverhill
* Flooding was reported to Suffolk County Council following an intense localised rainfall event on 25.07.2021. The event overwhelmed drainage networks causing surface water/urban flooding in Hazel Close. (p3)

### High Street, Haverhill
* Flooding was reported to Suffolk County Council following the intense localised rainfall on 25.07.2021. The report attributes impacts to overwhelmed drainage and surface water/urban flooding. (p3)

### Harewood Terrace, Haverhill
* Flooding was reported to Suffolk County Council as part of the 25.07.2021 intense localised rainfall event. The event caused surface water/urban flooding when drainage capacity was exceeded. (p3)

### Greenwood Close, Haverhill
* Flooding was reported to Suffolk County Council after the 25.07.2021 intense localised rainfall. The description attributes impacts to overwhelmed highway drainage and resulting surface water/urban flooding. (p3)

### Elm Close, Haverhill
* Flooding was reported to Suffolk County Council on 25.07.2021 linked to an intense localised rainfall event that overwhelmed drainage, producing surface water/urban flooding affecting Elm Close. (p3)

### Chauntry Road, Haverhill
* Properties on Chauntry Road were reported flooded during the intense localised rainfall on 25.07.2021. The properties lie at a low point north of Sturmer Road; surface water runoff from higher ground and from the nearby industrial estate (via Falconer Road) overwhelmed highway drainage and inundated gardens/properties. An embankment between gardens and Stour Brook prevented rapid discharge to the watercourse, contributing to water pooling. (p3)

### Withersfield Road, Haverhill
* Withersfield Road was reported flooded during the intense localised rainfall on 25.07.2021. Surface water flows from surrounding higher ground and from the nearby industrial estate overwhelmed drainage capacity, leading to inundation of properties and surrounding low-lying areas north of Sturmer Road. The embankment adjacent to Stour Brook limited discharge to the river, resulting in runoff ponding. (p3)


## Topics without coordinates

### West Suffolk Council
* {general}: At Motts Field the Stour Brook is culverted under an embankment; excess flows from the culvert combined with surface water overland flows and travelled eastwards through rear gardens adjacent to the recreation ground, causing inundation of those rear garden areas. (p10) [TAB]
* {general}: Identified responsibility to consider creating a low bund/embankment along the eastern edge of the recreation ground to temporarily hold accumulations of floodwater and avoid through-flow into adjacent rear gardens; actions listed as pending. (p11)

### Suffolk County Council
* {01 Aug 2020 - 31 Aug 2020, 25 Jul 2021}: Received reports of flooding from multiple streets during the intense localised rainfall and initiated several Section 19 flood investigations; holds and maps historical pluvial flood incident records used to inform this investigation. (p4) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
* {01 Aug 2020 - 31 Aug 2020, 25 Jul 2021}: Quant: Flooding reported to SCC in 7 named streets (Hazel Close, High Street, Harewood Terrace, Greenwood Close, Elm Close, Chauntry Road, Withersfield Road); at least 3 properties internally flooded in the Sturmer Road investigation area. (p4) [IMG, PAG, MUL]

### Motts Field Recreation Ground
* Surface water accumulated within the recreation ground and flowed eastwards through rear gardens, funnelled between the embankment and properties, causing damage to outbuildings, garden areas and vehicles. (p5)
* Accumulations of floodwater in the recreation ground (Motts Field) flowed eastwards through rear gardens where Stour Brook is culverted under an embankment, combining with surface water overland flows and increasing flood volumes affecting nearby properties. (p9) [IMG]

### Anglian Water
* An Anglian Water surface water sewer serving the industrial estate to the south is identified with unknown capacity/condition; any surcharge from this asset would have contributed additional flows into Sturmer Road and the local highway flooding. (p8)
* {general}: The surface water sewer network in Falconer Road is to be investigated to establish whether its capacity or condition contributed to the flooding; investigations are pending. (p10) [TAB]

### Industrial Estate
* A large impermeable area (the industrial estate) generated excess runoff during the intense rainfall; this runoff contributed to surface water flooding when highway drainage was overwhelmed, with flows travelling from the estate down Falconer Road towards the low-lying properties. (p3) [IMG]

### Environment Agency
* {general}: Identified as an asset owner to work with West Suffolk Council on riparian maintenance of Stour Brook to reduce blockages and the potential for river flows to exacerbate overland flow into rear gardens. (p10) [TAB]

### HA
* {general}: The Highways Authority (SCC - HA) was tasked to investigate the highway drainage network in Sturmer Road and to carry out reactive cleansing to address possible insufficient capacity contributing to surface water flooding. (p10) [TAB]
