

# Corby flooding 13 Jun 2016

## Place names with coordinates

### Corby
* {13 Jun 2016, 16 Jun 2016}: At least one property in Corby (the Medical Practice on Cottingham Road) experienced internal flooding due to heavy short-duration rainfall and overwhelmed surface water drainage. (p7) [MUL]
* {13 Jun 2016, 16 Jun 2016}: Intense short-duration rainfall produced surface water run-off in Corby that caused flooding at a Medical Practice (basement staff room and storage area). (p12) [TAB, MUL]
* Property-level flooding occurred in Corby; investigations identified that no external sources of surface water were considered a factor and that surveys of privately owned drainage systems on the affected property are required to fully understand the flooding issue and identify possible solutions. (p15)
* Mapped area around the site in Corby shows convergence of surface water flow and a schematic area of flooding indicating overland/surface water and urban flooding affecting parts of the town during the incident. The inset map highlights a catchment with a history of a flood incident, showing hydrological connectivity relevant to flood impacts in Corby. (p29) [IMG]
* {13 Jun 2016, 16 Jun 2016}: Quant: 1 property (p7) [MUL]

### Cottingham Road, Corby
* Internal flooding occurred within the Medical Practice premises (areas of the building were flooded internally). (p11)
* The road was flooded during the heavy rainfall event, predominantly to the side of the carriageway nearest the Medical Practice. (p13)
* Cottingham Road was affected by the flooding incident; Main Rivers were not considered to have been a factor in the cause of flooding at this location. (p18)


## Topics without coordinates

### Medical Practice
* {13 Jun 2016, 16 Jun 2016}: Internal flooding of the medical practice caused by heavy short-duration rainfall; surface water drainage systems were unable to convey and dispose of rainwater, resulting in excess surface water collecting at a low point of the property and entering the building. (p7) [MUL]
* {13 Jun 2016, 16 Jun 2016}: Internal flooding entered the medical practice building; the property experienced internal flooding on more than one occasion. (p9) [PAG, MUL]
* {13 Jun 2016, 16 Jun 2016}: Internal flooding of the Medical Practice building resulting in internal flooding of the property on the dates listed; the property has experienced internal flooding on more than one occasion in the previous five years. (p10) [PAG, MUL]
* Internal flooding of the Medical Practice premises (internal areas were flooded). (p11) [PAG]
* {13 Jun 2016, 16 Jun 2016}: Surface water run-off overwhelmed the ACO channel drain to a small paved area and entered the property beneath the external door to the basement staff room, flooding the basement staff room and a storage area. (p12) [TAB, PAG, MUL]
* Surface water overwhelmed an ACO-type channel drain and entered the building beneath the external door to the basement staff room. The basement area was flooded throughout and the external paved area adjacent to the basement door was also flooded; water appeared to be coming from the outflow of the ACO channel drain. Surface water drainage systems were unable to convey and dispose of the rainwater, causing accumulation at a low point of the property. (p13) [PAG]
* Surface water flooding at the Medical Practice caused by heavy, short-duration rainfall. On-site surface water drainage systems were unable to convey and dispose of the rainwater, resulting in excess surface water collecting at a low point of the property. Potential contributing mechanisms identified were overflow of a roof rainwater gutter due to blockage; backflow from an ACO-type channel drain due to surcharging of the downstream network; or a blockage in the outfall pipe from the ACO-type channel drain preventing disposal of surface water. (p14) [PAG]
