

# Warwick flooding 12 Aug 2020

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Flood Investigation: Flooding In Warwick 12th August 2020<br>140\_Warwickshire County/140\_2020\_Warwickshire County/WCCC-453486374-168.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Warwick
* Flooding affected the town. (p1)
* Internal property flooding to commercial and residential properties within Warwick (internal flooding reported to properties). (p3)
* The Warwick area experienced flooding on 12 August 2020 which prompted a Section 19 investigation by the Lead Local Flood Authority. (p4)
* Internal flooding of properties within the town during the event on 12 August 2020; highway flooding reported in the town. Flooding affected commercial frontages and streets in the investigation area. (p7) [IMG]
* Intense rainfall caused surface water to overwhelm highway and surface water drainage so flows followed surface water routes across the urban area. Surface water exceeded kerb heights and entered commercial and residential properties with level thresholds (internal flooding). Flow paths transported silt onto footpaths and into highway and sewer systems across Warwick on 12th August 2020. (p10) [PAG]
* The flooding event in Warwick produced surface-water flow paths across public highways and urban areas (for example flow from Priory Park onto the highway and a surcharged surface water sewer in Priory Road that damaged a manhole cover). (p11) [PAG, IMG]
* Surface water sewer systems surcharged in locations (notably Priory Road), damaging a manhole cover and surrounding highway. Highway drainage was overwhelmed in places, causing street flooding. Intense, wind-driven thunderstorm rain caused water ingress into some properties through poorly sealed windows and doors. (p12)
* Surface-water and sewer-related flooding inundated parts of Warwick (including the town centre and St John's Brook catchment), causing internal flooding of residential and business premises and surface-water ingress where finished floor levels were at footway level; highways and gullies were affected, and sewer infrastructure (Smith Street / Priory Road areas) required further investigation. (p13)
* Planning applications in the town centre were identified as connecting to the surface water sewer system upstream of the location where flooding occurred; the LLFA requires reduced surface water discharge rates (minimum 50PCSYM reduction where greenfield rates cannot be achieved) to reduce pressure on the sewer system that contributed to the August 2020 surface water flooding. (p14) [TAB, PAG]
* Quant: 9 reports of internal flooding; 5 commercial premises; 4 residential properties (p3)
* Quant: 5 commercial premises; 4 residential properties (p7) [IMG]
* Quant: 28 responses; 3 residential properties confirmed flooded; 1 residential property reported on site (p12)
* Quant: 4 residential properties (p13)

### St Johns, Warwick
* Highway at the junction flooded; bow waves from cars observed as drivers sped through flood water, increasing wave action. (p3)
* Highway flooding across the junction at the traffic lights (junction of St Johns, Smith Street and Priory Road) on 12 August 2020, with water over the entire highway and footway and up to the frontages of business premises; depths well in excess of kerb height. (p7) [PAG]
* Highway at the junction of St Johns, Smith Street and Priory Road flooded across the traffic-light junction and up to commercial frontages on St Johns. Floodwater covered the entire highway and footway and reached the frontages of business premises to depths well in excess of the kerb height; bow waves from vehicles driving through the floodwater contributed to internal flooding of premises during the 12 August 2020 event. (p8) [PAG]
* {01 Dec 2019 - 31 Dec 2019, 12 Aug 2020}: Surface water flows from surrounding streets cumulated at St Johns, causing local inundation of the highway and contributing to property flooding during 12 August 2020. Separately, flooding was reported along St Johns Brook upstream of the culverted section beneath the St Johns highway in December 2019; that report was passed to the Environment Agency as St Johns Brook is designated main river at that location. (p9) [PAG, MUL]
* Surface water flow paths (from Smith Street and Priory Road) cumulated at St Johns, causing surface water accumulation in the area. The St Johns Brook culvert passes beneath the highway here (though the brook was reported at low level), so surface water flows accumulated at this location during the 12th August 2020 event. (p10) [PAG]
* The junction with Coton Road experienced surface water flooding during the intense rainfall on 12th August 2020; existing gullies (and additional gullies previously installed) were insufficient, and vehicle bow waves increased kerb overtopping and ingress onto the highway. (p12)
* During the 12 August 2020 event surface water flooded the carriageway and footway in the St Johns area; multiple returns from residents reported highway flooding and gullies were overwhelmed leading to surface water build-up on the highway. (p13)

### Priory Road, Warwick
* Highway at the junction flooded (Priory Road forms part of the flooded junction). (p3)
* Part of the junction that was flooded on 12 August 2020; floodwaters covered the highway and footway and extended to business frontages. (p7)
* Surface water flow paths ran down Priory Road, carrying silt from Priory Park onto footpaths and carriageway; where properties had no threshold, surface water overtopped kerbs and entered buildings internally. (p9)
* Surface water sewer in Priory Road surcharged during the intense rainfall, damaging a manhole cover; overland flow from Priory Park issued onto the highway and the damaged manhole cover and adjacent highway were sectioned off on site. (p11) [PAG]
* Further investigation of the sewer system in Priory Road was recommended because of the likelihood of damage to a chamber (shown in Photo 2) reoccurring following the August 12 2020 event; the LLFA reported this for Severn Trent Water action. (p13)
* Surface water flooding led to damage/defect of a surface water manhole cover and chamber; repair of the manhole cover and chamber was completed following the August 2020 event. Highway surface water management actions (gully cleansing, kerb-raising investigation) were recommended to reduce carriageway flooding risk. (p14) [TAB]

### Snitterfield

### Smith Street, Warwick
* Highway at the junction flooded (Smith Street forms part of the flooded junction). (p3)
* Part of the flooded junction (with St Johns and Priory Road) on 12 August 2020; flooding covered carriageway and footway and reached commercial frontages. (p7)
* Part of the junction at Smith Street was inundated when the highway flooded across the traffic-light junction on 12 August 2020. Businesses and residents reported historic flooding of Smith Street, particularly prior to an upgrade of the surface water sewer system. (p8) [PAG]
* Observed surface water flow paths followed Smith Street toward St Johns, inundating highway and footpaths and contributing to downstream accumulation of floodwater at St Johns. (p9)

### Coten End, Warwick
* Highway drainage at the junction with St Johns was overwhelmed by the intense rainfall on 12th August 2020, causing surface water to overtop kerbs. Vehicles driving through the flooded junction created bow waves that increased surface water ingress and overtopping. (p12)
* Surface water flooding occurred at the StJohns/Coton Road (sic) junction during the 12 August 2020 event, producing carriageway/footway flooding and prompting prior installation of additional gullies; further measures (more frequent gully cleansing, suspension of parking during cleansing, and investigating raising kerb heights) were recommended. (p13)

### Exham Close, Warwick
* Exham Close is identified as part of the St. John's Brook catchment where options are being assessed to reduce flood risk to properties and businesses following the August 12 2020 event. (p13)

### Priory Park
* Overland flows issued from the higher ground in Priory Park onto the adjacent highway and footpaths; flows cut into footpaths and deposited silt onto the highway and into local sewer/highway drains, contributing to local surface water flooding and drainage blockages. (p9) [PAG]
* Overland flows issued from Priory Park onto the highway during the event, cutting into footpaths and washing silt onto footways and into the highway and surface water sewer network, contributing to surface water flooding and blockage of drainage on 12th August 2020. (p10) [PAG]
* Surface water from Priory Park issued onto the adjacent highway via an observed flow path (site photographs taken the day after the event show evidence of this flow path), contributing to highway flooding. (p11) [PAG, IMG]

### St Johns Brook
* Surcharging of surface-water systems discharging into the St Johns Brook culvert contributed to surface-water flooding in the catchment, increasing flood risk to properties and businesses within the St John's Brook catchment. (p13)

### Woodloes Estate
* Properties and businesses in the Woodloes Estate catchment were identified as being at risk following the August 12 2020 event; an Outline Business Case is being developed to assess measures to reduce flooding to these properties and businesses. (p13)


## Topics without coordinates

### Warwickshire County Council
* An officer from the WCC FRM team attended affected locations the day after the event to gather information on flooding extent and to offer support and guidance to those impacted by the surface water/highway flooding from 12th August 2020; on-site reports were collected and subsequent postal follow-up was issued to confirm affected properties. (p12)
* Quant: 28 responses; 3 residential properties confirmed flooded; 1 residential property reported on site (p12)

### Severn Trent
* The LLFA reported sewer-system issues observed during the investigation to Severn Trent Water and requested further investigation into potential sewer-related damage (including a chamber shown in Photo 2) that may have contributed to flooding in the area. (p13)
* Completed repair to a surface water manhole cover and chamber that were damaged/affected as part of the surface water flooding response. (p14) [TAB]

### WCC Highways
* Undertaking ongoing cyclic gully cleansing and investigating raising kerbs to reduce carriageway surface water flooding following impacts observed during the August 2020 event. (p14) [TAB]
