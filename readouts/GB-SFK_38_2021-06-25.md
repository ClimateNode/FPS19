

# Felixstowe flooding 25 Jun 2021

## Place names with coordinates

### Garrison Lane, Felixstowe
* Suffolk County Council (Lead Local Flood Authority) carried out a Section 19 investigation into a flood event affecting Garrison Lane (the subject of this report). (p1)
* Properties at a low point part way down the hill on Garrison Lane (IP11 7ST) were inundated when an intense, short-duration rainfall event produced overland/pluvial runoff that was funnelled by local topography (including Stour Avenue) toward the corner of Garrison Lane, causing flooding of properties to the west of Garrison Lane. (p3)
* A large volume of surface water flowed down Garrison Lane, overwhelming the highway drainage infrastructure and sending water southwards towards nearby properties; this runoff contributed to inundation of the nearby retirement housing complex and associated garages. (p5)
* Properties at a low point part way down the hill were inundated by rapid overland surface-water flow directed by local topography; emergency services pumped out basements and water largely receded after ~1 hour and mostly drained within 24 hours. (p6) [PAG]
* Surface water flowed along the carriageway, transporting and depositing sediments and debris adjacent to a storm drain; visible post-event traces document the flow path and low-point accumulation on the road surface. (p8) [IMG]
* {general}: A restriction in the existing 225 mm sewer between MH6304 and MH6101 has been identified as requiring upgrade to remove the restriction — action put forward to address sewer-related runoff/flooding issues affecting Garrison Lane. (p9) [TAB]
* A restriction in the existing 225 mm sewer (between MH6304 and MH6101) has been identified; an upgrade is proposed to remove this restriction as a measure intended to address sewer-related flooding and reduce sewer surcharges/overland flow along Garrison Lane. (p10)
* Quant: 9 No. of the flats flooded internally; 12 No. residents evacuated; 2 No. adjacent semi-detached residential properties impacted; internal flood depths up to 1.2m in access corridor and up to 0.6m in flats; basement external depths reached 1.5m and internal depths over 0.6m. (p5)

### Stour Avenue, Felixstowe
* Stour Avenue forms a shallow valley that funnels surface water (pluvial) runoff from surrounding areas toward the corner of Garrison Lane, contributing to the flooding that occurred at that low point. (p3) [IMG]
* Surface water flowed down Stour Avenue, crossed the railway line and flowed down the embankment, flooding the area from the north‑west and contributing to the inundation of the nearby retirement housing complex and properties. (p5)
* Forms a shallow valley and primary overland flow path that funnelled pluvial runoff from surrounding areas toward the corner of Garrison Lane, contributing to inundation of nearby properties. (p6) [PAG]
* {general}: Overland surface-water flows have been identified as affecting Stour Avenue, prompting evaluation of a potential SuDS retrofit to reduce overland flows. (p9) [TAB]
* A potential SuDS retrofit scheme is proposed to reduce overland flows (surface water runoff) affecting Stour Avenue; the scheme is to be evaluated and assessed to reduce surface-water flooding. (p10)
* Quant: 9 No. of the flats flooded internally; 12 No. residents evacuated; 2 No. adjacent semi-detached residential properties impacted; internal flood depths up to 1.2m in access corridor and up to 0.6m in flats; basement external depths reached 1.5m and internal depths over 0.6m. (p5)

### Felixstowe
* The report concerns a flood event in Felixstowe (Garrison Lane area) investigated by Suffolk County Council LLFA; the provided map shows a central waterway and blue overlays concentrated along roads and waterways within the urban area consistent with areas of interest for the investigation. (p1)
* A severe, short-duration rainfall event on 25.06.2021 caused widespread surface water flooding across much of Felixstowe; flooding was reported in multiple roads and properties to the west of Garrison Lane (low point on the hill) were inundated as overland flows were directed past the site. (p3)
* A severe, localised pluvial event impacted the majority of Felixstowe on 25.06.2021; flooding was reported to the local authority in multiple streets (including Undercliff Road, Platters Road, Melford Way, Langer Road, Charles Road, Garrison Lane and High Road West). (p6)

### Charles Road, Felixstowe
* Surface water (pluvial) flooding occurred during the intense short-duration rainfall; flooding was reported to SCC and properties/road areas were affected as drainage networks were overwhelmed. (p3)

Flooding was driven by an intense local rainfall event and topography that funnels runoff toward the low point on Garrison Lane/Charles Road; water began to recede after about one hour and had mostly drained away within 24 hours.
