

# Lower Bullingham flooding 26 Oct 2019 and Lower Bullingham flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Lower Bullingham<br>055\_Herefordshire County/02\_Hereford\_Lower\_Bullingham.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Lower Bullingham
* {26 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Parts of Lower Bullingham lie within Flood Zone 3 adjacent to the River Wye; the map and event table link key flood events (26 October 2019 and 16 February 2020) to the area, indicating fluvial flooding risk/impact to residential and industrial areas within the investigation oval. (p1) [IMG, MUL]
* {15 Feb 2020, 16 Feb 2020}: Fluvial flooding affected Lower Bullingham: roads (Lower Bullingham Lane / Bullingham Lane) were closed on 15 and 16 February 2020; properties have a history of flooding (including one property flooded in 1998) and some properties experienced flood durations of up to 4 days, indicating inundation influenced by the River Wye and smaller watercourses (Withy Brook, Red Brook). (p3) [PAG, MUL]
* {15 Feb 2020, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: one property; flood durations up to 4 days (p3) [PAG, MUL]

### Hereford
* {16 Feb 2020}: Central Hereford experienced very high river levels: the River Wye reached its highest recorded level at the Old Wye Bridge in February, producing a fluvial flood event affecting central Hereford. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: High River Wye levels were recorded in central Hereford (Old Wye Bridge) and these elevated river levels contributed to flooding impacts in the local area. (p2)

### Holme Lacy Road, Lower Bullingham
* {16 Feb 2020}: Affected by fluvial flooding where Withy Brook re-joined nearby; the road was closed to traffic on 16 February 2020. (p2)
* {16 Feb 2020}: Flooding exacerbated by backwater from the River Wye affected Holme Lacy Road and led to its closure to traffic. (p3)

### Lower Bullingham Lane, Lower Bullingham
* {26 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Shown on the local flood risk map within the Lower Bullingham investigation area and lies in proximity to Flood Zone 3 adjacent to the River Wye, indicating exposure to river (fluvial) flooding during the recorded events. (p1) [IMG, MUL]
* {15 Feb 2020}: Closed due to fluvial flooding; road closure recorded on 15 February 2020. (p2)

### Straight Mile, Lower Bullingham
* {26 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Located within the mapped investigation area in Lower Bullingham and identified on the flood risk map that shows Flood Zone 3 adjacent to the main river; therefore exposed to river (fluvial) flooding during the key events. (p1) [IMG, MUL]
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: Part of the strategic highway network reported as impacted during the fluvial flood event (disruption unspecified). (p2) [TAB]

### Watery Lane, Lower Bullingham
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: Water entered properties via Watery Lane during the event. (p2)
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: Quant: 2 accounts (p2)

### Bullingham Lane, Hereford
* {16 Feb 2020}: Closed to traffic due to fluvial flooding during the main flood event. (p2)

### Red Brook
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: Fluvial flooding occurred from Red Brook and contributed to flooding of low-lying ground and properties in the Lower Bullingham area. (p2)
* {15 Feb 2020, 16 Feb 2020}: Red Brook is identified as a smaller watercourse contributing to local, short-duration flooding; mitigation proposals include a flood storage area on the Red Brook and a flap valve to prevent River Wye backwater from exacerbating flooding at Holme Lacy Road. (p3) [MUL]
* {15 Feb 2020, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: less than a day (p3) [MUL]

### Withy Brook
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: Broke its left-hand bank, flooding low-lying adjacent ground; re-joined near Withy Close close to Holme Lacy Road and contributed to inundation of adjacent land. (p2)
* {15 Feb 2020, 16 Feb 2020}: Flooding from the Withy Brook is identified as a likely source of short-duration local flooding in the area; flood storage on the Withy Brook is proposed to mitigate downstream flooding. (p3) [MUL]
* {15 Feb 2020, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: less than a day (p3) [MUL]

### Old Wye Bridge
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: River Wye reached its highest recorded level at the Old Wye Bridge (indicator of the fluvial flood severity at central Hereford). (p2)


## Topics without coordinates

### River Wye
* {16 Feb 2020}: The River Wye reached its highest recorded level at the Old Wye Bridge in central Hereford in February, driving the fluvial flooding described in the report and affecting adjacent flood zones. (p1) [IMG]
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: Reached its highest recorded level at the Old Wye Bridge in central Hereford in February and contributed to exacerbating flooding from local watercourses (Red Brook and Withy Brook). (p2)
* {15 Feb 2020, 16 Feb 2020}: High River Wye levels exacerbated flooding in the Lower Bullingham area, contributing to longer flood durations (up to 4 days) and causing backwater that worsened flooding at Holme Lacy Road. (p3) [MUL]
* {15 Feb 2020, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: up to 4 days (p3) [MUL]

### Withy Close
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: Withy Brook re-joined near Withy Close after breaking its bank, flooding adjacent low-lying ground. (p2)

### Welsh Water
* {general}: Overflowing sewer/drain reports related to the flood event were recorded and are being addressed by Welsh Water; these overflows contributed to flooding reports. (p2)
* {general}: Quant: 2 accounts (p2)

### Care Home
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: Identified as critical infrastructure impacted by the fluvial flood event. (p2) [TAB]
