

# Syresham flooding 21 Nov 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Wappenham Road, Syresham, 21st November 2012<br>090\_West Northamptonshire/Syresham - November 2012.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Wappenham Road, Syresham
* {01 Jul 2007 - 31 Jul 2007, 17 Oct 2012, 21 Nov 2012}: Properties and gardens flooded: at least one property internally flooded and one garage flooded; gardens of multiple properties flooded. Measures were taken to prevent internal flooding following the incidents. (p9) [TAB, MUL]
* Surface water flowed along and overtopped kerbs on Wappenham Road, entered gardens and a garage, and reached the rear ditch. At least one property on Wappenham Road was internally flooded; doorways were defended using sandbags to try to prevent ingress. (p10) [PAG]
* Ponding filled the full width of Wappenham Road adjacent to the ordinary watercourse and Wappenham Road bridge, preventing drainage to the watercourse and causing surface water to overtop into a property garden; at least one property on Wappenham Road was internally flooded as a result of the ponding. (p12) [PAG]
* Surface water pooling/flow at the junction with The Hill, with water present on the road verge and carriageway near the junction. (p38) [IMG]
* {01 Jul 2007 - 31 Jul 2007, 17 Oct 2012, 21 Nov 2012}: Quant: three properties on Wappenham Road flooded (July 2007); property on Wappenham Road internally flooded (21/11/2012); garage of property on Wappenham Road flooded (21/11/2012); gardens of two properties on Wappenham Road flooded (21/11/2012); flooding of garden on Wappenham Road (17/10/2012) (p9) [TAB, MUL]
* Quant: 1 property internally flooded; 1 garage flooded; gardens of 2 properties flooded (p10) [PAG]
* Quant: 1 property internally flooded (p12) [PAG]

### The Hill, Syresham
* Surface water flowed down The Hill; surface-water from agricultural land ran down access tracks (including the access track at The Hill) towards Wappenham Road, contributing to local property flooding. (p10)
* Springs and groundwater surfaced in rear gardens and the access track; the ditch and pipework system at The Hill flowed strongly during the event, moving debris and affecting a property. Groundwater flow was observed beneath a garage foundation. (p11)
* During the flooding incident there was a blockage in the ditch system to the rear of No.4 The Hill (the blockage has since been cleared). (p12)
* Persistent emergence of groundwater beneath the tarmac causing continuous flow down the road surface; water rising through the surface course layer. Ditch at this location enters a pipe system. Groundwater/flow has undermined foundations at a garage on The Hill. (p38) [IMG, PAG]
* A ditch connected to the main watercourse entered a pipe system alongside a garage, causing erosion that has undermined the garage foundations and exposed the base of the building; evidence of ongoing or past water flow and risk to structural stability. (p39) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 1 property; 1 garage (p11)

### Syresham
* Excess surface water flowed overland to low points in the village, causing internal flooding of properties; at least one property experienced internal flooding. (p5) [PAG]
* Intense rainfall on a saturated catchment caused excess surface water to flow over ground to low points in the village. Internal flooding occurred to at least one property; flooding has been experienced on more than one occasion in the preceding five years. (p6) [PAG, MUL]
* At least one property in the village flooded internally on 21st November 2012; various other properties in the village were identified as being at high risk of flooding. Foul-water flooding and odours were reported in Abbey Road and High Street (areas within the village). (p8)
* Intense rainfall onto a saturated catchment overwhelmed existing drainage. Drainage systems were unable to cope, causing water to flow uncontrolled onto the highway and through private properties. Drainage was observed to be silted and blocked by debris; parts of historic drainage were in poor condition, unknown or removed, reducing conveyance to the ordinary watercourse and contributing to uncontrolled flows. (p17)
* Surface water from higher ground was not collected by historic drainage (which are in poor condition or removed) and flowed uncontrolled onto the public highway and into private properties, causing flooding of the highway and properties in the village. (p18)
* Quant: at least 1 property; internal flooding experienced on more than one occasion in the last 5 years (p5) [PAG]
* Quant: at least one property (p6) [PAG, MUL]
* Quant: 1 property (p8)

### Wappenham
* Surface water ponding occurred in multiple locations in the village: main ponding on Wappenham Road adjacent to the bridge (leading to at least one internal property flood), ponding in fields at the north of the village which overtopped into a Wappenham Road garden, and ponding reported at road junctions and bends (including near Long Meadow Farm). (p12) [PAG]
* Quant: 1 property internally flooded (p12) [PAG]

### Broad Street, Syresham
* {01 Jul 2007 - 31 Jul 2007}: Properties flooded on Broad Street during a recorded incident. (p9) [TAB]
* Surface water flowed down Broad Street during the event and collected in a large pond in the highway adjacent to the ordinary watercourse. (p10)
* {01 Jul 2007 - 31 Jul 2007}: Quant: two properties on Broad Street flooded (July 2007) (p9) [TAB]

### Church End, Syresham
* Surface water flowed down Church End and collected in a large pond in the highway adjacent to the ordinary watercourse in the village. (p10)
* Road gullies in Church End were reported to allow water to flow around them rather than into them during rainfall, leading to surface water on the highway. (p12)

### High Street, Syresham
* Ponding in the highway occurred on High Street immediately north of Syresham C of E Primary School; further west surface water rose out of the road at gullies and tarmac cracks during heavy rain; ponding was also reported at the junction of High Street and Main Road to the south of the village. (p12)
* Groundwater emergence beneath the tarmac and resulting surface water on the carriageway edge adjacent to the school; the location is documented as an area prone to flooding during the 21 November 2012 flood. (p37) [IMG]

### Abbey Road, Syresham
* Properties experienced foul water flooding and associated odours on Abbey Road. (p8)

### High Street, Northampton
* Properties experienced foul water flooding and associated odours on High Street. (p8)

### Bell Lane, Syresham
* Surface water flowed down Bell Lane during the event and contributed to pooling in the highway adjacent to the watercourse. (p10)

### Main Road, Syresham
* Ponding reported at the junction of High Street and Main Road to the south of the village; road gullies were reported to pass water around them rather than into them during rainfall, causing highway ponding at the junction. (p12)

### Syresham Primary School
* Surface water and groundwater emergence occurred on High Street immediately adjacent to the school's boundary, with the area recorded as prone to flooding during the 21 November 2012 event (affecting access/grounds adjacent to the school edge). (p37) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Syresham Catchment
* Significant rainfall fell on the Syresham catchment on 21 November 2012, producing saturated ground and generating surface-water overland flow that contributed to flooding in the village. (p10)

### Leyland House
* Surface water from agricultural land flowed down the access track adjacent to Leyland House towards Wappenham Road during the overland flow event. (p10)

### Wappenham Road Bridge
* The main area of flooding was adjacent to the ordinary watercourse and the Wappenham Road bridge; ponding at this location filled the full width of Wappenham Road and was unable to drain to the watercourse sufficiently quickly. (p12)

### Long Meadow Farm
* Ponding in the highway was reported on Wappenham Road to the north of the village on the bend adjacent to Long Meadow Farm. (p12)

### Environment Agency
* Involved in the local response to surface water / groundwater emergence flooding in the village: to be consulted on consenting for works on ordinary watercourses and to instate, train and liaise with a Flood Warden to support production of a village community flood plan. (p19)
