

# Retford flooding 01 Jan 2024 - 31 Jan 2024

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Retford – Storm Henk, January 2024<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/retford-s19-storm-henk-jan-2024.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Retford
* Retford is the town subject of this Section 19 investigation into flooding associated with Storm Henk (January 2024). (p1)
* Localized internal flooding of residential properties in the southern part of the town, notably on Cobwell Road, Darrell Road, Meden Way and Poulter View. (p2) [IMG]
* Flood Warning issued for the River Idle at West Retford; Environment Agency installed pumps at Grove Lane and Blackstope Lane which were operated 24 hours a day for three days as flood waters receded; EA and partners conducted patrols and cleared blockages; multi‑agency community drop-in and engagement with affected communities. (p6) [PAG]
* Retford was affected by flooding associated with the River Idle and local watercourses; planned actions include a review of pumping strategy, River Idle de-silting and conveyance/tree works through the town centre and downstream, and Retford Beck culvert renewal/replacement to address flooding impacts. (p7)
* Quant: 5 properties (p2) [IMG]

### River Idle
* High River Idle levels contributed to further rising of local watercourses and are understood to have contributed to flooding that affected properties (including gardens/outbuildings and internal flooding at nearby streets). It is also implicated in flooding affecting Meden Way and Poulter View (investigation ongoing as to overtopping vs drainage discharge failure). (p3) [PAG]
* River levels rose during Storm Henk and are implicated in the local flooding. The river reached 1.36 m at the River Level gauge at Ordsall; it is currently unclear whether overtopping occurred or whether drainage discharge issues were the primary cause of the internal property flooding on nearby streets. (p4) [PAG]
* Rise in flows on the River Idle prompted a Flood Warning for West Retford and Ordsall and led to EA flood patrols, clearance of blockages, pump installations and a multi‑agency response. (p6)
* The River Idle is identified as the source of flooding affecting Ordsall and Retford; planned interventions include modelling of interactions with Carr Dyke, de-silting through the town centre and downstream, conveyance and tree works, and investigations into upstream flood storage to reduce future flooding. (p7)
* Quant: 1 property on Darrell Road; 1 property on Meden Way; 1 property on Poulter View (p3) [PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* As Lead Local Flood Authority (NCC) responded to River Idle flooding: provided 24-hour emergency planning support; closed roads to promote safety and avoid additional flood damage; delivered sandbags to affected areas; supported district/borough partners and shared resources; investigating source of flooding on Cobwell Road. (p6)

### Ordsall
* The River Idle gauge at Ordsall recorded a rise to 1.36 m during Storm Henk; this measurement is used in assessing whether local flooding (affecting Meden Way and Poulter View) was due to river overtopping or drainage issues. (p4)
* Named in the Flood Warning for the River Idle (issued 20:32 on 2 January 2024); subject to EA patrols and community engagement; attended by multi‑agency drop‑in event to gather impact information. (p6)
* Ordsall experienced flooding related to the River Idle; the Environment Agency is investigating upstream storage options that may reduce flooding affecting Ordsall (and Retford). (p7)

### Grove Lane, Retford
* The Environment Agency installed and operated pumps at Grove Lane in anticipation of forecasted rain; pumps were run continuously while floodwater receded, indicating the lane experienced floodwater requiring pumping. (p6)
* Retford Beck culvert renewal / replacement proposed for Grove Lane to address issues arising from the flooding incident. (p7)

### Blackstope Lane, Retford
* The Environment Agency installed and operated pumps at Blackstope Lane in anticipation of forecasted rain; pumps were run continuously while floodwater receded, indicating the lane experienced floodwater requiring pumping. (p6)
* Retford Beck culvert renewal / replacement proposed for Blackstope Lane to address issues arising from the flooding incident. (p7)

### Carr Dyke
* Raised levels in the Carr Dyke (driven by rainfall and saturated ground) contributed to flooding into gardens, outbuildings and internal flooding of one property on Darrell Road. (p3)
* Quant: 1 property (p3)

### Retford Beck
* Retford Beck is targeted for culvert renewal/replacement (serving Grove Lane and Blackstope Lane) as part of recovery works to address flooding impacts associated with local watercourse conveyance in Retford. (p7)


## Topics without coordinates

### Environment Agency
* Issued Flood Warnings using the national Flood Warning System and managed main rivers and associated flood risk as part of the response to river flooding. (p5)
* Issued a Flood Warning for the River Idle at West Retford and Ordsall (20:32 on 2 January 2024); instigated flood patrols and cleared blockages; installed and operated pumps at Grove Lane and Blackstope Lane (24 hours a day for 3 days); coordinated community information officers and organised a multi‑agency drop‑in event. (p6) [PAG]
* Flooding from the River Idle and local watercourses in the Retford/Ordsall area prompted the Environment Agency to review pumping strategy for Retford, investigate upstream flood storage options, and plan recovery works (de-silting, conveyance and tree works, culvert renewal) to reduce future flood risk. (p7)

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Responded to the major flooding incident: initiated a Community Flood Recovery Grant for individuals affected by internal flooding; attended sites to verify internal flooding and provide support, guidance and advice; gathered and fed site information to RMAs and partners; collated and published verified flood impact data; visited affected residents and businesses to establish flooding sources and severity; chaired/attended Strategic/Tactical/Recovery coordination meetings; provided 24-hour Emergency Planning team support; led procurement and delivery of DEFRA Property Flood Resilience repair. (p5)
* As Lead Local Flood Authority, provided 24-hour support via Emergency Planning; delivered sandbags to affected areas and communities; closed roads across the county to protect at‑risk areas; shared resources with district/borough partners; used communications channels to update the public and attended a public drop-in in Retford in response to the flooding. (p6)

### Bassetlaw District Council
* Provided emergency response support for the flooding: participated in LRF strategic/tactical/recovery coordination, actively deployed sandbags and aquasacs to vulnerable premises and residents, maintained community resilience stores for access to sandbags/aquasacs, cleansed detritus from roads/car parks/paths, and promoted/administered recovery grants and council tax discount schemes; engaged in the s.19 Flood Investigation. (p6)
* Flooding within the district (notably Retford and Ordsall) has led the council to establish a dedicated working group reporting to Cabinet, lead a multi-agency flood communications group, and coordinate recovery and lobbying actions to address local flood risk. (p7)
