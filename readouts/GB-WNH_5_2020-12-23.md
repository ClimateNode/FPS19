

# West Northamptonshire flooding 23 Dec 2020

Reference document:<br>2020 Flood Investigations<br>090\_West Northamptonshire/2023\_west\_northamptonshire.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Yelvertoft
* Internal flooding of buildings occurred during a single flood incident. (p11) [PAG]
* The Flood Alleviation Scheme on Yelvertoft Brook was exceeded and the Yelvertoft Brook culvert through the village was overtopped, producing heavy overland surface water flows onto Crick Road (south of High Street) and elsewhere in the village. Two separate areas of flood water on Crick Road and Swinnertons Lane deepened, met on High Street and formed a single body of water covering Crick Road, Swinnertons Lane and High Street. Flood waters deepened further and flowed towards properties, entering the lower-level rear doorway of a property on High Street; surface water flowing along School Lane from Stanford Road/High Street deepened at low points and caused internal flooding at a property on School Lane. (p12)
* The village experienced flooding caused by intense heavy rainfall; watercourses and drainage systems were overwhelmed, contributing to inundation of streets (Crick Road, Swinnertons Lane, High Street) and internal flooding of properties. (p13)
* Quant: more than five buildings (p11) [PAG]
* Quant: two separate areas of flood water; 3 streets covered by a single body of water (Crick Road, Swinnertons Lane, High Street); 1 property on High Street (water entered lower-level rear doorway); 1 property on School Lane (internal flooding) (p12)
* Quant: 1 property (High Street); 1 property (School Lane) (p13)

### Patricks Lane, Deanshanger
* {01 Dec 2020 - 31 Dec 2020}: The site (Patricks Lane/The Green) has flooded on multiple occasions over the past two decades, with the most detrimental event occurring in 2020. (p8)
* Internal flooding of buildings on Patricks Lane/The Green; properties experienced internal floodwater during the incident. (p9) [PAG]
* By 15:15 residents were wading through flood water on Patricks Lane. One of the twin culverts beneath Patricks Lane was partially blocked prior to the rainfall, contributing to restricted conveyance and local flooding. (p10)
* Quant: more than five buildings (p9) [PAG]
* Quant: 600 mm water depth in places (p10)

### Northampton
* A property in Northampton (on Ring Way) experienced internal flooding during the flood incident investigated. (p14) [PAG]
* Area of Northampton (specifically the Ring Way area) experienced intense heavy rainfall leading to surface water/overland flow flooding. Rear garden ponding occurred and at least one property experienced internal flooding when water deepened against doorways. (p15) [PAG]
* Quant: 300-400mm standing water depth (externally ponded at rear of property) (p15) [PAG]

### Park End
* {2020}: Once water depth exceeded the kerb height, runoff spilled over the verge and ponded against a building (the flooded/affected property). (p8)
* {2020}: Quant: 1 property (p8)

### Croughton
* The area experienced flooding caused by intense heavy rainfall; local drainage systems were unable to collect and convey rainwater effectively, resulting in ponding and spillover at localised low points within the catchment (contributing to flooding at Park End). (p7)

### Deanshanger
* Flooding occurred following intense heavy rainfall over a short period of time. (p11)

### Crick Road, Yelvertoft
* Overland surface water flow occurred onto Crick Road (south of High Street) when the Flood Alleviation Scheme on Yelvertoft Brook was exceeded and the brook culvert overtopped. Flooding on Crick Road deepened during the day, met flood water from Swinnertons Lane and High Street to form a single body of water, and produced flows toward nearby properties. (p12)
* Flooded by water from the breach of Yelvertoft Brook; formed standing water which later merged with other bodies of water and covered nearby streets; flood depth on Crick Road was approximately 600 mm and floodwater flowed from Crick Road toward the rear of a property on High Street. (p13) [PAG]
* Quant: two separate areas of flood water; 3 streets covered by a single body of water (Crick Road, Swinnertons Lane, High Street) (p12)
* Quant: approximately 600 mm depth (p13) [PAG]

### Wood Burcote, Towcester
* Internal flooding affected buildings in the settlement during a single flood incident; internal flooding occurred to properties in close proximity. (p16)
* Water ponded in the courtyard where most of the damage occurred; local drainage systems across the catchment were unable to collect and convey rainwater effectively. Alterations were underway to open up the courtyard to allow water to pass through external areas more easily, intended to reduce flood depth if a similar event occurs. (p18)
* Quant: more than five buildings (p16)
* Quant: water depths of up to 900mm (p18)

### Burcote Road, Towcester
* Surface water runoff from higher agricultural land exceeded or bypassed ditches and flowed overland along the local valley, crossed Burcote Road and flowed through the adjacent property into a west courtyard where water ponded (most damage occurred). Floodwater also flowed north through the property into a ditch and entered several agricultural buildings. (p17)
* Quant: 1 property; water depths up to 900 mm (p17)

### Swinnertons Lane, Yelvertoft
* A separate area of flood water on Swinnertons Lane deepened throughout the day, later meeting floodwater from Crick Road to form a single body of water that covered Swinnertons Lane and contributed to flows towards properties. (p12)
* Became part of a body of standing flood water formed after the breach of Yelvertoft Brook; localised bowl on Swinnertons Lane experienced deep standing water. (p13)
* Quant: depth of flood water in excess of one metre (p13)

### Ring Way, Briar Hill
* Internal flooding of a building on Ring Way (internal flooding of a building occurred as part of the flood incident investigated). (p14) [MUL]
* Surface water/overland flow flooding on and around Ring Way caused by intense, short-duration rainfall and possible exceedance of local drainage. Water flowed from the car park, footways and grass open space from south to north towards the property; water ponded at the rear of the affected property and deepened against doorways, causing internal flooding. (p15)
* Quant: 300-400mm standing water depth (externally ponded at rear of property) (p15)

### The Green, Deanshanger
* Internal flooding of buildings on Patricks Lane/The Green during the flood incident. (p9) [PAG]
* Quant: more than five buildings (p9) [PAG]

### High Street, Deanshanger
* A manhole cover on High Street was surcharging with water bubbling out (initial event). Surface water flowed across the street as drainage was overwhelmed and discharge was restricted, contributing to local inundation. (p10)
* Quant: 600 mm water depth in places (p10)

### High Street, Yelvertoft
* Separate floodwaters from Crick Road and Swinnertons Lane met on High Street forming a single body of water that deepened and caused flows towards properties; at least one property on High Street had water enter the lower-level rear doorway from flows originating on Crick Road and High Street. (p12) [PAG]
* Water flowed from Crick Road and High Street to the rear of a property where it entered the lower-level rear doorway; standing floodwater (from the breach of Yelvertoft Brook) covered areas of High Street as part of a combined body of water. (p13) [PAG]

### Park End, Croughton
* Surface water flowed to a low spot on the carriageway across the frontage and access to properties; as water depth increased it flowed over the highway verge via the access path, exceeded the drainage network capacity and caused internal flooding to the property's entrance hall via the front door and seepage through walls. Standing water occurred on the carriageway, verge and path serving the property. (p7) [PAG]

### School Lane, Yelvertoft
* Surface water flowed west-to-east from Stanford Road/High Street, turned north to flow along School Lane, deepened at low points and flowed towards a property causing internal flooding. (p13)


## Topics without coordinates

### Yelvertoft Brook
* The capacity of the Yelvertoft Brook culvert through the village was exceeded, causing overtopping of the upstream watercourse on Crick Road south of High Street and generating heavy overland flows toward roads and properties (contributing to water entering a property's rear doorway on High Street and internal flooding on School Lane). (p12)
* A breach of Yelvertoft Brook released water that flooded Crick Road and Swinnertons Lane as two separate bodies of standing water which merged and covered Crick Road, Swinnertons Lane and High Street; the quantity of rainfall exceeded the capacity of the Flood Alleviation Scheme on Yelvertoft Brook. (p13)
* Quant: 1 property (rear doorway entry on High Street); 1 property (internal flooding on School Lane) (p12)

### Kings Brook
* Kings Brook water levels rose and the brook overtopped, contributing to flooding of adjacent areas and driving surface water flow southwards. (p10)
