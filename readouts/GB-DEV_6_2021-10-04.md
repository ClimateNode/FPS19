### Exeter District

Internal flooding depths for some properties reached approximately 100mm; other properties experienced minor dampness and flooded garages. The road network in Exeter was also affected (e.g. Longbrook Street). Historic flood incidents in Exeter are also recorded (see quant). (p4) [TAB, PAG, MUL]

### Devon
* Heavy rainfall between 4 and 5 October 2021 caused flash flooding across Devon, producing internal property flooding across the county. (p1)
* County-wide surface water flooding during the event on 4–5 October 2021, causing property flooding across multiple towns and villages (see town-level entries). The incident map and table show concentrated impacts in Exeter and several coastal and inland settlements. (p2) [IMG]
* Disruption to highways across Devon: roads and walkways impassable due to flood water and secondary effects of heavy rainfall (e.g. mud slides). Surface water from an intense, short-duration localised storm overwhelmed drainage systems adjacent to properties; the event exceeded system capacity (no evidence of blockage). (p5)
* Quant: 25 properties (p1)
* Quant: 25 properties flooded (total across county); Exeter 9; Blackawton 4; Dartmouth 3; South Huish 2; Chulmleigh 1; Combe Raleigh 1; Dawlish 1; Honiton 1; Kingskerswell 1; Ottery St Mary 1; Strete 1 (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 14 properties flooded across the listed areas (13 residential, 1 doctor's surgery) (p5)
