

# Stretham flooding August 2014

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Stretham<br>021\_Cambridgeshire County/021\_2014\_Cambridgeshire County/Flood\_Investigation\_Report\_Stretham.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Stretham
* Central Stretham — specifically the area around Chapel Lane — was affected by a localized flood; the report does not provide quantified extent or damage. (p2) [IMG]

### Chapel Lane, Stretham
* Chapel Lane experienced localized flooding as the focus of the reported incident; extent and specific impacts are not quantified in the report. (p2) [IMG]
* Gullies on Chapel Lane were identified as capacity-limited and implicated in the surface water flooding incident; the Highways Team cleared the gullies on Chapel Lane as a remedial action. (p3) [TAB]

### High Street, Stretham
* Gullies on High Street were identified as capacity-limited and implicated in the surface water flooding incident; the Highways Team cleared the gullies on High Street as a remedial action. (p3) [TAB]

### Newmarket Road, Stretham
* Sections of Newmarket Road had gullies cleared by the Highways Team; ECDC arranged for the Awarded watercourse along Newmarket Road to be cleared (reported completed October 2014). These works were undertaken in response to a surface water flooding incident believed to be caused by gully capacity issues. (p3) [TAB]
