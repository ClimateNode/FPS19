

# Lancaster flooding 04 Aug 2020 - 05 Aug 2020

## Place names with coordinates

### Lentworth Drive, Bailrigg
* Lentworth Drive in Lancaster was impacted by the flooding of 4/5 August 2020 and is the subject location of the Section 19 investigation. (p1)
* Following the river overflow, a temporary bund was installed along the boundary between the Lentworth Drive carriageway and the adjacent open land to provide a buffer against further river flooding; a permanent bund was later installed by the Environment Agency (2023). (p3)
* Homes on Lentworth Drive were flooded overnight as a result of intense rainfall on 4–5 August 2020. (p4)
* Burrow Beck overtopped/overflowed in the area between Newlands Avenue, Lentworth Drive and Whinfell Drive, causing localised inundation of nearby properties. Firefighters assisted moving ground‑floor residents to safety and pumping efforts were undertaken; evacuations included residents from a care home to a temporary rest centre. Road closures were put in place and multi‑agency response worked on reducing water levels; no injuries reported. (p5)
* Floodwater from Burrow Beck flowed out of bank and across open ground onto Lentworth Drive and then entered nearby residential premises. (p6)
* Burrow Beck overtopped its banks after an extreme deluge and, with channel capacity reduced by accumulated debris, flooded into residential properties on Lenthworth Drive; properties showed water marks and some subsidence and a temporary evacuation of residents was required. (p8) [IMG]
* Floodwaters from Burrow Beck overflowed into Lentworth Drive on the night of 4–5 August 2020 and entered local residential properties, resulting in the temporary evacuation of residents. Afterwards, obstructions were removed and a permanent bund was constructed east of Lentworth Drive to provide future protection. (p9) [IMG]
* Quant: 2 multi-storey residential properties; 10 domestic properties; 50 people (p5)
* Quant: 29 residents evacuated (p8) [IMG]

### Lancaster
* Lancaster experienced flooding during the 4/5 August 2020 event (subject of the Section 19 investigation). (p1)
* Heavy overnight rainfall in the Lancaster area caused Burrow Beck to rise and overflow in the south Lancaster area, with floodwater reaching Lentworth Road and entering a number of homes overnight. (p3)
* Homes in Lancaster (specifically at Lentworth Drive) were flooded overnight on 4–5 August 2020. (p4)
* Serious localised flooding overnight 4–5 August 2020 at Hala in South Lancaster close to Burrow Beck. Residents (including from a care home) were evacuated to a temporary rest centre; firefighters assisted moving ground-floor residents to safety; pumping and other measures were used to control water levels; road closures were in place. (p5)
* Overnight from 4–5 August 2020 properties in Lancaster district were affected by flooding driven by river overflow. Reported impacts include internal flooding of residential properties and flooding of a care home resulting in displaced residents. (p6) [TAB]
* Within Lancaster, Burrow Beck was overwhelmed by an extremely heavy deluge on 4–5 August 2020; the ensuing river overflow caused localised flooding into Lentworth Drive and affected nearby residential properties. (p9)
* Quant: 2 multi-storey residential properties; 10 domestic properties; around 50 people (p5)
* Quant: 10 residential internal properties; 3 residential external properties; 1 care home (p6) [TAB]

### Burrow Beck
* Flows in Burrow Beck increased rapidly during heavy overnight rainfall and, aided by a series of channel obstructions, the river overflowed its banks. The overflow carried water onto Lentworth Road and into nearby homes. (p3)
* Overflowed its banks near Lancaster (reported as due to an obstruction), causing a localised flooding incident close to Hala. Multiple properties were flooded and emergency services evacuated residents and undertook pumping to reduce water levels; road closures were put in place. (p5)
* The main river Burrow Beck was carrying high volumes of storm water and its channel was obstructed by excessive debris downstream of a local pedestrian footbridge. The river flowed out of bank, across open grass and onto Lentworth Drive before entering nearby residential premises. (p6)
* On the night of 4–5 August 2020 an extremely heavy deluge overwhelmed the capacity of Burrow Beck. The river's channel capacity had been temporarily reduced by a collection of loose debris and, combined with very high flows, the river overflowed its banks and discharged into Lentworth Drive, causing flooding to nearby residential properties. Obstructions were urgently removed to restore flow. (p9)
* The watercourse at the location of the flooding incident; its conveyance function and maintenance were central to the event. The report highlights the need to respect channel capacity and avoid obstruction by loose debris, and describes Environment Agency maintenance activity on the channel in the reach involved in the incident. (p10)
* Quant: 2 multi-storey residential properties; 10 domestic properties; around 50 people (p5)
* Quant: 10 residential internal properties; 3 residential external properties (p6)
* Quant: 2.6 km maintained section (p10)

### Lancaster City Council
* Carried out site investigations after the flood, removed obstructions from the Burrow Beck channel (including a footbridge identified as an obstruction) and later replaced the footbridge to restore pedestrian access; supported local mitigation measures. (p3)
* Council officers attended a serious flooding incident overnight (4–5 Aug 2020) at Hala; organised evacuation of residents from a care home to a temporary rest centre and alerted other authorities to investigate an apparent river bank overflow caused by an obstruction. (p5)
* Responded to flooding in Lancaster (river Burrow Beck overflow): brought in an excavator to aid management of flood waters; responded to flooding of a care home and supported displaced residents. (p6)
* {general}: Named as a relevant local flood risk management authority connected with the Lancaster flooding incident; the report states the council has no outstanding works or studies to deliver in connection with this incident. (p10)

### Lancashire Fire and Rescue Service
* Responded to flooding near Burrow Beck (05 Aug 2020), deploying appliances and crews to assist evacuations, move ground-floor residents to safety, support rest centre transfers, and conduct pumping to reduce water levels; worked alongside police, council, Environment Agency and housing group; no injuries reported. (p5)
* Emergency responders evacuated residents from properties flooded by Burrow Beck overtopping. (p8) [IMG]

### Environment Agency
* Investigated the incident and ensured obstructions were removed from the Burrow Beck channel following the heavy rainfall that caused the river to overflow; later installed a permanent bund barrier in 2023 as flood mitigation. (p3)
* Identified as a relevant flood risk management authority connected with the Lancaster flooding incident; Environment Agency officers carry out regular maintenance checks and manage aquatic and riparian vegetation on Burrow Beck to maintain the channel conveyance in the section implicated in the event. (p10)
