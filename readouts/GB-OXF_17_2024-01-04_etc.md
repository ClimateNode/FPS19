

# South Oxfordshire flooding 04 Jan 2024

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Flood Investigation Report: Henley-on-Thames and Goring, South Oxfordshire<br>095\_Oxfordshire County/S19-Henley-on-Thames-Goring-Report-v3.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### River Thames
* Flooding from the River Thames damaged landing stages and flooded the ground floors of the commercial properties in Henley and Goring. (p2)
* {2003, 2014, 04 Jan 2024}: Flooding from the River Thames produced high flood risk along its banks in the Goring/Henley area (functional floodplain). Business owners reported commercial properties in the mapped area were flooded in 2003 and 2014; the event on 4 January 2024 produced flooding reflective of 2014 but was less significant than 2003. (p5) [IMG, MUL]
* The River Thames channel running beside the commercial properties was overwhelmed by additional rainfall/flows on 4 January 2024 and flooded adjacent commercial premises in Henley-on-Thames and Goring, causing inundation of the businesses. Photographs indicate flood depth under 0.1 m. (p6) [IMG]
* The Thames was described as having its channel overwhelmed by additional exceptionally heavy rainfall, causing it to overflow and flood adjacent commercial properties. (p7)
* Quant: 2 commercial properties (p2)
* {2003, 2014, 04 Jan 2024}: Quant: both commercial properties (2) flooded in 2003 and 2014 (p5) [IMG, MUL]
* Quant: 2 commercial properties; flood depth under 0.1 m (p6) [IMG]
* Quant: flood depth under 0.1 m (at the commercial properties) (p7)

### Henley-On-Thames
* Landing stages were damaged and the ground floor of a commercial property in Henley was flooded by the River Thames. (p2)
* {2003, 2014, 04 Jan 2024}: Commercial property in the Henley-on-Thames site lies within EA Flood Zone 3/3b (functional floodplain) and was inundated by river (Thames) flooding. Business owners reported previous flooding in 2003 and 2014, and that the event on 4 January 2024 was similar to 2014. (p5) [PAG, MUL]
* A commercial property in Henley-on-Thames was flooded when the adjacent River Thames channel was overwhelmed by exceptionally heavy rainfall on top of antecedent saturated soils; photographic evidence suggests inundation depth was under 0.1 m. The business reported this event on 4 January 2024 as the worst since 2014 (but less severe than 2003). (p6) [IMG]
* Quant: 1 commercial property (p2)
* {2003, 2014, 04 Jan 2024}: Quant: 2 commercial properties; flood occurrences: 2003, 2014, 4 January 2024 (p5) [PAG, MUL]
* Quant: two commercial properties (across Henley-on-Thames and Goring) (p6) [IMG]

### Goring-On-Thames
* The ground floor of a commercial property in Goring was flooded and landing stages were damaged by flooding from the River Thames. (p2)
* {2003, 2014, 04 Jan 2024}: Commercial property in the Goring site lies within EA Flood Zone 3/3b (functional floodplain) and was inundated by river (Thames) flooding. Business owners reported previous flooding in 2003 and 2014, and that the event on 4 January 2024 reflected the 2014 flooding (but was less severe than 2003). (p5) [IMG, MUL]
* A commercial property in Goring was flooded when the adjacent River Thames channel was overwhelmed by the same period of heavy rainfall and saturated catchments, contributing to inundation of the premises on 4 January 2024; photographic review suggests flood depth was under 0.1 m. (p6) [PAG]
* Quant: 1 commercial property (p2)
* {2003, 2014, 04 Jan 2024}: Quant: 2 commercial properties; flood occurrences: 2003, 2014, 4 January 2024 (p5) [IMG, MUL]
* Quant: two commercial properties (across Henley-on-Thames and Goring) (p6) [PAG]

### South Oxfordshire
* Flooding from the River Thames damaged landing stages and flooded the ground floor of commercial premises in the district (specifically within Henley-on-Thames and Goring). Photographs and reports indicate internal flooding of businesses (ground floor) with limited depth. (p2) [TAB]
* Quant: 2 commercial properties; flood depth under 0.1 m (p2) [TAB]

### Oxfordshire
* The area crossed by Storm Henk received sustained rainfall in early January 2024 which contributed to conditions that led to the River Thames flooding affecting Henley and Goring on 4 January 2024. (p2)
