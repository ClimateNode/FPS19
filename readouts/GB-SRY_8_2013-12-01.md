### Surrey
* {24 Dec 2013}: A rest centre was set up in Dorking on 24 December 2013 to support people displaced by flooding. (p10) [PAG]

* The winter 2013/14 floods affected areas across Surrey. Lack of maintenance of riparian watercourses and ditches in Surrey contributed to the impacts of the winter 2013/14 flood event.
