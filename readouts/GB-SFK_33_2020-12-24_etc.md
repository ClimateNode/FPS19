

# Beccles flooding 24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020 and Beccles flooding 05 Apr 2021

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Beccles Quay (Fen Lane & Bridge Street) & Beccles Lido (Puddingmoor), Beccles<br>124\_Suffolk County/124\_2021\_Suffolk County/Section-19-Report-Beccles-Dec-2021(1).pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Beccles
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020}: The town of Beccles (investigation area covering Beccles Quay, Fen Lane, Bridge Street and Beccles Lido) experienced a flood incident that resulted in internal flooding meeting the criterion of five properties internally flooded in a single incident; there is ambiguity regarding the flood source/responsibility. (p2)
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020}: The majority of the northern part of Beccles (area between the River Waveney and the Beccles Town Drain) was impacted by floodwaters from multiple sources. Internal flooding occurred in properties; highway routes were impassable in places; high tide/tide locking raised river levels and contributed to flooding; flap valves and penstocks were closed (and one penstock reported infilled/non-functional), preventing surface water runoff discharging to the river. (p6) [PAG]
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020, 05 Apr 2021}: Flooding from multiple sources (pluvial, fluvial/tidal, rising groundwater and backed-up highway drains) caused some main highway routes in this part of Beccles to be impassable. (p7)
* {general}: The EA has initiated a project investigating flood risk to people and property in Beccles; the text describes high River Waveney flows causing seepage through flood boards and overtopping of embankments flooding low-lying areas, blocked/surcharging highway drainage inundating streets, and shallow groundwater entering properties. (p9) [MUL]
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020, 05 Apr 2021}: Anglian Water Services surface water sewer exceeded capacity and overflowed into the arterial watercourse (managed by BFCT); a nearby pumping station pumps out exceedance flows. Main river flooding increased the volume in the system, contributing to the above overflows and elevated flood risk in Beccles. These issues prompted recommendations such as deploying temporary pumps at Fen Lane, surveying watercourse and highway drainage assets, and producing an emergency flood plan. (p10) [TAB]
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020, 05 Apr 2021}: Winter flooding in Beccles prompted emergency response and longer-term flood risk management work: an emergency flood plan was produced and circulated; a long-term project was started to investigate people and properties at risk; consideration of a permanent surface-water pump as part of the Beccles Flood Risk Management Project; asset surveys (jetting and CCTV) of highway drainage commissioned. (p11)
* {01 Dec 2020 - 31 Dec 2020, 24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020}: Severe flooding in the town during the December 2020 event inundated streets, buildings, the quay and moored boats (notably at Fen Lane and the Quay). Floodwaters covered roads and open areas and encroached on buildings, demonstrating interaction of high river levels (River Waveney), overwhelmed surface water drainage and possible groundwater emergence. (p13) [IMG, PAG]
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020}: Beccles experienced flooding in December 2020 at locations including Fen Lane and the Quay from multiple sources (the River Waveney and surface-water/backflow). The event prompted inspections and maintenance of penstocks and flap valves, establishment of a temporary pump procedure, review of flood warning thresholds, and consideration of longer-term flood risk management measures such as replacing flood boards with flood gates. (p14) [IMG, PAG]
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020}: The December 2020 flood event affected residential and commercial properties in Beccles. Flood defences (including an existing flood wall) are present; proposals include replacing flood boards with flood gates. Five properties do not benefit from current defences and are identified as requiring Individual Property Flood Resilience (PFR) measures (e.g., waterproof external doors). (p15) [PAG]
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020}: Quant: 5 properties (p2)
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020}: Quant: Internal flooding experienced within at least 8 properties; approximately 21 properties reported to have been impacted in some form; floodwaters reached approximately 300 mm in depth in places. (p6) [PAG]
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020, 05 Apr 2021}: Quant: 300 mm depth (in places) (p7)
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020}: Quant: 5 properties (p14) [IMG, PAG]
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020}: Quant: 22 properties; 5 properties (p15) [PAG]

### Fen Lane, Beccles
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020}: Fen Lane is within the Beccles Quay investigation area where internal flooding occurred during a single incident; the event met the criterion of internal flooding of five properties in one incident and there is ambiguity about the source/responsibility. (p2)
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020}: Highway flooding was reported on Fen Lane; internal flooding was reported at The Maltings on Fen Lane. (p6)
* {prior to 2015}: SCC records indicate repeated surface water (pluvial) flood events have occurred in the Fen Lane area of Beccles. (p7) [MUL]
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020, 24 Dec 2026 - 26 Dec 2026}: Surface water runoff that could not discharge to the river backed up through highway drains and gullies and flooded onto Fen Lane. (p9)
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020, 05 Apr 2021}: Fen Lane is identified as a location where temporary pumps may need to be deployed to manage exceedance flows from the Anglian Water surface water sewer and associated overflow into the arterial watercourse; this indicates Fen Lane experiences flooding from sewer exceedance and increased main river flows. (p10) [TAB]
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020}: Severe inundation of Fen Lane during the December 2020 event: floodwater covered the road and surrounding open areas, encroached on adjacent buildings and the quay, and affected moored boats. (p13) [IMG, PAG]
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020}: Fen Lane was affected by the December 2020 flooding (river water backflow and surface-water/urban flooding). Penstocks at Fen Lane were inspected and cleaned to prevent backflow; flood wall and removable flood boards are in place protecting a car park; a temporary pump deployment procedure was established to help alleviate future flooding. (p14) [IMG, PAG]
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020}: Fen Lane has an existing brick flood wall and flood boards; viable options include maintaining the wall and replacing flood boards with flood gates. A procedure is in place for deploying a temporary pump to help alleviate flooding at Fen Lane, to be coordinated with the community (volunteer flood wardens/flood group). (p15) [PAG]
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020}: Quant: 5 properties (p2)
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020}: Quant: Floodwaters reached approximately 300 mm in depth in places (reported for some main highway routes in the area). (p6)
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020}: Quant: 5 properties (identified as not benefiting from current flood defences and requiring Property Flood Resilience measures) (p14)

### Suffolk

### River Waveney
* {general}: The River Waveney (tidally influenced at Beccles) is identified as a source of fluvial/tidal flooding that contributed to flooding locally, including the breach/damage to a private wall that permitted flow into the Beccles Lido area. (p7)

(p9) [MUL]


## Topics without coordinates

### Environment Agency
* {general}: The Environment Agency's brick flood wall in Beccles ties into adjacent private defences; the failure of the private section (6-7m gap) compromised the interconnected defence line, allowing river water to flood the low-lying area behind the wall (including the Lido and boatyard) and reducing the effectiveness of the defence system. (p8) [PAG, IMG]
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020, 05 Apr 2021}: Environment Agency engaged in response and recovery for the Beccles winter flooding: produced an initial assessment report summarising ways to improve flood risk management, investigated numbers of people and properties at risk as part of a longer-term project, and engaged with Beccles Town Council on emergency flood planning. (p11)
* {general}: The Environment Agency reviewed flood warning thresholds for the River Waveney after the December 2020 event; they note the EA flood warning system can only warn for river flooding, and that warnings for the December 2020 event were received after flooding had started in some locations because flooding occurred from multiple_sources. (p14)
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020}: Undertook an Initial Assessment of flood risk in Beccles; identified viable options including maintaining the flood wall at Fen Lane and replacing flood boards with flood gates; identified 5 properties that would require Individual Property Flood Resilience measures; developing business case and longer list of options and surveys to manage flood risk (including surface water) and planning capital projects for communities affected by December 2020 flooding. (p15) [PAG]
* {general}: Quant: 6-7m gap in adjacent private flood wall (p8) [PAG, IMG]
* {24 Dec 2020 - 29 Dec 2020}: Quant: 5 properties; 22 properties (p15) [PAG]
