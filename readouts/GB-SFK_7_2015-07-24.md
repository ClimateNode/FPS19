

# Lowestoft flooding 24 Jul 2015

## Place names with coordinates

### Waveney
* Kirkley Stream flooding is being addressed across the town (Lowestoft) with clearance works and options to improve flows; the Lowestoft Flood Management Project will consider longer-term improvements across the town to reduce flood risk. (p3) [PAG]

### Kirkley Stream
* S19 flood investigation covered the watercourse and clearance works were planned/undertaken (vegetation coppicing and removal of dense willow and shrubs, removal of overhanging branches, bulrush treatment) to restore channel capacity and reduce risk of debris snagging and blocking the channel. (p1)
* Vegetation, garden waste and other debris were snagging on vegetation and blocking the channel, contributing to blockages and local flooding risk. Clearance work to remove vegetation and garden waste was planned/undertaken to allow water to flow freely. Silt levels reported as low to moderate and generally accumulating near structures and at the toe of the channel where there are willows; silt removal not undertaken because it is not believed to be significantly contributing to flood risk. The flood storage area will not be cleared until further studies confirm whether that would reduce flood risk. (p2)

### Bloodmoor Roundabout
* The Environment Agency undertook clearance work upstream of Bloodmoor Roundabout (on the main river section) to increase flows in the stream; this clearance has partially solved the flooding/problem of blockages in that area. (p2)


## Topics without coordinates

### Environment Agency
* {01 Feb 2016}: Led and project-managed clearance works along Kirkley Stream to remove dense willow, shrubs and overhanging branches and spray bulrushes, to ensure the channel can flow freely and reduce the risk of debris snagging and blocking the channel (mitigating flood risk). (p1)
* The Environment Agency undertook clearance work on the Kirkley Stream (upstream of Bloodmoor Roundabout) to increase flows and reduce blockages; this action has partially addressed the flooding issue in that reach. The Agency is also a statutory consultee in planning matters related to flood risk. (p2) [PAG]
* Undertook clearance work on Kirkley Stream upstream of Bloodmoor Roundabout (main river section) to increase flows; this action partially solved the problem of reduced channel capacity and associated flooding caused by vegetation/debris blocking the stream. Further options being discussed as part of wider town flood management work. (p3) [PAG]

### Anglian Water
* Anglian Water is jointly funding initial clearance work and operates a trash screen at the junction of Tom Crisp Way and Southwell Road that catches debris flowing down the stream. Potential improvements to the trash screen are being reviewed and a telemetry monitoring system is being installed to warn when blockages occur, enabling more rapid debris removal and reducing chances of flooding caused by blockages. (p2) [PAG]
* Owns/operates a trash screen at the junction of Tom Crisp Way and Southwell Road that catches debris from Kirkley Stream; blockages at this screen have been identified as a cause of flooding. Anglian Water is reviewing improvements to the screen and installing a telemetry monitoring system to warn of blockages so debris can be removed more rapidly, reducing the chance of flooding from stream blockages. (p3) [PAG]

### Suffolk County Council
* Suffolk County Council is jointly funding and carrying out initial clearance work to remove vegetation and debris from the stream channel to prevent debris snagging and blocking the channel and thus reduce flood risk. The council asked residents not to replace removed garden waste and expects the initial work to take two to three weeks. (p2)

### Waveney District Council
* Responsible for maintenance of the channel adjacent to Meadow Park; confirms clearance work in this area is likely to take place in the autumn (after bird breeding season) to address flow capacity and reduce local flooding. Also acting as planning authority to ensure flood risk from new developments (discharges into Kirkley Stream) is considered in decisions. (p3) [PAG]
