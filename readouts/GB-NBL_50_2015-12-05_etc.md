

# Northumberland flooding 05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015, Northumberland flooding 26 Dec 2015 and Northumberland flooding 05 Jan 2016 - 06 Jan 2016

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Investigation of the Winter 2015/16 Floods<br>091\_Northumberland/Winter-2015-Flood-Report.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Northumberland
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015, 26 Dec 2015, 05 Jan 2016}: Widespread flooding across the county during the winter 2015/16 events (from heavy rainfall and rising rivers), including numerous incidents associated with the River Tyne rising to record levels; flood warnings issued for many locations in the county. (p5) [PAG, MUL]
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015}: Widespread flooding affected many communities across Northumberland on 05/12/2015 and 06/12/2015 (Storm Desmond), with many houses and businesses damaged by floodwater (internal depths reported up to about 1 metre in some properties). Several roads experienced flooding and closures; there were also isolated incidents elsewhere in the county (including a landslide). The River Rede overtopped at West Woodburn, flooding properties. (p6) [PAG, MUL]
* {26 Dec 2015, 05 Jan 2016 - 06 Jan 2016}: Multiple flooding impacts across the county during the winter 2015/16 events: parts of the county were affected by Storm Eva (river levels rose) and by Storm Frank (widespread surface water and ordinary watercourse incidents). The Wansbeck catchment flooding flooded one property and a catering van in Morpeth. Flood warnings/alerts were issued for the county during the events. (p7) [PAG, MUL]
* {winter 2015/16}: The Environment Agency, Northumbrian Water and Northumberland County Council co‑operated throughout and since the winter 2015/16 flood incidents in Northumberland to share information, deliver communications and carry out site investigations and recovery works. (p8) [PAG]
* {winter 2015/16}: Parts of the county experienced the winter 2015/16 flooding: flood alerts/warnings were issued for locations in Northumberland and some flood defences in the county were damaged (notably flood banks at Corbridge); recovery and permanent repair programmes were initiated. (p10) [PAG]
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015, 26 Dec 2015}: The River Tyne rose and breached its banks during the winter 2015/16 events, flooding numerous areas across the county; EA inspected and repaired defences and NW dealt with sewer flooding incidents as part of county-wide recovery. (p11) [PAG, MUL]
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015, 26 Dec 2015, 05 Jan 2016}: Quant: 3 major rainfall events (5–6 Dec 2015; 26 Dec 2015; 5 Jan 2016); 16 Flood Warnings issued in Northumberland; River Tyne reached its highest recorded levels in 10 areas. (p5) [PAG, MUL]
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015}: Quant: three properties (West Woodburn reported on 05/12/2015) (p6) [PAG, MUL]
* {26 Dec 2015, 05 Jan 2016 - 06 Jan 2016}: Quant: 4 flood warnings; 11 alerts; 12 Flood Alerts; 6 Flood Warnings; more than 30 towns or villages; 1 property; 1 catering van (p7) [PAG, MUL]
* {winter 2015/16}: Quant: 32 Flood Alerts; 26 Flood Warnings; over 3,500 properties protected by EA flood defences (p10) [PAG]
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015, 26 Dec 2015}: Quant: over 3500 properties protected by EA flood defences (p11) [PAG, MUL]

### River Tyne
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015}: During the Storm Desmond event (5–6 Dec 2015) the River Tyne experienced flows not observed since 1771 and contributed to widespread flooding across the north of England. (p3) [PAG]
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015}: The River Tyne rose to record‑breaking levels during the Storm Desmond event, resulting in numerous flooding incidents along its length in South Northumberland. (p5) [PAG]
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015}: Peak river levels associated with Storm Desmond produced river flooding at multiple Tyne locations on 05/12/2015 and 06/12/2015; these were record-high levels at several gauges and led to flooding impacts across the catchment. (p6) [TAB, MUL]
* {winter 2015/16}: Severe river flood risk was recorded during the winter 2015/16 period: 2 Severe Flood Warnings were issued for the River Tyne at Corbridge and Wellbank Riverside. (p10)
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015, 26 Dec 2015}: River Tyne rose and breached its banks, flooding numerous areas along the length of the river. (p12) [MUL]
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015}: Quant: approximately 16,000 properties affected across the north of England (p3) [PAG]

### Corbridge
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015, 26 Dec 2015}: Severe river flooding associated with the River Tyne on 05/12/2015 caused damage to houses and businesses (internal flooding with water depths reported up to around 1 metre in some properties) and required rescue of residents from upper floors. Corbridge experienced further flooding on 26/12/2015 with a small number of residential properties suffering repeat internal flooding. (p6) [TAB, PAG, MUL]
* {26 Dec 2015}: Repeat internal flooding to residential properties (residential properties experienced repeat internal flooding). (p7)
* {winter 2015/16}: Flood banks at Corbridge were damaged during the winter 2015/16 floods; a High Volume Pump was immediately deployed to assist clean-up and restore the area (and emergency repairs were carried out to flood defences). Diversions/one-way systems were used where landslips occurred and highway inspections and recovery works were undertaken. (p10) [PAG]
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015, 26 Dec 2015}: River Tyne/river-related flooding caused flood banks at Corbridge to be damaged; water rose to exceptionally high levels during the winter 2015/16 events and caused significant damage to properties and infrastructure in the area. (p11) [PAG, MUL]
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015, 26 Dec 2015}: During Storm Desmond (5–6 Dec 2015) and again during Storm Eva (26 Dec 2015) river levels rose and flooded the area; water rose to exceptionally high levels and caused a large amount of damage to both residential and business properties. (p12) [PAG, MUL]

### North England
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015}: Heavy rainfall from Storm Desmond across the North of England contributed to intense precipitation, record-breaking 24-hour rainfall in Carlisle and to river and surface-water flooding in the region. (p5)
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015, 26 Dec 2015}: The North of England was heavily affected by both events: Storm Desmond (5–6/12/2015) produced widespread river flooding (including exceptionally high River Tyne levels at multiple gauges) and Storm Eva (26/12/2015) also brought heavy rainfall and flooding, with the worst impacts noted in Cumbria, Lancashire and Yorkshire. (p6) [MUL]
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015, 26 Dec 2015}: Quant: 10 areas (River Tyne gauges) reached their highest recorded levels (p6) [MUL]

### Lancashire
* {26 Dec 2015}: Storm Eva (26/12/2015) produced heavy rainfall across the North of England; Lancashire is named as one of the areas worst affected by the event, with associated rises in river levels and flooding. (p6)

### Cumbria
* {26 Dec 2015}: Storm Eva (26/12/2015) brought heavy rainfall causing river levels to rise and flooding across the North of England, with Cumbria identified as one of the worst-affected areas. (p6)

### West Woodburn

* {26 Dec 2015}: Overtopping of the River Rede caused flooding of a property in the hamlet. (p7)
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015, 26 Dec 2015}: Quant: 3 properties; 1 property (p6) [PAG, MUL]
* {26 Dec 2015}: Quant: 1 property (p7)

### Haydon Bridge

(p6) [TAB]
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015}: Identified as one of the worst hit areas during the winter 2015/16 floods where water rose to exceptionally high levels and caused a large amount of damage to both residential and business properties. (p11) [PAG]

### Hexham

* {winter 2015/16}: A Rest Centre was opened in Hexham as part of the emergency response to the winter 2015/16 floods to support displaced or affected people. (p10)
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015}: During Storm Desmond (5–6 Dec 2015) river levels rose to exceptionally high levels and flooded the area, causing a large amount of damage to both residential and business properties. (p12) [PAG]

## Topics without coordinates

### Environment Agency
* {general}: Responded to Storm Desmond by issuing 24 Flood Warnings in the NE area (16 in Northumberland), including 2 Severe Flood Warnings at Corbridge. (p5)
* {winter 2015/16}: Co-operated throughout and since the winter 2015/16 flood incidents with Northumberland County Council and Northumbrian Water to share information, deliver joint communications, and carry out site investigations and recovery works; provided strategic overview and flood warning services (monitoring river levels, sea conditions) relevant to the floods. (p8) [PAG]
* {winter 2015/16}: Opened the North East Area Incident Room for the winter period; issued flood alerts and warnings across the county; issued Severe Flood Warnings for the River Tyne at Corbridge and Wellbank Riverside; inspected and made emergency repairs to flood defences damaged by the floods and developed a programme of permanent repairs to be delivered from summer 2016. (p10) [PAG]
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015, 26 Dec 2015}: Winter 2015/16 flooding caused damage to some EA flood defences, especially the flood banks at Corbridge; EA made immediate emergency repairs, inspected all other defences in the county and developed a programme of permanent repairs to be delivered from summer 2016. EA also carried out post-event data collection to validate and improve the flood warning system and inform modelling and economic assessments. (p11) [PAG, MUL]
* {winter 2015/16}: Quant: 54 Flood Alerts; 42 Flood Warnings; warnings issued to over 2,200 properties; 32 Flood Alerts and 26 Flood Warnings in Northumberland; 2 Severe Flood Warnings (for the River Tyne at Corbridge and Wellbank Riverside); over 3,500 properties protected by EA flood defences (p10) [PAG]
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015, 26 Dec 2015}: Quant: over 3500 properties protected by EA flood defences (p11) [PAG, MUL]

### Tyne Catchment
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015}: The catchment received an intense amount of precipitation during Storm Desmond causing the River Tyne to rise to record‑breaking levels and resulting in numerous flooding incidents along the river. (p5)
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015}: During Storm Desmond (5–6 Dec 2015) the River Tyne reached its highest recorded levels at multiple locations across the catchment, causing widespread river flooding. Many houses and businesses in the catchment were damaged by flooding; water depths of up to 1 m were reported within some properties. In Corbridge (within the catchment) residents had to be rescued from upper floors. (p6) [MUL]
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015}: Quant: 10 areas reached highest levels since records began; water depths up to 1 m in some properties (p6) [MUL]

### Northumberland County Council
* {winter 2015/16}: As Lead Local Flood Authority, NCC investigated the winter 2015/16 flood incidents, co-operated with the Environment Agency and Northumbrian Water to share information, delivered joint communications and jointly carried out site investigations and recovery works. As Highway Authority it managed road drainage responsibilities and handled reports of blocked or damaged culverts. (p8)
* {over the two-month winter 2015/16 flooding period, 05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015}: Activated Emergency Community Assistance and Flood Action Plans and opened Incident Rooms several times during the two-month winter flooding period; opened Rest Centres to support people displaced by flooding; liaised with partner agencies; contacted vulnerable clients and care providers; led recovery including cleaning silt and debris, providing skips, deploying a High Volume Pump at Corbridge to assist clean-up, setting up diversions and one-way systems where landslips occurred, inspecting highway infrastructure and implementing a recovery plan. (p10) [PAG, MUL]
* {over the two-month winter 2015/16 flooding period, 05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015}: Quant: 90 vulnerable clients contacted; Rest Centres opened at 5 locations (Hexham, Morpeth, Rothbury, Ponteland, Longframlington) (p10) [PAG, MUL]

### Wansbeck Catchment
* {05 Jan 2016 - 06 Jan 2016}: Significant rainfall in the Wansbeck catchment produced surface water flooding that caused internal flooding to one residential property and inundation of a catering van in Morpeth. (p7)
* {05 Jan 2016 - 06 Jan 2016}: Quant: 1 property; 1 catering van (p7)

### Northumbrian Water
* {winter 2015/16}: Investigated reported incidents of sewer flooding across the county, surveyed assets and cleansed sewers that had been inundated with flood water. (p11)

### Tyne Valley
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015, 26 Dec 2015}: Storm Desmond caused exceptionally high river levels along the Tyne; the River Tyne rose, breached its banks and flooded numerous areas along the River. Storm Eva (26 Dec 2015) also raised river levels along the Tyne Valley and caused further flooding. (p12) [MUL]
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015, 26 Dec 2015}: Quant: over 200 dwellings; 80 businesses; numerous highways (across Northumberland) (p12) [MUL]

### Bridge End
* {05 Dec 2015 - 06 Dec 2015}: Tyne river levels at Warden Bridge End reached very high/record levels on 05/12/2015, causing river flooding in the locality as part of the widespread Tyne flooding event. (p6) [TAB]
