

# Sheffield Metropolitan District flooding 28 Jul 2013

Reference document:<br>Investigation of Flooding on 28 July 2013 in Chapeltown, Sheffield<br>109\_Sheffield/Report into flooding in Chapeltown during July 2013.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Chapeltown
* A flooding event occurred in Chapeltown on 28 July 2013 and prompted an investigation/report by the local authority. (p1)
* {general}: Flooding occurred in the Chapeltown district; the City Council (as LLFA) determined the impact met its threshold for a statutory Section 19 investigation and prepared a report for publication and distribution to affected residents. (p3)
* Storm on 28 July 2013 caused flooding in Chapeltown (notably at Falding Street and Station Road), including internal flooding to 4 properties and damage to a garden wall on Falding Street. (p5)
* Area experienced a flood event prompting post-event investigations of surface water and combined sewer infrastructure; investigations found a silted surface water sewer in Cowley Lane and a CSO pump fault adjacent to Falding Street that was repaired. (p7)
* Quant: 4 properties; 1 garden wall (p5)

### Falding Street, Ecclesfield
* Overland flows from a surcharged manhole on a culverted watercourse contributed the majority of floodwater to the area, resulting in internal flooding to properties and damage to a garden wall. (p5) [PAG, MUL]
* Overland flow from a surcharged manhole on a culverted watercourse contributed the majority of floodwater, causing internal flooding to properties and damage to a garden wall; the combined sewer also surcharged causing sewage flooding in the area; some other properties were protected by flood boards and sandbags. (p6) [PAG]
* Combined sewer overflow (CSO) adjacent to Falding Street had an electrical fault in the overflow pump which was subsequently repaired; Yorkshire Water increased levels of maintenance at this CSO after the event. (p7) [MUL]
* Quant: 4 properties; 1 garden wall; 3 previous surcharge occasions since 2007 (p5) [PAG, MUL]
* Quant: 4 properties; surcharging on 3 previous occasions (since 2007) (p6) [PAG]

### Station Road, Sheffield Metropolitan District
* Flooding occurred at Station Road as part of the same storm event reported for Chapeltown on 28 July 2013 (no specific local impacts detailed in the text). (p5)
* A combined sewer manhole in front of the Chapel Lodge Care Home surcharged, causing sewage flooding to the area in front of the care home and to the rear gardens of nearby properties on Station Road. (p6)
* The area at Station Road was flooded. Staff at the Care Home on Station Road reported difficulties installing sandbags to entrances during the emergency. Yorkshire Water carried out cleansing operations to the flooded area at this location some days after the event due to delays in reporting of the incident. (p7) [PAG]
* Quant: Surcharging occurred during two previous storm events (over the last year) (p6)

### Cowley Lane, Ecclesfield
* Surface flow from the Cowley Lane highway flowed onto Falding Street and was reported to be due to surcharging of the surface water sewer adjacent to Cowley Lane; a build-up of silt near the surface water sewer outfall at Cowley Lane was later cleared as a precaution. (p6)
* Surface water sewer in Cowley Lane was found to contain silt during the post-event survey; Yorkshire Water to arrange desilting of the surface water sewer. (p7) [MUL]


## Topics without coordinates

### Chapel Lodge Care Home
* Sewage flooding occurred in front of the care home due to surcharging of the adjacent combined sewer manhole; staff reported difficulties installing sandbags to entrances during the emergency. (p6)
* Care Home staff reported difficulties installing sandbags to entrances during the emergency. The combined sewer in front of the Care Home has been identified as a potentially overloaded sewer and will be placed on Yorkshire Water's asset risk register; a more detailed engineering study will be undertaken to determine options to reduce the risk of sewer flooding at this location. (p7) [PAG]
* {general}: Entrances to the Care Home are at risk from an overloaded combined sewer located in front of the property; demountable flood boards will be installed at the entrances to improve flood resilience during emergency incidents and further engineering study will be undertaken to identify longer-term measures to reduce sewer flooding risk. (p8)
* Quant: Surcharging occurred during two previous storm events (over the last year) (p6)

### City Council
* {general}: The City Council concluded the Chapeltown flood on 28 July 2013 met its published threshold for investigation and carried out a section 19 investigation; report to be published and distributed to affected residents. (p3)
* {28 Jul 2013, 26 Aug 2013}: Through its Streets Ahead contractor, arranged and oversaw response actions at Falding Street on 28 July (replacement of blown manhole covers, removal of silt/debris) and a precautionary clearance of silt in Blackburn Brook near the Cowley Lane surface water sewer outfall on 8 August. (p6) [PAG, MUL]

### Yorkshire Water
* Carried out cleansing operations to the flooded area at Station Road some days after the event; carried out post-event surveys of combined and surface water sewers in the Chapeltown area (combined sewers found clear; surface water sewer in Cowley Lane found to contain silt); inspected the combined sewer overflow (CSO) adjacent to Falding Street, identified and repaired an electrical fault in the overflow pump; increased maintenance at the CSO adjacent to Falding Street; recommended/desilted the surface water sewer along Cowley Lane. (p7) [MUL]
* {general}: Identified the combined sewer in front of Chapel Lodge Care Home as potentially overloaded and a source of sewer flooding risk; will place the asset on its risk register, carry out a detailed hydraulic sewerage model study (model available July 2014) to determine options and investment to reduce sewer flooding risk, and arrange installation of demountable flood boards at the Care Home entrances as an interim resilience measure (including a demonstration to staff and local flood wardens). (p8) [PAG]
