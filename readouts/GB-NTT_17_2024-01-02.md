### Colwick
* Between 2 and 4 January 2024 the River Trent rose to near-record levels causing flooding in Colwick. As a result 16 residential properties and 5 businesses experienced internal flooding; additional properties suffered flooding to gardens and outbuildings. (p1) [PAG]
* Sustained rainfall over a saturated catchment caused River Trent levels at Colwick to rise to near‑record levels, leading to flooding across Colwick. Sixteen residential properties and five businesses experienced internal flooding; additional properties suffered flooding to gardens and outbuildings; roads were also flooded. (p2) [PAG]
* Parts of Colwick (specifically Vale Road, Charworth Road and Churchill Business Park) experienced internal flooding when a local watercourse could not discharge to the River Trent because of high river levels during Storm Henk, causing water to back up into the built area. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Colwick experienced flooding during Storm Henk; has affected areas under assessment and is the focus of investigations and community support (including meetings, drop-ins, and consideration of flood risk reduction measures). (p6)
* Quant: 16 residential properties; 5 businesses (p1) [PAG]

### River Trent
* Between 2 and 4 January 2024 water levels in the River Trent at Colwick rose to near‑record levels and were the primary cause of the flooding that led to internal flooding of properties in Colwick. (p1) [PAG]
* Water levels within the River Trent at Colwick rose to near‑record levels due to sustained rainfall over a saturated catchment, causing flooding of roads and properties in Colwick. (p2)
* High water levels in the River Trent during Storm Henk prevented local watercourses from discharging to the river, causing backwater in those channels and contributing to internal flooding of residential and business properties in Vale Road, Charworth Road and Churchill Business Park. (p3) [PAG]
* High water levels in the River Trent (driven by rainfall during Storm Henk) prevented the local watercourse from discharging, producing backwater that caused channel capacity exceedance and internal flooding to nearby residential and business properties. (p4) [PAG]
* Quant: 16 residential properties; 5 businesses (p1) [PAG]
* Quant: 16 residential properties; 5 business properties (p3) [PAG]
* Quant: 16 residential properties; 5 business properties (p4) [PAG]

### Vale Road, Colwick
* Properties on Vale Road were flooded as part of the Colwick event; properties experienced flooding (part of the residential impacts reported). (p2) [PAG]
* A watercourse flowing through and under Vale Road could not discharge into the River Trent because of high River Trent levels during Storm Henk. Water levels built up in the watercourse, exceeded channel capacity and caused internal flooding to properties on Vale Road. Residents reported vehicle bow waves exacerbating flooding; some properties experienced water entry from below floorboards (indicative of groundwater flooding). Litter in the downstream reach may have reduced channel conveyance. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Watercourse upstream of Vale Road could not discharge into the River Trent (high River Trent levels during Storm Henk), causing water levels to build in the channel and flood Vale Road; internal flooding to residential properties occurred, with reports of groundwater entering properties beneath floorboards and vehicle bow waves exacerbating impacts. (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 15 properties (p2) [PAG]

* Quant: 16 residential properties (total across Vale Road and Charworth Road) (p4) [IMG, PAG]

### Chaworth Road, Colwick

* Flooding occurred where a watercourse flowing under Vale Road and the Business Park could not discharge to the River Trent, causing rising water levels in the channel and internal flooding of nearby properties; residents reported groundwater ingress beneath floorboards and pumping into highway gullies.
