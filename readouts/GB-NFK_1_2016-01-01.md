

# Cromer flooding 01 Jan 2016 - 31 Dec 2017

Reference document:<br>Investigation Report Into the Flooding In Cromer In 2016-2017<br>084\_Norfolk/084\_2016\_Norfolk/Cromer\_16.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Cromer
* Urban surface water flooding and associated contamination in parts of the town; prompted emergency response and mitigation activities documented in images. (p1)
* Between 1 January 2016 and 31 December 2017 there were 17 reported flooding incidents for Cromer which led to internal flooding of 5 properties (plus 2 further unconfirmed internal reports). Flooding causes reported included overloaded or surcharging drainage systems, drainage/outfall blockages, surface water washed off public highways by vehicles, and surface run-off from roads; flood water entered properties via unprotected building features (low thresholds, unprotected air bricks and service conduits). (p3)
* Significant flooding affected the town, impacting people, property and infrastructure and triggering a Section 19 investigation. (p5) [IMG]
* Multiple residential properties and multiple commercial properties in Cromer were internally flooded. (p6) [PAG]
* Flood reports (both external and internal) were clustered mainly in the central and northern parts of Cromer during the event, particularly along the central flow line. Reported incidents were concentrated near key infrastructure (hospital and schools) and in more densely populated urban areas. (p7) [IMG, PAG]
* Internal flooding of properties within Cromer (Cromwell Road, Church Street, Overstrand Road) during the reported incidents. (p8) [TAB, MUL]
* Parts of Cromer experienced surface water/urban flooding: properties on Church Street and Overstrand Road were flooded on 08/08/2017 due to highway run-off, obstructed/overloaded surface water drains and low thresholds allowing entry; a property on Cromwell Road was flooded on 23/06/2016 when heavy rainfall caused the local ditch/watercourse to surcharge. (p11) [TAB, MUL]
* Parts of Cromer were affected by flooding during events on 23/06/2016 and 08/08/2017, including impacts on streets listed (Church Street, Overstrand Road and Cromwell Road). (p12) [MUL]
* Flood reports aggregate around the town centre and more built-up areas of Cromer; watercourses and low-lying zones near the town are shown in 1-in-100 flood risk areas. Main roads and bridges through the town intersect flood-prone areas, indicating local infrastructure vulnerability. (p14) [IMG, PAG]
* An internal flood affected a property in Cromer (on Brownshill). (p15) [TAB]
* The Brownshill area of Cromer experienced flooding on 23/06/2016 when significant rainfall overwhelmed the combined drainage network and caused foul-system surcharge, resulting in internal property flooding (water entering through toilets and sinks). (p17) [TAB]
* {21 Dec 2015}: A property in Cromer (on Bond Street) was internally flooded as recorded in the investigation. (p19) [TAB]
* {21 Dec 2015}: Properties on Bond Street in Cromer were flooded when the combined/foul drainage system became partially obstructed and failed upstream, and surface water systems surcharged during heavy rainfall, allowing floodwater into properties via low thresholds. (p21) [TAB]
* Quant: 17 reports; 5 internal properties; 2 unconfirmed internal instances (p3)
* Quant: 3 properties (p8) [TAB, MUL]
* Quant: 1 property (p15) [TAB]
* {21 Dec 2015}: Quant: 1 property internally flooded (p19) [TAB]

### Cromwell Road, Cromer
* Drainage system overload and surcharging, drainage/outfall blockage and surface run-off (including water washed off the public highway by vehicles) caused flood water to reach the road and enter nearby properties. (p3)
* {general}: Cromwell Road is identified in the report as a location where watercourse maintenance under the Land Drainage Act 1991 should be considered in relation to flooding, implying local flood risk linked to the watercourse. (p4)
* One property on Cromwell Road was internally flooded. (p8) [TAB]
* Significant rainfall was directed into the local ditch/watercourse causing it to surcharge, which contributed to flooding at the affected property on Cromwell Road. (p11) [TAB]
* Significant rainfall directed into the nearby ditch/watercourse caused it to surcharge; the surcharging contributed to flooding at a property on Cromwell Road by allowing water to enter the building via low thresholds at the entrances. (p12) [PAG]
* Significant rainfall was directed into the adjacent ditch/watercourse causing it to surcharge; the surcharging contributed to flooding of a property by entering the building via low thresholds at the entrances. (p13) [IMG]
* Quant: 5 properties (internal flooding across the listed locations) (p3)
* Quant: 1 property (p8) [TAB]

### Overstrand Road, Cromer
* Drainage system overload, surcharging and blockages combined with surface run-off from the road (including vehicle wash-off) led to flood water reaching and entering properties adjacent to the road. (p3)
* One property on Overstrand Road was internally flooded; residents were visited afterwards by agencies to offer advice and gather information. (p8) [TAB]
* Run-off from significant rainfall was directed towards the surface water drainage network which was already overloaded; the drainage system was also partially obstructed by debris/silt, reducing its efficiency and directing flood water towards affected properties. (p11) [TAB]
* Flooding occurred on Overstrand Road on 08/08/2017 and affected the road and nearby properties. (p12)
* The surface water drainage system was partially obstructed by debris or silt, causing the ditch/watercourse to overflow and flood into nearby properties. (p13) [IMG]
* Quant: 5 properties (internal flooding across the listed locations) (p3)
* Quant: 1 property (p8) [TAB]

### Cromer West Catchment
* This sub-catchment recorded flooding incidents that resulted in 1 internal and 2 external property flooding events. Causes reported included drainage system overload/surcharging, drainage or outfall blockages (unmaintained/obstructed) and surface run-off from roads directing flow into the network and towards properties. (p3)
* Flood reports are concentrated within the catchment, particularly towards Cromer town and built-up areas; watercourses and low-lying areas within the catchment lie in 1-in-100 flood risk zones. Infrastructure vulnerability noted where bridges and main roads cross flood-prone areas; the combined drainage system is pumped to the Sandy Lane Water Treatment Works. (p14) [PAG]
* One incident of internal flooding occurred within the catchment: a property was internally flooded (on Brownshill, Cromer). (p15)
* Quant: 1 internal; 2 external (p3)
* Quant: 1 property (p15)

### Cromer East Catchment
* This sub-catchment recorded flooding incidents resulting in 3 internal and 10 external property flooding events. Reported causes included an overloaded surface water drainage system, blockages and increased surface run-off from roads which the network could not accommodate. (p3)
* The catchment experienced clustered flood incidents during the event, with the majority of flood reports located along the central flow line from the south‑east to the outfall at the north‑west. Flooding affected densely populated areas along this flow path and coincided with main rivers and watercourses shown on the map. (p7) [IMG, PAG]
* Three incidents of internal (property) flooding occurred within the catchment: properties on Cromwell Road (23/06/2016), Church Street (08/08/2017) and Overstrand Road (08/08/2017) were internally flooded. (p8) [MUL]
* Quant: 3 internal; 10 external (p3)
