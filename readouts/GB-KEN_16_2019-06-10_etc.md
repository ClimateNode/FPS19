### Vigo Village
* Vigo was described as particularly badly affected by very heavy rainfall on 10 June 2019; properties in the Vigo area were flooded by surface water and many were affected internally. (p1)
* Multiple residential properties were affected by flooding with clusters of affected properties in the central and eastern residential zones and some on the western edge of the village. (p2) [IMG]
* The village experienced both external and internal property flooding during the event (including a report under the heading 'Vigo School' of external and internal flooding). Multiple residential properties on named streets in the village were affected (internal and external), and roads were impacted by surface water. (p4) [PAG]
* {2014, 2016, 2017, 2018}: Localised flooding of highways/roads associated with blocked drains; the report records no previous internal property flooding within the village. (p5) [IMG, MUL]
* Surface water flooding flowed from the woodland south of Admers Wood, crossed Erskine, travelled along Admers Wood and Beech Mast, then along footpaths into Ferndown Road. Fire Brigade attended and pumped flood water. (p7)
* Prolonged heavy rainfall and overland flows from surrounding woodland and rural catchment led to significant surface water flooding across the village. Surface water runoff combined with overwhelmed private and highway drainage; foul sewers (foul-only public sewers) contributed to sewer surcharging and manhole overflows in parts of the village. (p8) [PAG]
* A heavy rainstorm (reported as a 1-in-217-year event) overwhelmed highway drainage. Overland flows from developed areas, surrounding woodland and rural catchment travelled along dry valleys and collected at local topographical low points, causing flooding to properties in the village. (p11)
* Quant: around 100 properties (Vigo and Culverstone combined) (p1)
* Quant: 5 properties on Rhododendron Avenue; 1 property on White Post Lane; 1 property on Meadow Lane; 1 property on Wrotham Road (p4) [PAG]
* {2014, 2016, 2017, 2018}: Quant: reports in 4 years (2014, 2016, 2017, 2018) (p5) [IMG, MUL]
