### Lane Ends, Earby
* Floodwater flowed down the disused railway embankment and through Lane Ends, routing water toward residential areas and contributing to internal flooding of properties around Albion Road and Skipton Road. (p22)
* Quant: 69 properties (p22)

### Lancashire County Council
* Acted as the Lead Local Flood Authority during and after the December 2015 floods (recording event details and establishing timelines for investigation); concluded relevant authorities had exercised their functions; improved communications and contributed to adapting the multi‑agency response based on lessons learned. (p9)
* As the Lead Local Flood Authority, the council recorded and verified significant data from the December 2015 floods, established a flood timeline, determined that a countywide Section 19 investigation was appropriate, published the stage one findings and supporting appendix, and is coordinating stage two progress reports and improved communications and multi‑agency response arrangements arising from the December 2015 events. (p10) [PAG]

### Environment Agency
* Provided flood warnings and related services following the December 2015 floods; since the event many households have signed up to receive direct flood warnings from the Environment Agency. (p9)
* {01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015, 01 Sep 2016 - 30 Sep 2016}: Carried out gravel shoal removal on Earby Beck (September 2016) as a response to the December 2015 flood and will continue main river maintenance; identified and will deliver long-term schemes including refurbishment of the Victoria Clough culvert and a modelling/appraisal study of Earby Beck and its tributaries to reduce fluvial flood risk. (p22) [TAB, MUL]

### Victoria Clough
* The Victoria Clough culvert inlet could not accept the volume of water when the New Cut overtopped, contributing to floodwater routing down the disused railway embankment and causing internal flooding of nearby properties. Investigations identified blockages and constrictions within the culvert and a refurbishment scheme has been proposed. (p22) [TAB]
* Quant: 69 properties (p22) [TAB]

### Lancashire Resilience Forum
* Directed multi-agency response during the December 2015 floods; coordinated risk management authorities to provide practical support to police, fire & rescue and NHS including contacting vulnerable people, evacuations, road/bridge closures, diversions, clearing blocked drains and facilitating sandbag provision. (p9)

### National Health Service
* Received practical support from risk management authorities during the December 2015 floods—support included help to contact vulnerable people and assistance with evacuations from flooded areas. (p9)
