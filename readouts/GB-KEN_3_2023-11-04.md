

# Capel-Le-Ferne flooding 04 Nov 2023 - 05 Nov 2023

## Place names with coordinates

### Capel Street, Capel-Le-Ferne
* Runoff from agricultural fields and blocked drains caused surface water flooding. One property reported internal flooding to approximately 300 mm; one property reported external flooding to the driveway and garden. Some flooded properties in the area were evacuated. (p2) [TAB]
* A natural surface water overland flow path routes water across the catchment toward Capel Street, delivering overland flows to the junction with Lancaster Avenue. (p3)
* A topographic depression at the junction with Lancaster Avenue produced a high surface-water flood risk. This location is consistent with reported properties being affected during the flood event of 04-05 November 2023, exposing residential buildings at the junction to surface water inundation. (p4) [IMG]
* {2007}: Road and property flooding caused by surface water runoff from a blocked gully and from an agricultural field. (p5) [TAB]
* Surface water flowed onto and across the carriageway as drainage was overwhelmed. Overland flow passed the junction with Lancaster Avenue and contributed to accumulation in the nearby topographic depression; a blocked local drain (Northern Capel Street Drain) and insufficient capacity in the connected Flood Alleviation Drain network exacerbated the flooding. (p7)
* This drain did not receive floodwater from the topographic depression until a manhole at the southern corner of Lancaster Avenue and Capel Street was lifted; once opened it accepted water that bypassed the Northern drain, helping floodwater to drain away (lifting was reported to produce a sucking/vacuum sound as water entered the system). (p8)
* A blockage downstream of the flooding at the junction between Capel Street and Elizabeth Drive prevented the connected gullies from draining floodwater away during the early November 2023 flood, contributing to local surface water inundation. Gullies to the east (at the entrance to Elizabeth Drive) lay beyond the blockage and could drain, which likely limited the depth and extent of flooding until water entered the unblocked network. (p9)
* Properties along Capel Street experienced surface water flooding because a 225mm foul sewer obstruction likely restricted flow, preventing gullies and the overflow connection between the Capel Street network and the flood-alleviation network from draining floodwater away during the event. (p10) [MUL]
* Surface water runoff from saturated agricultural land accumulated in a localised topographic depression between the junction of Capel Street and Lancaster Avenue, overwhelming highway drainage; flooding occurred within the road and contributed to property flooding nearby. (p11)
* High-confidence flooding of the highway along Capel Street, concentrated at the intersection with Lancaster Avenue. The map shows the road carriageway inundated; the estimated extent covers the highway only and does not mark properties as flooded. (p13) [IMG]
* Flooding affected Capel Street and flood surveys were sent to residents following the event. (p15) [TAB]
* Quant: 1 property internal flooding; 1 property external flooding (p2) [TAB]
* Quant: 1 manhole lifted (p8)
* Quant: 300mm diameter concrete pipe (blocked) (p9)
* Quant: At least 6 properties affected; 5 properties flooded internally (p11)

### Lancaster Avenue, Capel-Le-Ferne
* Runoff from arable fields and blocked highway gullies led to surface water flooding affecting properties on Lancaster Avenue. One property flooded internally to approximately 200–300 mm. Damage in the vicinity to garages and gardens was reported; some flooded properties in the area were evacuated. (p2) [TAB]
* Overland flows are described as reaching the junction at Capel Street and Lancaster Avenue, indicating Lancaster Avenue is on the route of surface water overland flow. (p3)
* The junction with Capel Street forms a topographic depression where surface water accumulates; identified as a high-risk area and consistent with locations of reported properties affected by the 04-05 November 2023 surface-water flooding event. (p4) [IMG]
* {2007}: Road and property flooding caused by surface water runoff from a blocked gully and from an agricultural field. (p5) [TAB]
* Overland flow passed the junction of Capel Street and Lancaster Avenue; drainage gullies within Lancaster Avenue failed to drain runoff, contributing to accumulation of floodwater in the adjacent depression. (p7)
* Floodwater accumulated in the local topographic depression at the junction with Capel Street; lifting of a manhole at the junction of Lancaster Avenue and Capel Street allowed floodwater to drain away and reduce local flood levels. (p8)
* A flood alleviation drain under Lancaster Avenue connects two high-capacity gullies to a 750mm diameter pipe that discharges to a soakaway (with a 225mm diameter overflow). The hydraulic performance of the 750mm pipe was impaired by an obstruction (a foul sewer passing through and blocking a significant part of the surface-water pipe), reducing drainage capacity during the early November 2023 flood and contributing to local surface water flooding. (p9)
* Runoff collected in a depression between the junctions of Capel Street/Lancaster Avenue and Lancaster Avenue/Elizabeth Drive, overwhelming the highway drainage and causing flooding of the road and nearby properties. (p11)
* Flooding affected part of Lancaster Avenue adjacent to Capel Street. The map shows high-confidence extent on part of the road and a low-confidence dashed outline extending further into Lancaster Avenue, indicating additional possible road inundation. (p13) [IMG]
* Flooding affected Lancaster Avenue and flood surveys were sent to residents following the event. (p15) [TAB]
* Quant: 1 property flooded internally; approximately 6 properties affected in the area; 5 properties flooded internally (area total) (p2) [TAB]

### Capel-Le-Ferne
* Flooding was recorded in the settlement during the evening of 04 November into the early morning of 05 November 2023 following a period of prolonged rainfall and a short, intense burst of rain; floodwaters affected Capel‑le‑Ferne. (p1)
* Properties near the junction of Lancaster Avenue and Capel Street experienced overland surface water runoff leading to internal and external flooding, causing damage to garages and gardens and some properties being evacuated. (p2)
* {general}: The primary flood source in the village is surface water (overland flow). Natural surface water overland flow paths are described: one flowing west–east from Great Cauldham Farm across an agricultural field to the junction at Capel Street and Lancaster Avenue, and a larger path flowing from Dawkinge Wood towards that junction and then towards Dover. Soils with impeded drainage lead to increased runoff. Two drainage ditches were removed/infilling following the 2007 flood event, affecting local drainage. (p3)
* Residents reported properties were affected by surface water flooding associated with the event on 04-05 November 2023. The report identifies a topographic depression at the junction of Capel Street and Lancaster Avenue as a high-risk location consistent with where properties were reported flooded; resident testimony about the 04-05 November 2023 event was collected at a meeting on 05 December 2023. (p4)
* {1974, 1976, 1996}: The village experienced surface water flooding on multiple historic occasions (recorded years 1974, 1976 and 1996). (p5) [TAB, MUL]
* A flood incident occurred in Capel-le-Ferne overnight linked to intense local rainfall. The incident took place between 23:00 on 04 November 2023 and 00:45 on 05 November 2023, coinciding with heavy short-duration rainfall and preceded by high antecedent rainfall in the previous days. (p6)
* Intense, antecedent rainfall produced large runoff from surrounding agricultural land which flowed southeast into the village. Water entered the village and travelled along the street corridor (past the Baptist Church and onto Capel Street), overwhelming local drainage and causing surface water flooding within the village. (p7)
* Surface water runoff from saturated agricultural fields accumulated in a local topographic depression between the junction of Capel Street and Lancaster Avenue and the junction of Lancaster Avenue and Elizabeth Drive. The highway drainage system was overwhelmed and obstructed, resulting in flooding of the carriageway and at least six properties (five of which flooded internally). KCC Highways attended the incident and carried out inspection and jetting of road gullies and mainlines on 09 November 2023. Kent Fire Rescue Service attended with vehicles and pumped floodwater out of affected properties and gardens into the highway drainage network. (p11)
* Flooding affected the highway network in Capel-le-Ferne: a high-confidence flood extent is shown on Capel Street with flooding concentrated at the intersection with Lancaster Avenue, and a lower-confidence extent extends into Lancaster Avenue and adjacent residential streets. The map notes the estimated extent covers roads only and does not mark properties as flooded. (p13) [IMG]
* Quant: approximately six properties affected; five properties flooded internally (p2)
* {general}: Quant: 2 drainage ditches removed; 1 property on Elizabeth Drive reported 300 mm external flooding (p3)
* Quant: 6 properties; 5 properties internally flooded; 3 fire engines/vehicles (p11)

### Kent County Council
* {2007}: Following the 2007 surface water flooding, implemented a highway flood-alleviation scheme including installation of 4 additional high-capacity road gullies, a new drainage pipe connecting into a new 750mm perforated pipe and chamber alongside the village hall, and connection into the existing highways drainage network. (p5) [TAB]
* KCC Highways attended the flood incident in Capel-le-Ferne and carried out post‑event inspection and cleaning of highway drains and gullies (including jetting of road gullies and mainlines) on 09 November 2023 as part of the response/aftercare to the surface water flooding. (p11)
