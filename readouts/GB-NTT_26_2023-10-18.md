

# Edingley flooding 18 Oct 2023 - 20 Oct 2023

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Edingley – Storm Babet, October 2023<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/edingley-s19-storm-babet-oct-2023.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Edingley
* Subject of a Section 19 investigation following flooding during Storm Babet (October 2023). The village is served by Edingley Beck and its tributaries which run through the village centre, providing channels/pathways for floodwaters during the October 2023 event. (p1) [PAG]
* Internal flooding of residential properties; additional flooding of gardens and outbuildings. Flooding occurred more rapidly because ground was already saturated from persistent rain. (p2) [PAG]
* Internal flooding concentrated in the central and eastern residential area. Surface water runoff from the west overwhelmed ditches and highway gullies and was unable to enter Edingley Beck at the Main Street / Edingley Hill / Station Road junction, resulting in internal property flooding in the highlighted area. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Surface water runoff from the west of the village overwhelmed surface water assets (ditches and highway gullies) and was unable to enter Edingley Beck at the junction of Main Street / Edingley Hill / Station Road, causing flooding in the village and overwhelming local surface water infrastructure. (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* {general}: Parts of the village are shown on the maps as being within or adjacent to Flood Zone 2/3 (predicted main-river flood zones) and in medium to high surface-water risk areas, indicating predicted flood risk to properties near watercourses. (p5) [IMG]
* Quant: 17 residential properties (p2) [PAG]

### Edingley Hill, Edingley
* At the junction of Edingley Hill / Main Street / Station Road runoff could not enter Edingley Beck, which contributed to internal flooding in the adjacent residential area. (p3) [IMG]
* {general}: Edingley Hill is shown on the maps running close to the main river flood zones and surface-water risk shading, indicating it lies within or adjacent to predicted Flood Zone 2/3 and medium/ high surface-water risk areas. (p5) [IMG]

### Station Road, Edingley
* Station Road forms the junction where surface water runoff was unable to enter Edingley Beck, contributing to internal flooding in the central/eastern village area. (p3)
* Surface water runoff from the west of the village overwhelmed surface water assets (ditches and highway gullies) and was unable to enter Edingley Beck at the junction of Main Street / Edingley Hill / Station Road, resulting in localised surface water flooding and flow across Station Road. Witnesses at a public meeting noted some surface water assets may not have been operating effectively. (p4) [IMG, PAG]

### Main Street, Edingley
* Runoff from the west was unable to enter Edingley Beck at the junction of Main Street / Edingley Hill / Station Road, contributing to internal flooding in nearby properties and the central/eastern area of the village. (p3)

### Mansfield Road, Edingley

### Little Lane, Edingley

* {general}: Shown on the predicted flood zone map intersecting the mapped Flood Zone 2/3 shading along the main river course, indicating the road lies within the mapped river-flood extents and may be inundated in those scenarios. (p5) [IMG]

### Edingley Beck
* Named watercourse that runs through Edingley and its tributaries; identified in the report as the channel managing flows through the village and therefore the route by which high flows from the October 2023 event passed through the settlement. (p1)
* Edingley Beck was unable to accept surface water runoff at the junction of Main Street / Edingley Hill / Station Road, contributing to internal flooding in the central and eastern residential area of Edingley. (p3)
* Surface water runoff from the west could not enter Edingley Beck at the junction of Main Street / Edingley Hill / Station Road, contributing to surface water flooding on the roads and in the village. Witnesses noted Edingley Beck was free-flowing downstream of this location. (p4) [IMG, PAG]

### St Giles
* Internal flooding recorded in the area around St Giles' Church as part of the central/eastern Edingley internal flooding caused by surface water runoff and overwhelmed local drainage. (p3) [IMG]
* {general}: The church is mapped in or adjacent to Flood Zone 2/3 on the predicted flood zone map and appears in proximity to medium/high surface-water risk areas, indicating predicted risk of inundation from the main river and surface-water flooding. (p5) [IMG]

### The Old Reindeer
* Internal flooding recorded at the building as part of the area of internal flooding concentrated in central/eastern Edingley caused by surface water runoff overwhelming ditches and highway gullies and being unable to enter Edingley Beck. (p3) [IMG]

### Old Schoolroom
* Internal flooding recorded at the building as part of the highlighted internal flooding area caused by surface water runoff overwhelming local surface water assets and inability to discharge to Edingley Beck. (p3) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Verified and recorded internal flooding at affected properties; attended sites to assess scale of internal flooding and offered support, guidance and advice to residents and businesses; collated and published verified flood impact data; delivered sandbags; provided 24-hour emergency planning support; chaired and attended Strategic / Tactical / Recovery Coordination Group meetings; led the Section 19 Flood Investigation; initiated Community Flood Recovery Grant and led procurement/delivery of the DEFRA Property Flood Resilience Repair Grant Scheme; supported community flood signage and shared resources with district/borough partners. (p7)
