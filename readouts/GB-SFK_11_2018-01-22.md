### Quay Street, Halesworth
* Internal flooding of a property on Quay Street; the property was flooded internally on multiple occasions during the event(s) investigated. (p2)
* {14 Nov 2015, 30 Dec 2017}: Surface water flooded a domestic property internally on multiple occasions; water seeped through brickwork into the property with the kitchen worst affected, causing property damage. Flooding was driven by combined roof and internal drainage flows overwhelming a small yard gully and surcharging of highway gullies; nearby development works diverted an existing highway surface water pipe into the site's drainage, contributing to the issue.
