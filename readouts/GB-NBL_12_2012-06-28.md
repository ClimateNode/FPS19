

# Knaresdale With Kirkhaugh flooding 28 Jun 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Burnstones (Knarsdale)<br>091\_Northumberland/Burnstones-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Burnstones
* Residential flooding: four properties in Burnstones were flooded. Flooding was caused by ordinary watercourse levels rising and going out of bank behind properties (water flowed around to the front and entered via front doors) with surface water from the A689 also contributing; standing water on the A689 and vehicle-generated waves worsened flooding. (p1) [IMG]
* Annotated flow routes show water/runoff moving south and southeast across fields and roads within Burnstones, converging toward lower-lying areas; imagery also documents a culvert at Merit Hall in bank‑full condition, indicating high flow within the Burnstones area. (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 4 properties (p1) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### A689
* Surface water on the A689 contributed to flooding of at least one adjacent property; drainage along this road was overwhelmed by the exceptional rainfall on 28 June 2012 and standing water plus waves from passing vehicles caused or exacerbated flooding of properties off the road. (p1)
* Annotated flow routes on the map cross the A689, showing surface-water runoff moving across and over the road alignment; indicates the road is a flow path and may be subject to overtopping or flowing surface water during high flow/runoff events. (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 1 property (p1)

### Northumberland County Council
* Produced and exercised a Flood Investigation Report for the Burnstones (Knarsdale) event of 28/06/12; proposed seeking funding for potential flood alleviation works. The report and associated map documented impacts to local residents and roads (see quant). (p1) [IMG]
* Carried out a Flood Investigation Report into flooding in Burnstones and proposed to seek funding for potential flood alleviation works in the MTP (action by the local authority in response to flood impacts/risks). (p2) [TAB]
