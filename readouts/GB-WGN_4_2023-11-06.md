

# Wigan Metropolitan District flooding 06 Nov 2023

## Place names with coordinates

### May Tree Drive, Wigan Metropolitan District
* Residents of May Tree Drive experienced varying degrees of flooding during the event; the United Utilities network and the pumping station located in May Tree Drive had issues related to the event. (p5)
* {06 Nov 2023, 07 Nov 2023}: Surface water ponding on the highway could not drain to the submerged sewer outfall into Barley Brook and spread towards properties; United Utilities officers reported the foul sewer network reached capacity and the local pumping station was overwhelmed. Response crews attended May Tree Drive to clear the water (attending until ~03:00). (p7) [IMG, MUL]
* Surface water from the highway was unable to drain (outfall submerged) leading to ponding that spread towards properties; flooding on May Tree Drive was reported and later dissipated. (p8) [PAG]
* {07 Nov 2023}: May Tree Drive experienced surface water flooding associated with the same event as Havenwood Road; the flooding began to dissipate at approximately 03:00 on 7 November. (p9) [PAG]
* Standing surface water covered the carriageway; highway gullies were submerged; surface water encroached onto residential properties and entered multiple properties causing internal flooding. (p19) [PAG, IMG]
* Surface water flooding on the evening of 06/11/2023 (approximately 19:00) submerged highway gullies and caused internal flooding to multiple residential properties on May Tree Drive. Photographic evidence shows indoor flooding in a storage/utility room with water covering the floor and damage to stored items. United Utilities reported that the local foul pumping station was inundated with surface water, worked at capacity throughout the evening and continuously for several days afterwards, and that excess surface water backed up into residents' properties and entered the foul sewer. (p20) [PAG, IMG]

### Barley Brook
* {06 Nov 2023, 07 Nov 2023}: Barley Brook rose in response to persistent heavy rainfall and overtopped its banks (around 06:00 on 6 November 2023), causing fluvial flooding of the adjacent low-lying woodland and footpaths. The high water level submerged the sewer outfall, preventing drainage of surface water from nearby roads and contributing to highway and property flooding. (p7) [IMG, MUL]
* The sewer outfall into Barley Brook became submerged, preventing surface water from draining away and contributing to ponding on roads. A culverted section of Barley Brook beneath the railway was reported blocked by debris, linked to the local flooding. (p8) [IMG, PAG]
* {07 Nov 2023}: Barley Brook is the receiving watercourse for the surface water network serving the estate and is identified as a main river. Network Rail reported that the culverted section of Barley Brook beneath the railway had likely become blocked by debris, causing water levels to remain high on both sides of the structure and contributing to the local flooding; blockage removal downstream (Spencer Road West culvert) allowed upstream levels to recede. (p9)
* Multiple blockages along the Barley Brook watercourse contributed to flooding; open channel was reported in good condition but culvert blockages and an extended culvert length increased risk of unnoticed restrictions on flow. (p11)
* Water levels within Barley Brook could rise above the United Utilities outlet if a blockage is present, risking overtopping of the outlet and localised flooding related to the surface water drainage system. (p16)

### Wigan Metropolitan District
* Following severe overnight rainfall Barley Brook rose, causing flooding of adjacent woodland and resulting in flooding affecting properties on Havenwood Road and May Tree Drive. Flooding in the surrounding woodland occurred at approximately 09:00 on 6 November. Flooding on Havenwood Road and May Tree Drive began to appear at approximately 17:00 on 6 November; response crews attended those streets and worked until about 03:00 to clear the water. The brook level was observed to peak at approximately 00:00 on 7 November. (p6) [MUL]
* Localised fluvial flooding from Barley Brook inundated low-lying woodland and adjacent footpaths; surface water/ponding affected Havenwood Road and May Tree Drive with sewage backing up due to overwhelmed sewers. (p7) [IMG, MUL]
* Parts of the local surface water network discharged to Barley Brook which experienced blockages and restricted flows; excess surface water backed up into residents' properties and caused a United Utilities foul pumping station to work at capacity and become inundated with surface water. (p10) [PAG, IMG]

### Havenwood Road, Wigan Metropolitan District
* Following substantial upland rainfall on 6th November 2023 Havenwood Road flooded; residents experienced varying degrees of flooding. (p5)
* {06 Nov 2023, 07 Nov 2023}: Surface water ponding on the highway exceeded the kerb and spread towards properties; surface water was unable to drain because the sewer outfall into Barley Brook was submerged. The foul sewer became inundated with surface water, causing sewage to back up onto Havenwood Road. United Utilities attended and the pumping station was reported to be running to capacity; response crews worked on site to clear water until about 03:00. (p7) [PAG, MUL]
* Surface water ponded on the highway, eventually exceeded the kerb height and spread towards properties. Sewage backed up onto Havenwood Road after the foul sewer system became inundated with surface water; surface water entered foul sewers via private drainage contributing to the pumping station becoming overwhelmed. (p8) [PAG]
* Surface water levels on the highway exceeded the kerb and spread towards properties. The foul sewer system became inundated with surface water causing sewage to back up onto Havenwood Road. Surface water entered the foul sewer via properties' private drainage and the local foul pumping station became overwhelmed; United Utilities officers attended to keep the pumping station operational. (p9) [PAG, MUL]

### Spencer Road, Wigan Metropolitan District
* The culvert beneath Spencer Road West (Spencer Road area) had its inlet blocked, causing elevated water levels upstream of the culvert near the railway and contributing to localised surface water flooding until blockages were cleared. (p10) [PAG, IMG]
* The debris screen at the inlet to the Spencer Road culvert had been damaged and was non-operational, allowing debris to contribute to blockages within the culvert. Following clearance works to the Spencer Road culvert, water levels remained high immediately upstream of the culvert beneath the railway, indicating the culvert blockages contributed to local flooding. (p11) [PAG]
* Blockages in the Spencer Road culvert were implicated in the local flood risk; works to reinstate a debris screen on the Spencer Road culvert were planned to help prevent blockages and reduce future flooding. (p15)

### Westfield Grove, Wigan Metropolitan District
* Woodland upstream of the Network Rail culvert became heavily flooded; adjacent footpath by the railway became inaccessible. (p7)

### United Utilities
* The United Utilities network and pumping station located in May Tree Drive experienced issues related to the flooding event. (p5)
* United Utilities' foul sewer network and a local pumping station reached capacity after surface water entered the foul system. Sewage backed up onto Havenwood Road; UU officers attended to keep the pumping station operational and assist with surface water flooding. (p7) [PAG, MUL]
* UU officers attended site and reported the foul sewer system had been inundated with surface water, causing sewage to back up and the local pumping station to be overwhelmed; officers worked to keep the pumping station operational and assist with surface water flooding. (p8)
* United Utilities officers reported the foul sewer system had been inundated with surface water leading to sewage backing up onto Havenwood Road. Their officers attended site to ensure the foul pumping station remained operational and to assist with surface water flooding; the nearby foul pumping station was reported as running to capacity. (p9) [MUL]
* Multiple restrictions downstream of the United Utilities sewer discharge caused a backup in the sewer/system which contributed to flooding during the extreme weather event. (p15)

### Network Rail
* A blockage of the Network Rail culvert was reported due to high water in Barley Brook; extent of blockage was unknown. Water did not reach the track and rail services were not affected. (p7)
* Network Rail officers were on site to investigate reports that the culverted section of Barley Brook beneath the railway had become blocked by debris, a condition linked to the local flooding on Havenwood Road and May Tree Drive. (p8)
* {07 Nov 2023}: Network Rail officers attended to investigate reports that the culverted section of Barley Brook beneath the railway had become blocked by debris, which was linked to the local flooding. Network Rail is a riparian owner of the Brook alongside Wigan Council and provided a report indicating a likely downstream blockage. (p9) [PAG]
* Network Rail (NWR) investigated a partial blockage in the railway culvert that had restricted downstream flows and contributed to the flooding. The partial blockage was cleared immediately following the investigation to remove the flow restriction. (p11)

### Wigan Council
* {07 Nov 2023}: Wigan Council's flood risk officer investigated downstream sections after reports of blockage beneath the railway. The officer found the culvert beneath Spencer Road West completely blocked by debris (fly‑tipped tyres and tree branches). Once the blockage was removed the water level receded and conditions within the watercourse upstream of the culvert returned to normal. (p9) [PAG]
* A Wigan Council flood risk officer investigated downstream sections and found the Spencer Road West culvert completely blocked by debris (tyres and branches). The blockage was cleared and following removal water levels receded and upstream conditions returned to normal. Council has ordered a replacement debris screen for the culvert inlet. (p10) [IMG, PAG]
* Wigan Council undertook works to remove blockages from the watercourse which had the most impact on reducing flood risk; they plan to reinstate a debris screen on the Spencer Road culvert and need to implement a maintenance and inspection regime to ensure continued effectiveness. (p15)

### Spencer Road Culvert
* The culvert beneath Spencer Road West became completely blocked at the inlet by fly‑tipped tyres and tree branches, severely restricting downstream flows. Once the blockage was removed water levels receded and upstream conditions returned to normal. The debris screen at the inlet was found damaged and a replacement screen has been ordered to prevent future blockages. (p10)

### National Rail Culvert
* A partial blockage was reported within the National Rail culvert that had restricted downstream flows and contributed to high water levels upstream beneath the railway; the blockage was cleared immediately following investigation. The culvert was found to extend further than initially believed, increasing blockage risk; an underwater survey/inspection was requested. (p11)

### Environment Agency
* The Environment Agency worked in partnership with other authorities during the event, instructed Network Rail to conduct an underwater survey, and considered options to maintain the main river annually and to understand fluvial flood risk affecting United Utilities' modelled flood risk. (p15)
