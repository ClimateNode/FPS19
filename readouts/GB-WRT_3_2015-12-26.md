## Place names with coordinates

### Stone Pit Lane, Croft
* Subject of a Section 19 flood investigation by Warrington Borough Council indicating that flooding affected Stone Pit Lane; no specific flood mechanism or impacts are given in the provided extract. (p1)
* {12 Dec 2014, 26 Dec 2015}: Carriageway flooding occurred; the flood affected the entrance to Turret Hall and the Stables. (p4) [TAB, MUL]
* Stone Pit Lane was severely affected by flooding; water covered the full width of the road and extended onto verges and adjacent land, rendering the road impassable and posing risk to nearby properties (as shown in the provided image). (p7)
* {26 Dec 2015, 13 Jan 2016}: Severe localised flooding of the lane verge and adjacent land during the event on 26 December 2015. Water encroached onto the roadside, prompting deployment of diversion signage, cones and sandbags. Entrances to nearby properties (The Stables and Turret Hall) off Stone Pit Lane were observed still flooded and the ditch water level remained high at the site visit on 13 January 2016. (p8) [PAG, IMG, MUL]
* Described as the 'affected area' during the flood event. Nearby gauging stations recorded extremely high flows (Atherton Lake Brook >1 in 1000 year), and high flows on Cockshot Brook are reported as likely to have prevented drainage discharge from the culverted outfall, allowing water to back up the culvert and contribute to flooding at Stone Pit Lane. (p11)
* Referenced as the nearby 'affected area' (Stone Pit Lane) connected to the observed overland flow on Heath Lane; the weather front that produced heavy rainfall is reported to have passed over Stone Pit Lane during the same event. (p12)
* Stone Pit Lane and accesses to nearby properties (including accesses to The Stables and Turret Hall) were flooded on 26 December 2015 due to blockage of the culvert from the drainage ditch to the outfall into Cockshot Brook causing the ditch to overflow. (p16)

### Cockshot Brook
* High flows on Cockshot Brook during the 26 December 2015 event are described as likely to have prevented water discharging from the drainage system; the outfall from the culverted section into Cockshot Brook appears to have no non-return mechanism, which may have allowed water to back up into the culvert during high flow. (p11) [PAG]
* High / extreme flows on Cockshot Brook prevented discharge from the connected drainage/culverted system; absence of a non‑return mechanism on the outfall may allow water to back up the culvert during times of high flow. (p12) [PAG]
* Receives discharge from the drainage outfall (within Kenylo Bridge abutment) and forms part of the downstream conveyance network related to the localised flooding. (p15) [IMG]
* High water levels in Cockshot Brook during the 26 December 2015 event are recorded as a likely factor that reduced the efficiency of the drainage outfall and contributed to local flooding. (p16) [PAG]
* Outfall into Cockshut Brook is located within the abutment of Kenylo Bridge; a culverted section feeding the brook was recorded with blockages which impaired flow and were identified during the flood investigation, necessitating remedial works. (p19) [IMG]
* Quant: outfall into the brook located approximately 120 metres west of the flooded area. (p15) [IMG]
* Quant: 4 blockages (p19) [IMG]

### The Stables

* Flooding occurred at the entrance to Turret Hall/The Stables; entrance is a localised low spot that flooded and is identified as high risk on the Environment Agency 'Risk of Flooding from Surface Water' mapping. (p13)
* Localised surface-water flooding affecting The Stables. Micro-topography (building levels and kerbing not visible in LiDAR) channelled water into/around the building, contributing to flooding of the asset. (p15)
* The Stables was flooded on 26 December 2015. The council concluded the cause was surface water runoff from surrounding fields and the event may have been exacerbated by culvert blockage and high water levels in Cockshot Brook reducing drainage efficiency. (p16)

### Kenyon Hall

A headwall was later constructed at the culvert inlet to reduce future blockages. (p9) [MUL]
* {26 Dec 2015, 09 May 2016}: Quant: four blockages removed (p9) [MUL]
