

# Hope Under Dinmore flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Hope Under Dinmore<br>055\_Herefordshire County/04\_Hope\_under\_Dinmore.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Hope Under Dinmore
* {2007, 2014, 16 Feb 2020}: Cherry Brook broke its banks and caused localised fluvial flooding that flooded the highway (spilling below the railway via a bridge) and contributed to internal flooding of nearby properties. (p2) [MUL]
* {2007, 2014, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 15 properties; 3 properties at Cherry Brook Close; flooding depths typically 6 inches to 2 ft, with one basement/cellar reported at 2–3 ft (p2) [MUL]

### Cherrybrook Close, Hope Under Dinmore
* Highway flooded signs were installed at Cherrybrook Close on 16 February 2020; properties in the immediate area reported flooding related to road runoff and the adjacent brook. (p2)
* Quant: 3 properties (p2)

### A49 at Hope Under Dinmore
* Residents reported overland flows from the A49 contributing to property flooding in the area. (p2)
