### Laxfield Road, Fressingfield

* Internal flooding of a property on Laxfield Road occurred on more than one occasion. (p2)

## Topics without coordinates

### Suffolk County Council
* Highway drainage capacity was insufficient during the event; runoff from a neighbouring unbound driveway without a kerb face allowed surface water to flow from the road toward and into adjacent properties. Suffolk County Council (as Highway Authority) was asked to investigate highway drainage and to install additional gullies and other measures to capture surface water. (p4) [TAB]

### Mid Suffolk District Council
* An unpermitted unbound driveway installation in a front garden (lacking a 25mm kerb face) allowed surface water to flow from the road towards nearby properties, causing flooding impacts that prompted Mid Suffolk District Council (as Local Planning Authority) to investigate planning status and take/enforce remedial action. (p4) [TAB]
