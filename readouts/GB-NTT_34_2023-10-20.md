## Place names with coordinates

### Hucknall
* {01 Oct 2023 - 31 Oct 2023}: Flooding associated with Storm Babet (October 2023). The report notes multiple watercourses running west–east through the town discharging to the River Leen and records very high rainfall nearby, linking the town to the October 2023 flood event. (p1)
* Internal (internal property) flooding was recorded across multiple parts of the town during the Storm Babet event; affected locations and property counts are shown on Figure 3. (p3) [IMG]
* Widespread flooding across the town during Storm Babet, affecting areas adjacent or very near to watercourses and large open spaces; flooding attributed to saturated ground and intense rainfall, with witness reports of watercourses overspilling onto roads. (p4) [IMG]
* Widespread flooding across the town during the Storm Babet event; multiple areas adjacent to open or culverted watercourses and large open spaces were affected as watercourses spilled over onto roads. (p5) [IMG, PAG]
* Localised flooding impacted the A611 Hucknall Bypass (surface water pooling, road overtopping in the low point) and caused significant flows down Greenwood Vale towards Greenwood Avenue resulting in devastating flooding to properties; drone footage shows vehicles driving through or stopped on partially flooded sections of the A611. (p6) [IMG]
* A residential area of Hucknall experienced severe flooding with muddy water inundating streets and frontages, water reaching doorsteps and partially submerging vehicles; emergency vehicles were present in response. (p7) [IMG, PAG]
* {19 Oct 2023}: Severely flooded domestic properties in Hucknall led to evacuees and the establishment/scaling of a rest centre; local inspections of watercourses and drainage assets were carried out (19 Oct 2023) and sandbags were deployed to prevent internal flooding of homes. (p9)
* Previous capital investment in flood risk measures reduced the impacts of Storm Babet on Hucknall; infrastructure (including Dob Park reservoir) prevented greater residential flooding. (p10)
* Quant: Greenwood Avenue: 39 properties; Ogle Street area: 20 properties; Wighay Road / Ward Avenue: 16 properties; Polperro Way: 5 properties; Oakenhall Avenue / Wenlock Drive: 4 properties; Linby Road: 3 properties; Dale Close: 2 properties; Papplewick Lane: 1 property; Thoresby Dale: 1 property; Brookside: 1 property. (p3) [IMG]

### Nottinghamshire

* Storm Babet flooding affected communities across the county; Nottinghamshire County Council (as Lead Local Flood Authority) led investigations, debriefing and will review/extend property flood resilience and support for vulnerable communities. (p10)

## Topics without coordinates

### Dob Park Balancing Reservoir
* Described as part of the flood management system: it was designed to hold 24,000 m3 and to catch upstream flows, discharging them at a controlled rate; it includes a spillway (approximately 320 mm lower than the A611) to allow excess flows to bypass the control structure and prevent water overtopping the A611 during storm conditions. (p5)
* Dob Park Reservoir was observed storing high volumes of water during the event. Drone imagery shows the reservoir storing water while surface water was pooling at the low point of the nearby A611 Hucknall Bypass. After the event the reservoir outlet trash screen was checked and debris cleared; there was no evidence of blockage. (p6) [IMG, PAG]
* Dob Park Reservoir was storing water during the event; surface water pooled at nearby low points (A611 Hucknall Bypass). The reservoir outlet trash screen had accumulated debris which was cleared after the event, with no evidence of blockage or operational failure; capacity of catchment assets including the reservoir may have been exceeded. (p7)
* Dob Park reservoir held back floodwater during Storm Babet, keeping water away from residential areas and reducing internal flooding. (p10)

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Officers attended sites after the Major Incident to verify the scale of internal flooding and to provide short-term internal flooding recovery aid. (p8)
* As Lead Local Flood Authority, engaged in Section 19 investigation into the Storm Babet event; conducting rest centre review with ADC colleagues; Flood Risk Management team will review suitability of the Property Flood Resilience Programme for communities affected by Storm Babet and take recommendations to the Cabinet Member. (p10)

### Ashfield District Council
* {19 Oct 2023}: As the local authority, ADC carried out inspections of watercourses/drainage (19 Oct 2023), provided emergency response support, deployed sandbags to prevent internal flooding of domestic premises, and opened a rest centre for evacuees from severely flooded domestic properties. (p9)
* Responded to Storm Babet flooding in its area: offered mutual aid to Newark and Sherwood by making a Rest Centre available for evacuees; scaled the Rest Centre to a temporary place of safety once evacuee numbers were established; issued flood advice via website and social media; held an internal Storm Babet debrief and proposed follow-up actions to reduce future flood impacts. (p10)
