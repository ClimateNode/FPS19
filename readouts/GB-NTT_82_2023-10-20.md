

# Treswell flooding 20 Oct 2023

## Place names with coordinates

### Treswell
* Treswell village suffered a flood event during Storm Babet caused by sustained heavy rainfall. (p1)
* The village experienced fluvial flooding concentrated along Townside Lane and Town Street after the Lee Beck overtopped following heavy sustained rainfall. Five residential properties were internally flooded and additional properties suffered external flooding. (p3) [IMG]
* Lee Beck rose out of its channel and flowed along Townside Lane; Townside Lane was flooded to a depth of around 1–2 ft and water also ran down Town Street into the low point and back down Townside Lane. Five residential properties on Townside Lane and Town Street were internally flooded and additional properties suffered external flooding. (p4) [IMG]
* {general}: Surface-water flood risk concentrated along main watercourses through the village, especially near Rectory Road and Cottam Lane. Medium and low risk zones extend into adjacent fields and some residential areas; some buildings are close to medium/low risk areas, indicating potential for surface water to affect properties during heavy rainfall. (p5) [IMG]
* Quant: five residential properties; lane flooded to a depth of around 1-2 ft (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: five residential properties; Townside Lane flooded to a depth of around 1-2 ft (p4) [IMG]

### Town Street, Treswell
* Residential properties on Town Street were internally flooded as part of the event caused by the Lee Beck overtopping; other properties also experienced external flooding. (p3) [IMG]
* Water was observed running down Town Street to a low point and then flowing down Townside Lane, which further affected properties. Town Street was associated with internal flooding of residential properties (part of the five properties reported). (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* Water was observed running down Town Street to a low point; this flow then continued down Townside Lane and further affected properties (residential properties) downhill. (p5) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: five residential properties (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 5 residential properties (p4) [IMG, PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* Treswell (located in Nottinghamshire) experienced flooding during Storm Babet on 20 October 2023. (p1)
* County experienced flooding that required a coordinated emergency response: 24-hour Emergency Planning support, delivery of sandbags to affected areas and communities, liaison with community flood signage schemes, closure of roads to promote safety and avoid additional flood damage, sharing resources with district/borough partners, collation and publication of verified flood impact data, and initiation and leadership of the Section 19 Flood Investigation and delivery/procurement activity for the DEFRA Property Flood Resilience Repair Grant Scheme. (p7)
* Quant: 77 roads (p7)

### Bassetlaw
* Treswell, a village within the District of Bassetlaw, suffered a flood event during Storm Babet caused by sustained heavy rainfall on 20 October 2023. (p1)

### Townside Lane, Treswell
* Water flowed out of the Lee Beck at the end of Townside Lane and along the lane, flooding it to a depth of around 1-2 ft and contributing to internal flooding of residential properties on Townside Lane and nearby Town Street. (p3) [IMG]
* Water flowed out of the Lee Beck channel at the end of Townside Lane and along the Lane, flooding the Lane to a depth of around 1–2 ft and causing internal flooding of residential properties. Additionally, surface water from Town Street flowed into the low point and down Townside Lane, further affecting properties. (p4) [PAG]
* Quant: five residential properties; lane flooded to a depth of around 1-2 ft (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 1-2 ft depth; 5 residential properties (p4) [PAG]

### Lee Beck
* The Lee Beck (the ordinary watercourse running west–east through Treswell) is referenced in relation to the flood event affecting Treswell on 20 October 2023. (p1)
* Lee Beck (ordinary watercourse) rose rapidly due to heavy sustained rainfall and came out of its channel (overtopped), causing fluvial flooding that flowed along Townside Lane and Town Street. (p3) [IMG]
* Due to heavy sustained rainfall the water levels in the Lee Beck rose quickly and the water came out of channel (overtopped). This out-of-channel flow caused flooding of Townside Lane and Town Street and contributed to internal flooding of residential properties. (p4) [PAG]
* Quant: 5 residential properties; 1-2 ft depth (on Townside Lane) (p4) [PAG]

## Topics without coordinates

### Severn Trent
* STW provide emergency response and support to the management of flooding events and are responsible for maintaining the public sewerage system (surface water sewers, foul sewers and combined sewers) which relate to flood risk. (p6)
* The LLFA will liaise with Severn Trent to further understand the condition and role of Severn Trent assets in the area following the flood event (investigation into whether their assets contributed to the incident). (p8)

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* A Major Incident with significant impacts across the county led the Council to support affected residents: initiated a Community Flood Recovery Grant for internal flooding, attended flooded sites to verify internal flooding and offer support, collated flood impact data from RMAs/partners and published verified data on Resilience Direct. (p6)
* NCC (as LLFA/RMA) will work with partners to strengthen enforcement of riparian responsibilities and develop its asset inspection process in response to the flood event. (p8)

### Bassetlaw District Council
* Council provided emergency response to flood-impacted areas including deployment of sandbags and aquasacs to vulnerable premises and residents, provision of community resilience stores, operation of a rest centre at Retford Leisure Centre, provision of temporary accommodation, provision of skips and caged vans to dispose of flood-damaged property, cleansing of detritus from roads/car parks/paths, rehousing of affected council tenants and repairs to affected council housing properties, and administration/promotion of business and community recovery grants and discounts. (p7)
* Bassetlaw District Council participated in the LRF Storm Babet debrief and undertook an internal review of the response to Storm Babet following the flooding; these reviews identified future actions and commitments (including working with partners to lobby for funding for local flood alleviation schemes). (p8)

### Environment Agency
* Communities in the area were affected by flooding during Storm Babet; this prompted partners (including Bassetlaw District Council) to consider changes to the Environment Agency funding framework to enable more local flood alleviation schemes. (p8)
