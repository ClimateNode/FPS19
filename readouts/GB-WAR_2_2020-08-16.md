## Place names with coordinates

### Snitterfield
* Flooding occurred in the village on the reported event date; recovery and remedial works were undertaken and investigations of the drained (drainage) network carried out by WCC together with partner RMAs; parts of remedial works remain ongoing. (p4) [PAG]
* {12 Aug 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Short, intense convective rainfall (peaking late afternoon/early evening) caused localised highway flooding and led to water ingress through the fabric of multiple properties (anecdotal reports collated 17 Aug). Radar data for 16 Aug 2020 correlate peak rainfall rates with the period of reported flooding. (p6) [MUL]
* Short, localised intense rainfall caused water ingress through the fabric of buildings (not associated with fluvial mechanisms), resulting in internal property ingress across the village. (p7) [PAG]
* Short-duration, high-intensity rainfall on 16th August 2020 produced surface water flows and inundation of artificial drainage (including sewers). Flows exploited the built environment (highways, hardscaped gardens), channelled water, deposited large amounts of silt on the highway and caused flooding reported across three main areas of the village (including Duffins Piece and The Green). (p9)
* Short-duration, high-intensity rainfall on 16 Aug 2020 caused surface water flows and inundation/surcharging of artificial drainage (sewers), with flow routes exploiting highways and hardscaped areas. Flooding was reported in three main areas (Duffins Piece, The Green, School Road); drainage chambers were overwhelmed and silt was deposited onto highways. (p10) [PAG, IMG]
* On 16th August 2020 surface-water flows from higher ground impacted the village: properties on The Green experienced low‑level internal water accumulation through a flagstone floor, and flows along School Road (from Hales Close/Duttons Close) overwhelmed drainage causing drainage chambers to surcharge; central parts along the village waterway showed the highest surface-water extents on EA mapping. (p11) [IMG]
* {1998, 2007, 2012, 2016, 2019, 16 Aug 2020}: Snitterfield has a recorded history of property flooding in 1998, 2007, 2012, 2016, 2019 and 2020. During the 16 August 2020 event surface-water/overland flows associated with an ordinary watercourse and runoff from agricultural land caused internal flooding to properties in the Duffins Piece area of the village. (p14) [PAG, MUL]
* During the 16 August 2020 rainfall event Snitterfield experienced significant localised surface water accumulations and flows entering the highway from higher surrounding land. Reports recorded highway and garden flooding across the village and at least one property suffered low-level internal flooding (ingress through a flagstone floor caused by a raised local water table). Post-event inspections by Warwickshire County Council and the Environment Agency gathered further evidence but could not fully establish the precise mode of flooding. (p16) [PAG]
* Residential area in Snitterfield experienced internal flooding of properties (six properties internally flooded). Multiple investigatory and mitigation actions were taken including gully cleansing, CCTV of sewers, river/stream modelling and ditch maintenance discussions. (p19)

### Duffins Piece, Snitterfield
* Overtopping of a small unnamed ordinary watercourse led flows along a public right of way into the private tarmacked access road (which has no formal drainage). Resulting flows entered properties and adjacent arable land; the event caused internal flooding of properties and significant surface flow along the access. (p9) [IMG]
* {general}: The local watercourse on Duffins Peace was inspected and found in places to lack a sufficiently defined channel/profile to convey flows; this deficiency was identified as a constraint on conveying flows and prompted consideration of drainage options (new drain discounted for maintenance/ ecological constraints). (p11) [PAG]
* Two distinct surface-water/overland flow routes entered the Duffins Piece area during the 16 August 2020 event. Primary flow overtopped an ordinary watercourse and travelled down a public right of way onto Duffins Piece; a secondary overland flow breached a raised vehicle access and entered via a gated field access. Flows entered two properties where internal thresholds were lower than or comparable to the unadopted highway. Inspection found the ordinary watercourse had lost much of its original profile and was obstructed by self-set trees and saplings, reducing conveyance and likely increasing flood risk. (p14) [PAG]
* Internal flooding of properties caused by exceedance of the ordinary watercourse at Duffins Piece; inundation affected nearby low-lying land and resulted in property interiors being flooded. (p15)
* Quant: 2 properties (p9) [IMG]
* Quant: 2 properties internally flooded; 0 properties externally flooded; 0 critical infrastructure flooded (p14) [PAG]
* Quant: 2 properties internally flooded (p15)

### The Green, Snitterfield
* Surface water flows from higher ground caused low-level water ingress through a flagstone floor, resulting in internal flooding of at least one property; residents were inconclusive whether this was from overland flow routes or a localised rising water table. (p10) [IMG]
* Surface water flows from higher ground to the west reached properties on The Green causing low-level water accumulation through a flag stone floor; residents reported flooding but were inconclusive on whether flows or a localised rising water table caused it. (p11) [IMG]
* The Green area experienced surface water flows from higher agricultural land to the north-east that accumulated on impermeable areas (driveways, highway) and led to external garden and highway flooding during the 16 August 2020 event. One property at The Green is reported to have suffered low-level internal flooding (ingress through flagstone floor due to raised water table). (p16) [PAG]
