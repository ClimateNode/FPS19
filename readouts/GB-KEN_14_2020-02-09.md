## Place names with coordinates

### Five Oak Green
* A concentrated cluster of reported flood incidents (map shows majority clustered in and around Five Oak Green) — residents were surveyed in September 2020 to collate information about the flood extents and impacts in the area. (p2) [IMG]
* Localised flooding in Five Oak Green caused by rapid response of the Alder Stream to intense, locally exceptional rainfall during Storm Ciara. Flooding of this magnitude was restricted to Five Oak Green within the county. (p5) [PAG]
* During Storm Ciara the village experienced flooding driven by surface water runoff and fluvial overflow of the Alder Stream. Flood extents in the village matched high-risk areas on Environment Agency maps and locations of reported properties affected by the Storm Ciara event. (p8)
* Five Oak Green has experienced fluvial flooding associated with the River Medway. The report records multiple historic flood events affecting the settlement and indicates it was affected by the flood event on 9 February 2020. (p9) [IMG, PAG]
* {01 Nov 1960 - 30 Nov 1960, 01 Nov 1963 - 30 Nov 1963, 01 Sep 1968 - 30 Sep 1968, 01 Oct 2000 - 31 Oct 2000, 2008, 2009, 01 Feb 2009 - 28 Feb 2009, 01 Nov 2009 - 30 Nov 2009, 01 Jul 2010 - 31 Jul 2010, 01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011, 01 Jan 2012 - 31 Jan 2012, 01 Sep 2012 - 30 Sep 2012, 01 Oct 2012 - 31 Oct 2012, 01 Mar 2013 - 31 Mar 2013, 01 Jul 2013 - 31 Jul 2013, 01 Dec 2013 - 31 Dec 2013, 01 Oct 2014 - 31 Oct 2014, 01 Feb 2015 - 28 Feb 2015, 01 Aug 2015 - 31 Aug 2015, 01 Aug 2017 - 31 Aug 2017, 01 Nov 2017 - 30 Nov 2017, 01 Jan 2018 - 31 Jan 2018, 01 Dec 2019 - 31 Dec 2019, 01 Jan 2020 - 31 Jan 2020}: Village flooded in association with River Medway on multiple historic occasions (river flooding in November 1960, November 1963, September 1968 and October 2000). The village also experienced repeated localized surface-water and sewer/road flooding incidents recorded between 2008 and January 2020 (blocked drains/gullies, blocked ditches and road flooding; some incidents caused internal property flooding). (p10) [TAB, PAG, MUL]
* The settlement experienced river (fluvial) flooding associated with the Alder Stream during the event; properties in the village were affected by overtopping flows. (p11) [IMG]
* On 9th February 2020 (Storm Ciara) intense rainfall led to Alder Stream overtopping through the centre of Five Oak Green and, concurrently, highway drainage and the local sewer network were overwhelmed and unable to discharge into the stream. Properties located along Alders Road were affected by the resulting flooding. Local natural flood management measures in the catchment were operating but were limited in extent and not considered likely to have significantly reduced flooding at Five Oak Green. (p12) [PAG]
* Surface water drainage in the main area of Five Oak Green surcharged and ponded because the watercourse overtopped, resulting in surface water flooding of local roads and internal flooding of properties (water entered properties and ponded around them). Fire and rescue attended multiple streets but could not pump properties because there was nowhere for surplus water to be discharged; the police implemented a road closure on Five Oak Road. (p13) [IMG, PAG]
* Experienced flooding during Storm Ciara (9 Feb 2020). Highway drains and the local combined sewer network were unable to cope; the culvert beneath Five Oak Green may have contributed to the flooding (potential surcharging of the culvert). (p16)
* Quant: flood depths up to approximately 30 cm internally (p13) [IMG, PAG]

### Alder Stream
* River levels rose quickly and overtopped the riverbanks; this overtopping contributed to overbank and overland flow into Five Oak Green, causing flooding of properties. (p1)
* Rapid rise in flows in the Alder Stream during intense rainfall on 9 February 2020, causing localised flooding in the Five Oak Green catchment. Peak flow on the Alder Stream was reported earlier (approximately 18:30–19:30) than peaks recorded on nearby gauged rivers. (p5)
* The Alder Stream was the locus of fluvial flood risk in Five Oak Green; areas adjacent to the stream (including along Alders Road and through the centre of Five Oak Green) experienced flooding consistent with reported properties affected during Storm Ciara. Flows are noted as being well contained within the watercourse upstream of Capel, but surface water flow paths and local depressions also contributed to flooding in the village. (p8)
* {general}: Flood Zone 2 and Flood Zone 3 are concentrated along Alder Stream on the Flood Map for Planning, indicating significant fluvial flood risk to adjacent low-lying areas and nearby settlement (Five Oak Green). (p9) [IMG]
* {01 Jan 2012 - 31 Jan 2012}: Alder Stream caused road flooding on Alders Road (recorded January 2012). (p10) [TAB, PAG]
* High flows in the Alder Stream overtopped banks during the flood event and contributed to flooding that affected the nearby settlement of Five Oak Green. (p11) [IMG, PAG]
* During Storm Ciara (9th February 2020) intense evening rainfall caused flows in Alder Stream to overtop its banks. Overtopping occurred upstream of the culvert under Five Oak Green Road; peak flows overtopped the channel and contributed to floodwater entering the locality while highway drainage and the local sewer network became overwhelmed and could not discharge to the stream. (p12) [IMG, PAG]
* Alder Stream overtopped its banks upstream of the culvert beneath Five Oak Green Road (overtopping reported at approximately 20:00). The overtopping caused the local surface water drainage system to be unable to discharge to the watercourse, leading to surcharging of drains and surface water flooding of roads and properties in central Five Oak Green. (p13) [IMG, PAG]
* Overtopped its banks during Storm Ciara (9 Feb 2020), producing high river levels that contributed to local drainage systems surcharging and to flooding in downstream areas. (p16)
* Quant: approximately 40 properties (p1)
* Quant: flood depths up to approximately 30 cm internally (reported in main area of Five Oak Green) (p13) [IMG, PAG]

### Southern Water
* Reports of hydraulic overload within the sewer network on 9 February 2020 resulting in sewerage flooding being reported to Southern Water; Southern Water provided flood-incident data but did not supply locations of the hydraulic overloads. (p2)
* A failure of the pumping station maintained by Southern Water (reported to have stopped during power cuts) contributed to sewerage backing up and internal flooding of nearby properties; Southern Water did not provide supporting information for the Section 19 report. (p14)
* Failure of the Larkfield pumping station (operated by Southern Water) due to a power cut caused internal sewerage flooding of nearby properties. Southern Water subsequently installed an automatic reset system at the pumping station to restore power without an engineer on site. (p15)
* Local combined sewer network was unable to cope with the volume of rainfall and surcharged; failure of the Larkfield pumping station led to internal sewerage flooding of nearby properties. (p16)

### Alders Catchment
* Localized flooding occurred in the Alders catchment during Storm Ciara on 9th February 2020, with the most significant impacts concentrated at Five Oak Green within KCC's area. (p4)
* The Alder Stream catchment (steep slopes with loamy/clayey soils and impeded drainage) generated high surface runoff during Storm Ciara, producing overland flow into the Alder Stream and contributing to downstream fluvial and surface-water flooding in Five Oak Green. (p8)
* The catchment produced peak river flows during the flood event; Natural Flood Management (leaky woody structures) were installed in a tributary of the catchment and were observed to be operating during the event. (p11) [IMG]
* Alder Stream overtopped its banks during the storm, producing high river levels that contributed to the local drainage systems surcharging and flooding in the catchment (including Five Oak Green). (p16)

### Larkfield Pumping Station
* The Larkfield pumping station (located in/near Larkfield) stopped operating during power cuts associated with Storm Ciara, leading to sewage backing up and internal flooding of nearby properties (notably on Badsell Road). (p14)
* The Larkfield pumping station failed due to a power cut, contributing to internal sewerage flooding of nearby properties. (p15) [PAG]
* Failure of the Larkfield pumping station due to a power cut during Storm Ciara (9 Feb 2020) caused internal sewerage flooding of nearby properties. (p16)

### Kent Fire and Rescue Service

(p13) [PAG]
