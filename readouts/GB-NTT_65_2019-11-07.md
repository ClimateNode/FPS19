

# Rhodesia flooding 07 Nov 2019 - 08 Nov 2019

## Place names with coordinates

### Rhodesia
* The village experienced extensive flooding as part of the wider Nottinghamshire flood event driven by intense rainfall on 7 November 2019. (p1)
* Significant local flooding on Mary Street: internal property flooding, public highway and some gardens/curtilages affected. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Sustained heavy rainfall and saturated agricultural land caused ordinary watercourses and ditch systems to overflow across this part of Rhodesia; surface water ran off land into gardens and onto the highway and increased volumes in the combined sewer network, contributing to internal property flooding in the area (notably Mary Street). (p3) [IMG]
* The area experienced significant surface water accumulation after intense rainfall; predicted surface water flows and observations indicate collection concentrated along Mary Street and adjacent to the ditch, with runoff entering gardens and onto the highway and a ditch overflowing. (p4) [IMG]
* Community experienced internal property flooding during the November event; local volunteers distributed sandbags from a community flood resilience store to reduce internal flooding; Rhodesia has a history of flooding (most recently 2007). (p5) [PAG]
* The community experienced flooding during the event; local volunteers distributed sandbags from a community flood resilience store and these were used to reduce internal property flooding. The text indicates properties were internally flooded during the event. (p6)
* Quant: 9 reported incidents of internal property flooding (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 9 reported incidents of internal property flooding (in Mary Street) (p3) [IMG]

### Nottinghamshire
* Nottinghamshire experienced extensive flooding due to heavy rainfall on already saturated ground, resulting in county-wide impacts including road closures, flood signage and sandbag distribution. (p1) [PAG]
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Severe weather across the county led to a major incident being declared by the Nottinghamshire Local Resilience Forum; following intense rainfall additional surface water and fluvial (river) flooding occurred; the Environment Agency monitored levels and issued warnings/alerts across the county. (p2)
* Quant: 66 road closures; over 750 flood signs; over 5000 sandbags (p1) [PAG]
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Quant: 38 flood warnings and 16 flood alerts issued across Nottinghamshire (p2)

### Nottingham Unitary Authority
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: County-wide severe weather led to additional surface water and fluvial (river) flooding; Nottinghamshire Local Resilience Forum declared a major incident in response and the Environment Agency monitored levels and issued warnings/alerts across the county. (p2) [IMG]
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Quant: 38 flood warnings; 16 flood alerts (p2) [IMG]

### East Midlands
* Parts of the region experienced an intense 24-hour rainfall (a month's worth in 24 hours) which led to flooding across the region. (p1)

### Worksop
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Severe weather and subsequent intense rainfall caused surface water and river flooding across the county; local resilience partners declared a major incident and the EA issued multiple warnings and alerts. (p2) [IMG]
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Quant: 38 flood warnings; 16 flood alerts (p2) [IMG]

### Mary Street, Rhodesia
* Significant localised flooding of properties with internal flooding incidents; flooding also affected the public highway and some gardens and curtilages. (p3) [IMG]
* Surface water from intense rainfall collected on the road in front of properties producing ponding that grew until it entered front doors. Additional runoff from land to the rear entered rear gardens. The combined public sewer was overloaded and was observed to be surcharging via manholes, adding to the flooding on the highway and properties. (p4) [IMG]
* Quant: 9 reported incidents of internal property flooding (p3) [IMG]
