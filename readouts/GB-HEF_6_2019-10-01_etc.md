

# Staunton-On-Wye flooding October 2019 and Staunton-On-Wye flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: North of Staunton On Wye<br>055\_Herefordshire County/05\_North\_of\_Staunton\_on\_Wye\_.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Herefordshire
* {October 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Internal flooding of residential properties north of Staunton on Wye caused by fluvial (river/tributary) flooding from the Letton Lake tributary / River Wye. Properties experienced depths typically 1 ft to 5 ft (outbuildings >6 ft). Flooding persisted for between 1 and 4 days. One property had water reach the top of the stairs, trapping residents for c.24 hours until rescued by the fire service. (p1) [IMG, TAB, MUL]
* {October 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 12 properties (February 16th 2020); 3 properties (October 2019); 2 properties flooded in both events; outbuildings >6 ft (p1) [IMG, TAB, MUL]

### Staunton-On-Wye
* {October 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Internal flooding of residential properties north of Staunton on Wye during fluvial events. Depths typically 1 ft to 5 ft (outbuildings recorded >6 ft). Flooding durations reported 1–4 days. One household reported water reaching top of stairs and occupants trapped for 24 hours until rescued by fire service. (p1) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
* {October 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Properties north of Staunton on Wye experienced internal flooding caused by overflow of the River Wye and/or its tributary. Flooding depths at affected properties were typically 1ft to 5ft (with outbuildings >6ft); respondents reported flood durations of 1 to 4 days; one property had residents trapped for 24 hours and rescued by the fire service; some properties reported historic flooding (1998, 2000, 2002, and ~20 years ago). (p2) [MUL]
* {October 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 12 properties (February 2020); 3 properties (October 2019); 2 properties flooded in both events (p1) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
* {October 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 12 properties internally flooded; 3 properties in October 2019; 2 properties flooded in both events; one property trapped occupants for 24 hours; depths 1ft to 5ft; >6ft in outbuildings; durations 1 to 4 days (p2) [MUL]

### Letton
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: The A438 (strategic highway) flooded at Letton; area experienced fluvial flooding linked to River Wye / the tributary Letton Lake as described in the report. (p2) [TAB]

### Hereford
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: Central Hereford recorded very high River Wye levels (the Wye reached its highest recorded level at Old Wye Bridge). (p2)

### Old Wye Bridge
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: The River Wye reached its highest recorded level at the Old Wye Bridge in central Hereford (high fluvial levels recorded at this asset). (p2)


## Topics without coordinates

### River Wye
* {October 2019, 01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: High River Wye levels overflowed and travelled across the common, causing or exacerbating flooding of properties north of Staunton on Wye. High Wye levels likely reduced the capacity of the tributary (Letton Lake), producing backwater effects and longer-duration, deeper flooding at properties. (p2) [MUL]
* {1998, 2000, 2002, October 2019, 01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: Stated as the primary source of flooding; many properties are within its flood plain and experienced flooding influenced by Wye levels, including long-duration inundation at some properties. (p3) [MUL]
* {October 2019, 01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: Quant: 4 properties reported an overflowing river/stream as the source; depths reported typically 1ft to 5ft (greater than 6ft in outbuildings); flood durations reported 1 to 4 days; 12 properties internally flooded; 2 properties flooded in both events; one property trapped occupants for 24 hours (p2) [MUL]
* {1998, 2000, 2002, October 2019, 01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: Quant: 3 properties (October 2019); 12 properties (February 2020) (p3) [MUL]

### Letton Lake
* {October 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Letton Lake (a tributary/ordinary watercourse) is implicated in the fluvial flooding that caused internal flooding of properties north of Staunton on Wye. The event led to property inundation with depths typically 1–5 ft (outbuildings >6 ft) and effects lasting 1–4 days. (p1) [PAG, MUL]
* {October 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Letton Lake (a tributary of the River Wye) overflowed across the common and contributed to internal and external flooding of nearby properties; high levels in the Wye may have impaired its capacity, exacerbating local flooding. The event is also associated with flooding of the A438 at Letton. (p2) [MUL]
* {October 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 12 properties (February 2020); 3 properties (October 2019); 2 properties flooded in both events (p1) [PAG, MUL]
* {October 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 1 property reported surface water coming from the direction of Letton; A438 flooded at Letton (p2) [MUL]

### A438
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: A438 (strategic highway) was flooded at Letton and recorded as an impact on the strategic highway network. (p2) [TAB]
