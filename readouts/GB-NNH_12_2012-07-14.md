

# Northamptonshire flooding 14 Jul 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Flooding on the A45 Eastbound between Great Doddington and Wilby Way 14th July 2012<br>090\_North Northamptonshire/A45-Flood-Investigaton-Report.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### A45 Between Great Doddington and Wilby Way
* Excess surface water flowed over and accumulated on the eastbound carriageway due to intense rainfall on a saturated catchment, inundation of the drainage network and an identified blockage. (p4)
* Road carriageway inundated by water flowing from an adjacent field and an inundated drainage network; the A45 (critical infrastructure) was closed and diversion routes put in place. (p5)
* Eastbound carriageway was inundated as water from an adjacent field crossed the carriageway due to an inundated and blocked drainage network; traffic flow disrupted, central reserve barrier damaged after a single RTC associated with the event; drainage blockage required specialist jetting to clear. (p6) [IMG, PAG]
* Surface water runoff from adjacent higher ground entered the carriageway; the highway drainage system (gullies, drains and drainage network) was completely inundated by the heavy rainfall and runoff, allowing water onto the carriageway. (p7) [PAG]
* Flooding on the A45 caused a full road closure and required manpower to alleviate flooding. The closure created knock-on impacts on the surrounding road network due to diversion routes, causing localised congestion. (p8)
* Photo (Highways Agency 14.07.12 09:55) and text reference indicate water running off surrounding land associated with flooding on the A45; surface water runoff affected the highway/roadside drainage and was a reported cause of flooding on this road. (p10)
* Intense rainfall onto a saturated catchment and an inundated/drainage network (with an identified blockage) caused excess surface water to flow over and accumulate on the carriageway of the A45 eastbound between Great Doddington and Wilby Way. (p11)
* Quant: 1 Road Traffic Collision causing central reserve barrier damage; suspected drainage blockage depth ~40 metres; 1 jetter (from Telford) attended and cleared the blockage (arrived 13:21, cleared 14:18) (p6) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: road closure duration: 7 hours and 8 minutes (p8)

### Northamptonshire
* A piece of critical infrastructure (the A45) within the county was impacted by surface water flooding and was closed, causing disruption within the county. (p4)
* A piece of critical infrastructure within the county (the A45) was affected by flooding and was closed; the event triggered a formal investigation by the Lead Local Flood Authority. (p5)

### Great Doddington
* The A45 eastbound from Great Doddington (as the western endpoint) experienced surface water flowing over and accumulating on the carriageway as a result of intense rainfall, drainage inundation and a blockage. (p4)
* The stretch of the A45 eastbound between the village and the Wilby Way roundabout was affected by flooding — carriageway inundated by runoff and an inundated/blocked drainage network leading to closures and traffic disruption. (p6) [IMG]

### Earls Barton
* Police closed the eastbound slip road and carriageway at Earls Barton and diversion routes were put in place due to flooding of the A45; closures contributed to traffic disruption. (p6) [IMG]

### Wilby Way, Great Doddington
* The A45 eastbound flooding extended to Wilby Way (as the eastern endpoint), with excess surface water flowing over and accumulating on the carriageway due to intense rainfall, inundated drainage and a blockage. (p4)

### Wilby Way Roundabout
* The A45 eastbound stretch up to the Wilby Way roundabout was affected by surface water flooding from adjacent land and a blocked drainage network, causing road closures and requiring drainage clearance. (p6)
