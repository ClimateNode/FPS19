## Topics without coordinates

### Staffordshire County Council
* {16 Feb 2020, 16 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020 - 17 Aug 2020}: As Lead Local Flood Authority, received reports of a significant number of flooding incidents across South Staffordshire District (including Great Wyrley, Cheslyn Hay and Featherstone); distributed flood questionnaires to residents; partnered with the Environment Agency and Severn Trent Water to investigate causes and identify actions. (p6) [MUL]
* {16 Feb 2020, 16 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020 - 17 Aug 2020}: Widespread flooding of highways and properties across Staffordshire prompted SCC, as the Lead Local Flood Authority, to undertake Section 19 flood investigations, publish investigation results and notify relevant risk management authorities. (p7) [MUL]
* {general}: Highway drainage and highway assets were exceeded during storm events, contributing to flooding of the highway and directing water towards properties; recommended actions include considering channel drainage and reviewing the gully cleaning regime. (p42) [TAB]
* {16 Jun 2020}: Staffordshire County Council (Highways) were notified and recommended to review and maintain drainage and highway assets within their remit in response to flooding. (p44) [TAB]
* {general}: Surface water accumulated on the highway; SCC (Highways) have been notified and works have been undertaken to redirect surface water accumulations and improve drainage infrastructure. SCC to consider reviewing the gully-cleaning regime; ongoing drainage maintenance is being carried out in line with policy. (p46) [TAB]
* {16 Jun 2020, 17 Aug 2020}: Highway drainage and gullies under SCC (Highways) were unable to adequately capture intense surface water runoff; highways channelled runoff and ponded in low points, spilling onto properties when capacity was exceeded and contributing to internal and external property flooding and highway flooding. (p50) [MUL]

### Midlands

### Black Country
* {16 Feb 2020, 16 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020 - 17 Aug 2020}: Storms on 16 February, 16 June and 16–17 August 2020 produced widespread flooding to highways and properties within the Black Country (including Walsall and Wolverhampton). (p7) [MUL]

### Severn Trent
* {16 Feb 2020, 16 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020 - 17 Aug 2020}: Partnered with Staffordshire County Council and the Environment Agency to investigate areas where internal property flooding was reported, to identify most likely causes (including flooding from sewer infrastructure); assisted distribution of questionnaires. (p6) [MUL]
* {general}: Sewer infrastructure was exceeded during the storm events and contributed to flooding of properties; Severn Trent Water have been notified and asked to ensure their assets perform as designed. (p42) [TAB]
* {16 Jun 2020, 17 Aug 2020}: The capacity of Severn Trent Water infrastructure was overwhelmed by the storm events, leading to sewer and drain flows escaping (eg. escaping from manholes) and contributing to flooding of properties and external areas. (p50) [MUL]

### Wyrley Brook
* {16 Jun 2020, 17 Aug 2020}: Flows and volumes along the Wyrley Brook likely exceeded available capacity at constrictions (culverts or changes in direction), causing backing up, overtopping of banks and spilling over, contributing to flooding of adjacent areas. (p50) [MUL]
* {general}: Wyrley Brook produced flooding in its catchment that affected properties and prompted questionnaires, asset surveys, flow monitoring and hydrological modelling to inform a collaborative flood alleviation scheme. (p51) [PAG]
* {general}: Hydrological analysis and modelling identify the Brook as the source of the flood event; affected properties within the Wyrley Brook catchment were contacted by Severn Trent Water and surveys/monitoring were carried out to inform a collaborative flood alleviation scheme. (p52) [PAG]

### South Staffordshire District Council
* {16 Feb 2020, 16 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020 - 17 Aug 2020}: Assisted Staffordshire County Council by distributing flood questionnaires to residents in, or close to, areas where flooding was reported; supported investigations into flood causes in the district. (p6) [MUL]

### Wyrley Brook Catchment
* {general}: Flooding from the Wyrley Brook affected properties within the catchment; the catchment (including areas through Great Wyrley and parts of Cheslyn Hay) has been identified for a collaborative flood alleviation scheme. (p51)
