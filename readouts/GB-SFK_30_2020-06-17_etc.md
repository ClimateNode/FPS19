## Place names with coordinates

### Icklingham
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Within Icklingham, surface water flooding affected one property on the southern edge of The Street (internal flooding, <0.3 m) and caused deep surface water flooding on the A1101 carriageway opposite those properties. (p2)
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Localized intense storm runoff along the A1101 through Icklingham caused surface-water flooding with internal and external property flooding and deep surface water on the carriageway at the village low point. (p3) [IMG, MUL]

### The Street, Icklingham
* {general}: Surface water entered a property on the southern edge of The Street (internal flooding) with depth reported as <0.3 m; the A1101 carriageway opposite these properties was also affected by deep surface water flooding. (p2) [PAG]
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Surface-water flooding of the carriageway (A1101) and internal and external flooding to a property on the southern edge of The Street; pooling of deep surface water in front of the property due to obstruction of the natural overland flow path. (p3) [MUL]
