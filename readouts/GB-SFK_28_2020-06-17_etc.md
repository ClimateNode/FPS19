

# Sicklesmere flooding 17 Jun 2020 and Sicklesmere flooding 16 Aug 2020

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Sudbury Rd (A134) Sicklesmere and Hambrook Close, Stanningfield Rd, Great Whelnetham<br>124\_Suffolk County/124\_2020\_Suffolk County/section-19-report-staningfield-rd-gt-whelnetham.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Hambrook Close, Sicklesmere
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Surface water entered properties with depths reported between 0.1–0.4 m; internal flooding of properties occurred. The nearby A134 carriageway opposite these properties was also affected by extensive deep surface water flooding. (p2) [MUL]
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Hambrook Close drains to a NW low point where drainage measures can be overwhelmed during intense storms, causing localised ponding that flooded gullies and led to water following exceedance routes north into properties; blocked drainage (root intrusion) and shallow fall caused gully surcharging and contributed to internal/external flooding risks to nearby houses. (p4) [PAG, MUL]
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: The drainage system was found blocked by heavy root intrusion where it meets Stanningfield Rd, with no identified positive outfall. Gullies were observed bubbling/surcharging as water backed up; houses lie within the natural exceedance route and driveway/alleyway obstructions exacerbated flooding. (p5)
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Highway drainage in Hambrook Close was blocked (heavy root intrusion at connection with Stanningfield Rd) causing gullies to surcharge and ‘bubble out’; as gullies surcharged water escaped into properties along the natural exceedance route, with drive elevations and alleyways obstructing escape routes and exacerbating flooding of those houses. (p6) [PAG]
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Surface water runoff impacted Hambrook Close; the report recommends investigating improvements to the road drainage system serving Hambrook Close. (p7) [TAB]
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Quant: 3 properties; A134 carriageway (p2) [MUL]
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Quant: Surface water <0.3m within 1 property in the described localised ponding area. (p4) [PAG, MUL]

### Stanningfield Road, Sicklesmere
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Fast, intense runoff along Stanningfield Road during the storms overwhelmed largely absent/blocked highway drainage, contributed to overland flow into the low point and led to internal and external flooding of downhill properties; Anglian Water foul chambers at the foot of Stanningfield Rd also burst and added to internal flooding. (p4) [PAG, MUL]
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Blockages within the road drainage at the A134/Stanningfield Rd junction caused surface water to back up. Anglian Water foul network chambers on Stanningfield Rd burst and flooded a private access and rear gardens, contributing to internal flooding experienced downhill. (p5)
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Roadside drainage and holding ditches on and above Stanningfield Road were severely choked/obstructed; obstruction of overland flow routes and blocked drainage contributed to surface water flooding. In addition, Anglian Water reported foul sewer chambers that flooded/burst at the foot of Stanningfield Road and adjacent rear gardens, which likely added to internal flooding experienced downhill. (p6) [PAG]
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Surface water runoff and blocked/insufficient road drainage affected Stanningfield Road; report recommends investigation and cleansing of road drainage, maintenance of local ditches at the top end of the road, and clearance to provide an escape route for water. (p7) [TAB]
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Quant: 0.1–0.4m surface water within properties; 3 properties affected (in the described low point area); ~600m of Stanningfield Rd largely undrained; combined road catchment ~5,200m2 of which ~4,000m2 effectively undrained. (p4) [PAG, MUL]
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Quant: 2 chambers reported as flooding (IDs 8203 and 9202) (p5)

### Sudbury Road, Sicklesmere
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: The A134 carriageway (Sudbury Road) experienced extensive deep surface water flooding opposite the affected properties; localised ponding in the highway resulted in surface water (<0.3 m) within one property. (p2) [MUL]
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: The A134 carriageway (the main north–south route identified on the map as Sudbury Road) opposite flooded properties was affected by extensive deep surface water flooding, causing road inundation and disruption to the carriageway. (p3) [IMG]
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Runoff from Sudbury Road (A134) converged with runoff from Stanningfield Rd and contributed to pooling at the low point; the A134 carriageway opposite the affected properties experienced extensive deep surface water flooding. (p4) [MUL]
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Quant: 1 property; A134 carriageway (p2) [MUL]
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Quant: 3 properties affected by 0.1–0.4m surface water in the described area; carriageway flooding on A134 opposite those properties. (p4) [MUL]

## Topics without coordinates

### Little Whelnetham Road
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: The low spot and pooling area extended as far as Little Whelnetham Road; this low spot is adjacent to a Flood Zone 3 watercourse and property defences there obstructed the natural flow path, resulting in pooling of deep surface water that contributed to property flooding downslope. (p4) [MUL]
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: The low spot at A134/Little Whelnetham Road experienced surface water flooding; report recommends creating overland escape routes into verges/ordinary watercourse to reduce flood depths at this location. (p6)

### A134
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: The A134 carriageway opposite the affected properties experienced extensive deep surface water pooling during the intense storms. Blockages in highway drainage at the A134/Stanningfield Rd junction (notably the penultimate chamber before the outfall) contributed to carriageway inundation; vehicles were observed being forced to displace water when driving through the flooded highway. (p4) [PAG, MUL]
* {17 Jun 2020}: The A134/Stanningfield Road junction and the A134/Little Whelnetham Road low spot were identified as locations of road drainage failure and low spot flooding; recommendations note overland escape routes are required to reduce depths of flooding on the road. (p6) [PAG]
* {17 Jun 2020}: A low spot on the A134 experienced surface water ponding/runoff; the report recommends investigation of this low spot and drainage improvements. (p7) [TAB]
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Quant: carriageway surface water depths reported up to 0.1–0.4 m opposite three properties; blockage observed at the A134/Stanningfield Rd junction (penultimate chamber). (p4) [PAG, MUL]

### Anglian Water
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Bursts in Anglian Water's foul network (chambers) on Stanningfield Road and in private/rear garden locations contributed to internal flooding experienced downhill. (p5)
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Anglian Water's foul sewer network burst/severely surcharged several chambers in Stanningfield Road; chambers reported as flooding at the foot of Stanningfield Road (ID 8203) in a private access and in rear gardens (ID 9202). This likely contributed to internal flooding experienced by properties downhill. (p6) [PAG]
* {17 Jun 2020}: Foul sewerage system and Sewage Treatment Works (STW) capacity issues were identified; chambers are described as currently surcharging, contributing to sewer flooding/pressures on the system. Anglian Water is to assess capacity and investigate improvements to reduce sewer-related flooding. (p7) [TAB, PAG]

### Suffolk County Council
* {17 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020}: Highway drainage blockages and shallow fall in the drainage system caused gully surcharging and back-up of water; blocked roadside ditches and obstructed overland flow routes led to water following exceedance routes through properties and highway, exacerbating property and highway inundation. (p5) [PAG]
* {17 Jun 2020}: Council-owned highway drainage and roadside assets in the area were described as blocked/failed and contributed to surface water flooding of roads and properties; SCC are the Lead Local Flood Authority and responsible for investigating and restoring drainage capacity. (p6) [PAG]

### SCC Highway
* {17 Jun 2020}: SCC Highways surveyed the drainage (July 2020) and found the highway drainage system blocked and failing (notably at Hambrook Close/Stanningfield Rd), which resulted in surcharging gullies and surface water flooding of highway and nearby properties; SCC Highways are tasked with cleansing, restoring and possibly upgrading highway drainage capacity. (p6) [PAG]
* {17 Jun 2020}: Highways drainage in the affected area was compromised, allowing runoff onto the highway. SCC Highways is responsible for investigating and cleansing existing road drainage and for investigating improvements to the road drainage system on Stanningfield Road, Hambrook Close and the A134 low spot. (p7) [TAB]
