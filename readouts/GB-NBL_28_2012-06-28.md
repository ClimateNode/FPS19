

# Middleton flooding 28 Jun 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Middleton<br>091\_Northumberland/Middleton-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Northumberland
* A localized ordinary-watercourse (Middleton Burn) flood event affected the village of Middleton in Northumberland, causing residential flooding (driveways and a garage) and threatening internal flooding in properties. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 4 properties (p1) [IMG]

### Middleton
* Intense rainfall caused Middleton Burn (an ordinary watercourse) to rise; culvert under the road reached full capacity and excess flow backed up. Floodwater inundated driveways of properties on both banks, entered the garage of one property, and posed a significant threat of internal flooding (internal flooding was prevented at that property by sandbags). Flooding was concentrated in the central residential zone adjacent to the burn. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 4 properties; garage of 1 property (p1) [IMG]

### Middleton Burn
* Intense rain caused a sharp rise in flow in the culverted Middleton Burn; with the culvert at capacity the flow backed up and flooded driveways on both banks. Flood water entered the garage of one property; internal flooding was prevented by sandbags. Water levels dropped sharply after the rain subsided. (p1) [IMG]
* The burn was swollen and in flood, with water levels significantly above normal — nearly reaching the top of a stone wall and overtopping or bypassing stone walls. Floodwater encroached onto a road/driveway and the entrance to a property (partially submerging it). Sandbags were deployed at the property entrance, and buildings were threatened by the high water. (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 4 properties (driveways flooded); 1 garage (water entered but internal flooding prevented) (p1) [IMG]
