

# Moreton On Lugg flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Moreton On Lugg<br>055\_Herefordshire County/13\_Moreton\_on\_Lugg.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### River Lugg
* Backwater effects from the River Lugg likely increased fluvial flood levels in the vicinity of Moreton on Lugg, contributing to internal flooding of nearby properties; at least one property on the east side experienced internal foul (sewage) flooding when drains backed up. Surface-water from a small tributary close to the east-side property also likely contributed and was mapped on the Surface Water Flood Map. (p2) [TAB]
* The River Lugg is identified as the source of fluvial flood risk affecting the area. (p3)
* Quant: Five properties reported internal flooding in Moreton on Lugg during the February 2020 event; the report table records 6 internally flooded properties related to the key flood event. (p2) [TAB]

### Moreton On Lugg
* Internal flooding of domestic properties occurred during the event: internal flood depths recorded between 6 inches and 3 ft. One home on the east side of the village was surrounded by floodwater and experienced internal foul (sewage) flooding when drains backed up into the property and garden. Flood risk from a small local watercourse and surface water may also have contributed. (p2)
* Quant: Five properties reported internal flooding in Moreton on Lugg during February 2020; report table records 6 internally flooded properties; internal depths recorded between 6 inches and 3 ft; one property had a previous flood in July 2007. (p2)
