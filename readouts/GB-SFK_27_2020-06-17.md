### Sicklesmere
* Residential area experienced surface water flooding when local surface water drainage capacity was exceeded during an intense storm. A total of 9 properties were internally flooded (up to 400 mm depth), a further 2 properties had external flooding; the main private forecourt and garages were flooded. Total affected area approx. 0.18 ha. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 9 properties internally flooded; 2 properties externally flooded; 0.18Ha; depth up to 400mm (p3) [IMG]

### St Thomas's Way, Sicklesmere
* Location of the flood event investigated under Section 19 by the Lead Local Flood Authority (Suffolk County Council). (p1)
* Properties and local assets on St Thomas's Way experienced flooding: nine properties flooded internally and two properties experienced external flooding. The main private forecourt and garages were flooded. The total affected area was approximately 0.18 ha (1,800 m2). (p2)
* Highway drainage is inadequate or blocked, allowing upslope runoff to travel down the slope and contribute to surface water flooding risk to the residential blocks; recommended actions include investigating and upsizing highway drainage, adding larger/more gully lids and sump pits, installing double lids toward the bottom of the slope, and providing drains for footways currently without them. (p5)
* Quant: 9 properties internally flooded; 2 properties externally flooded; 0.18Ha (1800m2) affected (p2)
