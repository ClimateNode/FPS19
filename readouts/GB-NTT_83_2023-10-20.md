### Trowell
* Sustained heavy rainfall associated with Storm Babet on 20th October 2023, on already saturated ground, caused flooding in the village with roads and properties flooded. (p1)
* Localized internal property flooding across the village; internal flooding affected residential streets listed in the report. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Trowell held a public meeting concerning flood risk; community officers and NCC visited affected communities and residents; a community flood signage scheme and development of a local flood action group are underway. The community was affected by the flooding event (Storm Babet) prompting these actions. (p6) [PAG]
* Quant: 25 properties (p2) [IMG, PAG]

### Boundary Brook
* The Boundary Brook overtopped Trowell Road (A6007) and was the route of surface water flood risk in the area; its overtopping caused floodwater to flow over the road and into adjacent gardens and properties. (p3)
* Boundary Brook is the focus of post-event inspections to identify maintenance opportunities and to gather evidence to validate the Flood Warning Service; upstream flows into Boundary Brook are being managed via planning to reduce future flood risk, indicating it was implicated in the local flood impacts. (p6)


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Officers from across the County Council played a key role in the response to a Major Incident caused by Boundary Brook overtopping Trowell Road (A6007); the Council led emergency response and recovery actions following the flooding. (p4) [PAG]
* Led county-wide flood response and short-term recovery: initiated Community Flood Recovery Grant for those affected by internal flooding; attended sites to verify internal flooding and to offer support, guidance and advice to affected residents and businesses; collated and published verified flood impact data on Resilience Direct; visited affected residents and businesses to establish sources, mechanisms and severity of flooding; chaired and attended Strategic/Tactical/Recovery Coordination Group meetings; provided 24-hour Emergency Planning support; delivered sandbags; closed roads across the county to promote safety during flooding. (p5)
* As Lead Local Flood Authority, NCC witnessed and responded to community flooding (including in Trowell) during the event. Actions described include instigating flood patrols, attending a public meeting in Trowell, coordinating community information officers and surveyors, visiting affected residents, commissioning effectiveness surveys of installed Property Flood Resilience (PFR) measures, and prioritising support for the most vulnerable. (p6)
* Quant: 77 roads closed (p5)

### Highway Authority
* A culvert beneath Trowell Road (A6007), owned and maintained by the Highway Authority, was overtopped when Boundary Brook overtopped the road. Water then flowed through the gardens of adjacent properties and toward Cranbourne Close. (p4)

### Broxtowe Borough Council
* Provided emergency response support during the flooding; delivered sandbags to affected residents; managed and continues to manage recovery in flood-affected Broxtowe communities; administered a flood hardship fund and council tax/business rate relief grants; actively engaged in the Section 19 Flood Investigation. (p5)
