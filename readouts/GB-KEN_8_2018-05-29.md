### Swale
* The Swale area was affected by flooding. (p1)
* Multiple flood events were recorded across the district on 29 May 2018, with clustering of event locations near Sittingbourne and along the main east–west corridor towards Faversham. Both urban and adjacent rural locations within the district were affected. (p9) [IMG]
* On 29 May 2018 intensive rainfall produced surface runoff in parts of the district (notably the Iwade catchment underlain by less permeable soils). Highway drainage dependent on soakaways and infiltration features was overwhelmed, causing localized surface water/overland flow and flooding of carriageways and low-lying areas. (p12)
* Heavy rainfall across Swale led to high flows in the Iwade Stream and surface water flooding; water from an adjacent field overtopped a low bund and flooded Meadow Brown View and Purple Emperor Grove on the south‑western edge of Iwade; overland runoff from fields and the highway caused flooding at Culnells Farm businesses (Iwade area). (p21)
* Teynham, within the borough of Swale, experienced surface water/urban flooding on 29 May 2018 that affected properties in the village and parish; drainage was overwhelmed causing water to pond in low-lying areas and reach depths sufficient to enter properties. (p29)
* Widespread post‑flood highway drainage maintenance: gullies and soakaways across the Swale area were inspected and cleansed following the May 2018 flood to restore free flow in the highway drainage network. (p35)
