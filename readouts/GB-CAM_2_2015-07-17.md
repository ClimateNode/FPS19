

# Barrington flooding 17 Jul 2015

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Barrington<br>021\_Cambridgeshire County/021\_2014\_Cambridgeshire County/Barrington flood investigation.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Barrington
* Formal flood incident recorded affecting various locations in Barrington on 17 July 2015 (Formal Flood Investigation reference FI/1/60). Risk Management Authorities recorded as Environment Agency and Cambridgeshire County Council. (p2) [TAB]
* Extremely intense, localised rainfall overwhelmed drainage networks and inundated watercourses and the road network in the village, producing surface water on roads, overtopping of watercourses and surcharging of local drains; at least three properties in the community flooded internally. (p3) [IMG, TAB]
* Area experienced flooding associated with the incident on 17 July 2015; properties were flooded and the County will add Barrington to its list of wetspots. (p4)
* Quant: At least 3 properties flooded internally (p3) [IMG, TAB]

### Haslingfield Road, Barrington
* The culverted outfall that runs under Haslingfield Road was implicated in the flooding incident and is the focus of the commissioned surface water drainage assessment. (p4)


## Topics without coordinates

### Cemex
* Consulted as the owner of a nearby closed quarry in relation to flood risk for the Barrington incident. (p2)
* Cemex, which owns a disused quarry near the flooded location, undertook remedial measures after the incident and commissioned consultants to prepare a surface water drainage report addressing the combined impact of two nearby planning applications on a culverted outfall running under Haslingfield Road. Cemex also commissioned JBA Consulting to assess the rainfall event probability. (p3) [TAB]
* Following the flooding incident on 17 July 2015 near its disused quarry, Cemex undertook remedial measures and commissioned consultants to prepare a surface water drainage report assessing the combined impact of two planning applications on the culverted outfall under Haslingfield Road. (p4)
* Quant: at least three properties (p3) [TAB]

### Cambridgeshire County Council
* Identified in the report as the Lead Local Flood Authority (LLFA) for Barrington and recorded as a Risk Management Authority to which the incident was reported; responsible for ordinary watercourses, surface water and groundwater matters in relation to the incident. (p2) [TAB]
* Following the extreme rainfall, County Highways arranged cleansing and surveying of the surface water drains they manage (work started and was due to finish in 2016/17). The County (as LLFA) will review future development applications, add Barrington to its wetspots list and provide advice to affected residents. (p3) [TAB]
* Quant: at least three properties (p3) [TAB]

### Environment Agency
* Recorded as a Risk Management Authority to which the incident was reported and as the reporter of the incident; the table identifies the Environment Agency as the RMA for main river, reservoirs and the sea in relation to flood sources. (p2)

### Anglian Water
* Identified as the water company RMA relevant to the incident; the table records responsibility for surface water sewer flooding from water company systems and foul sewer flooding. (p2)
