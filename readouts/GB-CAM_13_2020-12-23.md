

# Brampton flooding 23 Dec 2020

## Place names with coordinates

### Brampton
* {general}: Land to the south of Brampton is at risk of fluvial (river) flooding from the River Great Ouse and associated tributaries (largely constrained to the floodplain). Surface water flooding affects some isolated patches of the village (primarily roads and streets) where saturated ground increases runoff and causes ponding. Flooding risk from sewers is also identified. (p4)
* {general}: The village is at risk of flooding from local watercourses (which feed into the River Great Ouse), from surface water ponding on roads/streets, and from sewer flooding. Flooding can pond in certain parts of the village and land to the south is at risk from fluvial flooding constrained to the floodplain; flood risk is exacerbated when the River Great Ouse remains high and contributory watercourses cannot discharge efficiently. (p5) [PAG, IMG]
* Significant flooding from surface water sources caused by intense short-duration rainfall on already saturated ground, producing very high runoff that led to flooding in Brampton. (p7)
* Significant surface-water/urban flooding across the area; 24 flood reports were received by the LLFA; roads and gardens were flooded; internal flooding to residential properties occurred. (p9) [PAG]
* Area-wide flooding in Brampton with 24 flood reports to the LLFA. Internal flooding affected residential properties (roads, gardens and some public/green land were also flooded). Thrapston Road area: two residential properties, industrial outbuildings, gardens, land and fields flooded to a maximum depth of approximately 500 mm; residents and businesses were evacuated for up to one month. (p10) [PAG, IMG]
* Areas around Brampton experienced surface water flooding due to exceptional runoff (soil capacity vastly exceeded) and concurrent high flows entering the River Great Ouse from other watercourses, which exceeded channel capacity. The LLFA carried out site walkovers and met residents known to have flooded as part of the investigation. (p13) [TAB, PAG]
* Flooding occurred in the village, particularly the south of Brampton where nearby watercourses are thought to have contributed to flooding; maintenance actions have been taken but further work is required to ensure watercourses convey water away from the village effectively. (p18)
* Quant: 24 flood reports; 9 residential properties flooded internally (p9) [PAG]
* Quant: 24 flood reports; 9 residential properties flooded internally; 2 residential properties (Thrapston Road); up to 1 month evacuations; max depth c.500 mm (p10) [PAG, IMG]

### Brampton Park Golf Club
* {general}: Mapped within the area influenced by Flood Zone 2 on the Brampton flood risk map, adjacent to parkland and watercourses. Indicates a risk of fluvial (river) inundation from nearby watercourses and of surface-water ponding on the course during sustained rainfall. (p5) [PAG, IMG]
* Flood incident cluster located adjacent to Brampton Golf CP was identified as one of three areas where internal property flooding occurred during the event (map circle 2 / 'The Fairways'). (p10) [PAG, IMG]
* Surface water runoff from a saturated catchment caused a tributary and an on‑channel pond to exceed capacity on 23rd December 2020; floodwaters travelled north, inundating operational buildings of the golf club and the golf club car park and contributing to flooding of nearby residential properties. (p12)
* Flooding prompted the club (and landowners/operators) to renew inlet and outlet culverts for an on‑channel pond, perform channel maintenance on sections of the watercourse on club land, and engage in discussions about groundworks to reduce future flood risk; unconsented works by a private landowner were also identified during the post‑flood investigations. (p13) [TAB, PAG]
* Flooding associated with the adjacent riparian watercourse affected the golf course (including The Fairways area and an on-channel pond). Works undertaken in response included renewal of inlet and outlet culverts for the on-channel pond, channel maintenance on sections of the watercourse on club land, and planning of groundworks and riparian maintenance to reduce future inundation risk. (p14)

* Surface water flooding inundated one residential property, its garden and outbuildings. Local drainage was overwhelmed by exceptional runoff and concurrent high flows in the River Great Ouse. (p13) [TAB, PAG]
* The Park Road area experienced increased flood risk during the event; the LLFA is working with Highways England to determine whether the new A14 has contributed to that increased flood risk and RMA operational issues were noted. (p18) [PAG]

* Quant: 1 residential property (p13) [TAB, PAG]

### Thrapston Road, Brampton
* Identified as one of three key risk areas where internal property flooding was reported during the event; included among the most significantly impacted areas. (p9) [PAG]
* Two residential properties, outbuildings, gardens, land and fields were flooded (surface water ponding exacerbated by backing up of the drainage system caused by high flows in Alconbury Brook). Residents and businesses were forced to evacuate properties for up to one month. Floodwaters held upstream at Alconbury prolonged discharge through this area. (p11) [TAB]
* Flooding from Alconbury Brook affected the north of the village in the Thrapston Road area; Risk Management Authority operational issues were identified and measures are being explored to reduce future risk. (p18) [PAG]
* Fluvial (river) flooding affected Thrapston Road and the report records associated RMA operational issues at this location. (p19) [TAB]
* Quant: 2 residential properties; c.500mm depth; up to 1 month evacuation (p11) [TAB]

### East Anglia
* Widespread surface-water flooding resulting from intense afternoon rainfall on 23rd December 2020 combined with very wet antecedent conditions (high water table, saturated ground) that produced very high runoff across the region. (p7) [PAG]
* On 23rd December 2020 East Anglia experienced intense heavy rainfall on saturated ground (very low soil moisture deficit), producing very high runoff and significant surface-water flooding across the region. These conditions contributed to localized flooding incidents such as those reported in Brampton. (p9) [PAG, IMG]

### Alconbury
* Floodwaters were held upstream at Alconbury, which prolonged discharge through downstream areas (including Thrapston Road) and contributed to extended flooding impacts. (p11)

### Alconbury Brook
* {general}: Flood Zone 3 is concentrated along Alconbury Brook, indicating high risk of fluvial (river) flooding affecting adjacent low-lying areas north and east of Brampton; the brook is a primary determinant of local flood zones and can contribute to extended high water when discharge is impeded. (p5) [PAG, IMG]
* Alconbury Brook experienced a significant increase in flow which caused the downstream drainage system to back up and contributed to surface water flooding at Thrapston Road; its high flows also meant floodwaters were held upstream and discharged over a longer period. (p11)
* Alconbury Brook contributed to flooding affecting the north of the village (Thrapston Road area). Measures to alleviate flooding at Alconbury Brook are being explored by the Alconbury Brook Flood Partnership to reduce this flood risk. (p18)

### Stirling Farm
* {general}: Identified within surrounding farmland on the map where Flood Zone 2 extends from the watercourses; Stirling Farm is therefore at risk of fluvial flooding from nearby watercourses and of surface-water ponding during sustained or intense rainfall events. (p5) [PAG, IMG]
* Flood incident cluster located near Stirling Farm was identified as one of three areas where internal property flooding occurred during the event; properties in this area are part of the cluster labelled 'The Fairways' on the report map. (p10) [PAG, IMG]

### A141 at Brampton
* A flood incident cluster is shown north‑west of Brampton near the A141 (map circle 1); the report identifies that one of the three circled areas experienced internal property flooding during the event. (p10) [PAG, IMG]
