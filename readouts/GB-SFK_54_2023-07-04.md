## Place names with coordinates

### Oulton Road, Lowestoft
* Subject of a Section 19 flood investigation by Suffolk County Council (Lead Local Flood Authority). (p1)
* {2015, 04 Jul 2023 - 05 Jul 2023}: Internal flooding of the main living area of a dwelling associated with this address; flooding of the road outside the properties. (p3) [MUL]
* Internal flooding of the main living area of dwellings and flooding of the road outside the properties. Local surface water drainage (highway drains and Anglian Water surface water sewer) was exceeded during the event. (p4) [PAG, IMG]
* Nighttime surface water flooding covered much of the road surface; water ponded across the carriageway with visible reflections and debris. Adjacent properties that are lower than the road are naturally vulnerable to flows and ponding when surface water drainage is blocked, presenting a threat to those properties. (p6) [IMG, PAG]
* Flooding on Oulton Road is recorded (Figure 4). The event prompted gully maintenance on Oulton Road (May 2023) as part of the response. (p7) [TAB, PAG]
* {2015, 04 Jul 2023 - 05 Jul 2023}: Quant: 1 dwelling (part of the two dwellings internally flooded during the event) (p3) [MUL]
* Quant: two dwellings; one of these had been previously flooded in 2015 (p4) [PAG, IMG]

### Millenium Way, Lowestoft
* A 525mm surface water pipe from Millenium Way connects into a single 500mm main upstream of Worlingham Way; the report identifies that this configuration (larger upstream pipes feeding a smaller single pipe) is susceptible to exceedance during severe/intense rainfall and could have contributed to surface water flooding in the catchment. (p5)

### Cavendish Close, Oulton Broad
* A 225mm pipe from Cavendish Close connects into the local Anglian Water surface water sewer; the report notes the surface water drainage system could be susceptible to exceedance during intense rainfall and this connection may have been a factor in recent surface water flooding. (p5)

### Berkeley Gardens, Lowestoft
* A 150mm pipe from Berkeley Gardens connects into the surface water sewer network; the report states that multiple incoming pipes into smaller downstream pipes make the system susceptible to exceedance during severe rainfall and this may have contributed to surface water flooding. (p5)


## Topics without coordinates

### Anglian Water
* The Anglian Water surface water sewer network serving the area is described as being susceptible to exceedance during the severe rainfall because larger upstream pipes discharge into a smaller downstream pipe; exceedance of this surface water sewer system was identified as a possible factor in the recent flooding (further assessment required). (p5)
* {general}: Identified as an asset owner required to assess the maintenance and capacity of surface water drainage assets; latest update records maintenance and capacity assessment as outstanding. (p7) [TAB]

### Suffolk County Council
* {01 Apr 2023 - 30 Apr 2023, 01 May 2023 - 31 May 2023, 01 Jun 2023 - 30 Jun 2023}: As Highway Authority and Lead Local Flood Authority SCC carried out gully cleansing (Worlingham Way June 2023; Oulton Road May 2023; roundabout outer circle April 2023), commissioned cleansing and jetting across the area and undertook some survey/investigation; capacity assessment of surface water assets outstanding. (p7) [TAB, MUL]
