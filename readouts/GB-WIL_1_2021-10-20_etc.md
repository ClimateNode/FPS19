

# Donhead St Andrew flooding 20 Oct 2021 and Donhead St Andrew flooding 31 Oct 2021

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Donhead St Andrew<br>145\_Wiltshire/145\_2014\_Wiltshire/S19\_Donhead\_St\_Andrew\_Oct21.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Donhead St Andrew
* {20 Oct 2021, 31 Oct 2021}: Multiple properties in the village were flooded by a combination of fluvial flooding from the River Nadder (river overtopping bank capacity) and localised pluvial/surface water runoff; old mill properties adjacent to the Nadder were directly threatened by river flooding. The Nadder reached its highest recorded level on the 20–21 October event and surveyed levels behind Court Street matched a 150‑year return period model, supporting significant river overtopping. (p1) [MUL]
* {20 Oct 2021, 31 Oct 2021}: Properties in the parish experienced pluvial (surface water) flooding caused by local high‑intensity rainfall; combined with the Nadder coming out of bank, this produced property and highway flooding. Surface water from higher land flowed onto and along the highway (using it as a conduit) and overwhelmed the highway drainage system. A programme of repairs and improvements to aid highway safety is underway. (p2) [MUL]

### Court Street, Tisbury
* {20 Oct 2021 - 21 Oct 2021}: A surveyor recorded a water level against the flood scheme behind Court Street that matched the modelled 150-year return period outline, indicating river levels at this location reached that design level during the 20–21 October 2021 event. (p1)

### River Nadder
* {20 Oct 2021, 31 Oct 2021}: The River Nadder reached its highest recorded level during the 20–21 October 2021 event; it flowed at bank capacity and, when combined with intense localised pluvial runoff, came out of bank and caused fluvial flooding through the parish, threatening riverside properties including the old mills. (p1) [PAG, MUL]
* {20 Oct 2021}: The Nadder came out of bank during the event; river-based flooding followed the EA Flood Zone 3 extents. The river was driven to bank capacity by the base heavy storm rainfall and, with local intense rainfall flowing overland into the Nadder, produced out-of-bank flows that contributed to flooding in the area. (p2)


## Topics without coordinates

### South Wiltshire
* {20 Oct 2021, 31 Oct 2021}: Two storm events (20 Oct 2021 primary; 31 Oct 2021 lesser) caused combined fluvial and pluvial flooding affecting Donhead St Andrew and surrounding areas in South Wiltshire; multiple properties in the village and surrounding area were flooded. (p1) [MUL]

### Wiltshire Council
* {20 Oct 2021, 31 Oct 2021}: Combined river Nadder out-of-bank flows (river flood) and intense local pluvial rainfall (surface water flooding) caused surface water to flow onto and along the highway, overwhelming the highway drainage system; CCTV surveys found the drainage was functioning but overwhelmed during the storm events and age-related defects were identified; a programme of repairs and improvements to highway drainage is underway in response. (p2) [MUL]
