

# Morpeth flooding 25 Sep 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Morpeth<br>091\_Northumberland/Morpeth-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Morpeth
* Morpeth experienced combined river and surface-water flooding on 25/09/2012. The River Wansbeck burst its banks producing a peak recorded at the town gauge; 45 residential properties were flooded. Surface-water and river flooding affected multiple streets (High Stanners flooded from 04:45, Dawson Place flooded around 06:00–07:00, Copper Chare affected by the Cotting Burn), Mitford Road flooded from the River Wansbeck (surface water had begun earlier), Oldgate Bridge was closed during the event, and evacuations of residents (e.g. High Stanners) took place. (p1) [IMG]
* {1963, 2008, 25 Sep 2012}: The town experienced flooding during the event on 25 September: High Stanners flooded (commenced 04:45); Dawson Place flooded from the Cotting Burn (~06:00–07:00); Oldgate Bridge was closed (~06:15–07:00); a fallen tree on Curly Kews hindered the evacuation route; evacuation of residents began in High Stanners and ended at 11:00; 1000 Mitford Road began to flood from the River Wansbeck at 10:00. The town also has historic major floods (2008 and 1963). (p2) [TAB, MUL]
* Residential areas and streets in Morpeth were heavily flooded; streets submerged to ankle/knee depth with residents wading through water, vehicles partially submerged and emergency services conducting rescues. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 45 residential properties; 1 road (Mitford Road) (p1) [IMG]
* {1963, 2008, 25 Sep 2012}: Quant: approximately 1000 properties (2008); 482 properties (1963) (p2) [TAB, MUL]
* Quant: 1 partially submerged car (p3) [IMG, PAG]

### Mitford Road, Morpeth
* Surface water flooding affected the road earlier in the event and Mitford Road began to flood from the River Wansbeck (reportedly beginning to flood at 10:00 on 25/09/2012). (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* 1000 Mitford Road began to flood from the River Wansbeck at 1000; by 1100 the evacuation of residents had ended and the river peak had passed. (p2) [MUL]
* Mitford Road was heavily flooded with water covering the entire road surface; several people were walking through above‑ankle floodwater and terraced houses on both sides were fronted by floodwater. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 1 road (p1) [IMG, PAG]

### High Stanners, Morpeth
* Flooding commenced in High Stanners at 04:45 on 25/09/2012 and residents were evacuated. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* High Stanners was the first area to experience flooding (commenced at 0445 on 25 September) and residents were evacuated from this area. (p2)

### Curly Kews, Morpeth
* A fallen tree on Curly Kews hindered the evacuation route for residents during the flood event. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* A fallen tree on Curly Kews around 0615–0700 hindered the evacuation route for residents during the flood event. (p2)

### Abbey Terrace, Morpeth
* Residential properties on Abbey Terrace were flooded during the Morpeth flood event. (p1) [IMG]

### Dawson Place, Morpeth
* Flooding from the Cotting Burn began around 0600–0700, causing Dawson Place to flood. (p2)

### River Wansbeck
* The River Wansbeck burst its banks in Morpeth in the early hours of 25/09/2012, causing widespread flooding in the town; floodwaters from the river inundated Mitford Road and contributed to property inundation in Morpeth. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* {1963, 2008, 25 Sep 2012}: Flooding from the River Wansbeck caused inundation at 1000 Mitford Road beginning at 10:00 on 25 September; the river peak had passed by 11:00. The River Wansbeck is also implicated in historic major floods in Morpeth (2008, 1963). (p2) [MUL]
* Quant: 45 properties; 1 road (Mitford Road) (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* {1963, 2008, 25 Sep 2012}: Quant: approximately 1000 properties (2008); 482 properties (1963) (p2) [MUL]

### Oldgate Bridge
* Oldgate Bridge (location of the Morpeth level gauge) was closed during the flood event due to high river levels; the Morpeth level gauge recorded the flood peak for the event. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* Oldgate Bridge was closed around 0615–0700 due to the flood event. (p2)
* Quant: 27.11 m AOD (recorded peak level at Morpeth level gauge) (p1) [IMG, PAG]

### Cotting Burn
* Flows from the Cotting Burn came out of bank and caused flooding in the Copper Chare area of central Morpeth, resulting in internal flooding of properties. (p1) [PAG]
* Quant: 8 properties (p1) [PAG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Wansbeck Catchment
* Following sustained heavy rainfall within the River Wansbeck catchment the River Wansbeck burst its banks in Morpeth in the early hours of 25/09/2012. A peak level of 27.11m AOD was recorded at the Morpeth gauge. Flooding associated with the river event affected properties in Morpeth and flows from tributaries (eg Cotting Burn) came out of bank contributing to flooding in central Morpeth. (p1)
* Quant: 45 properties; 700 properties (p1)

### Environment Agency
* {24 Sep 2012, 25 Sep 2012}: The Environment Agency issued a Flood Alert at 18:00 on 24/09/2012 and upgraded this to Flood Warnings at 04:00 and 07:30 on 25/09/2012. Existing Environment Agency flood defences protected approximately 700 properties during the event. (p1) [IMG, MUL]
* {24 Sep 2012, 25 Sep 2012}: Quant: 700 properties (p1) [IMG, MUL]

### Northumberland Fire and Rescue
* Emergency rescue team deployed and operating an inflatable rescue boat in knee‑deep floodwater on a residential street in Morpeth, conducting flood response operations. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 3 rescue workers; 1 inflatable rescue boat; 1 partially submerged car (p3) [IMG]
