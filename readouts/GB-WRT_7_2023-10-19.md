

# Warrington Unitary Authority flooding 19 Oct 2023 - 20 Oct 2023

## Place names with coordinates

### Mustard Lane, Warrington Unitary Authority
* Flooding affected properties on Mustard Lane due to overwhelmed drainage and failure of underground pipework (including failure of the pipe adjacent to the chamber near No.1 Mustard Lane), causing internal and external flooding of properties. (p6) [TAB]
* {general}: Designated by the Environment Agency Flood Map for Planning as Flood Zone 1 (land having less than a 1 in 1000 annual probability of river or sea flooding) — considered low probability of river or sea flooding. Historic mapping shows no obvious historic watercourse or ponds/drainage features through the area. (p16)
* Surface water/sewer-related flooding along Mustard Lane required sandbags to be deployed to control flow. Highway gullies on Mustard Lane (south of the Memorial Hall) operated and drained excess water. The existing drainage/sewer infrastructure runs under Mustard Lane and conveyed significant volumes of highway, property and land drainage into the area during the event. (p18) [IMG]
* Overland flow was controlled with sandbags delivered by the Council; highway gullies to the south of the Memorial Hall drained excess water. The statutory maps and CCTV show a combined public sewer and a larger drainage system running along Mustard Lane; defects identified included a failed pipe adjacent to the chamber near No.1 Mustard Lane (creating a significant void) and a defective liner that was later removed. The drainage system was cleaned to restore operation, but pipe weakening and subsequent failure occurred due to pressures during the event. (p19) [PAG]
* A failed section of the drainage system was confirmed 1.0m from the chamber outside No.1 Mustard Lane, extending for approximately 10.0m upstream. The failure is attributed to increased pressure on the drainage system during Storm Babet; open-cut repair was required and completed in January 2024. Final remedial works include installing a continuous liner for the full length of the system to the chamber in Sandy Lane (approx. 50.0m). (p20) [PAG]
* Flooding to property on Mustard Lane between 19 and 20 October 2023 was attributed in the report to factors including defects following lining works to the local drainage system; properties on Mustard Lane were directly affected and repairs (a continuous liner) have been required. (p21) [PAG]
* Properties on Mustard Lane were flooded between 19 and 20 October 2023 due to surcharging of the drainage system driven by Storm Babet and prolonged heavy rainfall. Contributing factors cited include lack of drainage capacity for overland flow, significant tree roots, failure of a CIPP liner in sections (installed circa 2016) and failure of a pipe adjacent to a chamber near No1 Mustard Lane which created a significant void. (p22)
* Quant: 2 properties (internal flooding); 2 properties (external flooding) (p6) [TAB]

### Croft
* The village experienced flooding driven by prolonged heavy rainfall, saturated catchment and insufficient drainage capacity; drainage failures led to property flooding and localised infrastructure impacts. (p6) [IMG, TAB]
* {general}: The area contains a significant north–south surface water flow path and includes assets (Croft Primary School and Croft Memorial Hall) shown at high risk of surface water flooding; indicates parts of Croft are susceptible to localised surface water inundation. (p16) [IMG]
* Surface water and sewer-related flooding concentrated in a localised topographic depression around Croft Memorial Hall: water was reported to be surcharging from a sewer chamber on Sandy Lane, flowing down the road and accumulating in the low area around Croft Memorial Hall. The hall is verified on site as being in a localised depression that collects this water. (p17) [IMG, PAG]
* Areas within Croft experienced surface water/sewer-related flooding during the event: water surcharged from a chamber on Sandy Lane, flowed along Mustard Lane and accumulated around the Memorial Hall area, and Croft Primary School experienced internal flooding via underground ducting. (p18) [IMG]
* Surface water and surcharging drainage flowed from a chamber on Sandy Lane down the road and accumulated in the low area around Croft Memorial Hall. Flooding to Croft Primary School occurred when water entered the building via underground ducting (ankle-deep, restricted to a couple of rooms). (p19) [PAG, IMG]
* Quant: 4 properties (total reported: 2 internal, 2 external) (p6) [IMG, TAB]
* Quant: a couple rooms (at Croft Primary School) (p18) [IMG]
* Quant: 2 rooms (Croft Primary School) (p19) [PAG, IMG]

### Warrington Unitary Authority
* The incident in Croft (Warrington) was driven by Storm Babet and prolonged heavy rainfall, combined with drainage system failures (liner failure, pipe failure adjacent to a chamber near No1 Mustard Lane) and lack of drainage capacity; this resulted in internal flooding to two properties on Mustard Lane (Croft Memorial Hall and Croft Primary School) and external flooding to two further properties in the vicinity. (p6) [TAB, PAG]
* {18 Oct 2023 - 21 Oct 2023}: Flooding event in Warrington during October 2023. Included external flooding to properties in Croft (Mustard Lane). (p7)

(p23) [TAB, PAG]
