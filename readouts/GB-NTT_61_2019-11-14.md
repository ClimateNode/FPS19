### Nottinghamshire
* {07 Nov 2019, 14 Nov 2019}: Intense rainfall resulted in flooding across parts of Nottinghamshire (including Jacksdale). The event led to a major incident declaration and county-wide response measures (road closures, flood signage and sandbag distribution). (p1) [PAG, MUL]
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Experienced intense rainfall leading to additional surface water and fluvial (river) flooding; Nottinghamshire Local Resilience Forum declared a major incident; numerous flood warnings and alerts were issued for the county. (p2)
* {07 Nov 2019, 14 Nov 2019}: Quant: 66 road closures; over 750 flood signs; over 5000 sandbags (p1) [PAG, MUL]
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Quant: 38 flood warnings and 16 flood alerts (p2)

### Selston Road, Jacksdale
* The affected area is located at the junction of Selston Road and the B6016; surface water collecting at this low spot overwhelmed drainage and caused flooding that affected the public highway and adjacent properties/businesses. (p3) [IMG]
* Surface-water flowpaths converge toward Selston Road (notably at the junction with Main Road), causing surface-water flooding in the area marked on the map. The context also notes a drainage channel/gully in a local low spot (approx. 300 mm below the kerb edge) that contributes to local pooling. (p5) [IMG]
* Surface water flowed from upstream along the Selston Road catchment and collected at a low spot where a drainage channel and gully sit (noted to be approximately 300mm lower than the kerb). This led to localized flooding of the pavement and carriageway immediately in front of terraced properties; older properties at this location have low doorstep thresholds that make them susceptible to water ingress, whereas newer properties have higher thresholds. (p6) [IMG, PAG]

### Franklin Road, Jacksdale
* The mapped affected area is described as near the intersection with Franklin Road; this suggests Franklin Road vicinity may have been impacted by the same surface water flooding, but the text does not explicitly confirm internal flooding on Franklin Road properties. (p3) [IMG]

### Albert Avenue, Jacksdale
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: During the rainfall event surface water assets were overwhelmed, causing water to collect at a low spot in the catchment and flood properties and businesses on Albert Avenue. The affected properties are low relative to surrounding ground levels and sit within shaded/predicted surface-water flood risk shown on the Environment Agency map. Unconsented modifications to the upstream watercourse may have contributed. (p4) [IMG]

### Dixie Street, Jacksdale
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: During the rainfall event surface water assets were overwhelmed, causing water to collect at a low spot in the catchment and flood properties and businesses on Dixie Street. The properties are identified as low-lying compared to surrounding area and lie within the shaded predicted surface-water risk on the Environment Agency map that aligns with the November flooding. (p4) [IMG]

### Main Road, Jacksdale
* Surface-water flowpaths from the north and east funnel into the area around the intersection of Main Road and Selston Road, causing surface-water flooding at that location as shown on the map. (p5) [IMG]

### The Co-Operative Food
* Surface water flooding from overwhelmed highway drainage and culverts collected at a low spot at the junction adjacent to the store, affecting the public highway and potentially restricting access to The Co‑operative Food - Jacksdale; internal flooding occurred in the nearby area (properties and gardens). (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 7 reported incidents of internal property flooding in parts of Jacksdale (area including this location) (p3) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Acting as Lead Local Flood Authority, the council has been engaged following the surface-water flooding event: it is proactively looking at ways of managing the watercourse adjacent to Selston Road with responsible landowners, pursuing remediation of unconsented upstream modifications, and may require legal support to resolve responsibilities and carry out works. (p4)
* Co‑ordinated emergency planning and response after the flood event: delivered sandbags, investigated and carried out maintenance on a culvert near flooded properties, checked highway gully connectivity, liaised with private landowners over unconsented watercourse modifications, administered the Flooding Hardship Fund, and initiated and led the Section 19 Flood Investigation. (p7)
* As Lead Local Flood Authority, directly involved in the flood event response and recovery: administered a Flooding Hardship Fund; initiated and led the Section 19 Flood Investigation; liaised with landowners regarding ordinary watercourses on a decommissioned colliery site that contribute to catchment flows; administers/supports a Flood Warden scheme, provision of local sandbag stores and a Community Flood Signage Scheme to assist affected communities. (p8)
* Quant: 100 sandbags (p7)
