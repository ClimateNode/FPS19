

# Canon Pyon flooding October 2019 and Canon Pyon flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Canon Pyon<br>055\_Herefordshire County/11\_Canon\_Pyon.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Wellington Brook
* {16 Feb 2020}: Wellington Brook (and its tributary) is identified as an ordinary watercourse under Herefordshire Council and is associated with the flood event affecting Canon Pyon on 16 Feb 2020; it is therefore a watercourse relevant to the flooding shown on the map. (p1)
* {October 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: The tributary feeding Wellington Brook is identified as the fluvial source for the flooding affecting nearby properties; photographic evidence from October 2019 supports a fluvial mechanism linked to this watercourse. (p2) [MUL]
* {2019}: A tributary to the Wellington Brook was identified as the primary source of flooding in 2019. The brook's flood zone is referenced in relation to 2020 flooding, where houses outside the brook's flood zone were flooded by overland flows (the 2020 flooding is attributed to overland flows rather than fluvial flooding of the brook). (p3)
* {October 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 6 properties internally flooded (related to the tributary to Wellington Brook); 3 properties also flooded in October 2019. (p2) [MUL]

### Canon Pyon
* {16 Feb 2020}: Flooding concentrated along the main river channel in and immediately adjacent to Canon Pyon during the key event; area mapped as Flood Zone 3 (highest risk) with some areas shown as benefiting from flood defences. (p1) [IMG]
* {October 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Properties in and adjacent to Flood Zone 3, and others in Flood Zone 1, experienced internal flooding attributed to fluvial inundation from a tributary and to surface water runoff/blocked highway drainage. Photographic evidence documents October 2019 flooding. (p2) [MUL]
* {general}: Identified as having existing fluvial (river) flood risk; a feasibility study is proposed and DEFRA grant funding is being sought to address the fluvial flood risk in the village. (p3)
* {October 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 6 properties internally flooded; 3 of these also flooded in October 2019; 3 listed properties located within the Flood Zone. (p2) [MUL]
