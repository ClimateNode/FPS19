### Egmanton

### Nottinghamshire
* Experienced extensive flooding across the county during and following the extreme rainfall event; a major incident was declared in the county in response to the flooding. (p1) [PAG]
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Extensive flooding across the county following a very wet autumn and intense rainfall; a major incident was declared by the Nottinghamshire Local Resilience Forum. The Environment Agency monitored water levels and issued multiple warnings and alerts across the county. (p2) [IMG]

### Newark and Sherwood
* The district experienced flooding which required emergency response support; Newark and Sherwood District Council provided emergency response support in management of the flooding and actively engaged in the Section 19 Flood Investigation. (p8)

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* County experienced extensive flooding; the council, via its contractor, coordinated and enabled response measures including road closures, flood signage and sandbag distribution. (p1)
* Responded to the flood event affecting the area: delivered sandbags to affected properties, administered a Flooding Hardship Grant, and initiated and led the Section 19 flood investigation. (p7)
* As the Lead Local Flood Authority the Council responded to the flooding by delivering sandbags to affected properties, administering a Flooding Hardship Grant, initiating and leading the Section 19 Flood Investigation, and providing ongoing liaison, support and advice to the community. (p8) [PAG]
