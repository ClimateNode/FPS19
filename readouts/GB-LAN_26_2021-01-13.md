### Pilling
* Local surface water drainage networks were overwhelmed by heavy rainfall, and a residential property was flooded. (p4)
* Quant: 1 home (p4)

### Smallwood Hey Road, Pilling
* {01 Jan 2018 - 30 Nov 2023, 13 Jan 2021}: The public highway experienced blocked gullies and/or flooding; local surface water drainage was overwhelmed leading to flooding affecting nearby property. The highway had multiple recorded incidents of flooding-related problems. (p4) [PAG, MUL]
* {01 Jan 2018 - 30 Nov 2023}: Reports of blocked gullies and/or flooding in or from the public highway; highway drainage and gullies were overwhelmed by surface water runoff during heavy/localised downpours. (p5)
* {01 Jan 2018 - 30 Nov 2023, 13 Jan 2021}: Quant: 15 recorded incidents; 1 home (p4) [PAG, MUL]

### West View
* {01 Jan 2021 - 31 Jan 2021}: A resident's home flooded due to a nearby field dyke being obstructed; household ran a pump to manage conditions; required replacement of flood-damaged carpets on two previous occasions. * {01 Jan 2021 - 31 Jan 2021, 01 Apr 2021 - 30 Apr 2021}: Residents experienced flooding when local drainage systems were temporarily unable to disperse high volumes of rainfall; at least one home was flooded. (p5) [MUL]

* {01 Jan 2021 - 31 Jan 2021, 01 Apr 2021 - 30 Apr 2021}: Quant: 1 home; two flooding occasions (January 2021 and April 2021) (p5) [MUL]
