

# Ovingham flooding 12 Aug 2019

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Ovingham<br>091\_Northumberland/Ovingham-FIR\_Aug19.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Ovingham
* {12 Aug 2019 - 13 Aug 2019}: Persistent heavy rainfall over the weekend of 9-12th August 2019 caused flooding in three areas of the village (playing field, Horsley Road, Whittle Burn), resulting in highway and driveway inundation and internal flooding to properties. (p1) [IMG]
* {12 Aug 2019 - 13 Aug 2019}: Ovingham was affected by flooding driven by sudden rises in the Whittle Burn — caused when the Whittle Dene Reservoir overtopped during storm events — with high river levels and debris accumulating against a road bridge (documented on 13 August 2019). (p2) [TAB]
* {12 Aug 2019 - 13 Aug 2019}: Quant: 3 areas; 2 properties; 2 roads (p1) [IMG]

### Horsley Road, Ovingham
* {12 Aug 2019 - 13 Aug 2019}: High water levels in the burn caused the watercourse to overtop its banks; water flowed between two chalets towards the footpath alongside Horsley Road. Residents cleared debris from a culvert inlet to divert flows; no internal property flooding reported. (p1)
* {12 Aug 2019 - 13 Aug 2019}: Horsley Road experienced flooding-related issues that are to be addressed by stage 2 of the Ovingham surface water flood alleviation scheme. (p2)

### Windsor Crescent, Ovingham
* {12 Aug 2019 - 13 Aug 2019}: Water assumed to be from a spring behind the pavilion on the playing field flowed east along the footway into Windsor Crescent and drained into the gullies; no reports of internal property flooding. (p1)

### West Road, Ovingham
* {12 Aug 2019 - 13 Aug 2019}: Breach of the Whittle Burn led to flooding of the highway on West Road and affected driveways adjacent to the road; associated with the internal flooding of nearby properties. (p1)

### Dene Garth, Ovingham
* {12 Aug 2019 - 13 Aug 2019}: Dene Garth experienced flooding-related issues that are to be addressed by stage 2 of the Ovingham surface water flood alleviation scheme. (p2)

### Whittle Burn
* {12 Aug 2019 - 13 Aug 2019}: High flows and debris caused the burn to back up and breach its banks, flooding the highway and driveways and causing internal flooding of properties; two properties suffered internal flooding (one had property level protection which was overtopped; another had PLP deployed and experienced minor internal seepage). (p1)
* {12 Aug 2019 - 13 Aug 2019}: High river levels with muddy, fast-flowing water and accumulation of large amounts of natural debris against a road bridge, partially blocking flow and causing turbulence; these high levels and debris are associated with flooding in the area. (p2) [IMG, TAB]
* {12 Aug 2019 - 13 Aug 2019}: Quant: 2 properties (p1)


## Topics without coordinates

### Whittle Burn Bridge
* {12 Aug 2019 - 13 Aug 2019}: Debris caught on the bridge caused the burn to back up and breach its banks, contributing to flooding of the highway, driveways and nearby properties. (p1)
* {12 Aug 2019 - 13 Aug 2019}: Quant: 2 properties (p1)

### Recreation Ground
* {12 Aug 2019 - 13 Aug 2019}: Water from a spring behind the pavilion flowed across the playing field east along the footway into Windsor Crescent and into gullies. No properties reported internal flooding at this location. (p1) [IMG]

### Whittle Dene Reservoir
* {12 Aug 2019 - 13 Aug 2019}: The reservoir can overtop during storm events and start to contribute additional flow to the Whittle Burn, explaining sudden rises in river levels that increase downstream flood risk. (p2) [TAB]

### Bridge
* {12 Aug 2019 - 13 Aug 2019}: Debris accumulated on the upstream side of the road bridge during high flows, partially blocking the channel and increasing turbulence and flood risk at the structure. (p2) [IMG]
