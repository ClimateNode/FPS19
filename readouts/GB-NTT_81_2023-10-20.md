## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Major Incident with significant impacts across the county: internal flooding of residences and businesses verified; road network disruption including closure of roads; sandbags delivered to affected communities; verified flood impact data collated and published to support response and recovery; Community Flood Recovery Grant initiated to support those with internal flooding. (p5)
* As Lead Local Flood Authority, NCC coordinated response actions during the flooding: closed roads across Nottinghamshire to promote road safety and avoid additional flood damage, delivered sandbags to affected areas, utilised communications to update the public and will consider property flood resilience measures and strengthen riparian enforcement. (p6)
