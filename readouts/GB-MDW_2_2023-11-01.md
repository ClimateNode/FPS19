

# Medway flooding 01 Nov 2023 - 02 Nov 2023

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Flood Investigation Report – Storm Ciaran<br>078\_Medway/Section\_19\_Flood\_Investigation\_November\_2023.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Medway
* Heavy rainfall on 1–2 November 2023 caused rapid overland and surface water flow across parts of Medway, producing several reported incidents of internal and external property flooding and highway flooding. (p4)
* Widespread surface water (urban) flooding across the Medway administrative area in connection with Storm Ciaran, with recorded incidents reported to the council and observed during site inspections. Some locations with prior flooding history did not flood during this event. (p9) [IMG]
* The storm produced the heavy rainfall that triggered excessive overland flow and sewer capacity exceedance across Medway, causing surface water and sewer-related flooding. (p11)

### Chatham
* Surface water / urban flooding affected built-up areas in and around Chatham during the event on 1–2 November 2023; the report and figure indicate flood‑risk overlays and reported surface water flooding in urban centres including Chatham. (p9) [IMG]
* Flooding in Chatham was observed as rapid overland flow originating from the High Street; floodwaters receded quickly and no sewer blockages were suspected for these incidents. (p11)

### Rochester
* The report's flood-risk map highlights Rochester as a town with surface-water flood risk and the document states areas across Medway experienced widespread surface water flooding on 1–2 Nov 2023. However, the text does not give site-specific confirmed incidents for Rochester, so actual impacts there are inferred from the map and general statement and are therefore uncertain. (p9) [IMG]

### Gillingham
* Surface water / urban flooding affected built-up areas in and around Gillingham during the event on 1–2 November 2023; the ROFSW figure and report identify Gillingham as within overlapping surface water flood risk zones and part of the areas where flooding was reported. (p9) [IMG]

### High Street, Chatham
* Overland flow from High Street produced rapid surface water flooding of the urban roadway and contributed to flooding in Chatham; floodwaters receded quickly and sewer blockages were not suspected except where high volumes prevented discharge. (p11)

### Grasmere Grove, Wainscott
* Flooding from 01–02 Nov 2023 prompted proposal of a new highways drainage system for this street. (p15) [TAB]

### Capstone Road, Luton
* Area experienced flooding during the event; Southern Water carried out inspections of relevant pipework and surveys due to suspected structural deficiencies. (p15) [TAB]

### Hopewell Drive, Chatham
* Area experienced flooding during the event; Southern Water carried out inspections of relevant pipework and surveys due to suspected structural deficiencies. (p15) [TAB]


## Topics without coordinates

### Medway Council
* Highway surface water drainage and council-owned highway soakaways were unable to cope with the rainfall, leading to excessive overland flow on roads and contributing to urban surface water flooding in the area. (p11)
* Medway Council (LLFA and Highways) led a Section 19 flood investigation into the flooding of 01–02 Nov 2023; carried out additional gully inspections and carriageway repairs following the event; is investigating further works at several locations reported by the public. (p15) [TAB, PAG]
* As Lead Local Flood Authority, carried out a Section 19 flood investigation into the flooding of 1–2 Nov 2023; Highways carried out additional gully inspections and carriageway repairs following the event; investigating and planning remedial works at several locations reported by the public; provided information to affected homeowners and businesses on property‑level protection and guidance for reporting blocked gullies. (p16)

### Southern Water
* Public surface water and combined sewers maintained by Southern Water were exceeded during the event, contributing to surface water flooding where sewer capacity was overwhelmed and preventing discharge in locations with the highest rainfall. (p11)
