

# Chathill flooding 28 Jun 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Chathill<br>091\_Northumberland/Chathill-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Chathill
* Surface water flooded the highway and residential properties west of the railway line. Four residential properties experienced internal flooding; access problems and 'near misses' were reported to at least two further properties. The local drainage system was overwhelmed and one road was affected. (p1) [IMG]
* Surface water flooded a roadway and a parking/yard area, inundating areas adjacent to buildings and affecting transport access (including water over a road near a railway crossing). Vehicles were partially submerged and normal use of the road and parking area was impeded. (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 4 residential properties; 1 road; at least 2 further properties (access problems/'near misses') (p1) [IMG]

### Bamburgh Court, Chathill
* Likely one of the residential dwellings in the central affected area that experienced surface-water inundation during the 28/06/2012 event; part of the residential internal flooding and access issues reported for the village. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 4 residential properties (total reported in Chathill); 1 road affected (p1) [IMG]
