### East Suffolk Council
* {general}: East Suffolk Council, as local planning authority and asset owner of the open drainage channel (Walton Creek) within Langer Park, prepared a maintenance plan to address vegetation and blockage issues in the channel. ESC planned major channel clearance (costing undertaken for works in late summer 2022) and a programme of ongoing clearance to reduce future surface-water flooding risk to the park and adjacent areas. (p6) [TAB, PAG]
* {general}: Named as a risk management authority involved in reviewing drainage capacity for the Langer Rd area and supporting community engagement (residents/Town Council) around flood resilience measures. (p7)

### Walton Creek
* {general}: Walton Creek's grates and open drainage channel within Langer Park were implicated in local surface water flooding; blockage/vegetation in the channel and grates contributed to water overtopping into adjacent residential areas and a maintenance/clearance programme was recommended. (p6) [TAB]
* {general}: Open drainage channel (Walton Creek) within Langer Park identified for maintenance and major vegetation/channel clearance to reduce flood risk; a maintenance plan has been written and a major channel clearance was being costed/scheduled (late summer 2022) followed by a 3‑year cycle of future clearance. (p8)

### Suffolk County Council
* {general}: Surface water (and groundwater-linked) flooding affected the Langer Road area, prompting LLFA and Highways actions. SCC led reviews of the hydraulic capacity of the surface water drainage system serving Langer Road, considered traffic-control measures for future storms (closure not possible; proposal for permanent flip-down warning signs), and established a highway drainage maintenance plan including a 12-month gulley cleaning schedule to reduce future blockages. SCC also planned to collect contact details of at-risk residents for potential property-level mitigation. (p6) [TAB]

### Anglian Water
* {general}: Anglian Water, as an asset owner, was involved in addressing surface-water-related blockages (grates/sewer assets) associated with Walton Creek within Langer Park. AW implemented a maintenance plan and committed to regular check-and-clear visits to reduce the risk of asset-related surface water flooding. (p6) [TAB, PAG]

### SCC Highway
* {general}: SCC Highways is responsible for highway drainage maintenance on Langer Road and adjoining streets; agreed in principle to permanent 'flip-down' warning signs and is implementing a 12-month gulley cleaning schedule to reduce surface water flood risk. (p7)
