

# Wigan Metropolitan District flooding 22 Jun 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Beresford Street/Gorman Street, Wigan<br>144\_Wigan/144\_2016\_Wigan/Beresford-Street-Flood-Investigation-Report.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Barley Brook
* Identified as the primary source of flood risk for the event; a blockage/obstruction in the culverted section (under the Leeds–Liverpool Canal) caused surcharging of the culvert and the highway drainage it serves, contributing to internal flooding of nearby properties. (p6)
* {2008, 01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: Barley Brook (culverted in this location) is implicated in local flooding: highway drainage connects into the culverted Barley Brook and, at times of hydraulic incapacity, the culvert cannot accept discharge and water/surcharging occurs (manholes surcharge), contributing to flooding of the adjacent streets and properties. The culverted watercourse and its capacity limitations are shown on the mapped flood extent for the investigated area. (p7) [MUL]
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: A culvert on the main river (Barley Brook) within Pagefield Industrial Estate was identified as either partially collapsed or blocked; this impeded flow and was implicated in the flood event investigated for September 2011 (survey evidence recorded April 2012). (p10)
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: Barley Brook (classified as a Main River) experienced conveyance blockage in its culvert from silt and debris which was identified as causing the flooding event. (p12)
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: Barley Brook (the watercourse) was identified in the report as the watercourse whose blockage and culvert/siphon condition influence flooding; modelling and mapping by the Environment Agency show the brook can cause flooding under blocked or larger event conditions. (p14)
* Quant: 14 residential properties (p6)

### Gorman Street, Wigan Metropolitan District
* Floodwater covered the road and reached up to the base of garden/boundary walls of houses, impeding access to homes and risking property damage; front gardens and vegetation were partially submerged. (p1)
* {26 Apr 2012 - 29 Apr 2012, 22 Jun 2012, 01 Jan 2012 - 31 Jul 2012, early 2012 to mid July 2012}: Flooding (backing up from a blocked culvert) required the council to deploy resources to protect residents; on 26-29 April 2012 and 22 June 2012 flooding entered properties, necessitating evacuations and causing damage to household furniture and fittings. (p4) [MUL]
* {2008, 01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: Included within the mapped flood extent at the Beresford Street / Gorman Street intersection for the investigated event. Historically properties on Gorman Street suffered internal flooding in 2008 when the combined sewer surcharged; the 2008 surcharge resulted in raw sewage mixing with floodwater and affected properties on Gorman Street. A manhole on the culverted section of Barley Brook is located adjacent to the rear garden of number 14; neither the Council nor the householder recorded surcharging from that manhole. (p7) [IMG, MUL]
* {general}: Properties on Gorman Street would flood in a 5PCSYM (1 in 20 year) return period flood event even if the Barley Brook culvert and canal siphon remained clear. Property-level flood protection measures were suggested for flooded properties not currently in receipt of protection. (p14)
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: Floodwater covered the road surface in the residential street, partially submerging a parked car and reaching the doorsteps of terraced houses, causing access disruption and likely ground-floor water ingress. (p17) [IMG]
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: Quant: 1 parked car (p17) [IMG]

### Beresford Street, Wigan Metropolitan District
* Floodwater covered the road and reached up to the base of garden/boundary walls of houses, impeding access to homes and risking property damage; vegetation and front gardens were partially submerged. (p1)
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011, 01 Jan 2012 - 31 Jul 2012, 26 Apr 2012 - 29 Apr 2012, 22 Jun 2012}: Surface water surged from the highway drain; residents reported repeated surface water flooding from September 2011 and escalating to internal flooding in early 2012. Council staff regularly attended to deploy sandbags and remove water; on 26-29 April 2012 and 22 June 2012 flooding entered properties leading to evacuations and damage to furniture and fittings. (p4) [MUL]
* {2008, 01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: The lower end of Beresford Street has a history of surface water flooding with water accumulating under the railway bridge and backing up towards Beresford Street and subsequently Gorman Street. The highway drain on Beresford Street connects directly to the culverted Barley Brook and, when the culvert is hydraulically incapable, discharge is restricted and the manhole opposite number 11 Beresford Street can surcharge. (p7) [IMG, MUL]
* {general}: The report's analysis indicates properties on Beresford Street would flood in a 5PCSYM (1 in 20 year) flood event even if the culvert and siphon remained clear. (p14)
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: Floodwater covered the entire road surface and reached the front gardens and building entrances of terraced houses; water levels reached the base of doors and partially covered pavements, threatening entry to homes and affecting the street infrastructure. (p16) [IMG]

### Wigan Metropolitan District
* {September 2011}: Residential streets in the Beresford Street/Gorman Street area experienced surface water flooding with roads covered by water up to house boundary walls, impeding access and posing risk of property damage. (p1) [PAG]
* {September 2011, 01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011, 01 Jan 2012 - 31 Jul 2012, 26 Apr 2012 - 29 Apr 2012, 22 Jun 2012}: Areas within Wigan (notably Beresford Street and Gorman Street) experienced surface water flooding caused by a blocked culvert; flooding entered properties, caused evacuations and damaged furniture and fittings. (p4) [MUL]
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: Internal flooding of residential properties resulting in damage to furniture and fittings within the homes. (p5)
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: Quant: five properties (p5)

### Miry Lane, Wigan Metropolitan District
* Street-level flooding covered the turning circle and adjacent areas, with water several inches deep, submerging fences/gates and affecting access; emergency measures (sandbags, pumps) were used. (p6) [IMG]
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: The culvert section between Miry Lane and the canal siphon was identified as a critical section where silt and debris build-up contributed to the flooding; this section was prescribed 3-monthly inspections for two years. (p12)
* Quant: 300 mm deep (p6) [IMG]

### Spencer Road, Wigan Metropolitan District
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: Upstream open sections near Spencer Road West were identified as being affected by the culvert blockage; residents in this upstream area were to be informed of the problems and responsibilities (evidence of local flood impact/risk to properties near Spencer Road West). (p12)

### Leeds and Liverpool Canal
* {2008, 22 Jun 2012}: A timber siphon under the Leeds and Liverpool Canal became blocked by a large tree trunk in 2008, causing internal flooding of properties. Further significant blockages in the culvert/upstream contributed to surcharging of the culvert and highway drainage and a major flood incident on 22 June 2012. (p8) [MUL]


## Topics without coordinates

### Canal and River Trust
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011, 26 Apr 2012 - 29 Apr 2012, 22 Jun 2012, mid July 2012}: The Canal and River Trust owned the Pagefield Industrial estate under which the Barley Brook culvert was blocked. The culvert blockage on land they own led to backed-up water that entered properties on Beresford Street and Gorman Street, causing evacuations and internal property damage; the Council engaged with landowners (including the Trust) as part of the response and investigation. (p4) [MUL]
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: A culvert on land owned by the Canal and River Trust was heavily blocked upstream of a siphon by mixed debris (silt, litter, timber, brickwork, clothing). The Trust commissioned Aquajet (GB) Ltd to investigate and remove the blockage; removal works recovered in excess of 32 tonnes of silt and four skips of debris. (p9)
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: The Trust is the landowner of the culvert (including the siphon) where an obstruction of debris and silt was identified as the main cause of the flood; they were required to remove the obstruction and increase inspection/maintenance to reduce recurrence. (p12)
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: Quant: in excess of 32 tonnes of silt; 4 skips of debris (p9)

### Culvert
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: A significant blockage upstream of a siphon was identified in the culvert. The obstruction comprised silt, litter, timber, brickwork and clothing and required contractor clearance. Removal recovered in excess of 32 tonnes of silt and four skips of debris. (p9)
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: An obstruction within the culvert (including a canal siphon section owned by the Canal and River Trust) caused conveyance failure; build-up of silt and debris was identified as the main cause and led to flooding. Recommendations include removal of the obstruction, increased inspection frequency and installation of debris catchers/cleaning points to reduce recurrence. (p12)
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: Quant: in excess of 32 tonnes of silt; 4 skips of debris (p9)

### Barley Brook Culvert
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011, 26 Apr 2012 - 29 Apr 2012, 22 Jun 2012, mid July 2012}: A blockage in the culvert running under the Pagefield Industrial estate caused water to back up into Beresford Street and Gorman Street, leading to surface water/ internal flooding of properties, evacuations and damage to furniture and fittings until the blockage was removed in mid July 2012. (p4) [MUL]
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011}: The culvert had been blocked and this blockage contributed to flooding; following removal of the blockage the culvert and siphon were reported to be functioning efficiently with no further flooding incidents reported to the Council. (p14)

### Pagefield Industrial Estate
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011, 26 Apr 2012 - 29 Apr 2012, 22 Jun 2012, mid July 2012}: A blockage in the Barley Brook culvert running under the Pagefield Industrial estate caused water to back up into Beresford Street and Gorman Street. Floodwaters entered properties on those streets, requiring evacuations and causing damage to furniture and fittings; council staff regularly attended to deploy sandbags and remove surcharging surface water until the culvert was cleared. (p4) [MUL]
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011, 01 Apr 2012 - 30 Apr 2012}: A culvert located within the estate was found to have either a partial collapse or a blockage that impeded flow and was identified as a cause of the September 2011 flood; the culvert is owned by the Canal and River Trust who are riparian owners responsible for maintenance (issue recorded in an April 2012 survey). (p10) [MUL]

### Miry Lane Industrial Estate
* Floodwaters reached the Miry Lane turning circle (industrial estate), inundating the estate entrance/turning area, impeding access and prompting deployment of sandbags and pumps to limit property damage. (p6)
* Quant: 300 mm deep; 14 residential properties (p6)

### British Waterways
* {2008}: British Waterways contractors removed a large tree trunk that had been blocking the timber siphon under the canal in 2008; the obstruction had caused internal flooding of properties. (p8)
* {2008}: Quant: one tree trunk (1700mm long and 650mm wide) (p8)

### Wigan Council
* {01 Sep 2011 - 30 Sep 2011, 26 Apr 2012 - 29 Apr 2012, 22 Jun 2012, mid July 2012}: As Lead Local Flood Authority, the council regularly attended the incident, assisted in deployment of sandbags, removal of surcharging surface water from the highway drain on Beresford Street and deployed resources to protect residents and properties (including during the events of 26-29 April 2012 and 22 June 2012). (p4) [MUL]

### United Utilities
* {2008}: The combined sewer for which United Utilities is responsible surcharged during a flood event in 2008; that surcharge caused raw sewage to mix with floodwater and affected properties on Gorman Street. (p7)

### Environment Agency
* A CCTV survey carried out on behalf of the Environment Agency identified a significant blockage upstream of the canal siphon; that blockage obstructed the culvert, causing surcharging of the culvert and highway drainage and contributing to the flood incident on 22 June 2012. (p8)
