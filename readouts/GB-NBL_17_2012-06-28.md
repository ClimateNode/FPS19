

# Felton flooding 28 Jun 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Felton<br>091\_Northumberland/Felton-FIR-v3.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Felton
* Flooding on 28/06/2012 affected properties and roads. Surface water from the highway (including B3645 and an unnamed road adjacent to the Riverside) overwhelmed drainage (a highway drain adjacent to the B3645 appeared broken) causing water to enter dwellings and properties. The Back Burn (an ordinary watercourse) rose rapidly and overtopped its banks, flooding properties and gardens on the right-hand bank. Some properties' flood defences were effective. Businesses off Main Street were also affected as surface water flowed down the steep Main Street towards the river Coquet. (p1) [IMG]
* Surface water collected in Main Street and flowed southerly toward the River Coquet; steep slope increased flood velocities. The majority of this water flowed into the river, but some businesses off Main Street were affected. The war memorial at the junction of the B6345 and Riverside was inundated when the two gullies could not accept the flow. (p2) [IMG]
* Upstream runoff from the A1, land drainage works carried out by a landowner, and reported felling of trees were identified on the map and in the report as possible contributors that increase flood risk in Felton, including increased flow into the Bach Burn (Back Burn) and potential flooding within the village. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 2 residential properties; 1 business; 4 roads (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: water depth reached 2 ft (p2) [IMG]

### West Thirston
* Map and report indicate the central Felton–West Thirston area was affected by flooding on 28/06/2012. Flooding was associated with surface water flowing along roads and with an ordinary watercourse (Back Burn) rising and overtopping where watercourses converge; the map shows both dwelling and business areas within the affected zone. Specific properties/asset-level impacts in West Thirston are not detailed in the text. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 2 residential properties; 1 business; 4 roads (p1)

### Riverside, Felton
* The war memorial located off the junction of the B6345 and Riverside was affected because only two gullies were present at the corner and the volume of water exceeded the drainage capacity; local reports state flood depth here reached 2 ft. (p2)
* Quant: 2 ft depth (p2)

### Main Street, Felton
* Surface water collected on Main Street and flowed southerly toward the River Coquet; the steep slope increased flood velocities. The majority of this water flowed into the river, but some businesses off Main Street were affected by floodwater. (p2) [IMG]

### River Coquet
* Surface water from Main Street and surrounding higher ground flowed toward and into the River Coquet; the river was the primary receptor for the majority of the runoff. (p2)

### Cowslip Hill
* Reported felling of trees near Cowslip Hill is identified on the map as a possible contributor to increased runoff, which may have reduced interception and increased downstream flood risk in Felton and towards the Bach Burn. (p3) [IMG]

### Highways Agency
* Runoff from the A1 was identified as contributing to surface water discharge in the area; the Highways Agency was contacted regarding this runoff and surface water discharge from the A1. (p2) [TAB]
* {general}: The Highways Agency were contacted to obtain drainage plans for the A1 and asked to check/clear any surface water drainage features that could be increasing runoff to the Back Burn, implying A1 drainage may have contributed to surface-water runoff linked to flooding in Felton. (p3)
