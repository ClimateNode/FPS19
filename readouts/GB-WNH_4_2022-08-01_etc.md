### Benefield Road, Oundle
* {01 Jun 2023 - 30 Jun 2023, 18 Jun 2023}: Internal flooding of a property on Benefield Road occurred, prompting a Section 19 flood investigation. (p22) [PAG, MUL]
* {01 Jul 2020 - 31 Jul 2020, 18 Jun 2023}: During significant rainfall, rainwater ponded on Benefield Road in front of dwellings. Ponded water overtopped the retaining wall upstand (either due to depth or cars splashing water) and then ponded on the lower footway, entering the affected property through the door and wall, causing internal flooding. (p23) [MUL]
* {2023}: Rainwater ponded on the carriageway in front of dwellings, overtopped the retaining wall upstand (due to depth or cars splashing water) and then ponded on the lower footway. Ponded water entered affected properties through doors and walls. Highway gullies and the linear drainage channel on the lower footway were unable to cope with the volume of water. Rainwater pipes from the terraced houses discharged onto the lowered footway, increasing flow onto the drainage and footway. Residents have since installed flood barriers on external doors and used sandbags to prevent water entering properties. (p24) [PAG]
* {01 Jun 2023 - 30 Jun 2023, 18 Jun 2023}: Quant: 1 property flooded twice (p22) [PAG, MUL]
* {01 Jul 2020 - 31 Jul 2020, 18 Jun 2023}: Quant: 2 flood events affecting the property (July 2020; 18 June 2023) (p23) [MUL]

### Drill Hall Car Park
* {01 Jul 2020 - 31 Jul 2020, 18 Jun 2023}: The car park slopes southwards toward Benefield Road; during heavy rainfall water flowed down this fall and towards the footway and dwelling, contributing to ponding on Benefield Road and the subsequent overtopping of the wall that led to property flooding. (p23) [MUL]
* {2023}: Surface water flowed off the Drill Hall car park onto the highway during the extreme rainfall event, increasing the volume of water on the highway and contributing to the surface water flooding observed on Benefield Road. The car park is served by isolated gullies rather than a linear drain, so discharge from the car park onto the highway occurred during the event. (p24)
* {18 Jun 2023}: Drainage arrangement on the car park allows water to flow onto the highway, increasing the volume of water on the highway and exacerbating inundation of highway drainage beyond its designed capacity. A possible blockage of one car park gully was noted; the car park discharge may have contributed to overloading a linear drain. (p25)
* {18 Jun 2023}: Localized flooding of the car park and adjacent carriageway; water flowed across Broadmead Road into the car park following a significant flow route. (p26) [IMG]
* {18 Jun 2023}: Quant: one gully (p25)
