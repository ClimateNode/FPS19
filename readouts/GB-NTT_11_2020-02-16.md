

# Bingham flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Bingham – February 2020<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/binghamsection19reportfebruary2020.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Nottinghamshire
* {15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020}: Flooding across the county led to numerous flood warnings and alerts and prompted local response actions by the highway contractor on behalf of the county council (road closures, signage and sandbag distribution). (p1) [PAG]
* Extensive flooding across the Nottinghamshire area during the heavy rain associated with Storm Dennis. Local authority response (via contractor) facilitated road closures, placed flood signage and distributed sandbags across the network. (p2)
* {15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 38 flood warnings; 16 flood alerts; 51 road closures; over 70 flood signs; over 4000 sandbags (p1) [PAG]
* Quant: 51 road closures; over 70 flood signs; over 4000 sandbags delivered (p2)

### Bingham
* Internal flooding of residential properties on Western Avenue following prolonged heavy rainfall associated with Storm Dennis. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Part of the Nottinghamshire area that experienced extensive flooding during the heavy rainfall event; Western Avenue within Bingham suffered internal property flooding linked to an overwhelmed adjacent watercourse and surface water runoff. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 10 properties (p2) [IMG, PAG]

### Western Avenue, Bingham
* Internal flooding of residential properties on Western Avenue caused by an adjacent watercourse being overwhelmed and additional surface water entering from the recreation ground (blocked grill on an unnamed watercourse) that exacerbated flooding at the front of properties. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 10 properties (p3) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* {general}: Experienced widespread flooding in the county and, via its contractor, had 51 road closures facilitated, over 70 flood signs placed on the network and over 4000 sandbags delivered as part of the response to Storm Dennis. (p2)
* {general}: Initiated and coordinated emergency planning and response; administered the Flooding Hardship Fund; initiated and led the Section 19 flood investigation into the flooding. (p4)
* Co‑ordinated emergency planning and led the Section 19 flood investigation; administered the Flooding Hardship Fund and provided ongoing flood risk reduction support (Flood Warden scheme, Community Flood Signage Scheme) to communities affected by the flood event. (p5)
* {general}: Quant: 51 road closures; over 70 flood signs; over 4000 sandbags (p2)

### Via East Midlands
* Facilitated immediate flood-response actions on the county road network during Storm Dennis: arranged 51 road closures, placed over 70 flood warning signs to warn motorists and delivered sandbags to protect infrastructure and properties. (p2)
* {general}: Acting on behalf of the highway authority: maintenance of highways and highway drainage assets and carrying out site-based investigations immediately after the event. (p4)
* Quant: 51 road closures; over 70 flood signs; over 4000 sandbags (p2)

### Network Rail
* A watercourse located within land owned by Network Rail was overwhelmed by prolonged rainfall and resulted in flooding of adjacent properties; Network Rail has identified a need for maintenance (tree/vegetation works and further channel works) to reduce future flood risk. (p3) [IMG]
* Responded to issues arising from the flood event and is investigating maintenance works on a watercourse to reduce the risk of future flooding; Nottinghamshire County Council and Rushcliffe Borough Council are liaising with Network Rail on proposed works. (p5)
* Quant: 10 properties (p3) [IMG]

### Rushcliffe Borough Council
* Provided emergency response support for the flooding event and delivered sandbags to affected residents; managed flood-response activities. (p4)
* Provided emergency response support in management of the flooding event (delivered sandbags, staffed the rest centre at Bingham Leisure Centre), managed recovery in affected Rushcliffe communities, administered flood hardship and community recovery grants, and continues to administer property flood resilience grants and support (including arranging British Red Cross visits). (p5) [PAG]

### Environment Agency
* Recorded and issued a record-breaking number of flood warnings and alerts in England in response to Storm Dennis on 16 February 2020. (p2)
* {general}: Participates as a Risk Management Authority in investigations of the incident and supports community flood resilience (co‑administers Flood Warden scheme and Community Flood Signage Scheme alongside the county council) to reduce future flood impacts. (p5)

### Highway Authority
* {general}: Maintenance of public highways and highway drainage assets; provided site-based presence and investigations immediately following the flood event. (p4)
