

# Woodborough flooding 01 Jan 2024 - 02 Jan 2024

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Woodborough – Storm Henk - January 2024<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/woodborough-s19-storm-henk-jan-2024.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Woodborough
* Flooding on 2 January 2024 caused by sustained rainfall over a saturated catchment. A peak water level of 1.048 m was recorded at Woodborough Brook (16:15). Consequent internal flooding of residential and commercial properties, with additional flooding to gardens, outbuildings and roads. (p2) [PAG]
* Central area of the village experienced surface water flooding; multiple residential streets were affected (Main Street, Smalls Croft, Shelt Hill, Pinfold Close). (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* {01 Oct 2023 - 31 Oct 2023, 01 Jan 2024 - 02 Jan 2024}: Village experienced severe disruption from repeated exceedance of channel capacity leading to flows along Main Street; residents were cut off for many hours and properties were internally flooded during events. (p4) [PAG, MUL]
* Internal flooding affected residents and businesses in the village; officers visited sites to verify scale of internal flooding, offer advice and deliver sandbags. Flooding mechanisms recorded for the village include overtopping/impacts from the Woodborough Brook and sewer-related issues. (p8) [PAG]
* Village experienced flooding: sewer-related issues were reported; the culvert conveying the watercourse through Woodborough has been CCTV inspected and is being reviewed by the Environment Agency; debris screens at Church Yards and at 124 and 128 Main Street (in Woodborough) were identified for improvement; a natural flood management project is being delivered in the upper catchment to provide temporary storage and reduce flood risk. (p9)
* Quant: 24 residential properties; one business; Main Street: 21 properties; Smalls Croft: 1 property; Shelt Hill: 2 properties; Pinfold Close: 1 property (p2) [PAG]
* Quant: 20 residential properties and 1 business on Main Street; 1 property on Smalls Croft; 2 properties on Shelt Hill; 1 property on Pinfold Close (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* {01 Oct 2023 - 31 Oct 2023, 01 Jan 2024 - 02 Jan 2024}: Quant: 20 residential properties; 1 business (p4) [PAG, MUL]
* Quant: 3 screens in Woodborough; 12 screen improvements in East Midlands programme (p9)

### Main Street, Woodborough
* Internal flooding of residential properties on Main Street; properties also experienced associated flooding to gardens and outbuildings as part of the village-wide event. (p2) [PAG]
* Flooding impacted properties along Main Street; the Woodborough Brook flows along the south side of Main Street and is joined there by two tributaries, contributing to flooding at this location. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* {01 Oct 2023 - 31 Oct 2023, 01 Jan 2024 - 02 Jan 2024}: Surface water flowed eastward along Main Street when the local watercourse exceeded channel capacity; water entered several properties (flowing through property boundaries where properties are lower than the road). The flooding cut off many residents who were unable to get into or out of the village for many hours. (p4) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
* Water from Woodborough Brook flowed down Main Street after the brook’s channel capacity was exceeded, carrying flow toward lower-lying residential areas and enabling pooling that affected nearby properties. (p6) [IMG]
* Quant: 21 properties; part of a village total of 24 residential properties and 1 business internally flooded (p2) [PAG]
* Quant: 20 residential properties; 1 business (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* {01 Oct 2023 - 31 Oct 2023, 01 Jan 2024 - 02 Jan 2024}: Quant: 20 residential properties; 1 business (p4) [IMG, PAG, MUL]

### Shelt Hill, Woodborough

* Flooding occurred on Shelt Hill; investigations into connectivity and condition of drainage assets are ongoing and meetings with residents have been held to discuss the flooding experienced. (p9)

Led and initiated the Section 19 Flood Investigation and led procurement/delivery of a property flood resilience repair grant scheme. (p8)

## Topics without coordinates

### Woodborough Brook
* The Brook reached a peak water level of 1.048m (recorded at 16:15 on 2 Jan 2024) and contributed to flooding that caused internal flooding of residential properties and one business, and flooding of gardens and outbuildings in the village. (p1) [PAG]
* Woodborough Brook overtopped its banks (exceeded channel capacity) causing out-of-bank flow that flowed along Main Street and into properties; contributed to internal flooding (including the property on Smalls Croft) and wider surface water flow along the road. (p4)
* The brook exceeded channel capacity and flowed down Main Street, contributing to overland flow/pooling in lower-lying areas (notably Pinfold Close) and causing property flooding downstream. (p6) [IMG]
* The Environment Agency issued a Flood Warning for the Woodborough Brook (19:00 on 1 January 2024); EA operatives carried out patrols and cleared blockages in the area before and after peak flows, indicating the brook was a source/contributor to local flooding. (p8)
* Quant: 24 residential properties; one business (p1) [PAG]
* Quant: 1 residential property (Smalls Croft); contributed to flooding affecting 20 residential properties and 1 business on Main Street (p4)

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Responded to a Major Incident that caused internal flooding of properties across the county. Initiated a Community Flood Recovery Grant to support individuals affected by internal flooding; attended sites after flooding to verify the scale of internal flooding and provide support, guidance and advice; visited flood-affected residents and businesses to establish flood sources. (p7)
