

# Shilbottle flooding 28 Jun 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Shilbottle<br>091\_Northumberland/Shilbottle-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Shilbottle
* Surface water runoff from steep fields south of the village (location of a spring) and additional runoff from fields west of Colliers Close inundated clusters of residential and business properties along the main east–west road through the village, causing disruption to transport and local access. (p1) [IMG, MUL]
* Surface water was observed to flow from higher elevations in the southwest toward lower areas in the northeast across fields and through residential zones of Shilbottle; a spring in the southwest was identified as a source directing runoff toward residential areas (including Colliers Close and Shilbottle Grange), creating surface water flooding risk for the village. (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 15 residential properties; 3 businesses; 6 roads (p1) [IMG, MUL]

### Haven, Shilbottle
* Residential properties in the Haven were flooded by surface water runoff from surrounding fields during the event, forming one of the clusters of affected dwellings along the main route through the village. (p1)
* Quant: 15 residential properties (across Haven, Lee Avenue, Farmers Rise and Colliers Close) (p1)

### Lee Avenue, Shilbottle
* Residential properties on Lee Avenue were affected by surface water flooding driven by runoff from the surrounding fields; Lee Avenue is part of the cluster of impacted dwellings in the village. (p1)
* Quant: 15 residential properties (across Haven, Lee Avenue, Farmers Rise and Colliers Close) (p1)

### Grange Road, Shilbottle
* Businesses on Grange Road were flooded by surface water runoff during the event; Grange Road formed part of the continuous affected route through the village, causing local transport disruption. (p1)
* Quant: 3 businesses; 6 roads affected in total (Grange Road part of main affected route) (p1)

### Colliers Close, Shilbottle
* Properties in Colliers Close were inundated by surface water flooding caused by runoff from fields to the west of the street, contributing to the village impacts. (p1) [MUL]
* Observed surface water runoff from higher ground and a mapped spring flowed toward Colliers Close, directing runoff into the residential area and creating surface-water flooding risk that prompted investigation and proposed mitigation. (p2) [IMG]

### Shilbottle Grange
* Surface water runoff from a spring and overland flow following the terrain gradient was directed toward lower-lying residential development at Shilbottle Grange, contributing to surface water flow through the area and indicating a need for runoff management. (p2) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Tyelaw Burn Catchment
* Intense rainfall produced overland flow from the relatively steep fields (and spring) within the upper Tyelaw Burn catchment, generating runoff that flowed into Shilbottle and contributed to surface water flooding of residential and business areas. (p1)

### Northumberland County Council
* Northumberland County Council investigated surface water runoff affecting Colliers Close and proposed exploring mitigation measures as part of a development (Ref No.:12/02093/FUL) and further work to manage runoff to the west of Colliers Close. (p2)
