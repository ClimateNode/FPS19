

# Lowestoft flooding 06 Oct 2019

## Place names with coordinates

### Aldwyck Way, Lowestoft
* Subject of a Suffolk County Council Section 19 investigation into a flood event (document focuses on Aldwyck Way as one of the affected streets). (p1)
* Highway flooding for approximately 100 m with an approximate maximum depth of 450 mm at the lowest point; internal flooding of domestic properties in the area (part of the total 18 properties). Anglian Water stormwater sewer and highway drainage system both surcharged. (p2) [PAG]
* Kirkley Stream overtopped adjacent to Aldwyck Way and the local Anglian Water stormwater outfall at bed level suffered fluvial locking (reverse flow/surcharging), causing river water to push out of gullies and manholes and preventing positive discharge of surface water runoff. (p4)
* {06 Oct 2019 - 07 Oct 2019}: Properties (Cotman Housing Association) experienced internal flooding despite property-level resilience measures; audible early-warning system on Aldwyck Way is present but there is no evidence it was active during the event. (p5) [PAG]
* The Velda/Aldwyck Way reach is subject to flood risk from both fluvial (Kirkley Stream) and pluvial (surface water) sources during the documented event; interventions were recommended because the reach requires a higher standard of protection and pumps were reviewed to draw down upstream water levels. (p6) [TAB, PAG]
* Quant: Approx. 100 m of highway flooded; max depth approx. 450 mm at lowest point; 18 domestic properties (total across Velda Close and Aldwyck Way); total affected area 0.7Ha (7000m2) (p2) [PAG]
* Quant: 1:100yr (target standard of protection) (p6) [TAB, PAG]

### Kirkley Stream
* {01 Sep 2002 - 30 Sep 2002, 2013, 01 Jul 2015 - 31 Jul 2015, 06 Oct 2019, September/October 2006}: Fluvial capacity of Kirkley Stream was exceeded during the intense storm and contributed to the exceedance of the drainage system, leading to internal flooding of properties and highway inundation. (p3) [MUL]
* Kirkley Stream's capacity was exceeded during the intense storm, causing it to overtop banks adjacent to Velda Close/Aldwyck Way and to flood gardens along Long Road and Birds Lane. The stream caused fluvial locking of the stormwater outfalls (reverse flow into Aldwyck Way) and experienced a partial blockage upstream of Blackheath Crossroad (large fallen branch and washed-in waste). Anglian Water deployed screw pumps and inspected/cleared trash screens as part of the response. (p4) [PAG]
* {06 Oct 2019 - 07 Oct 2019}: Partial blockage observed just upstream of Blackheath Crossroad from a large fallen branch and washed-in waste, contributing to local channel obstruction; stream flooding contributed to properties being flooded in the existing Flood Zone 3/pluvial-prone area. Rack marks indicate water levels either side of the partial blockage were similar so flow was able to pass forward but debris contributed to local hydraulic effects. (p5) [PAG]
* Kirkley Stream carried excess water during the event and is identified as a source of fluvial/pluvial flood risk in the area; the report notes there is no evidence that the flood storage area failed to take excess water from the stream during the event, and recommends continued maintenance and consideration of additional attenuation to reduce future impacts. (p6) [TAB, PAG]
* {01 Sep 2002 - 30 Sep 2002, 2013, 01 Jul 2015 - 31 Jul 2015, 06 Oct 2019, September/October 2006}: Quant: Contributed to flooding reported for 18 domestic properties (see site report). (p3) [MUL]
* Quant: Two screw pumps utilised on 6 Oct for approx. 12 hrs; Anglian Water on site from 9am on 6 Oct and remained for almost 24 hours (p4) [PAG]

### Kirkley
* {01 Sep 2002 - 30 Sep 2002, 2013, 01 Jul 2015 - 31 Jul 2015, 06 Oct 2019, September/October 2006}: Residential area experienced internal flooding of properties (up to 400mm deep) and inundation of outbuildings and gardens. The public highway (Aldwyck Way) flooded for approx. 100m with a maximum depth of about 450mm. Anglian Water stormwater sewer and highway drainage were surcharged, giving a total affected area of about 0.7 ha (7000 m2). (p3) [IMG, MUL]
* Kirkley Stream rose and overtopped its western bank, flooding a wider area including Velda Close, Aldwyck Way and gardens along Long Road and Birds Lane. Fluvial locking/surcharging of the local stormwater/public sewer outfalls caused reverse flow into Aldwyck Way, drowned out gullies and manholes, and prevented positive discharge, exacerbating localised flooding; properties in the area (an existing Flood Zone 3/pluvial risk area) were flooded. (p4) [PAG]
* {06 Oct 2019 - 07 Oct 2019}: Partial blockage observed in Kirkley Stream just upstream of Blackheath Crossroad (large fallen branch and washed-in garden/fly-tipped waste) during the event; water levels either side of the partial blockage indicated flow could pass. Anglian Water inspected and cleared trash screens at Fen Park and operated screw pumps to lift water into Lake Lothing; some properties in the area were flooded despite property-level resilience measures failing to prevent water ingress. (p5) [PAG]
* {01 Sep 2002 - 30 Sep 2002, 2013, 01 Jul 2015 - 31 Jul 2015, 06 Oct 2019, September/October 2006}: Quant: 18 domestic properties; 100m of highway flooded (Aldwyck Way); 450mm maximum highway flood depth; 400mm internal property flood depth; 0.7Ha (7000m2) total affected area (p3) [IMG, MUL]
* {06 Oct 2019 - 07 Oct 2019}: Quant: 2 screw pumps utilised on 6 October (approx. 12 hrs); Anglian Water on site from 09:00 on 6 October for almost 24 hours (p5) [PAG]

### Suffolk

(p3) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
* {01 Sep 2002 - 30 Sep 2002, 2013, 01 Jul 2015 - 31 Jul 2015, 06 Oct 2019, September/October 2006}: Quant: 18 domestic properties; 100m of highway flooded (Aldwyck Way); 450mm maximum highway flood depth; 400mm internal property flood depth; 0.7Ha (7000m2) total affected area (p3) [IMG, PAG, MUL]

### Velda Close, Lowestoft
* Subject of a Suffolk County Council Section 19 investigation into a flood event (document focuses on Velda Close as one of the affected streets). (p1)
* Internal flooding of domestic properties (part of the incident affecting this location); inundation to outbuildings and gardens reported. Anglian Water stormwater sewer and highway drainage surcharged during the event. (p2)
* Kirkley Stream overtopped its western bank adjacent to Velda Close, causing flooding of the area. (p4)
* Quant: 18 domestic properties (total across Velda Close and Aldwyck Way); total affected area 0.7Ha (7000m2) (p2)

### Long Road, Lowestoft
* Gardens along Long Road were overtopped when the Kirkley Stream exceeded its banks, contributing to local pluvial/fluvial flooding of properties/land. (p4)

### Birds Lane, Lowestoft
* Gardens along Birds Lane were flooded when Kirkley Stream overtopped its banks; surface water runoff and reversed flow from the surcharged stormwater system caused water to emerge from gullies and manholes in the area. (p4)

### Fen Park
* Anglian Water staff were on site at Fen Park checking and clearing trash screens and inspecting structures and assets along Kirkley Stream as part of flood response operations. (p4)

### Anglian Water

* {06 Oct 2019, 07 Oct 2019}: The Anglian Water stormwater sewer was exceeded/surcharged (fluvial locking at its outfall into Kirkley Stream), preventing positive discharge and contributing to localised flooding; Anglian Water staff inspected/cleared trash screens and operated screw pumps to lift water into Lake Lothing as part of the response. (p4) [MUL]
* {06 Oct 2019 - 07 Oct 2019}: Operated screw pumps to lift water from the Kirkley Stream into Lake Lothing as part of the event response; staff on site inspected and cleared trash screens at Fen Park and inspected stream structures and assets to manage flows and reduce impacts. (p5) [PAG]
* {06 Oct 2019, 07 Oct 2019}: Quant: Two screw pumps used on 6 October for approx. 12 hrs; Anglian Water on site from 9:00 on 6 October and remained for almost 24 hours (inspecting/clearing). (p4) [MUL]
* {06 Oct 2019 - 07 Oct 2019}: Quant: Two screw pumps utilised on 6 October; pumps operational over 6th and 7th October 2019; personnel on site from 09:00 on 6 October for almost 24 hours; two screw pumps used on the 6th for approx. 12 hrs. (p5) [PAG]

### Cotman Housing Association
* Properties owned by Cotman Housing Association experienced internal flooding when Kirkley Stream overtopped its banks adjacent to Velda Close/Aldwyck Way and when stormwater/highway drainage and the public sewer became surcharged; property-level resilience measures fitted to some Cotman properties did not prevent water entering the properties. An audible early warning system on Aldwyck Way (owned by Cotman Housing Association) is noted but there is no evidence it was active. (p4)
* {06 Oct 2019 - 07 Oct 2019}: Properties owned by Cotman Housing Association were flooded despite having property-level (non-passive) resilience measures fitted; those measures failed to prevent water entering the properties. Cotman also own an audible early warning at Aldwyck Way for which there is no evidence it operated during the event. (p5) [PAG]

### Highways
* The highway surface water drainage system exceeded its design capacity during the storm, contributing to surface water/urban flooding because runoff could not drain away. (p4)

### Blackheath Box Culvert
* Surcharging/afflux was observed at the Blackheath box culvert (rack marks indicate small head loss downstream) during the event, indicating hydraulic stress and contributed to localised flood conditions. (p4)

### Blackheath Crossroad
* A large fallen branch (and potential fly-tipped/garden waste) created a partial blockage in Kirkley Stream just upstream of Blackheath Crossroad; although rack marks indicate levels either side were similar and flow could pass, the blockage contributed to local hydraulic conditions during the event. (p4)
