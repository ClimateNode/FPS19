

# Willersley flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Willersley<br>055\_Herefordshire County/17\_Willersley.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Willersley
* The Environment Agency Flood Map for Planning (Rivers and Sea) shows much of the area around Willersley, particularly to the north and south, is in Flood Zone 3 (high flood risk); some areas are shown as benefiting from flood defences. (p1) [IMG]
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020, 16 Feb 2020}: Five residential properties flooded internally; flood depths recorded internally between 6 inches and 3 ft, and one property experienced 4 ft externally. Respondents described water from a watercourse (River Wye/tributaries) and contributory issues including an overflowing ditch, a blocked gully and a blocked bridge/culvert. A road closure was implemented on the A438 during the flooding. (p2)
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 5 properties; internal depths 6 inches to 3 ft; one external depth 4 ft (p2)


## Topics without coordinates

### River Wye
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020, 16 Feb 2020}: The River Wye (and two unnamed tributaries) is identified as the primary source of fluvial flooding that affected properties in Willersley in February 2020; water from the Wye is mentioned by respondents as part of the flood mechanism. (p2)
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 5 properties (p2)
