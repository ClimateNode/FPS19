### Horninglow Channel
* Marked on the map as an ordinary watercourse (with a culverted section) near the flood location; RoFSW mapping shows elevated surface-water flood zones around the channel. The proximity of the channel to the marked flood location corresponds with reports that multiple properties at Price Court experienced internal flooding and external flooding of gardens, driveways and adjacent sports fields in the early hours of 16th February 2020. (p22) [IMG]
* Capacity of the channel was exceeded and it overflowed; water from the channel flowed across the playing fields toward Price Court and contributed to inundation of the leisure fields and nearby properties. (p23) [PAG]
* Capacity of the Horninglow Channel was exceeded; water flowed across the playing fields toward the properties on Price Court, contributing to flooding of the leisure fields and inundation of nearby residential properties. (p24) [IMG]
* Experienced high flows and elevated water levels during Storm Dennis on 16th February 2020; received discharge from Shobnall Brook and contributed to localised flooding around the south‑eastern corner of Shobnall playing field and the Price Court vicinity. (p27)
* Channel capacity was exceeded during the February 2020 event and flood water flowed westward across Shobnall playing fields towards Price Court, contributing to ponding and extensive flooding of the fields north of Price Court. (p29) [IMG]
* The channel exceeded capacity and backed up, spilling onto the adjacent playing fields; water from the open section of the channel flowed across the playing fields toward Price Court and contributed to the observed flooding of properties and sports fields. (p30)
* {general}: High River Trent levels during the February 2020 event likely prevented the Horninglow Channel from discharging to the river, causing the channel to back up. The channel and the culvert downstream of Price Court were reported in poor condition with significant siltation, meaning connecting surface water sewers/drainage networks would have been unable to discharge, reached capacity and inundated low-lying areas. (p31)
* The Horninglow Channel contributed river flooding during the event; the channel was found to contain significant vegetation and debris which may have obstructed flow and contributed to flooding in nearby properties including Price Court. (p32)
* Horninglow Channel had reduced capacity due to vegetation and debris; if the downstream flap valve closed because of high River Trent levels, the channel would have been unable to discharge, leading to backing-up, capacity exceedance and spilling onto Shobnall Fields and flooding connected drainage (including the private network serving Price Court). (p33)
* Identified by the investigation as a watercourse requiring improvement and reclassification to Main River to ensure future maintenance and to reduce its contribution to the flooding on 16th February 2020; subject of planned works and responsibility transfer discussions between STW and the EA. (p34) [TAB]
* Water levels within Horninglow Channel were elevated following the event (flap valve at its downstream extent closed due to high River Trent levels), causing the drainage network to back up and contributing to flooding of the adjacent highway, gardens, driveways and residential properties at Price Court. The brook lies immediately south of Price Court and is therefore hydrologically relevant to the residential properties impacted during the 16 February 2020 event. (p18) [PAG, IMG]
* {01 Jul 1999 - 31 Jul 1999, 16 Feb 2020}: Shobnall Brook has overtopped and flowed northerly into Price Court, causing the highway at Price Court to be underwater and flooding the adjacent car park at the western side of Price Court when the brook reaches capacity. (p20) [MUL]
* {general}: Described as the watercourse that can overtop and inundate adjacent lower ground; overtopping from Shobnall Brook is identified as a source of flooding that will flow into neighbouring areas. (p21)
* Mapped as an Environment Agency main river adjacent to the marked flood location; RoFSW maps and the map annotation link elevated surface-water flood risk along the watercourse to the flooding reported at Price Court. (p22) [IMG]
