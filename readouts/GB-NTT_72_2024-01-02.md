

# Stapleford flooding 02 Jan 2024

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Stapleford – Storm Henk, January 2024<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/stapleford-s19-storm-henk-jan-2024.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Stapleford
* Stapleford suffered a flood event during Storm Henk caused by sustained rainfall over a saturated catchment. (p1)
* Residential properties in Stapleford were flooded, specifically properties on Bessell/Bessel Lane and Nottingham Road. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Areas across Stapleford experienced flooding caused by overland flows from the River Erewash combined with surface water. Blocked gullies reduced drainage effectiveness and highway flooding occurred, contributing to runoff into properties (notably on Nottingham Road). (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 8 properties on Bessell Lane; 4 properties on Nottingham Road (document lists total 12 properties) (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 4 properties (p3) [IMG, PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* County-wide flooding affected communities: internal flooding of properties prompted a Community Flood Recovery Grant, delivery of sandbags to affected areas/communities, and the closure of roads to avoid additional flood damage. (p5)
* Quant: 69 roads (p5)

### Bessell Lane, Stapleford
* Domestic properties on Bessell Lane were flooded. (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 8 properties (p2) [IMG]

### Nottingham Road, Stapleford
* Domestic properties on Nottingham Road were flooded. (p2) [PAG]
* Flooding on the highway due to limited network capacity caused runoff from the road into adjacent properties; vehicle bow waves were reported to exacerbate flooding impacts on properties. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 4 properties (p2) [PAG]
* Quant: 4 properties (p3) [IMG]

### River Erewash
* High river levels in the River Erewash contributed overland flows that were a main factor in the flooding; elevated river levels also prevented gullies from discharging effectively, worsening local flooding. (p3)


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* A Major Incident with significant impacts occurred across the county. Officers played a key role in the response, initiated a Community Flood Recovery Grant to support individuals who experienced internal flooding, and visited flood-affected residents and businesses to establish sources and data. (p4)

### Severn Trent
* Received reports of flooding from sewers in the area and undertook extensive investigations; followed up directly with some affected residents about sewer-related issues. (p5)

### Broxtowe Borough Council
* Provided emergency response support to manage the flooding event in the area, including provision of some temporary accommodation; actively engaged in the Section 19 flood investigation. (p5)
