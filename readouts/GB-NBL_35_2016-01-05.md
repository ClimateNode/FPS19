

# Ovington flooding 05 Jan 2016 - 06 Jan 2016

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Ovington<br>091\_Northumberland/Ovington\_FIR\_2015.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Ovington
* {04 Dec 2015, 05 Jan 2016 - 06 Jan 2016}: Flooding from the River Tyne (overtopping during Storm Desmond) and subsequent surface water/overland flow (Storm Frank) affected the village: roads and residents' gardens were flooded. (p1) [IMG, MUL]
* {04 Dec 2015, 05 Jan 2016 - 06 Jan 2016}: Quant: 9 residential buildings; 1 road segment (p1) [IMG, MUL]

### Jubilee Road, Bywell
* Overland flows collected on Jubilee Road and ran down towards the village; the flows carried debris from fields and road surfaces which progressively blocked gullies and contributed to flooding of roads and residents' gardens. (p1) [PAG]
* {07 Jan 2016}: Surface water visibly flowed along the carriageway, documenting accumulation/flow of runoff along the rural road and demonstrating vulnerability of the highway to surface water during heavy rainfall; cited as evidence supporting proposed localised highway improvements and resilience measures. (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 1 road segment (p1) [PAG]

### Ferry Landing, Ovington
* {04 Dec 2015}: River Tyne overtopped its banks at Ferry Landing, causing flooding that affected properties. (p1)
* {04 Dec 2015}: Quant: 9 properties (p1)

### River Tyne
* The River Tyne and its tributaries swelled and overtopped their banks at Ferry Landing, causing riverine flooding that affected residential properties. (p1)
* Quant: 9 properties (p1)
