

# Caunton flooding 18 Oct 2023 - 20 Oct 2023

## Place names with coordinates

### Caunton
* During Storm Babet the village experienced extensive surface water flooding: 39 residential properties suffered internal flooding; additional properties experienced flooding to gardens and outbuildings and roads were flooded. The scale of flooding in the village was greater than any on record. (p1) [PAG]
* The village experienced significant internal flooding during the Storm Babet event: 39 residential properties suffered internal flooding; further properties experienced flooding to gardens and outbuildings. Flooded areas included central village clusters and an outlying farm area (highlighted in Figure 2). (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* The Caunton community is considering how to access the Government's Property Flood Resilience Repair Grant; the council is delivering grant funding to eligible individuals and working with parish council/residents on recovery (including restocking aqua sacs and continuing partnership working). (p8)
* Quant: 39 residential properties (p1) [PAG]
* Quant: 39 residential properties (p2) [IMG, PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* Within Nottinghamshire, the village of Caunton experienced surface water flooding during Storm Babet, resulting in 39 residential properties being internally flooded and further garden/outbuilding and road flooding in the village. (p1)
* County-wide flood event included surface water flooding and a continued risk of river flooding. Flood-affected residents and businesses experienced internal flooding that required verification on site; sandbags were delivered to affected areas and communities; 77 roads were closed across the county to promote road safety and avoid additional flood damage; actions to clean up roads, paths and remove bulky waste from flooded homes were carried out. An investigation was commissioned into surface water runoff/highway drainage in the Mill Lane area as part of the aftermath. (p7)
* Quant: 39 residential properties (p1)
* Quant: 77 roads (p7)

### Newark and Sherwood
* Part of the district (the village of Caunton) experienced surface water flooding during Storm Babet, with 39 residential properties internally flooded and additional garden/outbuilding and road flooding. (p1)
* The district experienced surface water flooding which then included a continued risk of river flooding. Council staff visited impacted communities and vulnerable individuals, assisted evacuations, supplied aqua sacs, provided emergency accommodation where necessary, coordinated clean-up (roads, paths, bulky waste removal) and began recovery and grant funding activity. (p7)
* Quant: 39 residential properties (p1)

### Mill Lane, Caunton
* An investigation was commissioned into surface water runoff and highway drainage on the Mill Lane area following flooding to establish sources and mechanisms of flooding and severity of impacts. (p7)

### River Trent
* Predicted extreme River Trent levels created a continued risk of river flooding, prompting coordinated in-person visits to high-risk communities to reinforce the need to prepare for potential flooding. (p7)


## Topics without coordinates

### North Nottinghamshire
* The IDB continued its annual maintenance regime in Northern Nottinghamshire to reduce flood risk and identify potential risk areas in relation to the flood event. (p8)

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Responded to a Major Incident with significant impacts across the county: attended sites after flooding to verify the scale of internal flooding in properties, offered support, guidance and advice to affected residents and businesses; initiated a Community Flood Recovery Grant to support individuals affected by internal flooding; collated and published verified flood impact data and fed information to other RMAs and partners. (p6)

### Internal Drainage Board
* The IDB attended sites during and after the flood event, cleared watercourse debris, and implemented/continued its regular maintenance regime to reduce flood risk; it is working with partners on solutions and funding/support for recovery. (p8)

### Trent Valley
* The IDB delivered its regular annual maintenance regime in the Trent Valley district during the response to help reduce flood risk and identify potential risk areas. (p8)

### Isle of Axholme Internal Drainage Board
* The IDB reported that its regular annual maintenance regime has been and will continue to be delivered in the Isle of Axholme district to reduce flood risk and identify potential risk areas following the event. (p8)
