

# Northamptonshire flooding 27 Dec 2017

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: B645 East of Hargrave, 2017-12-27<br>090\_North Northamptonshire/28647\_FIR-B645-east-of-Hargrave\_Rev-04\_301018.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Hargrave
* Flood incident on 27 December 2017 at the B645 east of Hargrave where surface water drainage systems were unable to convey rainwater, causing excess surface water to flow to low points around private property. Internal flooding of one dwelling has occurred (on more than one occasion within the last five years). (p7)
* Internal flooding of a property located adjacent to the B645 east of Hargrave; internal flooding has been experienced on more than one occasion in the last five years, triggering a formal investigation. (p9) [PAG]
* Recommendations are made to undertake regular highway drainage cleansing throughout Hargrave to manage highway runoff and reduce flood risk to properties and the highway network. (p16)
* Heavy sleety snow and rainfall on a saturated catchment caused drainage systems to be unable to collect and convey rainwater. Excess surface water flowed over ground following localised topography to low points around private properties; an affected private property was located in a high risk area for surface water flooding. (p18)

## Topics without coordinates

### B645
* Roadside ditches either side of the B645 held standing water upstream of culverts; culverts on the north side of the road (two locations) were wholly or partially blocked, impeding drainage and contributing to surface water accumulation adjacent to the carriageway. (p10)
* {27 Dec 2017, 05 Jan 2018}: Surface water from adjacent ditches overtopped the upstream headwall of a culvert on the north side of the B645 and flowed over ground onto a nearby dwelling. Flood water entered the ground floor of the property (most likely via the front door), flooding the hallway, kitchen, dining room and living room. The front of the house and driveway were also flooded; water stood in the low spot around the house with no positive drainage. The culvert beneath the public footpath and driveway had insufficient capacity and is considered blocked or damaged, leading to overtopping. The resident used a sump pump and a sweeping vehicle to remove water into downstream ditches; standing water and saturated carpets were still present nine days after the event. (p12) [MUL]
* {27 Dec 2017, 05 Jan 2018}: Quant: 200 mm water depth inside the house; 1 property affected (p12) [MUL]

### Anglian Water
* {02 Dec 2017, 04 Dec 2017, 05 Jan 2018}: Anglian Water were attending a burst water main on the north verge of the B645 (approximately 600 metres southwest of the affected property). The leaking main was contributing to standing water in the nearby ditches and fields; Anglian Water confirmed the leak had been reported on 02/12/2017 and repaired on 04/12/2017 and was reported again on 05/01/2018. The report notes the leak may have made some contribution to saturation of the catchment and drainage systems. (p12) [MUL]
* {general}: Anglian Water Services' sewer infrastructure can be overwhelmed in extreme weather, leading to sewer flooding affecting properties (public sewers designed for normal wet weather can be exceeded during extreme events). (p20)

### Northamptonshire Highways
* A flood incident affected a property in the area; Northamptonshire Highways is identified to take the lead in response and to coordinate inspection and maintenance of highway drainage to reduce risk to properties. (p16)

### Water Authority
* {general}: Public and private sewer systems can become overwhelmed in extreme weather, resulting in sewer flooding (flooding caused by overloaded foul, surface water or combined sewer systems). (p20)
