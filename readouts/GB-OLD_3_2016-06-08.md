

# Oldham Metropolitan District flooding 08 Jun 2016 - 10 Jun 2016

Reference document:<br>8th and 10th June 2016 Flood Investigation Report<br>094\_Oldham/S19\_\_8th\_and\_10th\_June\_2016\_Rev\_1.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Oldham Metropolitan District
* Intense/prolonged rainfall on 8 and 10 June 2016 caused widespread flooding across the borough, impacting homes, businesses, local infrastructure and the environment; flood water entered properties (internal habitable space) and gardens/outbuildings (non-habitable space). (p4)
* {general}: The borough (including its urban west/central areas and densely settled valleys) is identified as having areas where rapid runoff and limited drainage capacity lead to flash flooding and surface water flooding. (p10)
* Oldham was annotated as experiencing 'above normal' rainfall in June 2016. The report links high rainfall, increased soil moisture and overland runoff to flooding where river levels are high and local drainage is impeded — conditions that apply to the borough and can produce flooding from ordinary watercourses and surface water during the 8 and 10 June events. (p11) [IMG, PAG]
* {07 Jun 2016 - 10 Jun 2016}: Twelve of Oldham's twenty borough wards reported flooding incidents during the event. Intense downpours/flash flooding caused high runoff leading to surface and urban flooding across the borough; the south‑west parts of the borough (Failsworth East and Hollinwood wards) were the worst affected and reported the most incidents. (p13) [IMG, PAG]
* {01 Jun 2016 - 30 Jun 2016}: The borough experienced intense rainfall in June 2016 leading to surface-water flash flooding. Sewer hydraulic capacity was exceeded in multiple locations across the borough, causing localised highway flooding and flooding of residential properties, worst in south-west parts (Failsworth East and Hollinwood Wards). (p14) [PAG]
* Internal property flooding in the Oldham ward caused by surface water following intense rainfall and surcharging of road gullies and manholes. (p15) [TAB]
* Quant: 62 incidents of internal property flooding; 33 incidents of garden and/or outbuilding (non-habitable space) flooding (p4)
* {07 Jun 2016 - 10 Jun 2016}: Quant: 12 of 20 borough wards; 2 named wards (Failsworth East and Hollinwood) reported the most incidents (p13) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 4 properties flooded internally from surface water (p15) [TAB]

### Failsworth
* {general}: As a densely populated, highly urbanised part of the borough, Failsworth is described in the report as susceptible to limited drainage capacity and build-up of debris (including leaf litter) that can prevent water draining away, increasing risk of surface water/urban flooding during intense rainfall. (p10)
* {01 Jun 2016 - 30 Jun 2016}: Failsworth is identified as particularly prevalent for residential property flooding following the intense rainfall and sewer capacity exceedance; localised highway flooding also occurred in the district. (p14) [PAG]
* Internal property flooding across Failsworth from multiple sources (main river, ordinary watercourse, sewer and surface water). Flooding followed intense short-duration rainfall causing surcharging of road gullies and manholes; combined sewers were suspected to have been running at full capacity leading to surcharge and effluent entering properties. (p15) [TAB]
* Part of the town (Somerset Road area) experienced flooding from surface water surcharging and flash flooding, with inspection chamber surcharging and local property/road inundation. (p21) [IMG]
* Quant: 19 properties flooded internally; 1 property flooded from main river; 6 properties from ordinary watercourse; 6 properties from sewer; 6 properties from surface water (p15) [TAB]
* Quant: 2 properties (flood water entered habitable space) at Rear of Somerset Road; 3 properties (flood water entered garden and/or outbuilding) at Rear of Somerset Road (p21) [IMG]

### Golburn Clough
* The culverted ordinary watercourse (Golburn/Gulbourne Clough) suffered hydraulic incapacity during heavy rainfall. Its trash screen became blocked (poor design), causing water to back up, overflow onto a bridleway, and lead to downstream surcharging and property flooding. (p19)
* Culverted ordinary watercourse reached hydraulic incapacity during heavy rainfall on 8 June 2016. Its trash screen blocked with debris (poor design), causing water to collect and overflow onto a nearby bridleway, surcharging a manhole; water then cascaded down onto Arthurs Lane causing garden/outbuilding flooding and internal flooding of some properties. (p20) [PAG]
* Quant: 3 properties (internal flooding); 21 property gardens and/or outbuildings (p19) [IMG]

### Grasmere Road, Oldham Metropolitan District
* Flood water entered properties at this location: incidents of internal (habitable space) flooding and of water entering gardens/outbuildings were recorded. (p16)
* Heavy rainfall on 8th of June caused surface water, culvert and hydraulic incapacity to result in flooding of properties. Water from an adjacent farmer's field overflowed into rear gardens, escalating quickly and flooding properties; culvert capacity/subsidence led to upstream surcharging contributing to the flooding. (p17) [IMG, PAG]
* On 8th June heavy rainfall caused surface water, combined with culvert and hydraulic incapacity, to overflow from an adjacent farmer's field into rear gardens on Grasmere Road, escalating quickly and resulting in properties being flooded. Following the event remedial works were carried out on the farmer's field: a 150mm perforated pipe was installed and a bund constructed to divert potential flood water into a local brook. (p18) [IMG, PAG]
