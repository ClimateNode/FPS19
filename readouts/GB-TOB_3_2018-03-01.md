### Paignton
* Flooding was reported in Paignton around The Esplanade, Kernou Road, Adelphi Road and Berry Square. Tourism infrastructure at nearby Goodrington, Broadsands and Preston was flooded and damaged. ### Torbay
* {28 Feb 2018 - 04 Mar 2018}: Sea defences in Torbay were overtopped due to a combination of easterly winds, heavy seas and high tides, resulting in coastal flooding of the area. (p5) [TAB]
* A number of tourism facilities in Torbay were flooded, specifically named facilities in the report include: Breakwater Lighthouse; Shoalstone Pool kiosk; Broadsands kiosk and Broadsands kayak hire storeroom; beach kiosk (Preston); and multiple kiosks in Goodrington. (p14) [PAG]

## Topics without coordinates

### Torbay Council
* Torbay Council (as the Lead Local Flood Authority) produced a Section 19 flood investigation report to determine the main causes of flooding in its area, identified actions for other Risk Management Authorities to consider, and will monitor and assist delivery of those actions. (p3) [PAG]
* {late February to early March 2018, February - March 2018}: As Lead Local Flood Authority, Torbay Council was responsible for overseeing flood risk from ordinary watercourses, groundwater and surface water run‑off during the late February–early March 2018 severe winter weather (Storm Emma). Responsibilities described in the text that relate to the event include consenting works on ordinary watercourses, enforcing removal of unlawful structures or obstructions in watercourses, and ensuring a Section 19 flood investigation was carried out and the flood investigation report published following the flooding. (p4)
* {unknown}: Torbay Council's 24-hour control room received flood reports and coordinated the response across the authority; CCTV for incident management was unavailable because the Torbay Council CCTV camera on Paignton Sea Front was destroyed at the beginning of the storm. Engineering and Highways teams were mobilised to manage flooded locations (diversions, warning signs, sandbagging, replacing blown manhole covers, clearing blocked road gullies and trash screens, and post-flood clean-up). (p8) [MUL]
