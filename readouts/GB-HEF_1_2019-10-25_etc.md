### Herefordshire
* {25 Oct 2019 - 26 Oct 2019, February 2020}: Flooding across the county during the October 2019 and February 2020 events resulted in properties being flooded internally above ground-floor level, critical installations being flooded causing loss or potential loss of service, and some facilities rendered inoperable due to impassable access. (p5) [MUL]
* {February 2020}: Multiple locations within Herefordshire experienced river flooding in February 2020, producing internal property flooding and transport disruption: Cusop (internal property flooding), Letton and surrounding area via Dulas Brook (internal property flooding), highways closed (A438 north and south of Letton), and bridges closed (Bredwardine bridge and Bridge Sollars bridge). Hereford experienced internal property flooding including impact to Hereford Leisure Centre plant room and Manor House Care Home. (p7) [TAB]
* {25 Oct 2019 - 26 Oct 2019, February 2020}: Property flooding occurred across Herefordshire during the October 2019 event (intense rainfall on 25–26 October 2019) leading to fluvial flooding; February 2020 produced record-breaking flows and significant flooding across Herefordshire following successive storms (Ciara, Dennis, Jorge) and saturated antecedent conditions. (p16) [MUL]
* {February 2020}: Experienced very wet antecedent conditions and high river flows in February 2020 that produced serious fluvial flooding across the county, with a large number of properties flooded and the highest ever recorded peak flows on the River Wye. (p18)
* {February 2020}: Quant: 15 properties (Cusop); 22 properties (Letton and surrounding area); 94 properties (Hereford); 2 bridges (p7) [TAB]
