

# Haltwhistle flooding 30 Apr 2014

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Haltwhistle<br>091\_Northumberland/Haltwhistle\_FIR\_30Apr2014.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Haltwhistle
* Town-scale flooding from a short period of high-intensity rainfall with flow routes from higher ground and ordinary watercourse (Hemel Burn) and surface water converging in the town centre; multiple residential and some commercial/other buildings affected and flow convergence identified as a potential hotspot near the Police Station. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 15-20 residential; 1-2 business; 1 other building (p1) [IMG, PAG]

### The Mart, Haltwhistle
* Ponding occurred in the Mart from water flowing down Aesica Road/Meadow Close and from surcharging manhole on the culverted section of the watercourse; property-level protection limited damage but there was dampness and seepage through flood doors. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 5-10 properties affected; three properties with dampness over the threshold; 1 surcharging manhole (p1) [IMG]

### Willia Road, Haltwhistle
* Surface water flowed down Willia Rd and contributed to flooding in the Mart area; the flow from Willia Rd also affected properties on nearby Fair Hill. (p1)
* Quant: 2-3 properties (p1)

### Fair Hill, Haltwhistle
* Surface water affected properties on Fair Hill (dampness/entry-level impacts reported as part of wider event). (p1)
* Quant: 2-3 properties (p1)

### Newholme Av, Haltwhistle
* Surface water crossed steep ground above Newholme Av and flowed around and through several properties on Newholme Av; flows continued toward Newholme Rd and other streets. (p1)

### Woodhead Lane, Haltwhistle
* {unknown}: Floodwater from the Hemel Burn flowed down Woodhead Lane as part of the main flow route through the town. (p1)

### Aesica Road, Haltwhistle
* Floodwater travelled along Aesica Road before crossing Meadow Close and contributing to ponding in the Mart. (p1)

### Meadow Close, Haltwhistle
* Floodwater crossed Meadow Close en route to ponding in the Mart. (p1)

### Comb Hill, Haltwhistle
* Surface water flowed onto Coombe Hill (sic) as part of flows originating from higher ground / Newholme Av. (p1)

### Fairfield Park, Haltwhistle
* Floodwater flowed from higher ground / north-northeast through residential streets including Fairfield Park as part of the mapped flow routes during the 30 April 2014 event, contributing to town-centre flooding patterns. (p1) [IMG]

### Moorland Avenue, Haltwhistle
* Floodwater flowed along Moorland Avenue as part of the main flow routes from higher ground toward the town centre, conveying surface water toward lower-lying areas. (p1) [IMG]

### Capel Avenue, Haltwhistle
* Floodwater flowed along Capel Avenue as part of the mapped flow routes originating from higher ground and moving south/southwest through the town. (p1) [IMG]

### Beech Grove, Haltwhistle
* Floodwater flow routes passed through Beech Gr as part of the network of surface water paths moving toward lower elevations in the town. (p1) [IMG]

### Westlands
* Flooding occurred on the entrance to Westlands (surface water flow impacted the access/entrance). (p1) [PAG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Haltwhistle Community Campus
* The Hemel Burn spilled at the culverted entrance adjacent to the campus, indicating local overtopping/spill from the culvert at the campus location and contributing to downstream flows. (p1)
