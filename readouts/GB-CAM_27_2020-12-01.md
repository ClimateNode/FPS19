

# Waterbeach flooding December 2020/January 2021

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Waterbeach<br>021\_Cambridgeshire County/021\_2020\_Cambridgeshire County/Waterbeach-December-2020-Flood-Investigation.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Waterbeach
* {December 2020 - January 2021}: External flooding reported at a property on Ely Road within Waterbeach (gardens/outbuildings/roads affected). (p1) [IMG, TAB]
* {23 Dec 2020}: Surface water flooding affected properties in Waterbeach. The LLFA received information and photographs indicating surface water flooding to multiple properties in the area; no internal flooding was reported to the LLFA. (p2) [IMG]
* {23 Dec 2020 - 24 Dec 2020}: Flooding occurred across Waterbeach on 23/24 December 2020 after a prolonged period of higher-than-average rainfall in autumn 2020 followed by intense rainfall on 23 December. Saturated soils and high groundwater levels prevented infiltration, leading to inundation of the village. (p4)
* {December 2020 - January 2021}: Quant: 1 reported external flooding; 1 property affected (p1) [IMG, TAB]

### Cambridgeshire
* {December 2020 - January 2021}: County experienced flooding incidents during Dec 2020/Jan 2021. The LLFA received one report for Waterbeach describing external flooding to the external land of a property adjacent to the A10 (gardens, outbuildings and/or roads). (p1) [IMG, TAB]
* {23 Dec 2020}: Surface water flooding affected properties in Waterbeach; information and photographs provided to the LLFA indicate flooding to several properties (external/surface water impacts). No internal (internal property) flooding was reported to the LLFA, but conditions matched wider county reports of surface water flooding. (p2) [IMG]
* {December 2020 - January 2021}: Quant: 1 flood report in Waterbeach; 0 reported internal flooding at that Waterbeach location; 1 reported external flooding of gardens/outbuildings/roads; 788 reports across the county; approximately 280 involved internal property flooding (p1) [IMG, TAB]

### Ely Road, Waterbeach
* {December 2020 - January 2021}: External land of a property adjacent to the A10 was flooded (gardens/outbuildings/roads). (p1) [TAB]
* {December 2020 - January 2021}: Quant: 1 reported external flooding of gardens/outbuildings/roads; 1 property affected (p1) [TAB]

### Internal Drainage Board
* {December 2020 – January 2021}: The IDB inspected its nearby watercourse (piped and open sections) following reports of external flooding at the site and found no significant concerns with the system from a flood risk perspective; the investigation could not conclude that the IDB-maintained watercourse was the direct cause of residential flooding. (p3)
