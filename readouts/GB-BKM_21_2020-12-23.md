

# Buckingham flooding 23 Dec 2020 - 24 Dec 2020

## Place names with coordinates

### Buckingham
* {April 1998, July 2007, November 2012, March 2016, 23 Dec 2020 - 24 Dec 2020}: Internal flooding of residential properties in the town. The December 2020 event caused internal flooding to properties; previous events in April 1998 and July 2007 also resulted in substantial property flooding, with additional smaller events in November 2012 and March 2016. (p5) [MUL]
* Properties in Buckingham flooded during the storm; the Environment Agency issued a Flood Warning only after many properties had already flooded, limiting residents' ability to respond. Roads in and around the town were closed. Community volunteers used pumps and buckets to protect properties; the Community Centre was opened for food, drink and rest. The University of Buckingham sand‑bagged at‑risk buildings, moved electrical equipment, provided emergency accommodation to students whose homes were flooded, and set up a local grants fund. (p6)
* Flooding caused internal flooding of properties (including non-residential), external flooding to properties, flooding of roads and car parks, displacement of residents, damage to possessions and stock, and financial and mental-health impacts for residents. (p7) [PAG]
* Internal flooding of residential properties, including basements; the flooding event caused internal flooding to at least 72 properties in the town. (p14)
* The town, situated on the River Great Ouse which flows through the centre, was impacted by flooding during the 23rd–24th December 2020 event. (p15) [PAG]
* {July 2007}: In the most severe recorded event (July 2007) intense rainfall on a saturated catchment caused rivers to rise rapidly and surface water to overwhelm drainage. Floodwater spread through low-lying areas of the town and properties were inundated; the river gauge recorded a new peak (80.66 mAOD) though the gauge likely under-read due to drowning out. (p23) [IMG]
* {1823, 1875, 1908, March 1947, December 1979, April 1998, January 1999, January 2003, July 2007, November 2012, March 2016}: Repeated flooding of the town from the Main River (River Great Ouse) and from overwhelmed surface water/drainage. Major historic impacts include town-centre and low-lying area inundation, properties flooded, and local infrastructure impacts. Specific recorded impacts: July 2007 widespread flooding with 96 properties flooded and a new record peak at the Buckingham river gauge (peak reading recorded at 80.66 mAOD, noting the gauge likely under‑read); April 1998 flooding with 75 properties flooded; March 2016 surface-water related flooding with 6 properties flooded; multiple earlier historic events (1823, 1875, 1908, March 1947, December 1979, January 1999, January 2003) that caused town-centre and low-lying area flooding (including inundation of businesses/public houses and recreational sites). (p24) [TAB, PAG, MUL]
* River Great Ouse floodwaters affected low‑lying areas of Buckingham adjacent to the river. Flood warning areas and zones included specific streets and neighbourhoods in the town, indicating river flooding risk and exposure during the extreme flows experienced in December 2020. (p29) [IMG]
* Areas of Buckingham alongside the River Great Ouse were subject to extreme river flows during the event on 23rd and 24th December 2020. The town contains low-lying streets and locations (e.g. Fulwell, Radclive, Brackley Road, Newcombe Crescent, Foundry Drive, School Lane, Nelson Street, Hunter Street, Chandos Road, Victoria Row, Ford Street, Stratford Road, Marsh Edge, West Street, Fisher's Field, Candleford Court, Cornwall's Meadow and Wharfside Place) that are within Environment Agency flood warning areas and were at risk from River Great Ouse flooding during the December 2020 event. (p30) [PAG]
* The town experienced flooding in December 2020 which has prompted a review of the Buckingham Flood Plan. (p32) [PAG]

### Buckinghamshire
* Across the county there were widespread flood impacts during the storm: Transport for Buckinghamshire managed many road closures and issued sandbags prioritised by need. Anglian Water reported very high flows in its network, pumping stations ran at full capacity, staff visited locations experiencing surface water and foul sewage flooding and advised some residents not to flush toilets. Council incident teams and volunteer LALOs were deployed countywide to support communities. (p6)

### River Great Ouse
* {1823, 1875, 1908, March 1947, December 1979, April 1998, January 1999, January 2003, July 2007}: The Main River (River Great Ouse) overtopped or rose rapidly during multiple historical events, causing widespread regional flooding that inundated low-lying areas of Buckingham and led to property and town-centre flooding. Events attributed to the river include major rises and bank overflow in 1823, 1875, 1908, March 1947, December 1979, April 1998, January 1999, January 2003 and July 2007 (the latter produced a record gauge reading and extensive property flooding). (p24) [TAB, MUL]
* The River Great Ouse experienced extreme flows during the event on 23rd and 24th December 2020, producing elevated flood risk along its corridor through Buckingham. This prompted designated flood warning areas for low-lying locations alongside the river (Fulwell, Radclive and parts of Buckingham) and threatened streets and residential areas adjacent to the channel. (p29) [IMG]
* {1823, 1875, 1908, March 1947, December 1979, April 1998, January 1999, January 2003, July 2007}: Quant: 96 properties; 75 properties (p24) [TAB, MUL]

## Topics without coordinates

### Environment Agency
* The Environment Agency inspected river channels and assets prior to the December 2020 event (last inspection November 2020) and reported the channel at target condition. The EA noted some localised debris, siltation and obstructions in the floodplain but concluded there were no major blockages thought likely to have significantly affected water levels during the extreme flows on 23rd–24th December 2020. The EA also operates flood warning areas for the River Great Ouse in Buckingham and responds to blockages and patrols specific locations during flood events where resources allow. (p30) [PAG]
* {2008/09, 2012-13}: Undertook maintenance and clearance works in the Buckingham area to manage fluvial flood risk (de-silting, back-channel bushing and access clearance). Responds to reports of blockages and obstructions and carries out patrols of specific locations during flood events where resources permit. (p31) [PAG, MUL]

### Buckinghamshire County Council
* The River Great Ouse was the source of river flooding that affected Buckingham on 23rd–24th December 2020; this led to planned further flood modelling and mapping for the river at Buckingham. (p8)
* {general}: Funds and supports flood risk reduction work in the catchment upstream of Buckingham, including funding a River Thame Conservation Trust project to identify and deliver natural flood management (NFM) measures (baseline hydraulic modelling, landowner engagement and options appraisal) intended to reduce flood risk from lower-intensity, higher-frequency events. (p31)

### Anglian Water
* Managed very high flows across Buckingham; pumping stations were running at full capacity throughout the incident. Staff visited locations where flooding from surface water and foul sewage occurred and issued advice to residents (including warnings not to flush toilets). (p6)

### Buckingham Tennis Club
* {January 1999, November 2012}: Chandos Park and the Buckingham Tennis Club were flooded during a Main River - River Great Ouse event in January 1999. Chandos Park was also associated with flooding in November 2012 (Chandos Park/Cornwalls Meadow Car Park) which contributed to 1 property being flooded. (p24) [TAB, MUL]
