

# Didcot flooding 16 Sep 2016

Reference document:<br>Flood Incident Report: Didcot Rail Station Didcot<br>095\_Oxfordshire County/Flood-Investigation-Report-Didcot-Rail-Station.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Didcot
* Flooding occurred at Didcot Railway Station, prompting a Section 19 flood investigation. (p3)
* Flood incident investigated at Didcot Railway Station (reporting a flood event affecting the town's railway station). (p4) [PAG]
* Severe surface water flooding occurred across areas of Didcot on 16th September 2016 as a result of an extreme rainfall event, causing widespread local flooding. (p7)
* Surface water from the adjacent public highway and station car parks flowed overland into Didcot Railway Station, entering and surcharging the station's foul water system because the surface water sewer was overloaded. An obstructed TWA outfall to Network Rail's ditch (requiring maintenance) was identified as contributing to the surcharge. (p8) [PAG]
* Flooding affected Didcot Railway Station and surrounding area (station entrance and adjacent car park). Response actions recorded in the report include Network Rail de-silting an outfall ditch, checks of a private foul sewer, proposals to restrict station entrance access to flood waters, Highway Authority clearing gullies in Station Road, South and Vale District Council cleansing adjacent car park drainage, and TWA checking surface water sewer outfalls. (p11) [PAG]
* Flooding affected Didcot Railway Station and adjacent areas, triggering interventions: Network Rail to clean and de-silt its outfall ditch and check its private foul sewer and consider entrance adjustments to restrict flood water ingress; the Highway Authority cleared gullies in Station Road; South and Vale District Council to cleanse adjacent car park drainage; Thames Water (TWA) checked surface water sewer outfalls. (p12) [PAG]

### Station Road, Didcot
* Surface water/urban flooding affected Station Road drainage; gullies were inspected and cleared to restore capacity and address flooding issues near the railway station. (p12)

### Didcot Parkway Station
* Site of a flood incident and the subject of a formal flood investigation. (p1)
* Surface water from extreme rainfall entered and surcharged the station's foul water system; surface water flowed overland from the adjacent public highway and carparks, causing flooding of the station buildings and infrastructure. (p7)
* Subject of a flood incident investigation following the flood event at Didcot; the station experienced a flood incident and was the focus of investigation and follow-up actions. (p13) [PAG]
* Map annotation shows floodwater entered a building adjacent to the station platforms; water ingress to that building is recorded and may have affected building interior and nearby station infrastructure or operations. Exact depth and extent are not specified on the map. (p18) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Thames Water Authority
* The TWA outfall to Network Rail's ditch was obstructed due to required maintenance on the ditch system, which contributed to surcharge of the surface water sewer and flooding at Parkway Station. (p7)
* Checked and ensured surface water sewer outfalls were clear and able to operate at full capacity as part of the response to the flood incident. (p12)

### Network Rail
* Investigated reports that a flap valve on Network Rail's foul sewer system may have malfunctioned; their foul sewer system was surcharged and their ditch was affected by an obstructed outfall, contributing to flooding at Parkway Station. (p7)
* Floodwater affected Network Rail infrastructure at Didcot Railway Station: Network Rail was required to clean and de-silt its outfall ditch watercourse, check its private foul sewer system and consider adjustments to the station entrance to restrict access by flood waters. (p12)

### Oxfordshire Fire and Rescue Service
* Attended Parkway Station during the flood and attempted to pump out water from the station buildings. (p7)

### Highway Authority
* Checked and cleared gullies in Station Road to restore/ensure drainage function as part of the response to the flood incident. (p12)

### Vale of the White Horse Council
* Asked to check and ensure adjacent car park drainage systems were cleansed and working to address flooding impacts at/near the railway station. (p12)

### Oxfordshire County Council
* The Lead Local Flood Authority and OCC Emergency Planning Team were engaged in the flood response: supporting community-based Flood Wardens, working with the Environment Agency and other authorities to support production of a Community Flood Plan, and providing advice to residents on property-level protection and preparedness. OCC actions also included coordinating checks on drainage assets (e.g. gullies). (p12) [PAG]

### Environment Agency
* The Environment Agency was engaged in supporting the community response: working with OCC and the Emergency Planning Team to support Flood Wardens, advising on property-level protection and community flood planning during the incident. (p12)
