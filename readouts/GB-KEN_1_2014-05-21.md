

# Deal flooding 21 May 2014

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Albert Road – Deal Dover<br>063\_Kent/Albert-Road-section-19-flood-investigation.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Albert Road, Deal
* Residential properties on Albert Road were damaged by the flood event. (p1) [IMG]
* Road and adjacent residential properties were flooded following heavy rainfall; internal flooding and property damage occurred and vehicles were damaged. (p2) [IMG]
* Foul sewers surcharged (due to Golf Road pumping station failure) and excess foul and rainwater discharged onto Albert Road; water could not escape quickly and the surface water sewer lacked capacity, resulting in inundation of the road; road drains were subsequently cleared of foul effluent. (p3)
* Identified as a low point where overland flow from the south is trapped by the railway embankment, creating local flood risk during the flood event; properties here are at risk from surface water/overland flow (noted that properties do not have basements). (p4)
* {1973, 1979, 1988, 2005, 2006, 01 Jun 2007 - 30 Jun 2007, 01 Jan 2008 - 31 Jan 2008, 01 Nov 2009 - 30 Nov 2009, 01 Aug 2010 - 31 Aug 2010, 01 Dec 2013 - 31 Dec 2013}: Repeated historical flooding events on Albert Road (road and vicinity affected), linked in the report to local surface water and foul sewer infrastructure and road drains. (p5) [TAB, MUL]
* Quant: 22 properties (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 22 properties flooded internally; approximately 30 cm deep floodwater on the road (p2) [IMG]

### Matthews Close, Deal
* The ditch at the back of Matthews Close (an ordinary watercourse) is part of the local drainage system; surface water sewers discharge into this ditch which is then culverted to the Penfield Sewer. Improvement works on the ditch had commenced but were incomplete at the time of the flood; the works did not prevent the culvert from functioning and inspection showed the culvert was working during the flood. (p4)


## Topics without coordinates

### Southern Water
* Owns and maintains the nearby foul and surface water sewers and the Golf Road pumping station; reported the pumping station failed due to an electrical fault, surveyed the sewer systems after the flooding and removed silt and debris from their sewer. (p3)
* Owns and maintains surface water and foul sewers in the vicinity of Albert Road and the foul water pumping station; the report notes an electrical fault that resulted in failure of the pumping station and recommends investigation to minimise recurrence. (p5) [TAB]

### Golf Road Pumping Station
* Electrical fault at the Golf Road foul water pumping station caused the pump to fail; the foul sewers backed up and surcharged, sending sewage and rainwater downstream (discharging at Albert Road). (p3)
* Location of the foul water pumping station that experienced an electrical fault resulting in pumping station failure (identified in the report as requiring investigation). (p5) [TAB]

### Kent County Council
* Responsible for road drains and connections on Albert Road and for the culvert; inspected and found road drains, culvert and ditch to be clear and working; cleared road drains of foul effluent resulting from the sewer surcharge/flooding and inspected the ditch behind Matthews Close. (p3)

### River Stour Countryside Management Partnership
* The Partnership recently carried out works to improve the ditch behind Matthews Close intended to increase conveyance capacity; these improvement works were underway but not completed at the time of the flood and thus were a factor in local flood management during the event. (p4)

### Penfield Sewer
* The culverted flow from the ditch behind Matthews Close discharges to the Penfield Sewer (a main river); the culvert conveying flows to the Penfield Sewer was inspected and found to have been functioning during the flood event, indicating it was part of the flow path in the event. (p4)
