

# Burton Upon Trent flooding 12 Mar 2018

## Place names with coordinates

### Kitling Greaves Lane, Burton Upon Trent
* Fluvial flows in the adjacent watercourse, augmented by surface-water runoff from the road and saturated ground, contributed to flooding affecting Kitling Greaves Lane and properties adjacent to it. The road conveys surface water; a culverted section runs beneath part of the lane and a trash screen and gabion structure were noted at the channel; the rain on 12 March 2018 resulted in properties in the area being flooded. (p7) [PAG]
* On 12th March a significant blockage on the upstream face of the culvert under Kitling Greaves Lane (debris and tyres) compromised the culvert's ability to pass flow downstream. This blockage was a major contributory factor to flooding on the lane and affected nearby properties at high risk. The road conveys surface water toward the watercourse; gullies are not designed to convey water from the watercourse. (p8) [PAG]
* Quant: approximately 6 properties (p7) [PAG]

### Highways
* Rain on saturated ground on 12 March 2018 caused flooding which resulted in property flooding in the county. The County Council (through its Highways Department and Staffordshire Civil Contingencies Unit) provided an emergency response to protect and assist local communities and coordinated the Section 19 investigations where properties were internally flooded. (p4)
* Quant: 6 properties (p4)
