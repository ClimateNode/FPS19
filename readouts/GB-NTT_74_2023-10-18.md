

# Sutton-In-Ashfield flooding 18 Oct 2023 - 20 Oct 2023

## Place names with coordinates

### Ashfield
* Areas within the district (notably Sutton in Ashfield) experienced localised internal property flooding, with surface water flows from adjacent fields exacerbating impacts. (p3) [PAG]
* Domestic properties in the district experienced internal flooding; ADC provided emergency response support including prioritising attendance to affected properties, deploying sandbags to prevent internal flooding, opening a Rest Centre at Hucknall Leisure Centre for evacuees, and carrying out inspections of watercourses and drainage assets in Sutton, Kirkby and Jacksdale. (p7)
* Quant: 16 properties (across Sutton in Ashfield) (p3) [PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* County-wide major incident with significant flood impacts: officers verified internal flooding at properties, visited affected residents/businesses, collated and published verified flood data, provided 24‑hour emergency planning support, delivered sandbags, and closed roads to promote safety and prevent further flood damage. The County led the Section 19 Flood Investigation. (p6)
* As Lead Local Flood Authority (NCC) recognised the severe impacts on communities, prioritised support to the most vulnerable, and committed to continuing investigations, improving asset inspection and strengthening enforcement of riparian responsibilities following the flood event. (p7)

### Sutton-In-Ashfield
* Sustained heavy rainfall during Storm Babet caused surface water/urban flooding across Sutton in Ashfield, resulting in flooding of roads and properties as ground was already saturated. (p1) [PAG]
* Highway drainage assets were overwhelmed during Storm Babet, producing highway flooding that, combined with runoff from adjacent fields and bow waves from vehicles, led to internal property flooding in multiple localised areas of Sutton-in-Ashfield. Five distinct localised areas experienced internal flooding; affected roads and properties are identified in the report's figures. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Localised internal property flooding across multiple neighbourhoods; flooding was exacerbated by additional surface water flows from adjacent fields, farmland and green spaces. (p3) [PAG]
* Quant: 16 properties (p3) [PAG]

### Hucknall
* Severe internal flooding of domestic properties led to evacuations; ADC opened a rest centre for evacuees and provided emergency support including prioritised attendance to affected properties and deployment of sandbags to prevent internal flooding. (p7)

## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* {general}: Described as the Lead Local Flood Authority with responsibilities for investigating significant local flooding incidents, leading emergency planning and recovery after flood events, determining which risk management authorities have powers to investigate incidents, and working with communities to raise awareness and encourage participation in local flood risk management. (p5)
* {general}: County Council-led response to flooding: verified and recorded internal flooding at properties, provided on-site support and advice to affected residents and businesses, collated and published verified flood-impact data on Resilience Direct, chaired Strategic/Tactical/Recovery coordination groups, delivered sandbags, closed roads to protect safety and reduce further flood damage, initiated Community Flood Recovery Grant and led the Section 19 flood investigation. (p6)
* As the Lead Local Flood Authority NCC engaged in investigations and response actions following the flooding, prioritising support for vulnerable communities and committing to work with partners on enforcement of riparian responsibilities and strengthening asset inspection processes. (p7)

### Ashfield District Council
* Responded to the flood event across the district: carried out inspections of watercourses and drainage assets (19 Oct 2023), stood up an internal Incident Management Team, prioritised attendance to affected properties, deployed sandbags to prevent internal flooding of domestic premises, opened a rest centre for evacuees, and provided flood communications and advice. (p7)
