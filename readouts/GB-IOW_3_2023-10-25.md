### Monktonmead Flood Alleviation Scheme
* The operation of the Monktonmead Flood Alleviation Scheme during the flood of 25 October 2023 is being reviewed to establish causes of the flooding and how the scheme performed during that event. (p8)

* The scheme was filled by floodwater during the event; floodwater then overtopped installed floodwater boards at Simeon Recreation Ground and flowed onto surrounding streets. (p21)
* The Environment Agency is undertaking a more detailed review of the operation of the scheme during the 25 October 2023 flood event to assess the significance of actions taken and their effect on the flooding; a detailed quantification of incident response impacts requires further flood modelling. (p23)
* The operation of the Monktonmead Flood Alleviation Scheme is identified as requiring detailed review to determine whether and how it influenced the flooding on 25 October 2023. (p26)
