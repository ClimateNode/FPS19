### Northumberland
* A tidal surge (5th/6th December 2013) caused coastal flooding and coastal erosion along the Northumberland coastline, affecting roads, paths and fields and causing cliff erosion; at least eight residential properties across four settlements were flooded. (p3) [PAG]

* Quant: 8 residential properties; four settlements (p3) [PAG]

### North Sea
* {1953, 1978, 05 Dec 2013 - 06 Dec 2013}: Storm/tidal surges originating in the North Sea produced coastal flooding and destruction along the Northumberland coastline (historical events cited: 1953 and 1978) and were responsible for the 5th/6th December 2013 surge. (p5) [PAG, MUL]
* Provided the source of the tidal surge/storm surge that affected the Northumberland coastline on 5/6 December 2013; sea-level changes associated with the surge caused coastal/tidal flooding impacts along the county's coast. (p6)
* A low pressure system over the North Sea pushed water southwards and, combined with strong northwest winds and spring tides, produced a tidal surge that raised sea levels and generated large waves that caused coastal flooding and erosion along the Northumberland coast. (p7) [PAG]
* {1995, 1997, 01 Feb 1997 - 28 Feb 1997, 01 Sep 2002 - 30 Sep 2002, 01 Oct 2005 - 31 Oct 2005}: High tides/sea action from the North Sea caused coastal flooding and sea spray that flooded roads and overtopped/piers and led to properties and roads flooding at places such as Blyth, Berwick and Seahouses; waves exceeded the pier wall at Holy Island. (p19) [TAB, MUL]
* {1995, 1997, 01 Feb 1997 - 28 Feb 1997, 01 Sep 2002 - 30 Sep 2002, 01 Oct 2005 - 31 Oct 2005}: Quant: 1 property (Blyth, 1995); 1 property (Berwick, 1997); roads flooded to 100mm (Blyth, Feb 1997) (p19) [TAB, MUL]
