

# Yalding flooding 20 Jul 2021

## Place names with coordinates

### Yalding
* Intense rainfall on 20 July 2021 caused surface water runoff from surrounding land that overwhelmed highway surface water and sewerage drainage infrastructure, resulting in property flooding in Yalding. (p1)
* The village experienced multiple instances of property flooding reported on specific streets (internal and external flooding across the village as summarised in the table). (p2) [TAB]
* Intense rainfall on 20th July 2021 produced rapid overland flow across the southern parts of Yalding (where soils are slowly permeable with high groundwater), causing surface water flooding that inundated properties. The locations of affected properties align with High Street, Kenward Road and Vicarage Road in the southern area of the village. (p4)
* Yalding experienced the intense rainfall on 20 July 2021 that formed the July flood event. The Event Rarity Calculator assessed the whole event at Yalding as approximately a 1 in 49-year event, and the intense 15:00–15:30 burst as approximately a 1 in 108-year event. The heavy, short-duration rainfall contributed to flooding in Yalding and its history of surface-water incidents. (p5)
* Short, intense rainfall overwhelmed the highway surface water system and caused overland flow from adjacent fields onto the High Street, Vicarage Road and Yalding Hill; gullies and sewers surcharged with foul debris; fire service attended two property flooding incidents on the High Street and police implemented a road closure on Vicarage Road. (p7)
* A short, very intense period of rainfall overwhelmed local surface-water drainage, causing flooding of properties and disruption to local roads. (p8)
* Multiple reported flood incidents across central and eastern residential areas of Yalding, affecting streets labelled on the map: Oast Court, High Street, Mount Avenue, Blunden Lane and Vicarage Road; flooding clustered near main streets and close to the River Beult. (p11) [IMG]
* Quant: 15 properties; 13 properties flooded internally; 2 properties flooded externally (p1)
* Quant: 10 properties (High Street internal) ; 2 properties (Vicarage Road internal) ; 1 property (Oast Court internal) ; 2 properties external (Mount Avenue and Blunden Lane) (p2) [TAB]
* Quant: two flooding incidents; one road closure (p7)
* Quant: 15 properties (p8)

### River Beult
* {general}: Identified as a source of fluvial flood risk for Yalding; previous fluvial flood events associated with the River Beult have affected properties on its southern bank, prompting the fitting of Property Flood Resilience measures to a number of properties (majority along the southern bank and southern end of the High Street). (p4)
* Properties located within the floodplain of the River Beult were affected and pumps from those properties were used to remove floodwater. (p7)
* {general}: Flood Zone 3 closely follows the course of the River Beult; areas adjacent to the river are shown within high flood-risk zones, contributing to flood exposure of nearby rural and some built-up areas, including the Yalding area. (p9) [IMG]
* {general}: Identified on the map as the main river channel associated with the highest flood risk; highest-risk (dark blue) floodplain areas are concentrated along the River Beult and its immediate floodplain, indicating the river is the primary source of flood risk in the mapped area. (p10) [IMG]
* Flooding associated with the River Beult inundated nearby central and eastern parts of Yalding, contributing to reported flood incidents at Oast Court, High Street, Mount Avenue, Blunden Lane and Vicarage Road. (p11) [IMG]
