

# Penwortham flooding 01 Jul 2021 - 31 Jul 2021

Reference document:<br>July 2021 Flooding In Lancashire Factory Lane, Penwortham, South Ribble<br>069\_Lancashire County/section-19-flood-investigation-report-factory-lane-penwortham-july-2021.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Factory Lane, Penwortham
* Lancashire Factory Lane was flooded during the July 2021 event. (p1)
* Three separate floodings between 3 July and 12 July 2021 disrupted access along the lane for residents and businesses, encroached into drives and garden areas, and caused repeated damage to one particular home. (p3)
* Flooding associated with defects in the local culverted watercourse occurred downstream of Factory Lane; investigations link these defects to reported flooding that affected a home (on 3, 10 and 12 July 2021) and other nearby premises. (p4) [PAG]
* Flooding occurred on Factory Lane associated with the organised, heavy and persistent rain on 3 July 2021; the street area was highlighted as a key flooding location in the investigation map (localised inundation from intense rainfall and/or overflow from the nearby watercourse). (p5) [IMG, PAG]
* Blocked gullies caused flooding of the highway and adjacent private property. The Council's local Highways team attended to clear gullies between 3 and 12 July 2021, but flooding recurred in further heavy rain. Subsequent investigation identified defects in a downstream carrier drain / culverted watercourse; tree roots and trapped silt formed a significant obstruction that prevented flows draining away in heavy rainfall. (p6) [PAG, MUL]
* {01 Jul 2021 - 31 Jul 2021, 01 May 2023 - 31 May 2023, 01 Sep 2023 - 30 Sep 2023}: Following clearance of an upstream blockage in May 2021, the drainage systems around Factory Lane became inundated during heavy rainfall in early July 2021. A shallow-gradient culverted watercourse had trapped silt and tree roots, creating a significant obstruction to flows; the culvert was subsequently cleared/repaired. Separate new reports of flooding at a different location on Factory Lane were made in May 2023 and September 2023 (under investigation). (p7) [MUL]
* Quant: three flooding events; one home (p3)
* Quant: 1 home (p4) [PAG]

### South Ribble
* South Ribble (the local authority area) was the administrative area where the July 2021 flooding occurred. (p1)

### Penwortham
* Penwortham experienced flooding during the July 2021 event. (p1)
* An historic culverted watercourse in Penwortham had fallen into disrepair, preventing proper drainage of the watercourse and connected highway surface-water drainage. This contributed to inundation of local drainage systems in heavy rainfall in early July 2021. The culvert was later cleared/repaired and no repeat of the flooding at that location has been reported since those works. (p7)


## Topics without coordinates

### Lancashire County Council
* Acting as the Lead Local Flood Authority, Lancashire County Council has outstanding Section 19 investigations into flooding downstream of Factory Lane linked to defects in the local culverted watercourse; the reported flooding included a home that flooded on 3, 10 and 12 July 2021 and other nearby premises on other dates. (p4)
* The Council's local Highways team attended Factory Lane to clear blocked gullies reported between 3 and 12 July 2021. Highways and Flood Risk Management teams later carried out localised investigations to map surface water drainage which identified a culverted watercourse obstructed by tree roots and silt. (p6)
* Quant: 1 home (p4)

### United Utilities
* United Utilities staff attended on 18 July 2021 after a report of highway flooding; they found no issues with the public sewer network and agreed that water was failing to drain from the highway in heavy rain because of defects in a carrier drain downstream from Factory Lane. (p6)
