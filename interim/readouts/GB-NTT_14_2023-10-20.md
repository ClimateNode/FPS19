

# Carlton-On-Trent flooding 20 Oct 2023

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Carlton On Trent – Storm Babet, October 2023<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/carlton-on-trent-s19-storm-babet-oct-2023.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Nottinghamshire
* The county experienced a Major Incident with significant impacts across the county during the event, including highway flooding and internal flooding of properties which officers attended to verify and record. (p4)
* Surface water flooding which then progressed to a continued risk of river flooding across the county, causing road closures and flooding of homes and communities. (p5)
* Quant: 77 roads (p5)

### Newark and Sherwood
* District council played an intensive role in the flood response: formed a large multi-department staff team, visited impacted communities and vulnerable individuals, supported evacuations and provided emergency accommodation, delivered aqua sacs and other assistance, coordinated public communications and briefings, conducted clean-up of roads/paths and removal of bulky waste from flooded homes, coordinated recovery and grant delivery to eligible residents. (p5)

### Carlton-On-Trent
* Village suffered a flood event caused by sustained heavy rainfall during Storm Babet; saturated ground led to flooding of roads and properties. (p1)
* Area within Carlton on Trent was affected by surface water flooding during Storm Babet; Old North Road within Carlton experienced internal property flooding driven by pluvial/surface-water runoff and overwhelmed highway drainage. (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 8 properties (p2) [IMG]

### Old North Road, Carlton-On-Trent
* Highway drainage assets were overwhelmed during Storm Babet causing highway flooding and runoff from the highway and adjacent agricultural land to enter properties on Old North Road, resulting in internal property flooding. (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 8 properties (p2) [IMG]

### Whiteley Plantation
* {general}: Mapped by the Environment Agency on the fluvial flood map as lying within the extent of flooding from rivers; therefore the plantation area is shown at risk of fluvial inundation (no specific reports of physical damage in the text). (p3) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Highway Authority
* {general}: Most highway drainage assets were unable to discharge the volume of water falling during the event, causing highway flooding; this highway flooding subsequently led to internal flooding of properties due to a combination of highway runoff and runoff from adjacent agricultural land. (p4)

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Acted as the Lead Local Flood Authority in response to flooding from Storm Babet: coordinating multi-agency debriefs and continuing investigations, developing Flood Warden / Road Closure training, supporting community volunteer groups, and reviewing Property Flood Resilience options for affected communities. (p6)
