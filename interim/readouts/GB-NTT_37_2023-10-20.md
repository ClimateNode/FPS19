

# Kirklington flooding 20 Oct 2023

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Kirklington – Storm Babet, October 2023<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/kirklington-s19-storm-babet-oct-2023.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Kirklington
* Sustained heavy rainfall during Storm Babet caused surface water flooding in Kirklington; saturated ground led to rapid flooding of roads and properties. (p1)
* Internal property flooding occurred across areas of Kirklington, affecting properties on multiple streets (listed below). (p2) [PAG]
* Surface water runoff during Storm Babet flowed along the A617 into the village, overtopping the highway and causing internal property flooding on Main Street; the EA surface water risk map extract shows highest surface-water risk following the river corridor through the centre of Kirklington. (p3) [IMG]
* Within Kirklington multiple watercourses overtopped and surface water ran into the village: overtopping of Northern Drain, Southern Drain, Edingley Beck and River Greet caused internal property flooding; surface water flows along the A617 also caused internal flooding on Main Street. (p4) [PAG]
* Quant: 4 properties on Main Street; 1 property on Church Lane; 3 properties on Southwell Road; 2 properties in The Crops (p2) [PAG]
* Quant: 6 properties (p4) [PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* Provided short-term recovery aid for internal (property) flooding via a Community Flood Recovery Grant; visited flood-affected residents and businesses to establish sources/mechanisms and severity of flooding; delivered sandbags to affected areas; closed 77 roads across the county to promote road safety and avoid additional flood damage; provided 24-hour emergency planning support; led the Section 19 flood investigation and collated/published verified flood impact data on Resilience Direct; led procurement and delivery of DEFRA Property Flood Resilience Repair Grant Scheme. (p6)
* Quant: 77 roads (p6)

### Newark and Sherwood
* Visited impacted communities and vulnerable individuals; delivered aqua sacs and assisted with evacuations; provided emergency accommodation where necessary; arranged cleanup of roads and paths and removal of bulky waste from flooded homes as risk shifted from surface water flooding to ongoing river flooding; contacted affected individuals to identify humanitarian needs and deliver or arrange support; commenced recovery role including delivering grant funding to eligible residents. (p6)

### Bilsthorpe
* Community was affected by flooding associated with Storm Babet; prompted development of a community flood signage scheme, engagement with the Flood Risk Management team, restocking of aqua sacs and consideration for Property Flood Resilience support. (p7)

### Main Street, Kirklington
* Internal property flooding affected properties on Main Street (A617). (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* During Storm Babet substantial surface water runoff from agricultural land north and south of the A617 flowed down the road, overtopping the highway at low/dropped kerbs and causing internal property flooding along Main Street. Flows from the east and adjacent agricultural land further exacerbated flooding. (p3) [PAG]
* Surface water flowing down the A617 overtopped the highway and entered Main Street (A617), leading to internal property flooding across the street. (p4) [PAG]
* Quant: 4 properties (p2) [IMG, PAG]

## Topics without coordinates

### A617
* Floodwater affected the A617/Main Street in Kirklington causing internal property flooding and likely access disruption along the main thoroughfare. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Substantial surface water runoff from surrounding agricultural land flowed along the A617, overtopping the highway at locations with low or dropped kerbs and carrying water toward the village; these flows contributed to internal property flooding on Main Street. (p4) [PAG]
* Quant: 4 properties (p2) [IMG, PAG]

### Northern Drain
* The Northern Drain overtopped during the event and caused internal property flooding due to the quantity of water falling in the catchment. (p4)
* Quant: 1 property (p4)

### Southern Drain
* Overtopping of the Southern Drain during the event caused internal flooding of properties in Kirklington. (p4)
* Quant: 2 properties (p4)

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Played a key role in response to the major flood incident on 20th October 2023: initiated a Community Flood Recovery Grant to support people affected by internal flooding; gathered information on site and fed it back to relevant RMAs and partners; collated and published verified flood impact data. (p5)
