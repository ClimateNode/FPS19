

# Bolton flooding 09 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Bolton Council Flooding Investigation Report: Flooding at Riverside Drive and Smiths Road on 9th February 2020 – Storm Ciara<br>010\_Bolton/010\_2020\_Bolton/Storm\_Ciara\_2020\_\_\_Flood\_report.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Riverside Drive, Prestolee
* Internal flooding of properties at around 1.30pm on 9 February 2020; flood depths were significant with around 1 metre depth in some properties. (p3)
* Flooding occurred at Riverside Drive, a residential area; the location is one of the two specific flood locations investigated in the report. (p6) [IMG]
* {26 Dec 2015}: Internal flooding of residential properties on Riverside Drive caused by the River Irwell overtopping during Storm Eva. (p13) [PAG]
* Inundation during the river flooding caused siltation of highway drainage gullies; gullies were found with high silt levels and were cleaned after the flood event. (p14) [PAG]
* {general}: High silt levels in highway drainage gullies due to inundation of material during river flooding; gullies were cleaned after the flood. (p15) [PAG]
* During the flood the River Irwell rose and back-filled the surface water sewer outfall serving Riverside Drive, causing water to issue from highway gullies and resulting in flooding of the highway before the river overtopped its bank. Debris (a rolled-up section of chain link fencing) was found trapped at the sewer outfall hindering discharge; the outfall brickwork showed structural damage likely from impact by floating debris. (p16)
* Floodwaters from the River Irwell spilled onto Riverside Drive (via the left bank), depositing a sandy mix of debris with a high proportion of man-made material on the embankment and making the embankment marginally stable; the road and adjoining embankment were directly affected by the river flooding. (p17) [PAG]
* River water spilled onto Riverside Drive via the left bank during the storm, resulting in flooding affecting the Riverside Drive community. (p18)
* River Irwell swelled and flooded into its floodplain at Riverside Drive, inundating properties. A Forward Incident Officer was deployed to Riverside Drive at ~11:00 on 9 February to carry out door-knocking and advise evacuation; some residents evacuated while others remained. Follow-up visits and clean-up were carried out, with river silt and debris deposited on external areas and in highway gullies. (p19)
* Flooding affected Riverside Drive: the surface water outfall was identified as structurally impaired and there was risk of backflow into the surface water sewer during flood events. This led to continued development of a flood defence project for Riverside Drive and recommendations to consider reconstruction of the outfall and installation of non-return valves to reduce property flooding. (p21) [TAB]
* The surface water outfall at Riverside Drive is structurally impaired, contributing to flooding risk and allowing potential backflow into the drainage system during flood events; this has led RMAs to recommend reconstruction of the outfall and consideration of non-return valves to reduce property flooding. (p22) [PAG]
* Quant: 25 properties; 5 businesses; 1 metre depth in some properties (p3)
* {26 Dec 2015}: Quant: 36 properties (p13) [PAG]
* Quant: 10 Adult Social Care Officers; 2 Housing Officers; 2 Forward Incident Officers (p19)

### Smith's Road, Darcy Lever
* Internal flooding of properties and businesses on 9 February 2020 caused by fluvial flooding from the Rivers Irwell and Croal. (p3)
* Map identifies a flood location at the downstream edge of the catchment upstream of Smiths Road, indicating river flooding occurred at or immediately upstream of the area near Smiths Road. (p11) [IMG]
* Properties on Smiths Road were flooded on 9th February 2020 (the River Croal monitoring station at Moses Gate is approx. 1 km downstream of these flooded properties). (p12)
* {14 Jun 2002, 26 Dec 2015}: Internal flooding of properties and businesses due to River Irwell flooding: 5 residential properties and 2 businesses flooded internally on 26 December 2015 (Storm Eva). There is also a recorded flooding event at this location on 14 June 2002. (p13) [MUL]
* {14 Jun 2002, 26 Dec 2015, 09 Feb 2020}: Historic and recorded flooding occurred at Smith Road: river/surface-water related inundation affected residential and commercial properties; anecdotal evidence indicates areas served by sewers drained away after the river flooding associated with the 9 February event. (p14) [MUL]
* Highway drainage performed poorly during the flood, with gully gratings having become proud of the carriageway and the sag point served by only a single gully, reducing interception and flow capacity; gullies needed resetting and additional capacity to intercept water during the event. (p17)
* Location where the River Croal swelled on 9th February 2020 and flooded into the floodplain, resulting in properties being inundated. (p19)
* Surface water flooding and potential backflow into surface water sewer outfalls affecting the road and nearby properties; actions suggested include more frequent gully maintenance and examination of non-return valves on outfalls. (p21) [TAB]
* Surface water and sewer-related flooding affected properties at Riverside Drive and Smiths Road, with overflow risk into private property (specifically risk of overflow into the vehicle access and into no. 3 Smiths Road). The locations experienced issues with potential backflow from surface water/sewer outfalls during flood events. (p22)
* Quant: 25 properties; 5 businesses (Riverside Drive and Smiths Road) (p3)
* {14 Jun 2002, 26 Dec 2015}: Quant: 5 residential properties; 2 businesses (p13) [MUL]
* {14 Jun 2002, 26 Dec 2015, 09 Feb 2020}: Quant: 5 residential properties; 2 businesses (p14) [MUL]

### River Irwell
* Fluvial flooding from the Rivers Irwell and Croal (after significant rainfall on saturated ground) caused internal flooding of properties and businesses in Riverside Drive, Prestolee and Smiths Road. (p3)
* {20 Sep 1946, 26 Dec 2015, 09 Feb 2020}: On 9 February 2020 the River Irwell rose to a peak of 1.31m at 13:30 (likely the fifth highest since 1948). A Flood Warning for the River Irwell at Prestolee and Ringley Bridges was issued at 09:05 on 9 February and reports of flooding to property in the area were made from approximately 12:45 (Community Flood Group). Historically the River Irwell has overflowed at Prestolee causing internal flooding (20 September 1946) and on 26 December 2015 (Storm Eva) flooding from the River Irwell caused internal flooding to residential properties on Riverside Drive and to properties on the opposite bank at Stoneclough Rise; Smiths Road also recorded internal flooding on 26 December 2015. (p13) [MUL]
* The River Irwell rose and flooded over its banks; rising river levels filled the surface water sewer system at the outfall serving Riverside Drive, causing backflow into highway gullies and resulting in highway flooding before the river bank was overtopped. Floating debris from the river impacted and damaged the brickwork outfall structure. (p16)
* The River Irwell overtopped/impacted its embankment adjacent to Riverside Drive, leaving a sandy mix of debris (including man‑made material) on the embankment and contributing to marginal embankment stability and downstream flooding of Riverside Drive. (p17)
* The River Irwell at Riverside Drive swelled on 9th February 2020 and flooded into its floodplain, inundating property; a Flood Alert and Flood Warning were issued for the Irwell (Prestolee and Ringley area) with a targeted Flood Warning issued at 09:05 and the first threshold crossed at 10:30. (p19) [PAG]
* River Irwell rose to levels that triggered a Flood Alert and a Flood Warning for the Prestolee/Stoneclough/Ringley area; the first Flood Warning threshold for the area was crossed on the day of the event. After the event the EA Operations team removed sediment from the river in the area, assessed the viability of temporary defences and treated invasive riverbank vegetation to enable assessment of a potential flood wall; the EA also surveyed and re-modelled the river as part of a Strategic Business Case and progressed to an Outline Business Case for a proposed flood scheme. (p20)
* Quant: 25 properties; 5 businesses; 1 metre depth in some properties (p3)
* {20 Sep 1946, 26 Dec 2015, 09 Feb 2020}: Quant: 36 properties; 9 properties; 5 residential properties; 2 businesses (p13) [MUL]
* Quant: first Flood Warning threshold crossed; 81 properties within GM25 Flood Warning Area; 74 properties potentially received the flood warning; 52 properties registered with Floodline; 22 properties via Extended Direct Warnings (p20)

### Bolton
* Flooding affected Smiths Road (described as being to the south east of Bolton centre, mixed residential and commercial). Smiths Road is listed as a location for the 9 February 2020 flooding event and is the subject of the investigation. (p6) [IMG]
* River Irwell flooding in the Bolton area (notably Prestolee and Ringley Bridges) generated Flood Warnings and reports of property flooding from approximately 12:45pm on 9th February 2020. (p13)

### Prestolee
* Prestolee (including Riverside Drive and Smiths Road) was directly impacted by river flooding on 9 February 2020, resulting in internal flooding of properties and businesses; properties on Riverside Drive experienced significant inundation (around 1 metre depth in some cases). (p3)
* {20 Sep 1946, 26 Dec 2015, 09 Feb 2020}: River Irwell flooding at Prestolee/Ringley Bridges led to reports of flooding to property starting at approximately 12:45pm following the Flood Warning issued on 9th February 2020. Prestolee has also been the location of past River Irwell overflows (20th September 1946) and the Boxing Day 2015 event associated with Storm Eva. (p13) [MUL]
* The Prestolee community was specifically targeted by a Flood Warning issued on the morning of the event; EA staff provided real-time forecasting information to the local community flood group during the incident and used the group to organise a post-event community drop-in. (p20)
* Quant: 25 properties; 5 businesses; up to 1 metre depth (in some Riverside Drive properties) (p3)

## Topics without coordinates

### Environment Agency
* The Environment Agency issued and managed the Flood Alert and Flood Warning for the GM25 area, issued a targeted Flood Warning for Prestolee/Stoneclough/Ringley, provided real-time forecasting information to the community flood group during the incident, organised post-event community engagement (drop-in on 11 March), and carried out post-event operational works (sediment removal, temporary-defence viability assessments, invasive species treatment) and technical appraisal work (surveying, re-modelling, Strategic Business Case and commencement of an Outline Business Case for a flood scheme). (p20)
* The Environment Agency is recorded as continuing maintenance activities under its prioritised system and progressing a flood defence project for Riverside Drive following flooding. It is also referenced in relation to advising and considering measures to reduce overflow into properties (e.g., kerb height, additional gullies). (p21) [TAB, PAG]
* Quant: 81 properties within GM25 Flood Warning Area; 74 properties potentially received the flood warning; 52 properties registered with Floodline; 22 properties via Extended Direct Warnings (p20)

### Bolton Council
* As Highway Authority, the council surveyed and cleaned highway gullies after the flooding and recorded siltation, blockages and condition issues resulting from the inundation. (p14)
* {09 Feb 2020, 10 Feb 2020, 12 Feb 2020, 17 Feb 2020}: Responded to flood warnings and the event on 9 February by deploying staff to affected areas (including Riverside Drive), carrying out door-knocking and evacuation advice, arranging alternative accommodation for displaced families, organising clean-up of external areas and removal of river silt and debris, arranging additional waste collections, and cleaning highway drainage gullies which had received heavy silt input. (p19) [MUL]
* Acted as the Lead Local Flood Authority and worked with the Environment Agency and United Utilities to support communities affected by the river flooding on 9th February 2020, and to facilitate sharing of information between Risk Management Authorities and the community. (p20) [PAG]
* {09 Feb 2020, 10 Feb 2020, 12 Feb 2020, 17 Feb 2020}: Quant: 10 Adult Social Care Officers; 2 Housing Officers; 2 Forward Incident Officers; 2 additional waste collections (week commencing 10 Feb); 1 additional waste collection (17 Feb) (p19) [MUL]

### Riverside Drive Pumping Station
* Partially submerged by the flooding; its location within the flood zone makes it susceptible to outage during serious flooding, with the potential for pollution. During the 9th February 2020 event the pumping station remained operational despite the flood exposure. (p14)
* The foul pumping station is described as susceptible to flooding and not sited above the 1-in-200-year flood level. Alterations made by United Utilities after the 2015 flood (raising control equipment within the kiosk) ensured the asset remained resilient and operational during the 9 February 2020 flood. (p16)

### United Utilities
* The foul drainage pumping station on Riverside Drive was partially submerged by flooding; United Utilities reported the pumping station performed during the 9 February event except for a short incoming power failure. (p14)
* Responded to the flood by undertaking a CCTV survey of the local sewer network (no defects/operational issues found) and removed the debris trapped in the surface water sewer outfall within 24 hours of notification. United Utilities had previously raised control equipment in the Riverside Drive pumping station following the 2015 flood, which ensured the asset remained resilient during this flood. (p16)
* Surface water sewer outfalls at Riverside Drive and Smiths Road were susceptible to backflow during Storm Ciara (9 Feb 2020), contributing to flood risk to nearby properties; a surface water outfall at Riverside Drive is identified as structurally impaired. United Utilities were recommended to consider non-return valves on surface water sewer/river outfalls and reconstruction of the impaired outfall to reduce backflow and property flooding. (p21) [TAB, PAG]

### Prestolee Weir
* {2013, 09 Feb 2020}: The weir was in a significant state of disrepair and was removed as part of the Environment Agency's programme; its condition/removal (weir situated ~450 m upstream of Prestolee Bridge and ~500 m from the Riverside Drive spill point) was cited by a resident as having a possible influence on recent flooding at Riverside Drive. (p17) [MUL]
