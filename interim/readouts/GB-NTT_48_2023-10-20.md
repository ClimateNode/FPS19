

# Ordsall flooding 20 Oct 2023

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Ordsall – Storm Babet, October 2023<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/ordsall-s19-storm-babet-oct-2023.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Ordsall
* Sustained heavy rainfall (Storm Babet) caused a flood event in Ordsall resulting in internal flooding of residential properties and a business, and additional flooding to gardens and outbuildings. (p1)
* Residential properties and at least one business experienced internal flooding; additional flooding affected gardens and outbuildings. Roads and properties were flooded more quickly because the ground was already saturated after a prolonged period of rain. (p2) [PAG]
* Parts of Ordsall were affected by flooding associated with the River Idle rising; specific streets within Ordsall were identified as flood-affected. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Ordsall experienced fluvial flooding driven by the River Idle rising and overtopping its defences; floodwaters affected highways, adjacent fields and caused internal flooding to residential and business properties in the town. (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* {19 Oct 2023, 21 Oct 2023, 22 Oct 2023}: Subject of Flood Warning (21/10/2023) and Severe Flood Warning (22/10/2023) for the River Idle at Ordsall; Environment Agency installed flood gates at Ordsall and deployed pumps; EA liaised with police and fire & rescue services to evacuate residents as conditions worsened. (p7) [PAG, MUL]
* Ordsall was affected by flooding from the River Idle during Storm Babet; the Environment Agency is investigating upstream flood storage options that may benefit Ordsall, and a multi-agency drop-in event is planned. (p8)
* Quant: 6 residential properties; 1 business (p1)
* Quant: 6 residential properties; 1 business (p2) [PAG]
* Quant: Three residential properties and one business internally flooded on All Hallows Road; three residential properties internally flooded on Goosemoor Lane (p4) [IMG, PAG]

### River Idle
* The River Idle rose through 20th, 21st, 22nd and the early morning of 23rd October 2023, reaching a peak level of 1.79m and exceeding the previous record. This high river level caused flooding in the Ordsall area. (p3) [PAG]
* The River Idle rose through 20th–23rd October and overtopped its defences. The overtopping released water onto the highway at the south side of the bridge, initiating flows across nearby streets, into adjacent fields and contributing to internal flooding of properties in Ordsall. (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* {19 Oct 2023, 21 Oct 2023, 22 Oct 2023}: Triggered a Flood Alert (19/10/2023), Flood Warning (21/10/2023) and a Severe Flood Warning (22/10/2023) for locations along the river; Environment Agency carried out flood patrols, cleared blockages before and after peak flows, installed flood gates (at Ordsall) and set up pumps in advance of the storm; EA plans to de-silt the River Idle through the town centre and downstream and carry out tree works to improve flow. (p7) [MUL]
* The River Idle caused flooding through Retford town centre and downstream during Storm Babet; the Environment Agency will de-silt the river through the town centre and downstream, carry out tree works to improve flow, and is investigating upstream flood storage to reduce future flooding. (p8) [PAG]
* Quant: peak level 1.79m (p3) [PAG]
* Quant: Reached a peak level of 1.79m (above previous record of 1.65m) (p4) [IMG, PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* County-wide flooding led to internal flooding of properties (county officers attended sites to verify internal flooding), community support actions (sandbag deliveries, visits to affected residents and businesses, provision of 24-hour emergency planning support), and transport disruption (roads closed to promote safety and avoid additional flood damage). Nottinghamshire County Council also initiated recovery and grant support (Community Flood Recovery Grant; led procurement and delivery of DEFRA Property Flood Resilience Repair Grant Scheme) and collated/published verified flood impact data. (p6)
* {19 Oct 2023, 21 Oct 2023, 22 Oct 2023}: Parts of Nottinghamshire were affected by the River Idle flood incident: a Flood Alert for the River Idle in Nottinghamshire (19/10/2023), Flood Warning and Severe Flood Warning issued for locations within the county; Environment Agency patrols, blockage clearance and coordination of evacuations took place in the county; planned river de-silting and tree works are intended to improve flows. (p7) [MUL]
* Quant: 77 roads (p6)

### Retford
* Flooding from the River Idle affected Retford town centre during Storm Babet; planned/ongoing actions include de-silting the River Idle through the town centre and downstream, tree works to improve flow, investigation of upstream flood storage options, and a multi-agency drop-in event. (p8)

### All Hallows Street, Ordsall
* Identified as one of the areas affected by the flood event; properties and street areas experienced flooding (including gardens and outbuildings in the affected area). (p2)
* All Hallows Street is identified on the map and in text as a flood-affected street in Ordsall; the road was inundated during the River Idle flood event, affecting the connecting residential area. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* When the River Idle overtopped defences at the south side of the bridge, water poured onto All Hallows Street, flowed across the front of Goosemoor Lane and down All Hallows Street, flowing off the carriageway and into properties. Rising river levels later flooded the carriageway across its full width, causing internal flooding. (p4)
* Quant: Flooded across the carriageway to a depth of approximately 2 foot; contributed to three residential and one business being internally flooded (on All Hallows Road) (p4)

### Goosemoor Lane, Ordsall
* Identified as one of the areas affected by the flood event; properties and street areas experienced flooding (including gardens and outbuildings in the affected area). (p2)
* Goosemoor Lane is identified on the map and in text as a flood-affected street in Ordsall; the lane was inundated during the River Idle flood event, impacting the adjacent residential cluster and route connections. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Properties on Goosemoor Lane were surrounded by floodwater from All Hallows Street and by River Idle overtopping into adjacent fields; flood levels caused internal flooding and isolation of properties. (p4)
* Quant: Three residential properties internally flooded (p4)

### All Hallows Road

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Major countywide flooding / major incident prompted council response: officers verified internal flooding at residences and businesses, offered support and advice, collated and published verified flood impact data, delivered sandbags, provided 24-hour emergency planning support, chaired and attended Strategic/Tactical/Recovery coordination, led the Section 19 Flood Investigation and led procurement/delivery of the DEFRA Property Flood Resilience Repair Grant Scheme. The council also closed roads to promote safety and shared resources with district/borough partners. (p6)
* {22 Oct 2023}: As Lead Local Flood Authority, continued involvement in investigation and response to the river-flooding event; committed to working with partners and communities, prioritising the most vulnerable, and continuing investigations into causes and future actions following the event. (p7)
* Quant: 77 roads (p6)

### Bassetlaw District Council
* {22 Oct 2023}: Provided emergency response during the river-flooding event: set up a rest centre (Retford Leisure Centre), provided temporary accommodation to affected residents, rehoused affected council tenants, undertook repairs to affected council housing properties, provided skips/caged vans and staffing to dispose of flood-damaged property, and carried out cleansing of significant detritus from roads, car parks and paths. Promoted and administered Business Recovery Grant, Business Rates Relief, Community Recovery Grant and Council Tax Discount schemes; worked with partners on Property Flood Resilience Grant verification. (p7)
