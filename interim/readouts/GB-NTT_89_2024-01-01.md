

# Worksop flooding 01 Jan 2024 - 02 Jan 2024

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Worksop – Storm Henk, January 2024<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/worksop-s19-storm-henk-jan-2024.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Worksop
* {01 Jan 2024 - 31 Jan 2024}: Flooding occurred in the town during Storm Henk (January 2024). (p1) [PAG]
* The River Ryton, which flows through Worksop, rose after sustained heavy rainfall and exceeded the property-flooding indicator level; properties on several streets in central Worksop were flooded. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Parts of Worksop experienced internal and external property flooding. Data show a total of 14 residential and 3 business properties suffered internal property flooding in the affected area, with additional external flooding affecting gardens and outbuildings. Named streets affected include Allen Street (12 properties), Hardy Street (5 properties) and Bridge Street (1 business property). (p3) [PAG]
* The Environment Agency issued Flood Warnings for the River Ryton at Worksop (including Worksop town centre). Multi-agency activity (EA flood patrols and blockage clearance, community information gathering and a multi-agency drop-in) was carried out to understand and respond to impacts on affected communities; local authorities provided sandbags/resilience stores and other support to vulnerable premises/residents in the district. (p5)
* Conveyance and tree works and desilting through the town centre were proposed as recovery works, indicating the river's channel and banks were affected by the flood. (p6)
* Quant: 18 properties (12 on Allen Street; 5 on Hardy Street; 1 on Bridge Street) (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 14 residential properties; 3 business properties; 12 properties on Allen Street; 5 properties on Hardy Street; 1 property on Bridge Street (p3) [PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* Flooding across the county prompted a coordinated emergency response: 24-hour support from the Emergency Planning team; initiation and leading of a Section 19 Flood Investigation; delivery of sandbags to affected communities; closure of roads to protect safety and prevent further flood damage; and procurement/delivery support for the DEFRA Property Flood Resilience Repair Grant Scheme. (p5)
* Quant: 69 roads (p5)

### Shireoaks
* Included in an Environment Agency Flood Warning for the River Ryton at Worksop town centre; subject to EA patrols and clearance of blockages and multi-agency response. (p5)

### Allen Street, Kilton

* Floodwater flowed down Allen Street as part of flows from the Bull Brook, causing property flooding; Allen Street is specifically listed as affected in Figure 2. (p3) [PAG]
* Quant: 12 properties (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 12 properties (p3) [PAG]

### Bridge Street, Kilton
* At least one property on Bridge Street was flooded associated with the rise in the River Ryton during the rainfall event. (p2) [IMG]
* One business property on Bridge Street suffered internal property flooding caused by localised surface water pooling. (p3) [PAG]
* Quant: 1 property (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 1 business property (p3) [PAG]

### Hardy Street, Kilton
* Properties on Hardy Street were flooded as the River Ryton rose during the sustained heavy rainfall event. (p2) [IMG]
* Floodwater flowed down Hardy Street as part of flows from the Bull Brook, causing internal property flooding. (p3) [PAG]
* Quant: 5 properties (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 5 properties (p3) [PAG]

### Central Avenue, Kilton
* Floodwater from the Bull Brook appeared at Central Avenue and flowed down Central Avenue, contributing to internal and external flooding in the local area; Environment Agency data records a total of 14 residential and 3 business properties suffered internal property flooding in this area, with additional external flooding affecting gardens and outbuildings. (p3)
* Quant: 14 residential properties; 3 business properties (p3)

### King Street, Kilton
* Floodwater flowed down King Street as part of flows from the Bull Brook, contributing to internal flooding in the local area; included within the area totals recorded by the Environment Agency. (p3)
* Quant: Part of area total of 14 residential properties and 3 business properties (p3)

### Brook Terrace, Kilton
* Bull Brook rose at Brook Terrace but remained within its channel; this did not cause any internal property flooding at Brook Terrace and did not produce flows down Newcastle Avenue. (p3) [MUL]

### Shelley Street, Kilton
* A Flood Warning was issued for the River Ryton at Shelley Street; area was included in EA patrols and response activity. (p5)

### The Canch, Kilton
* Desilting and conveyance recovery works proposed downstream at the Canch following the flood (desilting of River Ryton through town centre and downstream Canch). (p6)

### Priorswell Road, Kilton
* Identified as a location where a section of new floodwall may be required at the Canch to manage river flooding risk; included in proposed recovery/defence works. (p6)

### River Ryton
* The River Ryton rose during the event, peaking at the Worksop gauge on Stubbing Lane. Levels exceeded the Defra-noted level at which property flooding is possible (noted as 1.3m on the river level website), though were lower than levels recorded during Storm Babet. (p3)
* The River Ryton was the focus of Flood Warnings (for Worksop, Shelley Street and Shireoaks) and prompted EA patrols and clearance of blockages and multi-agency response to flooding impacts in the area. (p5)
* Conveyance and tree works and desilting through the town centre were proposed as recovery works, indicating the river's channel and banks were affected by the flood. (p6)


## Topics without coordinates

### Bull Brook
* Reports indicate flood water appeared to come from the Bull Brook at Central Avenue and flowed down Central Avenue, King Street, Allen Street and Hardy Street, causing internal property flooding in the area. At Brook Terrace the brook rose but remained in channel and did not cause internal property flooding there. (p3)
* Quant: 14 residential properties; 3 business properties; 12 properties on Allen Street; 5 properties on Hardy Street (p3)

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Responded to a Major Incident with significant county‑wide flood impacts: verified internal flooding at properties, provided support, guidance and a Community Flood Recovery Grant to assist individuals affected by internal flooding, fed on‑site information to other RMAs and chaired coordination meetings. (p4)
* As Lead Local Flood Authority, NCC coordinated response and recovery activity after Storm Henk, prioritised support for the most vulnerable, committed to continuing investigations, and to continue flood warden/road-closure training and property flood resilience support for affected communities. (p6)

### Environment Agency
* Issued Flood Warnings for the River Ryton at Worksop, Shelley Street and Shireoaks on 2 January 2024; instigated flood patrols and operated structures, cleared blockages before and after peak flows; coordinated community information officers and organised a multi-agency community drop-in to gather information on impacts. (p5)
* The Environment Agency led and will deliver a package of recovery and revenue works in 2024/25 and beyond in response to the flood event: enhanced maintenance, desilting of the River Ryton through the town centre and downstream at the Canch, conveyance/tree works, investigating operational control and required works on Canch Canal/sluices, exploring feeder channel improvements, potential floodwall at Priorswell Road and Natural Flood Management in the upper Ryton catchment; and gathering data to validate the flood warning service. (p6) [PAG]

### Bassetlaw District Council
* Bassetlaw District Council led multi-agency local communications and recovery coordination after the flooding, worked with partners on lobbying for funding changes and agreed joint recovery works with the Environment Agency to be delivered following the event. (p6) [PAG]

### Canch Canal
* Recovery works and investigations proposed for the Canch Canal feeder and channel (options to improve the channel bringing water back into the River Ryton downstream of the penstocks) following the flood event. (p6)

### Ryton Catchment
* The River Ryton (within the Ryton catchment) caused flooding requiring recovery works: desilting through the town centre and downstream Canch, conveyance and tree works, investigation of Canch Canal feeder and Canal & River Trust sluice gates, and exploration of Natural Flood Management measures in the upper Ryton catchment to reduce and slow flows. (p6)
