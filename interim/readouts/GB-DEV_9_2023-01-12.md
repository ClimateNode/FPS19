### Mid Devon
* Watercourses overtopped and drainage systems were overwhelmed in Mid Devon, which led to internal flooding of properties. Property flood resilience (PFR) measures prevented internal flooding in some cases; five properties are known to have prevented internal flooding using PFR, and two further properties are believed would have been prevented had large vehicles not overtopped PFR during a road closure. (p1) [PAG]
* Internal property flooding was reported in towns and villages within Mid Devon (table lists Bampton, Bickleigh, Cove, Exebridge, Oakfordbridge) as part of the January flood event; some properties in the area had PFR that prevented internal flooding. (p2) [PAG]
* Internal property flooding in towns within the district (reports of internal flooding to domestic properties across multiple villages). (p3) [TAB]
* Countywide heavy rainfall (notably affecting southern Exmoor within the district) on saturated ground during 11–13 January 2023 led to a significant response in the River Exe, with a peak on 12 January 2023 associated with the flood incident described in the report. (p5) [TAB]

* Areas within Mid Devon (notably rural Bampton) experienced river flooding resulting in internal flooding of properties and debris accumulation against bridges. (p16)
* Quant: 27 properties recorded internal flooding; 24 of these on the River Exe (23 residential and 4 commercial); 5 properties prevented internal flooding by PFR; 2 properties believed would have been prevented by PFR (p1) [PAG]

### Devon County Council
* Devon County Council delivered an individual property flood resilience (PFR) scheme and council staff witnessed PFR measures being overtopped by bow waves from vehicles driving through a closed road during the flood; the Council-funded or supported PFR measures prevented internal flooding in some properties during the event. (p1)
* Devon County Council funded an individual PFR scheme (and administered PFR funding) and council staff witnessed PFR being overtopped by bow waves from vehicles through a closed road; PFR measures funded either by DCC or homeowners prevented internal flooding in properties during the event. (p2)
* Devon County Council Highways Team closed the A396 at Bickleigh Bridge in response to the River Exe overtopping its banks. (p9) [PAG]
* Cleared debris that had washed up against bridges at the earliest feasible opportunity as part of the flood response; provided post-event photographic evidence (photo dated 17 Jan 2023) showing the large material washed against bridges. (p17)
* DCC Highways visited areas identified by residents in response to surface water flooding to check that drainage networks were functioning and to investigate potential exacerbating factors. (p19)
* Received reports of external flooding to residential properties across the county and will monitor and record incidents, consider them within its action plan and future investment programme as the Lead Local Flood Authority. (p20)
* Flooding of the River Exe during the 12 Jan 2023 - 14 Jan 2023 event caused flow issues and pressures on river structures (culverts, millstream banks and other river structures) along the Exe in Mid Devon. DCC has been asked to inspect these structures, ensure they are flowing freely and consider maintenance and improvements to reduce future blockages and flood impacts. (p22) [TAB]
* Quant: 5 properties prevented internal flooding using PFR; 2 properties believed would have been prevented from internal flooding had drivers adhered to road closure; 27 properties recorded internal flooding in the event (24 on the River Exe: 23 residential and 4 commercial). (p1)
* Quant: 5 properties prevented internal flooding; 2 properties overtopped (p2)

### Exe Catchment
* The Exe catchment was affected by prolonged heavy rainfall that caused watercourses to overtop and drainage to be overwhelmed, resulting in internal flooding of properties along the River Exe. Past flood risk reduction works in the catchment are reported to have been effective in protecting properties. (p1)
* An already-saturated Exe catchment received intense rainfall (11–12 Jan 2023) producing a significant river response in the River Exe with a peak on the afternoon of 12 January 2023; the hydrograph rising limb rose moderately steeply, contributing to the reported flood incident. (p5) [TAB]
* Quant: 24 properties on the River Exe recorded internal flooding (23 residential and 4 commercial) (p1)

### Environment Agency

(p11) [MUL]
