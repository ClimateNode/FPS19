### Wickham Market
* {01 Oct 2023 - 31 Oct 2023}: This investigation concerns flooding in Wickham Market associated with Storm Babet (October 2023). The report covers the town and its immediate surroundings. (p1) [IMG]
* Properties on High Street, Spring Lane, Ashe Road, and the Deben Mill and Rackham estates experienced front‑and‑rear flooding as flood water from the overtopped river and from surface water flow paths entered homes (including through front doors). Flooding persisted on the highway during the morning of 20 October, later surges inundated gardens and caused internal flooding. Flood flows damaged brickwork and mortar, caused walls to collapse, required livestock rescues and led to evacuations of some homeowners. Highway erosion and mud deposition created hazardous driving conditions. (p13)
* Surface water runoff from surrounding fields caused flooding across parts of the town; the eastern area was the least affected but low-lying streets (Mill Lane and Chapel Lane) acted as surface-water flow paths that concentrated runoff and exacerbated local flooding. (p15)
* Southern Wickham Market experienced surface water flooding from significant rainfall and runoff. Water ran off the highway into low spots and into properties; highway drainage capacity was exceeded and some gullies were not operating at the time, increasing flood severity. (p17)
* Significant rainfall produced high levels of surface water runoff across the western catchment that overwhelmed drainage systems, leading to inundation of properties in the village (notably Broad Road, Broadway and Orchard Place areas). (p18)
* Significant rainfall produced large volumes of surface water runoff from fields to the west that overwhelmed highway and local drainage, causing pooling at low points, garden inundation and internal flooding of properties across the village. Historic filled-in ponds likely reduced attenuation locally. Some reactive highway drainage works were carried out during the event. (p20)
* Significant rainfall produced large volumes of surface-water runoff from fields to the west which overwhelmed highway drainage, causing flood water to flow to lowest points and pool within the town (including the area around the primary school and low-lying highways). (p21) [IMG]
* {general}: Community and local organisations undertook flood-related actions across the village: landowners removed debris from the river and local watercourses to aid flow; the Parish Council reviewed and updated the flood emergency plan; SCC LLFA is investigating ownership of a piped watercourse section near the tennis courts; constructed water management features at the Wickham Gate development were confirmed built to specification; the primary school is investigating clearing playground drainage. (p31) [PAG]
* Quant: 60cm internal flooding; 100cm external flooding (wrack marks) (p13)
* Quant: more than 10 properties (known affected in the west of the village) (p18)
* Quant: external flooding reached 60 cm in gardens; internal flooding ranged 10–20 cm at various properties (p20)
