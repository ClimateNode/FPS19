## Place names with coordinates

### Lancashire
* An intense downpour on 16 June 2016 caused widespread surface water flooding across western Lancashire. Urban drainage and other drainage networks were overwhelmed, producing surface water inundation of roads and homes in multiple towns (notably Skelmersdale). (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: over 60 residential properties (Skelmersdale) (p1) [TAB]

### West Lancashire
* Heavy downpours on 16 June 2016 produced surface water flooding across West Lancashire (including Skelmersdale). Drainage systems were overwhelmed in the catchment, leading to flooding of roads, homes and businesses; RMAs carried out gully and outfall cleaning and investigations. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: over 60 residential properties (Skelmersdale, within West Lancashire) (p1) [TAB]
