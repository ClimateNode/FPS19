## Place names with coordinates

### Stockport Metropolitan District
* Exceptional rainfall on 13 September 2016 exceeded the capacity of the urban drainage network causing public combined sewers and private drains to flood and back up (including foul flooding). No significant infrastructure damage was recorded for this event. (p3)
* The borough received a very large proportion of its monthly rainfall in one day which overloaded local drainage systems causing public combined sewer and private drain flooding across large parts of the borough; roads became impassable causing local traffic disruption. Heatons and Reddish communities were worst affected; worst affected wards included Davenport and Cale Green, Stepping Hill and Heatons North. Some of the same properties had also been flooded in June 2016. (p5)
* The September 13, 2016 flood event affected the town: a large number of residential properties and some commercial properties, together with critical services and infrastructure, were flooded. (p6)
* A major flood on 13th September 2016 affected the Stockport area: a large number of residential properties and some commercial properties, plus critical services and infrastructure, were impacted by flooding. (p7) [TAB, PAG]
* The borough experienced a flooding event on 13th September 2016 that prompted a Section 19 investigation; hydrological conditions and datasets were reviewed to characterise the impacts across Stockport. (p8) [PAG]
* On 13th September 2016 Stockport experienced intense convective downpours that delivered a significant proportion of the monthly rainfall over a short period, causing surface water/flash flooding and rapid responses in local watercourses (as identified from radar, rain gauge and river gauge data reviewed in the investigation). (p9) [PAG]
* Intense, localized torrential rainfall on 13 September 2016 (two downpours at ~15:15–15:45 and ~17:00–18:15) produced very high rainfall intensities centred over Stockport. Radar imagery shows the heaviest precipitation over Stockport, which generated rapid overland flow and surface-water/urban flooding across parts of the town during the event. (p11) [PAG, IMG]
* Two torrential downpours on 13 September 2016 affected the borough (western half hit by both downpours; eastern half by the second), overwhelming the urban drainage system in parts of the borough and causing surface water (pluvial) and public/private sewer flooding. (p12) [PAG]
* Short-duration, high-intensity rainfall overwhelmed the urban drainage/sewer system across the borough on 13th September 2016, resulting in widespread pluvial and sewer-related flooding. (p13)
* Widespread property flooding across the borough on 13 September 2016; urban drainage systems were overwhelmed by short-duration, high-intensity rainfall resulting in surface water and sewer-related flooding. (p14) [PAG, IMG]
* Widespread flooding across the borough on 13 September 2016: properties across all areas of Stockport were affected. Borough-wide impacts included many residential internal floods, a high proportion of basement-only internal flooding, and spatial clustering of higher impacts in the more urbanised western half of the borough. (p15) [PAG, IMG]
* Central Stockport experienced both internal and external flooding during the September 2016 event, affecting predominantly residential properties with some commercial premises also impacted. (p16) [IMG]

### Metrolink
* Sections of the Metrolink network experienced track flooding during the event: a points issue on the Rochdale line and flooded tracks on the Manchester Airport and East Didsbury lines, causing service disruption until flooding subsided later the same evening. (p19)

### Rochdale Metrolink Line
* Flooding on the Rochdale Metrolink line caused a points issue that affected services during the rainfall event. (p19)

### Sports Field
* Properties in the Sports Field area were flooded during the 13 September 2016 event. Flooding in this warning area was caused by a combination of sources, mainly sewer flooding, rather than Chorlton Brook. (p22) [TAB]

### Network Rail
* Closed and cleaned Gatley Station in response to flooding associated with the 13 September 2016 event. (p23) [TAB]

### Micker Brook Tributary
* Contributed to fluvial (river) flooding affecting the Cheadle and Gatley area; report recommends considering options to reduce fluvial flood risk and reviewing culverts. (p27) [TAB]
