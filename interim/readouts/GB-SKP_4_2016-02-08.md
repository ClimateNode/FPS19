

# Cheadle Hulme flooding 08 Feb 2016

Reference document:<br>Flood Site Information Sheet: Dorset Avenue, Cheadle Hulme<br>121\_Stockport/121\_2016\_Stockport/Flooding\_Investigation\_Report\_March\_2016\_-\_Dorset\_Avenue\_-\_Cheadle\_Hulme.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Cheadle Hulme
* Residential area experienced flooding in the Dorset Avenue locality — gardens of properties around Nos. 94 to 104 were flooded due to a blocked trash screen on the culverted watercourse. (p1)
* The town experienced a flooding incident on 8 February 2016 centred on Dorset Avenue, impacting residential properties and nearby watercourses. (p3)

### Dorset Avenue, Adswood
* Gardens of houses on Dorset Avenue became flooded; flooding was linked to a blocked trash screen at the inlet to the culvert watercourse (private land to the rear of No. 86) that culverts through the estate. (p1)
* A flooding incident occurred at Dorset Avenue on 8 February 2016 affecting the residential area and local watercourse, inundating gardens and properties in the street. (p3) [IMG]
* {19 Feb 2016}: Flow was restored to the watercourse at the location of a screen behind 86 Dorset Avenue (map annotation and Photo 2 caption indicate flow restoration on the watercourse adjacent to Dorset Avenue). The map shows access route to the screen (alleyway, behind metal fencing). (p4) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Stockport Metropolitan Borough Council
* {16 Feb 2016, 19 Feb 2016}: As Lead Local Flood Authority (LLFA) Stockport Council undertook emergency works to clear a blocked trash screen that was impeding a watercourse. The LLFA used enforcement powers to clear the trash screen (action begun 16 February 2016) and, with a framework contractor using specialist equipment to draw down flood waters, cleared the screen and restored flow on 19 February 2016 to reduce the risk of further flooding. The council also established the riparian owner's details by land registry search and informed the owner of their duties under the Land Drainage Act 1991. (p2) [MUL]

### Stockport Homes
* Was the recorded maintainer of the trash screen at the inlet to the culvert behind No. 86 Dorset Avenue; were contacted/reported to clear blockages linked to repeated flooding and asked to address the unresolved blockage (inspected and reported to them on 12 February); on 15 February they confirmed they were not the riparian owner and did not carry out remedial works. (p1) [PAG]
