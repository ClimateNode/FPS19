

# Kirkby-In-Ashfield flooding 18 Oct 2023 - 20 Oct 2023

## Place names with coordinates

### Kirkby-In-Ashfield
* Sustained heavy rainfall during Storm Babet caused surface water flooding in Kirkby in Ashfield, flooding roads and properties as the ground was already saturated. (p1)
* {18 Oct 2023 - 20 Oct 2023}: Localised internal flooding occurred across four areas of Kirkby in Ashfield. Specific affected roads/locations identified in the report include Cherry Avenue (1 property), Sutton Road (2 properties), Kirkby Mill View (identified as an affected area) and Lindley's Lane (1 property). Highway drainage assets were overwhelmed during Storm Babet, producing highway flooding that led to property internal flooding via runoff and vehicle bow waves. (p2) [PAG]
* {19 Oct 2023}: Ashfield District Council officers inspected watercourses and drainage assets in Kirkby on 19 October 2023 as part of the flood response, clearing grids and checking flows to manage flooding risk. (p6)
* {19 Oct 2023}: ADC carried out inspections of watercourses and drainage assets in Kirkby on 19 October 2023 to ensure grids were clear and water was flowing as part of the flood response; LRF local flood plan principles were applied to the Kirkby response. (p7) [PAG]
* {18 Oct 2023 - 20 Oct 2023}: Quant: 4 areas; 1 property (Cherry Avenue); 2 properties (Sutton Road); 1 property (Lindley's Lane) (p2) [PAG]

### Sutton-In-Ashfield

(p2) [PAG]

### Ashfield
* {18 Oct 2023 - 20 Oct 2023}: Within Ashfield district (Sutton in Ashfield / Kirkby in Ashfield) there were localised incidents of internal property flooding in four identified areas due to surface water/highway flooding when drainage assets were overwhelmed during the storm. (p2) [PAG]
* {18 Oct 2023 - 20 Oct 2023}: The report describes Storm Babet causing highway drainage assets in Ashfield to be unable to cope, producing highway flooding across Sutton in Ashfield and flooding properties due to runoff and vehicle bow waves. (p3) [IMG]
* {19 Oct 2023}: ADC (District of Ashfield) delivered emergency response across the district: inspected watercourses/drainage assets, stood up an internal Incident Management Team, prioritised attendance to affected properties, deployed sandbags to prevent internal flooding, opened the Rest Centre at Hucknall Leisure Centre and provided flood advice communications. (p7)
* {18 Oct 2023 - 20 Oct 2023}: Quant: 4 areas; 1 property (Cherry Avenue); 2 properties (Sutton Road); 1 property (Lindley's Lane) (p2) [PAG]

## Topics without coordinates

### Ashfield District Council
* {19 Oct 2023}: Responded to the flooding by inspecting watercourses and drainage assets for which it is responsible, stood up an internal Incident Management Team and referenced local flood plans to manage the situation. (p6)
* {18 Oct 2023 - 20 Oct 2023}: Provided emergency flood response: prioritised attendance to affected properties, deployed sandbags to prevent internal flooding of domestic premises, opened a rest centre for evacuees from severely flooded domestic properties in Hucknall, carried out inspections of watercourses and drainage assets in Sutton, Kirkby and Jacksdale ahead of/during the event, and issued flood advice communications. (p7)

Council officers verified internal flooding on-site, collated and published flood impact data, visited affected residents and businesses, led coordination groups and provided 24-hour emergency planning support. (p6)
