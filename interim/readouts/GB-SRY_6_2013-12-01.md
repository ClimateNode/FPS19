## Topics without coordinates

### Guildford Borough Council
* Internal property flooding at multiple addresses and road closures within Guildford during the winter 2013/14 flooding. (p2) [TAB]
* Fluvial, surface water and groundwater flooding across Guildford Borough during the winter 2013/14 event, causing internal property flooding across the borough. (p7)
* Quant: approximately 160 incidents of internal property flooding (p7)

### Environment Agency
* Lead RMA for fluvial flooding from Main Rivers during the winter 2013/14 event; operated Flood Alert and Flood Warning services, deployed flood ambassadors and data recorders, erected temporary barriers on the River Wey in Guildford to prevent further fluvial flooding, and supported recovery engagement (National Flood Forum). (p7)
* Quant: three visits by the flood recovery trailer; approximately 160 incidents of internal property flooding across the borough (p7)

### Thames Water
* Thames Water focused on maintaining customer services and protecting assets during the event; since the flooding in Avondale estate TW has been surveying the sewer system. (p7)

### Surrey County Council
* Implemented road closures across Guildford Borough during the winter 2013/14 flooding; established a Task Group to review lessons from the flooding; progressing mitigation works as part of the Wetspots Capital Programme (reprofiling, localised repairs and a new ditch system on Ockham Road North carriageway; construction of a new carrier system on Hogs Back Road in Puttenham). (p7)
