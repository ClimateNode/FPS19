

# Ilminster flooding 23 Jul 2017

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Ilminster 23rd July 2017<br>113\_Somerset County/Flood Investigation Report Ilminster.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Ditton Street, Ilminster
* {general}: Past flood events at Ditton Street have been linked to the capacity of the Shudrick Stream culvert and its effect on local drainage, causing flooding in the street and local drainage issues. (p10) [PAG, IMG]
* On 2017-07-23 Ditton Street (part of the Ditton Street / Wharf Lane / Silver Street grouping) was flooded during an exceptional rainfall event; highway and properties were affected as part of an event that flooded approximately 10 properties across the area. Reported depths: 300 mm and 200 mm (surface water/drainage-related). (p11) [PAG, TAB, MUL]
* Intense surface water run-off described as 'like a river' flowed into Ditton Street and subsequently flooded properties. Water overflowed from a manhole outside 14b Ditton Street. Significant pooling and flooding occurred outside the Tesco filling station on Ditton Street between the Shudrick Lane and Orchard Vale junctions. Silt-laden run-off from Listers Hill compounded the flooding, and some properties on Ditton Street were threatened from the rear by flows through the District Council's Shudrick Lane car park originating on the northern slopes of Pretwood Hill. (p12) [IMG, MUL]
* Ditton Street outside the Tesco filling station was flooded on 2017-07-23 with water covering the road surface; emergency responders (firefighters) were present and traffic/ pedestrian movement was impeded. (p14)
* Exceedance flows reached the Ditton Street area during the 2017-07-23 intense rainfall event. Blocked or partially blocked highway gullies and local drainage systems allowed surface water to reach and inundate this area. (p18)
* Ditton Street was specifically identified for review of gully cleansing frequency because gullies were reported blocked/partially blocked or surging during the 23 July 2017 event, indicating localised road/surface-water flooding and elevated flood risk to the street. (p20) [TAB]
* Identified as affected by the 23 July 2017 Ilminster flood and targeted for reactive highway gully cleansing. SCC Highways raised a works order; initial cleansing works commenced on 2017-09-11 and initial works were completed, with further reactive maintenance planned within the 2017/18 financial year. (p21)

### Ilminster
* On 2017-07-23 intense rainfall produced surface water run-off into the town centre causing internal flooding to approximately 10 properties (maximum internal depth ~200 mm). Internal flooding lasted about 2–3 hours for properties. Strategic infrastructure (the highway in the town centre) was also affected by surface water flooding, with highway flooding present for up to about 1 hour. (p4) [PAG]
* A flood event at Ilminster on 2017-07-23 was investigated by Somerset County Council. The town centre (which sits in a hollow) experienced surface water/urban flooding during the event. Past and local impacts include flooding at Ditton Street attributed to the capacity of the culvert on the Shudrick Stream (culverted between the Tesco carpark on Shudrick Lane and Lamplighters on Wharf Lane), which affected local drainage and contributed to flooding in the town centre. (p10) [PAG, IMG]
* On 2017-07-23 Ilminster experienced intense rainfall (25 mm in under one hour) causing surface water flooding across the town; highways and multiple streets were inundated and properties were flooded in the town during the event. (p11) [PAG]
* On 2017-07-23 intense rainfall (about 25 mm in <1 hour) produced fast surface water run-off through the town. Runoff flowed down Old Road across the High Street into North Street, across into Silver Street and into Ditton Street where flows were described as 'intense' and 'like a river' and flooded properties; water overflowed from a manhole outside 14b Ditton Street. Water pooled at the southern end of North Street (junction with Silver Street, East Street and Ditton Street) and extensively outside the Tesco filling station on Ditton Street between Shudrick Lane and Orchard Vale, where additional silt-laden runoff from Listers Hill worsened flooding. Runoff from the northern slopes of Pretwood Hill passed through Shudrick Lane car park and threatened properties on Ditton Street from the rear. Surface water also flowed down New Road across the High Street into West Street and down Court Barton into Wharf Lane, where a number of properties were directly affected. Numerous community reports indicated highway drainage was restricted and unable to accept flows. Reports to Wessex Water referenced sewer-system impacts but none were classified as reportable sewer flooding incidents. (p13) [PAG, IMG]
* A pluvial (surface water) flood occurred on 2017-07-23 after a particularly intense 30-minute downpour. The extreme rainfall saturated and/or overwhelmed the urban drainage system and surrounding Beacon Hill catchment, producing localised surface-water/urban flooding across Ilminster. (p17) [PAG, IMG]
* The town experienced pluvial (surface water) flooding on 2017-07-23 caused by an intense short-duration rainfall event on an already-saturated, largely impermeable catchment. Surface water exceeded drainage capacity and inundated low-lying areas of the town. (p18) [PAG]
* Multiple properties experienced internal flooding during the 2017-07-23 event. Residents identified roads with blocked, partially blocked or surging gullies contributing to local flooding; actions recommended include gully cleansing, review of gully cleansing frequency (notably Ditton Street and surrounding roads), and Property Level Resilience/Protection (PLR) inspections and potential PLR works for properties that flooded. The report also recommends reviewing and supporting local community flood response and the local flood plan. (p20) [TAB]
* The town experienced surface/urban flooding that led to blocked gullies and silt deposition. This triggered a reactive gully‑cleansing programme targeting North Street, Market Square, East Street, Silver Street, Ditton Street and Shudrick Lane (programme commenced 2017-09-11; initial works complete), a recommendation to CCTV the culvert from Shudrick Lane to Wharf Lane to check for obstructions or damage caused by increased flow, and clearance of a silt‑trap on Old Road adjacent to The Bell Inn (twenty‑four bags of sediment removed). (p21)

### Shudrick Lane, Ilminster
* Highway flooding attributed to drainage issues. (p11) [TAB, MUL]
* Surface water run-off from the northern slopes of Pretwood Hill flowed through the District Council's Shudrick Lane car park during the event, carrying flow towards and threatening some properties on Ditton Street from the rear; the car park was a conveyance route for the flood flow. (p12)
* Blocked highway gullies and local surface-water/urban flooding following the intense rainfall on 2017-07-23. SCC Highways programmed reactive gully cleansing for Shudrick Lane (works commenced 2017-09-11; initial works complete, further reactive maintenance to follow). The culvert running from Shudrick Lane to Wharf Lane was identified for CCTV survey to check for obstructions or damage introduced by increased flow during the event. (p21)

### High Street, Ilminster

### Old Road, Ilminster
* Surface water run-off was reported to have flowed at speed down Old Road and across the High Street, contributing to downstream flooding into North Street, Silver Street and Ditton Street. (p12)
* Old Road formed part of the natural route for surface water run-off during the intense rainfall on 2017-07-23, experiencing overland flows as drainage was exceeded and conveying water toward lower streets. (p18)
* Silt-trap adjacent to The Bell Inn accumulated sediment (presumably deposited during the flooding). SCC Rights of Way cleared twenty-four bags of sediment as a one-off action; Highways temporarily made the silt-trap safe. Ownership remains to be established so permanent repairs/maintenance are outstanding. These actions followed the 2017-07-23 event. (p21)

### Beacon Hill
* Beacon Hill's saturated soils in the upper catchment contributed to rapid surface water run-off during the intense rainfall period, driving flows into the Ilminster catchment. (p4) [MUL]

### Tesco
* Supermarket frontage/adjacent street area affected by floodwater; emergency responders (firefighters and a fire truck) were present at the flooded location, indicating disruption to store access/operations and an active flood response. (p1) [IMG]
* Surface water pooled to a much greater extent outside the Tesco filling station on Ditton Street (between the Shudrick Lane and Orchard Vale junctions), flooding/ponding the forecourt and immediate frontage. Flooding was compounded by silt-laden run-off from Listers Hill. (p12)
* The Tesco filling station forecourt/adjacent road was bordered by floodwater on 2017-07-23; water covered the road in front of the pumps, responders were present, and site access was disrupted. (p14) [IMG]

### Culvert

### SCC Highways
* During the 23 July 2017 event residents reported blocked, partially blocked or surging gullies on highways. SCC Highways were identified to carry out gully cleansing and to coordinate access to gullies as part of the immediate reactive response to road flooding. (p20) [TAB]

### Environment Agency
* The Environment Agency was engaged in post-event actions for the 23 July 2017 flooding: the LLFA planned to discuss joint working and scheme opportunities with the EA regarding properties that experienced internal flooding, and to share CCTV survey findings of the culvert from Shudrick Lane to Wharf Lane to check for obstructions or damage resulting from the event. (p20) [PAG, TAB]

### SCC
* Provided immediate post-flood response: SCC Highways raised a works order for a reactive gully‑cleansing programme for North Street, Market Square, East Street, Silver Street, Ditton Street and Shudrick Lane (works programme commenced 2017-09-11; initial works complete with further reactive maintenance to follow). SCC Rights of Way cleared twenty‑four bags of sediment from the silt‑trap on Old Road and Highways temporarily made the silt‑trap safe. SCC Highways to review gully cleansing frequency for Ilminster.
