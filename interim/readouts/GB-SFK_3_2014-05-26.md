

# Kesgrave flooding 26 May 2014 - 27 May 2014

## Place names with coordinates

### Kesgrave
* Internal flooding of residential properties in the town (area: Terry Gardens) during the flood event. (p2)
* Internal flooding of domestic properties (main living areas, garages and gardens). Public highway completely flooded for an approx. 50 m length causing the road to be closed. Anglian Water surface water system was surcharged; attenuation basin and outlet to Mill River were implicated in the incident. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 5 properties (p2)
* Quant: 5 domestic properties; approx. 50 m public highway; approx. 450 mm flood depth (p3) [IMG]

### Terry Gardens, Kesgrave
* Subject of a Section 19 flood investigation carried out by Suffolk County Council (Lead Local Flood Authority) following a flood event. (p1)
* Internal flooding of residential properties in Terry Gardens during the flood event. (p2)
* Quant: 5 properties (p2)

### Mill River
* The attenuation basin outlet pipe to the Mill River was reported blocked; this blockage impaired discharge to the river and was cited as a cause contributing to the flooding of properties and the highway. (p3) [PAG]
* The Mill River was the discharge receptor for the attenuation basin outlet; blockage of the outlet prevented discharge to the Mill River, contributing to surface water flooding of nearby domestic properties and a road closure. (p4) [TAB, PAG]
* Quant: Five domestic properties (p3) [PAG]


## Topics without coordinates

### SCC Highway
* Public highway drainage appears to have been exceeded/failed resulting in complete flooding of an approximate 50m length of road to an estimated depth of 450mm, and the road was closed as a result. (p3)
* Quant: Approx. 50m length of highway; approx. 450mm depth (p3)

### Anglian Water
* Anglian Water's surface water drainage system and attenuation basin were surcharged/impacted; lack of maintenance and a blockage in the attenuation basin outlet pipe to the Mill River contributed to the inability to discharge stormwater, which in turn contributed to internal flooding of domestic properties and flooding of the highway. (p3) [PAG]
* Anglian Water's surface-water drainage assets (attenuation basin and its outlet pipe to the Mill River) were implicated in the event: lack of maintenance and a blockage in the attenuation-basin outlet contributed to surface-water flooding that flooded domestic properties and led to road closure in Kesgrave. Anglian Water completed actions (calculated basin volume, cleared basin of waste, CCTV inspection of outlet). (p4) [TAB, PAG]
* Quant: Five domestic properties (p3) [PAG]

### Attenuation Basin
* An Anglian Water attenuation basin had lack of maintenance and contained waste material, reducing its capacity and contributing to surface water flooding of nearby domestic properties and closure of a road. The basin was subsequently cleared of waste material and its volume calculated (actions recorded as completed). (p4) [TAB]

### Suffolk County Council
* Suffolk County Council's highway drainage was identified as possibly exceeding design capacity during the event, contributing to surface-water flooding that flooded domestic properties and caused a road closure; SCC cleared road gullies as a completed action. (p4) [TAB]
