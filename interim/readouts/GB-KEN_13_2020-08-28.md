### Albert Road, Deal
* Internal flooding to 4 properties (reports of up to 3 cm of floodwater inside); external flooding to 21 properties affecting gardens and garages (reported depths up to 50 cm). (p2) [TAB]
* {1973, 1979, 1988, 09 Sep 1989, 13 Sep 1989, 2005, 2006, 01 May 2014 - 31 May 2014}: Properties internally flooded on multiple historic occasions (internal property flooding recorded). (p5) [TAB, MUL]
* {2007, 2008, 2009, 2010, 2013, 21 May 2014, 01 Sep 2015 - 30 Sep 2015, 2016, 2020}: Multiple properties internally flooded on multiple occasions (including 2020); pumping station failure recorded in September 2015. (p6) [TAB, MUL]
* Intense afternoon rainfall caused the sewer network to surcharge; manholes along Albert Road surcharged and sewage and surface water ponded and accumulated. The road flooded from the junction with Middle Deal Road to the railway. Internal flooding was reported by properties; some properties experienced external flooding where sandbags or flood boards prevented internal ingress. Southern Water did not initiate their emergency response and the road was not initially closed; vehicles drove through the floodwater creating bow waves that threatened internal flooding. KCC highways and the police later implemented a road closure and KCC provided a tanker to clear flood water. (p8) [PAG]
* Manholes along the sewer network surcharged causing sewage and surface water to pond and accumulate. Road flooding occurred from the junction of Middle Deal Road to the railway; vehicles driving through the floodwater created bow waves that nearly caused internal flooding to properties. KCC Highways and police closed the road (approx. 16:45) and KCC provided a tanker to clear floodwater. Some properties avoided internal flooding using sandbags or flood boards. (p9) [PAG]
* Foul (sewer) drains were surcharged/unblocked and water levels were reduced by KFRS; debris cleared to prevent further blockage. Post-event highway works included cleansing of 10 road gullies. Southern Water later reinstated emergency response measures for Albert Road. (p10) [TAB, PAG]
