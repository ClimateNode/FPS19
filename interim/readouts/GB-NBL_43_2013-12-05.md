

# Tweedmouth flooding 05 Dec 2013

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Tweedmouth<br>091\_Northumberland/Tweedmouth\_Flood\_Investigation\_Report\_05Dec13.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Northumberland
* Coastal locations in the county experienced a tidal surge coinciding with a spring tide on 5 December 2013 (sea levels on the east coast reached their highest in 60 years), leading to localised coastal/tidal flooding and county flood alerts. (p2)

### Tweedmouth
* Tweedmouth experienced a tidal surge that moved inland from the River Tweed across the Royal Tweed Bridge, inundating adjacent roads and properties; the area near the riverbank/bridge was the most affected. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* Area near the Royal Tweed Bridge in Tweedmouth was affected by a tidal surge on 5 December 2013; roads (including Blakewell Street and West End) were inundated, outfalls without tidal flaps allowed rising water into drainage causing surcharging manholes, and one property suffered internal flood damage. Floodwaters retreated within an hour as the tide ebbed. (p2)
* Quant: 1 residential property; 2 roads (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: one property (p2)

### West End, Tweedmouth
* Road was highlighted on the event map as being affected by the Tweedmouth tidal surge; water flowed inland from the river across the bridge, inundating roads and adjacent properties near the riverbank. (p1) [IMG]
* Roads along West End were affected by flood waters during the tidal surge. (p2)

### Blakewell Road, Berwick-Upon-Tweed
* Water flooded Blakewell Street from the boat slipway to the south as the tidal surge peaked on 5 December 2013; outfalls without tidal flaps allowed rising tidal water into the drainage system causing surcharging manholes and roads to be affected. One property is known to have suffered internal flood damage. Floodwaters retreated within an hour as the tide ebbed. (p2)
* Quant: one property (p2)

### Royal Tweed Bridge
* The area near the Royal Tweed Bridge was the location of the tidal surge on 5 December 2013; nearby Blakewell Street was flooded from the boat slipway and surrounding roads were inundated, with surcharging manholes reported. (p2)


## Topics without coordinates

### East Coast Northumberland
* Sea levels along the east coast reached their highest in 60 years during the tidal surge on 5 December 2013, driving the coastal/tidal flooding that affected localities such as Tweedmouth. (p2)

### River Tweed
* Numerous outfalls into the River Tweed lacked tidal flaps, which allowed the rising tidal water to enter the drainage systems and contributed to surcharging manholes and local flooding during the tidal surge on 5 December 2013. (p2)
