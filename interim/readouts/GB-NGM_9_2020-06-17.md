## Topics without coordinates

### Nottingham City Council
* As the Lead Local Flood Authority and Highway Authority, the council recorded highway and property flooding in Wollaton when an intense storm on 17 June 2020 overwhelmed local drainage. Overland flows and surcharging of the sewer system caused highway flooding and internal property flooding; the City Council completed a formal Section 19 investigation and recommended flood risk modelling to inform future schemes. (p4)
* Quant: more than 5 properties (p4)

### Severn Trent
* The surface water sewer that runs across the impacted roads was exceeded by the extreme rainfall event; Severn Trent Water surveyed the sewer and confirmed no defects but that the event exceeded the network's capacity, leading to surcharging flows (manholes lifting) which contributed to surface water flooding of properties. (p10)
* Quant: 1 in 30-year (typical sewer design capacity exceeded); 1 in 90-year (maximum rainfall return period estimated at Kingswood Road) (p10)

### Highway Services
* During the storm road gullies were unable to discharge when the surface water sewer was at capacity, contributing to surface water remaining on the highway. In response, Highway Services will add affected gullies to a 'Hotspot List' for increased cleansing and maintenance following the event. (p10)
