### Bethersden
* Intense rainfall on 25 July 2021 caused local river levels (Bethersden Stream and an unnamed tributary of the River Beult) to rise quickly. The highway surface water drainage network surcharged and culverts under Church Hill and Norton Lane surcharged. The sewer network was reported unable to cope in isolated areas, causing sewer flooding. Properties throughout the village were flooded (internal and external). (p1)
* Internal flooding of a residential property with floodwater entering the building. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Bethersden was the location of the rain gauge recording the extreme rainfall on 25 July 2021; flooding in the village began around 15:00 on 25 July 2021 when intense rainfall led to surface water runoff and tributary flows that flooded properties. (p4) [PAG]
* Intense convective rainfall generated flooding in Bethersden, with reports indicating flooding started at approximately 15:00 on 25 July 2021. The Bethersden rain gauge recorded 101.75 mm between 11:15 and 21:45 on 25 July, with 58.77 mm between 14:00 and 15:00, producing rapid surface-water/flash flooding in the locality. (p5)
* {01 Nov 1960 - 30 Nov 1960, 01 Jan 1988 - 31 Jan 1988, 01 Dec 1993 - 31 Dec 1993, 01 Jan 2011 - 31 Jan 2011, 01 Nov 2012 - 30 Nov 2012, 01 Dec 2012 - 31 Dec 2012}: The village flooded on multiple historic occasions where flooding was associated with the Bethersden Stream. (p7) [MUL]
* On 25th July 2021 intense rainfall overwhelmed drainage and watercourses; watercourses overtopped and surface water runoff caused property flooding across the village. (p8)
* Intense rainfall on 25 July 2021 overwhelmed drainage and local ditches which overtopped, causing flooding of properties along Ashford Road and adjacent areas (Bailey Fields). Debris was reported near drainage pipes south of Bailey Fields which contributed to reduced discharge capacity. KFRS attended Ashford Road on 25 July 2021 and pumped out floodwater from multiple properties. (p9) [PAG, IMG]

### Bethersden Stream
* Local river levels rose quickly within the Bethersden Stream, causing the highway surface water drainage network to be unable to discharge into the watercourse and to surcharge; this contributed to culverts under Church Hill and Norton Lane surcharging and to property flooding in Bethersden. (p1)
* The stream is the main watercourse running through the village and is associated with the Environment Agency flood survey of the 25 July 2021 event. Properties adjacent to the stream (gardens at Forge Field and Chester Avenue) are described as being alongside the channel; flood survey records for the village show 1 property flooded internally (approx. 90 cm depth reported) and 2 properties flooded externally during the event, so the Bethersden Stream is a plausible source/contributor to those impacts (attribution to the stream is not explicitly stated in the text). (p3) [IMG]
* A small area along Bethersden Stream falls within high-risk Flood Zone 3 and the stream and its tributaries contributed to fluvial and surface-water flooding that corresponded with the properties affected during the 25 July 2021 event. (p4)
