## Place names with coordinates

### Hargrave
* Heavy rainfall on a snow‑covered, saturated catchment overwhelmed surface water drainage, causing excess surface water to flow overland to low points around private property and resulting in internal flooding of a dwelling. The flood incident occurred at the B645 east of Hargrave. (p7)
* Internal flooding of a property adjacent to the B645 east of Hargrave; the affected property has experienced internal flooding on more than one occasion in the last five years, prompting a formal flood investigation. (p9) [PAG]
* A property in Hargrave was affected by surface water flooding on 27 December 2017, prompting recommendations for regular highway drainage cleansing and capacity assessments to reduce runoff and future flood risk. (p16)
* Heavy sleety snow and rainfall on a saturated catchment caused drainage systems to be unable to collect and convey rainwater. Excess surface water flowed over ground following local topography toward low points around private properties, resulting in flooding at the B645 east of Hargrave and impacts to at least one affected private property in a high-risk surface water area. ## Topics without coordinates

### B645
* Roadside ditches either side of the B645 held standing water upstream of culverts; culverts on the north side of the road (two locations) were wholly or partially blocked, impeding drainage and contributing to surface water accumulation adjacent to the carriageway. (p10)
* Flood water overtopped a culvert on the north side of the B645 and flowed over ground to an adjacent dwelling. The front of the house and driveway were flooded; floodwater entered the ground floor via the front door, affecting the hallway, kitchen, dining room and living room. Internal water depth was approximately 200 mm. Standing water remained around the dwelling and in upstream ditches and fields nine days after the event. A sump pump and sweeping vehicle were used to remove water into downstream ditches. (p12)
* {27 Dec 2017, 06 Jan 2018}: Overland flow converged on the B645 east of Hargrave causing standing water and localised flooding on the carriageway. A culvert at incident point 01 was observed blocked/damaged, exacerbating flow routing; a separate water‑main leak (point 02) is noted on the plan and linked to the incident area. (p29) [IMG, MUL]
* Quant: 200 mm water depth inside the house; standing water present 9 days after the event (p12)
* {27 Dec 2017, 06 Jan 2018}: Quant: 1 culvert blocked/damaged (point 01); 1 water main leak (point 02, reported 06/01/2018) (p29) [IMG, MUL]

### Anglian Water
* A burst water main on the north verge of the B645 (approximately 600 metres southwest of the affected property) was leaking and contributing to standing water in the ditches and fields near the property, increasing catchment saturation and potentially exacerbating the flooding. Anglian Water attended the leak. (p12)
* The report directs Anglian Water Services to assess sources of water entering the public sewerage system and to assess asset capacity following the flooding incident, indicating AWS's sewer assets were reviewed in relation to the property flooding and potential contribution to flood risk. (p16)
* Quant: approximately 600 metres; leak reported 02/12/2017 and repaired 04/12/2017; reported again 05/01/2018 (p12)

### East Northamptonshire Council
* Identified in the report as the lead local authority responsible for response to the flooding incident affecting the property in Northamptonshire; expected to coordinate response, planning consultation and enforcement related to ordinary watercourses after the flood. (p16)

* The culvert at the B645 appeared to be blocked/damaged (marked as incident point 01) and was identified as a main cause of the flood incident by impeding overland flow routes; overland flows converged at this location producing standing water and contributing to the flooding observed on 27th December 2017.
