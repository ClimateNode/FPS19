## Place names with coordinates

### Windsor Close, Towcester
* Surface water ponded on the carriageway because drainage systems were blocked and gullies/levels were inefficient; ponded water spilled over into low points around private property causing internal flooding of a property. (p6)
* Excess surface water ponded in the carriageway due to blocked and inefficient drainage; ponding spilled over into low points around private property causing internal flooding of at least one property. Drainage blockages were reported and subsequently cleared. (p7)
* Internal flooding of a residential property (water crossed the building threshold). The property experienced internal flooding on 2 November 2014 and has been flooded on more than one occasion in the previous five years, prompting a formal flood investigation. (p9) [PAG]
* Surface water flooding occurred at the north/end of the cul‑de‑sac of Windsor Close. A property at this location was reported flooded. Ponding at the kerb line was noted following a previous resurfacing that reduced road camber; silt deposits were observed. Two road gullies drain this section (east gully collects water; west gully is at a higher level and does not). A piped drainage system within the curtilage of a private property at the end of the cul‑de‑sac conveys runoff north beneath properties and may connect to the surface water public sewer in Buckingham Way (connection not verified). Runoff from private driveways, shared parking and the wider catchment (including areas of Buckingham Way) can flow to the low point at the end of Windsor Close. (p10) [PAG]
* Surface water from the carriageway flowed north across the carriageway channel, ponded at the north kerb line at the end of the cul-de-sac, overtopped the back of the footway and spilled onto private property, flowing into a garage and around the front of a house. (p11) [TAB, PAG, MUL]
* Flooding occurred at Windsor Close causing a property to be flooded; drainage systems serving the area were not operating effectively during the incident due to blockage and poor location; the property sits at a low level in the natural flow path and is vulnerable to high volumes of surface water runoff. (p16)
* {general}: Low-lying properties in the Windsor Close area lie in the natural flow path for surface water (low relative level to Windsor Road) and are vulnerable to high volumes of surface water runoff; drainage at the end of Windsor Close (on private property) is noted for ownership/maintenance action and property-level protection and community measures are recommended. * A flood incident occurred at the low point of Windsor Close, influenced by runoff from the wider catchment and drainage located on private property at the end of the street. Impacts and risks led to recommendations to establish ownership/maintenance of private drainage, consider additional drainage and flood gates, and undertake kerbing and carriageway/gully re-levelling at the low point. ## Topics without coordinates

### Northamptonshire County Council
* Surface water runoff and insufficient highway/private drainage capacity in the NCC area (Towcester) contributed to flood risk, including the flood incident at the low point of Windsor Close. NCC is assigned responsibility for highway drainage cleansing, asset mapping, capacity assessment and consideration of improvement works to reduce flood risk to properties. (p18) [PAG]

### Anglian Water
* {02 Nov 2014, 09 Mar 2016}: Public sewer receiving water that may contribute to flooding; potential insufficient capacity of sewer assets that could lead to flood risk to properties adjacent to the incident. Anglian Water instructed to assess sources entering the public sewer, assess asset capacity and identify areas of insufficient capacity, and to develop a detailed plan of their assets to share with the LLFA and the Community. (p19)

### Highway Authority
* Flooding at a low point on Windsor Close adjacent to the flood incident affecting the highway; works considered include improved kerbing and carriageway/gully re-levelling at that low point. Highway Authority is to work with Anglian Water Services and property owners to ascertain ownership and responsibilities for drainage systems adjacent to the incident. (p19)

### South Northamptonshire Council
* Council actions relate to managing flood risk from watercourses and development: continuing consultation with the Environment Agency on planning to reduce flood risk and undertaking enforcement under Section 25 of the Land Drainage Act 1991 where riparian owners fail to maintain watercourses. Council will also assist other flood management authorities in preparing a detailed plan of drainage/flood-risk assets to share with the LLFA and the Community. (p19)
