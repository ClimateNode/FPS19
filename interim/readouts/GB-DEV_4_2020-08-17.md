### Devon
* Intense, very localised rainfall overwhelmed drainage systems across Devon, causing widespread surface water runoff and high numbers of internal property floodings. (p1)
* Widespread surface water flooding across the county on 17 August 2020 causing internal flooding to properties and disruption to transport networks; totals reported for the event were 175 properties flooded across Devon, concentrated in Barnstaple. (p2) [IMG]
* {03 Jan 2014, 19 Jan 2016, 03 Sep 2016, 05 Sep 2016, 19 Nov 2016, 20 Nov 2016}: Repeated short‑duration, high‑intensity rainfall events caused surface water flooding in central Barnstaple. Highway flooding made key roads impassable (the Square noted as impassable) and the A361 and the A39 were blocked, causing congestion on surrounding roads. Specific local impacts recorded on past occasions include property flooding in residential areas (Signal Terrace/Clifton Terrace, Bradiford, Sherrats Oak/Raleigh) and multiple instances of highway flooding in central streets (Market Street, Raleigh Road). * Widespread, localised surface water flooding across the town. Some areas experienced mainly external flooding to gardens and outbuildings while others suffered internal flooding to dwellings and businesses. Significant disruption to the highway network in Barnstaple town centre (including The Square); A361 and A39 were reported blocked by flooding, causing congestion. (p2) [IMG, TAB, PAG]
* {17 Aug 2020, 17 Aug 2026}: Very localised, high-intensity rainfall on 17 August produced large volumes of surface water that overwhelmed drainage. Some areas experienced external flooding to gardens and outbuildings while others suffered internal flooding to dwellings and businesses. Localised but widespread property flooding concentrated in central Barnstaple and along the river. (p3) [IMG]
