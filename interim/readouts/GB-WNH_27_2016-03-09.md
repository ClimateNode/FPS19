### West Farndon

* At least one residential property in West Farndon experienced internal flooding (water entered the property). Flooding was caused by heavy rainfall on a saturated catchment, with land drainage unable to convey flows and excess surface water following natural contours to low points around private property. Internal flooding has occurred on more than one occasion in the last five years. (p7)
* A residential property in West Farndon (on Byfield-Eydon Road) experienced internal flooding; the property has been internally flooded on more than one occasion in the last five years and the event on 9 March 2016 led to a formal investigation. (p9) [PAG]

### River Cherwell
* The River Cherwell is the nearby watercourse adjacent to the affected/flooded property. Mapping shows the flooded property located adjacent to an area at high risk of river flooding. Local channels and a brick culvert connect from the property area into the River Cherwell (connections ~100m and ~90m from the property), indicating the river and connected channels were implicated in the flooding at the affected property. (p10)
* River levels rose beyond the channel on the morning of 9 March 2016, overtopped banks, flooded land either side of the river, overtopped local drainage ditches and flowed down the mill wheel channel; high flows and overtopping contributed to culverts beneath Eydon‑Byfield Road being overwhelmed, creating a damming effect and leading to flooding to the road and nearby property. (p12) [PAG]
* Heavy rainfall over a saturated catchment caused high flows in the River Cherwell; water levels rose and strong flows overtopped the banks, producing excess surface water flowing to low points around properties. High water levels migrated through permeable soils and also caused groundwater flooding. (p13)
* {07 Feb 2014}: High water levels upstream of a culvert under the road (Byfield–Eydon Road) with floodwater spreading beyond the normal riverbanks and encroaching onto adjacent land. The upstream entrance of the culvert was nearly submerged and partly obscured by overhanging branches/debris, indicating restricted flow and a risk of blockage that contributed to local flooding and threatened the road/bridge infrastructure. (p40) [IMG, PAG]
* The river was in flood, overflowing its banks and inundating adjacent land and infrastructure (evidenced by the submerged footbridge and flooded fields). (p41) [IMG, PAG]

### Northamptonshire
* Internal flooding of a residential property (listed former mill house) on Byfield‑Eydon Road, West Farndon, which lies within Northamptonshire; the property experienced internal flooding on 9 March 2016 and on other occasions in the previous five years. (p9)
* {01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015, 01 Feb 2016 - 29 Feb 2016, 09 Mar 2016}: Above-average monthly rainfall in December 2015 and February 2016 led to saturated ground across catchments in Northamptonshire, and intense rainfall on 09/03/2016. These conditions contributed to groundwater rising and to flooding of properties (groundwater ingress through stone tiled flooring and flood water around sides and front of properties). (p11) [MUL]
* {general}: The county contains recorded flooding 'hotspots' and is the area over which the LLFA (NCC) maintains an asset register and investigates flood incidents under Section 19; county-level responsibilities and records are used to identify and manage flood risk. (p15)
* Quant: one property (p9)

### Eydon-Byfield Road, West Farndon
* Culverts beneath the road were overwhelmed on the morning of 9 March 2016, producing a damming effect that led to flooding over the road and to the front of a nearby residential property; external flooding across the road reached a depth of approximately 300mm. (p12)
* The River Cherwell culvert under the Eydon‑Byfield Road was overwhelmed (capacity exceeded), creating a damming effect that held water back and caused flooding upstream and across the surface of the road. (p13)
* Quant: approximately 300mm external flood depth across the road; approximately 150mm internal flood depth in the property's wash/cloakroom (p12)
