

# Derbyshire flooding 18 Oct 2023 - 21 Oct 2023

Reference document:<br>Derbyshire County Council Section 19 Investigation Report – Storm Babet 18/10/23 – 21/10/23<br>034\_Derbyshire County/034\_2023\_Derbyshire County/section-8-post-event-response-and-recovery-final-storm-babet-dcc-section-19-report.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Derbyshire
* County‑wide impacts from Storm Babet: site visits and flood investigations were undertaken; over 400 structures/assets affected were inspected by Derbyshire County Council; a strategic recovery group was set up and Community Information Officers were deployed by the Environment Agency. Water companies investigated drainage networks, wastewater treatment works, pumping stations and combined sewer overflow facilities. (p2)
* Communities across the county were affected by flooding during the event(s), prompting council-led investigations (e.g., Ashton-on-Trent), guidance publication by district/town councils and plans to improve county-wide flood risk management and community resilience. (p7) [PAG]

### North East Derbyshire
* Local authority staff helped remove debris from a road (Loads Road) caused by runoff/overland flow and engaged with a landowner thought to be a source of runoff, as part of the flood-related response. (p3)

### Ridgeway
* The Fire and Rescue Service attended to pump floodwater and assist with any evacuations in the community. (p1)
* Severn Trent investigated the community and informed residents that the combined sewer network was overwhelmed by high flows originating from the River Amber. (p2)
* Local flooding led to a planned flood resilience scheme for Ridgeway; delivery of the scheme was delayed (due October 2023) following the flood investigation. (p5)

### Duffield
* The Fire and Rescue Service attended to pump floodwater and assist with any evacuations in the community. (p1)
* Derbyshire County Council is investigating flooding mechanisms from ordinary watercourses upstream of Duffield Meadows School to determine if maintenance actions are required to address flooding. (p6)

### Brampton
* The Fire and Rescue Service attended to pump floodwater and assist with any evacuations in the community. (p1)
* Flood risk from Holme Brook and the River Hipper affecting the community; authorities are looking to identify flood-storage sites within the Holme Brook catchment (including Linacre Reservoir) and exploring upstream storage on the River Hipper to reduce flood risk downstream. (p5)

### Breaston
* Community feedback following flooding led to an adjustment of the flood warning threshold for Breaston. (p2)
* Multi-agency investigations are underway into catchment characteristics, railway flooding and flooding from ordinary watercourses; DCC is investigating surface water drainage systems and watercourse conditions to identify maintenance needs to mitigate flooding in the community. (p6)

### Chesterfield
* Central Chesterfield was identified as being at risk from high flows entering the River Rother / Spital Brook and from Holme Brook and River Hipper; RMAs are seeking upstream flood storage sites (Spital Brook catchment, Holme Brook including Linacre Reservoir, and River Hipper) to slow flows and reduce flood risk to downstream communities in Chesterfield. (p5)

### Ironville
* Attended by the Fire and Rescue Service for pumping floodwater and assisting with evacuations. (p1)

### Ambergate
* A request was made to raise the flood warning threshold at Ambergate affecting the Ridgeway community following the event. (p2)

### Stoney Middleton
* Community experienced flooding that was assessed by Derbyshire County Council; feasibility studies for natural flood risk management were initiated following the damage assessment. (p3)

### Poolsbrook
* Drainage issues were reported in Poolsbrook during the event; Derbyshire County Council requested maintenance of an unnamed ditch ('Ditch 2') south of Poolsbrook to enhance flow and reduce overland flow risk. Yorkshire Water is investigating drainage issues in Poolsbrook that may be associated with a pumping station defect. (p3)

### Eckington
* North East Derbyshire District Council supported cleanup efforts after the flood event and worked to return the local depot to operating standard to maintain statutory functions. (p4)

### Hilton
* South Derbyshire District Council attended following the flood event to record the event and provide support to the local community. (p4)

### Bradwell
* Flooding in Bradwell prompted investigation of the condition of highway culverts through the village to assess flood risk. (p5)

### Clowne
* Derbyshire County Council liaised with riparian landowners and Bolsover District Council to remove restrictions and improve capacity in an unnamed watercourse adjacent to properties on Station Road; after this, the Highways Team planned to clear blockages in the twin culvert beneath Creswell Road to alleviate flooding. (p6)

### Draycott
* Derbyshire County Council is investigating surface water drainage systems and the condition of ordinary watercourses in the community to identify maintenance needs to mitigate flooding. (p6)

### Langley Mill
* Drainage issues at Deans Street in Langley Mill led to local flooding concerns; Severn Trent have been made aware and are investigating the drainage problems. (p6)

### Aston-On-Trent
* Investigations by Derbyshire County Council into flooding mechanisms from ordinary watercourses to identify maintenance needs following flood incidents. (p6)

### Chatsworth Road, Chesterfield
* {13 Nov 2023}: Post-event inspections identified flood-risk asset damage: a retaining wall had collapsed into the channel, sections of natural high ground showed movement/erosion, and one outfall flap was missing; defects were reported to riparian owners for repair and monitoring. (p3)
* Flooding from the River Hipper affected Chatsworth Rd West/Holymoorside; the Environment Agency is exploring upstream flood-storage options on the River Hipper to reduce downstream flood risk to this area. (p5)
* {13 Nov 2023}: Quant: 18 flood risk assets inspected; defect reports completed for 6 lengths of natural high ground; remaining 5 lengths noted for monitoring (p3)

### Tapton Terrace, Chesterfield
* Raised flows and flood risk near Tapton Terrace (River Rother / adjacent catchments) prompted investigations into dredging, bridge removal and upstream storage to reduce future flood risk after the event. (p5)

### Lilac Way, Shirland
* Attended by the Fire and Rescue Service for pumping floodwater and assisting with evacuations. (p1)
* {22 Oct 2024}: Clean‑up of silt and blockages from an access chamber following the event; senior officers coordinated the clean‑up. Responsibility for ongoing maintenance was transferred to Severn Trent who will include the chamber in their maintenance programme. (p2)

### Church Lane, Baslow
* Gravel accumulated around the park-side area downstream of the bridge at Church Lane; the Environment Agency removed the accumulated gravel as part of post-event works. (p2)

### Staveley Road, Poolsbrook
* Floodwater outflow from land/ordinary watercourse south of Erin Court flowed into Erin Court and down Staveley Road, inundating the road and contributing to overland flow through the area. (p3)

### Loads Road, Holymoorside
* Runoff/overland flow deposited debris onto the carriageway; debris was removed using a sweeper as part of the response. (p3)

### Chapel Lane West, Chesterfield
* {general}: Derbyshire County Council Flood Team carried out a site inspection and meetings with affected residents and businesses to investigate the mechanisms and extent of flooding on Chapel Lane West. (p3)

### Dock Walk, Chesterfield
* {24 Oct 2023}: A culvert immediately north of Dock Walk was reported to be about 60PCSYM blocked, prompting Environment Agency clearance to restore flows and mitigate flood risk in the locality. (p3)

### Festival Avenue, Breaston
* Experienced flooding where a manhole was surcharging; attended by Severn Trent to inspect and identify the blockage. (p4)

### Church Street, Bradwell
* Drainage surcharging (potential sewer/drain surcharge) was reported on Church Street during the flood investigation; Severn Trent is investigating the issue. (p5)

### Crow Lane, Tapton
* The bridge at Crow Lane (near Tapton Terrace) was identified as a potential constraint on flow; RMAs are exploring removing the bridge to reduce flood risk after high flows during the event. (p5)

### Station Road, Chesterfield
* The Environment Agency is investigating the performance of raised flood defences at Station Road to assess options and feasibility for alterations to reduce flood risk following the event. (p5)

### Meadow Close, Chesterfield
* Excess water in the ordinary watercourse currently risks flowing through the culvert beneath Meadow Close; a channel bypass has been used to divert water into Barkley Wetlands. Authorities plan to lower the weir wall to divert flow earlier and are investigating a bypass channel around Meadow Close and NFM upstream. (p5)

### Coachmans Close, Ashbourne
* Lowest sections of flood defences at Coachmans Close were identified for raising to a consistent level to provide additional short-term protection following flooding; works are planned by the Environment Agency. (p6)

### School Lane, Ashbourne
* Flooding affected the School Lane area, prompting the Environment Agency to consider raising defences, raising access ramps/embankments and to investigate lowering a downstream bank to allow flood water into the floodplain earlier to reduce flood levels. (p6)

### Station Road, Clowne
* An unnamed watercourse adjacent to properties on Station Road has restrictions reducing capacity; liaison and maintenance actions are planned to remove restrictions to improve capacity and reduce flooding of properties. (p6)

### Creswell Road, Clowne
* Twin culvert beneath Creswell Road has blockages that restrict flow; planned clearing is required to reduce flooding from the adjacent watercourse. (p6)

### Dean Street, Langley Mill
* Experienced drainage-related flooding issues during the event; investigations by Severn Trent are ongoing to address the drainage problems. (p6)

### River Hipper
* {24 Oct 2023}: The river was reported about 60PCSYM blocked at a culvert immediately north of Dock Walk; the blockage restricted flows and was cleared by the Environment Agency to reduce flood risk. (p3)
* {24 Oct 2023, 13 Nov 2023}: Debris blocked a culvert (reported ~60PCSYM blocked) restricting flows; post‑event inspections identified undermined banks, a missing flap valve from an outfall and sections of walls in poor condition requiring riparian owner repairs. (p4) [PAG, MUL]
* River Hipper flooding caused elevated flood risk to downstream communities (Holymoorside, Chatsworth Rd West, Brampton, Central Chesterfield, Tapton Terrace); the Environment Agency is exploring upstream flood-storage options to reduce this river flooding. (p5)

### River Rother
* High flows and channel capacity issues on the Rother caused or contributed to flooding in adjacent communities (including near Tapton Terrace and Central Chesterfield); the Environment Agency is assessing whether updated dredging is required and examining flood-defence performance to reduce future risk. (p5)

### Holme Brook
* High flows in the Holme Brook catchment increased flood risk to downstream communities; RMAs are identifying potential upstream flood storage sites (including at Linacre Reservoir) to slow flows after the event. (p5)

### Erin Court
* Outflowing floodwater entered Erin Court and flowed down Staveley Road; an unnamed ditch passing through Erin Court ('Ditch 1') was identified for maintenance to better accommodate floodwater capacity. Investigations were carried out to determine whether drainage arrangements to the south of the southern ordinary watercourse contributed to the outflow into Erin Court. (p3)

### Ravenside Retail Park
* {13 Nov 2023}: Environment Agency inspections found undermined river banks at the retail park following the flood event; riparian owners were notified to make repairs. (p4)

### Bradwell Brook
* The Environment Agency is considering modifications to the middle of Bradwell Brook to increase capacity because the brook contributed to flooding and flood risk in the village. (p5)

### Spital Brook
* Catchment experienced high flows that contributed to flooding downstream into the River Rother; authorities are identifying potential sites for upstream flood storage to slow flows and reduce downstream flood risk. (p5)

### Horns Bridge Roundabout
* Flooding/raised flood risk at Horns Bridge led Yorkshire Water to investigate the feasibility of a storage measure to reduce future flood risk. (p5)

### Henmore Brook
* Subject of a new Environment Agency flood model to establish the standard of protection provided by defences after flooding; the brook’s behaviour was being assessed to understand and reduce flood risk in the School Lane area. (p6)

### School Lane Bridge
* Sediment and vegetation restricted flow during flooding; the Environment Agency carried out (and planned) desilting and vegetation clearance at the bridge to help flood water pass through more easily and investigated changes downstream to reduce flood levels in the School Lane area. (p6)

### Duffield Meadows Primary School
* Flooding from ordinary (upstream) watercourses affected the area immediately upstream of the school; Derbyshire County Council are investigating the flooding mechanisms to determine required maintenance actions. (p6)

* Reviewed river level thresholds for multiple communities (resulting in an adjustment at Breaston and a request to raise the threshold at Ambergate/Ridgeway), attended community drop-in events, and inspected and planned repairs to EA assets following the event. (p2)
* {24 Oct 2023, 13 Nov 2023}: Cleared debris blocking the River Hipper (about 60PCSYM blockage at the culvert immediately north of Dock Walk) to restore flows following a report on 24 Oct 2023. Conducted inspections of 18 flood-risk assets near Chatsworth Road West on 13 Nov 2023, identifying a collapsed retaining wall in the channel (reported to the riparian owner) and a missing outfall flap (reported for remediation); completed defect reports for six assets and noted others for monitoring. (p3) [MUL]
* {24 Oct 2023}: Cleared debris from the River Hipper culvert (reporting ~60PCSYM blockage) immediately north of Dock Walk following a report; patrolled the Avenue Flood Storage Reservoir (screens heavily blinded). (p4) [PAG]
* Channel gravel build-up in the local watercourse that increases flood risk; Environment Agency monitoring gravel accumulation and considering removal where there is a tangible flood-risk benefit, and developing a new flood-warning system for the community. (p5)
* Carrying out works and investigations in response to flooding in Ashbourne: planning to raise defences at Coachmans Close, raise access ramps and embankment, desilt and clear vegetation at School Lane Bridge, considering lowering a downstream bank to allow earlier floodplain inundation, and developing a new flood model for Henmore Brook to establish current standard of protection. (p6)
* Named partner working with Derbyshire County Council on engagement and mitigation; provides the flood warning service referenced for affected communities. (p7)
* {24 Oct 2023, 13 Nov 2023}: Quant: about 60PCSYM of the River Hipper blocked; 18 flood risk assets inspected; 1 outfall flap missing; defect reports completed for 6 assets (p3) [MUL]

### Severn Trent
* Responded to flood-related infrastructure issues: completed checks, addressed immediate network operational issues, dispatched tankers across impacted communities and operated a 24/7 call centre to receive and prioritise reports of flooding. (p1)
* Investigated affected sewer and drainage infrastructure (including CCTV surveys of drainage networks), reviewed wastewater treatment works, pumping stations and combined sewer overflow facilities; took over maintenance responsibility for the access chamber at Lilac Way; investigating ordinary watercourse connected to combined sewer at Little Eaton to understand impacts. (p2)
* Severn Trent undertook investigations into the combined sewer network following the event; follow-up reviews of the sewer network were ongoing at the time of publication. (p3)
* Attended reports of flooding along Festival Avenue and identified a blocked manhole that was surcharging. (p4)
* Severn Trent were notified of drainage issues at Deans Street (Langley Mill) and are investigating the drainage problems that contributed to local flooding. (p6)

### Derbyshire County Council
* Led post-event recovery actions: inspected and investigated affected sites, undertook site visits and community engagement, attended drop-in events, and made the case for extra government funding to repair roads, bridges and pavements. (p1) [PAG]
* {general}: Undertook site visits, performed flood investigations, engaged with communities and inspected over 400 structures/assets that had been affected by Storm Babet. (p2)
* Conducted site visits (eg. Stoney Middleton) to assess flood damage and gather information; requested riparian maintenance for an unnamed ditch south of Poolsbrook ('Ditch 2') and have been involved in investigations into drainage arrangements south of Erin Court that may have contributed to floodwater outflows into Erin Court and down Staveley Road. (p3)
* {January 2024}: Arranged a meeting with a riparian landowner to discuss ownership after the event and confirmed contractors would attend the site to clear access in January 2024 as part of post‑flood actions. (p4)
* Proposed investigation into condition of highways culverts through Bradwell to assess flood risk following the flood event. (p5) [PAG]
* {general}: Investigating and delivering a range of responses to flooding across the county: looking at bypass feasibility around Meadow Close, working with landowners on maintenance and land management, investigating ordinary watercourse mechanisms upstream of Duffield Meadows School and Aston/Aston-on-Trent, coordinating multi-agency investigations in Breaston and Draycott, and carrying out other site visits, meetings and repairs following Storm Babet. (p6) [PAG]
* As the Lead Local Flood Authority, investigating flooding mechanisms from ordinary watercourses in Aston-on-Trent to identify maintenance needs; coordinating with the Environment Agency, water companies and other RMAs; establishing a Derbyshire Flood Risk Partnership to improve planning, prioritisation and co-ordination of flood risk management and mitigation. (p7) [PAG]

### Riverview, Chesterfield
* Floodwaters passed the bridge at River View Cottages and flowed past the properties, prompting investigation of bridge ownership and planned installation of an upstream flood wall with a return to stop further water passage. (p5)

### Chesterfield Borough Council
* Participated in investigations with Derbyshire County Council, the Environment Agency and Yorkshire Water into whether drainage arrangements south of the southern ordinary watercourse contributed to floodwater outflow into Erin Court and down Staveley Road. (p3)
* Chesterfield Borough Council will reduce the height of the weir wall to allow earlier diversion of high flows into the existing channel bypass at Meadow Close as a measure to manage flooding. (p5)

### North East Derbyshire District Council
* Supported cleanup efforts in Eckington and returned the depot to operational standard to continue statutory functions; investigated impact of M1 drainage on flows through Renishaw along Smithy Brook (found no evidence drainage exacerbated the flooding). (p4)

### Derbyshire Fire and Rescue Service
* Attended numerous locations to pump floodwater and assist with evacuations across affected communities. (p1)

### Derbyshire Dales District Council
* Carried out welfare checks on affected properties in Baslow East in the days following the event. (p2)

### Yorkshire Water
* Investigating drainage issues in Poolsbrook which may be associated with a pumping station defect that could have contributed to local flooding during the event. (p3)

### Linacre Reservoirs
* Identified as a potential location for upstream flood storage to reduce peak flows in Holme Brook following high flows during the event. (p5)

### Barkley Wetlands
* Barkley Wetlands is being used as the recipient of a channel bypass to receive excess water from the ordinary watercourse instead of allowing it to pass through the Meadow Close culvert, to reduce local flood risk. (p5)

### Bolsover District Council
* Participated in liaison about maintenance of the unnamed watercourse adjacent to properties on Station Road to remove restrictions and improve capacity following flooding, supporting subsequent culvert clearance under Creswell Road. (p6)
