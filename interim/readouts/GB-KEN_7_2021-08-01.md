

# Ulcombe flooding 01 Aug 2021

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Ulcombe<br>063\_Kent/Flooding-report-affecting-Ulcombe-on-1-August-2021.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Ulcombe
* Drainage ditches, local watercourses and parts of the local sewer network were unable to cope with intense, localised rainfall, resulting in property flooding across Ulcombe and the surrounding area. (p1)
* Survey responses and reports indicate properties within the village were flooded. Of the survey responses, five properties reported internal flooding and one reported external flooding. Within Ulcombe, The Street had 27 properties reported flooded (internal flooding reported up to 15 cm) and Pye Corner had 3 properties reported flooded. (p2)
* Quant: approximately 27 properties (p1)
* Quant: 5 properties internal; 1 property external; 27 properties; 3 properties; 15 cm internal flood depth (p2)

### The Street, Ulcombe
* The Parish Council reported 27 properties flooded during the flood event. Internal flooding at properties on The Street was reported up to a depth of 15 cm. (p2) [TAB]
* Quant: 27 properties; 15 cm internal flood depth (p2) [TAB]

### Pye Corner
* Three properties at Pye Corner reported flooding; available information does not state whether flooding was internal or external. (p2) [TAB]
* Quant: 3 properties (p2) [TAB]
