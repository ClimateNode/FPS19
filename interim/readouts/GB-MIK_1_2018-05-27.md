## Topics without coordinates

### Anglian Water
* Sewer/drainage infrastructure serving affected areas was overwhelmed by intense, short-duration rainfall, contributing to surface water flows that reached low points and caused internal flooding of residential properties. The organisation is identified to undertake surveys of drainage pipes, manholes and gullies to confirm functionality. (p11) [TAB]
* The historic watercourse has been integrated within the public sewer system maintained by Anglian Water; this sewer system was implicated in the observed flooding within Oldbrook, including a burst sewer at Kirkstall Place/Grace Avenue. (p25)
* A pipe burst on the Anglian Water sewer caused flooding that entered retail units at Cairngorm Gate and undermined paved areas. (p33)
* Anglian Water attended drainage/sewer incidents and undertook post-event actions in response to the storm. They confirmed root ingress to the surface water system in the Beanhill area and have planned maintenance; they assessed that even if maintenance had been completed before the storm the effect on the extent of flooding would have been negligible. (p38) [TAB]
* Anglian Water reported that maintenance was in progress during the storm but considered it would have had negligible effect on flood extent; they noted rapid rises in lake levels (indicating rapid conveyance by the surface water network), acknowledged that strategic drainage was locally overwhelmed, and are undertaking a review of network capacity around Newport Pagnell. (p39)
* Evidence of sewer surcharging indicated significant volumes of water were present in the sewer system; Anglian Water reported the surface water system performed well during the event. (p40) [PAG]
* Quant: over 315 residential properties (p11) [TAB]
* Quant: 300 mm deep floodwater within retail units (p33)
* Quant: 1 Flooding From Land incident (recorded in Anglian Water incident attendance data for 2016–2018) (p38) [TAB]

### Saxon Street Overpass
* Identified as the origin of a surface water flow path that proceeded from the overpass along Larwood Place and down Hutton Avenue, contributing to flooding on adjacent streets during the event. (p25)

### Netherfield Catchment
* Indicators of surface water flooding were observed in the Netherfield catchment during the site inspection. Properties with garages and door thresholds below the carriageway and low kerbs showed signs of inundation. Flooding was observed along the southern portions of Broadlands, Farthing Grove, The Hide, Farnborough, Beadlemead and Langland Road. A flow route was identified between the Netherfield catchment and the Hospital, indicating the catchment contributed to flood flow toward the Hospital and is therefore relevant for mitigation. (p31)

### Milton Keynes Council Highway Team
* Managed and recorded highway drainage performance during the storm; identified localised areas affected by the flood event (notably Wolverton Road). Raised concerns about the effectiveness of perimeter filter drains around open space areas and noted changes of ownership of carrier pipes on the sewer network leading to differing maintenance standards that could influence local drainage performance. (p37)
