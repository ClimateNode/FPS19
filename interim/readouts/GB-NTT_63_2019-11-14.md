

# Lowdham flooding 14 Nov 2019

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Lowdham – November 2019<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/section19reportlowdhamnovember2019.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Nottinghamshire
* {07 Nov 2019 - 14 Nov 2019, 14 Nov 2019}: Extensive flooding occurred across the county following heavy and prolonged rainfall between 7 and 14 November 2019; a major incident was declared on 7 November 2019 and county-level response actions (road closures, flood signage and sandbag distribution) were implemented. (p1) [PAG, MUL]
* Extensive flooding across the county associated with the severe rainfall event; Nottinghamshire Local Resilience Forum declared a major incident; Environment Agency monitored water levels and issued multiple flood warnings during the period. (p2)
* {07 Nov 2019 - 14 Nov 2019, 14 Nov 2019}: Quant: 66 road closures; over 750 flood signs; over 5000 sandbags (p1) [PAG, MUL]
* Quant: 38 flood warnings; 16 (in November) (p2)

### Lowdham
* Extensive flooding across the village following heavy rainfall on 14 November 2019; flooding affected the village which is served by the Cocker Beck (a Main River). (p1) [PAG]
* Parts of the village suffered a significant flood event causing internal property flooding, public highways were flooded, and several gardens/curtilages were affected. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* {2000, 2007, 2012, 2013, 14 Nov 2019}: The Cocker Beck overtopped on 14th November, with surface water running from saturated fields and using the Epperstone By‑Pass and the Main Street as flow paths; surface water overwhelmed highway drainage and properties on Main Street and Southwell Road in Lowdham were flooded. (p4) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
* Quant: 12 reported incidents of internal property flooding (p2) [IMG, PAG]

### Southwell Road, Lowdham
* Surface water overwhelmed the highway drainage on Southwell Road and properties along Southwell Road were flooded as floodwaters flowed along the road corridor. (p4) [IMG]
* {2019}: Surface water affected the highway drainage during the 2019 event; a scheme (starting January 2020) to improve surface water and highway drainage on Southwell Road is planned to increase resilience. (p7)

### Main Street, Lowdham
* Floodwater from the overtopping Cocker Beck and surface water flow used Main Street as a flow route; properties along Main Street were flooded and highway drainage was overwhelmed. (p4) [IMG, PAG]

### Station Road, Lowdham
* {2019}: Following the 2019 event vegetation clearance and repair work is planned to ensure the wall around the storage area and on Station Street (sic) are in good condition, addressing damage/weaknesses caused by the event. (p7)

### Cocker Beck
* Cocker Beck (classed as a Main River and serving Lowdham) likely contributed to the fluvial flooding that affected the village. (p2)
* The Cocker Beck rose to near-record levels and overtopped its banks; overtopping spilled out onto the Main Street and contributed to flooding of nearby properties. The high river levels also conveyed large volumes of runoff from the hills into the valley. (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 1.273m (river level recorded on 14th November) (p4) [IMG, PAG]

### Epperstone by-Pass
* Public highways in the village were affected by flooding during the event; the A6097 Epperstone By-Pass runs along Lowdham's western boundary and is adjacent to the impacted highway network (may have experienced inundation or disruption). (p2)
* During the event surface water from saturated fields flowed along the Epperstone By-Pass alignment (referred to as the by-Pass) and the bypass acted as a channel for overland flow, conveying water that otherwise might have reached the Beck. (p4) [PAG]
