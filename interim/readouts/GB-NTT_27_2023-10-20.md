

# Edwinstowe flooding 20 Oct 2023

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Edwinstowe – Storm Babet, October 2023<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/edwinstowe-s19-storm-babet-oct-2023.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Nottinghamshire
* Major flood incident affected the county on 20th October 2023. County officers attended sites to verify and record internal flooding in properties, provided 24-hour emergency planning support, delivered sandbags, collated and published verified flood impact data to partners, supported district partners with resources and closed roads to promote safety. (p4)
* {19 Oct 2023, 20 Oct 2023}: Parts of the county experienced river-flooding risk from the River Maun (Flood Alert on 19/10/2023 and Flood Warning on 20/10/2023) prompting patrols, community visits and multi-agency response. (p5) [MUL]
* Quant: 77 roads (p4)

### Newark and Sherwood
* District experienced surface water flooding which then transitioned to a continued risk from river flooding. The council coordinated and delivered multi-agency response actions: formed a large response team, visited impacted communities and vulnerable individuals, assisted evacuations, delivered aqua sacs, provided emergency accommodation, carried out road/path clean-up and bulky waste removal, offered recovery support and grant funding, and restocked community stores of aqua sacs. (p5)

### Edwinstowe
* Sustained heavy rainfall during Storm Babet caused surface water flooding in Edwinstowe; roads flooded and properties experienced rapid onset flooding due to saturated ground, with internal flooding of residences. (p1)
* Received a Flood Warning for the River Maun on 20/10/2023; was visited by agency and council staff as part of patrols and community visits to assess impact and advise residents during the flood event. (p5)
* Quant: 8 residential properties (p1)

### Ollerton
* A Flood Warning was issued for the River Maun at Ollerton (EA Flood Warning); community information officers and surveyors visited affected communities to understand the impact of flooding. (p5)

### Mill Lane, Ollerton
* The River Maun flowed out of bank and into properties on Mill Lane during Storm Babet, causing internal flooding of residential properties; two bridges on the reach may have obstructed flow contributing to out-of-bank flooding. (p2) [PAG]
* During Storm Babet the River Maun flowed out of bank and entered properties on Mill Lane. Two bridges on the reach are identified as possible flow obstructions that may have led to overtopping and property flooding. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 7 residential properties (p2) [PAG]

### High Street, Edwinstowe
* The River Maun flowed out of bank and into properties on High Street during Storm Babet, causing internal flooding to at least one property. (p2) [PAG]
* During Storm Babet the River Maun flowed out of bank and entered properties on High Street. Two bridges on the reach are noted as potential obstructions that may have contributed to out‑of‑bank flow and property flooding. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 1 property (p2) [PAG]

### Whitewater Bridge
* {20 Oct 2023, 21 Oct 2023}: The Whitewater Bridge water level station recorded a record high river level (1.74 m) on 21 October 2023, indicating exceptionally high river levels downstream of Edwinstowe associated with the flooding event. (p2) [MUL]
* {20 Oct 2023, 21 Oct 2023}: Quant: record water level of 1.74 m (p2) [MUL]

### River Trent
* Predicted extreme levels for the River Trent led the council to coordinate in-person visits to communities at high risk of flooding to reinforce immediate preparedness and evacuation actions. (p5)


## Topics without coordinates

### River Maun
* {20 Oct 2023, 21 Oct 2023}: The River Maun flowed out of its banks during the event and inundated properties at Mill Lane and High Street; two bridges on the reach are noted as obstructions that may have contributed to out‑of‑bank flow. (p2) [MUL]
* {19 Oct 2023, 20 Oct 2023}: Subject of a Flood Alert (19/10/2023) and a Flood Warning (20/10/2023) affecting communities including Edwinstowe and Ollerton; prompted patrols and clearance of blockages before and after peak flows. (p5) [MUL]
* {20 Oct 2023, 21 Oct 2023}: Quant: 7 residential properties on Mill Lane; 1 property on High Street (p2) [MUL]

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Responded to a major flooding incident across the county: initiated a Community Flood Recovery Grant, attended sites to verify internal flooding and provide support to affected residents and businesses, collated and published verified flood impact data, chaired Strategic/Tactical/Recovery coordination groups, provided 24-hour emergency support, led the Section 19 Flood Investigation, delivered sandbags, and closed roads to protect public safety. (p4)
* Quant: 77 roads (p4)

### Environment Agency
* Issued a Flood Alert for the River Maun in Nottinghamshire on 19/10/2023 and a Flood Warning for the River Maun at Edwinstowe (and Ollerton) on 20/10/2023; instigated flood patrols, cleared blockages where safe, and coordinated community information officers and surveyors to visit and gather information from affected communities. (p5) [PAG, MUL]
