

# Knebworth flooding 07 Feb 2014

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report Knebworth, Hertfordshire<br>056\_Hertfordshire/hcc-flood-investigation-s19knebworth.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Knebworth
* Rural field inundated with standing water covering a significant area and partially submerging a footpath; a residential street was flooded across its full width with water extending into driveways and yards, surrounding parked cars and disrupting access to properties. (p1)
* Surface water runoff from saturated farmland overflowed onto an adjacent road and subsequently flooded properties. Eighteen properties were flooded internally and two properties suffered external flooding; floodwater affected properties in a further two streets. (p5)
* The western part of Knebworth (site east of Gypsy Lane) was affected by flooding on 7 February 2014, which resulted in internal flooding of residential properties in Orchard Way and Broom Grove. (p7)
* Southwestern part of Knebworth (near Park Lane and the railway line) was flooded. The mapped flood-affected area (red circle and blue shading) covers land west of the main settlement including areas adjacent to the Recreation Ground and nearby residential/public areas. (p8) [IMG]
* {1978, 1987, 07 Feb 2014, 08 Aug 2014, 19 Sep 2014, 13 Oct 2014}: Parts of Knebworth (notably Orchard Way and Broom Grove) experienced surface water flooding affecting roads and external property areas and causing internal property flooding on 7 February 2014. (p9) [TAB, MUL]
* Heavy rainfall on saturated ground produced surface water runoff and overland flow across the catchment that flooded local streets and residential properties in the Knebworth area (flow route described from Gypsy Lane into Orchard Way and Broom Grove). (p10)
* Knebworth experienced a flooding event that prompted a Section 19 flood investigation by the Lead Local Flood Authority. (p13)
* Properties in Knebworth experienced internal flooding during the 2013–14 period and the area was the subject of a Section 19 investigation following the flood event on 7 February 2014. (p16)
* Quant: 18 properties; 2 properties; 2 streets (p5)
* Quant: 20 properties flooded; 18 properties flooded internally (p10)

### Orchard Way, Knebworth
* Residential properties in Orchard Way suffered internal flooding following the flood event on 7 February 2014. (p7)
* {1978, 1987, 07 Feb 2014, 08 Aug 2014, 19 Sep 2014, 13 Oct 2014}: Internal and external areas of the property were flooded following surface water / highway drainage failure; a resident reported a surface water drain was constructed through a neighbour's garden after the 1987 flood to try to alleviate the issue. (p9) [MUL]
* Overland flow entered rear gardens, entered properties through rear doors, and also moved around the sides of properties into front gardens and onto the road, flowing downslope toward garages and the rear gardens of Broom Grove properties. (p10)
* Surface water runoff was directed to the field opposite the rear gardens of Orchard Way; the estate highway drainage and road gullies were overwhelmed and unable to cope, causing local flooding in the Orchard Way area. (p11)
* Highway drainage underperformance and blocked gullies were identified as factors in flooding on Orchard Way during the investigation into the 7 February 2014 event; recommendations included CCTV survey of the drainage and increased gully cleaning to restore performance and reduce future surface water flooding of the highway and nearby properties. (p16) [TAB]
* Surface water runoff affected Orchard Way; highway drainage works included cleaning gullies and the connection between Orchard Way and Gypsy Lane to manage runoff. (p18)
* Surface water runoff reached Orchard Way following Gypsy Lane, causing surface-water flooding of the street. (p19)

### Broom Grove, Knebworth
* Residential properties in Broom Grove suffered internal flooding following the flood event on 7 February 2014. (p7)
* {1978, 1987, 07 Feb 2014, 08 Aug 2014, 19 Sep 2014, 13 Oct 2014}: Roads and external areas of properties were flooded during historical events and multiple 2014 events (roads and external property areas affected). (p9) [TAB, MUL]
* Overland flow continued into rear gardens, entered properties via rear doors, flowed out of front doors into front gardens and the road, and ponded in the road at the entrance to the walkway to Gibbons Way. (p10)
* Broom Grove is the lowest point in the catchment so runoff accumulated there; its road gullies/soakaways and highway drainage were overwhelmed and unable to cope, leading to flooding at Broom Grove. (p11)
* Highway gullies and drainage in Broom Grove were implicated in the flooding; recommendations include CCTV survey, removal of blockages and increasing gully‑cleaning frequency (currently an 18‑month cycle) to reduce flood risk after the event. (p16) [TAB]
* Surface water runoff entered Broom Grove; recommended drainage actions include surveying/cleaning gullies and potentially increasing gully cleaning frequency to reduce future surface water flooding. (p18)
* Surface water runoff reached Broom Grove (subsequent to Gypsy Lane), causing surface-water flooding of the street. (p19)

### Gipsy Lane, Knebworth
* Gypsy Lane is identified on the map bounding box near the blue-shaded flood area in southwest Knebworth; the map indicates streets in this vicinity were affected by flooding during the event. (p8) [IMG]
* Surface water runoff from adjacent farmland flooded the lane; water then flowed from the lane into rear gardens of Orchard Way and entered properties through rear doors. (p10)
* Runoff was funnelled toward the field adjacent to Gypsy Lane; highway drainage (including the single road gully at the rear of Orchard Way) was overwhelmed and unable to cope with the volume of water, contributing to local flooding. (p11)
* Highway drainage in Gypsy Lane was implicated in the flooding response; recommendation to CCTV-survey the drainage and remove blockages to restore performance following the flood event. (p16) [TAB]
* Surface water runoff entered Gypsy Lane during the event; drainage-related remediation carried out including cleaning soakaways and gullies, clearing the connection to Orchard Way, and re-digging ditches. (p18)
* Surface water runoff entered Gypsy Lane during the event, causing surface-water flooding of the street and contributing runoff downstream. (p19)
* Quant: 1 road gully (p11)

### Park Lane, Knebworth
* Map and image description show the flood-affected area concentrated in southwest Knebworth adjacent to Park Lane and the railway; residential streets and nearby public areas around Park Lane were within the blue-shaded/flood-affected zone. (p8) [IMG]

### Gibbons Way, Knebworth
* Road ponding occurred at the entrance to the walkway to Gibbons Way as a result of the overland flow from surrounding streets and gardens; the report notes ponding at this location (it is not explicit that properties on Gibbons Way were internally flooded). (p10)


## Topics without coordinates

### Hertfordshire County Council
* Acted as Lead Local Flood Authority to coordinate measures to minimise and manage surface water runoff entering Gypsy Lane and subsequently Orchard Way and Broom Grove; worked with partners to develop and test surface water management measures following the event. (p18)

### Highways Authority
* Surveyed and cleaned highway drainage in and around Gypsy Lane, Broom Grove and Orchard Way; cleaned main soakaways and associated gullies; cleared the connection between Gypsy Lane and Orchard Way; re-dug ditches on Gypsy Lane and proposed increasing gully cleaning frequency to reduce future surface water flooding. (p18)
