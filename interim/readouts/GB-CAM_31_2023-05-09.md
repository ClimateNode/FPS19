

# Wisbech flooding 09 May 2023

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Wisbech<br>021\_Cambridgeshire County/021\_2023\_Cambridgeshire County/wisbech-may-2023-flood-investigation.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Wisbech
* Internal property flooding reported across the town; LLFA received five flood reports describing internal property flooding. (p1) [TAB]
* A high-intensity storm on 9 May 2023 caused surface water flooding in Wisbech (notably Osborne Road). The local surface water drainage and sewer system were overwhelmed; excess water flowed to the lowest point in the road and accumulated to roughly 300 mm above the drain cover. Five properties were flooded. The fire service attended and pumped water away and levels had subsided by 20:00 the same day. (p2)
* Intense rainfall on 9th May 2023 overwhelmed the capacity of the surface water drainage network, causing flooding in Wisbech. (p3)
* Quant: 5 flood reports (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: 5 properties; approximately 300 mm accumulation above drain cover; one road gully at collection point (p2)

### Osborne Road, Wisbech
* Approximate location of reported flooding indicated on the map; flooding led to internal property flooding reported to the LLFA. (p1)
* On 9 May 2023 a high-intensity storm rapidly filled the local surface water drainage to capacity; excess surface water flowed down Osborne Road to the low point where it accumulated to about 300 mm above the drain cover. Only one road gully served the collection point, so water levels rose quickly and five properties were flooded. The sewer system was reported to be overwhelmed. The fire service pumped water away and levels had subsided by 20:00. (p2)
* Surface water collected at a low spot where the drainage was overwhelmed/restricted; gullies required cleaning and further inspection of the surface water system to its outfall was recommended. (p3) [TAB]
* Quant: 5 properties; approximately 300 mm accumulation above drain cover; one road gully (p2)
