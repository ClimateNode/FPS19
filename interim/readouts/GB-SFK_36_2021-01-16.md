### Bury St Edmunds
* Location of the flood event under investigation (the report concerns flooding at Blomfield Street in Bury St Edmunds); no specific property- or infrastructure-level impacts within the town are detailed in the provided excerpt. (p1)
* {Historically - circa 2006, 16 Jan 2021 - 12 Feb 2021}: Rising groundwater levels/groundwater flooding were recorded in parts of Bury St Edmunds. Internal flooding was reported on Blomfield Street (at least six adjacent properties) during January–February 2021; other areas in the town (Tayfen Road, Thomas Close, Eastgate Street and Barn Lane) also recorded rising groundwater/groundwater flooding during the same period. (p2) [PAG, MUL]
* Internal flooding reported within at least six adjacent properties; continued ingress of water into below-ground structures (basements and cellars) to approximately 300 mm. Some properties required pumping (reports of up to 2,500 litres removed) and one tenant was rehoused. Some affected properties had pre-existing water-resistant membranes and pumps that were overwhelmed. Other areas of the town (Tayfen Road, Thomas Close, Eastgate Street and Barn Lane) recorded rising groundwater levels/groundwater flooding during the same period. (p3) [PAG]
* {08 Jun 2017, 22 Sep 2017, 16 Jan 2021 - 12 Feb 2021}: The location has recorded flooding on at least two separate occasions in the last decade; historic groundwater flooding has been associated with the area. At least one property experienced basement water ingress (a basement water sample tested positive for potable-water markers including chloride and nitrates on 29/01/2021). Flood incident records on the mapped area show medium-priority residential flood incidents. (p4) [MUL]
* Groundwater levels rose markedly, leading to basement flooding in properties. At least one basement water sample (29/01/2021) tested positive for chemicals associated with potable water (chloride and nitrates). Groundwater rise is identified as the highly probable source of the basement flooding; groundwater flood alerts were issued for Bury St Edmunds. (p5) [MUL]

### Blomfield Street, Howard Estate
* Subject of a Section 19 flood investigation following a flood event. The report identifies Blomfield Street as the location investigated; no further specific impacts to the street are described in the provided excerpt. (p1)
* {Historically - circa 2006, 16 Jan 2021 - 12 Feb 2021}: Internal flooding of adjacent properties occurred as a result of rising groundwater/groundwater flooding; internal flooding was reported within at least six adjacent properties during the January–February 2021 period. The event history also notes flooding historically circa 2006. There is ambiguity noted in the report about the source/responsibility for the flood incident. (p2) [PAG, MUL]
* Internal flooding within at least six adjacent properties; continued ingress into basements/cellars (reported to ~300 mm). Some properties had large volumes pumped out (up to 2,500 litres) and one tenant required rehousing. Previously fitted water-resistant membranes and pumps were reported to have been overwhelmed. (p3) [IMG]
* {general}: Surface water flooding is identified in the local mapping context; limited surface water was reported in the road to the south of Blomfield Street (in Bishops Road) and the properties in question are indicated to be at low to very low pluvial flood risk. The sewer in Blomfield Street was inspected and reported to have no visible defects. (p4) [PAG]
* Water meter boundary boxes at properties were surcharged; at least one property reported surface water and foul drainage infrastructure at the rear. Surcharging could be due to surface water, groundwater or mains water flooding affecting properties on the street. (p6) [TAB]
* {Historically - circa 2006, 16 Jan 2021 - 12 Feb 2021}: Quant: at least 6 adjacent properties; 6 reports (reported to SCC between 16/01/2021 and 12/02/2021) (p2) [PAG, MUL]

* Quant: 7 of the 9 properties (p6) [TAB]

### Anglian Water
* {general}: Anglian Water manholes were reported to be surcharging on an adjacent street (Bishops Hill). AWS investigated basement water ingress (source ID tests and noise logging) and concluded the water ingress was not due to leakage from AWS pipes; AWS confirmed the Blomfield Street sewer was in good order with no visible defects. (p4)

### Bishops Hill
* {01 Jan 2021 - 28 Feb 2021}: Anglian Water manholes were reported to be surcharging on Bishops Hill (surcharging of sewer/manholes reported adjacent to the investigation area). (p3)

### Highways Authority
* {19 Mar 2021}: SCC Highways inspected highway drainage (19/03/2021); found all drainage operational and noted a couple of gullies full on arrival. Concluded that blocked gullies would cause surface ponding rather than flooded basements and therefore highways drainage had not contributed to the reported cellar/basement flooding. Also confirmed previous AWS investigations had not adversely affected highway drainage. (p5)
