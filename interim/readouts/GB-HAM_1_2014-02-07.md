

# Buckskin flooding 07 Feb 2014 - 31 Mar 2014

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report On Flooding Incident Investigation Into Buckskin<br>050\_Hampshire/050\_2014\_Hampshire/buckskin-executive-summary.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Buckskin
* High groundwater levels instigated flooding in Bucksin: a dormant spring became active, surface water soakaways failed, and runoff followed the historic river course to accumulate in low-lying areas. Foul sewer network was overwhelmed by the inundation. (p1)
* Quant: 45 properties reported as flooding (36 internally and 9 externally); up to 88 properties could have been flooded; 36 properties reported foul (sewer) flooding. (p1)

### Grampian Way, Buckskin
* The area surrounding Grampian Way was affected by fluvial (river), surface water, groundwater and foul/sewer flooding. High groundwater activated a dormant spring and soakaways did not function, causing runoff to follow a historic river course and accumulate in low-lying areas; nearby properties were flooded and the foul sewer network was inundated leading to foul flooding at properties. (p1)
* Quant: Part of the investigation area where 45 properties were reported flooded (36 internally and 9 externally); 36 properties reported foul flooding; up to 88 properties estimated flooded (p1)
