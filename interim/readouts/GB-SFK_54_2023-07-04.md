### Millenium Way, Lowestoft
* {general}: A 525mm surface water pipe from Millenium Way connects into the Anglian Water surface water sewer which then reduces to a single 500mm pipe prior to Worlingham Way. During the intense rainfall the pipe configuration (larger diameter feeder pipes converging into a smaller single pipe) may have contributed to exceedance of the surface water drainage system and thus to flooding (further detailed assessment required). (p5)
* {general}: Quant: one 525mm pipe; connects into a single 500mm pipe downstream (p5)

### Cavendish Close, Oulton Broad
* {general}: A 225mm surface water pipe from Cavendish Close connects into the Anglian Water surface water sewer. This connection into the shared system may have contributed to exceedance of the surface water drainage system during the intense rainfall, potentially worsening flooding (further assessment required). (p5)
* {general}: Quant: one 225mm pipe (p5)

### Berkeley Gardens, Lowestoft
* {general}: A separate 150mm surface water pipe from Berkeley Gardens connects into the surface water sewer network. During the intense rainfall the presence of multiple feeder pipes, including the 150mm connection, into a system that narrowed downstream may have contributed to exceedance of the drainage capacity and resultant flooding (further assessment required). (p5)
* {general}: Quant: one 150mm pipe (p5)


## Topics without coordinates

### Anglian Water
* The Anglian Water surface water sewer is described as the local surface water sewer serving a large urban area and connecting through the location of flooding; the system geometry (two separate upstream 525mm pipes and a 225mm pipe connecting into a single 500mm pipe, with further 150mm connections downstream) is identified as logically susceptible to exceedance during severe/intense rainfall and thus as a contributing drainage system to the reported flooding. (p5)
* Identified as an asset owner required to assess maintenance and capacity of surface water drainage assets in the area; maintenance and capacity assessment are recorded as outstanding in the report. (p7) [TAB]

### Suffolk County Council
* As Lead Local Flood Authority and Highways Authority, SCC is responsible for assessing and responding to surface water drainage issues. SCC recorded maintenance actions in the area (gullies on Worlingham Way cleansed June 2023; Oulton Road cleansed May 2023; roundabout outer circle April 2023) and have commissioned cleansing, jetting and investigation; capacity assessment remains outstanding. (p7) [TAB]

### Highways Authority
* {01 Apr 2023 - 30 Apr 2023, 01 May 2023 - 31 May 2023, 01 Jun 2023 - 30 Jun 2023}: Suffolk County Council (as LLFA and Highways Authority) undertook maintenance actions in response to the flood risk: gullies cleansed on Worlingham Way (June 2023), Oulton Road (May 2023) and the roundabout outer circle (April 2023); commissioned whole-area cleansing, jetting and survey/investigation. Capacity assessments remain outstanding. (p7) [TAB, MUL]
