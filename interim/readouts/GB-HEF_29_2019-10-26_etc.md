

# Llangarron flooding 26 Oct 2019, Llanwarne flooding 26 Oct 2019, Llangarron flooding 16 Feb 2020, Llanwarne flooding 16 Feb 2020, Llangarron flooding 23 Dec 2020 and Llanwarne flooding 23 Dec 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Llangarron & Llanwarne<br>055\_Herefordshire County/28\_Llangarron.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Llanwarne
* {general}: Areas of the village (centre and eastern parts) fall within Environment Agency Flood Zone 3 and 2 along the main river, indicating exposure to fluvial flooding; flood defences are present in the eastern section protecting some high-risk areas. (p2) [IMG]
* {26 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020, 23 Dec 2020}: Fluvial flooding in Llanwarne affected two properties and a parish church (episodes during each reported storm, lasting up to 6 hours). There was also a report of internal flooding at one other property in Llanwarne (noted as being in Flood Zone 1). (p4) [TAB, MUL]
* {February 2020, December 2020}: Properties in Llanwarne flooded internally. Flooding is associated with Flood Zone 3 adjacent to the Garren Brook; properties that flooded from the Gamber Brook are also in Flood Zone 3. The Surface Water Flood Map shows a fluvial flow route passing by the property adjacent to the Garren Brook on the southern side. The Gamber Brook has been diverted for industrial purposes and can allow floodwater to spill away from the watercourse when it breaks its banks, contributing to property flooding. (p5) [PAG, MUL]
* {26 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020, 23 Dec 2020}: Quant: 2 properties; 1 parish church; 1 additional internally flooded property (in Flood Zone 1) (p4) [TAB, MUL]

### Llangarron
* {26 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020, 23 Dec 2020}: Internal fluvial flooding of properties in Llangarron caused by the nearby brook. Reported internal flooding events affected properties and adjacent gardens/outbuildings; one property experienced repeated flooding across multiple storm events. Descriptive accounts attribute flooding both to surface water runoff and an overflowing watercourse where a bridge caused flow backup and water to flow around a wall. (p4) [MUL]
* {general}: Herefordshire Council LLFA identified existing fluvial flood risk in the village (part of the justification for a proposed feasibility study on the Garren Brook). (p5)
* {26 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020, 23 Dec 2020}: Quant: 4 accounts of internal flooding; 1 property flooded in all three reported events; flood depths recorded from less than 2 ft to 4 ft (internal); garden/outbuilding depths up to 5 ft; flood duration persisted up to 2 days (p4) [MUL]


## Topics without coordinates

### Garron Brook
* {26 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020, 23 Dec 2020}: The brook overflowed and caused fluvial (river) flooding into Llangarron properties and surrounding gardens/outbuildings. Flood mechanism descriptions include flow backup at a bridge leading to water flowing around a wall. (p4) [MUL]
* {February 2020, December 2020}: Properties adjacent to the Garren Brook in Llanwarne flooded internally; these properties are adjacent to Flood Zone 3 and a fluvial flow route was identified passing by the property on the southern side. (p5) [PAG, MUL]
* {26 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020, 23 Dec 2020}: Quant: 4 accounts of internal flooding (Llangarron); 1 property flooded in all three reported events; internal flood depths <2 ft to 4 ft; garden/outbuilding depths up to 5 ft; durations up to 2 days (p4) [MUL]

### Gamber Brook
* {26 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020, 23 Dec 2020}: Fluvial flooding from the Gamber Brook caused internal flooding of two properties and a parish church in Llanwarne; flooding episodes occurred during each reported storm and lasted up to 6 hours. The Gamber Brook has been diverted for industrial purposes so when it breaks its banks floodwater can spill away from the channel. (p4) [MUL]
* {February 2020, December 2020}: When the Gamber Brook breaks its banks floodwater can spill away from the watercourse; properties that flooded from the Gamber Brook are located in Flood Zone 3. (p5) [PAG, MUL]
* {26 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020, 23 Dec 2020}: Quant: 2 properties; 1 parish church (p4) [MUL]
