

# Syresham flooding 21 Nov 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Wappenham Road, Syresham, 21st November 2012<br>090\_West Northamptonshire/Syresham - November 2012.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Wappenham Road, Syresham
* {01 Jul 2007 - 31 Jul 2007, 17 Oct 2012, 21 Nov 2012}: Properties and gardens on Wappenham Road were flooded on multiple occasions. On 21/11/2012 a property was internally flooded, the garage of a property was flooded and the gardens of two properties were flooded; measures were taken to prevent internal flooding. Earlier incidents include flooding of a garden (17/10/2012) and three properties flooded (July 2007). (p9) [TAB, MUL]
* Surface water overtopped kerbs on Wappenham Road, flowed through gardens and into garages and properties; at least one property experienced internal flooding and doorways were defended with sandbags. (p10) [PAG]
* Evidence of historic ditching and overgrown highway grip drains in Wappenham Road and possible connections from upstream pipework to the road gully system indicate surface water routes and drainage issues during the event. (p11) [PAG]
* A property located on Wappenham Road was internally flooded due to surface-water ponding on the road. (p12)
* Surface water flow and pooling at the junction with The Hill where groundwater emergence follows the slope; ditch at the junction links into the main watercourse and conveys water from the road area. (p38) [IMG]
* {01 Jul 2007 - 31 Jul 2007, 17 Oct 2012, 21 Nov 2012}: Quant: three properties (July 2007); one property internally flooded (21/11/2012); one garage flooded (21/11/2012); gardens of two properties flooded (21/11/2012); one garden flooded (17/10/2012) (p9) [TAB, MUL]
* Quant: 1 property internally flooded; garage of 1 property flooded; gardens of 2 properties flooded (p10) [PAG]
* Quant: 1 property (p12)

### Syresham
* Intense rainfall on a saturated catchment caused excess surface water to flow over ground following natural contours to low points in the village, resulting in internal flooding of at least one property. The village has experienced internal flooding on more than one occasion in the last five years. (p5) [PAG]
* Intense rainfall on a saturated catchment caused excess surface water to flow over ground to low points in the village, resulting in internal flooding of properties. Internal flooding of at least one property occurred; the same or similar internal flooding has been experienced on more than one occasion within the previous five years. (p6) [PAG]
* At least one property in the village flooded internally on 21 November 2012; various other properties were identified as being at high risk of flooding. Foul-water flooding and associated odours were reported in parts of the village (Abbey Road and High Street). (p8)
* Intense rainfall on a saturated catchment overwhelmed existing drainage. Drainage systems (including historic drains) were silted, partly removed or in poor repair, reducing conveyance capacity. As a result water flowed uncontrolled onto the highway and through private properties. (p17)
* Surface water from higher ground was not collected by historic/remaining drainage (some in poor condition, unknown or removed) and flowed uncontrolled onto the public highway and through private properties in the village, causing flooding of the highway and private properties. (p18)
* Quant: at least one property; more than one occasion in the last 5 years (p5) [PAG]
* Quant: at least 1 property (p6) [PAG]
* Quant: at least one property (p8)

### The Hill, Syresham
* Surface water flowed down The Hill on 21 November 2012; an access track at The Hill conveyed runoff toward Wappenham Road as part of the overland flow routes. (p10)
* Springs and groundwater flowed to the surface at locations on The Hill; water flowed beneath the foundations of a garage (potentially undermining it). The local ditch and pipe system flowed strongly during the flood, moving debris and affecting a property. (p11)
* During the flooding incident there was a blockage in the ditch system to the rear of No.4 The Hill; the blockage was later cleared. (p12)
* Continuous emergence of groundwater on high ground resulting in flowing water down the paved road surface; ditch at this location connects to the main watercourse and a ditch/pipe arrangement has undermined the foundations of a garage on The Hill. (p38) [IMG, PAG]
* A ditch alongside The Hill enters a pipe system at the base of a garage and has eroded/undermined the garage foundations, exposing the footing and creating a risk of structural instability. (p39) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 1 garage; 1 property (p11)

### Wappenham
* Surface-water ponding occurred in multiple locations in the village: ponding in fields at the north that can overtop into a garden on Wappenham Road, ponding in the highway (including at the Wappenham Road bridge area), and ponding on High Street and at the junction of High Street and Main Road. Road gullies failed to accept runoff in several places. (p12) [PAG]
* Quant: 1 property (p12) [PAG]

### High Street, Syresham
* Foul-water flooding and associated odours were reported affecting High Street during the investigated incidents. (p8)
* Ponding in the highway immediately north of Syresham C of E Primary School; surface water rising out of the road at gullies and cracks during heavy rain; ponding reported at the junction of High Street and Main Road to the south of the village. (p12)
* Properties on High Street were affected by surface water runoff and potential emergence of groundwater; the public sewerage system serving High Street was identified for review to assess satisfactory performance in relation to these flood impacts. (p19)
* Groundwater was observed rising through the tarmac carriageway surface and producing wet/darker patches at the road edge adjacent to the primary school; the location is documented as an area prone to flooding and surface water accumulation on the carriageway. (p37) [IMG]

## Topics without coordinates

### Syresham Catchment
* Significant rainfall on 21st November 2012 produced surface water overland flow across the catchment, causing surface water to flow down village streets and collect in a large pond in the highway adjacent to the ordinary watercourse. Surface water flowed from agricultural land down access tracks towards Wappenham Road. Properties on Wappenham Road were affected (internal flooding, garage and gardens flooded). (p10)
* Quant: 1 property internally flooded; garage of 1 property flooded; gardens of 2 properties flooded. (p10)

### Wappenham Road Bridge
* The main area of flooding was adjacent to the ordinary watercourse and the Wappenham Road bridge; ponding filled the full width of Wappenham Road beside the bridge, preventing drainage to the watercourse and contributing to internal flooding of a nearby property on Wappenham Road. (p12)
* Quant: one property (p12)

### Long Meadow Farm
* Ponding in the highway was reported on Wappenham Road to the north of the village on the bend adjacent to Long Meadow Farm. (p12)
