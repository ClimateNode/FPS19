## Place names with coordinates

### Rowan Avenue, Boothville
* Heavy rainfall on impermeable road surfaces overwhelmed drainage so excess surface water flowed overland to low points around private property, resulting in internal flooding of residential properties on Rowan Avenue. (p7) [MUL]
* Two residential properties experienced internal flooding (water entered the buildings). The two properties on Rowan Avenue have also experienced internal flooding on more than one occasion in the previous five years. (p9) [MUL]
* Runoff from the rising road (approx. 5 m drop along the street) and surface water collection contributed to flooding that affected two properties at the bottom of the slope; gullies and a surface water sewer serve the street and are relevant to the flooding. (p10)
* {2014, 2015, 09 Mar 2016, 17 Mar 2016 - 19 Mar 2016, 27 Apr 2016, 11 May 2016}: Repeated surface water flooding on the north side of Rowan Avenue resulted in water flowing across the full width of driveways, onto garden borders, into garages adjoining houses and through to back gardens. Several incidents also note surface water flooding to driveways and garages adjoining houses on the north side of the street. (p11) [TAB, MUL]
* Surface water runoff flowed down both carriageways bypassing blocked/ineffectively located road gullies, across the footpath into front driveways of two properties. Floodwater overwhelmed a private channel drain, entered a garage and flowed through to the back garden. At the second property the floodwater covered the whole driveway and was laden with silt and debris which was deposited across the driveway and into the back garden; the garden drain required repeated clearing. (p12) [PAG]
* Surface water flowed across the whole driveway of the second affected property, laden with silt and debris from surcharging out of an adjacent footway gully; silt deposited across the entire driveway and into the back garden (took hours to clean up). Surface water surged down the road due to the natural gradient and lack of drains, producing a 'tidal wave' effect. Driveways were a few inches deep, which overwhelmed a private drain allowing water to enter and flood garages (garages needed sweeping out). Garden drain required full clearing on each occasion. The runoff problem is long‑standing linked to a gap in the central reservation altering flow paths. (p13) [PAG]
* Rowan Avenue was affected by a flooding incident; the Environment Agency states that Main Rivers are not in the vicinity and were not a factor in this flooding incident. (p17)
* {23 May 2016}: Flood incident investigation photographs show flood-protection measures at a residential property on Rowan Avenue: a channel-type drain and a grated channel/downpipe arrangement at the property, and a row of sandbags placed at the garage threshold to prevent water ingress. The driveway/garage entrance and front of the property were the focus of protection, indicating surface-water threat to the property access and garage. (p36) [IMG, PAG]

### Northamptonshire
* On 9 March 2016 (approx. 30 mm in first 12 hours) a combination of agricultural runoff, surface water runoff and ordinary watercourse flooding across Northamptonshire caused widespread flooding, including internal flooding of properties. (p7)
* Approximately 30 mm of rain fell across the county on 9 March 2016. Over 70 properties were reported to have been flooded internally and there were a total of over 200 reports of flooding during the event, attributed to a combination of agricultural runoff, surface water runoff and ordinary watercourse flooding. (p9) [PAG]
* Across Northamptonshire the event caused widespread flooding from a combination of agricultural runoff, surface water runoff and ordinary watercourse flooding; properties were flooded internally and numerous reports were received. (p10) [PAG]
* {01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015, 01 Feb 2016 - 29 Feb 2016}: Above‑average monthly rainfall in December 2015 and February 2016 led to saturated ground across catchment areas, increasing the likelihood and severity of surface water flooding across the county. (p12) [MUL]

### Anglian Water
* {general}: Public sewer systems can become overwhelmed in extreme weather, resulting in sewer flooding affecting properties drained by those sewers. AWS is the sewerage undertaker responsible for managing risks from surface water, foul water and combined sewer systems. (p16)
