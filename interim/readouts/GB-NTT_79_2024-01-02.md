

# Tollerton flooding 02 Jan 2024

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Tollerton – Storm Henk, January 2024<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/tollerton-s19-storm-henk-jan-2024.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Tollerton
* The village suffered a flood event caused by sustained rainfall over a saturated catchment (surface water flooding) during Storm Henk. (p1)
* Parts of Tollerton experienced surface water flooding during the event centred on Cotgrave Road/Cotgrave Lane; residential properties were flooded as a result of intense, short-duration rainfall on already saturated ground. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* During the rainfall event highway drainage and surface water culverts were overwhelmed, resulting in flooding of properties in the village. Flooding was exacerbated by runoff from surrounding fields and saturated ground following a prolonged period of rainfall. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 5 properties (p2) [IMG, PAG]

### Cotgrave Road, Tollerton
* Surface water flooding from intense rainfall and runoff affected properties on Cotgrave Road. (p2)
* Quant: 5 properties (p2)

### Kingston Brook
* An ordinary watercourse in the area is described as flowing toward Kingston Brook; the report and surface-water mapping show high flood risk along the central watercourse. Kingston Brook (and connected ordinary watercourses) is implicated in the local runoff/flooding dynamics and likely contributed to the surface-water flooding observed during the event. (p3)


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Responded to a Major Incident with significant county-wide flood impacts; verified and recorded instances of internal flooding at residences and businesses; visited affected properties to establish sources and severity; provided guidance and short-term recovery aid via a Community Flood Recovery Grant; collated and published verified flood impact data; chaired and attended Strategic/Tactical/Recovery coordination meetings; provided 24-hour emergency planning support; initiated and led the Section 19 Flood Investigation and led procurement/delivery of the DEFRA Property Flood Resilience Repair Grant Scheme. (p4)
