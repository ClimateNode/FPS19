## Place names with coordinates

### Old Homes Road, Thorpeness
* Identified as the subject location in a Section 19 flood investigation by Suffolk County Council following a flood event. (p1)
* Internal flooding of properties and vehicles: internal flooding of two properties and a car submerged on Old Homes Road. (p2)
* Public highway flooded (road closed); domestic properties internally flooded; a vehicle was submerged by surface water. (p3)
* Surface water and granular material from unbound private tracks entered the surface water drainage system along Old Homes Road during the event, causing blockages and prompting mitigation works (installation of a non-return valve and increased storage capacity); drainage remains reliant on local soakaways, limiting further capacity improvements. (p5) [TAB]
* Quant: internal flooding of two properties; one car submerged (p2)

### Aldringham

### Oaks Court
* Post‑event investigation required because the site's surface water drainage was insufficient during the flood investigation; a topographical/CCTV survey was recommended to verify the built drainage against the approved design. The site is constrained and reliant on local soakaways, limiting potential to increase drainage capacity. (p5) [TAB]
