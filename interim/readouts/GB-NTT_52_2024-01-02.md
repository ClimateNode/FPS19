

# Radcliffe On Trent flooding 02 Jan 2024

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Radcliffe On Trent – Storm Henk, January 2024<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/radcliffe-on-trent-s19-storm-henk-jan-2024.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Radcliffe On Trent
* Radcliffe on Trent experienced a flood event caused by sustained rainfall over a saturated catchment during Storm Henk. (p1)
* Localized flooding in Radcliffe on Trent: the Clumber Drive area experienced property inundation associated with the Sykes Drain ordinary watercourse. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* {1977, 2020, 02 Jan 2024}: The area of Radcliffe on Trent along the line of Sykes Drain is shown at risk on Environment Agency surface water maps and has suffered flooding events in 1977 and 2020; during the Storm Babet event the volume of water caused flooding along the drain and into adjacent parts of the village via the culverted route and low‑lying areas. (p3) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
* Radcliffe on Trent was implicated in the flood event — residents were met to discuss flood risk and community recovery actions (community flood signage, local flood action group) are being developed; NCC are planning Property Flood Resilience installations and additional flood water storage works relevant to the area. (p5)
* Quant: 12 properties (Clumber Drive) (p2) [IMG, PAG]

### Rushcliffe
* Communities across Rushcliffe were affected by the flooding; emergency response support was provided and sandbags were delivered to affected residents, with ongoing recovery management and liaison on site. (p5)

### Clumber Drive, Radcliffe On Trent
* Properties on Clumber Drive were inundated where the road lies along the Sykes Drain (an ordinary watercourse that flows into the village via a largely culverted route). (p2) [IMG]
* {1977, 2020, 02 Jan 2024}: Floodwater flowed down Clumber Drive after Sykes Drain surcharged the trash screen; the street was part of the flow path carrying water towards Penrith Avenue. The area has also experienced past flooding (1977 and 2020), with 2020 linked to a trash‑screen blockage behind 87 Clumber Drive. (p3) [PAG, MUL]
* Quant: 12 properties (figure caption also lists 11) (p2) [IMG]

### Penrith Avenue, Radcliffe On Trent
* Floodwater from Sykes Drain flowed down Clumber Drive towards Penrith Avenue, indicating Penrith Avenue was affected by the downstream flow of surface water during the event. (p3)

### Grantham Road, Radcliffe On Trent
* {general}: Shown on the Environment Agency surface water flood risk map as intersecting surface water flood extent. The map analysis notes roads and other infrastructure intersect the flood risk zones, indicating Grantham Road is vulnerable to surface water inundation where it crosses or lies adjacent to the mapped waterway. (p3) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Played a central role in the response to a Major Incident affecting the county: initiated a Community Flood Recovery Grant to support individuals affected by internal flooding; attended sites after flooding occurred to verify the scale of internal flooding and provide support, guidance and advice to affected residents and businesses; collated and published verified flood impact data; visited flood-affected properties to establish sources/mechanisms and severity of flooding; chaired and attended Strategic/Tactical/Recovery Coordination Group meetings; initiated and led the Section 19 Flood Investigation; led procurement and delivery of the DEFRA Property Flood Resilience Repair Grant Scheme; provided continuous (24-hour) support through the Emergency Planning team; supported district and borough partners by sharing resources. (p4)
* Quant: 24-hour support (p4)

### Sykes Drain
* {1977, 2020, 02 Jan 2024}: Sykes Drain surcharged at the trash screen located behind the boundary of 87 Clumber Drive; the surcharged water then followed the natural topography and flowed from the drain towards Clumber Drive and onward to Penrith Avenue, contributing to localised flooding. (p3) [MUL]
