### Hailey
* Surface water flooding on 23rd December 2020 internally flooded residential properties in Delly End (part of Hailey). Flood defences at properties that had them were overwhelmed within minutes and flood levels rose higher than in 2007. (p2)
* {01 Dec 2020 - 31 Dec 2020}: The wider Hailey catchment is affected by elevated groundwater/perched water tables and increased surface runoff from recent land drainage and ploughing, releasing trapped water that contributes extra volume and pressure on the drainage system and downstream flooding (including Delly End). Community working group actions focus on reducing this flood risk. (p3) [TAB]
* {01 Jul 2007 - 31 Jul 2007}: Surface water run-off from surrounding agricultural land caused highway flooding that cut off access routes to Hailey village; highway drainage was insufficient to convey run-off. Floodwater collected in the local low spot at Delly Pool making the road impassable during the July 2007 event. (p9) [PAG]
* {01 Jul 2007 - 31 Jul 2007}: Highway (B4022) flooding affected properties in the village; roadway flooding contributed to property impacts and prompted at least one Grant Aid claim. (p10) [PAG]
* Intense rainfall on 23/12/2020 reportedly overwhelmed the drainage system in Delly End (within Hailey), causing flooding to a greater extent; local land drainage/ploughing associated with a proposed solar farm was anecdotally reported as a possible contributing factor. (p13)
* The Hailey community experienced flooding issues and has an active working group that identified and progressed multiple actions (highway attenuation at the Triangle on Whitings Lane, upstream attenuation at Singe Wood and Solar Farm land, and arranging regular maintenance with OCC and landowners) to minimise flooding in the village and to benefit Delly End. (p19)
* Hailey experienced flooding that prompted an active local working group to progress mitigation measures. Actions identified and progressed to address the flooding (and to benefit Delly End) include creating a highway attenuation area at the Triangle on Whitings Lane, modelling existing drainage systems and catchments, providing upstream attenuation at Single Wood and on land proposed for a solar farm, and carrying out regular maintenance of drainage systems by OCC and landowners. (p20) [PAG]
* Overland surface water and overwhelmed highway drainage entered the village during the event, causing flooding issues within Hailey and prompting recommendations to increase cleansing/maintenance of highway drainage assets. (p25) [TAB]
