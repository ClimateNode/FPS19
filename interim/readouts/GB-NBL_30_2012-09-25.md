

# Netherton flooding 25 Sep 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Netherton<br>091\_Northumberland/Netherton-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Netherton
* {01 Sep 2008 - 30 Sep 2008, 25 Sep 2012}: Prolonged rainfall raised the Netherton Burn and on 25/09/2012 the culvert adjacent to Netherton Northside First School reached capacity. Excess runoff flowed eastwards along the road through the village; two residential properties adjacent to the road were flooded and one road was affected. The culvert also backed up in September 2008, when overflow caused flooding to 11 properties in the village. (p1) [IMG, MUL]
* {01 Sep 2008 - 30 Sep 2008, 25 Sep 2012}: Quant: 2 residential properties; 1 road; 11 properties (September 2008) (p1) [IMG, MUL]

### Netherton Northside First School
* {01 Sep 2008 - 30 Sep 2008, 25 Sep 2012}: The culvert adjacent to the school reached capacity on 25/09/2012, contributing to overflow/runoff along the road through the village. The same culvert backed up in September 2008 and was implicated in that event's overflow. (p1) [IMG, MUL]


## Topics without coordinates

### Netherton Burn
* Prolonged rainfall raised the water level in the burn. On 25/09/2012 the culvert adjacent to Netherton Northside First School reached capacity and excess runoff flowed eastwards along the road, flooding two residential properties and inundating the road. Beany blocks on the southern edge of the road were reported to be backing up, preventing runoff on the road from draining back into the burn. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 2 residential properties; 1 road (p1) [IMG]
