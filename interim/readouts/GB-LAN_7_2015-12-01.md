### Lancashire
* Lancashire was hit by significant flooding, in particular the December 2015 event that prompted review of communication and the development of district-level flood reports for affected communities. (p3) [PAG]
* The December 2015 flood event caused widespread flooding across the county, with extensive impacts on homes and businesses. (p8) [TAB]
* Extreme and unprecedented storms and rainfall of November and December 2015 caused flooding throughout December 2015 affecting the county. Over 2,500 properties were flooded and the floods damaged private gardens, garages and outbuildings, business premises, agricultural property and critical public infrastructure including roads, bridges and retaining walls, water treatment plants, power and communications installations, and community buildings such as schools, village and town halls. (p9)
* Multiple communities across the county experienced flooding during two major storm events in December 2015, prompting a countywide Section 19 investigation to identify affected communities and recommend prioritised actions; stage two reports will track progress on actions. (p10) [PAG]
* Quant: over 2,500 properties flooded (p8) [TAB]
* Quant: over 2,500 properties (p9)
