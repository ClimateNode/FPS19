## Place names with coordinates

### London Borough of Barnet
* On 12 July 2021 heavy rainfall caused widespread flooding across the London Borough of Barnet. The borough received 40 reports of flooding (17 internal, 4 external and 19 highway). The evidence indicates the sewer network became overwhelmed due to lack of capacity and high surface-water runoff, causing internal, external and highway flooding. High water levels in the Mutton Brook reduced discharge from surface water sewers and further impacted sewer capacity. (p3) [PAG]
* The borough experienced multiple flood incidents on 12th July 2021, including culvert/trash-screen issues on Shirebourne Brook (Chipping Barnet), surface-water accumulation at King George's Fields (Chipping Barnet), surface-water/sewer flooding issues around Hertford Road and Oakview Gardens (East Finchley), drainage failures affecting Bute Mews and Kingsley Close (Mutton Brook corridor), surface-water flow-path issues on Wildwood Road (Hampstead Garden Suburb), and storage/capacity concerns on Decoy Brook/Hampstead Heath (Golders Green). (p4) [PAG]

* Intense rainfall on 12 July 2021 caused widespread surface water and sewer-related urban flooding across the borough, disrupting roads and causing internal flooding to properties in multiple locations. (p16) [PAG]
