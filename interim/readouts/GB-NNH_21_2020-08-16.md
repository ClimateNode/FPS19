### Fields View, Wellingborough
* Location was the subject of a flood investigation by North Northamptonshire Council into a flooding incident affecting the area. (p2)
* Excess surface water from intense, short-duration rainfall flowed over ground to low points and entered buildings on Fields View, causing internal flooding of properties. (p6)
* Internal flooding of properties on Fields View during the flood incident; part of a single event where internal flooding of more than five buildings in close proximity was experienced, triggering a formal investigation. (p8)

### Wellingborough
* Parts of Wellingborough (town centre area) experienced flooding during the rainfall and flooding event; public foul and surface water sewers exist throughout the town centre and overland surface water flow from exceedance higher in the catchment is described as a mechanism affecting the area. (p14)
* {01 Sep 2019 - 30 Sep 2019}: A flood event in September 2019 resulted in internal flooding of properties in the town (specifically on Castle Street). (p16)
* Various areas of Wellingborough were affected by flooding; Fire and Rescue received emergency calls from affected areas and attended properties and external areas where relatively deep flood water was held and was pumped to safe areas. Follow-up teams cleared debris, street surfaces and delivered sandbags to some properties. (p18)
* Area experienced surface water flooding caused by intense short-duration rainfall. Surface water drainage systems across the catchment were unable to convey the rain; cumulative flows lower in the catchment were deep and fast-moving, occupying the full width of roads and footways. Surface water flowed to low points that have no effective overland relief, with reliance on buried drainage systems; some localised drainage points required maintenance. Properties in the area were recorded as being affected and details of affected properties were shared with the LLFA. (p19)
* Wellingborough is identified as part of a community resilience project because communities there are at risk of surface water flooding; the risk/flood incident prompted recommendations for drainage inspection, maintenance and community preparedness measures. (p23)
* {01 Sep 2019 - 30 Sep 2019}: Quant: Five properties (p16)

### Midland Road, Wellingborough
* {2010 to 2020}: Surface water sewer regularly flooded from a manhole at Midland Road adjacent to the Fields View junction. (p11) [TAB]
* {general}: Road lies within the mapped flood risk zones adjacent to the waterway; shown as being at risk of inundation with highest risk close to the watercourse which could affect access along the road. (p12) [IMG]
* Strong surface water flows moved south along Midland Road onto Castle Street and Fields View after drainage systems were exceeded; the road acted as a conduit increasing flow velocity and reducing the effectiveness of drainage. (p17)

### Swanspool Brook
* Flows were directed toward Swanspool Brook but were restricted from entering the brook at the residential block perimeter, causing water to deepen locally and enter properties; elsewhere a strong flow from a car park discharged into Swanspool Brook. (p17)
* Excess surface water flowed downhill toward Swanspool Brook; low points in the catchment had no effective overland flow routes to divert excess surface water to Swanspool Brook, resulting in reliance on buried drainage systems which were overwhelmed during the event. (p19)
