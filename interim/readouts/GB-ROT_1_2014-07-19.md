### Rotherham Metropolitan District
* On 19 July 2014 localised, intense short-duration storms produced surface-water/urban flooding across the borough. Internal flooding was reported in South Anston, North Anston, Dinnington and Hellaby (southeast of the borough). Roads and gardens were also flooded throughout the borough, with incidents concentrated in the south east. A relatively small number of properties were affected; many had experienced similar flooding previously. Affected properties were in discrete, hydraulically independent catchments. (p5) [IMG]
* Multiple locations within the borough experienced flooding when highway drainage systems were overwhelmed by high-intensity rain, causing water to flow from highways toward residential properties. Properties experienced peripheral flooding (e.g. inside toilets) and some internal flooding incidents were recorded; overland flood routes around properties were blocked by extensions, outbuildings, walls and raised driveways. (p8)
