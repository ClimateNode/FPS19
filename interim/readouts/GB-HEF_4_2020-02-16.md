

# Hereford flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Hereford (Racecourse and Widemarsh Brook)<br>055\_Herefordshire County/03\_Hereford\_Race\_City\_Led.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Hereford Racecourse
* {general}: The eastern boundary of Hereford Race Course is highlighted as the main flood site in the investigation (red oval) and lies adjacent to Ayles Brook and low elevation contours; this area is mapped as high flood risk (Flood Zone 3) and is susceptible to river/fluvial flooding. (p1) [IMG]
* Properties at the racecourse area experienced internal flooding during the event; flooding depths reported between 1 ft and 5 ft and duration between 1 and 2 days. (p2) [PAG]
* The Ayles Brook culvert beneath the racecourse had limited capacity so water that could not be conveyed became overland flow across the racecourse area, contributing to local surface water flooding. (p3)
* Quant: 23 properties (p2) [PAG]

### Ayles Brook
* {general}: Ayles Brook is identified on the map as the nearby watercourse contributing to flood risk; its proximity to the eastern edge of the Race Course and lower elevation contours indicates it is a likely source of fluvial flooding affecting the racecourse and adjacent urban area. (p1) [IMG]
* Ayles Brook was identified as the source of fluvial flooding that contributed to internal flooding of properties in the 'Racecourse to Ledbury Road' area of Hereford. The brook drains into Widemarsh Brook and is culverted under the Racecourse and the A49; its flooding contributed to internal property flooding at depths reported from 1 ft up to 5 ft, with flood durations of about 1–2 days. Six respondents recorded outbuildings flooded to depths less than 6 inches. (p2) [PAG]
* Identified as the source of fluvial flooding for properties in Flood Zones 2 and 3. The Ayles Brook is culverted under Hereford Racecourse; the culvert has limited capacity and could not convey water beneath the A49, causing water to become overland flow and contribute to surface water flooding in the racecourse area and nearby streets. (p3) [PAG]
* Identified as a location requiring a feasibility study because of existing fluvial flood risk to properties; DEFRA grant funding bids are based on flood risk in this area. (p4)
* Quant: 34 properties; 6 outbuildings; depths of 1 ft up to 5 ft; duration 1–2 days (p2) [PAG]

### Hereford
* Areas of central Hereford — specifically the racecourse and the Widmarsh area — are shown as being within Flood Zone 3 (high probability of fluvial flooding). Proximity to Ayles Brook and low elevation contours (circa 56–60m) indicate susceptibility to river (fluvial) flooding; parts of the urban area benefit from flood defences but key infrastructure and built-up areas lie within the high-risk zone. (p1) [IMG]
* The Racecourse to Ledbury Road area of Hereford experienced fluvial and surface water flooding, resulting in internal flooding of residential properties; critical infrastructure impact included Holmer C of E Academy Primary School. (p2) [TAB, PAG]
* Central Hereford experienced fluvial flooding from the River Wye (backwater causing Widemarsh Brook to burst banks at Edgar Street Grid and Ledbury Road) and surface-water flooding where the Ayles Brook culvert and local drainage were overwhelmed (notably around Hereford Racecourse and Roman Road). Holmer C of E Academy Primary School is recorded as having flooded during February 2020. (p3) [PAG]
* {general}: Central Hereford experienced fluvial flood risk that drove the construction of a Flood Alleviation Scheme on Yazor Brook to reduce flood risk to properties and enable redevelopment of the Edgar Street Grid area. (p4)
* Quant: 34 properties; 1 school (Holmer C of E Academy Primary School) identified as affected critical infrastructure (p2) [TAB, PAG]

### Holmer Church of England Academy
* {general}: Labelled on the Widmarsh map inside/near Flood Zone 3, indicating significant fluvial flood risk to the school site. This suggests potential for inundation of school grounds and buildings during a river flooding event; the map does not specify whether the school is protected by defences. (p1) [IMG]
* Holmer C of E Academy Primary School is listed as critical infrastructure affected in the key flood event; no injuries or loss of life were reported. (p2) [TAB]
* The school was recorded as flooded during the February 2020 event; it has flooded on multiple occasions. No injuries or loss of life were reported. (p3)
* Quant: no injuries or loss of life (p3)

### Holmer
* {general}: Holmer area has ongoing surface water flooding issues related to altered drainage and a tributary diversion upstream of Holmer West; these issues contribute to local overland flows and flooding (including impacts on the A4103 Roman Road). (p3) [PAG]

### Edgar Street, Hereford
* Edgar Street properties experienced internal fluvial flooding during the key event; properties in the Racecourse to Ledbury Road area (including Edgar Street) were flooded to depths of about 1 ft up to 5 ft with durations of 1–2 days. (p2)
* Widemarsh Brook burst its banks (partly due to backwater from the River Wye), causing fluvial flooding across the Edgar Street grid area; properties in Flood Zones 2 and 3 reported flooding. (p3)
* {general}: The Edgar Street Grid area was identified as being subject to existing fluvial flood risk to properties; flood risk reduction (including the Yazor Brook scheme and proposed feasibility studies) was promoted to enable redevelopment. (p4) [PAG]
* Quant: 7 properties (p2)

### Ledbury Road, Hereford
* Part of the area that experienced fluvial flooding during the event; properties were reported internally flooded as part of combined Ledbury Road and Eign Road impacts. (p2)
* Affected by fluvial flooding when the Widemarsh Brook burst its banks due to backwater from the River Wye. (p3)
* Quant: 4 properties (combined Ledbury Road and Eign Road) (p2)

### Eign Road, Hereford
* Eign Road (grouped with Ledbury Road) was affected by fluvial internal flooding as part of the Racecourse to Ledbury Road area during the key event. (p2)
* Quant: 4 properties (Ledbury Road and Eign Road combined) (p2)

### Grandstand Road, Hereford
* {general}: Grandstand Road is shown within the Widmarsh map area that is largely Flood Zone 3 (high flood risk) with the main river nearby; the road is therefore at risk of inundation from river/fluvial flooding (some sections may benefit from defences as indicated). (p1) [IMG]

### Newtown Road, Hereford
* {general}: Mapped within Flood Zone 3 adjacent to the main river through Widmarsh — therefore at high risk of fluvial (river) flooding. The map indicates potential for inundation of the road and adjoining properties; some nearby areas are shown as benefiting from flood defences but it is not clear from the map whether Newtown Road itself is defended. (p1) [IMG]

### Priory Place, Hereford
* {general}: Shown among the major roads on the Widmarsh map which is largely covered by Flood Zone 3; therefore Priory Place is likely within or adjacent to high fluvial flood-risk areas and liable to inundation in a river flood event. The map does not make clear whether Priory Place itself benefits from flood defences. (p1) [IMG]

### Roman Road, Hereford
* {general}: A4103 Roman Road experiences surface water flooding issues linked to upstream drainage alterations (a tributary previously diverted into a highway drain), contributing to local flooding. (p3)

### Widemarsh Brook
* Widemarsh Brook is identified in the catchment context (Ayles Brook drains into Widemarsh Brook) and was part of the river system involved in the February 16th 2020 fluvial flooding in the Racecourse to Ledbury Road area. (p2) [PAG]
* Burst its banks (fluvial flooding) in the Edgar Street Grid area due in part to backwater effects from the River Wye, causing flooding that affected streets including Ledbury Road. (p3)

### Old Wye Bridge
* The River Wye reached its highest recorded level at the Old Wye Bridge during the February 2020 event, indicating major fluvial flood conditions at this central Hereford location. (p3)

### Yazor Brook
* {general}: A flood alleviation scheme was constructed on Yazor Brook at Credenhill (functional since 2012) which diverts flows into a 1.4 km culvert discharging to the River Wye; the scheme was implemented as a strategic solution to flood risk in central Hereford. (p4)


## Topics without coordinates

### River Wye
* Identified as a main river in the catchment (with Widemarsh Brook) implicated in fluvial flooding that affected the Racecourse to Ledbury Road area of Hereford. (p2)
* High River Wye levels produced backwater that caused the Widemarsh Brook to burst its banks, contributing to fluvial flooding in central Hereford (including Edgar Street Grid and Ledbury Road). (p3)
* {general}: Receives discharge from the Yazor Brook culvert at the Old Weir as part of the flood alleviation scheme intended to reduce flood risk in central Hereford. (p4)
* Quant: 34 properties (within the affected area) (p2)

### Environment Agency
* {general}: Invited bids for DEFRA funding and has raised a bid for grant funding on the basis of existing fluvial flood risk to properties to support delivery of Flood Risk Management projects. (p4)

### Old Weir
* {general}: Acts as the discharge point on the River Wye for the 1.4 km culvert carrying flows from the Yazor Brook as part of the flood alleviation scheme aimed at reducing central Hereford flood risk. (p4)
