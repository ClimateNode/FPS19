## Place names with coordinates

### Victoria Road, Southend-On-Sea Unitary Authority
* Victoria Road area experienced flooding during the flood event on 20th October 2021 (subject of this investigation). (p1)
* Victoria Road and its immediate junctions with Manilla Road, Northumberland Crescent, Jetty Mews, Retort Close and Shaftesbury Avenue were within the area affected by the 20 October 2021 flood event and were investigated for flooding impacts (roadway and adjacent properties impacted). (p4) [PAG]
* {01 Aug 2013 - 31 Aug 2013, 01 Sep 2014 - 30 Sep 2014}: Significant flooding in the Victoria Road area caused internal (property) flooding. Multiple properties in the Victoria Road / study area experienced internal flooding; the event contributed to wider city-wide internal flooding during the same events. (p7) [PAG, MUL]
* {24 Aug 2013, 19 Sep 2014, 20 Oct 2021}: Internal flooding to properties (including at least one property during the 20 October 2021 event). Widespread flooding of carriageways and footways gathering at the local low point between Collier Way and Shaftesbury Avenue. Floodwaters reached the localised low point and deposited contaminated silt which persisted for several days until cleared by SCC and Anglian Water. Flooding persisted for approximately 2–3 hours and receded by around 02:00 on 21 October 2021. (p8) [TAB, PAG, MUL]
* Surface water from higher ground flowed along road surfaces to Victoria Road; flows mixed with foul effluent surcharging from the Manilla Road area, moved south along Victoria Road and accumulated at the localised low point between Collier Way and Shaftesbury Avenue, impacting areas along Victoria Road. (p9)
* Surface water from surrounding higher ground flowed along road surfaces toward Victoria Road. Surface water mixed with foul effluent surcharged from the Manilla Road area, flowed east to Victoria Road and then south, affecting stretches of Victoria Road and junctions at Northumberland Crescent, Collier Way, Jetty Mews and Shaftesbury Avenue. Flows collected at the local low point on Victoria Road between Collier Way and Shaftesbury Avenue, producing the mapped flood extent and flow routes. (p10) [PAG, IMG, TAB]
* Floodwater covered the road surface at the Victoria Road and Collier Way junction, reaching up to vehicle wheel wells and partially submerging parked cars; floating debris and stagnant water indicate impaired drainage at the street. (p15) [IMG, PAG]
* Nighttime flooding at the Victoria Road and Collier Way junction with water covering the carriageway, reaching up to parked vehicles and encroaching on the bases/entrances of terraced houses; disruption to residential access and risk of property inundation. (p16) [IMG, PAG]
* Street-level flooding at the Victoria Road (including the junction with Collier Way) that partially submerged parked vehicles; floodwater reached above the bottom of car doors and above wheel rims, creating hazards to vehicles and contributing to post‑flood debris on the street. (p17) [IMG, PAG]
* Post-flood deposition of mud, silt and debris across the carriageway and pavements; visible surface damage to road and footway requiring clean-up and likely repairs; obstruction of the highway in a residential terraced street. (p19) [IMG]
* {01 Aug 2013 - 31 Aug 2013, 01 Sep 2014 - 30 Sep 2014}: Quant: 8 properties (internal flooding in the study area); 255 properties (city-wide internal flooding, August 2013); 61 properties (city-wide internal flooding, September 2014) (p7) [PAG, MUL]
* {24 Aug 2013, 19 Sep 2014, 20 Oct 2021}: Quant: at least 1 property; water levels approximately 200-250 mm at the low point; flooding persisted for 2-3 hours; contaminated silt deposits remained for several days (p8) [TAB, PAG, MUL]

### Southend-On-Sea Unitary Authority
* Southend-on-Sea experienced a flood event on 20th October 2021 that affected the Victoria Road area (report subject). (p1)
* Repeated internal property flooding occurred within the area surrounding Victoria Road between Manilla Road and Shaftesbury Avenue. (p3) [PAG]
* The report identifies that the properties impacted by the investigated flooding event are located entirely within the City of Southend-on-Sea; the low-lying area around Victoria Road and surrounding junctions was affected by surface water/urban flooding on 20th October 2021. (p4) [IMG]
* {24 Aug 2013, 19 Sep 2014, 20 Oct 2021}: Victoria Road area (City of Southend) experienced surface water flows and sewer surcharging on 20th October 2021: foul-contaminated flows discharged from Manilla Road east to the junction of Victoria Road and Shaftesbury Avenue, pooling at the low point between Collier Way and Shaftesbury Avenue. Water depths reached approximately 200–250 mm; flooding persisted ~2–3 hours (receding by ~02:00 on 21st October). Significant contaminated silt deposits remained for several days and were cleared by SCC and Anglian Water. Historical SCC LFRMS records also show internal flooding in the Victoria Road area on 24th August 2013 and 19th September 2014. (p8) [MUL]
* The Victoria Road area of Southend-on-Sea experienced intense rainfall on 20 October 2021 that overwhelmed highway drainage and sewer systems, causing surface water runoff and sewer surcharging that impacted streets and properties in the local area. (p10) [PAG, IMG, TAB]
* Urban/ surface water flooding affected streets in Southend-on-Sea, including Victoria Road, with vehicles partially submerged and evidence of post‑flood debris and disruption to local streets. (p17) [IMG]
* The Victoria Road area of Southend-on-Sea experienced surface water/urban flooding with mud, silt and debris deposited along residential streets and pavements, as documented in post-flood photographs. (p19) [IMG]
* {24 Aug 2013, 19 Sep 2014, 20 Oct 2021}: Quant: 8 properties; 2 properties; at least 1 property (p8) [MUL]

### Manilla Road, Southend-On-Sea Unitary Authority
* The junction of Manilla Road with Victoria Road was within the immediate area affected by flooding on 20 October 2021 and forms part of the investigated impact area. (p4)
* Surface water from surrounding higher ground flowed into Manilla Road following intense rainfall and foul effluent surcharged from the Manilla Road area; these contaminated flows moved east to the junction with Victoria Road and contributed to flooding of streets and properties. (p8) [PAG]
* Surface water mixed with foul effluent started surcharging from the Manilla Road area and flowed east toward Victoria Road, initiating downstream inundation along Victoria Road and adjacent junctions. (p9)
* The Manilla Road storm tank was identified as a feature whose contribution to the flooding on 20th October 2021 needs confirming; its suspected role prompted recommendations to defer capital works until its influence on the event is established. (p13) [TAB]

### Shaftesbury Avenue, Southend-On-Sea Unitary Authority
* The junction of Shaftesbury Avenue with Victoria Road was included in the immediate area impacted by the 20 October 2021 flooding and investigated for impacts to properties and highway. (p4)
* Impacted by eastward flows that gathered at the local low point on Victoria Road between Collier Way and Shaftesbury Avenue; carriageway and footway flooding occurred and contributed to localised water depths and contaminated silt deposition. (p8) [PAG]
* Surface water flowed from higher ground and foul effluent surcharged from the Manilla Road area, flowing to Victoria Road and impacting areas around the Shaftesbury Avenue junction; flows collected at the low point on Victoria Road between Collier Way and Shaftesbury Avenue, producing localised flooding at the junction. (p9)
* Quant: water levels approximately 200-250 mm at the low point; flooding persisted for 2-3 hours (p8) [PAG]

### Northumberland Crescent, Southend-On-Sea Unitary Authority
* The junction of Northumberland Crescent with Victoria Road falls within the investigation area and was affected by the 20 October 2021 flood event as part of the immediate impacted area. (p4)
* Areas around Northumberland Crescent were affected by flows that moved from Victoria Road (fed by Manilla Road surcharges), causing surface water inundation around the junctions. (p9)

### Jetty Mews, Southend-On-Sea Unitary Authority
* The junction of Jetty Mews with Victoria Road is within the immediate investigation area and was affected by the flooding on 20 October 2021 as part of the impacted local area. (p4)
* Surface water from surrounding higher land flowed along road surfaces and mixed with foul effluent surcharging from the Manilla Road area; these flows moved east to Victoria Road then south, affecting the area around the Jetty Mews junction with Victoria Road and causing localised flooding and contamination of road surfaces. (p9)

### Retort Close, Southend-On-Sea Unitary Authority
* Retort Close was included in the immediate area subject to the Section 19 flood investigation for the event on 20th October 2021. The street is a local low point within the investigation area and highway drainage (noted to be at higher density in Retort Close) was relevant to the surface water/urban flooding that affected the highway and nearby properties. (p4) [PAG]

### Southend Sewage Treatment Works

### Southend-On-Sea Council
* {20 Oct 2021, 21 Oct 2021, 01 Nov 2021 - 30 Nov 2021}: Conducted road sweeping to clear debris from carriageways and footways, and checked and cleansed highway drainage (including gully cleansing) as part of recovery and to restore drainage following the flood. (p12) [TAB, MUL]
* Southend-on-Sea Council led recommendations and response actions after the 20th October 2021 flooding, including proposing joint investigations with Anglian Water, considering capital drainage/SuDS improvements, implementing gully cleansing/jetting as part of maintenance, promoting riparian ownership, and exploring Property Flood Resilience measures and funding. (p13) [TAB]

### Environment Agency
