

# Oldham Metropolitan District flooding 13 Sep 2016

Reference document:<br>13th September 2016 Flood Investigation Report<br>094\_Oldham/S19\_13\_September\_2016\_Rev\_1.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Oldham Metropolitan District
* Widespread flooding across the borough caused by intense rainfall and interacting flooding mechanisms. Homes, businesses, local infrastructure and the environment were affected; local drainage systems were overwhelmed and river levels prevented drainage discharge. (p4)
* Parts of the borough (steep catchments and narrow river valleys) experience rapid runoff that sends large volumes of floodwater quickly through confined river systems causing flash flooding; urban drainage capacity is problematic so high rainfall and debris/leaf litter can prevent drainage and exacerbate surface water and urban flooding. (p10)
* Affected by the 13th September 2016 intense downpour event described in the report, with flash-flooding and high surface runoff identified as the mechanism of flooding for the area. (p12) [IMG]
* Heavy downpours produced intense, localized rainfall and flash flooding that generated high surface runoff across the borough. Twelve of the twenty borough wards reported flooding incidents; the south‑west parts of the borough (Failsworth East and Hollinwood Wards) were the worst affected and reported the most incidents. (p13) [IMG, PAG]
* Borough-wide intense rainfall in September 2016 caused surface water and flash flooding across Oldham; sewer hydraulic capacity was exceeded in multiple locations across the borough, causing localised highway flooding and flooding of residential properties (particularly in Failsworth). (p14) [PAG]
* Internal property flooding from surface water. (p15) [TAB, PAG]
* Quant: 79 incidents of internal property flooding; 34 incidents of flood water entering gardens and/or outbuildings (p4)
* Quant: 12 borough wards; 2 wards (Failsworth East and Hollinwood) reported the most incidents (p13) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 1 property flooded internally from surface water. (p15) [TAB, PAG]

### Grasmere Road, Oldham Metropolitan District
* During a short period of intense rainfall on 13 September 2016, water flowing from a farmer's field overspilled and entered rear gardens of properties on Grasmere Road. Contributing factors included a culvert and the hydraulic incapacity of the existing drainage system. Flood water entered the habitable space of properties and gardens/outbuildings at this location. (p24) [IMG]
* During a short period of intense rainfall on 13th September 2016, water flowing from a farmer's field overspilled and entered the rear gardens of properties on Grasmere Road, causing properties to flood. Culvert subsidence and the hydraulic incapacity of the existing drainage system contributed to upstream surcharging and the entry of water into properties. (p25) [PAG]
* Post‑event mitigation works were carried out on land at the rear of properties: a 150mm perforated pipe and a bund were installed to direct water to a local brook to greatly reduce the risk of surface water entering properties on Grasmere Road. (p26) [IMG, PAG]
* Grasmere Road in Royton is identified as a high priority location for a business case and funding to establish project options to address flooding following the flood event. (p32)
* Quant: 3 properties; 18 properties (p24) [IMG]
