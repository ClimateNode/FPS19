

# Eastwood flooding 17 Sep 2023 - 18 Sep 2023

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Eastwood – 18 September 2023<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/eastwood-s19-september-2023.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Greenhills Road, Eastwood
* Floodwater that breached from the development site entered the highway and then travelled between properties, affecting residential properties on Greenhills Road (internal flooding to some properties and external damage to gardens, sheds, garages and outbuildings in the area). (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Residential properties on Greenhills Road were affected when water that breached the bund on the development site entered the highway and then travelled between properties, impacting further residences. (p3)
* Council liaised with affected residents on Greenhills Road following the flooding. (p4)
* Quant: 5 residential properties internal flooding; 9 properties external damage (gardens, sheds, garages, outbuildings) (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 9 properties (additional properties in the area suffered external damage to gardens, sheds, garages and outbuildings) — area impact also affected Garden Road (p3)

### Garden Road, Eastwood
* Properties on Garden Road suffered flooding from the rear due to the direct breach of the earthen bund on the adjacent development site; this released water then entered the highway and propagated to nearby properties. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Properties on Garden Road suffered flooding from the rear caused by a direct breach of the bund on the adjacent development site; floodwater then entered the public highway and travelled between properties, affecting nearby residences. (p3)
* Debris from the flooding was cleared from Garden Road and its pavements; residents were liaised with by the council. (p4)
* Quant: part of the 5 residential properties that suffered internal flooding; contributed to the wider 9 properties with external damage (gardens, sheds, garages, outbuildings) (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 9 properties (additional properties in the area suffered external damage to gardens, sheds, garages and outbuildings) — area impact also affected Greenhills Road (p3)

### Nottinghamshire
* Heavy localised rainfall on the evening of 17 September 2023 caused internal flooding of residential properties and external flooding of gardens and roads in the Eastwood area. (p1)
* Quant: 5 residential properties (p1)

### Eastwood
* Heavy localised rainfall (evening of 17 September 2023) caused internal flooding to residential properties and external flooding to gardens and roads in the Eastwood area. (p1)
* Quant: 5 residential properties (p1)

### Breach Lane, Eastwood
* Council unblocked a drain on Beach Lane (sic) as part of the flood response; residents on the street were liaised with. (p4)


## Topics without coordinates

### Broxtowe Borough Council
* Responded to residents affected by the flooding: cleared debris from Garden Road and pavements; unblocked a drain on Beach Lane; assisted an elderly resident to clear his drive; liaised with residents on Garden Road, Beach Lane and Greenhills Road; distributed sandbags; released communications; Planning Enforcement attended and has since visited repeatedly to monitor and check for planning breaches. (p4)

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Attended the site immediately after flooding; initiated and led the Section 19 flood investigation; carried out on-site investigations; liaised with affected residents and the development site owner; undertook follow-up site visits. (p4)
