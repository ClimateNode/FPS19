

# Burscough flooding 01 Nov 2020 - 30 Nov 2020

Reference document:<br>November 2020 Flooding In Lancashire<br>069\_Lancashire County/november-2020-flooding-warpers-moss-lane-burscough.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Lancashire
* Lancashire experienced flooding during the November 2020 flood event. (p1)
* Localised flooding occurred due to an overwhelmed surface water drainage system and capacity constraints/obstructions on local watercourses; the report documents repeated highway flooding on Warpers Moss Lane (2019–2020) and flooding of a home on 24 November 2020. (p4)
* Individual lengths of ditched watercourses in Burscough had fallen into disrepair, obstructing highway drainage outfalls. Under heavy rainfall the road flooded and a local house flooded on multiple occasions. The ditched watercourses were later cleared by the responsible parties and there has been no repeat of the flooding since. (p6) [PAG]
* Quant: 1 property (p4)
* Quant: 1 property (p6) [PAG]

### Warpers Moss Lane, Burscough Bridge
* Warpers Moss Lane was flooded as part of the November 2020 flood event. (p1)
* Flooding of a house and road at Warpers Moss Lane caused by an obstructed culvert and capacity constraints / obstructions in local ditched watercourses; connected highway drainage systems failed to function as historically expected. Culvert and highway drainage were cleaned and jetted earlier in the year; remaining risk was addressed when excess silt and vegetation were cleared from the private land watercourses. (p3)
* Surface water drainage on the public highway was overwhelmed and failed to discharge because downstream ditched watercourses were obstructed by silt and vegetation, causing floodwater to back up into the highway and into at least one home. Repeated reports of flooding were recorded in 2019 and 2020; a resident reported his home flooded on 24 November 2020 and that this had occurred on three separate occasions. The council identified three obstructed ditched watercourses, notified landowners, and remedial works were completed with no further reports of home flooding after clearance. (p5)
* Quant: 3 previous incidents; 1 house (p3)
* Quant: 3 separate ditched watercourses; 3 separate occasions; 1 home (p5)

### Burscough
* Burscough was affected by flooding during the November 2020 Lancashire flood event. (p1)
* Local ditched watercourses had fallen into disrepair, which prevented proper drainage and obstructed highway drainage outfalls. Under heavy rainfall the road flooded and a local house flooded on a number of occasions directly from the road. The ditched watercourses were subsequently cleared and there have been no repeat reports of flooding to homes since the works were completed. (p6)
* Quant: 1 property (p6)
