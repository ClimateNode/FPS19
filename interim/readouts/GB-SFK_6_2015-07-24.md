

# Lowestoft flooding 24 Jul 2015

Reference document:<br>Kirkley Stream Flood Report – March 2016<br>124\_Suffolk County/124\_2016\_Suffolk County/2016-March-Kirkley-update-.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Tom Crisp Way, Lowestoft
* {18 Mar 2016}: Anglian Water's trash screen located at the junction of Tom Crisp Way and Southwell Road intercepts debris carried by Kirkley Stream; a telemetry monitoring system has been installed to warn when blockages are developing, allowing more rapid removal of debris and reducing the risk of flooding caused by blockages in the stream. (p2)

### Southwell Road, Lowestoft
* {18 Mar 2016}: Anglian Water's trash screen at the junction with Tom Crisp Way catches debris flowing down Kirkley Stream; telemetry monitoring was installed to warn of developing blockages so debris can be removed quickly, reducing flood risk caused by stream blockages. (p2)

### Kirkley Stream
* Dense willow and other shrubs in the stream channel caused snagging and blockages which were the cause of flooding in July 2015. Clearance works (coppicing willows to near trunk base, shredding/cutting material, removing overhanging branches and planned spraying of bankside vegetation) were carried out Feb–Mar 2016 to improve channel flow and reduce future blockages during high flows. (p1) [PAG]
* {general}: Blockages within the channel created a risk of flooding. Clearance, vegetation removal and dredging works have been undertaken (and further works planned) to improve channel capacity and reduce likelihood of blockages and resulting flooding. A full topographical survey has been completed to inform longer-term flood risk reduction options. (p2)

### Bloodmoor Roundabout
* Clearance works were carried out on the stretch of Kirkley Stream from Bloodmoor Roundabout to Kirkley Ham to remove dense willow and shrubs from the channel and banks to reduce snagging and blockages. The blockages in the channel (snagging) were identified as the cause of the flooding in July 2015. (p1)


## Topics without coordinates

### Environment Agency
* {24 Jul 2015, 01 Feb 2016 - 31 Mar 2016}: The Environment Agency led and delivered clearance works (coppicing willows, removing overhanging branches and shredding/cutting material, and planning follow-up vegetation control) on the Kirkley Stream to reduce natural debris and prevent snagging and blockages that caused the July 2015 flooding. (p1) [MUL]

### Anglian Water
* {general}: Anglian Water's trash screen at the junction of Tom Crisp Way and Southwell Road catches debris from Kirkley Stream; a telemetry monitoring system has been installed to warn when blockages are developing so debris can be removed more rapidly, reducing the risk of flooding caused by stream blockages. (p2)

### Waveney District Council
* {autumn 2016}: Waveney District Council is responsible for maintenance of the channel adjacent to Meadow Park and will work with the Environment Agency to carry out scrub and vegetation clearance in this area (planned for autumn 2016) to allow EA machine access to Kirkley Stream for vegetation clearance and dredging to improve channel capacity and reduce the likelihood of blockages and consequent flooding. (p2)
