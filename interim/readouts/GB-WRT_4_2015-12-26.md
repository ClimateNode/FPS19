

# Penketh flooding 26 Dec 2015 - 15 Jan 2016

Reference document:<br>S19. (1) Flood Investigation Report: A562 Widnes Road, Penketh<br>139\_Warrington/139\_2016\_Warrington/s19\_report\_-\_widnes\_road.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Widnes Road, Penketh
* Left lane of the two-lane A562 (Widnes Road) was partially submerged, creating a temporary hazard; traffic cones were placed to segregate the flooded section and traffic was directed to the remaining passable lane. (p1)
* A 200m long section of the inbound lane flooded, causing major disruption to traffic; Warrington Borough Council deployed three-way traffic signals on numerous occasions to maintain highway safety. (p4) [TAB]
* Floodwater from adjacent waterlogging occupied part of the roadway (left lane), encroaching from the adjacent land and requiring cones/traffic control to isolate the flooded section. (p5) [IMG]
* The watercourse under Widnes Road was prevented from discharging (due to poorly maintained ditches and culverting), which contributed to the flood event at this location. (p7)
* Widnes Road was overtopped / flooded; photographic evidence (Image B) shows the road flooded with cones in place and the mapped flood extent includes sections of Widnes Road. (p9) [IMG]

### Warrington Unitary Authority
* Inbound approach into Warrington on the A562 experienced a 200m flooded lane causing major traffic disruption; local authority deployed three-way traffic signals to maintain highway safety. (p4)

### Penketh
* Area adjacent to the entrance to Fiddlers Ferry Power Station experienced fluvial/surface water flooding with downstream culvert blockage; agricultural land flooded and external flooding to properties (Rose Tree Farm and boarding kennels). (p4) [TAB]

### Fiddlers Ferry
* Ditches on land belonging to Fiddlers Ferry were poorly maintained and a culvert under a farm access was blocked or collapsed, preventing a watercourse from discharging under Widnes Road and causing upstream water to back up and flood the adjacent access road and nearby highway. The site manager agreed to clear ditches and investigate the culvert; after clearing works the water level receded. (p6) [PAG]
* Poorly maintained ditches on land belonging to Fiddlers Ferry obstructed an ordinary watercourse, preventing discharge and contributing to the flood event. (p7)

### Fiddlers Ferry Power Station
* The riparian ditches on land of Fiddlers Ferry Power Station were not maintained; as the riparian owner this lack of maintenance contributed to obstruction of the watercourse and the resulting flood event. (p7)
* Watercourses/ditches in the vicinity of the power station were in poor condition and flooding was mapped nearby; the power station commenced cleaning out ditches on 15 January 2016 in response to the observed conditions following the 13 January flooding. (p9) [IMG, MUL]

### Rose Tree Farm
* External property flooding at Rose Tree Farm. (p4)


## Topics without coordinates

### A562
* Inbound lane into Warrington on the A562 experienced flooding (200m section), resulting in major disruption to the flow of traffic. (p4) [TAB]
* Carriageway and adjacent access road were flooded because watercourse/drainage could not discharge (blocked/collapsed culvert and poorly maintained ditches upstream), resulting in standing water covering the road surface and obscuring road markings. (p6)

### Warrington Borough Council
* Major disruption to flow of traffic on the A562 (200m long section of inbound lane). The Maintenance Team deployed 3‑way traffic signals on numerous occasions to maintain highway safety during the flooding. (p4)
