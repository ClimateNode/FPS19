

# Blyth flooding 05 Dec 2013

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Blyth and North Blyth<br>091\_Northumberland/Blyth\_Flood\_Investigation\_Report\_05Dec13.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Blyth
* The 5 December 2013 tidal surge (coinciding with a spring tide) caused coastal/tidal flooding in the Blyth harbour area. Roads adjacent to the harbour were affected; Crawford Street in Blyth was flooded (water reached the road) though water is not thought to have crossed property thresholds. Sandbagging was used at properties and is thought to have helped prevent internal property flooding. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 1 road; 0 residential properties; 0 businesses; 0 other buildings; 0 critical infrastructure (p1) [IMG, PAG]

### North Blyth
* During the 5 December 2013 tidal surge properties in North Blyth were surrounded and came very close to flooding; sandbags at properties are thought to have prevented internal flooding. No properties were thought to have actually flooded. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 0 properties flooded (p1) [IMG]

### Crawford Street, Blyth
* Crawford Street was flooded at approximately 16:00 on 5 December 2013 during the tidal surge. Water is not thought to have crossed property thresholds; sandbagging at properties is thought to have helped prevent internal flooding. The Environment Agency subsequently moved Crawford Street into the Blyth Quayside flood warning area. (p1) [PAG]
* Placed into the Blyth Quayside flood warning area and included in updated flood alert texts; exposed to tidal-surge-related flooding risk prompting issuance of flood alerts/warnings. (p2) [TAB]
* Quant: 1 road flooded (Crawford Street); 0 properties flooded (p1) [PAG]

### Gray Street, North Blyth
* Roadway identified on the map as being affected by the Blyth/North Blyth tidal surge (5 December 2013); indicated as within the cluster of roads adjacent to the harbour that experienced coastal inundation during the tidal surge. (p1) [IMG]

### Blyth Harbor
* A tidal surge coinciding with a spring tide caused coastal/tidal inundation of roads adjacent to the harbor and waterfront (clusters of affected roads shown near the jetties and landing stages). Areas around the harbor were identified on the council map as having roads impacted and the event increased future flood risk as mean sea level rises. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 1 road (p1) [IMG]

### River Blyth
* The tidal surge at the river mouth (spring tide plus surge) elevated water levels adjacent to the River Blyth contributing to inundation of adjacent waterfront roads (report locates affected areas on either side of the River Blyth approximately one mile from the river mouth; Crawford Street was flooded by high water). (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 1 road; 0 properties (p1) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### East Coast Northumberland
* Sea levels along the east coast reached their highest in 60 years during the tidal surge on 5 December 2013; these exceptionally high sea levels contributed to the tidal surge that caused local flooding in Blyth and North Blyth. (p1)
* Quant: highest sea levels in 60 years (p1)
