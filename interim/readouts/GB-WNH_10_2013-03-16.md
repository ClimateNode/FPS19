

# Chacombe flooding 16 Mar 2013

## Place names with coordinates

### Silver Street North, Chacombe
* Intense rainfall on a saturated catchment caused excess surface water to flow over ground following natural contours to low points on Silver Street North, resulting in internal flooding of at least one property. (p5) [PAG]
* Intense short-duration rainfall on a saturated catchment caused excess surface water to flow over ground following natural contours to low points on Silver Street North. Historic drainage was ineffective, resulting in internal flooding of at least one property on Silver Street North; the same property has experienced internal flooding on multiple occasions over the previous five years. (p6) [PAG]
* Flooding occurred on Silver Street North on 16 March 2013; flood water crossed building thresholds and at least one residential property experienced internal flooding. Flooding on this street has been experienced multiple times in the five years prior to the investigation. (p8)
* {from October 2011 onwards, 02 Nov 2012, 16 Mar 2013}: Repeated flooding on Silver Street North during high rainfall events. Flooding previously reached the front door of one residential property on numerous occasions (prior to raised kerbs). After kerb works, flooding now affects the side doors and walls of that property. Flooding affected people and one property during the event on 16 March 2013. A residential property also flooded on 2 November 2012. (p9) [PAG, MUL]
* A large volume and high-velocity surface water flow moved south-to-north along Silver Street North, bypassing the road gully and flowing to the low point of the road. Kerbs at the low point prevented water entering the front door, but water built up against the building wall on the west side and entered a residential property through a stone wall and a west-facing doorway. Rooms within the property were affected and required constant attention to prevent spread; ponding at low points around the west of the property prevented flow away to the north. (p10)
* Flooding was reported at Silver Street North. The report notes the Main River accepting flow from the ordinary watercourse is not considered to have affected the flooding at Silver Street North. (p12)
* Highway drainage on Silver Street North was identified as needing full investigation because existing road drainage was implicated in being unable to manage the event, allowing surface water to flow from the highway toward low-lying residential areas. (p15)
* Highway drainage and its outfall on Silver Street North were identified as requiring examination because insufficient drainage capacity for highway runoff creates flood risk to properties; recommendations include cleansing, assessing capacity, adding or relocating road gullies and repairing/replacing outfall/culvert sections as required. (p16)
* Ponding/standing surface water observed along Silver Street North adjacent to a property at a low point, indicating local surface water flooding and increased inundation risk to the nearby property and carriageway. (p39) [IMG]
* {general}: The low point of Silver Street North lacks visible kerb drainage, leading to water accumulation/ponding at the road low point. Parked vehicles and bins near the kerb may further obstruct flow and exacerbate surface water pooling on the carriageway, increasing risk of inundation to the roadway and adjacent properties. ### Chacombe
* Low points to the west of a residential property caused ponding of water and prevented it flowing away to the north. (p11)
* Intense short-duration rainfall on a saturated catchment caused existing drainage systems and watercourses in Chacombe to be unable to cope. Surface water flowed from residential areas, the public highway and grassland into low areas at residential property across the village. (p15)

### St Peter and St Paul's Church
* The historical route and condition of the riparian-owned ditch on the eastern boundary of St Peter (part of 'St Peter and St Paul's Church') require investigation because poor or absent drainage contributed to surface water flow toward low areas adjacent to the church boundary. (p15)
* {general}: Ponding and flooding occurs at the side of the property within the church grounds; evidence of a historic riparian-owned ditch system is visible in the grounds (ditch appears as a depression in the grass). (p41) [IMG]

### River Cherwell
* The River Cherwell's tributary/main river network is the downstream watercourse for the ordinary watercourse north of Chacombe; this catchment connection is implicated in the flooding on Silver Street North during the event of 16 March 2013, when surface runoff/flows in the ordinary watercourse and tributary network were associated with flooding that affected Silver Street North and at least one property internally. (p8)
* Quant: at least one property (p8)


## Topics without coordinates

### Chacombe Catchment
* Significant rainfall fell on the Chacombe catchment on 16 March 2013, producing conditions that led to flooding in the area, including flooding on Silver Street North. (p8)
* Significant rainfall fell on the Chacombe catchment (approximately 20 mm on 16 March 2013), contributing to saturated ground and conditions that led to flooding which affected people and a property in the village. (p9)

### Northamptonshire County Council
* The county council (as LLFA and Emergency Planning) was required to provide advice to residents on property-level protection, instate and liaise with a Flood Warden, produce/update the village community flood plan, and undertake/coordinate highway drainage cleansing, develop an asset plan, assess asset capacity and identify/implement improvement works where highway runoff leads to flood risk to properties. (p16)
