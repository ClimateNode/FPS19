

# Ovingham flooding 28 Jun 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Ovingham<br>091\_Northumberland/Ovingham-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Ovingham
* {01 Sep 2008 - 30 Sep 2008, 28 Jun 2012}: The 28/06/2012 rainfall event produced severe surface water flooding across parts of Ovingham with properties inundated on Dene Garth, Piper Road, Dene Close and Wheatfield Close (surface water from steep fields to the north/west). In addition, one property to the west of the village was flooded by the Whittle Burn (ordinary watercourse); that property had also flooded in September 2008. Roads through and adjacent to affected areas were also impacted. (p1) [IMG, MUL]
* {general}: Ovingham has experienced repeated surface water flooding affecting residents and properties; photographic and written records from residents document flooding events over an extended period. (p2) [IMG]
* {01 Sep 2008 - 30 Sep 2008, 28 Jun 2012}: Quant: 8-20 residential properties; 4 roads; 1 property flooded by the Whittle Burn (also flooded in Sept 2008) (p1) [IMG, MUL]

### Northumberland
* Parts of Northumberland (specifically Ovingham) experienced surface water flooding during the 28/06/2012 event, with multiple residential properties and roads affected. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 8-20 residential properties; 4 roads (p1) [IMG]

### Piper Road, Ovingham
* Properties on Piper Road experienced surface water flooding caused by runoff from surrounding fields. (p1)
* {general}: Low-lying fields north of Piper Road are described as susceptible to flooding; runoff/flooding from these fields has increased flood risk to properties at lower elevations in Ovingham and is a concern for future development north of Piper Road. (p2) [IMG, TAB]

### Wheatfield Close, Ovingham
* Properties on Wheatfield Close were inundated by surface water runoff originating from steep fields to the north and west of the village. (p1)
* Quant: Part of the village-wide total of 8-20 residential properties affected (p1)

### Dene Garth, Ovingham
* Residential properties on Dene Garth were inundated by surface water runoff originating from the fields to the north and west of the village. (p1)

### Dene Close, Ovingham
* Residential properties on Dene Close were affected by surface water flooding from sheet runoff off nearby fields. (p1)
