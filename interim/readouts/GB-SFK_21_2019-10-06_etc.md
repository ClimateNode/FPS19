### Friston
* {06 Oct 2019, 21 Oct 2019}: Within the village of Friston a single property (on Aldeburgh Road) suffered internal and external flooding on multiple occasions in October 2019, causing damage to the property. (p2) [MUL]
* {01 Oct 2019 - 31 Oct 2019}: A single domestic property in Friston experienced internal and external flooding from surface water runoff from adjacent agricultural land. Surface water entered the lounge and kitchen carrying silt, causing property damage. The resident has been displaced while repairs are made. Externally the garden and patio were covered by a thick layer of silt. (p3) [IMG]
* {06 Oct 2019, 21 Oct 2019}: Quant: one property (p2) [MUL]
