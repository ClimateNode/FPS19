

# Blenkinsopp flooding 28 Jun 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Bankfoot / Blenkinsopp<br>091\_Northumberland/Blenkinsopp-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Blenkinsopp
* High flows in ordinary watercourses, combined with surface water runoff and debris-blocked culverts, caused water to come out of bank and flood residential areas and roads in Blenkinsopp. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 4 residential properties; 4 roads (combined Bankfoot/Blenkinsopp) (p1) [IMG]

### Bankfoot
* High flows in ordinary watercourses, combined with surface water runoff and debris-blocked culverts, caused water to come out of bank and flood residential areas and roads in Bankfoot. (p1) [IMG]
* A culvert entrance in Bankfoot was heavily blocked by vegetation and woody debris, obstructing the watercourse and reducing flow velocity. The blockage caused accumulation of sediment and backwater effects upstream of the culvert and likely contributed to local drainage issues and flooding risk. (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 4 residential properties; 4 roads (combined Bankfoot/Blenkinsopp) (p1) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### A69
* {28 Jun 2012, unknown}: Culverts running under the A69 became blocked by debris during high flows on 28/06/2012, causing upstream out-of-bank flooding which resulted in flooding of the A69 and a temporary road closure. (p1) [PAG]
* Floodwater ran alongside and encroached onto the A69 carriageway, disrupting traffic flow; vehicles were stopped/parked on the shoulder and emergency/maintenance presence was visible. (p2) [IMG]

### Highways Agency
* The culvert under the A69 (owned by Road Link on behalf of the Highways Agency) was associated with blocked drainage — debris/vegetation at the culvert entrance in Bankfoot impeded flow and contributed to localized flooding affecting the road. (p2)
