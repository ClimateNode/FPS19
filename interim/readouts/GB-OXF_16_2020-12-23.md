* {01 Dec 2020 - 31 Dec 2020}: EA to update the River Windrush flood risk model (including December 2020 data) because floodplain connectivity issues downstream of the Aquarius site and the raised footpath at Langel Common mean the 2014 model under‑represents embankments and flow pathways; EA to increase riparian maintenance, investigate modifying the river (particularly New Bridge Street to Langel Common) and install gauge boards to better monitor river levels. (p3) [TAB]
* The Environment Agency (with riparian landowners) were asked to increase maintenance along the River Windrush through and downstream of Witney and to investigate modifications to improve flow capacity between New Bridge Street and the footpath at Langel Common. Actions also include improving floodplain connectivity at the Langel Common footpath and installing gauge boards from Woodford Mill through to Langel Common to record levels during flood events. (p4)
* {01 Jul 2007 - 31 Jul 2007}: Numerous properties in the town were flooded during the July 2007 events; flooding arose from multiple sources driven by intense rainfall and antecedent accumulation. (p8)
* Environment Agency modelling and flood warnings were directly involved in the incident: the EA provided the Windrush (Worsham to A40) model used to assess the event and the EA-issued flood warning was reported by local groups to have been issued too late, after properties were flooding. The EA modelling showed peak flows and informed the post-event review and recommended actions (riparian maintenance, new gauge boards, investigation of footbridge/raised path). (p16) [IMG]
* Responded to the December 2020 flooding in Witney: sent two crews to investigate and check for blockages, deployed officers to speak with affected residents, and provided advice/support including on obtaining grants for property-level resilience at Riverside Gardens. Made immediate improvements to the Flood Warning Service after the late flood warning for the December 2020 event and implemented two changes following a January 2021 review. (p18) [IMG]
* Environment Agency (EA) responded to the December 2020 river flooding in Witney: they identified fallen trees and channel blockages and issued these to riparian owners for removal, provided advice encouraging riparian maintenance, and committed to updating the Windrush flood risk model and further investigations of drainage and Bridge Street/Langel Common issues. (p21) [PAG]
* The Environment Agency was involved in investigating flow improvements (including gauge boards from Woodford Mill to Langel Common), assessing upstream flood storage and nature‑based solutions for Witney and Crawley, and in coordinating works to manage flows following the 23–24 Dec 2020 river flooding. (p28)
* Quant: two crews; two changes (p18) [IMG]

### Windrush Catchment
* {19 Jul 2007}: Extraordinary rainfall in the upper Windrush catchment produced very high river flows that caused the majority of flooding in Witney town centre (river-driven flooding). (p9)
* High flows from the Windrush and intense rainfall resulted in surface water flooding on 23/12/2020 and river flooding reaching Witney on 24/12/2020 (river levels peaked just after midnight on 25/12/2020). The flooding led to internal flooding and evacuations in Witney: at least 54 residential and business properties flooded internally, including evacuations of Mill House Care Home, Riverside Gardens and Riverside House. Areas with confirmed internal flooding included Woodford Mill, Riverside Gardens, Bridge Street (including Bridge Street Mills), West End (including the Industrial Estate), Millers Mews, Newland (near Madley Brook) and Eastfield Road; one property was affected in New Yatt Road. Highway and external flooding occurred in Hailey Road (linked to Hailey Road Drain), Blakes Avenue, Park Road, Marriots Walk, Cherry Tree Way, Vanner Road and Woodford Way Car Park. (p11)
* {19 Jul 2007}: Quant: 29.26 m3/s peak flow recorded at the Worsham flow monitoring station (p9)
* Quant: 54 residential and business properties flooded internally; 1 unit in the Industrial Estate off Bridge Street flooded internally; 1 property affected in New Yatt Road (p11)

### Aquarius Bridge
* {01 Dec 2020 - 31 Dec 2020}: Areas downstream of Aquarius Bridge show reduced floodplain connectivity: LiDAR levels and field levels indicate flood flows (modelled 1 in 20 and parts of 1 in 100 events) would not fill and pass through to the downstream floodplain as the 2014 model suggests. The perimeter ditch of the Aquarius site is identified as a feature to investigate/modify to increase flow capacity. (p3) [TAB]
* Flood conveyance issues identified downstream of the bridge; recommendations to update the 2014 hydraulic model for levels downstream of Aquarius Bridge and to investigate modifying/diverting the perimeter ditch of the Aquarius site to increase flow capacity. (p27) [TAB]
* Elevated river levels and flooding downstream of Aquarius Bridge during the event; the report requires updated modelling of levels downstream of the bridge to understand and manage flood behaviour. (p33)
