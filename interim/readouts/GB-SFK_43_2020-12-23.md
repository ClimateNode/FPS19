## Place names with coordinates

### Queen Street, Stradbroke
* Perched water table and associated groundwater flooding combined with restricted highway drainage and a failing culvert/open watercourse overtopping caused flooding of the carriageway, footpath, neighbouring driveways and internal flooding to a room that is lower than external ground level. (p4)
* A culvert pipe under Queen Street has collapsed and is identified as requiring investigation and replacement; culvert issues are linked to highway drainage failure and overland flows in the affected area. (p5)
