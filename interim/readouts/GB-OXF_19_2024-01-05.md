

# Abingdon flooding 05 Jan 2024 - 06 Jan 2024

## Place names with coordinates

### Nash Drive, Abingdon
* Flooding occurred on Nash Drive and surrounding roads. (p1)
* Flood water flowed onto estate roads from the north end of Nash Drive; the north end and roadway were affected and residential properties on Nash Drive were flooded internally (mostly from the front/roadside); water came up out of road drains; gardens of properties closest to the rivers also rose. (p2)
* Properties in the Nash Drive area were flooded when the River Ock and Sandford Brook flooded following a wet autumn/winter and especially heavy rainfall on 03-04 January. By mid-morning on 06 January flood levels had dropped to a point where water was no longer entering properties. (p3)
* The Nash Drive area was flooded on 05–06 Jan 2024 when high flows from the River Ock and Sandford Brook inundated the neighbourhood; the area is also mapped as high risk of river flooding. (p6) [IMG]
* The Nash Drive area was flooded on 05–06 Jan 2024 due to converging high flows from the River Ock and Sandford Brook upstream; the map and caption identify Nash Drive as the area impacted by these fluvial flows. (p7) [IMG, PAG]
* The Nash Drive residential area lies between the River Ock and Sandford Brook; the report maps and context indicate the area experienced flooding associated with the nearby watercourses during the event. (p8) [IMG, PAG]
* Residential properties were flooded internally when floodwater entered from the front (roadside) after the highway drainage system reached capacity and road drains surcharged. (p10)
* The north end of Nash Drive was inundated on 06 January 2024; floodwater covered the street, reached into gardens and properties, and left vehicles stranded and access compromised. Sandbags were provided to help reduce water entering homes from the road. (p11) [IMG]
* The Nash Drive area was specifically identified as impacted by rising river levels and associated flooding of roads and nearby properties during the event. (p15)
* The Nash Drive area experienced flooding of roads and properties caused by very high river levels in the Ock and Sandford Brook, with additional contribution from local surface water runoff from the estate. (p16) [PAG]
* Flood water from the River Ock and Sandford Brook flowed onto estate roads from the north end of Nash Drive, affecting the north end of the street. Properties on Nash Drive suffered internal flooding between 05 Jan 2024 and 06 Jan 2024 (part of the 17 confirmed residential properties). Flooding mostly entered from the front (roadside) and water also surfaced from road drains; gardens at rear of properties nearest the rivers were inundated. (p18)
* Quant: 16 residential properties internally flooded in the Nash Drive area (including Chaunterell Way, Nash Drive and Orpwood Way); 12 properties had flooding of integral garages; 5 properties had flooding of grounds and gardens. (p2)
* Quant: 17 residential properties (across Nash Drive, Orpwood Road and Chaunterell Way) (p10)
* Quant: 1 property (Francis Little Drive) (p16) [PAG]

### Sandford Brook
* Flooding of Sandford Brook (alongside the River Ock) contributed to floodwaters entering estate roads and properties in the Nash Drive area, causing roadway inundation and internal property flooding; gardens adjacent to the watercourse rose. (p2) [PAG]
* Sandford Brook flooded (as a main river watercourse) following a wet autumn/winter and heavy rainfall on 03-04 January, contributing to flooding of the Nash Drive area on 05-06 January 2024. (p3)
* Sandford Brook produced runoff that contributed to the flooding near Nash Drive on 05–06 Jan 2024; flows from Sandford Brook converged with flows from the Ock upstream of the Nash Drive area, worsening local inundation. (p7) [IMG, PAG]
* Sandford Brook runs parallel to and converges near Nash Drive and is identified on the report maps as a principal watercourse; it is implicated as contributing to river flooding in the Nash Drive area during the event. (p8) [IMG, PAG]
* Sandford Brook flooded during the event and contributed to road and property flooding in the area (properties on Nash Drive, Orpwood Road and Chaunterell Way experienced internal flooding); highway drainage reached capacity and road drains surcharged. (p10)
* Sandford Brook levels rose alongside the River Ock and contributed to flooding that impacted roads and properties in the local area. (p15) [PAG]
* Sandford Brook levels rose and contributed to impacts on nearby roads and properties, forming part of the flooding observed in the locality. (p16) [PAG]
* Flooding of Sandford Brook contributed to the flooding of the Nash Drive area. Site visits in March 2024 observed natural woody material and silt in the channel, but these blockages were not judged to be sufficient to make a measurable difference to levels of flooding to properties or roads. (p17) [IMG, PAG]
* Sandford Brook (a main river watercourse) rose and contributed to the flooding that flowed onto estate roads and into gardens and properties in the Nash Drive area, forming part of the source of the internal flooding on 05–06 Jan 2024. (p18)
* Quant: Contributed to internal flooding of 16 residential properties; 12 garages flooded; 5 grounds/gardens flooded (p2) [PAG]
* Quant: 17 residential properties (p10)

### River Ock

* The River Ock flooded (as a main river watercourse) following a wet autumn/winter and heavy rainfall on 03-04 January, causing flooding to the Nash Drive area on 05-06 January 2024. (p3)
* River Ock generated runoff that contributed to the flooding that affected the Nash Drive area and surrounding parts of Abingdon on 05–06 Jan 2024; flows from the Ock converged upstream of Nash Drive, driving river flooding into the local area. (p7) [IMG, PAG]
* River Ock flows through the Nash Drive area and is a primary watercourse identified on the report maps; it is implicated as a source of river flooding affecting the Nash Drive locality during the flood event. (p8) [IMG, PAG]
* River Ock overtopped/caused flooding during the event, contributing to roads on the estate flooding and internal flooding of residential properties on Nash Drive, Orpwood Road and Chaunterell Way; highway drains surcharged and water came up through road drains. (p10) [TAB]
* The River Ock rose to high levels, with elevated flows causing flooding that impacted roads and properties in Abingdon (including the Nash Drive area). (p15) [IMG, PAG]

### Ock Catchment
* Runoff from the River Ock catchment (the larger of the two catchments shown) contributed substantial volume to the river flows that caused flooding at and upstream of Nash Drive on 05–06 Jan 2024. (p7) [IMG]
* Persistent and particularly heavy rainfall across the River Ock catchment led to elevated river levels that caused flooding of the River Ock and affected roads and properties downstream. (p15)

### Oxfordshire Fire and Rescue Service
* Requested delivery of sandbags to reduce floodwater entering homes from the roads due to risk posed by surcharged drains and flooded roads. (p10)
* Identified as a local resilience forum partner who could request sandbags from Oxfordshire Highways to try to prevent flooding of properties off the highway during the flood response. (p25)

### Sandford Brook Catchment
* Runoff from the Sandford Brook catchment contributed to flows that combined with the River Ock upstream of Nash Drive, leading to flooding in the Nash Drive area on 05–06 Jan 2024. (p7) [IMG]

### Oxfordshire County Council
* {general}: Worked with the Resilience Team and Environment Agency to support community awareness, resilience and preparation after the flood and to assist communities with producing Community Resilience Plans; considered upstream catchment measures (nature-based solutions) to manage flows affecting the Ock at Abingdon. (p25)

### Highway Authority
* Water was reported to be coming up from highway drains during the flood; main flow path onto roads was overland, with flood water entering highway drainage and coming up through low points, contributing to water on roads and into properties. Oxfordshire Highways provided sandbags in emergency situations and maintain highway drainage. (p25)
