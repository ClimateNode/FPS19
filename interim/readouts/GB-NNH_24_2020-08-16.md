### Middleton
* Location of the flood incident in which an internal building flood occurred on Main Street; the incident met the threshold for formal investigation. (p8)
* {general}: Main Street in the village experienced recorded flooding incidents; the village is identified as being in an area with a high chance of surface water flooding. (p11) [TAB, MUL]
* The area of Middleton contains unrecorded surface water sewer and a buried culvert; localized surface water ponding on the public highway was observed (westbound carriageway near the Manor House). (p15)
* The locality experienced surface water flooding that resulted in at least one internally flooded property (Main Street) and surface water on roads (Main Road) with standing water persisting for several weeks. (p16)
* Intense heavy rainfall caused surface water drainage systems across the catchment to be unable to collect and convey rainwater, resulting in excess surface water flowing over ground following localised ground levels to low points around properties; affected areas are shown to be at high risk of surface water flooding. (p19)
* Quant: 1 building; internal flooding experienced on more than one occasion in the last five years (p8)
* {general}: Quant: 2 recorded incidents (at Main Street) (p11) [TAB, MUL]
* Quant: 2 road gullies blocked (p15)
* Quant: 1 property flooded internally; external flood depth up to 0.5 m; standing water 4-6 weeks (p16)

### Manor House
* A large area of surface water ponding was evident over the westbound carriageway immediately in front of the Manor House (observed during the site visit). Blocked highway gullies were present nearby. (p15)
* Surface water emerged from beneath the driveway, ran down the driveway and entered the property via two back doors and the basement access; the whole ground floor and basement were flooded internally; standing floodwater remained around the property for 4–6 weeks. (p16)
* A buried watercourse through the grounds of the Manor House is considered highly likely to have an underground blockage that forced water to the surface, causing it to flow rapidly down the driveway. (p19)
* Quant: 2 road gullies blocked (p15)
* Quant: 1 property flooded internally; internal flood depth 4 inches; external flood depth up to 0.5 m; standing water 4-6 weeks (p16)

### North Northamptonshire
* Properties in North Northamptonshire were affected by flooding, prompting Section 19 investigations by the Lead Local Flood Authority (NNC) and the need to record assets affecting flood risk on the LLFA register. (p26)
