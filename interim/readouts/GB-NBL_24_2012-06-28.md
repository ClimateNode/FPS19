

# Hexham flooding 28 Jun 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Hexham<br>091\_Northumberland/Hexham-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Hexham
* Intense rainfall on 28/06/2012 produced isolated flooding incidents across central Hexham. Surface water runoff predominantly affected the town centre (affecting businesses and roads). Ordinary watercourses and blocked culverts caused water to back up, flooding gardens and creating near-misses to properties and impacting a gas terminal. Standing water occurred on roads including Battle Hill and Priestpopple (B6305). (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 2 businesses; 3 roads; 1 critical infrastructure; 0 residential; 0 other buildings (p1) [IMG]

### Battle Hill, Hexham
* Standing water on Battle Hill (local road) caused by intense rainfall, producing localized road inundation. (p1)

### Priestpopple, Hexham
* Standing water on the B6305 (Priestpopple) due to intense rainfall, causing localized road inundation. (p1)

### Leisure Centre
* Experienced ingress of flood water due to surface water runoff during the intense rainfall on 28/06/2012. (p1)
* Quant: part of the 2 businesses affected (p1)

### Waitrose
* Affected by ingress of flood water from surface water runoff during the intense rainfall on 28/06/2012. (p1)
* Quant: part of the 2 businesses affected (p1)

### Halgut Burn
* Water from the Halgut Burn may have entered a block of flats on the right bank at Tanners Yard; it was reported to have gone inside the hallway but did not reach the step level of the ground floor flat. (p2)
* Quant: 1 block of flats (p2)
