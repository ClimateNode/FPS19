## Topics without coordinates

### Suffolk County Council
* Received reports of flooding from multiple streets (Sturmer Road, Hazel Close, High Street, Harewood Terrace, Greenwood Close, Elm Close, Chauntry Road and Withersfield Road) after the intense localised rainfall on 25.07.2021 and initiated Section 19 flood investigations. (p3) [IMG]
* SCC LLFA led the investigation and issued multiple recommended actions in response to the flooding on 25 July 2021, including reactive jetting of highways drainage in Elm/Hazel/Greenwood Close, investigating additional gullies, supporting PFR delivery, and coordinating CCTV/survey work with Anglian Water to examine a potential bottleneck in Greenwood Close. They also recommended increased gully jetting for gullies identified as contributing to flood risk for Properties A, B, C and D. (p12) [TAB]
* Quant: 4 properties (Properties A, B, C and D) (p12) [TAB]

### SCC Highway
