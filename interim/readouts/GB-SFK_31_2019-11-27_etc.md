

# Kettleburgh flooding 27 Nov 2019 and Kettleburgh flooding 17 Dec 2019

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: The Street, Kettleburgh<br>124\_Suffolk County/124\_2020\_Suffolk County/section-19-report-the-street-kettleburgh.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Kettleburgh
* {27 Nov 2019, 17 Dec 2019}: Internal flooding of residential properties when the capacity of a local ordinary watercourse (partially piped) was exceeded during an intense storm event. Four residential properties were flooded internally; a further three properties were nearly flooded but were partly saved by reactive measures taken by property owners. Depths of internal flooding are not reported. (p3) [IMG]
* {27 Nov 2019, 17 Dec 2019}: Quant: 4 residential properties; 3 properties almost flooded (p3) [IMG]

### The Street, Kettleburgh
* {27 Nov 2019, 17 Dec 2019}: Internal flooding of residential properties: 4 residential properties flooded internally; a further 3 properties nearly flooded internally but were partly saved due to reactive measures taken by property owners. (p2)
* {27 Nov 2019, 17 Dec 2019}: Highways gullies along The Street surcharged during the intense storm, resulting in water flowing down the road and contributing to overland flow and surface water flooding of properties. (p4) [MUL]
* {27 Nov 2019, 17 Dec 2019}: Quant: 4 residential properties flooded internally; 3 properties almost flooded internally (p2)

### Low Street, Kettleburgh
* {27 Nov 2019, 17 Dec 2019}: Reported overflow of the foul sewer at the junction of The Street and Low Street (sewer overflowing possibly contributed to by ingress of surface water). (p4) [MUL]


## Topics without coordinates

### Anglian Water
* {27 Nov 2019, 17 Dec 2019}: Anglian Water's foul sewer was reported to be overflowing at the junction of The Street and Low Street; ingress of surface water into the foul sewer may have contributed to the overflow. (p4) [MUL]
