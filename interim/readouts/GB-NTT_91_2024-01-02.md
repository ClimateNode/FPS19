

# Zouch flooding 02 Jan 2024 - 04 Jan 2024

## Place names with coordinates

### Zouch
* River Soar levels peaked (during Storm Henk) and the river broke its banks at multiple locations in and around Zouch, causing internal property flooding across the village. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Internal property flooding across the village (fields, roads and residential areas inundated), central road partially submerged and access limited; aerial footage on 4 January shows extensive flooding of land and properties. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* {01 Jan 2024}: River Soar flooding affected the community: residents were issued warnings, multi-agency drop-in events and meetings with residents were held to understand impacts, and blockages were cleared. Access improvement works (including a radial gate) are planned in response to the flooding. (p5) [PAG]
* Flood-related blockages impeded access at the site; access improvement works (including a radial gate) are planned to enable machinery to remove blockages quickly. (p6)
* Quant: 27 properties (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 27 properties (p3) [IMG, PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* The county contained the flooded location (Zouch) where sustained rainfall during Storm Henk caused a flood event. (p1)
* {01 Jan 2024}: Major flood event affected the county leading to emergency response actions: delivery of sandbags to affected areas, visits to flood-affected residents and businesses, initiation of a Section 19 flood investigation and other response measures. Roads were closed to promote safety and avoid additional flood damage. (p5) [PAG]
* {01 Jan 2024}: Quant: 69 roads (p5) [PAG]

### River Soar
* During Storm Henk record-high River Soar levels caused the river to break its banks upstream and downstream of Zouch, resulting in internal property flooding across the village. The report states internal property flooding affected 27 properties in total; the referenced map identifies affected areas as Main Street (9 properties), Lower Holme (11 properties) and Upper Holme (4 properties). (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* River Soar reached record-high flows during Storm Henk, broke its banks at multiple locations upstream and downstream and caused internal property flooding in Zouch. (p3)
* Quant: 27 properties; Main Street (9 properties); Lower Holme (11 properties); Upper Holme (4 properties) (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 27 properties (p3)

### Zouch Island
* {01 Jan 2024}: A Flood Warning was issued specifically for the River Soar at Zouch Island; the Environment Agency carried out flood patrols and cleared blockages before and after peak flows at this location. (p5)
