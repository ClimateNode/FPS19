### Nidd Catchment
* {01 Nov 2015 - 30 Nov 2015, December 2015 - January 2016}: The Nidd catchment was saturated (including an exceptionally wet November) and experienced intense rainfall on 25–26 December 2015, producing large volumes of runoff. Saturation and heavy rainfall led to overwhelmed drainage, high river levels and overtopping of the River Nidd, contributing to property flooding in Knaresborough and to surface-water-driven flooding in Tockwith. (p5) [TAB, MUL]
* {01 Nov 2015 - 26 Dec 2015}: Exceptional rainfall in the Nidd catchment (November and December 2015) led to saturated ground and contributed to subsequent surface water flooding in the area, including incidents on 25–26 December 2015. (p8) [TAB]
* Widespread and substantial rainfall across the Nidd catchment in late December 2015 produced excessive runoff that contributed to flooding impacts in downstream communities in the Mid and Lower Nidd Catchment. (p10)

### Aire Basin
* {01 Nov 2015 - 31 Dec 2015, December 2015 - January 2016}: The Aire catchment received exceptionally high antecedent and event rainfall (near-record November and very high December totals), saturating the catchment and producing large volumes of surface water runoff that contributed to widespread river, surface water and groundwater flooding across the catchment during the event. (p6) [TAB, MUL]
* {01 Nov 2015 - 31 Dec 2015}: Catchment conditions (very wet prior to the event) contributed to the December 2015 flooding, with antecedent wetness increasing runoff into communities during the 23–26 December event. (p8) [TAB]
