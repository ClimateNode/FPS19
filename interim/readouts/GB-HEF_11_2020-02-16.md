

# Luston flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Luston<br>055\_Herefordshire County/10\_Luston.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Luston
* {general}: The Environment Agency Flood Map shows the eastern part of Luston (adjacent to the main watercourse) lying within Flood Zone 2 and Flood Zone 3, indicating exposure to river flooding. The map also shows areas benefiting from flood defences nearby. Flood-zone mapping for the local watercourse has not been completed, creating uncertainty about the full extent of flood risk to the village. (p1) [IMG]
* {01 Jul 2007 - 31 Jul 2007, 2012, 01 Oct 2019 - 31 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Internal flooding from a small unmapped watercourse/tributary through the village: properties experienced floodwater from the watercourse and from surface-water runoff (water running down the road). Surface water runoff also caused a dwelling to flood and contributed to flooding at Westland Primary School (critical infrastructure). Foul flooding is referenced. Fire Service attended a domestic property during the event. (p2) [MUL]
* {2007, 16 Feb 2020}: The village experienced fluvial (small watercourse/Ridgemoor Brook) and surface water flooding during the February 2020 event; respondents reported overland flows and overflowing streams, and foul/sewer flooding and blocked gullies were also reported as part of the episode. (p3) [MUL]
* {01 Jul 2007 - 31 Jul 2007, 2012, 01 Oct 2019 - 31 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: Five properties; 7 properties (p2) [MUL]

### Westland View, Luston
* {general}: Westland View is identified on the EA Flood Map near the main watercourse and appears to lie within or adjacent to Flood Zone 2/3 on the eastern side of Luston, indicating exposure to river flooding. Some nearby areas are indicated as benefiting from flood defences. The incomplete mapping of the local watercourse means the precise extent of risk is uncertain. (p1) [IMG]

### Ridgemoor Brook
* {01 Jul 2007 - 31 Jul 2007, 2012, 01 Oct 2019 - 31 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: The Ridgemoor Brook and its upper tributaries (an ordinary watercourse) are implicated in fluvial flooding that led to internal flooding of properties in Luston; tributary flows contributed to surface-water risk around the village. (p2) [MUL]
* {2007, 16 Feb 2020}: Identified as a small watercourse contributing to fluvial flooding in Luston. The brook, together with multiple overland flow routes, contributed to the complex flooding mechanism for the location. Downstream diversion of the channel and possible partial blockages where the channel runs deep may have reduced channel capacity. The brook has been targeted for Natural Flood Management measures and a feasibility study has been proposed by the LLFA. (p3) [MUL]
* {01 Jul 2007 - 31 Jul 2007, 2012, 01 Oct 2019 - 31 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: Five properties; 7 properties (p2) [MUL]

### Luston Primary School
* Recorded as being impacted during the February 16, 2020 flood event. Flooding arose from surface water runoff (drains could not convey water away quickly) and fluvial/surface-water interaction on low-lying land, causing internal/area flooding of the school (critical infrastructure). No injuries or loss of life were reported. (p2) [TAB]
* Surface water overland flow appeared to pool behind the school before discharging to the watercourse, contributing to local flood risk. (p3)
* Quant: 1 school (Westland Primary School) (p2) [TAB]


## Topics without coordinates

### Hereford and Worcester Fire and Rescue Service
* Attended a domestic property in response to flooding at 01:00 on 16 February 2020. (p2)
* Quant: 1 domestic property attended (p2)

### Herefordshire Council
* As the Lead Local Flood Authority (Land Drainage Authority) they proposed to exercise flood risk management functions following the recent flooding (16 Feb 2020). They have identified the need to promote a feasibility study on the Ridgemoor Brook (to address existing fluvial flood risk in the village of Luston) and are seeking DEFRA grant funding; further governance reports are required prior to any formal funding application. (p3)

### Welsh Water
* {general}: An episode of foul (sewer) flooding was reported to Welsh Water for investigation in connection with the recent flood event. (p3)
