### Thames Basin
* Widespread flooding in January–February 2014 from high River Thames flows and linked groundwater, surface water and sewer issues: properties flooded; roads inundated or impassable (reported impassable until 21 February); specific roads and areas inundated included Upper Thames Way, Riverside Road, Hedsor Road, Riversdale, Ferry Lane (A4094) and access to Cookham Bridge. Surface-water-driven flooding overwhelmed drainage on Cores End Road and Wharf Lane; culverts and drains were blocked and emergency works removed large quantities of debris (bricks and concrete). Sandbags were deployed to protect properties and a 'Supersucker' was used to remove floodwaters. In Abbotsbrook / The Drive low-lying land and gardens were inundated (no reports of properties flooding). Some properties in the Riversdale/Cookham Bridge vicinity were elevated on stilts and used flood barriers; residents of Riversdale used boats to reach higher ground. (p23) [MUL]
