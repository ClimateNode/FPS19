

# Longstanton flooding August 2014

## Place names with coordinates

### Longstanton
* Multiple residential roads within Longstanton (Colesfield Road, School Lane, Stokes Close, Hattons Road, Sheepwash Way, Thatchers Wood) were reported as affected in the August 2014 flood investigation, indicating a widespread flood event across the village. (p2) [IMG]
* Extensive surface-water flooding across the village with internal flooding to residences; 18 properties flooded internally. (p3) [TAB]
* Quant: 18 properties (p3) [TAB]

### School Lane, Longstanton
* School Lane is listed as a residential road affected by the August 2014 flood incident. (p2) [IMG]
* The watercourse at School Lane became blocked by debris (including straw) during the intense August 2014 rainfall; the District Council removed the blockages as part of the response. (p3) [TAB]

### Hattons Road, Longstanton
* Hattons Road is listed among locations (with Sheepwash Way and Thatchers Wood) reported to SCDC and CCC as affected by the flood incident. (p2) [IMG]
* Intense, short-duration rainfall caused large volumes of straw, silt and debris to wash into the Longstanton Brook and block culverts. Blockages were removed from the watercourse at the village centre and Hatton's Road as part of the response; the area experienced extensive flooding during the event. (p3) [TAB]

## Topics without coordinates

### Longstanton Brook
* The brook catchment received extremely intense rainfall; vegetation, silt and large quantities of straw and debris washed off agricultural land and caused a blockage at the School Hill culvert, contributing to flooding of the village. (p3)

### South Cambridgeshire District Council
* As the authority responsible for the awarded watercourse, SCDC had recently carried out maintenance (flail mowing and an earlier culvert clearance). After the intense rainfall event they carried out de-silting and removed large amounts of straw and other debris washed into the brook; they visited the site on multiple occasions to remove blockages at School Lane, the Village Centre and Hatton's Road. (p3) [TAB]
