

# Warwick flooding 07 Sep 2017, Warwick flooding 11 Aug 2020 - 13 Aug 2020, Warwick flooding 28 Dec 2020 and Warwick flooding 30 Jul 2021

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Flood Investigation: Flooding on Cape Road, Warwick on 7th September 2017, 11th & 13th August 2020, 28th December 2020 and 30th July 2021<br>140\_Warwickshire County/140\_2020\_Warwickshire County/WCCC-453486374-169.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Warwick
* {12 Aug 2020}: LLFA received multiple flood reports in Warwick during the intense, highly-localised thunderstorms on 12 August 2020 relating to short-duration heavy rainfall (surface water flooding incidents reported to the LLFA). (p5) [PAG]
* {30 Jul 2021}: On 30 July 2021 a band of rain associated with Storm Evert passed over Warwick. Radar data suggest the site of interest in Warwick received about 23 mm of rain in 24 hours; surrounding gauges recorded 17–31 mm. The event prompted warnings of potential surface water and fluvial flood risk. (p6)
* {12 Aug 2020}: Internal flooding of properties in the town: 5 commercial premises and 4 residential properties were internally flooded during the event. Highway flooding was also reported in parts of Leamington. Warwickshire County Council (WCC) received two flood reports from the site of interest and a separate Section 19 investigation was undertaken because the event exceeded threshold 3 of the Warwickshire LFRMS. (p7) [IMG, PAG]
* {12 Aug 2020}: Internal flooding of properties and standing highway water in the town following an intense rainfall event; the Local Lead Flood Authority (LLFA) carried out a separate Section 19 investigation for the event. (p8) [PAG]
* {30 Jul 2021}: Highway surface water flooding reports recorded in Warwick at Castle Lane and by St Nicholas' Church following the July 2021 rainfall event. (p9)
* {12 Aug 2020}: Quant: 5 commercial premises; 4 residential properties; two flood reports (p7) [IMG, PAG]
* {12 Aug 2020}: Quant: 5 commercial premises; 4 residential properties (p8) [PAG]
* {30 Jul 2021}: Quant: 2 highway flood reports (Castle Lane and by St Nicholas' Church, July 2021) (p9)

### Warwickshire
* {29 Jul 2021 - 31 Jul 2021}: Bands of rainfall associated with Storm Evert tracked over Warwickshire during 29–31 July 2021. A Flood Guidance Statement identified a potential significant risk of surface water and fluvial flooding (very low confidence) ahead of the event. (p6)

### Leamington Spa
* {12 Aug 2020}: Reports of highway/standing water on highways in areas of Leamington associated with the August 2020 rainfall event. (p8)

### Cape Road, Warwick
* {30 Jul 2021}: Intense/exceptional rainfall exceeded the capacity of existing drainage infrastructure in the site's vicinity, producing repeated flooding at the site including internal flooding of residential properties; maintenance works undertaken where possible and advice issued for private property; LLFA investigations underway. (p4)
* {30 Jul 2021}: Quant: fifth flood report at the site within five years; one or more residential properties internally flooded on five occasions (p4)

## Topics without coordinates

### Warwickshire County Council
* {12 Aug 2020}: The council received two flood reports from the site of interest during the August 2020 rainfall event. (p8)
* {01 Aug 2020 - 31 Aug 2020}: WCC arranged/undertook investigation and response: nearby highway gullies were cleansed following a site visit to relieve observed blockages (site visit August 2020). Investigations identified a collapsed gully at one site entrance (damage from HGV traffic); WCC County Highways were notified and repairs to the gully and raised kerb were completed. (p12) [TAB, PAG]
* {12 Aug 2020}: Quant: two flood reports (p8)

### Highways
* {11 Aug 2020 - 13 Aug 2020}: Highway gullies and the highway drainage system received rainfall exceeding their capacity; silt and debris washed from the carriageway into gullies caused blockages and reduced the system's ability to convey surface water away during rainfall, contributing to surface water ingress at the nearby commercial site. Blocked and damaged gullies by the site's access points were identified and a collapsed gully by one entrance was found (later repaired). Nearby gullies were cleaned to relieve observed blockages. (p11) [PAG]
* {11 Aug 2020 - 13 Aug 2020, after August 2020}: Blocked highway gullies were observed and cleansed following a site visit; a collapsed gully at a site entrance (caused by HGV damage) was identified and repaired by WCC County Highways to restore drainage and reduce the risk of surface water flooding. (p12) [TAB, MUL]
* {11 Aug 2020 - 13 Aug 2020}: Quant: one collapsed gully (p11) [PAG]
