

# Northumberland flooding 09 Aug 2019 - 12 Aug 2019

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report August 2019<br>091\_Northumberland/FIR-Aug19-Main-Report.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Northumberland
* Heavy persistent rain caused flash flooding across the county (9–11 Aug 2019), resulting in road closures, overwhelmed surface-water and sewer systems, closures of footbridges over the River Wansbeck and operation of flood gates; localized property and street flooding occurred in multiple localities (examples described elsewhere in the report). (p5)
* {01 Aug 2019 - 31 Aug 2019}: August 2019 rainfall events resulted in flooding across the county, specifically causing highways to flood. (p11)

### West Woodburn
* The River Rede broke its banks through West Woodburn, causing internal flooding of properties. Surface water runoff from the road also affected properties. (p6)
* {01 Aug 2019 - 31 Aug 2019}: Listed in Appendix A as one of the specific locations affected by the August 2019 floods and included in the maps of affected areas produced for the investigation. (p11)
* Quant: 6 properties (p6)

### Otterburn
* The River Rede at Otterburn reached its highest recorded level, causing internal flooding of properties. A combination of the River Rede and its tributary the Otter Burn caused internal flooding of additional properties. To the west of the village, properties were internally flooded due to surface water runoff and overwhelmed Northumbrian Water systems; other properties were protected by interventions. (p6)
* Quant: 6 properties; 3 properties (p6)

### Ovingham
* The Whittle Burn overtopped onto the C254 road due to debris in a culvert, and increased flows (later in the weekend) were caused by overflow from the Northumbrian Water Whittle Dene Reservoir. Three properties were affected in the early hours of 12th August (two of which had property-level protection). Additional local overflows (from the cemetery and a watercourse adjacent to Horsley Road) flowed onto roads/footpaths but did not cause internal property flooding at those locations. (p6)
* Quant: 3 properties affected; 2 properties had PLP (p6)

### Morpeth
* Flood gates were shut by the Environment Agency; footbridges over the Wansbeck were closed to the public as a response to high river/flash flooding. (p5)

### Rothbury
* Flood gates were shut by the Environment Agency; footbridges over the Wansbeck were closed to the public in response to high river/flash flooding. (p5)

### Warkworth
* High-intensity rainfall overwhelmed surface water sewers in the Amble area, causing flooding to the A1068 between Amble and Warkworth (road flooding and travel disruption). (p5)

### Alnwick
* Rain overwhelmed gullies and the surface water system causing water to pond around the war memorial, impeding vehicle movement. (p5)

### Christon Bank
* The T-junction at Christon Bank became inundated with surface water runoff, ponding in a low spot in the road. (p5)

### High Hauxley
* A watercourse broke its banks and flooded around properties; the fire service supplied sandbags to adjacent properties to deflect flood water. (p5)

### Holystone
* Road closures caused by fluvial (river) and surface water runoff issues. (p5)

### Callaly
* Road closures caused by fluvial (river) and surface water runoff issues. (p5)

### Netherwitton
* Road closures caused by fluvial (river) and surface water runoff issues. (p5)

### Greenhaugh
* Road closures caused by fluvial (river) and surface water runoff issues. (p5)

### Bellingham
* Road closures caused by fluvial (river) and surface water runoff issues. (p5)

### Amble
* High intensity rainfall overwhelmed the surface water sewer systems causing flooding to the A1068 between Amble and Warkworth. (p5)

### Bennett's Walk, Morpeth
* Northumberland Fire and Rescue Service operated a pump at Bennet's Walk to remove surface water. (p5)

### Bondgate Without, Alnwick
* Water ponded around the war memorial at Bongate Without, making it difficult for vehicles to pass. (p5)

### Remus Avenue, Heddon-On-the-Wall
* {general}: High-intensity (pluvial) rainfall overwhelmed the local surface water system causing water to pond in the street; water poured through the housing estate adjacent to the street. (p6)

### Aquila Drive, Heddon-On-the-Wall
* {general}: High-intensity (pluvial) rainfall overwhelmed the local surface water system causing water to pond in the street; water poured through the housing estate surrounding properties. (p6)

### Windsor Crescent, Ovingham
* {general}: Water flowed into the road gullies of Windsor Crescent (reported as originating from Ovingham Cemetery / possible groundwater or spring); no internal property flooding was reported. (p6)

### Horsley Road, Ovingham
* {general}: The adjacent watercourse overflowed around property to the footpath; residents cleared debris from the culvert inlet to prevent backup. No properties were internally flooded at these locations. (p6)

### River Rede
* The River Rede broke its banks. At Otterburn it reached its highest recorded level (10th August) causing internal flooding; combined with its tributary the Otter Burn it caused further internal flooding. Through West Woodburn the Rede broke its banks and properties around West Woodburn Bridge suffered internal flooding; surface-water runoff from the road also affected properties. (p6)
* Quant: 6 properties (Otterburn); 3 properties (combined with Otter Burn); 6 properties (West Woodburn) (p6)

### Whittle Burn
* Overtopped onto the C254 road due to debris caught in the culvert; caused flooding that affected nearby properties. Increased flows later in the weekend were caused by an overflow from Northumbrian Water's Whittle Dene Reservoir. (p6)
* Quant: 3 properties (2 with property-level protection) (p6)

### West Woodburn Bridge
* Six properties around West Woodburn Bridge suffered internal flooding when the River Rede broke its banks; properties were also affected by surface water runoff from the road. (p6)
* Quant: 6 properties (p6)

### River Wansbeck
* Footbridges over the Wansbeck were closed to the public due to flooding. (p5)


## Topics without coordinates

### Northumbrian Water
* {general}: NWL's surface-water systems were overwhelmed by high-intensity rainfall, contributing to ponding and surface-water impacts (e.g. Heddon-on-the-Wall) and to internal flooding west of Otterburn. Overflow from the Northumbrian Water Whittle Dene Reservoir increased flows in the Whittle Burn, contributing to flooding impacts in Ovingham. (p6)
* Quant: 3 properties (p6) [MUL]

### Northumberland Fire and Rescue Service
* Operated a pump at Bennet's Walk to remove surface water during the event. (p5)

### Otterburn Bridge
* Road closures caused by fluvial (river) and surface water runoff issues. (p5)

### Ovingham Cemetery
* {general}: Water was reported flowing from the cemetery to the playing-field car park and into the road gullies of Windsor Crescent; investigation suggested the source may have been groundwater or a spring. No properties were internally flooded at these locations. (p6)

### Northumberland County Council
* Acted as the Lead Local Flood Authority (LLFA) and carried out Section 19 investigations into flood incidents after the flood event; managed highway drainage responsibilities (gullies, highway drains) and took responsibility for culverts under highways where applicable. (p8)

### Highway Authority
* Responsible for managing road drainage (gullies and highway drains) on the trunk road network within Northumberland (including A1, A19 and A69) and for culverts under trunk roads; expected to address issues such as blockages or damage that can cause or exacerbate flooding on the road network. (p8)
