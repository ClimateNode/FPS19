## Place names with coordinates

### Northumberland

### West Woodburn
* {04 Dec 2015, 26 Dec 2015, 05 Jan 2016}: The River Rede overtopped its banks and flooded properties in the village; a collapsed NW sewer, blocked drainage and disconnected soakaways contributed to the severity of flooding. (p1) [IMG, MUL]
* {04 Dec 2015, 26 Dec 2015, 05 Jan 2016}: Quant: 6 residential properties (p1) [IMG, MUL]

### River Rede
* {04 Dec 2015, 26 Dec 2015, 05 Jan 2016}: The River Rede rose dramatically and spilled over its banks, causing flooding of properties in West Woodburn. (p1) [MUL]
* {04 Dec 2015, 26 Dec 2015, 05 Jan 2016}: Quant: 6 residential properties (p1) [MUL]
