### River Aire
* Further rainfall over Christmas and Boxing Day produced the highest river levels ever seen in many places along the River Aire catchment, contributing to widespread flooding of communities, homes and businesses. (p7)
* Record high flows in the River Aire (at Armley) produced very high flood levels that were the main cause of widespread flooding; water also came out of channel approximately 0.8 miles upstream of the Armley flow gauge and flowed down Kirkstall Road towards the city centre. (p8) [PAG]
* The River Aire is identified as a dominant feature and a principal cause of flooding to Leeds and adjacent communities. Its steep profile, rapid response to rainfall and many tributaries contributed to river flooding of Leeds city centre and surrounding areas during Storm Eva. (p9) [PAG]
* Source of deep, fast-flowing flood water that rapidly inundated streets (including Kirkstall Road and Wellington Street) and carried floodwater over a distance into the heart of the city; formal and informal defences were on occasion overtopped or bypassed. (p10)
* Flooding originated from the River Aire (a Main River) and is identified in the report as a source of Main River flooding affecting Leeds; the report references flood alleviation measures (River Aire Flood Alleviation Scheme). (p12) [PAG]
* Identified as one of the District's Main Rivers and a major characteristic of Leeds City Centre; the report states the 25–29 Dec 2015 flooding was almost exclusively a Main River fluvial event, implicating the River Aire as a principal source of fluvial flooding that affected communities along its corridor. (p16) [PAG]
* A Main River fluvial event on 25–29 Dec 2015 along the River Aire caused flooding of areas adjacent to the river and contributed to widespread inundation across Leeds MDC (including multiple Aire-focused sub-areas). (p17) [IMG]
* {25 Dec 2016 - 29 Dec 2016}: Dense clusters of flood incidents occurred along the River Aire through the central Leeds corridor, driving the most severe postcode-sector impacts in central and southern Leeds (City Centre, Kirkstall Bridge/Kirkstall Rd, Hunslet). (p18) [IMG]
* {1768, 1775, 1790, 1795, 1866, 1946, 01 Nov 2000 - 30 Nov 2000, 2002, 2004, 2005, 2007, 2008, 01 Dec 2015 - 31 Dec 2015, 25 Dec 2016 - 29 Dec 2016}: Historically a major source of fluvial flooding in Leeds (major floods recorded in 1768, 1775, 1790, 1795, 1866 and 1946; further minor instances recorded in 2000, 2002, 2004, 2005, 2007 and 2008). River Aire flooding has threatened the city centre (e.g. images of flooding in Dec 2015) and is a driver for major flood alleviation planning. (p19) [PAG, MUL]
