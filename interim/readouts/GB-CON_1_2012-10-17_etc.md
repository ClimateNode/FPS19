## Place names with coordinates

### Cornwall
* {17 Oct 2012 - 18 Oct 2012, 24 Nov 2012, 14 Dec 2012 - 16 Dec 2012}: Heavy and prolonged rainfall, together with storm surge and large swell at times, caused surface water, fluvial and coastal/tidal flooding across the county. Roads and properties were flooded (properties specifically reported in Bugle, St Austell, Penzance, Camborne and Redruth); sea defences were badly damaged at Long Rock; coastal flooding affected Looe, Mevagissey, Fowey and other settlements; tidal flood gates were shut in places (e.g. Truro, Polperro). (p1) [MUL]
* {17 Oct 2012 - 22 Dec 2012}: Widespread flooding across the county: coastal overtopping and wave-driven flooding of properties on the south coast when spring tides coincided with low pressure and strong south‑easterly winds; widespread river, surface water and groundwater‑related flooding during the late‑December rainfall event (starting 18 Dec and lasting ~2 weeks) affecting many of the same properties flooded in November. (p2) [PAG]
* {24 Nov 2012 - 27 Nov 2012}: Flooding occurred across Cornwall during the late-November 2012 event. (p5) [TAB, PAG]
* {24 Nov 2012 - 27 Nov 2012}: Widespread flooding occurred across Cornwall during the event; the report records internal flooding of properties in Hessenford as part of this event. (p7) [PAG]
* {22 Nov 2012 - 27 Nov 2012}: Widespread flooding occurred across Cornwall during the late-November 2012 event; the report states flooding occurred across Cornwall. (p9) [PAG]
* {22 Nov 2012 - 27 Nov 2012}: Widespread flooding occurred across Cornwall during the event; surface water runoff from hillsides and elevated river levels contributed to flooding in the county. (p11) [TAB, PAG]
* {22 Nov 2012 - 27 Nov 2012}: Flooding occurred across Cornwall during the November 2012 event; the incident in Newlyn formed part of this wider flooding. (p13) [TAB, PAG]
* {22 Nov 2012 - 27 Nov 2012}: Flooding occurred across Cornwall during the late-November 2012 event; widespread elevated river levels and saturated ground contributed to flooding in the area. (p15) [TAB, PAG]
* {22 Nov 2012 - 27 Nov 2012}: Flooding occurred across Cornwall during the event; at least six properties flooded internally. High groundwater, saturated soils and elevated river levels contributed to widespread flooding. (p17) [PAG]
* {22 Nov 2012 - 27 Nov 2012}: River water discharged from a culvert onto the road and then into rear gardens of a terrace of houses in Trenear, causing internal flooding of properties. (p19) [TAB, PAG]
* {17 Oct 2012 - 18 Oct 2012, 24 Nov 2012, 14 Dec 2012 - 16 Dec 2012}: Quant: 260 properties (p1) [MUL]
