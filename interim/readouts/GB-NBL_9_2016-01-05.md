

# Berwick-Upon-Tweed flooding 05 Jan 2016

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Berwick Upon Tweed<br>091\_Northumberland/Berwick\_FIR\_2015.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Northumberland
* Within Northumberland, the Berwick area experienced surface-water/ordinary-watercourse driven flooding on 05/01/16: runoff from farmland caused culvert failure/overtopping and surcharging, impacting dwellings, businesses and roads in Berwick. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* Floodwater pooled on a residential street in Newfields, partially submerging the road, driveways and front yards and preventing normal access to homes. (p2) [TAB]
* Quant: 3 residential properties; 4 businesses; 3 roads (p1) [IMG, PAG]

### Berwick-Upon-Tweed
* High-intensity rainfall (05/01/16) produced runoff from farmland north of the town that entered Berwick. A business and an adjacent dwelling were affected; a culvert from the farmland was unable to convey flow, causing overtopping and surcharging that affected multiple dwellings; the A1 roundabout north of town was also affected. Northumberland County Council mapping and summary record the event as concentrated in Berwick. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 3 residential properties; 4 businesses; 3 roads (p1) [IMG]

### Newfields, Berwick-Upon-Tweed
* Floodwater pooled across the residential street and extended up driveways and front yards, partially submerging the road and preventing normal access to homes; vehicles are stranded and people are standing at the water’s edge. (p2)

### A1157
* The inlet to the East Hope culvert overtopped onto the A1157 during the event, directly affecting two dwellings; the culvert also surcharged via manholes, affecting a further three properties. (p1)
* Quant: 2 dwellings; 3 properties (p1)

### Northumberland County Council
* {unknown, 05 Jan 2016}: Ordinary watercourse and surface water flooding in Berwick (5 Jan 2016) overwhelmed the East Hope culvert which overtopped at its inlet onto the A1157 and surcharged through manholes. NCC carried out a CCTV survey of the East Hope culvert, made property-level resilience grants available to residents and proposed exploring improvements to the culvert to reduce future risk. (p1) [IMG]
* {unknown, 05 Jan 2016}: Quant: 2 dwellings; 3 properties (affected via surcharged manholes); 1 business and 1 adjacent dwelling affected by runoff from farmland; overall in Berwick: 3 residential; 4 businesses; 3 roads (p1) [IMG]
