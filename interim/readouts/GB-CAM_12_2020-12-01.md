### Alconbury Weston
* Floodwater entered the settlements from surrounding agricultural fields and overland/surface runoff as a result of saturated ground and increased runoff velocity; settlements experienced flooding during the reported event. (p5)
* Significant flooding affected the settlement of Alconbury on 23 December 2020. (p7) [PAG, IMG]
* Flooding from river and surface water led to internal flooding of residential properties and damage to outbuildings/garages/gardens. (p10) [PAG]
* Combined reports from the LLFA and the Alconbury Brook Flood Group indicate widespread localised flooding across the two villages. A total of 95 properties were affected (59 in Alconbury; 36 in Alconbury Weston). Internal residential flooding occurred in multiple locations (46 residential properties internally flooded in Alconbury; 32 residential properties internally flooded in Alconbury Weston). Additional damage was noted to outbuildings, garages and gardens (13 properties in Alconbury; 4 properties in Alconbury Weston). The report groups impacted streets into seven key risk areas (Vinegar Hill, Hamerton Road, North Road, High Street (Alconbury Weston), Brookside, High Street and Rusts Lane, Hill Field and School Lane). (p11) [IMG]

* Alconbury Brook's limited conveyance caused flooding in Alconbury and Alconbury Weston during the December 2020 event. Partners have undertaken and plan further actions (including hydraulic modelling) and the Environment Agency and the local Flood Group are working to improve conveyance; the LLFA is supporting residents and community flood action measures are being delivered in 2021-22. (p21)

### Alconbury Brook
* Identified as a source of flooding in the catchment and contributed to flooding of downstream settlements (feeding into the Great Ouse system) during the reported event. (p5)
* {general}: Flood risk (Flood Zone 3 and Flood Zone 2) is mapped along the course of Alconbury Brook, with the highest risk tightly clustered adjacent to the brook and extending into nearby low-lying fields and some built-up areas (notably near Alconbury and Alconbury Weston). (p6) [IMG]
* Rising river levels in Alconbury Brook were identified as the primary driver of flooding in the Hamerton Road/Spires End area and caused sewer flooding in the Hamerton Road area. (p12)
* Rising river levels in Alconbury Brook caused river flooding that contributed to sewer and internal property flooding in nearby areas (notably North Road and High Street). (p13)
* Alconbury Brook was implicated in the December 2020 flooding of the catchment; flood flows caused erosion of the brook bank beneath the A1 flyover along High Street (work to stabilise the eroded bank has been discussed). The brook has since been the focus of desilting, bank vegetation works and installation of Natural Flood Management structures to reduce future flood risk. (p16)
* Alconbury Brook had insufficient conveyance during the December 2020 flood, prompting flood-alleviation actions and planned hydraulic modelling to increase its ability to convey water during flood events. (p21) [PAG]
* Alconbury Brook was associated with flood-related RMA operational issues noted in the report, indicating it contributed to flooding impacts that required authority action. (p22) [TAB]
* Quant: 7 residential properties (North Road); 8 residential properties (High Street) (p13)

* Surface water run-off flowed down Vinegar Hill into the centre of Alconbury Weston; water entered properties through external walls and floors and was conveyed away through a foul sewer in a wet room. Cellar, outbuilding and garden flooding also occurred. (p12) [TAB]

### Cambridgeshire County Council
* {June 2021}: The county council (via its highways function) was involved in the flood response in Alconbury — Cambridgeshire Highways ordered cleaning of pumps at Rusts Lane (June 2021) and the council issued public advice relating to flood risk for residents. (p17)

### Environment Agency
* The Environment Agency is the reporting authority for Main River flooding and is working with the local flood group to improve Alconbury Brook's ability to convey water following the December 2020 flood, as part of flood alleviation measures. (p21)

### Anglian Water
* {general}: Sewer blockages caused by fat, oil and grease (FOG) and unflushable items lead to sewer flooding and pollution incidents. Anglian Water runs the 'Keep it Clear' campaign with communities to reduce blockages and consequent flooding/pollution. (p21) [PAG]
