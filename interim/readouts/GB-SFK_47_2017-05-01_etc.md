

# Great Barton flooding 01 May 2017 - 31 May 2017 and Great Barton flooding 01 Dec 2020 - 31 Dec 2020

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: The Street, Great Barton<br>124\_Suffolk County/124\_2021\_Suffolk County/section-19-report-the-street-great-barton.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Great Barton
* {01 May 2017 - 31 May 2017, 01 Dec 2020 - 31 Dec 2020}: At least one property in Great Barton (on The Street) experienced internal flooding: May 2017 with foul effluent and surface water (~100 mm) and December 2020 surface water internally (150–225 mm), causing electrical safety issues and temporary rehousing. Several highway flooding incidents in adjacent roads have also been recorded since 2017. (p3) [IMG, MUL]
* {01 May 2017 - 31 May 2017, 01 Dec 2020 - 31 Dec 2020}: Quant: 1 property; internal flood depths approximately 100 mm (May 2017) and 150–225 mm (December 2020); at least 2 properties received retro-fitted non-return valves (p3) [IMG, MUL]

### The Street, Great Barton
* {01 May 2017 - 31 May 2017, 01 Dec 2020 - 31 Dec 2020}: Multiple incidents of flooding affected a single property on The Street: May 2017 internal flooding with foul effluent and surface water (encroached on garden, driveway and private access road); December 2020 internal surface water flooding causing electrical safety issues and toilets to be unusable, with the occupant rehoused in temporary accommodation over the festive period. (p3) [IMG, MUL]
* {since 2017, 01 Dec 2020 - 31 Dec 2020}: Highway gullies surcharged during the storm, resulting in flowing surface water in The Street that accumulated in areas; highway flooding incidents in the adjacent highway have been recorded since 2017. (p4) [MUL]
* {since 2017}: The Street and its access road were the focus of a CCTV survey to investigate and record the sewer network (potential unrecorded culverted watercourse / sewer/drain) after reports from the customer and neighbours; highways gullies on/adjacent to The Street were targeted for cleansing to minimise overland flow from the highway onto the affected site. (p5) [TAB]
* {01 May 2017 - 31 May 2017, 01 Dec 2020 - 31 Dec 2020}: Quant: 1 property; internal flood depths approximately 100 mm (May 2017) and 150–225 mm (December 2020) (p3) [IMG, MUL]


## Topics without coordinates

### Anglian Water
* {01 May 2017 - 31 May 2017}: Responded to the May 2017 foul and surface water flooding at the property and retro-fitted non-return valves to the combined sewer network located within the private access road to address sewer/foul ingress. (p3)
* {since 2017}: Completed actions to reduce sewer-related flooding risk to a property: fitted a non-return valve to a combined sewer, cleansed the Anglian Water sewer and commissioned CCTV investigation of the sewer network in The Street and the access road to identify unrecorded/culverted drains that were reported by the customer and neighbouring properties. (p5) [TAB]
* {01 May 2017 - 31 May 2017}: Quant: Non-return valves retro-fitted to at least 2 properties (p3)

### Suffolk County Council
* {since 2017}: Lead local flood authority role advising property owners on property-level resilience and supporting riparian owners; coordinated actions to ensure surface water conveyance/storage features are cleared and requested cleansing of highways gullies to reduce overland flow from the highway onto the site. (p5) [TAB]

### Highways Authority
* {since 2017}: Overland flow from the highway onto the site was identified as a contributor to flood risk; action recommended to cleanse highways gullies and verify they are functioning to minimise runoff onto the property. (p5) [TAB]
