

# Ollerton flooding 16 Aug 2020

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Ollerton – 16 August 2020<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/ollertonsection19reportaugust2020.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Ollerton
* Excessive surface water from a small privately owned car park flowed into the rear doors/pedestrian access of a cluster of businesses, causing internal flooding of business premises. The flooding was caused by a very isolated intense downpour producing runoff from the car park into the buildings. (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 5 adjacent businesses (p2) [IMG]

### The Forest Centre
* Internal flooding entered the building through rear doors and pedestrian access where surface water from an adjacent private car park ran off into the arcade. Five adjacent businesses were internally flooded; annotations and photos identify a central rear door that allowed ingress affecting four business units and a right‑hand rear door that allowed ingress to one business. Cleanup activity (mop, bucket) was visible at the entry threshold. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 5 adjacent businesses; 4 business units; 1 business (p3) [IMG]
