

# Rothbury flooding 25 Sep 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Rothbury<br>091\_Northumberland/Rothbury-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Rothbury
* Multiple flood impacts across the town on 25/09/2012: demountable flood barrier at Armstrong Cottages held but was close to overtopping with some seepage and gardens inundated (no properties flooded at Armstrong Cottages); surface water flooding on the road from Whitton View; rear of Riverside Pine Trees House flooded from the River Coquet (property itself reported not flooded); sewerage drain at River Close surcharged; Riverside Park public toilets inundated (approx. 7–8 ft reported) and park footbridge overtopped; wall downstream of the Maltings knocked down into the allotments; at the Maltings four properties were flooded by surface water when drains surcharged and a rapid surge entered the courtyard. (p1) [IMG]
* High river levels (River Coquet) led to flood warnings being issued and a floodgate in Rothbury holding back muddy floodwater close to its top; parts of the town (including the Maltings/playground areas) experienced surface water inundation. (p2) [IMG, TAB]
* Quant: 4 properties; courtyard flooded to 50 cm; public toilets flooded to approximately 7–8 ft; 1 road (p1) [IMG]

### Maltings, Rothbury
* Four residential properties at the Maltings flooded from surface water; drain covers surcharged producing a very quick, intense surge of water that filled the courtyard to a depth of about 50 cm. (p1) [IMG]
* Surface water inundated the courtyard area in the Maltings, filling it with floodwater. (p2) [TAB]
* Quant: 4 properties; 50 cm depth (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 50 cm (depth) (p2) [TAB]

### Whitton View, Rothbury
* Surface water flooding on the road from Whitton View; surface water contributed to highway flooding in combination with defence overflow. (p1) [IMG]

### Armstrong Cottages
* Demountable flood barrier held but came close to overtopping with reports of seepage. Overflow from the defence contributed to highway/surface water flooding which caused the wall surrounding Armstrong Cottages to overtop and flood the gardens; property-level protections and the defence prevented internal property flooding. (p1)
* Quant: 0 properties flooded at Armstrong Cottages (p1)

### Riverside Park
* Extensive flooding in the park: public toilets inundated to approximately 7–8 (reported as 7–8ft in the text); floodwaters overtopped the footbridge; downstream wall was knocked down into the allotments by the force of water. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 7-8ft (reported) of water in public toilets (p1) [IMG]

### River Coquet
* The River Coquet was a main source of flooding on 25/09/2012: it caused flooding at the rear of Pine Trees House, contributed to surcharge/overtopping effects that added to highway and surface water flooding (contributing to inundation of Armstrong Cottages' gardens and to surface water impacts at the Maltings), overtopped a footbridge in Riverside Park and contributed to downstream damage (wall collapse near the Maltings). (p1) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### River Close
* The sewerage drain at River Close surcharged during the event; this surcharge did not result in property flooding. (p1)

### Forestry Commission Car Park
* Wrack mark on the car park reached to the very back of the gravelled area, indicating the extent of inundation by floodwaters. (p1)

### Rothbury Playground
* Playground area was submerged by muddy floodwater, with playground equipment, fencing and a small building partially submerged; the flooding caused significant disruption to the site and likely damage to equipment and surfaces. (p2) [IMG]
