

# Kingstone flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Kingstone<br>055\_Herefordshire County/15\_Kingstone.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Kingstone
* Internal flooding of residential properties caused by overland/surface water flow and overflowing sewers; sewage contamination reported. Internal flood depths recorded ranging from less than 6 inches up to 1 ft. Sandbags were delivered to a property during the event. (p2)
* Quant: 5 properties; internal flood depths ranging from <6 inches up to 1 ft; sandbags delivered to 1 property on 16 February 2020 (p2)

### Cage Brook
* {general}: A separate site (located at a low point in the terrain) is at risk of flooding from the Cage Brook and is recorded as being in Flood Zone 3. (p2)


## Topics without coordinates

### Herefordshire Council
* {general}: The highway authority proposes to exercise its flood risk management function and has scheduled investigatory works on the highway drainage in the vicinity of the flood site (works planned for 2021/22). (p3)

### Welsh Water
* {general}: An episode of foul (sewage) flooding has been reported to Welsh Water. (p3)
