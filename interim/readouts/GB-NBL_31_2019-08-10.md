

# Otterburn flooding 10 Aug 2019

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Otterburn<br>091\_Northumberland/Otterburn-FIR\_Aug19.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Otterburn
* Persistent heavy rain caused the village to experience flooding: properties internally flooded from a combination of the River Rede and its tributary (Otter Burn), and additional properties (to the west of the village) internally flooded due to surface water runoff and overwhelmed Northumbrian Water systems. Roads through the village (B6320 and A696) were closed. (p1)
* Quant: 6 properties; 5 residential; 1 business; 3 properties internally flooded (River Rede + Otter Burn); 2 properties internally flooded (surface water/overwhelmed Northumbrian Water systems); 2 roads closed (p1)

### River Rede
* The River Rede peaked at 4.28 m (highest recorded), causing flooding across Otterburn that led to property flooding and road/bridge closures. The river (together with its tributary the Otter Burn) caused internal flooding to some properties and contributed to wider village flood impacts. (p1)
* Quant: 6 properties; 3 properties internally flooded (combined River Rede and Otter Burn) (p1)


## Topics without coordinates

### Northumberland Fire and Rescue Service
* Closed the B6320 (Otterburn road bridge) and A696 roads and used pumps to remove floodwater from affected properties; attempted to pump water away from internally flooded properties. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: 6 properties; 3 properties internally flooded; 2 roads closed (B6320 and A696) (p1) [TAB]

### Northumbrian Water
* Northumbrian Water systems were overwhelmed, contributing to internal flooding of properties to the west of the village. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: 2 properties internally flooded (attributed in part to overwhelmed Northumbrian Water systems) (p1)

### Otterburn Road Bridge
* The Otterburn road bridge (B6320) was closed due to the flood event. (p1)
* Quant: 1 bridge closed (p1)

### A696
* The A696 through Otterburn was closed during the flood event. (p1) [TAB]
* Quant: 1 road closed (A696); 2 roads closed in total in the village (p1) [TAB]
