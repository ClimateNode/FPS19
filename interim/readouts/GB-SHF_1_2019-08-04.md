

# Sheffield Metropolitan District flooding 04 Aug 2019

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report: Investigation of A Flooding Incident at Chesterfield Road, Sheffield<br>109\_Sheffield/Flood Investigation Report Chesterfield Road August 2019.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Sheffield Metropolitan District
* Localized flooding occurred on Chesterfield Road (between Valley Road and Saxon Road) within Sheffield on 4 August 2019, with sewer overflows and surface water cascading onto the highway and posing a safety risk to pedestrians and motorists. (p1)
* The Chesterfield Road area of Sheffield experienced sewer-related flooding and CSO escapes affecting the road corridor and adjacent car parks; flood extents are shown on the map and multiple sewer overflow/escape points are annotated. (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 1 stretch of Chesterfield Road (between Valley Road and Saxon Road) (p1)

### Chesterfield Road, Woodseats
* Floodwater issued from sewer manholes in the highway and cascaded onto the road from a raised car park known to contain an overflow sewer pipeline; flooding affected the Chesterfield Road carriageway and presented a risk to the safety of pedestrians and motorists; Streets Ahead installed warning signs and cones; Yorkshire Water made the site safe, repaired manhole covers and cleansed the area. (p1)
* Flood water issued from sewer manholes within the Chesterfield Rd carriageway due to an obstruction in the Chesterfield Road sewer, causing surcharging and flooding onto the carriageway. Photographic evidence indicates the volume and velocity of water presented a risk to the safety of pedestrians and motorists. (p2)
* Combined sewer overflow (CSO) escape flows and manhole/access cover discharges spilled onto Chesterfield Road: flows spilled over a retaining wall into the road, spilled down steps into the road, and large volumes escaped via manhole/access covers onto the carriageway and adjacent areas. (p4) [IMG]
* Quant: 1 raised car park area (p1)


## Topics without coordinates

### Yorkshire Water
* Yorkshire Water identified a blockage/restriction in a 900mm combined sewer that contributed to sewer backup and combined sewer overflow discharges which led to sewer flows escaping via access covers and manholes, flooding Chesterfield Road and adjacent car parks. (p4) [IMG]
* Quant: 900mm combined sewer blocked / restricted (p4) [IMG]

### Sheffield City Council
* Acted as Lead Local Flood Authority investigating the incident; its highways contractor (Streets Ahead / Amey LG) attended the scene during the incident installing warning signs and cones as immediate action. (p1)

### Car Park
* The car park was flooded by large volumes of sewer flow escaping via CSO access covers/manholes, as shown on the map. (p4) [IMG]
