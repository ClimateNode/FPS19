

# Stanwell flooding 28 Jan 2021

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Investigation Report: Milton Gardens, Stanwell, Staines<br>126\_Surrey County/126\_2021\_Surrey County/Investigation-Report-Milton-Gardens.v2-1.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Milton Gardens, Stanwell
* Subject of a Section 19 investigation into a flood event (report title indicates flooding occurred at Milton Gardens). (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* {28 Jan 2021, 30 Jan 2021, 31 Jan 2021}: Floodwater collected at the junction with Chesterton Drive, limiting access to and from Milton Gardens; external flooding to front gardens and in some cases up to the front door; vulnerable residents were unable to get help. (p2) [TAB, PAG, MUL]
* Surface water unable to drain (soakaways filled by high groundwater) flowed to the low point at the junction with Chesterton Drive, causing surface water ponding/flooding at that junction; the area around Milton Gardens is identified as at risk of groundwater flooding above surface. (p3)
* {general}: Surface water flood risk zones (RoFSW) intersect residential properties and corridors through Milton Gardens; the highest risk (1 in 30) forms linear bands through the area and the play area is adjacent to or partially within flood risk zones, indicating vulnerability of properties and local amenity. (p5) [IMG]
* {28 Jan 2021, 30 Jan 2021, 31 Jan 2021}: Quant: 2 properties flooded internally (across Chesterton Drive and Milton Gardens); 9 properties flooded externally (across both streets); approximately 15 tank loads of water removed from the highway during pumping (p2) [TAB, PAG, MUL]

### Spelthorne
* Low-lying areas in Spelthorne (including Staines) experienced surface water ponding because high groundwater prevented soakaways from draining; groundwater has risen to or above the surface in low-lying locations, causing flooding. (p3)

### Staines-Upon-Thames
* Named in the Section 19 investigation report title, indicating Staines was affected by the investigated flood event. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* The local geology and hydraulic link to the River Thames mean prolonged/high rainfall and high river levels raise groundwater; high groundwater and high river levels can result in surface water ponding and flooding in low-lying parts of Staines. (p3)

### Chesterton Drive, Stanwell
* {28 Jan 2021, 30 Jan 2021, 31 Jan 2021}: Highway flooding with water flowing over the surface toward the low point at the junction with Milton Gardens; properties experienced external flooding to front gardens and some up to the front door; two properties flooded internally due to bow waves from cars. (p2) [TAB, PAG, MUL]
* High groundwater had filled highway soakaways so surface water could not drain; surface water flowed to the low point at the junction with Milton Gardens, causing surface water ponding/flooding at the junction and leaving the highway unable to drain. (p3)
* {28 Jan 2021, 30 Jan 2021, 31 Jan 2021}: Quant: 2 properties flooded internally (across Chesterton Drive and Milton Gardens); 9 properties flooded externally (across both streets); approximately 15 tank loads of water removed from the highway during pumping (p2) [TAB, PAG, MUL]

### River Thames
* {28 Jan 2021 - 30 Jan 2021}: High levels in the River Thames contributed to rising groundwater which filled soakaways and reduced the capacity for highway surface water to drain, contributing to surface water and groundwater flooding at the junction of Milton Gardens and Chesterton Drive. (p2)
* {general}: High River Thames levels are hydraulically linked to local groundwater; when Thames levels are high groundwater rises to similar levels, contributing to surface water ponding and groundwater emerging at the surface in lower-lying areas. (p3)


## Topics without coordinates

### Surrey County Council
* {31 Jan 2021}: Surrey County Council's emergency crew attended late on 31 January 2021 to pump water away from the highway and removed water from the site. (p2)
* {general}: High groundwater filling highway soakaways prevented surface water from draining; water flowed to the low point at the junction of Milton Gardens and Chesterton Drive causing surface water ponding and local highway flooding. Surrey County Council's Local Highway Office has responsibility for keeping soakaways and highway drainage free of obstructions. (p3)

### Spelthorne Borough Council
* {general}: Surface water ponding and groundwater emergence at the low point of the junction of Milton Gardens and Chesterton Drive, causing flooding of the low-lying highway and surrounding area and putting residents at risk. (p3)

### Surrey Fire and Rescue Service
* {general}: Surface water ponding and groundwater emergence at the junction of Milton Gardens and Chesterton Drive created flood risk to residents; Surrey Fire and Rescue are recommended to carry out welfare checks and identify vulnerable residents as part of the response. (p3)
