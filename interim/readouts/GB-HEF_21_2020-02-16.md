

# Orleton flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Orleton<br>055\_Herefordshire County/20\_Orleton.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Orleton
* Internal flooding reported to residential properties on the west side of the village and one further downstream; internal flood depths ranged from less than 6 inches up to 1 ft, with duration typically less than one day. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* Multiple domestic properties experienced internal flooding (depths reported from under 6 inches up to 1 ft) with typical durations under 1 day. Surface-water flooding occurred on roads (notably B4362, King's Road and Millbrook Way). A basement at a property on the B4362 flooded for 6–7 days. Some flooded properties lie within or adjacent to Flood Zone 3, indicating fluvial influence; respondents also reported surface-water runoff and blocked gullies as sources. (p2)
* Quant: 4 properties; 1 property (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 4 properties on the west side internally flooded; a further downstream property; 6 internally flooded properties related to the key flood event; 8 internally flooded during the most acute flood; 1 basement flooded (6–7 days) (p2)

### Kings Road, Orleton
* Surface water runoff and blocked gullies caused surface-water flooding on the road; respondents recorded problems with surface water on King's Road during the flood event. (p2)

### Millbrook Way, Orleton
* Floodwater from Orleton Brook broke out of its channel and emerged onto the highway, draining along Millbrook Way as an ephemeral watercourse; respondents recorded surface-water flooding on Millbrook Way during the event. (p2)


## Topics without coordinates

### Orleton Brook
* Orleton Brook (an ordinary watercourse) contributed to fluvial/surface water flooding in Orleton during the February 2020 event, causing internal flooding to residential properties. (p1) [PAG]
* Orleton Brook (including culverted sections under the B4361) overflowed/diverted from its natural course during storms, breaking out of bank to flood an upstream field, then emerging onto the highway and draining along Millbrook Way; blocked or silted culverts/bridges reduced capacity and contributed to property flooding. (p2)
* {1976-2019, 2007, 16 Feb 2020}: The watercourse has been diverted and culverted below the B4361; during rainstorms it breaks out of its banks flooding the field upstream of the highway. Floodwater then emerges onto the highway and runs along Millbrook Way as an ephemeral watercourse. (p3) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
* Quant: 4 properties; 1 property (p1) [PAG]
* Quant: 4 properties on the west side flooded internally; a further property downstream flooded; 1 basement flooded for 6–7 days (p2)
* {1976-2019, 2007, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: two properties (p3) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
