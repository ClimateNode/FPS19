

# Derbyshire flooding 18 Oct 2023 - 21 Oct 2023

## Place names with coordinates

### Henmore Brook
* Responded rapidly to Storm Babet (short lag between peak rainfall and peak flow) and produced high flows that contributed to flooding in Ashbourne; peak flow during Storm Babet was the second highest on record at the Ashbourne gauging station. (p3) [PAG, MUL]
* Henmore Brook had a very large peak flow during Storm Babet (second highest on the gauging station record), with a short 3-hour lag from peak rainfall to peak flow; the brook on its floodplain contributed to internal flooding of properties to the west of Ashbourne. (p4) [PAG]
* Overbanking of Henmore Brook during the storm caused river flooding that inundated the adjacent residential community in central Ashbourne. Floodwaters entered homes in the Almshouses on School Lane and properties on Coachmans Close and affected non‑residential buildings including St Oswald’s Church Centre (hall), Kingdom Hall and the Bridge Public House on Dig Street. (p5) [PAG, IMG]
* Flooding from Henmore Brook overtopped flood defences and caused internal property flooding in the adjacent Ashbourne community (properties on School Lane, Coachmans Close and nearby non-residential buildings). Flood warnings were issued for the Henmore Brook at Ashbourne during the event. (p6) [IMG, PAG]
* Identified as the primary source of flooding during Storm Babet; overtopping of Henmore Brook banks and a retaining wall allowed flow to escape the channel and inundate adjacent streets and properties (notably School Lane and Coachmans Close). (p7) [IMG]
* Banks of Henmore Brook overtopped, sending water northwards along School Lane into the Almshouses courtyard and properties; overtopping adjacent to School Lane bridge also directed flows that flooded nearby buildings. Flood levels exceeded the previous 1PCSYM AEP design standard at this location and at Coachmans Close. The embankment protecting the Almshouses did not overtop but was purposefully lowered after Storm Babet's peak to allow trapped water to drain and was subsequently reinstated. (p8) [PAG]
* Described as the subject of post-event actions: the Environment Agency is updating the Henmore Brook flood model to investigate the current standard of protection provided by defences and to test whether water can be stored earlier in the Ashbourne Flood Storage Reservoir during lower-magnitude events. These actions follow flooding on 20 October 2023. (p9)
* Quant: Peak flow was the second highest in the station's record; lag time between peak rainfall and peak river flow recorded in Ashbourne was 3 hours. (p3) [PAG, MUL]
* Quant: 16 properties (p4) [PAG]
* Quant: 3 purpose built flood defences overtopped (p6) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 16 internally flooded properties (community total) (p7) [IMG]
* Quant: 10 residential properties; up to 15 cm depth (p8) [PAG]

### Hilton Brook
* Hilton Brook is the main river flowing through the community where internal property flooding occurred during Storm Babet; the text and map link watercourse locations and flooded streets, so the brook is likely a contributing source to the internal flooding in Hilton. (p9) [IMG]
* Overflow of Hilton Brook onto its floodplain caused internal flooding of properties in Hilton during Storm Babet; most flooded homes were west of the brook. (p10) [PAG, IMG]
* The community sits on the Hilton Brook floodplain; most flooded homes were west of Hilton Brook. Internal flooding of properties in the community occurred during Storm Babet. A flood relief channel and engineered high ground adjacent to Uttoxeter Road are identified along the brook. (p11) [PAG, IMG]
* Hilton Brook overflowed upstream of Hilton village, overtopped its channel (upstream of the relief channel) and caused flooding onto Uttoxeter Road and into nearby properties. A blocked culvert beneath Uttoxeter Road was reported to have contributed to upstream overtopping. The Sutton Mill gauge on Hilton Brook recorded its highest-ever level during the event. (p12) [PAG]
* Overflowed (about 600 m upstream of the Uttoxeter Road crossing) and overtopped upstream of the village; a blocked culvert beneath Uttoxeter Road caused the channel to overtop and flood onto Uttoxeter Road, with floodwater flowing westwards and along flow routes to Main Street and Mill Lane, internally flooding local properties. Internal depths reached almost 1 m at some properties; some semi‑detached properties are known to flood frequently; one property had a sump and pump; a few properties only flooded externally. (p13) [PAG, IMG]
* Hilton Brook overtopped/caused flooding at Sutton on the Hill and Uttoxeter Road (Hilton), leading to property flooding. Sandbags provided to some properties were overwhelmed by high flood levels. The flood relief channel was inspected and found to be operating as designed; investigations are exploring redirecting floodwater that currently escapes upstream back into the Hilton Brook channel to reduce future property flooding. (p14) [PAG]
* Quant: 4 residential properties; 2 commercial properties (p10) [PAG, IMG]
* Quant: 5 homes; 3 commercial properties (p11) [PAG, IMG]

### Coachmans Close, Ashbourne
* Residential properties on Coachmans Close, north of Henmore Brook, suffered internal flooding during Storm Babet; parts of Coachmans Close are also identified on long-term mapping as having a high surface-water flood risk. (p4) [PAG]

## Topics without coordinates

### Mansion House Garden
* The Mansion House Garden flood wall / embankment adjacent to Coachmans Close was overtopped when flood levels exceeded its design. The flood wall is reported at target condition; the embankment extension is below required condition and subject to a programme of works. (p6) [PAG]
* Quant: 1 flood wall overtopped (p6) [PAG]

### Ashbourne Flood Storage Reservoir
* The reservoir (FSR), approximately 550 m upstream of the flooded properties, is a purpose‑built storage facility designed to store water during flood events and provide a 1PCSYM AEP standard of protection. It was maintained and considered in good condition in post‑event assessment, though properties downstream still flooded during the event. (p6)
* The Ashbourne Flood Storage Reservoir recorded its highest-ever gauge level (2.52 m) on 20 Oct 2023 during the event; the reservoir functioned as a flood storage asset and is reported to have remained in good condition and is maintained by the Environment Agency. (p7)
* Environment Agency will use an updated flood model (action in response to the 20 Oct 2023 flooding) to test whether water can be stored earlier in this reservoir in lower‑magnitude flood events to reduce downstream flooding. (p9)
* Quant: peak gauge level reached 2.52 m (highest recorded) (p7)

### Dove Catchment
* The Dove sub-catchment experienced heavy rainfall during Storm Babet (18–21 Oct 2023) producing rapid river response; at the Henmore Brook gauging station in Ashbourne the peak flow recorded was the second highest on record, and the brook reacted within a ~3 hour lag time from peak rainfall to peak flow, contributing to property flooding in Ashbourne. (p4) [IMG]
* Quant: 16 properties (13 residential, 3 non-residential) (p4) [IMG]

### Church Hall Car Park Embankment
* The Church Hall Car Park embankment is a purpose‑built flood defence located west of School Lane and was inspected post‑Storm Babet (reported at target condition). It is one of the flood defence assets described in the report in the context of defences in the vicinity that had their design level exceeded by the flood. (p6)
* Quant: 3 purpose built flood defences overtopped (p6)

### Environment Agency
* Led response to the flooding on 20 October 2023 and set short- and long-term actions: short-term works include raising the lowest sections of the flood defences around Coachmans Close (to match current crest level) and raising the embankment to match the coping stones, and de-silting/removal of vegetation from School Lane Bridge (scheduled for 2024). Long-term actions include updating the Henmore Brook flood model to assess standards of protection and to test earlier use of Ashbourne Flood Storage Reservoir in lower-magnitude events. (p9) [PAG]
* {general}: Issued a Flood Alert for the Lower Dove Brooks and Egginton Brook catchment (20 Oct 2023, 10:44) and a Flood Warning for Hilton Brook at Sutton on the Hill and Uttoxeter Road, Hilton (20 Oct 2023, 13:15), alerting to the river flooding that affected the community. (p13) [IMG]
