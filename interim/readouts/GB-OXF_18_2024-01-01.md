### Sydenham
* Blocked/obstructed ditches through Sydenham were identified as contributing to flooding of highways; clearance of these ditches was recommended to reduce flood risk. (p2) [TAB]
* The B4445 toward Thame around Sydenham (and Towersey) lies within EA Flood Zone 3 and is likely functional floodplain (Zone 3b); this stretch experienced highway flooding and was subject to closures. (p5) [IMG, PAG]
* Flooding occurred around Sydenham with water overflowing from adjoining fields into ditches, causing highway flooding at the western end and creating risk to adjacent properties; local works planned to increase ditch capacity and install a gully and carrier pipe to reduce highway flooding. (p12)
* Ditches through Sydenham were identified as blocked/overgrown and contributing to flooding; recommendation issued to clear ditches and remove obstructions to reduce future flooding. (p17) [TAB]

### Sydenham Road, Sydenham

* Sydenham Road experienced highway flooding where water overflowed from fields into roadside ditches; works include clearing the north roadside ditch, excavating a road ditch to receive a new drainage channel across a field entrance, and planned culvert replacements and new gully/carrier pipe to reduce future highway flooding and risk to adjacent properties. (p12)
* Highway culverts on Sydenham Road were damaged during the event and were recommended for replacement due to their failure/impairment of drainage, posing a risk to the carriageway and local drainage. (p17) [TAB]

### Aston Rowant Road, Aston Rowant
* Sections of the B4009 (Aston Rowant Road) were flooded, resulting in road closures. (p5)

### Chinnor Road, Aston Rowant
* The B4009 at Aston Rowant (Chinnor Road) flooded and was impassable during the January event; vehicles broke down in flood water after attempting to drive through. Flooding originated as surface water running down from the Chiltern Hills and accumulated on the highway. Clearance of a root-obstructed culvert/pipe feeding to the nearby pond alleviated the highway flooding; further root cutting work is scheduled. (p8) [IMG]

### Aston Park, Aston Rowant
* The lake at Aston Park received released flood water after the culvert/pipe feeding from the B4009 was cleared of root ingress. OCC Highways inspected the lake with residents to ensure it could accept the influx without causing downstream flooding. (p8) [PAG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Oxfordshire County Council
* Oxfordshire County Council Highways carried out works during and after the January 2024 event to reduce flood risk in multiple locations where highways had become flooded and impassable around Chinnor. (p2)
* Oxfordshire County Council (Highways) inspected the receiving pond/lake at Aston Park with residents to confirm it could accept the drained water without causing downstream flooding; they confirmed the pipe connection to the lake and cleared root obstruction to restore drainage. (p8)
* OCC Highways is the responsible highways authority noted in the report as scheduling clearance (jetting) of the 300mm highway drainage on the B4009 after the January flooding event; this work is due in September 2024 to restore discharge from the highway system. The council is also identified as the body to consider removal of a willow tree obstructing the receiving ditch (subject to consents) to reduce future flood risk. (p9) [PAG]
* OCC Highways documented highway drainage issues during the event, including a ditch off the B4009 with a large tree in the channel that reduced flow and led to standing water. OCC identified overflow from fields into roadside ditches that caused flooding on main roads (reported on the B4445 and around Sydenham/Towersey) and has been involved in discussions and planning to clear ditches and increase capacity. (p11) [IMG, PAG]
* OCC Highways is scheduled to replace culverts under Sydenham Road (planned October 2024) to connect drainage ditches as part of the response to the flooding that occurred, and to install a new road gully and carrier pipe to the watercourse to alleviate highway flooding. (p12) [PAG]
* As Lead Local Flood Authority, OCC investigated the flood incident under Section 19, carried out/commissioned culvert condition surveying that arose from the incident, and is maintaining/updating an asset register of features affecting surface water and ordinary watercourse flooding. OCC has also notified third‑party asset owners and will seek support from other risk management authorities and communities to record and manage assets to reduce flood risk. (p13)
* OCC Highways identified as lead stakeholder to carry out root-cutting of a culvert from the B4009 to a pond in Aston Rowant and to replace damaged highway culverts on Sydenham Road as actions arising from the flood investigation. (p17) [TAB]
* Damage to highway culverts on Sydenham Road requiring replacement works carried out by OCC Highways as a response to the flooding; repair of highway drainage infrastructure to restore carriageway drainage function. (p18) [PAG]

### Highways
* Highway assets were affected by flooding: ditches through Sydenham require clearance and highway culverts on Sydenham Road were reported as damaged and recommended for replacement following the flooding that made routes impassable. (p2) [TAB]
* The highways function (OCC Highways) is identified as scheduling jetting and clearance of the 300mm highway drainage along the B4009 following the January flood event so that the highway drainage can discharge to the cleared ditch; this is an operational response to the flooding on the B4009. (p9) [PAG]
* The B4009 highway carriageway was submerged across its full width by surface water, with water obscuring road markings and partially submerging signs, bollards and a road island. A roadside receiving ditch with an in-channel tree was observed, indicating blocked/insufficient drainage that contributed to surface water pooling on the carriageway and made the road impassable/hazardous. (p10) [IMG, PAG]
* Highway drainage assets were impacted: a roadside ditch off the B4009 was obstructed by a large tree causing pooled water and impaired flow. Overflow from fields into ditches resulted in flooding on the B4445 and around Sydenham/Towersey. Remedial measures described include clearing the roadside ditch on the north side of Sydenham Road, excavating the field watercourse to increase capacity, and installing a road drainage channel across a field entrance to discharge into the excavated ditch adjacent to properties on Sydenham Road. (p11) [IMG, PAG]
* Highways teams (OCC Highways) have surveyed culverts and scheduled works including culvert replacement and installation of road gullies/carrier pipes to address surface water runoff and highway flooding identified during the event. (p12) [PAG]
* Following the January 2024 flood investigation, highway drainage assets were reported as damaged or blocked: highway culverts on Sydenham Road were identified as damaged and recommended for replacement; a culvert from the B4009 to a pond in Aston Rowant had root intrusion requiring root-cutting/clearance; ditches and a tree in a channel off the B4009 at the east end of Chinnor required clearance; clearance of ditches through Sydenham was recommended to reduce flood risk. These issues were raised as direct responses to flood impacts observed during the investigation. (p17) [TAB]
