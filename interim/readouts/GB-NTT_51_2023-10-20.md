

# Radcliffe On Trent flooding 20 Oct 2023

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Radcliffe On Trent – Storm Babet, October 2023<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/radcliffe-on-trent-s19-storm-babet-oct-2023.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Nottinghamshire
* Flooding across the county led to emergency actions: closure of roads, sandbag deliveries, 24-hour Emergency Planning support, initiation of a Section 19 Flood Investigation and delivery/planning of Property Flood Resilience measures for affected properties. (p5)
* Quant: 77 roads (p5)

### Broxtowe
* Communities in Broxtowe were affected by flooding and require/manage recovery actions; local recovery and liaison with affected residents and businesses is being undertaken. (p5)

### Radcliffe On Trent
* Roads and properties in the village were flooded due to sustained heavy rainfall on already saturated ground during Storm Babet. (p1)
* Surface water flooding concentrated along the line of Sykes Drain and adjacent low-lying areas in the village. Residential streets were affected: 14 properties on Clumber Drive, 3 properties on Penrith Avenue and 1 property on Bingham Road were flooded. The Storm Babet event flooding was driven by the volume of water rather than a trash‑screen blockage (although a blockage caused a 2020 event). (p3) [IMG]
* Flooding prompted development/support of a community flood signage scheme and collaboration between NCC officers and the community to develop a local flood action group; properties in the area are part of planned Property Flood Resilience measures. (p5)
* Quant: 14 properties; 3 properties; 1 property (p3) [IMG]

### Clumber Drive, Radcliffe On Trent
* Sykes Drain surcharged and water flowed over the trash screen behind the boundary of 87 Clumber Drive, then followed the local topography down Clumber Drive towards Penrith Avenue, causing flooding of properties on Clumber Drive. (p3)
* Quant: 14 properties (p3)

### Penrith Avenue, Radcliffe On Trent
* Floodwater flowing down Clumber Drive reached Penrith Avenue, causing internal flooding of properties. (p3)
* Quant: 3 properties (p3)

### Bingham Road, Radcliffe On Trent
* At least one property on Bingham Road experienced internal flooding during the event. (p3)
* Quant: 1 property (p3)


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Major surface water flooding associated with the reported incident caused significant impacts across the county; the council responded by attending sites to verify internal flooding, collating and publishing verified flood impact data, providing 24-hour emergency planning support and initiating a Community Flood Recovery Grant to support those affected. (p4)
* As Lead Local Flood Authority, NCC responded to flooding across the county: visited flood-affected residents and businesses to establish sources/mechanisms; provided 24-hour emergency planning support; initiated and led the Section 19 Flood Investigation (liaison with RMAs, parish councils, community groups, residents and businesses); delivered sandbags; closed roads to promote safety and avoid further flood damage; working with a developer to provide additional flood water storage; installing bespoke Property Flood Resilience measures to affected properties. (p5)
* Quant: 77 roads closed across Nottinghamshire (p5)

### Sykes Drain
* The ordinary watercourse (Sykes Drain) surcharged and overtopped/surcharged the trash screen located behind the boundary of 87 Clumber Drive; the resulting flow followed natural topography into Clumber Drive and downstream streets, causing the property flooding recorded. The report contrasts the Storm Babet event (driven by water volume) with a 2020 event caused by a trash‑screen blockage. (p3)

### Rushcliffe Borough Council
* Provided emergency response support during the flooding: delivered sandbags to affected residents, managed and continue to manage recovery in affected Broxtowe communities, actively engaged in the Section 19 Flood Investigation and liaised on site with affected residents. (p5)
