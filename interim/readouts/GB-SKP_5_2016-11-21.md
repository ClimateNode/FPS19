

# Mellor flooding 21 Nov 2016

Reference document:<br>Draft Flood Investigation Report Woodbrook Road, Mellor, Stockport<br>121\_Stockport/121\_2018\_Stockport/Woodbrook\_Road\_Flood\_Investigation\_Report\_March\_2018.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Longhurst Lane, Mellor
* Internal flooding of residential properties on Longhurst Lane caused by overland flood flows and a blocked culvert inlet/trash screen and impediments along the overland flow route; floodwater also discharged onto Longhurst Lane from an on‑site flood flow path at the rear of Woodbrook Road. (p3)
* The culverted section of Cataract Brook under the adopted highway was implicated in the flooding incident on 21 November 2016; the culvert beneath Longhurst Lane conveyed the brook and was central to the incident. (p5)
* Flooding affected properties on Longhurst Lane and disrupted traffic along the road between Marple Bridge and Mellor. (p7) [PAG]
* Floodwater surged across and onto Longhurst Lane on the night of 21st November 2016, overtopping the footway and carriageway and inundating the road. Pedestrians and at least one vehicle were affected. The flooding was associated with a blocked trash screen at a culvert inlet which contributed to water backing up onto the road. (p8) [IMG]
* Designed overland flood relief route was intended to discharge excess floodwater onto Longhurst Lane; obstructions in the channel and a negative gradient impeded flow, contributing to flooding as water exceeded the intended overspill level. (p12) [PAG]
* Overland flow was obstructed upstream by fences and trees, causing water to rise above the low-point and overspill onto Longhurst Lane. (p13)
* Flood flows from the overland flow channel and culverted watercourse discharged onto Longhurst Lane causing flooding of the carriageway/adjacent footpath. Recommendations include lowering the adjacent footpath and kerb by 100 mm, installing an additional gully to take excess flows, and increasing connection pipe diameter from 150 mm to 225 mm. (p16) [TAB, PAG]
* Overland/surface water flows from the local flood flow route affected the carriageway and adjacent footpath and overwhelmed local highway drainage, prompting recommendations to add gullies and improve culvert connections to manage excess flows. (p17)
* Quant: 12 properties (p3)
* Quant: 12 properties flooded; 1 property internally flooded (p7) [PAG]

### Woodbrook Road, Mellor
* Named in the report title (Flood Investigation Report Woodbrook Road) and spatially associated with the marked Woodbrook Road Site, implying the street was the location under investigation for flooding. (p1) [IMG]
* Internal flooding to properties on the residential development occurred during the November 2016 event; flow path and overland discharge arrangements at the rear of the site were noted. (p3)
* Internal flooding occurred to properties on Woodbrook Road during the incident. (p7) [PAG]
* Overland flood relief channel at rear of properties was impeded by obstructions causing backwater that flooded the rear of residential properties; five fences across the flow route restricted flow and led to water rising above the intended overspill level onto Longhurst Lane. (p12)
* Residential properties on Woodbrook Road were flooded. (p14) [PAG]
* Residential properties on Woodbrook Road were flooded by overland surface water flows (flows overtopping the channel/culvert and discharging across the land toward property thresholds). Report recommends re-profiling the overland flow channel so its level is 870 mm below property thresholds and other measures to prevent recurrence. (p16)
* Quant: 5 properties (p3)
* Quant: 5 properties internally flooded (p7) [PAG]

### Stockport Metropolitan District
* The metropolitan borough encompassing Mellor and the Woodbrook Road Site; the draft flood investigation was prepared by the borough’s highways/flood team and concerns a flood event within the borough. (p1)
* Flooding affected the Stockport area, with the event centred mainly in Marple Bridge and causing internal flooding to residential properties within the borough. (p3)

### Mellor
* The village within which the Woodbrook Road Site and Woodbrook Road are located; the report concerns a flood investigation at these locations in Mellor. (p1)
* Flooding caused internal flooding of properties and disruption to local traffic. All five properties on Woodbrook Road flooded internally; 12 properties on Longhurst Lane suffered flooding (one of those internally flooded); one property on Town Street flooded internally. Flooding disrupted traffic on Longhurst Lane between Marple Bridge and Mellor. (p7)
* Residential properties in Mellor (specifically Woodbrook Road, Longhurst Lane and Town Street) were affected by flooding due to a blocked culvert inlet trash screen and impeded overland flood flow route, resulting in ponding and discharge of excess water onto the streets and into properties. (p12)
* Quant: 5 properties (Woodbrook Road) internally flooded; 12 properties (Longhurst Lane) flooded with 1 internally flooded; 1 property (Town Street) internally flooded (p7)

## Topics without coordinates

### Cataract Brook
* Associated with the reported flooding incident on 21st November 2016 through its conveyance into the on-site culvert; the brook (a Main River) and its culverted reach were the subject of the flood investigation. (p5) [PAG]
* {01 May 2012 - 31 May 2012}: A blockage within a culverted section of the brook caused flooding of the derelict Cataract Mill building site. (p6) [IMG]
* The culvert inlet trash screen on Cataract Brook was found totally blocked on 21 November 2016; the blockage caused water to pond and flood adjacent land. The trash screen design and maintenance contributed to the blockage risk. (p12)
* A culvert associated with Cataract Brook was subject to a man‑entry inspection following the flood on 21 November 2016. The Environment Agency found no structural or operational defects within the culvert that could have contributed to the flooding. (p14)
* Flooding from Cataract Brook affected the site in Mellor, prompting recommended mitigation works and a requirement to keep a full record of flooding incidents at the site. (p19)

### Stockport Metropolitan Borough Council
* {21 Nov 2016, 22 Nov 2016}: Stockport Council attended the incident (Forward Incident Officer between 19:30 and 22:50), inspected the site after waters receded, identified a blocked trash screen at the culvert inlet and passed the incident to the Flood Risk Management Team (LLFA). (p7) [MUL]
* Carried out post‑flood maintenance: cleared a blocked trash screen at a culvert inlet and removed trees/obstructions from an overland flood flow channel following flooding. (p9) [IMG, PAG]

### Highway Authority
* Responded to the overland flow/flooding by removing obstructions — confirmed removal of one tree from the flow route after the event to aid flow. (p13)

### Environment Agency
* Undertook a man-entry survey of the culvert following the flood event and found no structural or operational defects that could have contributed to the flooding. (p13)
