### River Sankey Brook

* {26 Sep 2012, 26 Dec 2015 - 27 Dec 2015}: High river levels were recorded during the flood event; peak levels at this location can prevent gravity-drained surface water systems from discharging (outfalls blocked or flap valves closed), potentially causing or worsening surface water flooding. (p6) [MUL]
* River levels on Sankey Brook rose rapidly and exceeded the typical range on 26 December 2015, impeding the discharge of gravity-drained surface water sewers that discharge to the brook (outfalls blocked or flap valves closed) and contributing to flooding of areas served by those sewers (for example Cromwell Avenue). (p7) [IMG, PAG]
* {01 Sep 2012 - 30 Sep 2012, 26 Dec 2015 - 27 Dec 2015}: High brook level prevented the surface water sewer outfall from discharging; the elevated brook level closed the tide flap and led to sewer surcharge and subsequent highway flooding. (p8) [MUL]

### Cromwell Avenue, Warrington Unitary Authority
* Recorded as the location of a flood event and the subject of this Section 19 flood investigation report. (p1)
* A 100m section of the carriageway was inundated with surface water to a depth of 0.15m, causing major disruption to the flow of traffic; highway flood signs were erected by the Council (02:00–10:00 on 27/12/2015) while flooding persisted. (p4) [TAB]
* {01 Sep 2012 - 30 Sep 2012, 26 Dec 2015 - 27 Dec 2015}: Highway flooding at the lowest point caused by rainfall surcharging through gully pots when the UU surface water sewer could not discharge into Sankey Brook because the brook level was higher than the outfall; the closed tide flap caused water to surcharge back through the system and flood the highway. (p8) [MUL]
* Quant: 100m section; 0.15m depth (p4) [TAB]

## Topics without coordinates

### Warrington Borough Council
* {24 Sep 2012 - 26 Sep 2012, 26 Dec 2015 - 27 Dec 2015}: Recorded and responded to surface water flooding of a 100m section of Cromwell Avenue (Callands). Flooding reached approximately 0.15 m depth and caused major disruption to the flow of traffic; the council erected flood warning/signage at 02:00 on 27/12/2015 and removed it at 10:00 on 27/12/2015. Council records also note a similar event in September 2012. (p4) [MUL]
* {01 Sep 2012 - 30 Sep 2012, 26 Dec 2015 - 27 Dec 2015}: The United Utilities surface water sewer receiving the highway drainage was unable to discharge because Sankey Brook level was higher than the outfall; the closed tide flap caused water to surcharge back through the sewer/drainage system, contributing to highway flooding. (p8) [MUL]
* {24 Sep 2012 - 26 Sep 2012, 26 Dec 2015 - 27 Dec 2015}: Quant: 100m section; 150mm depth (p4) [MUL]

### United Utilities
* {01 Sep 2012 - 30 Sep 2012, 26 Dec 2015 - 27 Dec 2015}: The United Utilities surface water sewer serving the area was unable to discharge into Sankey Brook because the brook level was higher than the outfall; this caused the tide flap to close and the sewer to surcharge, sending water back through the drainage system and contributing to highway flooding. (p8) [MUL]

### Highway Authority
* {01 Sep 2012 - 30 Sep 2012, 26 Dec 2015 - 27 Dec 2015}: Rainfall surcharged through gully pots and flooded the highway at its lowest point because the highway drainage (fed to the UU surface water sewer) could not discharge into Sankey Brook; water surcharged back through the system and onto the carriageway. (p8) [MUL]
