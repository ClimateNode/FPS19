

# Widdrington Station flooding 28 Jun 2012 and Widdrington Station flooding 07 Jul 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Widdrington Station<br>091\_Northumberland/Widdrington-Station-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Widdrington Station
* {28 Jun 2012, 07 Jul 2012}: Central residential area experienced surface water runoff following heavy rainfall. Runoff from an unadopted road flowed downhill into Old Ferneybeds Road and Lintonburn Park, overwhelming the sewerage system (reports of manhole surcharging). Water flowed down driveways and flooded a residential property and affected nearby roads; floodwater may also have entered a nearby vacant plot. (p1) [IMG, MUL]
* {28 Jun 2012, 07 Jul 2012}: Quant: 1 residential property; 2 roads (p1) [IMG, MUL]

### Lintonburn Park, Widdrington Station
* {28 Jun 2012, 07 Jul 2012}: An unadopted road with poor drainage and standing water contributed to surface water flooding affecting Lintonburn Park; this has led to property flooding historically and prompted consideration of property-level protection grants. (p2) [IMG]
