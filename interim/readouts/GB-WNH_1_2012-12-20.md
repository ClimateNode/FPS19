### Cogenhoe

* Surface water runoff from neighbouring land and the emergence of groundwater create flood risk to properties; the village is referenced as the area where the public sewerage system and other interventions are needed to prevent internal and external flooding. (p19)

## Topics without coordinates

### Cogenhoe Catchment
* {01 Apr 2012 - 30 Apr 2012, 01 Jul 2012 - 31 Jul 2012, 01 Nov 2012 - 30 Nov 2012, 20 Dec 2012}: Experienced significant rainfall and antecedent well‑above‑average rainfall (April, July, November 2012) that saturated the ground and contributed to flooding events in the area, including the basement flooding on Whiston Road. (p8) [MUL]
* {up to seven times in the year to 2012, 14 Jul 2012, 04 Nov 2012, 21 Nov 2012, 20 Dec 2012}: The ordinary watercourse in the catchment rose (at a 450mm diameter pipe culvert) and water infiltrated through rear garden boundary brick walls to about 300mm above ground level, causing flooding of basements, gardens and outbuildings of two semi‑detached residential properties. Water may have flowed backwards up the basement drainage system into basements. Foul water solids were identified in the flow in the watercourse and an ornamental pond on neighbouring farm land. (p11) [MUL]
* {01 Apr 2012 - 30 Apr 2012, 01 Jul 2012 - 31 Jul 2012, 01 Nov 2012 - 30 Nov 2012, 20 Dec 2012}: Quant: two properties flooded (basements); rainfall events in April 2012, July 2012 and November 2012 (p8) [MUL]
* {up to seven times in the year to 2012, 14 Jul 2012, 04 Nov 2012, 21 Nov 2012, 20 Dec 2012}: Quant: two basements; two semi‑detached residential properties; 450mm diameter pipe culvert; approximately 300mm above ground level infiltration; flooding incidents up to seven times in the previous year; recorded dates 14/07/2012, 04/11/2012, 21/11/2012, main event 20/12/2012 (p11) [MUL]

### Northamptonshire County Council
* Highway drainage capacity and assets in the village catchment contributed to surface water runoff risk to properties. NCC Highways are instructed to cleanse highway drainage regularly, map assets, assess capacity and identify where insufficient capacity could lead to property flooding and consider improvement works to reduce flood risk. (p18)

### Environment Agency
* {general}: Requested to consider interventions relating to publicly and privately owned foul water treatment systems in the vicinity of watercourses and pollution prevention to address flood-related pollution risks. (p19)
