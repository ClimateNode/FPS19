## Topics without coordinates

### Midlands
* {16 Feb 2020, 16 Jun 2020, 16 Aug 2020 - 17 Aug 2020}: A number of storms in 2020 caused widespread flooding across the Midlands region, impacting many areas (including Cannock Chase District) and generating a significant number of reported flooding incidents. (p4) [MUL]

### Staffordshire County Council
* {16 Feb 2020}: The debris screen owned and maintained by SCC Highways became blocked, preventing flow through a culvert on an Ordinary Watercourse. Water breached the embankment and spilled onto the Burntwood Road, inundating the highway and properties in the vicinity (including Stag Crescent). SCC has approved a Business Case (authorised by the Environment Agency) for FDGiA-funded upgrade to a raking debris screen to reduce future blockages. (p23) [TAB]
* {2020}: Following the 2020 storm events, flooding to highways was reported in the area; as the highways authority the council has responsibility for highway drainage and managing this risk. (p32)
* {2020}: Following the 2020 storm events, incidents reported across the county included internal property flooding, external garden flooding and highway flooding. Surface water runoff was channelled by highways and ponded in low points, spilling from highways into properties; watercourses exceeded culvert capacity causing back-up and riverbank overtopping; highway gullies and drainage were unable to cope with intense runoff. (p33)

### Severn Trent
* {14 Jun 2020}: The main surface sewer operated by Severn Trent discharged to a local watercourse; the outfall showed subsidence and sewers at the location appeared restricted or became overwhelmed during the storm, contributing to flooding. Severn Trent was asked to carry out CCTV surveys and repair the outfall. (p27) [TAB]
* {2020}: In partnership the authorities identified causes and proposed actions: highway-channeled surface water ponded at road low points and spilled into properties; culvert constrictions caused river back-up and overtopping; overwhelmed sewer infrastructure discharged from manholes and gullies, exacerbating flooding of properties and highways. (p33)

### Cannock Chase Council
* {14 Jun 2020}: Cannock Chase Council was asked to monitor and consider embankment stabilising works along with Severn Trent where embankment slippage was reported, and to manage footways/hedgerow maintenance to reduce gully blockages that contributed to flooding. (p27)
* {2020}: Following the 2020 storm events, external flooding to gardens and public open spaces was reported; Cannock Chase District Council's Leisure Services are responsible for maintenance of public open space and watercourse features in these areas. (p32)

### Highways
* {14 Jun 2020}: Highways was advised to review footpath slopes/rises and assess gully channel drainage because drains and road gullies were believed to have become blocked; road/channel issues contributed to roads becoming inundated during the storm. (p27) [TAB]

### Environment Agency
* {2020}: Flow in watercourses in some areas exceeded available capacity, particularly where watercourses entered culverts, resulting in constricted flows, back-up, overtopping of riverbanks and river flooding spilling from channels into surrounding areas. (p33)
