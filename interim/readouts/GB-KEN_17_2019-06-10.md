

# Snodland flooding 10 Jun 2019

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Snodland<br>063\_Kent/Snodland-section-19-flood-investigation-report.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Snodland
* Around 20 properties were flooded by surface water; many of those were affected internally. (p1)
* Flooding across the town caused internal flooding to numerous properties, internal flooding at a retail depot, damage to vehicles at Lakeside, and internal flooding at St Katherine’s School (21 classrooms) which led to the school being closed for 2 days. Several roads experienced highway flooding. (p2) [IMG]
* Drainage network and watercourse infrastructure in Snodland were observed with blockages and reduced conveyance after the 10 June 2019 event: culvert inlets (e.g. at Paddlesworth Lake and Birling Road) had debris/litter lodged against security bars, a security screen at Saltings Way had significant debris accumulation, and standing water was observed in the open channel between Salting Road and the A228, indicating impaired drainage and local surface water pooling. (p4)
* Parts of the town experienced surface water flooding on the routes shown by the Environment Agency surface water flood map; locations of flooded properties correspond to the watercourse route through the town. The town's impermeable urban surfaces and local drainage arrangements contributed to surface water run-off and flooding of properties. (p5) [IMG]
* Intense rainfall produced significant overland flows that drained towards culverted watercourses and collected in topographical lows. Deep surface water flooding affected multiple streets (Recreational Avenue, Rectory Lane, Saltings Road). A blocked drain caused road flooding and external flooding to property (Covey Hall Road). Overland flow from a school playing field entered school buildings, affecting classrooms; sections of a flat roof collapsed and a private rising main surcharged within the main building causing extensive damage. KFRS attended reports of internal flooding at a property where water reached 6 inches. (p8) [TAB]
* Prolonged and intense rainfall on saturated ground caused widespread surface-water and overland flow across Snodland. Drainage capacity was exceeded and water accumulated in topographical low points, flooding multiple roads (Pridmore Road, Robert Road, Birling Road, Recreational Avenue, Rectory Close Oxford Street, Saltings Road) and entering buildings. At the school on St Katherines Lane overland flow entered the buildings affecting classrooms; sections of flat roof collapsed and the private rising main surcharged within the main building causing extensive damage. KFRS attended internal flooding at a property on St Katherines Lane; KCC Highways deployed a tanker at Lakeside to control floodwater. (p9)
* A heavy rainstorm (10 June 2019) produced extensive flooding from overland flows and overwhelmed drainage. Deep surface water collected at local low points and above the culverted watercourse, causing flooding to properties as water could not discharge quickly enough through the surface water sewer; floodwater drained away after the rainfall ceased. (p10)

* Quant: 18 classrooms; 1 property (internal flooding); water depth 6 inches (p8) [TAB]

### St Katherine's Lane, Snodland

### Lakeside, Snodland
* Floodwater reached sufficient depth to affect 6 properties and caused damage to vehicles. (p2) [TAB]
* Surface water collected at the topographical low point, flooding the highway and entering property. Drainage had nowhere to disperse and was overwhelmed. (p9)
* Tanker attended to clear flood water and all gullies were cleansed. CCTV of soakaways found all were holding water at inspection; groundwater ingress has reduced soakaway capacity and one soakaway had a foul misconnection from a private property, compromising their ability to accept surface water. (p10)
* Quant: 6 properties; damage to vehicles (number not specified) (p2) [TAB]
