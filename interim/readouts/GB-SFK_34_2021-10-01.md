### Green Road, Mendlesham Green
* During Storm Franklin (Feb 2022) surface water/overland flow cascaded down the road toward the pumping station, producing standing water outside properties (garden) and on a shared private driveway leading off the highway. Residents reported no internal flooding for at least one property, and concern that an extreme event could cause internal flooding at another property. (p4) [PAG, MUL]
* {2017, 01 Oct 2021 - 28 Feb 2022}: Surface water ran down Green Road toward the pumping station during Storm Franklin, causing standing water outside the nearby property; this section of Green Road has a historic record of road flooding (one report to the County Council in 2017). (p5) [MUL]
* {general}: Surface water runoff on Green Road flowed onto a private driveway at the front of a residential property, pooling and running across the gravel drive and onto the property, indicating inadequate roadside drainage and direct inundation of the driveway. (p7) [IMG]
* {general}: Surface water from the highway and adjacent private land drained southerly toward the private driveway and front door, allowing ingress of water into the property (including at the external side wall) and causing internal flooding and mould on the internal face of the external wall. (p10)
* Quant: 10 properties consulted; 4 consultation responses; 2 properties reported flooding (p4) [PAG, MUL]
* {2017, 01 Oct 2021 - 28 Feb 2022}: Quant: 1 report (2017) (p5) [MUL]
* {general}: Quant: 80 mm internal flood depth (p10)
