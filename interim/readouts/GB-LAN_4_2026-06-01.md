

# Lancashire flooding June - September 2012

Reference document:<br>Lancashire County Council Flood Investigation Report: Lancashire Summer 2012<br>069\_Lancashire County/flood-investigation-report.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Lancashire
* Between June and September 2012 Lancashire experienced a number of significant flooding events: several river levels and rainfall gauging stations hit record highs, monthly-average September rainfall fell in 2 days in parts of the region, and there was extensive localised and internal property flooding across the county. (p3) [PAG]
* {summer 2012}: Significant internal property flooding occurred across various parts of the county during the summer of 2012, prompting a Section 19 investigation. (p4)
* Internal property flooding occurred in various places across the county during the summer 2012 flood events; heavy rainfall on 22/23 June 2012 led to rapid river level rises across Lancashire. (p9)
* Severe flooding in June affected multiple communities (Croston, Whalley, Leyland, parts of Rossendale and Darwen). Causes recorded included river (Yarrow) overtopping, tributary exceedance and surface water blocking drainage; sewer overloads also caused property flooding. Post-event repair and flood-alleviation works were initiated. (p11)
* Widespread flooding across the county and multiple district areas prompted multi-agency emergency response and strategic debriefing. The report records property flooding including 7 properties flooded in Sunnyside Terrace. (p15) [PAG]
* County-wide response to the June 2012 floods: proactive use of pumping stations to increase watercourse storage capacity in advance of the event; organisation and promotion of community drop-in facilities to inform and support residents; convening multi-agency coordination through the Lancashire Resilience Forum debrief (25 July) and Lancashire County Council internal debrief (16 July); actions to improve notification and coordination of rest centres and evacuations; review and follow-up of flood warning/signup, contact information, trigger levels in LRF plans, teleconferencing and community messaging (including sandbag provision). (p17) [PAG]
* Flooding in June 2012 across the county prompted county and multi‑agency response, an LCC internal debrief on 16 July 2012 and additional Making Space for Water meetings; actions were allocated to agencies to improve warnings, communications and operational response. (p18) [PAG]
* Properties within the county were flooded: West Lancashire experienced properties flooded by both river and surface water flooding in September 2012; areas of Rossendale had properties flooded (70 properties in Rossendale were identified as flooded). (p25) [PAG]
* Lancashire was affected by the September 2012 event, with the primary property-flooding impact occurring in West Lancashire (within the county). (p26)
* County-wide impact from the 2012 floods that tested flood response and risk-management arrangements; triggered strategic-level investigations and operational responses in various districts (including investigations into highway drainage performance and rectification responsibilities for field flooding in West Lancashire); use of district 'Making Space for Water' partnership meetings to agree responsibilities, share investigation findings and identify actions; learning and changes to practice by risk management authorities and emergency services. (p29) [PAG]
* Quant: Up to 72 properties in Croston identified as having suffered some degree of flooding; UU sample contacts in Lancashire districts include 86 (South Ribble), 58 (Chorley) and 21 (Rossendale). (p11)
* Quant: 7 properties (p15) [PAG]
* Quant: 70 properties (p25) [PAG]
* Quant: 19 properties flooded (in and around Ormskirk, West Lancashire) (p26)

### Rossendale
* Various parts of Rossendale experienced severe flooding in June; United Utilities also responded to wastewater/sewer-related customer contacts from the June incident. (p11) [MUL]
