

# Hexham flooding 05 Dec 2015

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Hexham<br>091\_Northumberland/Hexham\_FIR\_2015.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Tyne Green, Hexham
* The River Tyne spilled onto the Tyne Green golf course and flowed through the Tyne Green rail underpass, flooding houses and a caravan park on Tyne Green. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* Identified as an area affected by the floods and subject to investigations and mitigation: worked on provision of a flood gate (including at the Tyne Green underpass) and a wider Tyne Green defence scheme; community engagement with residents regarding delivery and contributions. (p2) [TAB]

### Tyne Mills Industrial Estate
* Tyne Mills Industrial Estate sustained heavy flooding; average flood depth reported for the Tyne Mills area. (p1)
* Quant: average depth 150 mm (p1)

### Bridge End Industrial Estate
* Floodwater overtopped the flood bank at Bridge End Industrial Estate, causing heavy flooding across the estate. (p1) [PAG]
* Described as containing affected areas where investigations were to be carried out following the floods to assess impacts and inform any future works. (p2) [TAB]
* Quant: depths between 2 inches and 2 feet (p1) [PAG]

### Kielder Water
* {changes to maximum water level proposed to be implemented from 1 November 2016}: Actions taken to manage flood risk by changing reservoir operations: meetings held to consider reducing the maximum operating water level to provide greater downstream flood water attenuation; the reservoir’s operating rules/data were transferred to new water-resources modelling software (Aquator) to determine an acceptable maximum water level that would reduce downstream flooding. (p2) [TAB]

### River Tyne
* The River Tyne and its tributaries swelled and overtopped flood defences, causing water to spill onto adjacent land (notably Tyne Green Golf Course), flow through the Tyne Green rail underpass, and flood nearby properties and industrial estates. (p1) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Tyne Green Golf Course
* River Tyne spilled onto the golf course as the river swelled and overtopped defences during Storm Desmond, inundating the golf course area. (p1) [IMG]

### Northumberland County Council
* Responded to river flooding in the area: made a property-level resilience grant available to residents, opened a rest centre on standby, progressed investigations in affected areas, progressed provision of a flood gate at the Tyne Green underpass, engaged with the community and Parish Council, completed an initial economic assessment following the floods, and progressed delivery of the Tyne Green defence scheme in coordination with other RMAs. (p2) [TAB]

### Environment Agency
* {general}: Environment Agency engaged in post-flood response and planning: progressed delivery of Tyne Green defence scheme and met to discuss reducing the maximum water level in Kielder Reservoir to provide greater downstream flood water attenuation; proposed review of the Flood Warning Service following Tyne Valley Modelling Review. (p2) [TAB]
