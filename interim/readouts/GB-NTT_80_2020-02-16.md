

# Tollerton flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Tollerton – February 2020<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/tollertonsection19reportfebruary2020.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Nottinghamshire
* {15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020}: Widespread flooding across the county during Storm Dennis: flood warnings and alerts were issued and county-scale response actions (road closures, signage and sandbagging) were implemented. (p1)
* Extensive flooding occurred across the Nottinghamshire area on 16 February 2020 after prolonged heavy rainfall on saturated ground, including impacts to properties, gardens, curtilages and public highways (e.g. in Tollerton). (p2) [IMG]
* {15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 38 flood warnings; 16 flood alerts; 51 road closures; over 70 flood signs; over 4,000 sandbags (p1)

### Rushcliffe
* Communities in Rushcliffe were affected by the flooding event; residents received sandbags, emotional support visits, and recovery grants. Local community resilience and volunteer efforts mitigated impacts. (p7)

### Tollerton
* On 16 February 2020 Tollerton suffered a significant flood event: 14 reported incidents of internal property flooding; a number of gardens and curtilages were flooded; public highways were affected and both Tollerton Lane and Cotgrave Lane were closed. The flooding replicated events from November 2019. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Following prolonged wet weather, heavy rain on 16 February 2020 caused extensive surface-water/urban flooding in the village: 14 reported incidents of internal property flooding; gardens, curtilages and public highways were also flooded. The event replicated flooding from November 2019 and contributed to investigations and an action plan to reduce repeat incidents. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 14 reported incidents of internal property flooding (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 14 reported incidents of internal property flooding (p3) [IMG, PAG]

### Tollerton Lane, Tollerton
* Flooding on 16 February 2020 affected the public highway to the extent that Tollerton Lane was closed due to the severity of flooding. (p3) [IMG]

### Cotgrave Lane, Tollerton
* Flooding on 16 February 2020 affected the public highway; Cotgrave Lane was closed due to the severity of flooding and areas of internal flooding are highlighted along Cotgrave Lane (at the junction with Tollerton Lane and further east). (p3) [IMG]

### River Trent
* The River Trent corridor had concentrated flood warnings and alerts during the February 2020 Storm Dennis event; river-valley flooding along the Trent contributed to widespread flood risk across central England during the 16 February 2020 event. (p2) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* {15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020}: Lead local flood authority for the area; Nottinghamshire experienced widespread flooding during Storm Dennis with many warnings and alerts in place and a coordinated response (via contractors) including road closures, signage and sandbag distribution. (p1)
* {15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 38 flood warnings; 16 flood alerts; 51 road closures; over 70 flood signs; over 4000 sandbags (p1)

### Environment Agency
* {general}: Issued a record-breaking number of flood warnings and alerts in England on 16 February 2020 in response to the widespread heavy rainfall and flooding. (p2)

### Rushcliffe Borough Council
* Provided emergency response support during the flood event: delivered sandbags to affected residents; managed and continue to manage recovery; administered the Flood Hardship Fund, Community recovery grant and council tax and business rate relief grant scheme; continues to administer the property flood resilience grant scheme; arranged additional emotional support through British Red Cross team visits. (p7)
