

# East Markham flooding 07 Nov 2019

## Place names with coordinates

### East Markham
* The village was included in the report as experiencing extensive flooding during the November 2019 event associated with intense rainfall and saturated ground. (p1)
* Significant flood event in the village causing internal flooding of properties, flooding of public highways, and inundation of gardens and curtilages; the local watercourse serving the village was implicated. (p2) [IMG]
* Village-wide flooding during prolonged heavy rainfall: internal flooding of properties, inundation of public highways, and flooding of gardens and curtilages linked to a local riparian watercourse and overwhelmed highway drainage/culvert. (p3) [PAG]
* Quant: 4 internal property incidents on York Street; 1 internal property on Great Lane (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 4 properties (internal flooding) on York Street; 1 property (internal flooding) on Great Lane (p3) [PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* {07 Nov 2019 - 14 Nov 2019}: Nottinghamshire experienced extensive flooding following intense rainfall, including road network disruption and community impacts across the county (including East Markham). A major incident was declared and multiple flood warnings/alerts were issued. (p1) [PAG]
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Severe weather and prolonged heavy rainfall caused surface water and fluvial (river) flooding across the county; Nottinghamshire Local Resilience Forum declared a major incident. The period included sustained monitoring of water levels and widespread warnings/alerts. (p2)
* {07 Nov 2019 - 14 Nov 2019}: Quant: 66 road closures; over 750 flood signs; over 5000 sandbags; 38 flood warnings; 16 flood alerts (p1) [PAG]
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Quant: 38 flood warnings; 16 flood alerts (p2)

### Nottingham Unitary Authority
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Figure 2 and the report text place Nottingham within a north–south corridor with a high density of flood warnings and alerts in November 2019. Nottinghamshire declared a major incident and the Environment Agency issued 38 flood warnings and 16 flood alerts across Nottinghamshire that month. (p2) [IMG]
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Quant: 38 flood warnings; 16 flood alerts (p2) [IMG]

### East Midlands
* {07 Nov 2019 - 14 Nov 2019}: Parts of the East Midlands experienced extremely intense rainfall (a month's worth in 24 hours) which led to extensive flooding across the area, contributing to surface water and fluvial (river) flooding and a major incident declaration affecting the region. (p1)

### North England
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Figure 2 and accompanying text show a concentration of flood warnings and alerts issued across central and northern England in November 2019, indicating significant flood risk (clustered along river valleys and urban corridors). (p2) [IMG]

### York Street, East Markham
* Internal flooding of properties on York Street during the village flood event. (p2)
* Highway surface water system was overwhelmed during heavy rain, resulting in internal flooding of properties, and inundation of the road and adjacent curtilages. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 4 internal property incidents (p2)
* Quant: 4 properties (internal flooding) (p3) [IMG, PAG]

### Great Lane, East Markham
* Internal flooding of at least one property on Great Lane as part of the same flood event. (p2)
* Surface water ran down Great Lane into a riparian watercourse; one reported internal property flooding occurred where highway gullies and drainage were insufficient during prolonged heavy rainfall. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 1 internal property (p2)
* Quant: 1 property (internal flooding) (p3) [IMG, PAG]


## Topics without coordinates

### North Nottinghamshire
* {07 Nov 2019 - 14 Nov 2019}: Extensive county-wide flooding occurred in north Nottinghamshire during the November 2019 event; county response actions included road closures, placement of flood signs and distribution of sandbags, and numerous flood warnings/alerts were issued. (p1)
* {07 Nov 2019 - 14 Nov 2019}: Quant: 66 road closures; over 750 flood signs; over 5000 sandbags; 38 flood warnings; 16 flood alerts (p1)

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* {07 Nov 2019 - 14 Nov 2019}: Extensive flooding occurred across the county; actions were carried out on the county's behalf (Via East Midlands Ltd. facilitated road closures, placed flood signage and delivered sandbags) in response to the flooding and a major incident was declared. (p1)
* {07 Nov 2019 - 14 Nov 2019}: Quant: 66 road closures; over 750 flood signs; over 5000 sandbags (p1)

### Environment Agency
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: The Environment Agency monitored water levels constantly during the event and issued formal flood communications across the county in response to the flooding. (p1) [PAG]
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Quant: 38 flood warnings; 16 flood alerts (p1) [PAG]
