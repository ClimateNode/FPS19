## Place names with coordinates

### West Berkshire
* Flooding occurred across the West Berkshire area during the early part of 2024; the S19 investigation report was produced to review the event and inform prioritised mitigation and resilience measures. (p1) [TAB]
* {general}: Properties across West Berkshire are at risk of future flooding. The S19 report proposes measures intended to reduce future flood risk to those properties; the report notes those measures may have impacts on third‑party property and assets belonging to other flood risk authorities. (p2)
* Widespread flooding across the district between January and April 2024: many river level gauges and groundwater boreholes recorded their highest on record. The storms in January 2024 led to internal and external property flooding affecting residents and businesses and caused road closures. (p3) [PAG, MUL]
* Widespread internal and external property flooding to residents and businesses across the district and road closures. Flooding sources identified in the report include groundwater, fluvial (river), pluvial (surface water) and sewer flooding. (p4) [MUL]
* Widespread flooding impacted many communities across the West Berkshire district during the Storm Henk event, causing internal flooding of properties and prompting distribution of government grants and other recovery measures. (p6)
* Quant: 77 properties flooded internally (across 10 parish council areas) (p4) [MUL]
* Quant: 45 properties received Community Recovery Grant; 45 properties awarded Council Tax Discount; 9 businesses received Business Recovery Grant; 41 Property Flood Resilience applications approved in principle (7 completed and claimed); £120,450.80 total grant funding distributed (p6)
