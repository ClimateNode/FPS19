

# Riding Mill flooding 05 Dec 2015

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Riding Mill<br>091\_Northumberland/Riding\_Mill\_FIR\_2015.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Northumberland
* Areas within the county (notably Riding Mill) experienced river flooding during Storm Desmond when the River Tyne and its tributaries swelled and overtopped banks; the March Burn backed up and overtopped where it could not discharge to the Tyne, flooding properties adjacent to the watercourses. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 5 residential properties (p1) [IMG]

### Riding Mill
* River Tyne and its tributaries swelled and overtopped banks at Riding Mill. The March Burn could not discharge into the Tyne due to high river levels, causing it to back up, overtop its banks and flood properties. Residential properties were flooded and property-level resilience grants were offered. (p1) [IMG]
* Private garden in Riding Mill was flooded (inundation of residential garden area). (p2)
* Quant: 5 residential properties (p1) [IMG]

### River Tyne
* The River Tyne swelled and overtopped its banks at Riding Mill, causing flooding and preventing the March Burn from discharging which led to additional backing up and property flooding. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* EA proposed updating the Tyne model and to include recommendations for investigating the Tyne's interaction with the March Burn following the floods, indicating the river's involvement in the flood event under investigation. (p2)

### Kielder Water
* {general}: Discussed reducing the maximum water level at Kielder Reservoir to provide greater flood water attenuation; reservoir modelling was transferred to new water resources modelling software (Aquator) to determine acceptable maximum water levels to inform flood management. (p2)


## Topics without coordinates

### Northumberland County Council
* Following the floods NCC completed an initial economic assessment to evaluate whether future flood risk management improvement works would be cost beneficial; NCC made property-level resilience grants available for residents to install defences and increase property resilience. (p2)

### Environment Agency
* {general}: Carried out localised CCTV sewer surveys after the floods; engaged in discussions about reducing the maximum water level at Kielder Reservoir to provide greater flood water attenuation; proposed updating the Tyne model to include investigation of interaction with the March Burn. (p2)

### Northumbrian Water
* Proposed carrying out property‑level protection on a wastewater pumping station and manholes in response to the floods; proposed testing new modelling/software with the EA to decide an acceptable maximum water level at Kielder to inform flood attenuation decisions. (p2)
