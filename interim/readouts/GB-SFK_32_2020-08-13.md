

# Haverhill flooding 13 Aug 2020 - 14 Aug 2020

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Waveney Terrace, Haverhill, Suffolk<br>124\_Suffolk County/124\_2020\_Suffolk County/section-19-report-waveney-terrace-haverhill.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Waveney Terrace, Haverhill
* Subject of a Section 19 flood investigation by Suffolk County Council (LLFA) into a flood event affecting the street. (p1)
* Internal flooding of residential properties on Waveney Terrace during the flood event; properties experienced internal water entry. (p2)
* Internal flooding of properties: water entered to the front of main living areas. (p3)
* Surface water flooding affected the highway at the junction with Eden Road; the raised area of carriageway at this junction is to be reviewed as a result of the flooding. (p4) [TAB]
* Quant: 5 properties; 1 property experienced internal flooding on more than one occasion (p2)
* Quant: five properties; approximately 300mm (depth) in properties (p3)

### Haverhill
* Town identified in the report title as the location of the flood event investigated under Section 19 by the LLFA. (p1)
* The flood event at Waveney Terrace, Haverhill caused internal flooding of residential properties within the town. (p2)
* Internal flooding to the front/main living areas of properties in the investigation area; approximately 300 mm of water inside properties. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 5 properties; 1 property experienced internal flooding on more than one occasion (p2)
* Quant: 5 properties; 300 mm (p3) [IMG]

### Eden Road, Haverhill
* Surface water flooding affected the highway at the junction with Waveney Terrace; the raised area at the junction is to be reviewed and highway drainage investigated. (p4) [TAB]

### River Stour Brook
* Water levels were recorded about 0.9 m higher than normal during the flood event on 13 Aug 2020. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 0.9 m higher than normal (p3) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Suffolk County Council
* Public highway drainage and highway asset at the junction of Waveney Terrace and Eden Road were affected by surface water flooding; SCC (Highways) required to review raised area of highway and ensure surface water systems are maintained and free-flowing to their outfalls. SCC (LLFA) tasked to produce a Surface Water Management Plan for the wider area. (p4) [PAG]

### Anglian Water
* {general}: Anglian Water's public surface water sewer system was implicated in the event; they were asked to ensure their surface water system was maintained and free-flowing to its outfall and to investigate condition/maintenance of surface water systems following the flood. (p4) [TAB, PAG]
