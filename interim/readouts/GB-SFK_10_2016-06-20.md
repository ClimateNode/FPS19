## Place names with coordinates

### Mount Road, Bury St Edmunds
* {28 Jun 2014, 20 Jun 2016}: Mount Road carriageway adjacent to the affected properties was affected by deep surface water flooding. (p2) [PAG, MUL]
* Mount Road carriageway adjacent to Sheerwater Close was affected by deep surface water flooding; rapid runoff from the steep incline of Mount Road contributed to overloading road drainage and directing flow towards the low point opposite Sheerwater Close, worsening flooding to properties. (p3) [PAG]
* Rapid, high-runoff flow down the steep road overwhelmed road drainage capacity; runoff converged at a low point opposite Sheerwater Close and followed a natural flow path through Sheerwater Close. Properties built in the valley have dammed the flow path causing pooling of deep surface water behind properties. Highway interception measures exist but can be overwhelmed during high-intensity summer storms. (p4) [PAG]
* {20 Jun 2016, 01 May 2017 - 31 May 2017}: Surface water (urban) flooding impacted the highway, prompting investigation and works to improve road drainage including repositioning and upsizing gullies. Drainage improvements were implemented in Mount Road (May 2017). (p5) [MUL]
* Quant: approx. 8,000m2 combined catchment area (Mount Road and Tassel Road, crest to crest) (p4) [PAG]

### Bury St Edmunds
* {28 Jun 2014, 20 Jun 2016}: Locations in Bury St Edmunds (Sheerwater Close and Mount Road) experienced deep surface water flooding, including inundation of one property and carriageway flooding. (p2) [MUL]
* {01 Jun 2014 - 30 Jun 2014, 20 Jun 2016}: Part of the town experienced surface water flooding associated with an intense localised storm (properties on Sheerwater Close internally and externally flooded; adjacent carriageway on Mount Road affected). The event was driven by rapid runoff down steep local roads into a natural flow path in a valley where development has led to pooling. (p3) [IMG, MUL]
* {28 Jun 2014, 20 Jun 2016}: Quant: 1 property (p2) [MUL]
* {01 Jun 2014 - 30 Jun 2014, 20 Jun 2016}: Quant: 1 property (internal flooding, 20 June 2016); 3 properties (internal flooding, June 2014) (p3) [IMG, MUL]

### Sheerwater Close, Bury St Edmunds
* {28 Jun 2014, 20 Jun 2016}: Deep surface water (>0.3m) entered a property along the southern edge of Sheerwater Close. (p2) [PAG, MUL]
* {01 Jun 2014 - 30 Jun 2014, 20 Jun 2016}: Deep surface water (>0.3 m) pooled along the southern edge of Sheerwater Close causing both internal and external flooding to properties; internal flooding of at least one property during the described event. The pooling was exacerbated by properties damming a natural flow path and highway drainage measures being overwhelmed. (p3) [MUL]
* {28 Jun 2014, 20 Jun 2016}: Quant: 1 property (p2) [PAG, MUL]
* {01 Jun 2014 - 30 Jun 2014, 20 Jun 2016}: Quant: 1 property (internal flooding, 20 June 2016); 3 properties (internal flooding, June 2014) (p3) [MUL]

### Tassel Road, Bury St Edmunds
* Tassel Road, being steep and converging with Mount Road toward a low point, generated rapid runoff that flowed into the natural valley and contributed to deep surface water pooling opposite Sheerwater Close and subsequent internal flooding of properties. (p3)
* Steep slope promoted rapid runoff towards the same low point as Mount Road; contributed to overloading of existing road drainage and convergence of surface water into the valley low point, contributing to pooling of deep surface water behind properties when drainage was overwhelmed. (p4) [PAG]
* Quant: approx. 8,000m2 combined catchment area (Mount Road and Tassel Road, crest to crest) (p4) [PAG]

### Appledown Drive, Bury St Edmunds
* Anglian Water's surface-water sewer in Appledown Drive/Mount Rd was identified for investigation to determine if it contributed to surface water/sewer flooding affecting properties; investigation/maintenance was reported as incomplete and AW did not comment. (p5)


## Topics without coordinates

### Anglian Water
* Anglian Water's surface water sewer is described as able to become overwhelmed during high-intensity storms, causing connected systems (including road drainage) to back up and contribute to surface water flooding in the area. (p4) [PAG]
* Anglian Water was asked to investigate its surface water sewer in Appledown Drive/Mount Rd to identify if improvements were required to address sewer/surface-water flooding; the action remained incomplete and AW did not provide comment in the update. (p5) [TAB, PAG]

### Highways
* {20 Jun 2016, 01 May 2017 - 31 May 2017}: SCC Highways investigated and completed drainage improvements in the affected area (Mount Road), were tasked with improving gully positions/sizes and assessing use of green space to disperse surface water during extreme events; works in Mount Road completed May 2017. (p5) [TAB, MUL]
