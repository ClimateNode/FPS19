

# Brimfield flooding 26 Oct 2019 and Brimfield flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Brimfield<br>055\_Herefordshire County/24\_Brimfield.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Brimfield
* {26 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Internal fluvial flooding of properties in the village: properties experienced internal flooding with water depths recorded up to 3 ft; flooding events lasted less than one day. (p1) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
* {2007, 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Properties in Brimfield experienced fluvial flooding from Brimfield Brook (internal flooding) and at least one property was affected by surface water runoff; internal flooding depths reached up to 3 ft and lasted less than one day; eight properties experienced garage flooding (garages integral to homes); sandbags were delivered to a property on 26 October 2019; 1 listed building (out of 7) lies within the Flood Zone. (p2) [MUL]
* {26 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 3 properties (internal flooding, February 16th 2020); 1 property (internal flooding, October 26th 2019); depths up to 3 ft (p1) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
* {2007, 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 3 properties internally flooded in February 2020; 1 property internally flooded in 2019; 4 internally flooded properties related to the key flood event; 15 internally flooded properties during the most acute flood; 8 properties with garage-only flooding; depths up to 3 ft; 1 of 7 listed buildings within Flood Zone (p2) [MUL]

### Brimfield Brook
* {26 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Brimfield Brook (a tributary of the River Teme) was the fluvial watercourse responsible for flooding in Brimfield; it contributed to internal property flooding in the settlement. (p1) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
* {2007, 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Flows in Brimfield Brook caused fluvial flooding in Brimfield, resulting in internal flooding of properties and flooding of garages integral to homes; channel constrictions in the brook were identified as issues contributing to flood risk. (p2) [MUL]
* {2007, 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 3 properties internally flooded in February 2020; 1 property internally flooded in 2019; 8 properties with garage-only flooding; depths up to 3 ft (p2) [MUL]
