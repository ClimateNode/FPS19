

# Low Prudhoe flooding 04 Dec 2015 - 05 Dec 2015

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Low Prudhoe<br>091\_Northumberland/Low\_Prudhoe\_FIR\_2015.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Low Prudhoe
* Defences at Low Prudhoe overtopped as the River Tyne and tributaries swelled during Storm Desmond. Floodwater from the Tyne backed up into an ordinary watercourse flooding the eastern end of the business estate; floodwater was also conveyed by roads in and around the estate. Overall local impacts recorded for the area: businesses, a residential property and council buildings were flooded. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 21 businesses; 1 residential property; 3 Council buildings (p1) [IMG]

### River Tyne
* The River Tyne swelled during Storm Desmond and overtopped defences at Low Prudhoe. Floodwater from the Tyne backed up into an ordinary watercourse at the eastern end of the business estate and was conveyed by roads, flooding several businesses and other buildings. (p1)
* Quant: 21 businesses; 1 residential property; 3 Council buildings (p1)

### Kielder Water
* {general}: Subject of operational review to manage maximum water level to increase flood-water attenuation; being modelled in new water-resources software (Aquator) to determine acceptable maximum water level and inform any operational changes. (p2) [TAB]


## Topics without coordinates

### Northumberland County Council
* Three of the Council's buildings in the Low Prudhoe area were flooded when the River Tyne overtopped defences and floodwater conveyed into the estate. (p1)
* Responded to the flood event by making property-level resilience grants available to businesses to install defences and improve resilience; serviced the control structure at a railway underpass to make it operational again and is working to identify ownership to agree a long-term maintenance plan. (p2) [TAB]
* Quant: 3 Council buildings (p1)

### Environment Agency
* {general}: Reviewed the Flood Warning Service and implemented improvements based on data collected after the flood event. (p2) [TAB]
