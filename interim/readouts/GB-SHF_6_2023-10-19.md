### Sheffield Metropolitan District
* A flooding event occurred in Sheffield associated with the very heavy rainfall on 20 October 2023 (investigation report concerns the Sheffield flood event of that date). (p1)
* Within Sheffield, river and urban flood risk was significant: the Meadowhall (Sheffield Hadfield's) gauge recorded the 4th highest level on record during Storm Babet. Flood defences completed since 2007 prevented flooding to homes and businesses in Hillsborough and through the Lower Don Valley. (p2) [PAG]
* Sheffield experienced a combination of surface water, localised watercourse and main river flooding (mainly west/south-west and along the River Rother). Highway flooding occurred (eg around Little London Road, Weedon Street, Meadowhall Road). Extensive overtopping in Rivelin and Porter Valleys caused damage to paths, ponds and the rights of way network; riverside wall collapses occurred at Moscar Cottages, near Broadfield Mowers and Endcliffe Park. (p5)
* Sheffield experienced widespread flood-related impacts during the event (20 October) including multiple streets flooded (e.g. Meadowhall Road, Weedon Street, Loxley Road, Ringinglow Road, A625 Ecclesall Road South). Totley Brook Road suffered significant cellar flooding to homes causing loss of power and gas and limited temporary evacuation. Veolia's Blackstock Road HWRC flooded due to high flows and blockage in the Meers Brook. Wood Royd Road in Deepcar required extensive pumping and sandbagging to prevent property flooding. (p6) [MUL]
* Widespread response and recovery actions: Lower Don Valley gates were reopened and riverside walkways cleaned; gullies and highway drains were checked and cleaned; wall collapses were fenced off; damaged footpaths and bridleways were inspected and dangerous sections secured; highway flooding occurred around Little London Road and in Millhouses Park; emergency procedures at Clough Dike (Deepcar) were instigated to prevent more severe flooding. (p7) [PAG]
* Multiple locations within Sheffield experienced flooding during the event (see Table 2), affecting residential and commercial properties across the city. (p8) [PAG]
* Surface-water flooding associated with Storm Babet caused properties in Sheffield to flood (see Table 2: Flooded Properties, Sheffield, Storm Babet). (p9)
* Quant: 4th highest on record (Sheffield Hadfield's / Meadowhall gauge) (p2) [PAG]
* Quant: 135 urgent calls received by Amey Streetsahead OCR; 107 highway flooding jobs; 11 flooding-to-property jobs; sandbags deployed to 8 locations (p5)
* Quant: 11 homes; approximately 30 homes (p6) [MUL]
* Quant: 6 residential properties; 3 commercial properties (p8) [PAG]
* Quant: 2 properties (p9)

### River Sheaf
* Levels on the River Sheaf rose substantially during Storm Babet, reaching around or higher than the 2007 event. At Highfield on the lower Sheaf, levels were second only to the 1991 event (an event that severely flooded Sheffield Station). (p2)
* River levels rose from late morning on 20/10/2023 to around or higher than levels experienced in 2007; Highfield's on the lower Sheaf recorded levels second only to 1991. Flood warnings were issued for the River Sheaf. (p3) [PAG]
* Flood warnings were issued for the River Sheaf as part of the Storm Babet warnings listed in Table 1. (p4) [TAB]
* Flooding/overtopping from the River Sheaf caused highway flooding around Little London Road and flooding in Millhouses Park; limited asset damage occurred, including riverside wall collapses at Moscar Cottages and near Broadfield Mowers. (p5)
* Downstream impacts on the River Sheaf were reduced because significant volumes of water were held back in NFM features on the Limb Brook above the river. (p6)
* High river levels on the River Sheaf caused flooding to the highway around Little London Road and flooding within Millhouses Park. (p7)
* Quant: 2nd highest on record at Highfield gauge (lower Sheaf) (p2)

### River Don
* River levels on the Don were notably high during Storm Babet; levels were above those that would have caused flooding prior to the installation of major flood schemes. Completed flood defences prevented flooding to homes and businesses in Hillsborough and through the Lower Don Valley. (p2) [TAB, PAG]
