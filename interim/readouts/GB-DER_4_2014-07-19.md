### Spondon
* Surface water flooding across the area on 19 July 2014, including internal property flooding in multiple locations and concentrated severe flooding in the Sandringham Drive area. (p4)
* Up to 17 residential properties were internally flooded in the Spondon area (properties on Sandringham Drive, Caernarvon Close, Holyrood Close and Harlech Close experienced internal flooding). (p5)
* Many areas of Spondon were affected by the flooding event on 19 July 2014, including internal property flooding. The City Council identified a significant cluster of reported internal and external property and highway flooding incidents on Sandringham Drive, Caernarvon Close, Holyrood Close and Harlech Close. (p6) [IMG]
* {May 1997, July 2002, July 2007, 06 Jul 2012, October 2013}: Multiple incidents of surface-water and foul/sewer-related flooding affected properties and gardens in Spondon: garden flooding on Arnhem Terrace and Borrowfield Road (Oct 2013); internal property flooding from surface water on Dale Road and Derby Road (6 July 2012); foul and surface-water garden flooding on Locko Road (July 2007); an isolated internal property flood (July 2002); external garden flooding on Arnhem Terrace and Kirkleys Avenue (May 1997). Widespread surface water flooding in July 2012 also caused highway and garden flooding across Spondon streets (Leeway, Moor End, Nottingham Road, Derby Road, Gravel Pit Lane, Sitwell Street). (p9) [MUL]
* Experienced an extremely localised, short-duration intense storm (around 15:30 on 19 July 2014) that produced very high rainfall rates centred on and just southwest of Spondon. Because watercourse levels were not expected to be high, any flood impacts associated with this event would be due to rapid, localised surface-water/urban flooding rather than river overtopping. (p12) [IMG]
* Surface water flooding occurred across Spondon during the intense localised storm, with local drainage overwhelmed and rapid runoff accumulating in low spots leading to property flooding in parts of the area. (p14)
* An intense thunderstorm struck Spondon on the afternoon of 19 July 2014, producing extreme/localised rainfall that caused surface water flooding conditions in the area. (p16)
* Quant: up to 18 properties (p4)
* Quant: up to 17 residential properties (p5)
* {May 1997, July 2002, July 2007, 06 Jul 2012, October 2013}: Quant: Two properties (garden) Oct 2013; Two properties (internal) on Dale Road and one additional property (internal) on Derby Road — 6 July 2012; One garden on Locko Road — July 2007; One property internal — July 2002 (p9) [MUL]

### Derby
* During the intense rainfall event on 19 July 2014, large areas of impermeable urban surface drained to adopted highways gullies and to public surface water sewers which outfall to an unnamed tributary of the River Derwent. This contributed to flooding of local roads and drainage infrastructure within the Derby City area. (p8) [PAG]
* {06 Jul 2012}: Surface water flooding was widespread across all areas of Derby during the 6 July 2012 event. (p9)

### Derby City Council
* Identified a significant cluster of reported internal and external property flooding and highway flooding incidents (localised urban/property/highway flooding) in Spondon, specifically noting Sandringham Drive, Caernarvon Close, Holyrood Close and Harlech Close. (p6)
* The City Council is undertaking feasibility studies to identify potential highway drainage improvements following the extraordinary, localised surface water flooding event of 19 July 2014 and will prioritise funding for feasible flood risk management measures. (p16)

### Severn Trent
* Runoff from the storm entered and flooded some foul water sewers; one property experienced internal foul water flooding, and misconnections are possible whereby surface water was piped into foul sewers. (p14)
* The local surface-water sewer network's design capacity was likely exceeded by the intense rainfall, resulting in incidences of foul water flooding reported during the event, including at least one internal (property) foul-water flooding incident. (p16)
* Quant: one property (p14)
* Quant: one internal foul water flooding incident (p16)

### Highways Authority
* Highway infrastructure on Sandringham Drive was severely affected by surface water flooding, causing severe highway flooding and external property flooding in the immediate area. (p4)
* The capacity of the highway drainage was exceeded during the storm, resulting in surface water flooding on the adopted highway and contributing to flooding of external property areas. (p15)
