## Topics without coordinates

### West Wiltshire
* Widespread pluvial runoff from an intense storm (greater than 1 in 150 year) caused extreme overland flows across the western part of the county, overwhelming drainage systems and causing property flooding and damage. (p1)
* An extreme rainfall event on 18–19 September 2014 caused floodwaters to exceed channel capacity across the West Wiltshire area, affecting adjoining land and properties; the event produced isolated but damaging inundation following known flow routes. (p3)
* Quant: over 80 properties; around 20 businesses; 2 schools (p3)
