### Yazor Brook

### Credenhill
* Ten properties recorded internal flooding in February 2020. Flood depths inside ranged from less than 6 inches to 2 ft; some outbuildings were affected with depths up to 3 ft. Flooding persisted up to 1 day internally and up to 7 days in external areas. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* {1980s, 1990, 2007, 01 Mar 2010 - 31 Mar 2010, 01 Jul 2017 - 31 Jul 2017, 01 Jul 2018 - 31 Jul 2018, 01 Oct 2019 - 31 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Ten properties in Credenhill suffered internal flooding in February 2020 with depths reported from less than 6 inches to 2 ft; some outbuildings were affected with depths up to 3 ft. Surface water flooding affected five properties from three sources (an ephemeral watercourse originating at Credenhill Park Wood, direct runoff from fields south of the A480, and runoff impacting a low-lying property). A further four properties were flooded from Yazor Brook or its upstream tributaries. Drains/gullies were reported at capacity, with reports of overflowing ditches, blocked culverts/bridges and at least one report of groundwater/spring involvement. (p2) [MUL]
* Quant: 10 properties (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* {1980s, 1990, 2007, 01 Mar 2010 - 31 Mar 2010, 01 Jul 2017 - 31 Jul 2017, 01 Jul 2018 - 31 Jul 2018, 01 Oct 2019 - 31 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 10 properties internally flooded; 5 properties from surface water sources; 4 properties from Yazor Brook/tributaries; 3 listed properties located within the Flood Zone; internal depths less than 6 inches to 2 ft; outbuilding depths up to 3 ft; internal flooding lasted up to 1 day and external flooding up to 7 days (p2) [MUL]

### Credenhill Park Wood
* An ephemeral watercourse originating at Credenhill Park Wood contributed runoff that caused surface-water flooding which flowed onto the main road and towards nearby properties. (p2)
* {general}: An ephemeral watercourse in Credenhill Park Wood generates floodwater/runoff that affects the A480 highway; highway drainage improvement works are being delivered to facilitate diversion of this floodwater. (p3)

### A480
* Runoff from fields south of the A480 contributed to surface-water flooding affecting properties; drains/gullies were reported at capacity and an overflowing ditch by the main road was noted. (p2)

### Credenhill Flood Alleviation Scheme
* The Flood Alleviation Scheme (inlet at Credenhill) transferred peak flows from the Yazor Brook into a 1.4 km culvert discharging to the River Wye; the scheme influenced flood routing during the event and may have limited local flood impacts near the inlet. (p3)
