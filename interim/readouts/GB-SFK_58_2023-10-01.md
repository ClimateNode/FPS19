### Suffolk

### Bucklesham
* {04 Aug 2023, 08 Aug 2023, 01 Oct 2023 - 31 Oct 2023}: Two properties located on The Heath in Bucklesham experienced internal flooding (confirmed 04/08/2023 and 08/08/2023) and further internal flooding occurred during Storm Babet in October 2023; anecdotal reports indicate the problem has been present since 2014 and one property made an insurance claim. (p3) [MUL]
* {20 Jul 2014, 04 Aug 2022, 09 Aug 2022, 01 Oct 2023 - 31 Oct 2023}: Surface-water (pluvial) flooding affected properties in The Heath area of Bucklesham. The SCC map and annotations record two residential properties as flooded with dates 04/08/2022 and 09/08/2022. A separate 2014 heavy-rain event (20/07/2014) may have overwhelmed the ditch north of the properties and nearby drainage assets, causing local flooding. SCC records do not detail the exact nature of any impacts from Storm Babet (October 2023) at these properties. (p5) [IMG, MUL]
* {04 Aug 2023, 08 Aug 2023, 01 Oct 2023 - 31 Oct 2023}: Quant: two properties; one property submitted insurance claims (p3) [MUL]
* {20 Jul 2014, 04 Aug 2022, 09 Aug 2022, 01 Oct 2023 - 31 Oct 2023}: Quant: 2 residential properties (p5) [IMG, MUL]

### The Heath
* {04 Aug 2023, 08 Aug 2023, 01 Oct 2023 - 31 Oct 2023}: Internal flooding affected two properties on The Heath; incidents include an internal flood confirmed at one property on 04/08/2023, a neighbouring property on 08/08/2023, and further internal flooding during Storm Babet in October 2023. Anecdotal reports indicate the issue has been ongoing since 2014 and one property has submitted insurance claims. (p3) [IMG, MUL]
* {20 Jul 2014, 04 Aug 2022, 09 Aug 2022}: Map records show residential properties at The Heath were flooded (surface water/pluvial flooding). Two residential properties are specifically annotated as flooded in August 2022. A separate 2014 rainfall event is noted as having the potential to overwhelm the ditch north of the properties and nearby drainage assets. (p5) [IMG, MUL]
* {04 Aug 2023, 08 Aug 2023, 01 Oct 2023 - 31 Oct 2023}: Quant: two properties; one property submitted insurance claims (p3) [IMG, MUL]
* {20 Jul 2014, 04 Aug 2022, 09 Aug 2022}: Quant: 2 residential properties (p5) [IMG, MUL]


## Topics without coordinates

### Suffolk County Council
* {17 Aug 2023}: Carried out a site visit on 17/08/2023 and determined the overwhelmed ditch line north of the properties was the source of flooding; noted private drainage assets and highway gullies linked to the watercourse were overwhelmed. (p4)
* {17 Aug 2023}: Quant: two internal flooding reports (04/08/2023 and 08/08/2023) (p4)

### South Cottages
* {04 Aug 2023, 08 Aug 2023, 01 Oct 2023 - 31 Oct 2023}: Internal flooding affected South Cottages: an internal flooding event was confirmed on 04/08/2023, additional internal flooding was reported by the neighbouring property on 08/08/2023, and internal flooding was reported during Storm Babet (October 2023). The source was determined to be an overwhelmed ditch line north of the properties; private drainage assets and highway gullies linked to the watercourse were also overwhelmed. Remedial works were raised in April 2024 and a 300mm pipe and ditching works were installed (completed). A private ditch continuing for approximately 30m and an ancient brick structure/possible culvert under private land were noted; some of the watercourse has been cleared of vegetation. (p4) [IMG, MUL]
* {04 Aug 2023, 08 Aug 2023, 01 Oct 2023 - 31 Oct 2023}: Quant: one property submitted insurance claims; private ditch continues approximately 30m (p4) [IMG, MUL]

### Highways
* {04 Aug 2023, 08 Aug 2023, 01 Oct 2023 - 31 Oct 2023}: Highway gullies and the highway-maintained ditch linked to the watercourse were overwhelmed during the events, contributing to internal flooding of properties; the main watercourse is mostly maintained by the local highway authority. (p4) [MUL]
