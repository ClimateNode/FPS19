### Kingsnorth

* {general}: Parts of Kingsnorth (areas off Steeds Lane, including Steeds Close and Bond Lane) are described as lying in a low point of a valley and being at risk from flooding where local watercourses and drainage concentrate. (p5)

### Bond Lane, Kingsnorth
* {09 Nov 2014, 08 Jan 2015}: Roadside ditch to the rear of 3 Bond Lane was filled with muddy water; evidence of surface water accumulation in the ditch associated with local flooding. (p3) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
* {09 Nov 2014, 08 Jan 2015}: Surface water and muddy runoff inundated Bond Lane at the junction with Steeds Lane, covering the road and adjacent verge and reaching the base of the Bond Lane street sign; road surface was waterlogged and posed a hazard to vehicles and pedestrians. (p4) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
* {general}: A watercourse flows along the western side of Bond Lane in front of properties; the channel then turns northwest towards Steeds Close and the cricket ground, forming a flow path that places properties on Bond Lane at flood risk. (p5) [PAG]
* {09 Nov 2014, 08 Jan 2015}: The culvert connecting the ditches on either side of Bond Lane has been assessed and is thought to have collapsed. The report states this is unlikely to have had a significant impact on properties but could cause or exacerbate highway flooding in the area during the investigated events. (p6) [MUL]
* {09 Nov 2014, 08 Jan 2015}: The culvert connecting the ditches either side of Bond Lane was assessed and is thought to have collapsed; this collapse could cause or exacerbate highway flooding (though it was assessed as unlikely to have had a significant impact on properties). (p7) [TAB, PAG, MUL]

### Steeds Lane, Kingsnorth
* {09 Nov 2014, 08 Jan 2015}: Junction of Steeds Lane and Bond Lane was heavily flooded, with water covering the entire width of the road and extending onto adjacent grassy areas; a culvert beneath Steeds Lane is visible at the site. (p3) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
* {09 Nov 2014, 08 Jan 2015}: Surface water flooding occurred at the junction with Bond Lane; muddy water covered the junction and adjacent verges. A culvert beneath Steeds Lane is noted at the junction (visible behind railings) in the imagery associated with the flooded scene. (p4) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
* {general}: A drainage ditch on the northern side of Steeds Lane joins the main ditch at the Steeds Lane–Bond Lane junction; two culverts convey water and groundwater emerges on the southern side of Steeds Lane at this point, contributing to local flooding risk. (p5) [IMG, PAG]
* {09 Nov 2014, 08 Jan 2015}: One half of the twin-culvert beneath Steeds Lane is believed to be obstructed; this is reported as unlikely to have had a significant impact on properties but could cause or exacerbate highway flooding. Additionally, groundwater emergence to the south of Steeds Lane indicates a high water table in the wider area, which contributed to flood conditions. (p6) [IMG, MUL]
* {09 Nov 2014, 08 Jan 2015}: One half of the twin culvert beneath Steeds Lane was observed to be partially obstructed; this obstruction could cause or exacerbate highway flooding in the area. (p7) [TAB, PAG, MUL]

### Ashford Road, Kingsnorth
* {general}: The watercourse flows towards Ashford Road, turns sharply to the south and then passes west through a culvert beneath the highway; this culvert is part of the local drainage route and relevant to flood conveyance at the road. (p5) [IMG]
* {09 Nov 2014, 08 Jan 2015}: The culvert beneath Ashford Road was observed to be partially obstructed by silt and a nearby chamber (where the watercourse changes direction into the culvert) was also partially obstructed. These obstructions reduced free flow away from the lower-lying land and were identified as contributory factors to the surface water flooding that affected the area in the two investigated events. (p6) [MUL]
* {09 Nov 2014, 08 Jan 2015}: The culvert beneath Ashford Road at the western end of the watercourse and the adjacent chamber (where the watercourse changes direction into the culvert) were observed to be partially obstructed by silt, reducing free flow and contributing to the watercourse becoming overwhelmed and flooding nearby low-lying land. (p7) [TAB, PAG, MUL]


## Topics without coordinates

### Kent County Council
* {09 Nov 2014, 08 Jan 2015}: The local watercourse was overwhelmed during the rainfall events and flowed onto the lowest lying land, including some properties in Steeds Close. Culverts and a chamber (beneath/adjacent to Ashford Road, and beneath Steeds Lane and Bond Lane) were partially obstructed, silted or collapsed, exacerbating highway flooding and restricting free flow away from the area. KCC is the responsible authority for road drains and culverts and agreed to clear silt/debris between Bond Lane and Ashford Road, clear and repair culverts and the chamber adjacent to Ashford Road, investigate and repair the partially obstructed twin culvert beneath Steeds Lane, and undertake inspections every two years. (p7) [TAB, MUL]

### Cricket Ground
* {09 Nov 2014, 08 Jan 2015}: A local watercourse flows past the cricket ground toward Ashford Road; the cricket ground lies in the lowest (northwest) part of the valley and was affected by flows from this watercourse and associated surface water during the named flood events, causing inundation of the ground. (p5) [IMG, MUL]
