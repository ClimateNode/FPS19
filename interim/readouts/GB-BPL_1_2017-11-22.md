### Blackpool
* {22 Nov 2017, 23 Nov 2017}: Widespread surface-water flooding reported across the Blackpool and Lancashire area on 22 November 2017, affecting multiple streets and local communities. (p3)
* Town-wide flooding on 22 Nov 2017 affecting multiple streets and infrastructure: specific locations named include Limerick Road and Lockhurst Avenue; major flooding under Devonshire Road Bridge (road closure); Anchorsholme Academy was flooded. The report attributes the flooding to an exceptionally severe storm that overwhelmed highway drainage and sewers. (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* The sewer/tunnel system serving Blackpool was operating at full storage capacity during prolonged rainfall from 20 November and the sequential operation of pumping stations culminated in extreme conditions on the night of 22 November 2017. (p5)
* Widespread property-level flooding across the borough: council data recorded property-level flooding affecting properties and garages; roads and highways were flooded and in parts impassable. Water entered properties (often under the floor), sandbags were ineffective, some properties experienced electrical disruption. Water was recorded to have receded from affected properties during the morning of 23rd November 2017. (p7)
* Areas of Blackpool experienced flooding on 22 November 2017 prompting a Section 19 investigation; specific properties (notably on Valentia Road and Limerick Road) were identified for potential flood resilience measures; actions proposed include watercourse clearance, surface water management measures and partnership works to improve storage and flow in local brooks. (p9)
* {22 Nov 2017, 23 Nov 2017}: Quant: approximately 300 properties (Blackpool and Lancashire) (p3)
* Quant: approximately 300 properties (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 74 properties; 6 garages; road flooding up to 1 m deep at Valentia Road and Limerick Road; highway depths around 500 mm in East Anchorsholme (p7)

### Anchorsholme Pumping Station
* The pumping station reached capacity during the extreme rainfall on the night of 22nd November 2017. Pumps were called into duty sequentially as flows arriving from the tunnel rose; the station operated at capacity and could not reduce tunnel levels earlier because pumping only starts once tunnel levels exceed the start height. (p5)
* {22 Nov 2017, 23 Nov 2017}: High flows arriving at the station triggered sequential automatic activation of pumps; on the evening of 22nd November and during 23rd November the station automatically called the remaining two pumps into service to manage storm-water. Pump five experienced intermittent tripping during the evening of 22nd November. (p6) [PAG, MUL]
* Quant: 5 pumps (p5)
* {22 Nov 2017, 23 Nov 2017}: Quant: two pumps called into service on the evening of 22nd November; five pumps at the station (total) (p6) [PAG, MUL]

## Topics without coordinates

### United Utilities
* {22 Nov 2017, 23 Nov 2017}: United Utilities attended the flood event on 22 and 23 November 2017 and provided customer support in response to the flooding (including a customer bus in Morrison's car park staffed following the event). (p3) [MUL]
* {22 Nov 2017, 23 Nov 2017}: United Utilities' storm water tanks and sewer system were reported to be at full capacity following steady rain from 20th November 2017 and saturated ground; this contributed to the severe flooding on 22nd November 2017 that affected properties across Blackpool (reported ~300 properties), caused major flooding under Devonshire Road Bridge (road closure) and flooding at Anchorsholme Academy and other streets (e.g. Limerick Road, Lockhurst Avenue). (p4) [PAG]
* Recorded heavy rainfall from 20 November 2017 that led to storm water tanks and the Fylde Tunnel sewer system being at full storage capacity; monitored pumping stations and the tunnel were operating at/near capacity during the event and spill data was recorded. (p5) [PAG]
* {22 Nov 2017, 23 Nov 2017}: Operated local pumping infrastructure during the storm to manage high storm-water flows, including availability/use of beach pumps that activate automatically; United Utilities staff attended properties as part of response. Reported intermittent tripping of pump five at Anchorsholme during the evening of 22nd November 2017 but stated this did not change overall severity. (p6) [MUL]
* United Utilities diverted flows from Skipool pumping and surrounding areas away from the Anchorsholme catchment to provide additional sewer capacity; many United Utilities assets were continually spilling storm sewage to watercourses in accordance with Environment Agency consents. The diversion of storm water to the River Wyre is reported to have mitigated flooding in parts of the Anchorsholme catchment. (p7)
* Anchorsholme pumping station operation was investigated as a potential cause of local flooding; United Utilities reported the pumping station was operational and pumps were operating intermittently on the evening of 22nd November 2017. The wider sewer network was described as saturated during the event with no widespread blockages identified other than a separate blockage affecting Sevenoaks Drive. (p8)
* United Utilities were engaged in discussions about the impact of flooding during heavy rainfall (including disruption at Devonshire Road Bridge) and are a partner for works to improve water storage and flow in local watercourses to manage future flood impacts. (p9)
* {22 Nov 2017, 23 Nov 2017}: Quant: approximately 300 properties (p4) [PAG]
* Quant: 200 million litres storage capacity; storm water tanks and Fylde Tunnel at full capacity (p5) [PAG]
* {22 Nov 2017, 23 Nov 2017}: Quant: 600 minutes of 1-3 pumps operating; 1210 minutes of 1-3 pumps operating; 760 minutes of 1-3 pumps operating; five pumps at Anchorsholme (total) (p6) [MUL]

### Blackpool Council
* {22 Nov 2017, 23 Nov 2017}: Received numerous reports of street flooding from the event on 22 November 2017, activated emergency planning protocols and coordinated on-site response (officers attended the flood event on 22 and 23 November 2017 and remained on site for a number of days). (p3)
* Council officers visited properties reported as flooded, delivered letters to affected properties, deployed sandbags (which proved ineffective at some locations), and recorded observations of surcharge into properties and impassable roads. Council data collection recorded the extent of property-level flooding following the event. (p7)
* {22 Nov 2017, 23 Nov 2017}: Quant: approximately 300 properties affected (event-wide) (p3)
* Quant: over 74 properties; 6 garages (p7)

### Fylde Tunnel
* The Fylde Tunnel Sewer system was reported to be at full capacity prior to and during the storm, contributing to sewer surcharge and urban/surface water flooding across the area on 22nd November 2017. (p4)
* The Fylde Tunnel sewer system (the collection mechanism for Blackpool) reached full storage capacity during the rainfall event; the system stores wastewater at multiple locations and must be full before discharge to sea, leading to sequential operation of pumping stations and extreme conditions on the night of 22 November 2017. (p5) [PAG]
* Quant: approximately 300 properties (p4)
* Quant: 200 million litres (p5) [PAG]

### Devonshire Road Bridge
