

# Lancashire flooding 01 Jun 2018

Reference document:<br>1 June 2018 Floods In Lancashire: Section 19 Investigation Report<br>069\_Lancashire County/section-19-flood-investigation-report-june-2018-flooding.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Lancashire
* Parts of the county were affected by flooding during the event on 1 June 2018. (p1)
* On the evening of 1 June 2018, very localised high‑intensity downpours temporarily overwhelmed urban drainage systems across parts of Lancashire. Emergency responder records show flooding incidents in Leyland, Lea (Preston), Pilling (Wyre), Bacup, Accrington, Barnoldswick, Earby and Longridge. The event led to flooded highways and domestic property flooding. (p3)
* Part of Lancashire experienced flooding on 1 June 2018. (p10)
* Intense, concentrated rainfall on 1 June 2018 exceeded the capacity of local urban drainage (public and private) across the county, producing surface water flooding of highways, homes, gardens and garages and deep water running downhill on main roads. (p13) [PAG]
* Multiple reported flooding incidents across the county on 1 June 2018, affecting public highways and private property. Clusters of reports were noted particularly around Preston, Pendle and Rossendale; emergency services and water companies responded on the night. (p14) [IMG, PAG]
* On the night of 1 June 2018 intense rainfall overwhelmed many urban drainage networks across the county, producing standing water on public highways and causing towns and villages to be badly affected; people experienced damage to their homes and belongings and concern for personal safety. (p17) [PAG]
* Extreme rainfall on 1 June 2018 overwhelmed many urban drainage networks in parts of the county, producing surface water / urban flooding that entered homes and caused damage to people's homes and belongings and concern for personal safety. (p18) [PAG]
* Quant: 60 homes; 19 homes flooded internally to at least one habitable room (p3)

### Wyre

### Longridge
* Flooding incidents were reported to the county council on the evening of 1 June 2018. The report identifies that the flooding was caused by very localised, high-intensity downpours that temporarily overwhelmed urban drainage systems. (p3)
* Intense bursts of heavy rainfall exceeded local drainage capacity, causing surface water flooding in the town: highways, homes, gardens and garages were flooded; deep water rushed downhill on main roads and eyewitnesses reported rain being forced inside buildings through window frames. (p13)
* Quant: 60 homes flooded across all reported locations; 19 properties flooded internally across all reported locations (p3)

## Topics without coordinates

### Earby and Salterforth Internal Drainage Board
* Area within the IDB's drainage district was affected by flooding on 1 June 2018. (p10)

### Lancashire Fire and Rescue Service
* Responded on the night (1 June 2018) to reports of flooding of public highways and private property, providing on-the-ground emergency response. (p15)

### LCC Highways
* Attended every highway location reported or known to have been affected after the event, inspected conditions and removed silt from gullies and drainage systems following the 1 June 2018 incident. (p15)

### United Utilities
* Responded on the night to reports and, from the following morning, carried out inspections and clearing exercises on the public sewer network; inspections found obstructions from debris washed in during the storm and root ingress reducing capacity. (p15)

### Yorkshire Water
* Responded on the night to reports and carried out equivalent inspections and clearing actions on the public sewer network following the 1 June 2018 event. (p15)

### Highway Service
* The county council Highway Service inspected every reported or known flooded highway starting the morning after the storm (1 June 2018), removed silt from gullies and drainage systems, and recovered drainage capacity. Inspections found drainage obstructions from debris washed in during the rainstorm and longer‑running capacity reductions due to tree‑root ingress trapping silt and small debris. (p15)
