

# Felixstowe flooding 05 Jun 2015

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Langer Road, Felixstowe<br>124\_Suffolk County/124\_2021\_Suffolk County/section-19-report-langer-road-reformatted-updated.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Langer Road, Felixstowe
* Site of a flood event that prompted a Section 19 flood investigation by the Lead Local Flood Authority (Suffolk County Council). (p1)
* Internal flooding of domestic properties (main living areas, garages and gardens). Public highway partially flooded for approximately 50 m to an approximate depth of 200 mm. The Anglian Water surface water drainage system surcharged at this location. (p2)
* Area under Langer Road experienced high water table and surface/groundwater flooding; properties in the area flooded internally (main living areas, garages and gardens) to around 150 mm depth; local drainage systems (SCC Highway and Anglian Water) may have exceeded capacity or been blocked. (p3) [PAG]
* Surface water and groundwater flooding affected the Langer Rd area: properties are located within an area mapped as at risk of pluvial (surface water) flooding; high water table under the Langer Rd area contributed to groundwater risk; possible exceedance of the SCC highway surface water drainage and Anglian Water surface water system and blockages of drainage assets are cited as causes of flooding in the Langer Rd corridor (Undercliff Rd West to Beach Station Rd and side roads). (p4) [PAG]
* {general}: Langer Rd area is identified as subject to surface-water and groundwater flood risk (a high minor aquifer is present beneath the area). Actions undertaken/ recommended include review of surface-water drainage maintenance schedules, evaluation of the surface-water system and its design capacity for Langer Rd (from Undercliff Rd West to Beach Station Rd including side roads), a detailed survey with remedial works programmed, gully maintenance on a 12‑month cycle and reviews of road‑sweeping frequency to reduce flood risk. (p5) [TAB, PAG]
* Quant: 4 domestic properties; approx. 50 m of public highway; approx. 200 mm depth (p2)
* Quant: 4 domestic properties; depth ~150 mm (p3) [PAG]

### Felixstowe
* Flooding affected part of the town at Langer Road: four domestic properties flooded internally (main living areas, garages and gardens); local public highway flooded (approx. 50 m, approx. 200 mm depth); Anglian Water surface water drainage system was surcharged. (p2)
* Quant: 4 domestic properties; approx. 50 m of public highway; approx. 200 mm depth (p2)

### Undercliff Road West, Old Felixstowe
* {general}: Undercliff Rd West is named as the western extent of the Langer Rd corridor for which surface-water drainage system design capacity and maintenance schedules were reviewed; it is therefore part of the area affected by surface-water flood risk and associated drainage works. (p5) [TAB]

### Beach Station Road, Felixstowe
* Surface-water drainage assets associated with Beach Station Road (area covered by Langer Rd from Undercliff Rd West to Beach Station Rd) were subject to review and maintenance because of flood risk; gullies maintenance regime applied and detailed surveys/remedial works were programmed. (p5) [TAB]

### Langer Park
* Vegetation washed downstream in the Langer Park watercourse and blocked trash screens; blockages at multiple locations along the watercourse contributed to the exceedance of drainage capacity and the resulting surface water flooding. (p3) [IMG]
* Vegetation washed downstream and blocked trash screens in the Langer Park watercourse; blockages at several locations along the watercourse are described as contributing to flooding (blocking conveyance and trash screens). (p4) [PAG]
* {general}: Langer Park watercourse was implicated in surface water flooding risk. It was subject to recommended and implemented maintenance and remedial works (detailed survey completed and remedial works programmed), routine trash-screen cleansing (bi‑annual) and installation of a new trash screen and work access areas to reduce blockages and help manage flooding. (p5) [TAB, PAG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Anglian Water
* Anglian Water surface water drainage system surcharged during the event and blockages of Anglian Water assets are noted as a possible cause/contributor to internal flooding of properties. (p3)
* Anglian Water's surface water drainage assets and structures (including trash screens in the Langer Park watercourse) are described as blocked or potentially of insufficient design capacity, contributing to surface water flooding; Anglian Water is recommended to review maintenance, assess design capacity and assess/redesign trash screens. (p4) [PAG]
* Anglian Water's surface-water drainage assets and trash screen on the Langer Park watercourse were involved in the flood response: trash screen cleansing carried out bi‑annually/after significant rainfall; Anglian Water reviewed maintenance schedules, assessed and redesigned the trash screen and installed a new trash screen and work access areas. (p5) [TAB, PAG]
* Quant: 4 domestic properties flooded internally (p3)

### SCC Highway
* Public highway partially flooded (surface water) for approximately 50 m with depths around 150–200 mm; SCC highway drainage is implicated as potentially exceeding design capacity and having blockages that contributed to the flooding. (p3)
* Quant: public highway flooded for approximately 50 m; depth 150–200 mm (p3)

### Suffolk Coast Council
* As riparian land owner, the council's Langer Park watercourse is implicated in flooding due to vegetation and blockages; the council is recommended to review and enhance the watercourse maintenance schedule and road-sweeping frequency to reduce flood risk. (p4) [PAG]

### Suffolk County Council
* SCC highway surface water drainage assets on Langer Rd (Undercliff Rd West to Beach Station Rd and side roads) are cited as potentially exceeding design capacity and having blockages, contributing to surface water flooding affecting the highway corridor and nearby properties; SCC Highways is recommended to review maintenance and the design capacity of the surface water system. (p4) [PAG]

### Highways
* Suffolk County Council Highways reviewed and implemented maintenance schedules for drainage assets on Langer Rd (from Undercliff Rd West to Beach Station Rd) in response to flooding risk; gullies are maintained on a 12‑month cycle or as required; detailed survey completed and remedial works were programmed (detailed survey referenced Aug 2017). (p5) [TAB]
