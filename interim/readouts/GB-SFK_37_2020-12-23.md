## Place names with coordinates

### Downing Close, Mildenhall
* Internal flooding of residential properties on Downing Close. The report records internal flooding of five properties during the single 23/12/2020 incident and that one property experienced internal flooding on more than one occasion. (p2)
* {01 Aug 2020 - 31 Aug 2020, 01 Dec 2020 - 31 Dec 2020, 23 Dec 2020, 01 Jan 2021 - 31 Jan 2021}: Repeated highway flooding across the full width of the road and footway displaced by passing vehicles (bow waves), which pushed standing water onto surrounding footways, gardens and into nearby properties. Internal flooding occurred in ground-floor rooms (lounge, kitchen and hall), causing damage to furnishings and increased stress for residents despite use of property-level resilience (e.g. sandbags). (p3) [PAG, MUL]
* {01 Aug 2020 - 31 Aug 2020, 23 Dec 2020, 01 Jan 2021 - 31 Jan 2021}: Highway gullies surcharged during the storm, causing standing water to flow down the carriageway and accumulate in low-lying areas. Floodwater on the highway was displaced by passing vehicles onto lower-fronted properties, contributing to internal flooding of adjacent homes and preventing drainage for 24–48 hours following rainfall. (p4) [MUL]
* Highway gullies surcharged during the storm on 23rd December 2020 causing water to flow down the highway and accumulate in low-lying areas. Highways drainage discharges to a series of soakaways to the west of the highway which may require relocating to permit continued maintenance access. Highway gullies were cleansed and the soakaway inspected in February 2021 but a full condition survey/cleanse was prevented by high groundwater; a repeat visit was scheduled. (p5) [TAB, PAG]
* Highways surface-water drainage (specifically soakaways) were silted/blocked requiring cleansing and a CCTV survey to record points of discharge and address points of concern; actions instructed to restore highway drainage function and prevent further surface-water flooding. (p6)
* Quant: internal flooding of one property (on more than one occasion); internal flooding of five properties (during one incident) (p2)
* {01 Aug 2020 - 31 Aug 2020, 01 Dec 2020 - 31 Dec 2020, 23 Dec 2020, 01 Jan 2021 - 31 Jan 2021}: Quant: 16-20 m length of road; 100-150 mm depth (p3) [PAG, MUL]
* {01 Aug 2020 - 31 Aug 2020, 23 Dec 2020, 01 Jan 2021 - 31 Jan 2021}: Quant: 3 internal rooms (lounge, kitchen and hall) reported flooded in affected properties (p4) [MUL]

## Topics without coordinates

### SCC Highways Authority
* {23 Dec 2020, 01 Feb 2021 - 28 Feb 2021}: Responded to the highway flooding by cleansing highway gullies and inspecting soakaways (completed in Feb 2021). Full cleanse/condition survey of the soakaway was not possible due to high groundwater; a second visit and CCTV survey of the highways drainage (including point of discharge) were scheduled and further maintenance actions were recommended. (p5) [TAB, MUL]

### Anglian Water
* {01 Jan 2021 - 31 Jan 2021, 01 Feb 2021 - 28 Feb 2021}: Called out to flooding incidents within the highway reported by residents in January and February 2021. Residents reported these incidents were unrelated to the highway flooding caused by surcharging gullies. (p5) [MUL]
