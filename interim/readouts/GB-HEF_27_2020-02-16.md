

# Mordiford flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Mordiford<br>055\_Herefordshire County/26\_Mordiford.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Mordiford
* The village core (centred on Mordiford) is shown on the maps as being within Flood Zone 3 (highest probability) along the main river corridor. Flood defence lines and areas ‘benefiting from flood defences’ are shown running along the river through the village, with the school and much of the village centre located within or adjacent to defended areas. The mapping identifies the river as the primary source of flood risk affecting the village and local infrastructure (e.g. The Mill, Garlands Farm vicinity). (p1) [IMG]
* Four properties recorded internal flooding during February 2020; the affected properties are in or adjacent to Flood Zone 3 and are located directly adjacent to the Pentaloe/Penteloe Brook. Some low-threshold properties in the locality are at risk during flash floods. Additionally, six listed properties/structures are located within the Flood Zone. (p2)
* Quant: 4 internally flooded properties; 6 listed properties/structures located within the Flood Zone (p2)

### Hereford
* {01 Feb 2010 - 28 Feb 2010}: High river levels led to road flooding in central Hereford which led to domestic flooding (properties affected). (p3)

### Pentaloe Brook
* Impacted by high River Wye levels which cause the brook to spill out of its channel and flood Woolhope Road; properties located directly adjacent to the brook were recorded as internally flooded during the February 2020 event. (p2)
* Quant: 4 properties internally flooded (p2)

### River Lugg
* Part of the connected catchment (Penteloe Brook discharges into the River Lugg in the village); the catchment-level fluvial flooding contributed to internal flooding of properties in Mordiford. (p2)
* Quant: 4 properties (p2)


## Topics without coordinates

### Woolhope Road
* Overflow from Penteloe Brook (driven by high River Wye levels) spilled onto Woolhope Road causing the road to flood; the mechanism is documented in the Herefordshire Flood Alleviation Strategy and a resident supplied a video of the flooded road. (p2)

### River Wye
* High River Wye levels are described as impacting the Penteloe/Pentaloe Brook, contributing to the brook spilling onto Woolhope Road and to flooding in Mordiford. (p2)
* {01 Feb 2010 - 28 Feb 2010}: Reached the highest recorded level at the Old Wye Bridge in central Hereford; the resulting high river levels led to road flooding which in turn led to domestic flooding. (p3)
* Quant: 4 properties (p2)

### Dinedor Mill Bridge
* {28 Oct 2019}: The B4399 from Dinedor Mill Bridge to the junction with the B4224 was closed to traffic (reported under road closures in the flood description). (p2)
