## Place names with coordinates

### Caterham Drive, Croydon
* {2007, 2015, 07 Jun 2016}: Properties located in the topographic low along Caterham Drive flooded when surface water runoff accumulated and formed prominent flow paths; local residents provided photographic evidence of flooding in 2007 and 2015. (p9) [PAG, MUL]
* {20 Jul 2007, 2015, 07 Jun 2016}: Surface water runoff accumulates in a topographic low along Caterham Drive, forming prominent flow paths that led to properties being flooded. The local topography directs runoff along the road from southeast to northwest, causing inundation of properties located in the low point. (p10) [PAG, IMG, MUL]
* Surface water / urban runoff flowed from the vicinity of Caterham-on-the-Hill onto Caterham Drive during the 7 June 2016 event. Properties adjacent to the flow path reported flooding on 7 June 2016 and are located within areas the Environment Agency maps as 'high' risk (annual probability >3.3PCSYM). Numerous soakaways along Caterham Drive were identified and several local soakaways were inspected (site visit 05/08/2016; condition assessments by Flowline in Appendix B); soakaway condition (siltation/blocking) affects the ability to drain runoff and is relevant to the flooding experienced on Caterham Drive. (p11) [IMG, PAG]
* Properties on and adjacent to Caterham Drive reported flooding during the 7th June 2016 event caused by overland surface water flow routed from higher ground and by surcharging foul sewers following heavy rainfall; these properties lie adjacent to RoFSW 'high' risk flow paths. (p12) [PAG]
* Surface water runoff (likely originating from Rydon's Lane) propagated north down the road, producing fast-flowing floodwater along Caterham Drive and causing property flooding at both the northern and southern ends of the road. Properties experienced complete ground-floor and garden inundation, partial ground-floor flooding/damp creep, and internal flooding; at least one resident had to move out while a house dried. (p13) [TAB, PAG]
* Caterham Drive experienced flooding from surface water and from sewers during the event on 7 June 2016. The report identifies surface water and sewer sources as the reported causes of flooding at this location. (p14) [TAB, PAG]
* On 7 June 2016 the surface water drainage and soakaway network failed to drain the intense rainfall, causing rapid onset of surface water flooding across roads and into surrounding properties. Properties A and B experienced the deepest flooding (water reached thresholds and inundated the ground floor). Residents observed the foul sewer surcharging with lifted manhole covers, indicating surface water entry to the combined sewer. A CCTV survey found blocked gullies (9 and 10) opposite the severely flooded properties. (p15)
* Surface water/overland flow from intense rainfall overwhelmed drainage on Caterham Drive. Gullies were found blocked (gullies 9 and 10) and some gullies had suspected collapsed outlet pipes (gullies 4 and 10), reducing drainage capacity. Two properties (A and B) experienced severe internal flooding. A manhole on the carriageway was surcharging and there was physical damage to the carriageway around the manhole cover; video shows runoff from Rydon's Lane onto Caterham Drive. The drainage network was later inspected/cleaned after the event. (p16) [PAG]
* Surface water runoff from Rydon's Lane flowed onto Caterham Drive during the 7 June 2016 event, causing damage to the carriageway around a manhole cover and depositing flood debris on a recently tarmacked pathway leading onto Caterham Drive. (p17) [IMG]
* Road and adjacent properties flooded; surface water entered and hydraulically overloaded the foul sewer causing sewer surcharge and foul sewage to enter properties; standing water and debris on the carriageway; sandbags were delivered to affected residents; remedial works undertaken including gully cleaning, CCTV surveys and repair of identified defects. (p18)
* Surface water overwhelmed the drainage and entered the foul sewer, hydraulically overloading the sewer network and causing flooding into properties on Caterham Drive. Flood depths exceeded 900mm in places. Residents at the northern end (properties A–D) used personal property protection; at the southern end properties E–F were affected and attended by the fire service. A builder removed an earth bund on 7 June which subsequently allowed surface water to flow off the road into woodland in the following days. (p19) [TAB, PAG]
* On 7th June 2016 Caterham Drive experienced flooding that prompted resident complaints. Lanes for Drains were deployed to pump, suck and sweep floodwater and debris. Subsequent CCTV inspections of drains identified blockages and collapsed pipes that may have been caused by the flooding. The sewer network surcharged during the extreme event as surface water entered the system. (p20) [TAB, PAG]
* Flooding occurred on Caterham Drive during the event on 7th June 2016; residents reported incidents and provided photographic and video evidence of flooding, and blockages were noted as contributing factors to the flooding. (p21)
* The report describes overland surface water flooding affecting the northern end of Caterham Drive during the 7 June 2016 event. It recommends enhancing nearby land (Dollypers Hill) and installing a brow ditch or interceptor drain to intercept runoff from steep slopes and reduce the volume of overland flooding reaching Caterham Drive. (p22) [TAB, PAG]
* Experienced overland / surface-water flooding on 7th June 2016 when runoff from adjacent steep slopes reached the road; the report identifies this as the Caterham Drive flood event and proposes mitigation (brow ditch/interceptor drain, SuDS upslope) to reduce overland flows into the street. (p23) [PAG]
* Caterham Drive was the subject of a flood investigation and a Flowline CCTV survey following flood impacts in June 2016; the report headings indicate the road experienced flooding during the June 2016 event. (p26)
* Quant: 6 properties; up to 1m in some areas; two foot of fast-flowing water reported along the road (p13) [TAB, PAG]
* Quant: 2 properties (A and B) inundated; water depth approx 900 mm (p15)
* Quant: 2 properties flooded (A and B); 2 gullies blocked (9 and 10); 2 gullies with suspected collapsed outlet pipes (4 and 10); 1 surcharging manhole; damage to carriageway around 1 manhole (p16) [PAG]
* Quant: flooding in excess of 900mm deep; two collapsed gullies (p18)
* Quant: flooding in excess of 900mm deep; 4 properties (A–D); 2 properties (E–F) (p19) [TAB, PAG]

* Water from a surcharging soakaway on Coulsdon Common lifted a manhole cover and flowed down the grass verge onto Stites Hill Road, flooding the carriageway. (p16)
* Surface water flowed onto Stites Hill Road (adjacent to the park) during the 7 June 2016 event. (p17)
* Evidence of surface water flowing onto Stites Hill Road adjacent to City of London Park was recorded. A previously unrecorded sewer was identified connecting Stites Hill Road (in Tandridge) to Caterham Drive, which may have carried significant extra flow to Caterham Drive during the event. (p18) [PAG]
* Soakaways along Stites Hill Road have previously surcharged; the report identifies two soakaways that have surcharged and recommends further investigation as part of addressing the surface-water drainage failures linked to the event. (p20) [TAB]
* An unrecorded sewer connection between Stites Hill Road and Caterham Drive is specifically highlighted for further exploration as part of the flood investigation, indicating sewer infrastructure linked to flooding at the time of the event. RMAs were advised to detect unknown pipelines/infrastructure that may have contributed to flooding. (p22) [TAB]
* Quant: 2 soakaways (p20) [TAB]

### Caterham
* A surface water flow path is described as originating in the vicinity of Caterham-on-the-Hill and routed northwest onto Caterham Drive, contributing overland runoff that led to surface water flooding downslope during the 7th June 2016 event. (p12) [PAG]
* Intense local storm on 7 June 2016 caused surface water runoff and overland flow from steep slopes and Rydon's Lane, plus surcharging foul sewers, which propagated down Caterham Drive. Six properties (labelled A–F) were flooded: complete ground floor and garden flooding at properties A and B (up to 1 m in places), complete garden flooding and partial ground-floor flooding/damp at properties C and D, and internal flooding at properties E and F (one resident had to move out while the house dried). Bus drivers reported fast-flowing water (about two foot) along Caterham Drive and traffic diversions were put in place. Over 80 calls relating to flooding in the area were received by Thames Water Utilities on 7–8 June. (p13) [TAB, PAG]
* Surface water runoff overloaded the sewer network in Caterham on the Hill, causing hydraulic overload, sewer surcharge and flooding with reports of foul sewage entering properties. (p18)
* The Caterham area was affected by overland surface water and sewer-related flooding (event of 7th June 2016). Specific local impacts included flooding on/near Caterham Drive and Stites Hill Road, identification of an unrecorded sewer connection between Stites Hill Road and Caterham Drive, and community-level response including formation of the Caterham and Old Coulsdon Flood Action Group. Mitigation options were proposed for land at the northern end of Caterham Drive (Dollypers Hill) to reduce overland runoff to the area. (p22) [TAB, PAG]
* Quant: Over 80 calls; 6 properties (A–F) flooded (4 properties in the north, 2 in the south); up to 1 m flood depth in some areas; ~2 foot of fast-flowing water reported by bus drivers. (p13) [TAB, PAG]
* Quant: flooding in excess of 900mm deep (p18)

### Coulsdon Common
* Surface water flow pathways from Coulsdon Common fed into the main flowpath to Caterham Drive and contributed to the surface water flooding seen on 7th June 2016. (p12)
* Identified on the RoFSW map as part of a major flow pathway on 7 June 2016 conveying surface water northwards toward Caterham Drive; acted as a route for runoff contributing to flooding at Caterham Drive. (p15)
* The main storm drain from Coulsdon Common terminates in a soakaway on the Common; that soakaway has been observed to surcharge during heavy rainfall, lifting a manhole cover and allowing water to flow down the grass verge onto Stites Hill Road and via a drainage ditch through Rydon's wood onto Caterham Drive. The constraint and surcharging at this soakaway may have contributed to the severity of downstream flooding on Caterham Drive. (p16)
* A soakaway at Coulsdon Common is identified as being prone to surcharge; the report recommends this be investigated further as part of addressing local surface water drainage problems that contributed to flooding. (p20) [TAB]
* During the 7 June 2016 event surface water runoff overwhelmed or threatened the capacity of the existing surface water sewer and drainage infrastructure at Coulsdon Common (adjacent to the Tandridge/Croydon boundary). This led to concern about drainage capacity and prompted recommendation to assess sewer capacity and consider upstream SuDS/land interventions to reduce overland flow. (p22) [TAB]
* Quant: 1 soakaway surcharged (Soakaway A); 1 manhole cover lifted (p16)

### Croydon

(p22) [TAB, PAG]

### Thames Water
* Identified in the report as the water company (RMA) responsible for managing flooding from the public sewer system during the 7th June 2016 Croydon flood. Responsibilities noted include responding to sewer-related flooding (foul/combined) and managing risks where the sewer system may surcharge or be overwhelmed by heavy rainfall. (p7)
* {07 Jun 2016, 08 Jun 2016}: Thames Water received a large number of calls reporting flooding in the area around Caterham Drive during the event; report also notes previous resident reports of foul sewer surcharging following heavy rainfall (combined with overland flow). (p13) [MUL]
* The foul/combined sewer network managed by TWUL was observed to surcharge on Caterham Drive during the 7 June 2016 event (lifted manhole covers photographed), indicating surface water entered the foul/combined system and caused overcapacity and sewer surcharging. (p15)
* {07 Jun 2016, 08 Jun 2016}: TWUL reported the sewer network became hydraulically overloaded by significant surface water during the event, causing sewer flooding (depths in excess of 900 mm). Surface water entered the foul sewer system, resulting in foul sewage entering properties. TWUL attended Caterham Drive on 7 and 8 June, provided customer assistance and recovery help, inspected main sewer assets by CCTV and discovered an unrecorded sewer linking Stites Hill Road (Tandridge) to Caterham Drive via Coulsdon Common that may have carried extra flow. (p18) [PAG, MUL]
* {07 Jun 2016, 08 Jun 2016}: Attended flooding incidents in the area (Caterham Drive and Caterham on the Hill) on 7 and 8 June 2016. TWUL's initial investigation concluded the foul sewer network became hydraulically overloaded because surface water entered the system, causing surcharge and internal flooding. TWUL provided customer assistance and cleanup during recovery, inspected main sewer assets via CCTV (finding no defects), and reported discovery of a previously unrecorded sewer that may have conveyed extra flow to Caterham Drive. (p19) [PAG, MUL]
* Following the flood on 7th June 2016, TWUL deployed 'Lanes for Drains' to Caterham Drive to assist with pumping, sucking and sweeping of floodwater and debris. TWUL inspected all affected drains on Caterham Drive via CCTV and shared findings with Croydon Council. The event highlighted sewer system problems: surface water storm flows were entering the sewer causing it to surcharge, and CCTV identified blockages and collapsed pipes that may be a consequence of the flooding. (p20) [TAB]
* Thames Water Utilities (TWUL) took part in the response to the 7 June 2016 flood and is identified as a responsible manager to investigate sewer-network issues linked to the event. The report records TW's role in assessing surface water sewer capacity (particularly at Coulsdon Common), investigating possible 'mis-connections' to the foul network, and identifying assets/ownership in the catchment as part of addressing sewer-related flooding. (p22) [TAB, PAG]
* {07 Jun 2016, 08 Jun 2016}: Quant: over 80 calls (p13) [MUL]
