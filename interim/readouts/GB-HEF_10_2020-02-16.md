

# Eardisley flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Eardisley<br>055\_Herefordshire County/09\_Eardisley.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Eardisley
* Built-up parts of the village lie within high flood risk zones (Flood Zone 3) adjacent to the main river; areas to the west and south of the river are the most affected. Local flood defences and a flood storage area are present but do not cover all high-risk areas. (p1) [IMG]
* {26 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Internal flooding occurred to properties within the village during the key event; sandbags were also delivered to a property (separately noted on 26 October 2019). Some respondents recorded previous flood events (2007, ~13 years prior to report, and one reporting 'almost annually'). (p2) [IMG, MUL]
* {1958, 1959, 2007, 2010, 26 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Properties are located in Flood Zone 3 and experienced both fluvial and surface-water flooding. During the key event properties were internally flooded; watercourses (Willersey Brook and Folly Brook) overflowed, the Folly Brook culvert below Church Road can become overwhelmed causing surface-water flooding through the village, and flows out of bank on Willersey Brook near the weir caused surface-water flooding affecting properties alongside the A4111. Cultural heritage is affected: listed properties lie within the flood zone. (p3) [MUL]
* {26 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 6 properties; depth 6 inches to 1 ft; duration less than 1 day (p2) [IMG, MUL]
* {1958, 1959, 2007, 2010, 26 Oct 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 6 internally flooded properties related to key flood event; 22 internally flooded properties during most acute flood; 17 listed properties located within the Flood Zone (p3) [MUL]

### Church Road, Eardisley
* The Folly Brook is culverted below Church Road; the culvert can become overwhelmed during storms, causing surface water flooding throughout the village that affects Church Road. (p3)
* Quant: 6 internally flooded properties (related to key flood event); 22 internally flooded properties (most acute flood) — area-wide (p3)

### Woodseaves Road, Eardisley
* Woodseaves Road is labelled in the study area where Flood Zone 3 (high risk) and Flood Zone 2 are mapped along the river, so the road is indicated to be within or adjacent to the mapped river-flood risk zones. (p1) [IMG]

### Almeley Road, Eardisley
* Property flooding occurred on Almeley Road as a result of surface water flooding, which may have been exacerbated by the condition of a highway culvert. (p3)


## Topics without coordinates

### Willersley Brook
* Contributed to fluvial and surface-water flooding in the Eardisley catchment associated with the key flood event; flooding led to internal flooding of properties in the village. (p2) [IMG]
* Willersey Brook overflows into the Folly Brook (there is a weir on Willersey Brook), and flows also come out of bank near the weir causing surface-water flooding that affects properties alongside the A4111. The informal overflow places upstream properties at risk of fluvial flooding. (p3)
* Quant: 6 properties; internal flood depths between 6 inches and 1 ft; duration less than 1 day (p2) [IMG]

### Folly Brook
* The Folly Brook is culverted below Church Road; the culvert can become overwhelmed during storms, leading to surface-water flooding throughout the village. (p3)
