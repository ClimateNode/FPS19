

# Whitney-On-Wye flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Whitney On Wye<br>055\_Herefordshire County/23\_Whitney\_on\_Wye.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Whitney-On-Wye
* Five properties in Whitney on Wye flooded internally in February 2020 with internal depths up to 1 ft lasting 1–2 days; one outbuilding flooded to a depth of 3 ft. Properties that reported flooding are located in Flood Zone 3. Two listed (cultural heritage) properties are located within the Flood Zone. (p2) [PAG]
* {general}: Historic fluvial (river) flooding has been reported affecting Whitney on Wye; flood risk assessment for the village is based on flood mapping. (p3)
* Quant: 5 properties; 1 outbuilding; 2 listed properties (p2) [PAG]

### Whitney Village Road, Whitney-On-Wye
* Whitney Village Road is labelled on the EA flood map and lies close to the river where Flood Zone 3 covers the southern part of the village. The road is therefore exposed to river flooding and likely to have been within the high-risk flood area during the key event on 16 February 2020; this would make it liable to inundation or floodwater over the carriageway (specific reports of closure or damage are not provided). (p1) [IMG]

### Church Lane, Whitney-On-Wye
* Church Lane is shown on the Environment Agency flood map within the settlement area where Flood Zone 3 is concentrated near the River Wye. It is therefore exposed to river flooding and likely to have been within the high-risk mapped area during the 16 February 2020 event, making it susceptible to inundation or floodwater on the lane (no specific flood depths or property impacts are stated). (p1) [IMG]

### Millhalf Brook
* The diversion channel was built as an overflow to the Millhalf Stream because of fluvial flood risk. The Millhalf Stream is implicated in the fluvial flooding that affected Whitney on Wye during February 2020; properties in the village experienced internal flooding (see Key Flood Event). (p2)
* Quant: 5 properties; 1 outbuilding; internal depths up to 1 ft; outbuilding flooded to 3 ft (p2)


## Topics without coordinates

### River Wye
* Designated a Main River; flood risk from the River Wye is the primary driver of the high flood risk in Whitney-on-Wye (flood zones concentrated along the river banks). (p1)
* High River Wye levels are identified as the likely source of flooding to Whitney on Wye during the February 2020 event; when the Wye is high the flow capacity at Lyde Bridge can be restricted, contributing to fluvial flooding of properties downstream. (p2) [PAG]
* {general}: Identified as the most likely source of fluvial flooding affecting Whitney on Wye (i.e., river flooding from the Wye impacting the settlement). (p3)
* Quant: 5 properties (p2) [PAG]

### Herefordshire
* The February 16, 2020 River Wye flood event affected locations in Herefordshire including Whitney-on-Wye; the southern part of Whitney-on-Wye is shown on the EA map as Flood Zone 3 (high risk) adjacent to the river. (p1) [IMG, TAB]
