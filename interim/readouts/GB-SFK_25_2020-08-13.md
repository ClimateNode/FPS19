## Place names with coordinates

### Long Melford
* Long Melford (including the Little St Marys area) was the subject of a Section 19 flood investigation by Suffolk County Council into a local flood event. (p1)
* Internal flooding occurred in Little St Marys, a locality within Long Melford; internal flooding of one property (domestic or business) was experienced on more than one occasion. (p2)
* Internal flooding of a Grade II listed pre-1880 property on multiple occasions, causing damage to the building and its contents. Flooding was attributed to significant rainfall and a blocked surface water drain; recommendations include CCTV inspection of the surface water drainage outside the address and review of inspection/maintenance of surface water assets along St Marys, Long Melford. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* {general}: Actions recommend reviewing inspection and maintenance schedule of surface water drainage assets in Long Melford (specifically along St Marys), indicating surface water flooding impacts in the settlement. (p4) [TAB]
