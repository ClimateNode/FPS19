

# Alwinton flooding 28 Jun 2012, Alwinton flooding 07 Aug 2012 and Alwinton flooding 25 Sep 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Alwinton<br>091\_Northumberland/Alwinton-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Alwinton
* {28 Jun 2012}: Flooding from ordinary watercourses (with surface water flow converging southeast through the village) affected central and western parts of Alwinton, inundating dwellings, businesses and the main road through the village. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* {01 Sep 2008 - 30 Sep 2008, 28 Jun 2012, 07 Aug 2012, 25 Sep 2012}: Heavy rainfall caused the Hosedon Burn to overtop and flood properties in Alwinton, including the Rose and Thistle public house, Alwinton Farm and residential properties. (p2) [MUL]
* {28 Jun 2012}: Quant: 5 residential properties; 2 businesses; 2 roads (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* {01 Sep 2008 - 30 Sep 2008, 28 Jun 2012, 07 Aug 2012, 25 Sep 2012}: Quant: 5 residential properties; 1 public house (Rose and Thistle); 1 farm (Alwinton Farm) (p2) [MUL]

### Hosedon Burn
* {01 Sep 2008 - 30 Sep 2008, 28 Jun 2012, 07 Aug 2012, 25 Sep 2012}: The Burn overtopped, spilling into adjacent land and flooding nearby properties including the Rose and Thistle public house, Alwinton Farm and residential properties. (p2) [MUL]
* {01 Sep 2008 - 30 Sep 2008, 28 Jun 2012, 07 Aug 2012, 25 Sep 2012}: Quant: 5 residential properties; 1 public house (Rose and Thistle); 1 farm (Alwinton Farm) (p2) [MUL]

### Rose and Thistle
* {01 Sep 2008 - 30 Sep 2008, 28 Jun 2012, 07 Aug 2012, 25 Sep 2012}: The Rose public house was flooded when the Hosedon Burn overtopped and spilled onto adjacent land. (p2) [MUL]

### Alwinton Farm
* {01 Sep 2008 - 30 Sep 2008, 28 Jun 2012, 07 Aug 2012, 25 Sep 2012}: Alwinton Farm was flooded when the Hosedon Burn overtopped and spilled into adjacent land. (p2) [MUL]
