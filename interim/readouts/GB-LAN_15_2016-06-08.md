

# Lancashire flooding 08 Jun 2016

Reference document:<br>8 June 2016 Flood In Lancashire: Section 19 Investigation – Covering Report<br>069\_Lancashire County/section-19-flood-investigation-report-8-june-2016-flooding.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Lancashire
* Heavy rainfall across the county produced surface water flooding: roads and footways were inundated in various locations and properties flooded during the event. Overall 64 properties were reported as flooded in the event, of which 50 were homes that had been internally flooded. (p3) [PAG]
* Flooding events occurred on 8 June 2016; reports of those flooding events were made to the Lead Local Flood Authority by individuals, councillors and Food Action Groups, and reports were provided by Risk Management Authorities as part of the investigation. (p10)
* Widespread surface water flooding across the county during heavy downpours on 8 June 2016; roads and footways inundated in various locations and properties flooded in multiple towns. (p12) [PAG]
* Roads and footways were inundated in various locations across the county; Lancashire County Council Highways teams attended affected areas and many locations had begun recovering before 18:00 that evening. (p13) [PAG]
* The extreme rainfall event of 6 June 2016 (sic) overwhelmed natural and man-made drainage networks in Lancashire, producing particularly damaging flooding across Burnley and Lancaster districts. A culvert in the county was found dysfunctional in multiple locations, causing water to come out of the pipe and run across the ground and flood properties; correcting those defects restored capacity and functionality to the culvert. (p16) [PAG]
* Extreme rainfall on 6 June 2016 (sic) overwhelmed natural and man-made drainage networks across locations in Lancashire, producing particularly damaging flooding in parts of the county (notably Burnley and Lancaster districts). (p17) [PAG]
* Quant: 64 properties; 50 homes internally flooded (p3) [PAG]
* Quant: 64 properties reported as flooding; 50 homes internally flooded (p12) [PAG]
* Quant: 64 properties flooded overall; 50 homes internally flooded (p13) [PAG]

### Lowerhouse Lane, Burnley
* Officers from Lancashire Fire and Rescue Service and Burnley Borough Council attended in response to calls from residents reporting water 30–60 mm deep across the ground floor of their homes and standing water in garden areas; roads and footways were inundated in the area. (p3)
* Road/street flooding with internal property flooding on the lane; emergency services attended in response to resident calls. (p12)
* Area experienced flooding during the rainfall event; emergency services (Lancashire Fire and Rescue Service) attended and Burnley Borough Council provided assistance to residents. (p14)
* Devastating flooding where multiple houses were flooded at the same time; a dysfunctional culvert under/near Lowerhouse Lane caused water to come out of the pipe and run across the ground, inundating properties. Culvert defects were later corrected, restoring capacity and functionality. (p15)
* Quant: 30–60 mm deep (water across ground floors) (p3)
* Quant: 18 properties; water 30-60 mm deep across the ground floor of many homes (p12)
* Quant: 64 properties; 50 homes internally flooded (p14)

### Lowerhouse
* The most intense rainfall was experienced over the Lowerhouse area of Burnley; residents reported water 30–60 mm deep across ground floors of their homes and/or standing water in garden areas. Roads and footways in the area were inundated and properties were flooded. (p3)
* Most intense rainfall in the event occurred over the Lowerhouse area of Burnley; residents experienced their first flooding event for 36 years. Substantial but short‑lived impact to local roads and footways; telephone landlines were out of action for a period. (p12)
* In the Lowerhouse area of Burnley the adverse effects of the 6 June 2016 (sic) event were exacerbated by defects in a historic culverted watercourse, worsening flooding impacts locally. (p17)
* Quant: 30–60 mm deep (water inside/around homes) (p3)

### Lowerhouse Fold, Burnley
* Residential garden experienced standing surface water two hours after rainfall, with water pooling across a significant portion of the rear garden and extending toward a gazebo/shed, indicating poor drainage and localized inundation. (p14) [IMG]
* Multiple houses were flooded during the same flooding event affecting the Lowerhouse Lane area; flooding was linked to water emerging from a dysfunctional culvert that ran under/near the locality. (p15)

### Thornhill Street, Burnley
* Multiple houses were flooded during the same flooding event affecting the Lowerhouse Lane area; flooding was associated with a dysfunctional culvert that caused water to run across the ground and flood properties. (p15)

### Green Brook
* Identified as the outfall for a culvert that became dysfunctional; culvert overflow/discharge routed into Green Brook system and contributed to water running across ground and flooding nearby properties. (p15)
