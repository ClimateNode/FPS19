## Place names with coordinates

### Clowne
* Internal flooding of residential and commercial properties north of the village centre adjacent to an unnamed ordinary watercourse (flows through Harlesthorpe Dam); most flooded properties were east of the watercourse. (p3) [PAG]
* Seventeen residential properties and one commercial property north of the village centre suffered internal flooding during Storm Babet. Flooding was associated with an unnamed ordinary watercourse that flows through Harlesthorpe Dam and through culverted sections in the community; culvert blockage and overtopping of the watercourse are identified as contributory factors. Most flooded properties were east of the watercourse. (p4) [PAG, IMG]
* Internal flooding of residential and one commercial property in the Clowne community during Storm Babet. Floodwater affected properties on Station Road, the A616 (Creswell Road) and Hickinwood Lane; flooding was concentrated along the local watercourse and culverted watercourse shown in the surface-water risk map. (p5) [IMG]
* In Clowne the ordinary watercourse and associated surface water runoff caused internal flooding of properties (including 17 homes and 1 commercial property across Station Road, A616 and Hickingwood Lane). Culverts under roads may have been overwhelmed and garden walls along Station Road collapsed due to water depth. (p6) [IMG]
* Quant: 17 residential properties; 1 commercial property (p3) [PAG]

### Hickingwood Lane, Clowne

It is plausible that some properties adjacent to Hickingwood Lane were flooded during Storm Babet, but the report does not explicitly enumerate flooded properties by street. (p4) [PAG]
* Properties on Hickinwood Lane flooded internally during the Storm Babet incident; these properties are included among the seventeen homes and one commercial property reported to have flooded internally. (p5) [IMG]
* Properties on Hickingwood Lane experienced internal flooding. Surface water runoff from saturated ground north of Hickingwood Lane flowed towards the ordinary watercourse, increasing levels and contributing to flooding. (p6)

### Station Road, Clowne
* The watercourse diverts beneath Station Road via a 600mm culvert and flows adjacent to properties before diverting under Station Road. Residential properties adjacent to Station Road are within the part of the community where internal flooding occurred; culvert blockage/overtopping at these culverted sections is described as exacerbating flooding and therefore may have affected properties adjacent to Station Road. (p4) [PAG, IMG]
* Internal flooding of residential properties on Station Road during Storm Babet; properties were inundated by surface water causing internal property damage. (p5) [IMG, PAG]
* Properties on Station Road experienced internal flooding caused primarily by the adjacent ordinary watercourse (culvert under Station Road may have been overwhelmed). Flood depths caused garden walls at properties along Station Road to collapse. (p6) [IMG]
* Floodwater depth caused garden walls at properties along Station Road to collapse; floodwater overwhelmed sandbags and entered properties. (p7) [PAG]
* Quant: 17 homes and 1 commercial property (grouped across Station Road, Creswell Road and Hickinwood Lane) (p5) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 17 homes and 1 commercial property (across Station Road, A616 and Hickingwood Lane) (p6) [IMG]

### Derbyshire
* Extreme rainfall associated with Storm Babet produced peak river flows/levels (the highest ranked flows or levels on record at surrounding stations) in the Idle and Torne catchment. Communities within Derbyshire were affected; for example, in Clowne 17 residential properties and 1 commercial property suffered internal flooding. (p4) [PAG, IMG]
* Quant: 17 residential properties; 1 commercial property (p4) [PAG, IMG]

### Creswell Road, Clowne
* Internal flooding of properties on the A616 (Creswell Road) during Storm Babet; properties were inundated by surface water causing internal property damage. (p5) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 17 homes and 1 commercial property (grouped across Station Road, Creswell Road and Hickinwood Lane) (p5) [IMG, PAG]

### River Poulter
* Part of the Idle and Torne sub-catchment hydrology described for Storm Babet; included in the report's hydrological analysis (headwaters and flows through the sub-catchment were considered). (p1)
* Recorded a peak flow on 20 Oct 2023 (about 18 hours after peak rainfall) and produced one of the highest ranked flows/levels on record, contributing to flooding in nearby communities such as Langwith. (p3)

### River Idle
* The Idle sub-catchment experienced heavy rainfall during 19–21 Oct 2023, producing the highest ranked flows/levels on record at surrounding stations and contributing to flooding in nearby communities (including internal flooding of properties in Clowne). (p3)
* Quant: 17 residential properties; 1 commercial property (p3)

### Millwood Brook
* Millwood Brook flows into the River Poulter through Creswell and experienced elevated flows as part of the Idle and Torne sub-catchment response during Storm Babet, contributing to increased downstream river flows. (p1)

### Merril Sick
* Merril Sick flows into the River Meden near Pleasley and formed part of the catchment response during Storm Babet, contributing to elevated flows in the River Meden in that area. (p1)


## Topics without coordinates

### Idle and Torne Catchment
* Extreme rainfall associated with Storm Babet (19–21 Oct 2023) produced very high river responses across the sub-catchment with peak flows on 20 Oct (around 18 hours after peak rainfall). Monitoring stations surrounding the Idle and Torne catchment produced the highest ranked flows or levels on record. Towns across the sub-catchment (Clowne, Shirebrook, Whitwell, Pleasley, Creswell and Langwith) experienced extreme rainfall and river responses; in Clowne this contributed to internal flooding of properties north of the village centre. (p3)
* The catchment experienced heavy rainfall during Storm Babet (19–21 Oct 2023). Headwater rivers and surrounding gauging stations produced their highest-ranked flows or levels on record, which led to flooding across the sub-catchment and affected communities including Clowne, Shirebrook, Whitwell, Pleasley, Creswell and Langworth. (p4) [IMG]
* Quant: 17 residential properties; 1 commercial property (p3)

### A616
* Properties on the A616 (Creswell Road) experienced internal flooding. Flooding was caused by the ordinary watercourse and may have been exacerbated by the culvert under the A616 being overwhelmed; road gullies discharging to the watercourse were reported unable to discharge. (p6)
* Quant: 17 homes and 1 commercial property (across Station Road, A616 and Hickingwood Lane) (p6)

### Bolsover District Council
* Delivered additional sandbags at the junctions of Station Road and the A616 on 21 Oct 2023 in response to the flooding; part of the immediate flood response activities. (p7)

### Derbyshire County Council
* Undertook numerous site visits and local engagement after the flood event; Flood Risk Management and Highways teams investigated causes, engaged with residents, parish council and landowners, promoted a Property Flood Resilience scheme, coordinated clearing of the watercourse and publicised flood warden training. (p7)
