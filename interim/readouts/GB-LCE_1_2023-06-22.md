

# Leicester Unitary Authority flooding 22 Jun 2023

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report of the Flood Event Thursday 22nd June 2023 (South and East Leicester City)<br>071\_Leicester/flood-investigation-report-22-06-2023-section-19\_v2-0.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Leicester Unitary Authority
* Intense rainfall on 22 June 2023 caused surface water to flow across the ground and overwhelm drains and sewers, resulting in internal flooding of domestic properties. (p4)
* Properties across a large area of the city were recorded as flooded during the storm covered by this report. (p6)
* On 22 June 2023 intense rainfall caused many sewers to surcharge in the south and east of the city. Evidence included displaced manhole/inspection covers. The event caused rapid overland flow to low areas, overwhelmed highway gullies and outfalls, and resulted in damage to roads, paving and significant damage to highway surfacing. (p7) [IMG, PAG]
* Widespread surface water and sewer surcharging across the city (particularly south and east) caused displaced manhole/inspection covers, roads and paving damage, flooded roads, and internal property flooding. Overland flows moved rapidly to low areas; emergency response and highway repairs were required. (p8) [PAG]
* A map of reported internal property flooding incidents shows distributions across the city with the highest concentration of reports in central and northeastern Leicester; several locations on the map are indicated as having affected more than six properties. (p11) [IMG, PAG]
* Public highways across the city experienced flooding related to the interaction between highway drainage and the public sewer network, prompting reporting to the water company and targeted maintenance actions. (p21) [TAB]
* South and east areas of Leicester experienced flooding on 22nd June 2023: public highways were flooded and properties were flooded where rainfall exceeded the capacity of public sewer systems. (p23) [TAB]
* Flooding of public highways across Leicester occurred on 22nd June 2023 when public sewers and highway drainage interacted, causing transport disruption and prompting investigations. (p24) [PAG]
* Roads in Leicester experienced flooding on 22nd June 2023; this led to requests for sewer condition reports and localised modelling to identify capacity issues in drainage and sewer networks. (p25)
* Areas in the South Knighton area along the Hol Brook were affected by flooding; flooding occurred along the watercourse and affected the local area. (p28)
* Quant: over 80 domestic properties (p4)
* Quant: 18 flooding incidents; 95 personnel; 7 pumps (at 6 locations); over 60 properties visited (p8) [PAG]
* Quant: more than 6 properties at multiple locations (p11) [IMG, PAG]
