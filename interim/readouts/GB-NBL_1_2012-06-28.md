

# Acomb flooding 28 Jun 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Acomb<br>091\_Northumberland/Acomb-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Northumberland
* Overtopping of the Red Burn (main river) and intense surface water runoff caused flooding to residential properties and gardens in Acomb. Water left the river, entered back gardens and then entered some properties on Morrison Terrace, Cross Bank View and Tynedale Terrace. Surface water collected in the road on Morrison Terrace (gulleys and highway drainage overwhelmed) contributing to property flooding; two roads were affected. (p1) [IMG]
* Parts of Northumberland (specifically the settlement of Acomb) experienced river breach(s) on 28/06/2012 with consequent surface-water flow impacting dwellings and roads in the Acomb area. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 12 residential properties; 2 roads (p1) [IMG]

### Acomb
* Residential properties and gardens in Acomb were flooded when the Red Burn (main river) rose and came out of its banks, entering back gardens and then some properties. Surface water runoff during intense rainfall also collected in roads and overwhelmed drainage. Roads were affected in the village. (p1) [IMG]
* River breach(s) in the main river within Acomb produced surface-water flow southeastwards that inundated central and eastern parts of the settlement; breach zones overlapped residential areas and caused roads to be shown as affected on the map. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 12 residential properties; 2 roads (p1) [IMG]

### Morrison Terrace, Acomb
* Properties and gardens were flooded when the Red Burn overtopped into rear gardens and then into some properties. Additionally, intense rainfall caused runoff from a steep bank on the opposite side to collect in the road; gulleys and highway drainage were overwhelmed and low freeboard at front doors allowed water ingress. (p1)
* {general}: Heavy maintenance works were carried out following the June floods, indicating Morrison Terrace was affected by flooding and required remedial works. (p2) [IMG]

### Tynedale Terrace, Acomb
* Properties and gardens were flooded when the Red Burn overtopped its banks and water entered rear gardens and some properties. (p1)

### Bishop's Hill, Acomb
* {general}: Reports from residents at a parish/flood-group meeting of surface water flooding on Bishop Hill; unclear from reports whether properties were internally flooded. Flooding linked to ordinary watercourse or highways drainage. (p2)

### Redburn Crescent, Acomb
* Shown on the map as a road/area affected by flooding (roads_affected in legend) running through/adjacent to the breach zones; Redburn Crescent was impacted by floodwater from the breached river and surface water flow. (p3) [IMG]
