### Towcester

### Northamptonshire
* Heavy rainfall on a saturated catchment caused flooding from agricultural runoff, surface water runoff and ordinary watercourse flooding across the county; a blocked culvert and overtopping banks contributed. The event led to widespread reports including internal flooding of properties within the county. (p7)
* County-wide impacts from the 9 March 2016 event: over 70 properties reported internal flooding and there were over 200 reports of flooding arising from a combination of agricultural runoff, surface water runoff and ordinary watercourse flooding. (p9) [PAG]

## Topics without coordinates

### Anglian Water
* The LLFA's current understanding is that Anglian Water have accepted responsibility for the trash screen at the start of the culvert under Burcote Fields — the trash screen which, when blocked, led to the watercourse overtopping and surface water flowing onto Burcote Fields towards properties. (p12)
* {general}: Operate and maintain public sewers in Towcester with frequent inspections; where properties are at risk of flooding they assess network capacity and may undertake upgrades if appropriate. Regarding the trash screen at the entrance to the culvert under Burcote Fields: Anglian Water state it is not owned/adopted by them, it has had a number of issues, and Anglian Water have cleaned it in the past as a gesture of goodwill but do not continue to do so. (p13) [PAG]
* The public sewerage system linked to Burcote Fields was implicated in the flood risk: sources of water entering the public sewer and the capacity of AWS assets were identified as needing assessment because insufficient capacity could lead to flooding of properties; the trash screen at the culvert entrance beneath Burcote Fields is relevant to preventing blockages that can worsen sewer-related flooding. (p17)
* {general}: Public sewer systems (surface, foul or combined) can become overwhelmed in extreme weather, resulting in sewer flooding that can affect properties served by those sewers. (p22)
* Quant: two properties (p12)

### Housing Association
* The riparian-owned trash screen guarding the culvert was inspected during the flood investigation and found with a build-up of silt and debris and the screen was buckled and broken. (p10)
* Reported to have accepted responsibility for the watercourse and trash screen immediately south of Burcote Fields following flooding issues; considering options to reduce future ingress to properties (options include a flood barrier wall around frontages of affected houses or a more effective trash screen arrangement). (p12)
* Advised to work with residents and flood risk management authorities to reduce flood risk at Burcote Fields, and to ensure un-adopted drainage systems have a shared maintenance regime with the local planning authority. (p18)
* Quant: two properties (p12)

### Highway Authority
* {general}: The Highway Authority operate and maintain highway drainage systems and structures in Towcester (these works relate to managing surface water flood risk). They do not maintain unadopted road drainage systems on Burcote Fields. (p13)
* Highway drainage capacity issues associated with the event were identified; highway drainage cleansing and asset capacity assessments are required because insufficient capacity for draining runoff from the highway has led to flood risk to properties. Review/transfer of ownership and O&M of road drainage on Burcote Fields is proposed to manage the flood impacts. (p17)
* {general}: Highway drainage systems are limited to draining highway catchments; historic/adopted highway drainage may not meet design standards and can contribute to surface water flooding affecting the highway and potentially nearby properties. (p22)

### Environment Agency

### South Northamptonshire Council
* Responded to a flooding incident in the vicinity of Burcote Fields by reviewing planning policies, ensuring drainage and flood-risk measures are included in planning applications, using enforcement powers (Section 25 Land Drainage Act 1991) where riparian owners fail to maintain ordinary watercourses, assisting preparation of a drainage asset plan, and carrying out street cleaning to reduce blockage risk to highway drainage. (p18)
