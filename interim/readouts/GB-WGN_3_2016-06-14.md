

# Wigan Metropolitan District flooding 14 Jun 2016

## Place names with coordinates

### Hawkley Brook
* Hawkley Brook levels rose during the event so discharge from the public sewers to the brook was not possible. The brook overtopped (to the rear of 47 Atholl Grove) and its high levels caused sewers to back up and surcharge through manholes, and overtopping led to flooding of nearby properties (including 14 and 16 Atholl Grove) with damage to furniture and fittings. (p4)
* {14 Jun 2016, unknown}: Overtopping of Hawkley Brook previously caused garden flooding at 47 Atholl Grove; the brook is also identified as a primary source of flood risk (in combination with the public sewer system) for the 14 June 2016 event. (p5) [PAG, MUL]
* High water levels in Hawkley Brook on 14 June 2016 prevented discharge from the surface water sewer, causing sewer backup and surcharging through manholes; sandbags and pumps were deployed to site to limit property damage. (p6) [IMG]
* High levels in Hawkley Brook on 14 June 2016 prevented discharge from the surface water sewer, causing water to back up in the sewer system and surcharge through manholes; Hawkley Brook has also overtopped to the rear of 47 Atholl Grove, causing garden flooding on three prior occasions. (p7) [PAG]
* Overbanking from Hawkley Brook spilled floodwater onto Atholl Grove; limited capacity of the culvert immediately downstream was identified as a likely main driver of flood risk. The EA investigated a raised flood wall/fence and use of opposite-bank woodland for additional flood storage but found existing hydraulic model lacked required detail to confidently justify a scheme. (p10)
* Quant: 3 properties (47, 14 and 16 Atholl Grove) (p4)
* {14 Jun 2016, unknown}: Quant: 1 garden (p5) [PAG, MUL]
* Quant: 3 occasions (p7) [PAG]
* Quant: 1 in 50 and 1 in 100 year flood (p10)

### Atholl Grove, Wigan Metropolitan District

## Topics without coordinates

### United Utilities
* Public sewers (managed by United Utilities) backed up because Hawkley Brook could not accept surface water sewer discharge on 14 June 2016, leading to surcharging through manholes and associated flooding to properties; sandbags and pumps were deployed to limit damage. (p6)
* On 14 June 2016 high levels in Hawkley Brook prevented discharge from the surface water sewer, causing water to back up in the public sewer network (for which United Utilities are responsible) and to surcharge through manholes. (p7) [PAG]
* United Utilities maintain the DG5 register of properties/areas that have suffered or are likely to suffer flooding from public foul, combined or surface water sewers due to hydraulic overload. As part of obligations to Ofwat they are required to undertake capacity improvements to alleviate sewer flooding problems during the 2010–15 Asset Management Period, prioritising properties that experience more frequent internal flooding. United Utilities reported they have no records of call-outs to the area for this occasion and therefore have not carried out investigations; the area engineer noted the storm was of ~1-in-210-year return period which would account for extensive overland flow. (p8) [PAG]

### Wigan Council
* Council staff attended the June 2016 surface water flooding incident to assist with deployment of sandbags and removal of surface water as part of the immediate response. (p4) [PAG]
