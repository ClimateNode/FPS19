

# Collingham flooding 02 Jan 2023

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Collingham – Storm Henk, January 2024<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/collingham-s19-storm-henk-jan-2024.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Collingham
* Subject of a Section 19 flood investigation after Storm Henk brought heavy rain that resulted in widespread flooding across Nottinghamshire. (p1) [PAG]
* Parts of the village experienced internal flooding — specifically the two areas Low Street and Carlton Ferry Lane were affected. (p2) [IMG]
* Heavy, persistent rainfall caused the River Trent and The Fleet to surcharge and flood out of bank, resulting in internal flooding in the village. Surface-water outfalls were rendered unusable, exacerbating flooding to surrounding areas including the highway network. (p3) [IMG]
* {02 Jan 2024}: Subject of a Flood Warning for the River Trent (warning issued 07:42 on 2 January 2024); experienced flooding impacts prompting multi-agency visits, data collection and support visits from RMAs and county council to affected residents and businesses. (p6)
* Volunteers and community actions were required to limit flooding impacts: roads were closed to limit bow wave flooding on properties; properties in the village were at risk from bow wave/surface water flooding during the event. (p7)
* Quant: two areas (Low Street and Carlton Ferry Lane) (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 12 properties (p3) [IMG]

### Nottinghamshire
* {01 Jan 2024 - 31 Jan 2024}: Storm Henk brought heavy rain resulting in widespread flooding across Nottinghamshire. (p1) [PAG]
* Experienced widespread flooding caused by heavy rain from Storm Henk. (p2)
* Flooding across the county resulted in internal flooding incidents affecting residents and businesses; county-wide response included delivery of sandbags, closure of roads for safety and resource-sharing with district/borough partners; county led recovery actions including a Community Flood Recovery Grant and Section 19 investigation. (p6) [PAG]
* Quant: 69 roads (p6) [PAG]

### Newark and Sherwood
* {01 Jan 2024 - 31 Jan 2024}: The report is the Section 19 investigation for Collingham which lies within Newark and Sherwood following flooding during Storm Henk; Newark and Sherwood is the local authority area for the village under investigation. (p1)

### Low Street, Collingham
* Internal flooding affected the area; buildings on Low Street experienced internal flooding during the event. (p2) [PAG]
* Residential area highlighted as affected by internal flooding as part of the Collingham event. Surface-water outfalls were unusable due to river surcharging, exacerbating flooding in the built-up area and impacting the local highway network. (p3) [IMG, PAG]

### Carlton Ferry Lane, Collingham
* Internal flooding affected the area; buildings on Carlton Ferry Lane experienced internal flooding during the event. (p2) [PAG]
* Area highlighted as affected by internal flooding. Flooding was driven by surcharging of nearby rivers (River Trent / The Fleet) and a lack of usable surface-water outfalls, which exacerbated flooding of the surrounding area and highway network. (p3) [IMG, PAG]

### Westfield Lane, Collingham
* Shown on the planning map as being within the high-risk floodplain (Flood Zone 3 area to the west of Collingham). This indicates Westfield Lane is at high risk of fluvial (river) flooding from the nearby River Trent and would be affected by the same Trent-corridor flood mechanism described in the report. (p4) [IMG]

### River Trent
* Surcharged and flooded out of bank due to heavy persistent rainfall in upper catchments; this river flooding caused internal flooding in Collingham and meant surface-water outfalls were unusable, worsening flooding to surrounding areas and the highway network. (p3) [IMG]
* The River Trent is the main river running along the western edge of the map and is described as the corridor along which the flooding mechanism operated; it caused fluvial flooding that placed adjacent areas into Flood Zone 3 and drove the local river-flood impacts shown on the planning map. (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* {02 Jan 2024}: River Trent levels prompted a Flood Warning at Collingham; EA operated flood patrols and cleared blockages caused by high water levels once levels receded. (p6)
* Predicted extreme levels of the River Trent prompted the council to coordinate in-person visits to communities at high risk and indicated a continued risk of river flooding after the initial surface-water flooding. (p7)


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Responded to internal flooding affecting residents and businesses across the county: attended sites to verify internal flooding, provided support, guidance and 24-hour emergency planning support; delivered sandbags; closed roads to promote safety and avoid further flood damage; collated and published verified flood impact data and led recovery grants and the Section 19 investigation. (p6) [PAG]
* As lead local authority during Storm Henk, NCC mobilised a large multi-department response team and staff worked beyond normal hours to respond to surface water flooding that transitioned to river flooding. Staff visited impacted communities and vulnerable individuals, delivered and restocked flood protection (aqua sacs), assisted evacuations and provided emergency accommodation, ran communications and briefings, organised clean-up of roads/paths and removal of bulky waste from flooded homes, coordinated in-person visits to high-risk communities because of predicted extreme River Trent levels, and managed recovery including grant funding and multi-agency debriefing. (p7)
* Quant: 69 roads (p6) [PAG]
* Quant: many hundreds of aqua sacs (p7)

### The Fleet
* Surcharged and flooded out of bank due to heavy persistent rainfall; contributed to internal flooding of properties in Collingham and to surface-water outfall failure that exacerbated flooding in surrounding areas and the highway network. (p3)

### Collingham Community Flood Signage Scheme
* The scheme was activated during Storm Henk; volunteers closed roads within the village to limit the impact of bow wave flooding on properties. (p7)
