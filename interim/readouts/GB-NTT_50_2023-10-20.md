

# Pleasley flooding 20 Oct 2023

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Pleasley – Storm Babet, October 2023<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/pleasley-s19-storm-babet-oct-2023.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Pleasley
* Internal flooding of buildings and flooding of roads: 12 residential properties and 5 businesses experienced internal flooding during the event; roads and properties flooded more quickly due to already-saturated ground. (p1)
* At Pleasley the River Meden reached the highest recorded gauge level (peak 3.34 m) at the measuring station, and the high river levels caused flooding onto Chesterfield Road and Meden Square, resulting in flooded properties. (p3) [PAG]
* Eyewitnesses report flooding at Meden Square and Chesterfield Road: water entered Meden Square from the south side of Chesterfield Road, followed by water emerging/outletting from the millpond bank on the north side of Chesterfield Road. (p4) [IMG]
* {19 Oct 2023}: A Flood Alert and a Flood Warning for the River Meden were issued for Pleasley; communities experienced internal flooding of domestic properties and some residents were displaced and offered housing by local authorities; Environment Agency patrols and clearance activities were carried out in the area. (p6) [PAG]
* Associated with flood-risk activity following Storm Babet: a community flood signage scheme is being developed with support from NCC and officers are working with the community to help develop a local flood action group; suitability of the Property Flood Resilience Programme for communities affected by Storm Babet will be considered. (p7)
* Quant: 12 residential properties; 5 businesses (p1)
* Quant: Gauge peak 3.34 m; 12 residential properties; 5 businesses (p3) [PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* Major Incident with significant impacts across the county including internal flooding of properties and businesses; county teams attended sites, verified internal flooding, delivered sandbags and provided 24-hour support; 77 roads were closed to promote road safety and avoid further flood damage; led recovery actions including Section 19 Flood Investigation and grant delivery. (p5)
* A Flood Alert for the River Meden in Nottinghamshire was issued by the Environment Agency (area covered by the alert). (p6)
* Quant: 77 roads (p5)

### Chesterfield Road, Pleasley
* River Meden overtopped/rose and flooded onto Chesterfield Road, with floodwater entering and flooding properties on the road. (p3) [IMG]
* Eyewitnesses report floodwater moved from the south side of Chesterfield Road into Meden Square; water also came out of the millpond bank on the north side of Chesterfield Road, contributing to the flooding of Meden Square. (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 12 residential properties; 3 businesses (p3) [IMG]

### Meden Square, Pleasley
* Floodwater from the River Meden inundated Meden Square. Eyewitnesses indicate Meden Square flooded from the south side of Chesterfield Road before additional water came out of bank from the millpond north of Chesterfield Road, causing properties/businesses to be flooded. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 2 businesses (p3) [IMG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Pleasley Square
* {summer 2023}: The Environment Agency carried out a large desilt of the River Meden downstream of Pleasley Square as part of flood-related works. (p6)

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Major Incident with significant flood impacts across the county including internal flooding of residences and businesses. Officers attended sites to verify internal flooding, offered support and advice, visited affected residents and businesses to establish sources and severity, delivered sandbags, collated and published verified flood impact data, provided 24-hour emergency support, closed roads to promote safety and avoid additional flood damage, and initiated/led recovery and property flood resilience grant arrangements. (p5)
* Quant: 77 roads (p5)

### Environment Agency
* {summer 2023, 19 Oct 2023}: Warned and informed the community and partners; instigated flood patrols; cleared blockages before and after peak flows; coordinated community information officers and surveyors to visit affected communities; carried out a large desilt of the River Meden downstream of Pleasley Square (Summer 2023); issued a Flood Alert for the River Meden in Nottinghamshire and issued a Flood Warning for the River Meden at Pleasley. (p6) [MUL]

### A617
