

# Hereford flooding October 2019 and Hereford flooding February 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Hereford (Newton Farm, Redhill)<br>055\_Herefordshire County/27\_Hereford\_Newton\_Redhill.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Hereford
* {general}: The investigation area in Hereford (centred on Newton Farm/Red Hill) is shown on the Environment Agency Flood Map for Planning. The map identifies highest flood risk adjacent to the main river and Newton Brook; flood defences and a flood storage area are shown to protect nearby residential and school areas. Much of the urban area (including parts of Hereford) is mapped as flood zone 1 (lowest risk), while areas next to the watercourses are zone 3 (highest risk). (p1) [IMG]
* {October 2019, February 2020, 01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: The Hereford area experienced fluvial flooding from Newton Brook (two properties flooded) and surface water flooding at other local locations (reports from Charlton Avenue, Great Western Way, Falstaff Road); properties near Hereford Academy and a property off Great Western Way were affected or at risk due to topography and drainage issues. (p2) [TAB, MUL]
* {October 2019, February 2020, 01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: Quant: 6 properties (internally flooded); 2 properties (p2) [TAB, MUL]

### Belmont Road, Hereford
* {October 2019, February 2020}: A resident reported flooding caused by an overflowing drainage system, describing 'water and sewerage' entering the property (indicative of sewer/system surcharge contributing to internal flooding). (p2) [MUL]

### Charlton Avenue, Hereford
* {October 2019, 01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: Surface water flooding affected properties on Charlton Avenue; a resident reported flooding having come from The Great Western Way (flow route). (p2) [MUL]

### Falstaff Road, Hereford
* {October 2019, 01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020, February 2020}: Surface water entered properties associated with a blocked gully; the report supports surface water flooding being primary with possible contribution from sewer/surcharging. (p2) [MUL]

### Newton Brook
* {general}: Newton Brook is associated with the area of highest flood risk (Flood Zone 3). Flooding from Newton Brook and the adjacent main river concentrates highest risk in the northwest section of the map and threatens nearby residential and community areas; flood storage and defences are sited nearby to manage flows and protect those areas. (p1) [IMG]
* {October 2019, February 2020}: Fluvial flows from Newton Brook inundated properties, causing internal flooding. (p2) [MUL]
* {October 2019, February 2020}: Quant: 2 properties (p2) [MUL]

### Great Western Way
* {October 2019, 01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020, February 2020}: Acted as a flow route for surface water that inundated nearby properties; a property off Great Western Way was reported at risk and residents reported flood flows originating from this path/route. (p2) [MUL]
* {October 2019, 01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020, February 2020}: Quant: 1 property at risk (p2) [MUL]

### Hereford Academy
* {October 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Three properties adjacent to the Academy flooded when private surface-water gullies serving the properties blocked, or when the surface-water drainage network (into which the roofs' downpipes discharge) surcharged during the storm. (p3) [PAG, MUL]
* {October 2019, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: three properties (p3) [PAG, MUL]

### Newton Farm
* {general}: Newton Farm is the primary area highlighted by the red investigation circle. Flood risk mapping shows proximity to Newton Brook and the main river with higher-risk zones nearby; the area is the focus of the flood investigation and is shown in the EA Flood Map for Planning. (p1) [IMG]
