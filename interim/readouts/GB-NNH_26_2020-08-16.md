

# Rushden flooding 16 Aug 2020

## Place names with coordinates

### Northampton Road, Rushden
* Internal flooding of a building on Northampton Road when surface water drainage was unable to convey intense, short-duration rainfall and excess water flowed over ground to low points around properties. (p6)
* Internal flooding of a building on Northampton Road; the property reported internal flooding from the event on 16 August 2020 and had experienced internal flooding on more than one occasion in the previous five years. (p8)
* The commercial area on Northampton Road (north‑west of the town centre) was recorded as flooded; surface water/urban flooding impacted warehousing, delivery yards and car parking in the localised catchment. (p11)
* Surface water runoff from the A5001 Northampton Road flowed across the car park frontage to the front of the affected property. Highway gullies on the A5001 may have been blocked at the time of the event; the road gully immediately adjacent to the vehicle access into the site was observed blocked, contributing to poor drainage and runoff on the highway. (p17)
* Surface water runoff from an adjacent car park flowed to the front of a property, causing water to reach the property frontage and likely ingress. (p38) [IMG]
* Quant: 1 building (p6)

### Rushden
* Flood incident on Northampton Road in Rushden caused internal flooding of a building; intense heavy rainfall overwhelmed surface water drainage leading to overland flow to low points around properties. (p6)
* Rushden was the location of the flood incident at Northampton Road on 16 August 2020, where a building suffered internal flooding. (p8)
* Flooding occurred in the town area (including Northampton Road). Runoff from local watercourses and the localised catchment led to surface water/urban flooding affecting commercial areas in the north‑west of the town. (p11)
* Surface water runoff from car park areas to the front of a property and overtopping of a watercourse to the rear caused fast-flowing floodwater to enter a ground-floor workshop. Water entered via the main doors, back door and through blockwork walls. Standing water was pumped out, drains were rodded, and the water receded within 24 hours. (p16)
* On 16 August 2020 intense, short-duration heavy rainfall overwhelmed surface water drainage across Rushden. Excess rainwater flowed over ground to local low points around properties because buried drainage and overland flow routes could not convey the water, resulting in surface water pooling and flooding in the affected area. No community or property-level resilience measures were in place to mitigate impacts. (p19)
* Quant: 1 building (p6)
* Quant: 1 property; 150 mm internal flood depth; 0.3 m external flood depth; receded within 24 hours (p16)


## Topics without coordinates

### A45
* An open watercourse flows along the edge of the westbound carriageway; water within this watercourse exceeded its channel throughout the rainfall and flooding event and is recorded as a contributing factor to the flooding incident adjacent to the A45. (p14)
* The watercourse to the rear of the property, adjacent to the A45, was overgrown and partially blocked with debris and overgrowth and was not functioning effectively to convey surface water from the 600mm culvert; this contributed to the watercourse exceeding its banks and surface water flowing around and into the rear of the property. (p17)

### Anglian Water
* AWS-operated surface water public sewers (including a 600mm diameter surface water sewer) discharge into the open watercourse adjacent to the site. The watercourse exceeded its channel during the rainfall and is recorded as a contributing factor to the flooding incident; AWS outfalls to that watercourse are therefore implicated in the drainage pathways involved in the event. (p14)
* A 600mm Anglian Water culvert discharged surface water into a rear watercourse that was overgrown and partially blocked and thus not able to convey the discharge effectively, contributing to flooding in the area. AWS received no calls from the NN10 6A area during the event. (p18)

### Car Park
* Runoff from the car park flowed toward the property frontage, producing water and mud accumulation alongside the property and indicating inadequate drainage management between the parking area and the building. (p37) [IMG]
