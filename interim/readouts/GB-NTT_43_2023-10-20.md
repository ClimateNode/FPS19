

# Maythorne flooding 20 Oct 2023

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Maythorne – Storm Babet, October 2023<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/maythorne-s19-storm-babet-oct-2023.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Nottinghamshire
* County-level flood response actions: initiated and led the Section 19 Flood Investigation; liaised with communities and RMAs; supported community flood signage schemes; delivered sandbags to affected areas and communities; closed roads to promote safety and avoid additional flood damage; shared resources with district/borough partners; communicated key messages to the public and elected members; will review Property Flood Resilience Programme and strengthen riparian enforcement and asset inspection processes. (p5) [PAG]
* Quant: 77 roads (p5) [PAG]

### Newark and Sherwood
* District council response and community impacts: formed multi-department response team; staff visited impacted communities and vulnerable individuals; many aqua sacs were delivered and people were helped to evacuate homes; provided emergency accommodation for evacuated residents; communications warned and informed the public; as flooding shifted from surface water to river flooding the council helped clean roads and paths and remove bulky waste from flooded homes; coordinated in-person visits to high-risk communities in response to predicted extreme River Trent levels; commenced recovery actions including grant funding and restocking aqua sacs with parish/town councils. (p5)

### Maythorne
* Areas of the village experienced internal flooding; residential properties and a business were flooded internally due to sustained heavy rainfall during Storm Babet. (p1)
* Quant: 6 residential properties; 1 business (p1)

### Maythorne Lane, Maythorne
* A watercourse alongside the southeast side of Maythorne Lane flowed out of bank during Storm Babet when flow was reduced at multiple pinch points (road crossings and culverts). Floodwater flowed onto Maythorne Lane and entered properties. (p2) [PAG]
* A local watercourse on the southeast side of Maythorne Lane flowed out of bank during Storm Babet due to flow reductions at pinch points (road crossings and culverts). Floodwater flowed onto Maythorne Lane and then entered properties on Maythorne Lane. High water levels in the River Greet may have caused the watercourse to back up, reducing capacity and contributing to out-of-bank flooding. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 6 residential properties; 1 business (p2) [PAG]

### River Greet
* High water levels in the Greet during Storm Babet are reported to have possibly reduced capacity in a connected watercourse, causing the watercourse to back up and contribute to out-of-bank flooding onto Maythorne Lane. (p2)

### River Trent
* Predicted extreme river levels increased the risk of river flooding; this prompted coordinated in-person visits by the council to communities at high risk and contributed to the continued river-flood risk phase of the event. (p5)


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* A major flood event caused internal flooding across the county. Nottinghamshire County Council responded by initiating a Community Flood Recovery Grant for those with internal flooding; attending sites to verify internal flooding and offer support, guidance and advice to affected residents and businesses; collecting and publishing verified flood impact data on Resilience Direct; visiting affected premises to establish sources and severity of flooding; chairing and attending Strategic/Tactical/Recovery Coordination Group meetings; providing 24-hour support through the Emergency Planning team; carrying out actions requested by coordination groups; and initiating and leading the Section 19 Flood Investigation. (p4)
* As the Lead Local Flood Authority, carried out response actions (cleared the Southwell Trail trash screen and will assess the capacity of a downstream culvert), committed to continuing investigations into the causes of the incident, prioritising the most vulnerable communities, and reviewing the suitability of its Property Flood Resilience Programme for communities affected by Storm Babet; will work with partners to strengthen enforcement of riparian responsibilities and asset inspection processes. (p6)

### Environment Agency
* A watercourse alongside the southeast side of Maythorne Lane flowed out of bank and onto the road due to flow reductions at pinch points (road crossings and culverts) and possibly reduced capacity caused by high water levels in the Greet. Floodwater entered properties on Maythorne Lane. (p2) [IMG]
* Quant: 6 residential properties; 1 business (p2) [IMG]
