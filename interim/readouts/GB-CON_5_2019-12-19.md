

# Hayle flooding 19 Dec 2019

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report for the Hayle event of 19 December 2019<br>027\_Cornwall/027\_2019\_Cornwall/section-19-report-for-hayle.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Hayle
* Fore Street experienced flash flooding (seen on CCTV) and was closed by Hayle CFRS; Copperhouse area of Hayle was reported flooded and received a 999 call; the St Erth river gauge reached trigger level prompting an Environment Agency flood warning for Hayle; a riverbank collapse was reported at Philgray Close; water had completely receded from Fore Street by 13:15. (p2) [TAB]
* Major flood incident in the town: town centre inundated by agricultural surface water runoff and by the River Hayle rising rapidly with tide-locking; town-centre flooding aggravated by bow-wave from traffic; town-centre streets (Prospect Place, St Johns Street and Chapel Lane) were closed and feeder streets flooded; significant internal flooding of properties in the town centre (Fore Street). (p5) [PAG]
* Hayle experienced the most significant flooding on 19 December 2019, driven by an intense downpour between about 10:00 and 10:45 which led to surface water/urban flooding in the town. (p6) [PAG]
* Town drainage and highway drains were pushed to capacity by intense rainfall and by water flowing in from outside the urban drainage catchment (including agricultural runoff and A30 areas), resulting in flooding of the urban area. High river and tide levels in the River Hayle (around the same time) increased flood risk within the town. (p7)
* {19 Dec 2019, 20 Dec 2019}: Surface water flooding affected the town: flows down Lower Church Street approached house thresholds; internal flooding of residential and commercial properties occurred on Fore Street and Commercial Road due to bow waves from vehicles; property flooding by surface water run-off occurred on Strawberry Lane between Burnthouse Lane and High Lanes; a combined sewage overflow was observed on Chapel Lane. (p8) [PAG, MUL]
* Surface water flooding occurred in parts of Hayle on 19th December 2019. Observations indicate Copperhouse Pool levels were more than 1 metre below ground in the flooded area and therefore were not considered to have contributed to the extent of surface water flooding. (p9)
* Flooding in Hayle was driven in severity by agricultural runoff (also affected the A30). High tide coincided with extreme rainfall but tidal influence was judged not to be a significant contributor. Flood consequences included flood damage, mud and silt on the construction site at Trevassack, blocked drains, and main floodwaters (carrying sediment) flooding a property uphill of the development and then through the development. (p10) [PAG]
* On 19th December 2019 floodwaters carrying large amounts of sediment inundated properties and flowed through a development (including flooding a property uphill of a construction site). Mud and silt were deposited on site and drains became blocked in the aftermath; properties in the town sustained flood damage. (p11)
* Hayle experienced a major flooding incident on 19 December 2019 caused primarily by agricultural runoff from uphill fields combined with extreme rainfall and saturated ground; properties in Hayle were flooded and the town was the principal area affected that day. (p12) [PAG]
* Quant: 46 properties (p5) [PAG]
* {19 Dec 2019, 20 Dec 2019}: Quant: approximately 50 properties (p8) [PAG, MUL]

### Fore Street, Hayle
* Fore Street experienced flash/surface-water flooding visible on CCTV from about 10:18; Hayle CFRS closed the road; water had completely receded by 13:15. (p2) [TAB]
* Fore Street in Hayle town centre experienced knee‑deep flooding; internal flooding of properties occurred in the town centre concentrated at Fore Street. (p5)
* Evidence of flooding on Commercial Road and Fore Street; vehicle bow waves from passing vehicles caused internal flooding of properties. (p8)
* Quant: 46 properties (p5)
* Quant: approximately 50 properties (Commercial Road and Fore Street) (p8)

### A30 at Hayle
* Flooding forced closure of a section of the A30, causing transport disruption between Camborne West and St Erth Roundabout; the road was later reopened. (p2) [TAB]
* A 7-mile stretch of the A30 was flooded and closed westbound due to muddy surface-water runoff from nearby agricultural fields (fields set to daffodil cultivation); flooding was muddy and required pumping/clearance; the A30 was later reopened around 2pm. (p5)
* Flow exceedance from A30 areas fed additional runoff into the town’s drainage system, contributing to drainage overwhelm and urban flooding in Hayle. (p7)
* Agricultural runoff increased the severity of flooding that impacted the A30, causing the road to be affected by floodwaters. (p10)
* The A30 trunk road was flooded on 19 December 2019 due to agricultural runoff from ploughed fields downhill, resulting in inundation of the carriageway. (p12)
* Quant: 7-mile stretch (p2) [TAB]

## Topics without coordinates

### Environment Agency
* {19 Dec 2019, 20 Dec 2019}: Sent reconnaissance crews to Hayle and other locations; attended the centre of Hayle at 15:00 on 19 December and noted evidence of flooding on main roads and between specific streets; attended further Hayle locations on 20 December and observed property flooding by surface water run-off on Strawberry Lane; observed a combined sewage overflow on Chapel Lane and reported an environmental incident; recorded runoff evidence in upstream crop fields. (p8) [MUL]
* Attended St Erth (13:30) and Relubbus (14:20) on 19 December 2019. At St Erth they found the River Hayle not out of bank, observed debris on Chenhalls Road and water ponding on the golf course likely caused by surface water. At Relubbus they observed road, land outside properties and gardens flooded and one property flooded internally; residents pumped surface water into the Relubbus Stream which had capacity. (p9) [PAG]
* Secured funding after the 19 December 2019 event to establish a Land Management Team to advise farmers and landowners on water management and drainage practices aimed at reducing agricultural runoff and sediment entering floodwaters. (p11)
* Acted promptly and efficiently in response to flooding on 19 December 2019 in Hayle; alongside Cornwall Council is pressing Defra for funding for a Surface Water Management Plan for Hayle. (p12) [PAG]
* {19 Dec 2019, 20 Dec 2019}: Quant: approximately 50 properties; two high-risk crop fields (p8) [MUL]
* Quant: one property (p9) [PAG]

### Cornwall County Council
* Cornwall Council's CCTV captured much of the town flooding unfolding and the Highways Team were quickly on site to respond. (p8)
* Directly involved in immediate flood response after the 19 December 2019 event: arranged relocation of parked cars to a nearby car park so road gullies could be cleared; promoted Property Flood Resilience and facilitated grants so a number of Hayle properties were offered and equipped with subsidised PFR measures; pressing Defra for funding to produce a Surface Water Management Plan for Hayle. (p11)
* Acted promptly and efficiently in response to the Hayle flooding on 19 December 2019; is pursuing funding for a Surface Water Management Plan to identify drainage improvements and increase resilience to future surface water/urban flooding. (p12) [PAG]

### Cornwall Fire and Rescue Service
* Cornwall Fire and Rescue Service provided support throughout the day during the flood response in Hayle. (p8)
* Responded promptly and efficiently to the Hayle flooding incident on 19 December 2019, assisting with immediate emergency response to properties and streets affected by surface water/urban flooding. (p12)
