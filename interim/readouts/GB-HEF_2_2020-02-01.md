

# Wellington flooding 01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Wellington<br>055\_Herefordshire County/01\_Wellington.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Wellington
* Parts of the settlement fall within Environment Agency Flood Zone 3 along the main river corridor; areas adjacent to the river are identified at highest risk and some areas are shown as benefiting from flood defences. Wellington Primary School is shown outside the highest-risk zone. (p1) [IMG]
* Internal flooding of domestic properties across the village during the February 2020 fluvial event, caused primarily by Wellington Brook with additional surface water runoff and backwater from the River Lugg. Flood depths at ground floor ranged from under 6 inches up to 2 ft; some basements recorded depths greater than 6 ft. Flood durations ranged from less than 1 day up to 2 days. Two properties are recorded as flooding from surface water due to a bridge deck being higher than the road causing ponding when gullies blocked. (p2)
* During the February 2020 flood event internal flooding was reported across the village: road drainage and gullies were submerged so roads conveyed floodwater; a highway closure was implemented. Flooding depths reached up to 1 ft at ground-floor level and up to 6 ft in basements. Historic floods are recorded (one property in 1998; eight properties in 2007). (p3)
* Quant: 36 properties (questionnaire returns); 51 properties (Parish Council records); two properties (surface water due to bridge deck) (p2)
* Quant: 36 properties; depths up to 1 ft at ground level; basements up to 6 ft; 1 property (1998); 8 properties (2007) (p3)

### Canon Pyon
* {general}: Identified as having existing fluvial (river) flood risk and included in the scope for a proposed DEFRA-funded flood risk study. (p3)

### Mill Lane, Wellington
* The investigation maps show Mill Lane within the Environment Agency flood zones that follow the main river through Wellington, so the street was exposed to river (main-river) flooding associated with the key event and therefore at risk of inundation during that event. (p1) [IMG]

### Wellington Brook
* Wellington Brook is the main watercourse through the settlement and is shown on mapping where Flood Zone 3 follows the river corridor; it is designated an Ordinary Watercourse under Herefordshire Council and is associated with the flood risk in the town. (p1) [IMG]
* Fluvial flooding from the Wellington Brook was identified as the source of flooding for many properties in the village; almost every flooded property was within the brook's flood zone. There were multiple accounts of the brook or stream overflowing and some reports of blocked bridge/culvert or blocked gullies contributing to runoff and ponding. (p2)
* Fluvial flood water spilled onto the brook's right-hand bank where the bank is low; adjacent low-lying road conveyed floodwater and highway gullies and piped outfalls were submerged. Following the flooding, channel maintenance work was carried out on the length of Wellington Brook downstream of the A49. (p3)
* Quant: seven accounts of an overflowing river or stream; four accounts of a blocked bridge or culvert; six reports of surface water runoff; six respondents noted a blocked gulley (p2)

### River Lugg
* A backwater effect from the River Lugg contributed to the flooding in Wellington during the fluvial event. (p2)
