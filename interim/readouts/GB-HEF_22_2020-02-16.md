

# Madley flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Madley<br>055\_Herefordshire County/21\_Madley.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Madley
* Flooding associated with Cage Brook (an ordinary watercourse) affected parts of the village during the key event; flood risk mapping shows some areas of the village lie within higher risk zones (Flood Zone 2 and 3). (p1) [IMG]
* Internal flooding of residential properties during the 16 February 2020 surface water flood event. Four properties flooded internally (including one basement) and two of the flooded properties are close to an area identified as high risk on the Surface Water Flood Map; Herefordshire Council records indicate surface water runoff from the highway as the likely cause for at least some properties. (p2)
* Quant: 4 properties; one basement (flood depth < 6 inches) (p2)

### Cage Brook
* The Cage Brook catchment is the named watercourse/catchment for the area where the surface water flood event occurred; the surface water flooding in this catchment was associated with internal flooding of properties in Madley (recorded during the Feb 2020 event). Herefordshire Council historic records attribute flooding in the location to surface water runoff from the highway. (p2)
* Quant: 4 properties; one basement (flood depth < 6 inches) (p2)
