## Place names with coordinates

### St Ives
* {1947, Easter 1998, January 2003, August 2020}: Parts of the town have been flooded by the River Great Ouse and by surface-water flooding. The River Great Ouse at St Ives flooded in 1947, and parts of the town suffered seriously again at Easter 1998, January 2003 and August 2020. Some areas lie in the natural floodplain and are at significant risk from fluvial flooding; surface water flooding also affects certain areas. (A 2007 Environment Agency flood alleviation scheme reduces risk from the River Great Ouse but does not protect against other sources such as surface water.) (p4) [MUL]
* {1947, Easter 1998, January 2003, August 2020}: Parts of the town lie within the river floodplain and mapped fluvial flood zones; the River Great Ouse has flooded the town historically (serious flooding noted in 1947, Easter 1998, January 2003 and August 2020). A 2007 Environment Agency flood alleviation scheme provides protection to some areas adjacent to the river but does not protect against other sources (notably surface water). Flooding can be exacerbated when the River Great Ouse and/or Parsons Drove remain high and connected watercourses, gullies and sewers cannot discharge. (p5) [PAG, IMG, MUL]
* {1947, Easter 1998, January 2003, August 2020}: The River Great Ouse has caused repeated fluvial flooding of St Ives (historic events listed). Parts of the town (including areas developed on the natural floodplain on both banks) have suffered serious inundation. Surface water flooding is also a risk across the town when river levels remain high and contributory watercourses, gullies and sewers cannot discharge efficiently. A flood alleviation scheme constructed in 2007 reduced risk from the River Great Ouse but does not protect against surface water flooding. (p6) [PAG, IMG, MUL]
* Flooding began on 23 December 2020 due to intense rainfall and existing ground conditions; rapid runoff led to significant surface water flooding across the town, inundation of foul drainage and overwhelming of Anglian Water pumping stations. As a result foul water backed up into properties and there was a loss of toilet facilities. (p7)
* 96 flood reports were received by the LLFA for December 2020. Flooding duration was reported between 5 hours and 2 days. 32 properties reported internal flooding. 64 further properties reported external flooding of gardens, outbuildings and roads. 15 commercial premises reported internal flooding (in listed St Ives locations). (p10)
* Flooding across the town led to internal flooding of residential properties and commercial premises, and external flooding of gardens, outbuildings and roads. Commercial premises internally flooded were reported on Harrison Way, Bramley Road, Harding Way, Edison Road, Needingworth Road and Parson's Drove. (p11) [PAG, IMG]
* Multiple reports of internal residential property flooding within St Ives (for example at Wheatfields), indicating the town experienced property flooding. (p12)
* External flooding affected roads, gardens and fields in the town; streets and public areas experienced inundation during the event. (p14) [TAB]
* Intense antecedent and extreme rainfall caused watercourses throughout St Ives to exceed channel capacity, producing significant surface water (fluvial-related) flooding across the town. Anglian Water assets were overwhelmed by watercourse flooding, which led to foul water systems backing up. (p17)
* Quant: 96 flood reports; 32 properties; 64 properties; 15 commercial premises (p10)
* Quant: 96 flood reports; 32 properties internally flooded; 64 properties externally flooded; 15 commercial premises internally flooded (p11) [PAG, IMG]

## Topics without coordinates

### Parsons Drove
* {general}: Parson's Drove (Parson) is a main river whose prolonged high levels can exacerbate flooding in St Ives by preventing efficient discharge of contributory watercourses, highways gullies and surface-water sewers into the river. (p4) [TAB]
* Indicative flooded areas are shown adjacent to Parsons Drove Drain on the December 2020 map. Parsons Drove is also listed among locations where commercial premises reported internal flooding, indicating the drain/nearby watercourse contributed to property flooding in the area. (p11) [PAG, IMG]
* Parsons Drove Drain was identified as causing conveyance issues contributing to flooding: weed growth and vegetation required weed, grass and bramble clearance; conveyance checks identified obstructions to flow; silt build-up at Stocks Bridge was noted and actions planned to improve conveyance. The drain was inspected (whole channel March 2021, upstream again May 2021) to address flood-causing blockages. (p14) [TAB]
* Subject to repeated inspections and maintenance (annual upstream and biennial downstream inspections, whole watercourse inspected March 2021, upstream inspected May 2021), weed and vegetation control, conveyance checks, silt removal negotiations at Stocks Bridge, and hydraulic modelling to better understand flood mechanisms and to inform intervention options and maintenance strategy following the flood investigation. (p15)
* Parson's Drove experienced flooding when watercourses exceeded channel capacity; it is identified with capacity issues, RMA operational issues and riparian maintenance issues associated with the fluvial flooding during the event. (p17) [TAB]
* Parsons Drove Drain (a watercourse/drain) exceeded channel capacity during the event, contributing to fluvial/surface water flooding in St Ives. (p18)

### Anglian Water
* Anglian Water pumping stations were overwhelmed by surface water flooding; they continued to pump flood water which meant foul wastewater could not be pumped away, resulting in sewage backing up into properties and loss of toilet facilities. (p7)
* Carried out maintenance on Parsons Drove Drain (weed control, grass/bramble clearance, conveyance checks); Anglian Water jetted and CCTV'd the surface water sewer adjacent to Farthing Lane through to the boating lake as community support; visual inspections of Parsons Drove Drain completed in March and May 2021; ongoing hydraulic modelling review to inform interventions. (p14) [TAB]
* Anglian Water assets were overwhelmed by watercourse flooding during the event; although assets continued to operate, the volume being pumped forward caused foul (sewer) systems to back up. (p17) [TAB]

### Environment Agency
* Performed weed control (mid‑June and September) and grass/bramble clearance (October and November) on Parsons Drove Drain, carried out conveyance checks to identify/remove obstructions, liaised to address silt at Stocks Bridge, and initiated hydraulic modelling to understand flood mechanisms and maintenance needs. (p14) [TAB, PAG]
* Intense antecedent rainfall caused watercourses throughout St Ives to exceed channel capacity on 23–24 Dec 2020, producing significant surface water/fluvial flooding in the town; Anglian Water assets were overwhelmed by watercourse flooding, and pumping resulted in foul water systems backing up. (p18)
