### Kenton
* Significant flooding from overtopping watercourses and overwhelmed drainage systems leading to internal flooding of properties in the area. (p1)
* Surface water and ordinary watercourse flooding inundated properties in Kenton. (p3) [TAB]
* Surface water flooding impacted the A379 at/near Kenton causing the road to be impassable/major closure. (p6)
* Kenton required clean-up assistance; Teignbridge provided additional sandbags and skips for disposal of damaged goods as part of recovery operations following surface water flooding. (p7)
* On 17 September 2023 intense rainfall caused overwhelming runoff and the local watercourse to exceed capacity, producing flood flows that internally flooded properties across the village. Floodwater swept through Mamhead Road, down Church Road and into the Triangle, crossed Fore Street (A379) and affected properties at the lower end of the village (Bramley Close). Downstream on the River Kenn a further four properties on Powderham Estate were flooded and one property outside Kenton at Chiverstone was flooded. The flood depths reached up to 1 m in places; the force of the water ripped tall garden gates off their hinges and sent them into a school building. (p25)
* Intense rainfall caused the watercourse to exceed culvert capacity and burst its banks upstream of Mamhead Road; overland flows (around 13:00–13:30) inundated areas of the village — flowing through the school toward the car park and Fore Street — collapsing walls and causing widespread damage to river walls, gabions, gardens and the public footpath. (p27)
* Following the event temporary works were installed on the watercourse to support a footpath and maintain flow in the brook; the Environment Agency Land Management Team surveyed the catchment and identified areas for land management and Natural Flood Management improvements. Ongoing liaison with Devon County Council, district/parish councils and landowners to identify measures. (p28) [PAG]
* Kenton was affected by flooding prompting LLFA-led investigations and consideration of hydraulic modelling and other options to reduce flood risk and increase resilience. (p46)
