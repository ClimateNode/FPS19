### Cobhall Common
* Identified as the focus of a key flood event recorded on 16 February 2020; highlighted as the primary investigation area on the Environment Agency planning flood map supplied in the document. (p1) [IMG]
* Internal flooding of residential properties: four properties experienced internal flooding with depths reported under 6 inches; sandbags were delivered to one affected property. No injuries or loss of life were reported. (p2) [MUL]
* Three properties experienced internal flooding in Cobhall Common during the February 2020 event; reported depths were 6 inches or less. The report notes limited evidence of historic flooding but identifies overland (surface water) flows as the most likely mechanism for the area. (p3)

### Courtlands Farm
* There are reports that another property in the vicinity of Courtlands Farm flooded (extent unclear). A respondent attributed flooding to overflow from Goose Pool and the culvert under the A465; the report notes it is possible these properties were affected by fluvial flooding. (p2)
* {general}: The most likely flood mechanism for the area around Courtlands Farm is fluvial flows from an unnamed watercourse which passes under the A465, contributing to local flood risk. (p3)
