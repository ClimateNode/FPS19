

# Needham Market flooding 18 Oct 2023 - 21 Oct 2023

## Place names with coordinates

### Needham Market
* Severely impacted by intense rainfall from Storm Babet: multiple overland pluvial flow paths entered the town, flowed through open and piped watercourses and converged with the River Gipping. This overwhelmed natural flow routes and drainage/watercourse capacity, causing overtopping of main river channels and resultant internal flooding of properties, infrastructure and services. (p4)
* Needham Market experienced major flooding on 20 October 2023 from both fluvial and pluvial sources. The Lion Barn drain and Orchard Gate watercourse exceeded capacity and overtopped their banks causing a fluvial element to most property flooding; surface water run-off caused flooding on Foxglove and Hargrave Avenue. Anecdotal evidence indicates initial flooding on Pinecroft Way from the Lion Barn Drain. (p7) [IMG]
* Major flooding on 20 October 2023 during Storm Babet caused internal flooding to residential and commercial properties. Flooding sources were fluvial (River Gipping catchment / local watercourses including Lion Barn Drain and Orchard Gate overtopping) and pluvial (surface water run-off affecting streets such as Foxglove Avenue and Hargrave Avenue). (p8) [PAG, IMG]
* {01 May 2012 - 31 May 2012, 18 Oct 2023 - 21 Oct 2023}: Multiple residential streets across four investigation zones in Needham Market were affected by flooding during Storm Babet. Pinecroft Way experienced the first signs of flooding on the morning of 20 October caused by rapid runoff from the Lion Barn Drain. The River Gipping registered record peak water levels which contributed to flood warnings for the town and the downstream flood peak travel. (p9) [IMG, PAG, MUL]
* Significant parts of the town experienced both pluvial (surface water) and fluvial (river/tributary) flooding during the event. Multiple main flow paths enter the town leading to flooding on streets and low-lying areas adjacent to watercourses. (p10) [IMG, PAG]
* Needham Market experienced both pluvial (surface water) and fluvial (river/main watercourse) flooding during the event. Multiple overland flow paths delivered runoff into the town and flooding affected low-lying areas and streets. Specific impacts reported include surface water flooding on Barking Road, Foxglove Avenue, Hargrave Avenue, Pinecroft Way, Limetree Close, Hawks Mill Street, Crown Street and Highland Close, and fluvial influence from the River Gipping, the Lion Barn drain and the Orchard Gate watercourse affecting Hawks Mill area, Crown Street, Pinecroft Way, Maitland Road, Barking Road and parts of Foxglove Avenue. (p11) [IMG, PAG]
* Low-lying central areas of the town were inundated during Storm Babet; the town acted as temporary flood storage with overwhelmed drainage and watercourses. Low-lying streets such as Pinecroft Way and Lion Lane were identified among the worst affected. (p12) [IMG, PAG]
* Multiple overland pluvial flow paths flowed into Needham Market from higher ground (NW, W - Orchard Gate, SW - Barking Road), converging in the town's lower-lying areas (Pinecroft Way, Lion Lane). Low-lying land in the town acted as temporary flood storage, retaining excess flood water that slowly discharged into the River Gipping. Hawks Mill, Pinecroft Way, Limetree Close, Maitland Road and Lion Lane were identified as some of the worst affected areas following Storm Babet; overwhelmed infrastructure and watercourses were observed during the intense rainfall event. (p13) [PAG, IMG]
* Needham Market experienced surface water / urban flooding associated with extreme rainfall from Storm Babet. Low-lying topography, naturally high groundwater in floodplain alluvium and surrounding slowly-permeable loamy and clayey soils reduced infiltration so more runoff remained on the surface and flowed along multiple flow paths into the town. (p14) [IMG, PAG]
* On 20 October 2023 overland flows from surrounding fields and surcharging drainage entered the town, flowing along Barking Road into Foxglove Avenue; carriageways and private car parks were inundated and properties at the junction of Foxglove Avenue and Barking Road were flooded internally (also affected again during Storm Henk in January 2024). (p15) [PAG]
* Intense, prolonged rainfall produced excess surface-water runoff from fields to the west and south of Needham Market. Runoff overwhelmed highway drainage and some ordinary watercourses, causing surface water flooding across parts of the town and inundation of streets and properties. (p18)
* Intense/prolonged rainfall caused the Lion Barn drain to exceed capacity and overland flows and river overtopping (River Gipping) inundated the floodplain, entered roads and properties, overwhelmed highway drainage, and caused businesses and properties to flood; culvert constriction and a raised bund impeded drainage and increased local inundation. (p21)
* Residential and commercial properties on Crown Street were internally flooded. River levels rose close to overtopping the flood wall and the river flowed over the public footbridge. Highway drainage outfalls became submerged or were unflapped, allowing river water to enter the highway drainage and pond on the road behind the flood wall. (p23)

Floodwater overtopped banks and outfalls flooding properties on the northern and southern sides of Pinecroft Way and Limetree Close, backed up onto Lion Lane and flowed into Maitland Road affecting buildings on the Lion Barn industrial estate, and flowed onto Coddenham Road (reported flood depths ~2–3 ft on the road). Repeated internal flooding of properties on Pinecroft Way and Limetree Close was reported over the winter between Storm Babet and Storm Henk. (p25)
