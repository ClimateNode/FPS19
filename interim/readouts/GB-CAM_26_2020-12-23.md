

# Swavesey flooding 23 Dec 2020

## Place names with coordinates

### Swavesey
* Significant surface-water flooding occurred in Swavesey on 23 December 2020. The intense short-duration rainfall on that day, combined with a prolonged period of above-average rainfall that saturated the ground (very high water table / low SMD), produced very high runoff and significant flooding. Flooding is worsened by generally low land levels and can be exacerbated if the Webbs Hole sluice is closed, preventing discharge to the River Great Ouse. (p4)
* Five residential properties were flooded internally; additional flooding occurred in some roads and gardens. The LLFA received five flood reports and identified three key risk areas within the village. (p7)
* Internal flooding of residential properties (internal property flooding reported by LLFA); additional flooding of some roads and gardens. Three key risk areas were identified where internal property flooding was reported: Middle Watch, Ramper Road and Boxworth End. (p8) [PAG, IMG]
* The village experienced reported flooding during the December 2020 event; LLFA and RMAs carried out site walkovers, residents known to have flooded were contacted, gully cleansing was ordered and Anglian Water attended to remove foul sewer blockages; discussions opened about highway gully maintenance and sewer network capacity. (p11) [TAB]
* The village of Swavesey experienced surface water flooding (noted at Middle Watch, Ramper Road and Boxworth End) and had sewer-related issues requiring removal of blockages and capacity discussions; actions are ongoing to maintain drainage and reduce future flooding. (p15)
* Quant: 5 residential properties; 5 flood reports (p7)
* Quant: 5 residential properties; 5 flood reports (p8) [PAG, IMG]

### Middle Watch, Swavesey
* Internal property flooding was reported at this location; identified as one of three key risk areas within Swavesey. (p7)
* Road flooding was identified in the southern corridor of the village along the main north–south road, with outlined affected zones adjacent to and along this road. (p8) [PAG, IMG]
* Blocked highway gullies caused surface water to pond on the road and eventually enter residential properties through the floor cavity and front door. Properties also experienced foul sewer flooding, likely from surface water ingress to the foul system or high groundwater during prolonged rainfall. (p9) [TAB]
* Area was reported flooded during the December 2020 event; RMAs and the LLFA attended to investigate and perform walkovers. Actions taken/required related to blocked highway gullies and foul sewer blockages which contributed to flooding. (p11) [TAB]
* Surface water flooding affected Middle Watch during the 23 December 2020 event; the location is recorded in Table 3 as having surface water flooding and RMA operational issues. (p15) [TAB]
* Quant: 2 residential properties and gardens; maximum depth c.80mm (p9) [TAB]

### Ramper Road, Swavesey
* Internal property flooding was reported at this location; identified as one of three key risk areas within Swavesey. (p7)
* Surface water and foul sewer flooding occurred; sewers and surface water drains have been reported to overflow into residential gardens. The sewerage undertaker attended the area to remove blockages that caused flooding. (p9)
* Two residential properties were flooded internally (depth unspecified). The gardens and outbuildings of an additional five properties were inundated with floodwater. Occupants did not have to leave their premises. (p10) [TAB]
* Reported flooding location investigated by RMAs/LLFA; follow-up actions included consideration of highway gully maintenance and sewer network capacity and removal of foul sewer blockages to address flooding. (p11) [TAB]
* Surface water flooding affected Ramper Road during the 23 December 2020 event; Table 3 records RMA operational issues alongside surface water flooding for this location. (p15) [TAB]
* Quant: 2 residential properties; gardens and outbuildings of an additional 5 properties (p10) [TAB]

### Boxworth End, Swavesey
* Internal property flooding was reported at this location; identified as one of three key risk areas within Swavesey. (p7)
* Surface water flooding and foul sewer flooding occurred: five residential properties were flooded internally (depth unspecified) and the gardens and outbuildings of an additional five properties were inundated. Roads and footpaths were regularly inundated; gullies were ineffective at conveying water away, and properties on the western side experienced high‑velocity surface water runoff from adjacent agricultural fields. Sewers in gardens backed up during sustained rainfall, causing foul sewer flooding at the rear of properties. (p10) [TAB]
* Reported flooding location investigated by RMAs/LLFA; actions included inspections, gully cleansing arrangements and removal of foul sewer blockages to mitigate flooding impacts. (p11) [TAB]
* Surface water flooding affected Boxworth End during the 23 December 2020 event; it is listed in Table 3 with RMA operational issues and surface water flooding. (p15) [TAB]
* Quant: 5 residential properties; gardens and outbuildings of an additional 5 properties (p10) [TAB]

### Webbs Hole
* {general}: The sluice door at Webbs Hole, when closed, prevents local watercourses discharging into the River Great Ouse; this can exacerbate surface-water flooding in Swavesey and nearby low-lying areas during high runoff events. (p4)

### River Great Ouse
* Closure of the sluice door at Webbs Hole prevents watercourses discharging into the River Great Ouse, which can exacerbate surface-water flooding in Swavesey. (p4)
