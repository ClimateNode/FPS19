

# Gotham flooding 15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020

## Place names with coordinates

### Gotham
* On the morning of 16 February 2020 Gotham experienced significant flooding from heavy rainfall on saturated ground. Impacts included internal property flooding, inundation of gardens/curtilages and the public highway. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Parts of the village experienced internal property flooding (from both surface water and foul sewer), plus flooding of many gardens, curtilages and the public highway. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* The village experienced surface water run-off from saturated surrounding land entering properties and highways; highway drainage was overwhelmed. In the early hours of 16 February foul sewage backed up and spilled from private drains and public sewer manholes. Severn Trent investigations confirmed surface water ingress into the foul sewer system, causing capacity issues. (p4) [IMG]
* {2012, 2016, 2019, 01 Dec 2019 - 31 Dec 2019, 01 Jan 2020 - 31 Jan 2020, 01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: Repeated flood incidents noted (historical 2012, 2016, 2019 and the event in February 2020). Foul sewer flooding occurred (incidents in December 2019 and January 2020) causing internal property flooding; culverts and watercourses were damaged or identified as defective and required clearing/repair; community sandbag distribution and volunteer clearing reduced the number of internally flooded properties. Ongoing investigations and remedial works continue. (p7) [MUL]
* Quant: 8 reported incidents of internal property flooding (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 8 properties internally flooded (p3) [IMG, PAG]

## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* Acted as the lead local flood authority investigating the February 2020 flooding (Section 19 report). Through its contractor (Via East Midlands Ltd) facilitated flood response actions across the county during Storm Dennis: coordinated 51 road closures, deployment of over 70 flood warning signs and delivery of over 4,000 sandbags; the county experienced 38 flood warnings and 16 flood alerts during the event. (p1) [MUL]
* {15 Feb 2020 - 16 Feb 2020, 28 Feb 2020}: Residents were affected by flooding requiring delivery of sandbags; issues with culverts and private watercourses were identified and required modification/maintenance; the council initiated and led the Section 19 Flood Investigation into the event. (p6) [MUL]
* {01 Feb 2020 - 29 Feb 2020}: As Lead Local Flood Authority, Nottinghamshire County Council responded to foul sewer and surface water flooding in Gotham by surveying defective drainage, clearing and repairing damaged culverts, encouraging landowners to replace damaged/insufficient culverts and clear watercourses, undertaking remedial works where required, administering flood warden and sandbag support, and seeking funding for a full catchment study. They are continuing investigations into causes and supporting community resilience. (p7) [MUL]
* Quant: 51 road closures; over 70 flood signs; over 4,000 sandbags; 38 flood warnings; 16 flood alerts (p1) [MUL]

### Severn Trent
* Investigated foul sewage backups on the foul sewer network and confirmed surface water ingress into the foul sewer system that caused capacity issues and led to foul sewage spilling from private drains and public sewer manholes into the highway. (p5)
* Investigating foul sewer issues related to the flood and provided emergency response support to residents during the flooding event. (p6)
* {01 Jan 2020 - 31 Jan 2020, ongoing between 2019 event and February 2020}: Responded to foul sewer flooding incidents by carrying out extensive preventative measures and investigations in Gotham; tankers operated constantly to assist a system running at near full capacity; liaised with affected residents and implemented preventative works within properties; conducting investigations into possible surface water ingress and undertaking remedial works where required. (p7) [MUL]
