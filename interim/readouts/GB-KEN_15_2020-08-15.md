### Sittingbourne
* An intensive localised storm on 15 August 2020 produced heavy rainfall that caused surface water/flash flooding across Sittingbourne; the report notes many of the areas affected in this event had also been affected during the May 2018 flood. (p1)
* {general}: Sittingbourne town centre sits within a dry valley that channels surface water northwards toward Milton Creek; the valley features in and around the town are indicated on the EA map to be at medium to high risk of surface water flooding. (p3)
* The event produced very intense, highly localised rainfall in Sittingbourne on 15th August 2020, leading to a surface-water flood situation in the town (report identifies high localized runoff and surface water flood risk overwhelming normal drainage capacity). (p4) [TAB, PAG]
* Intense, short-duration rainfall overwhelmed the drainage network across the urban area; surface water collected in low points causing flooding on multiple streets (including Coombe Drive, Lansdowne Road, St Michaels Road, Crown Quay Lane, Lime Grove, Woodberry Drive and The Street) and inundation of properties at low points. (p5)
* A short, intense rainstorm on 15 August 2020 produced extensive surface water/urban flooding in Sittingbourne: intense overland flows collected in topographic low points, and highway drainage and surface water sewers were overwhelmed, resulting in flooding to streets and properties. Floodwater drained away once rainfall ceased. (p6)

## Topics without coordinates

### Kent Fire and Rescue Service
* Responded to emergency calls at Lansdowne Road and Coombe Drive; on arrival at Coombe Drive pumped water from two internally flooded properties (at Lansdowne Road the water had subsided on arrival). (p5)
* Quant: 2 properties (p5)

### Kent County Council
* Kent County Council (Highways) received first reports, requested KFRS attendance and sandbags, deployed a highways officer to site, arranged a tanker to assist with highway flooding, and carried out post-incident inspections and gully/soakaway cleansing on affected streets (Coombe Drive, Lansdown Road, Woodberry Drive). Gully records after the event showed some gullies full. (p5) [PAG]
* Kent County Council carried out immediate recovery and response actions following the surface water flooding on 15 Aug 2020: inspection and cleansing of gullies and soakaways on Coombe Drive, Lansdown Road and Woodberry Drive; highways engineers attended Lime Grove; replacement sandbags/floodsaxs were provided to residents; commissioning of consultant engineers to review drainage improvements on Landowne/Lansdowne Road and Coombe Drive and a SuDS/landscaping proposal for greenspace adjacent to Canterbury Road. (p6)

### East Hall
