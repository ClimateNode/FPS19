### Woodborough
* During the storm event Woodborough experienced river and surface-water flooding: Woodborough Brook recorded a peak water level (1.115 m at 11:45) and heavy sustained rainfall led to rapid saturation of the ground and flooding of roads, gardens, outbuildings and properties. Internal flooding occurred in multiple residential properties. (p2) [PAG]
* The village experienced surface-water flooding concentrated in the central area and at peripheral sites; multiple streets/addresses across the village were flooded as shown on the location and flood-risk maps. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Flooding in the village from the Woodborough Brook bursting its banks led to surface-water flows along Main Street that entered properties and left many residents unable to leave or access the village for many hours. (p4) [PAG]
* The community was the subject of a Flood Alert for River Trent tributaries (20/10/2023). The Environment Agency carried out flood patrols, operated structures and cleared blockages before and after peak flows; EA and other RMAs deployed officers/surveyors to visit affected households and gather data. The Woodborough Flood Action Group collated local flood mechanisms and impacts and shared these with NCC. (p11) [PAG]
* The village experienced flooding; the Woodborough Flood Action Group collated and shared the impacts and mechanisms of flooding with NCC officers. A natural flood management scheme is being delivered in the upper catchment to create temporary storage to attenuate water during flood events and reduce risk. (p12)

## Topics without coordinates

### Woodborough Brook
* High flows recorded on 20 Oct 2023 (peak water level 1.115m at the Woodborough Brook level gauge) during Storm Babet led to flooding in the village, resulting in internal flooding of residential properties and flooding of gardens and outbuildings. (p1) [PAG]
* The watercourse recorded a record water level during Storm Babet, burst its banks and released water that flowed eastward along Main Street and into properties. (p4) [PAG]
* The brook burst its banks and flowed down Main Street, causing internal flooding to properties on Smalls Croft and contributing to the isolation of village residents. (p5) [PAG]
* Woodborough Brook burst its bank; water from the brook flowed down Main Street and caused internal flooding in residential areas (notably Smalls Croft). The brook's overbanking was the source of floodwater that inundated properties. (p6) [PAG]
* The brook burst its bank and released floodwater that flowed down Main Street and into Pinfold Close, causing internal flooding of a residential property. (p9)
* Quant: 49 residential properties (p1) [PAG]
* Quant: 11 residential properties (p5) [PAG]
* Quant: caused flooding to 11 residential properties on Smalls Croft; contributed to flooding of 2 properties on Pinfold Crescent and 2 properties on Shelt Hill (p6) [PAG]

### Gedling Borough Council
* Delivered sandbags to residents in flood-impacted parts of the borough; coordinated clean-up (cleansing and road sweeping) in flood-impacted areas; participated in the Section 19 flood investigation. (p11)
* Quant: 1200 sandbags (p11)

### Environment Agency
* Issued a Flood Alert for River Trent tributaries (20/10/2023) warning Woodborough and partners; instigated flood patrols to operate structures and clear blockages before and after peak flows; coordinated community information officers and surveyors to visit affected communities and collect data; attended a public meeting on Woodborough flood risk. (p11)
