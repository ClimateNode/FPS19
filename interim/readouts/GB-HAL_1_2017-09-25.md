(p21) [IMG, PAG]

Flooding had subsided from the carriageway but high water remained in the adjoining ditch; contractor works and ditch clearance were undertaken to mitigate the problem. (p25)
* A blocked culvert at the end of a ditch carrying land drainage adjacent to Cranshaw Bridge formed the southernmost flood location and contributed to a highway closure; the blockage was cleared and silt, litter and vegetation were removed. (p28)

### Halton Unitary Authority

* Flooding occurred in the area on 25–26 Sep 2017 due to blocked ditches/watercourses, high field drainage levels and likely high groundwater; signs were erected warning of flooding and a road was later closed following a road traffic collision; emergency maintenance works were carried out post‑event to resolve the drainage blockage. (p30) [PAG]

* Flooding occurred across the borough during 25 Sep 2017 - 26 Sep 2017, prompting a Section 19 investigation and leading to an action plan to identify watercourses, inspect and maintain drainage assets and improve communications. (p35) [TAB, PAG]

### Halton Borough Council
* As riparian owner and the Highway Authority, Halton Borough Council responded to the road flooding by closing the carriageway, arranging and carrying out clearance works (removal of debris, silt and excess vegetation) at the ditch and culvert inlet, and deploying contractors to address the cause of the flooding. (p8)
* Managed the incident response for the Watkinson Way flooding (communications, road closure and deployment of contractors for debris clearing); identified need to update contact routes and produce a drainage asset database to improve future responses. (p9) [TAB]
* The Council were made aware of flooding at Model Farm and scheduled contractors to address the drainage issue (actions planned for the end of the week commencing 25 Sep 2017), indicating direct involvement in the flood response. (p18)
* Placed flood-warning road signs on the A557, coordinated response and contractor deployment, located and remedied a siltation/blockage at the head of the Cranshaw Bridge Culvert which was the cause of cascading water across the southbound carriageway; closed the A557 southbound overnight and managed its reopening the afternoon of 26 September; dealt with significant traffic disruption (including impacts at M62 Junction 7) and numerous complaints/calls. (p21) [TAB]
* Placed flood warning road signs on the A557; LLFA staff located the cause (siltation/blockage at the head of Cranshaw Bridge Culvert) and contractors were deployed to clear the blockage; managed road closure which remained until the morning and enabled reopening on the afternoon of 26 Sep 2017; received many complaints and calls about disruption. (p22)
* HBC Highways and Open Spaces responded to surface water flooding on the A557 caused by litter/debris blocking ditches and culverts: arranged gully cleaning and jetting of culvert crossings at Cranshaw Bridge, mobilised contractors (Lyons, Tarmac) and StreetScene/Landscape operatives to clear vegetation and remove self‑seeded trees, and cleared approaches to two culverts. Communications team provided public updates and the road was managed and partially reopened. (p23) [TAB]
* {general}: Blocked/insufficient ditch and culvert systems caused flooding across the road and into the farmer's field; the drainage assets required clearing and intervention to alleviate flooding. (p26)
* Acted as lead Risk Management Authority for the incident: enforced road closures for public safety during the flood event; inspected highways and drainage assets during and immediately after the event and carried out repairs; scheduled drainage improvement works were delayed by the flood and were then completed as emergency works following the event; signs were erected to warn of flooding. (p29) [TAB, PAG]
* Acted as Responsible Risk Management Authority (RMA), Highway Authority and LLFA in the response to flooding on 25–26 Sep 2017; had scheduled drainage/landscape works for the area that had not yet been carried out when the flood occurred; emergency road closure and works were carried out post‑event to resolve drainage/blockage problems. (p30) [PAG]
* Responsible service for response and afterwards identified gaps in maintenance and asset information — ditches were not adequately maintained which contributed to surface water reaching the highway; HBC Highways attended, implemented emergency repairs during the visit and identified need for inspections, a drainage/asset database and funding for future works. (p32) [TAB, PAG]
* Acting as LLFA, Highways Authority and Category 1 Responder, the council produced the flood investigation report, arranged warning signs and a road closure, cleared two blockages in watercourse/ditch systems during/immediately after the incident, coordinated with Open Spaces for further ditch clearance, reviewed assets and maintenance responsibilities, and assessed the Multi-Agency Emergency Response plan. (p34)
* Quant: 10-15 metres of ditch cleared on approach to each of two culverts; two affected ditches cleared; one vehicle aquaplaned (reported) (p23) [TAB]

### A557
* Construction of the A557 altered pre-existing land drainage: land drainage was intercepted by ditches within the highway boundary on the north side and surface water was routed across the highway via regularly spaced culvert crossings. This alteration to drainage/routing of surface water contributed to runoff being directed toward the reported flood location during the event. (p16) [IMG, PAG]
* Cascading water flowed across the A557 southbound carriageway, leading to placement of flood warning signs, police initially closing one lane, subsequent closure of the southbound carriageway, a diversion route and significant traffic disruption (including AM peak impacts) until the blockage was cleared and the road reopened the afternoon of 26th Sept. (p22)
* Surface water from blocked ditches/culverts cascaded across the carriageway, causing flooding of the southbound carriageway, lane closures and a full road closure overnight. The road was later reopened with one lane running while works continued. (p23) [TAB]
* Surface water cascaded across the carriageway from an adjacent field/ditch, resulting in a vehicle aquaplaning; one lane was closed by police and the A557 Southbound was closed and left closed overnight while a diversion was put in place and the flood investigated. (p24)
* The A557 was affected by flooding caused by blocked highway ditch/watercourse and high field drainage/groundwater. Warning signs were erected, the road was later closed due to a RTC during the incident, and emergency closure enabled post-flood drainage and landscaping works to be carried out. (p30)
