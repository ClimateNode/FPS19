

# Alnwick flooding 28 Jun 2012

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Alnwick (Town Centre)<br>091\_Northumberland/Alnwick-Town-Centre-FIR-v2.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Alnwick
* Intense surface water runoff overwhelmed drainage in Alnwick town centre on 28/06/2012, flooding multiple business premises and inundating several roads and a public car park. Surface water flowed from Market Street and the sloped area south of the Market Street/Bondgate Within junction into a depressed section of Bondgate Within, causing properties on the south side of Bondgate Within (just west of the Market Street junction) to be flooded. Runoff was also directed through the rear of the Iceland store to its rear car park and on to Greenwell Road and the public car park, which became inundated. Movement of large vehicles (notably fire engines) through street floodwater also produced waves that caused flooding into some premises. (p1) [IMG, PAG]
* Surface water runoff from intense rainfall overwhelmed drainage infrastructure in parts of the town centre, resulting in flooding of business premises; the most significantly affected area was the junction of Market Street and Bondgate Within where runoff accumulated and properties became flooded. (p2)
* Streets and public spaces in the town centre were submerged by floodwater, impeding traffic and pedestrian movement; vehicles were stranded or partially submerged and people were seen wading through deep water. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 15+ business premises; 4 roads (p1) [IMG, PAG]

### Market Street, Alnwick
* Runoff from Market Street flowed downhill towards a depressed section of Bondgate Within, contributing to accumulation of surface water that flooded nearby properties. (p2)

### Bondgate Within, Alnwick
* A depressed section of Bondgate Within accumulated runoff and properties on the south side (just west of the Market Street junction) became flooded; some properties were flooded indirectly by waves generated by large vehicles driving through street water. (p2)

### Greenwell Road, Alnwick
* Surface water directed through the Iceland store and its rear car park flowed onto Greenwell Road and the public car park, which became inundated. (p2)

### Narrowgate, Alnwick
* Runoff from Narrowgate (together with Bondgate Within and Pottergate) accumulated at the junction, locally a low point, and flooded several business premises both directly and indirectly via vehicle movements. (p2)

### Wagonway Road, Alnwick
* Flooding of the B6341 was reported around the junction with Wagon Way Road (road inundation reported at that location). (p2)

### Iceland
* Employees opened rear doors to direct surface water through the building to the rear car parking area, allowing water to flow on to Greenwell Road and the public car park; the store experienced through-flow of surface water. (p2)
* Floodwater covered the street in front of the storefront; commercial premises in the town centre were impacted by high water levels, causing disruption to access and activity. (p3) [IMG]
