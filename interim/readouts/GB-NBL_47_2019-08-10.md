

# West Woodburn flooding 10 Aug 2019

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: West Woodburn<br>091\_Northumberland/West-Woodburn-FIR\_Aug19.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### West Woodburn
* West Woodburn experienced flooding caused by the River Rede overtopping and by surface water from highways. Floodwaters entered the rear of properties via out-of-bank flows (including via Old Mill Pond) and the front of properties from surface water on the highway. The area has a recent history of flooding. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 6 properties; map also indicates 5 residential and 2 business properties (p1) [IMG]

### East Woodburn
* A property on the unnamed road between West Woodburn and East Woodburn was flooded due to surface water runoff from fields and an ordinary watercourse that backed up (could not discharge to the Rede because river levels were high), causing water to enter the property. (p1)
* Quant: 1 property (p1)

### River Rede
* The River Rede broke its banks and out-of-bank flows caused flooding in the vicinity of West Woodburn, contributing water into an Old Mill Pond area and into the rear of nearby properties. (p1) [IMG]
* Quant: 6 properties (p1) [IMG]

### West Woodburn Bridge
* Flooding occurred around the bridge area where the River Rede overtopped; affected properties clustered around the bridge experienced back-of-property flooding from the river and front-of-property ingress from surface water on the highway. (p1)
* Quant: 6 properties (p1)


## Topics without coordinates

### Old Mill Pond
* Anecdotal reports indicate river water came out of bank into the Old Mill Pond area and then flowed through the back of properties, linking the pond to the flood pathway from the Rede. (p1)
