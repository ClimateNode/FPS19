

# Oxton flooding 18 Oct 2023 - 20 Oct 2023

## Place names with coordinates

### Oxton
* Areas of the village experienced flooding of roads and properties; 28 residential properties and 1 business suffered internal flooding during the event. (p1)
* Multiple streets in the village were affected by flooding; residential properties on Nottingham Road, Sandy Lane, New Road, Water Lane and Main Street experienced flood impacts. (p2) [PAG]
* Multiple residential properties across the village experienced internal flooding caused by heavy rainfall during Storm Babet that produced high flows in the catchment and led to a watercourse overtopping into properties. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Residential properties within Oxton were internally flooded when a local watercourse flowed out of its banks due to high flows from heavy rainfall during Storm Babet. Affected streets include Nottingham Road, Sandy Lane, New Road and Water Lane. A flood relief channel along Sandy Lane was utilised but its effectiveness is unclear and requires further investigation. (p4) [PAG]
* Quant: 28 residential properties and 1 business (p1)
* Quant: Nottingham Road: 2 properties; Sandy Lane: 5 properties; New Road: 9 properties; Water Lane: 7 properties; Main Street: 6 properties (total 29 properties) (p2) [PAG]
* Quant: 29 residential properties (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 2 residential properties on Nottingham Road; 5 residential properties on Sandy Lane; 9 residential properties on New Road; 7 residential properties on Water Lane (p4) [PAG]

### Water Lane, Oxton
* Properties on Water Lane were flooded. (p2) [PAG]
* Residential properties on Water Lane experienced internal flooding during the flood event affecting Oxton. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* A watercourse flowing adjacent to Water Lane experienced high flows and flowed out of bank during heavy rainfall (Storm Babet), causing internal flooding to nearby residential properties; the watercourse has pinch points and engineered obstructions which contributed to flood risk. (p4) [PAG]
* The watercourse flows adjacent to Water Lane and, during Storm Babet, high flows caused the watercourse to flow out of bank into nearby properties. The watercourse’s condition and pinch points exacerbated flooding; interaction with this watercourse may have worsened flooding on Main Street. (p5) [IMG, PAG]
* A watercourse flowing along Water Lane is described as having interacted with surface water and exacerbated the flooding that affected Main Street. (p6) [PAG]
* Quant: 7 properties (p2) [PAG]
* Quant: 7 residential properties (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 7 residential properties (p4) [PAG]

### Sandy Lane, Oxton
* Properties on Sandy Lane were affected by flooding. (p2) [PAG]
* Residential properties on Sandy Lane experienced internal flooding during the same flood event affecting Oxton. (p3) [IMG, PAG]
* A nearby watercourse and associated flow paths overtopped during heavy rainfall (Storm Babet), causing internal flooding of residential properties on Sandy Lane. A flood relief channel along Sandy Lane was utilised during the event. (p4) [PAG]
* A flood relief channel that runs along Sandy Lane was utilised during Storm Babet to convey excess flow south across agricultural fields. It was used during the event, but the report states it is not fully understood whether the flow routing was optimal and further investigation is required. (p5) [IMG, PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* A major flood event caused significant impacts across the county including internal flooding to properties and road network disruption; response activities (verification of internal flooding, visiting affected residents/businesses, delivery of sandbags) were carried out county‑wide. (p8)
* Quant: 77 roads (p8)

### River Trent
* Predicted extreme river levels for the River Trent created a continued risk of river flooding and prompted council coordinated in-person visits to high-risk communities to prepare for potential flooding. (p9)


## Topics without coordinates

### Nottinghamshire County Council
* County-wide flooding that caused internal flooding to properties and disruption to transport. NCC verified scale of internal flooding on site, offered support/guidance to affected residents and businesses, published verified flood impact data, delivered sandbags to affected communities, closed roads to protect safety and reduce further flood damage, initiated a Community Flood Recovery Grant and led the Section 19 flood investigation and coordination groups. (p8)
* (NCC as Lead Local Flood Authority) coordinated community visits, support deliveries (aqua sacs), public communications, cleanup and recovery actions in response to flooding, and is developing training and resilience programmes for affected communities. (p9)
* Quant: 77 roads (p8)

### Newark and Sherwood
* Flooding impacted communities within the district; staff visited impacted communities and vulnerable individuals, aqua sacs were delivered and people were helped to evacuate their homes. (p8)

### Severn Trent
* {general}: A confirmed report of internal flooding was recorded within Severn Trent Water; the company will work with the Lead Local Flood Authority to investigate. (p9)
