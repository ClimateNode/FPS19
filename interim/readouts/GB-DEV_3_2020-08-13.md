### Devon
* County‑wide flash flooding affected multiple communities across Devon; properties were internally flooded across a range of towns and villages reported in the summary. (p1)
* Devon-wide flash flooding between 13 Aug 2020 and 15 Aug 2020. Multiple communities were affected; Feniton was the worst affected locality with internal property flooding. The event produced a spatial distribution of small clusters of flooded properties across the county. (p3) [PAG, IMG]
* Surface water flooding occurred across Devon during the event, affecting transport infrastructure and overwhelming drainage systems in many locations. (p4)
* Feniton was the area most adversely affected during the event: roads were blocked, railway lines submerged, rear gardens (including garden offices and garages) flooded, and properties experienced internal flooding. The footpath in Salisbury Close acted as a flow route, filling gardens as water flowed down to Salisbury Avenue. (p5)
* Quant: 22 properties (19 residential and 3 commercial) reported to have internally flooded; table later lists total properties flooded: 25 (p1)
* Quant: 10 properties (Feniton); 22 properties internally flooded in total (19 residential and 3 commercial) (p3) [PAG, IMG]

### Devon County Council
* Devon County Council (Flood Risk Management) collated reports and produced the map showing flood incidents from 13–15 August 2020. The event was Devon‑wide flash flooding that caused internal flooding to properties in multiple towns. Feniton was the worst‑reported location with 10 properties internally flooded. Other affected places named in the report include Crediton, Black Torrington, Ilfracombe, Okehampton, North Tawton and Whimple. The report states a total of 22 properties (19 residential and 3 commercial) were reported to have internally flooded. The map annotation also highlights a most severe cluster near Old Feniton/New Feniton affecting 7 properties. (p3) [PAG, IMG]
* Quant: 22 properties; 19 residential; 3 commercial; Feniton: 10 properties; Old/New Feniton cluster: 7 properties (p3) [PAG, IMG]
