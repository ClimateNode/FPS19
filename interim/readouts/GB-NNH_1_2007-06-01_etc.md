## Topics without coordinates

### East Brook Culvert
* {01 Feb 2024 - 29 Feb 2024}: Subject of a flood investigation following a flood event in February 2024. (p1)
* {01 Jun 2007 - 31 Dec 2023}: Properties adjacent to the East Brook Culvert were affected by flooding. The report states a relatively small number of properties next to the culvert have been affected and that some of those affected experienced multiple flood incidents. (p5)
* {01 Feb 2024 - 29 Feb 2024}: Residents identified the East Brook Culvert as a contributing cause of local flooding during the event; flooding was attributed to the culvert (likely capacity or blockage) and resulted in local inundation reported by residents. (p13) [TAB]
* {01 Aug 2022 - 31 Aug 2022, 01 Jun 2023 - 30 Jun 2023}: The route of the culvert lies in a natural valley and is in an area with a high risk of surface water flooding; properties along the route have experienced repeated historical flooding and three spatial clusters of incidents were identified (Kipling Road / Browing Avenue, Byron Road, and Waverley Road / Boddington Road). (p14) [MUL]
* {01 Aug 2022 - 31 Aug 2022, 01 Jun 2023 - 30 Jun 2023}: Quant: 607 properties surveyed; 45 properties reported historical flooding; 16 reports of flooding in August 2022; 31 reports of flooding in June 2023. (p14) [MUL]

### Drill Hall
* {01 Feb 2024 - 29 Feb 2024}: Water was observed flowing off the car park onto the highway during the significant rainfall event, increasing the volume of water on the highway and contributing to the highway drainage being overwhelmed (isolated gullies on the car park and lack of linear drain). (p24)
* {01 Feb 2024 - 29 Feb 2024}: Drainage arrangement on the car park discharged water onto the highway during the rainfall event, increasing highway runoff and exacerbating inundation of highway drainage beyond its designed capacity. A possible blockage of one car park gully was noted which may have reduced local drainage performance. Overflow from the car park and discharging rainwater pipes contributed to overloading the linear drain on the lowered footway. (p25)
* {01 Feb 2024 - 29 Feb 2024}: Localized flooding inundated the Drill Hall Car Park and the adjacent section of Broadland Road; a significant water flow route passed through the affected area, impacting access and the parking area. (p26) [IMG]
