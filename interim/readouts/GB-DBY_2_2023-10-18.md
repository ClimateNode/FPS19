### Derbyshire
* Within Derbyshire 26 Flood Alerts, 99 Flood Warnings and 1 Severe Flood Warning were issued during Storm Babet. Across 35 investigated communities a total of 1,284 properties were flooded internally. The internally flooded properties were distributed by WFD catchment as: Derwent Derbyshire 267; Don and Rother 514; Dove 23; Idle and Torne 15; Trent and Lower Erewash 465. 38PCSYM of internally flooded properties were in the Don and Rother catchment. Worst-affected communities included Breaston and Sandiacre (more than 100 residential properties flooded in each) and Chesterfield (over 400 residential properties flooded, including areas such as Tapton Terrace and Brampton). (p8) [TAB, PAG]
* Widespread internal property flooding across the county during the Storm Babet event: 1,284 properties flooded internally across 35 investigated communities; flooding was associated with river systems (e.g. River Erewash and River Derwent) and prompted multiple Flood Alerts, Flood Warnings and a Severe Flood Warning for the River Derwent at Racecourse Park, Chaddesden (no properties reported flooded at that Severe Flood Warning location). The Don and Rother catchment accounted for 38PCSYM of internally flooded properties, with Breaston, Sandiacre and several Chesterfield communities heavily affected. (p10) [PAG, IMG]
* Quant: 26 Flood Alerts; 99 Flood Warnings; 1 Severe Flood Warning; 1,284 internally flooded properties; 267 (Derwent Derbyshire); 514 (Don and Rother); 23 (Dove); 15 (Idle and Torne); 465 (Trent and Lower Erewash); >100 properties in Breaston; >100 in Sandiacre; >400 in Chesterfield. (p8) [TAB, PAG]
* Quant: 1,284 internally flooded properties; 35 communities investigated; 19 of 26 areas with a Flood Alert had property flooding; 56 of 99 areas with a Flood Warning had property flooding; catchment totals — Derwent Derbyshire: 267; Don and Rother: 514; Dove: 23; Idle and Torne: 15; Trent and Lower Erewash: 465. (p10) [PAG, IMG]
