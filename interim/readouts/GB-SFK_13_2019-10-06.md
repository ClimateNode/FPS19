* Public highway partially flooded for approximately 50 m to an approximate depth of 200 mm; local surface water drainage (Anglian Water system) surcharged contributing to the flood of the highway and nearby properties. (p2)

## Topics without coordinates

### Highway Authority
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Langer Road and adjacent side roads are identified as an area of high flood risk due to exceedance of the surface water drainage system capacity. Flooding occurs on the highway when rainfall exceeds the system capacity; gullies have been recorded as part-full (routine cleans when ~50PCSYM full) and are managed on an annual/bi-annual/6-monthly regime depending on fullness. (p5)
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: Quant: gullies recorded at 50PCSYM full; 100PCSYM full triggers 6 monthly regime (p5)

### Anglian Water
* {general}: Undertaking further modelling of the surface-water drainage system for the Langer Road to Beach Station Road area (with data sharing support from Suffolk County Council) to evaluate system performance and design capacity; if modelling shows maintenance issues with the East Suffolk Council watercourse, Anglian Water will agree an appropriate new regime with East Suffolk Council. (p5)

### East Suffolk Council
* {general}: Potentially responsible for maintenance of a local watercourse which modelling may identify as contributing to surface-water flooding in the Langer Road/adjacent area; if modelling determines maintenance is an issue, a revised maintenance regime is to be agreed with Anglian Water. (p5)

### Suffolk County Council
* {01 Nov 2019 - 30 Nov 2019}: As the highway authority, SCC describes gully maintenance regimes for the area: Langer Road and surroundings are identified as high flood risk; gullies are recorded at ~50PCSYM full when cleaned and are on an annual cleaning regime; SCC states recent inspections (Nov 2019) following reported flooding found no gullies blocked and concludes flooding at this location can occur when rainfall exceeds system capacity. (p5)
