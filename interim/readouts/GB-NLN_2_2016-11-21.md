

# Gunness flooding 21 Nov 2016

## Place names with coordinates

### Gunness
* Internal flooding of properties in the village (properties on Doncaster Road) caused by heavy rainfall combined with a temporary blockage in a foul sewer in rear gardens. (p1)
* Quant: 5 properties (p1)

### Doncaster Road, Gunness
* Internal flooding of rear-garden-connected systems led to internal flooding of dwellings on Doncaster Road after heavy rainfall combined with a temporary blockage in a foul sewer in rear gardens. (p1)
* Quant: 5 properties (p1)


## Topics without coordinates

### Severn Trent
* A temporary blockage in a foul (sewer) system in rear gardens contributed to internal flooding; Severn Trent Water cleared the blockage the day after the incident was reported and carried out jetting/clearing as part of the investigation. (p1)
* A blockage was identified within the foul drain in the rear garden of property No.79 which contributed to sewer-related flooding impacts to rear gardens/properties; dye testing confirmed rear roof water from most properties discharged into the foul drain (connecting surface water roof waste into the foul system). A consented Combined Sewer Outfall (CSO) into the culverted section was downstream of the blockage and did not operate. STW operatives reported that surface water run-off/overland flow was a contributing factor. The foul water pumping station maintained by Severn Trent Water (STW) ran for 26 hours during the event and did not fail. (p4)
* Carried out initial reactive works to jet and cleanse both foul and surface water drainage; removed a temporary blockage in the foul (sewer) system at the rear of properties (blockage cleared the day after the event was reported). (p6)
* Concerns over capacity of the foul (sewer) water system in relation to existing roof connections, raising risk of sewer flooding; STW were asked to consider system capacity and to continue periodic inspections and cleansing of foul and surface water drains following the event. (p7)
* Quant: 5 properties (p1)
* Quant: 26 hours (p4)

### North Lincolnshire Council
* NLC carried out investigations and asset inspections following internal flooding of properties on Doncaster Road; systems were jetted and cleared and the location placed on a programme of routine and wet weather inspections. No immediate capital upgrade of NLC assets was required. (p1)
* Conducted initial reactive works including jetting and cleansing highway (surface water) and foul drains; carried out wet weather walkover survey recording roof water runoff directions; performed CCTV surveys on highway and foul drains (both found to be in sound condition); to add highway drainage to the significant drainage asset register and consider localised kerb-raising based on inspections. (p6)
* Council-managed highway drainage was affected by surface water run‑off; works included jetting and cleansing. The Council was directed to add highway drainage systems to its significant drainage asset register and to continue periodic inspections and wet‑weather monitoring to identify and mitigate run‑off impacts. (p7) [IMG]
* Quant: 5 properties (p1)
