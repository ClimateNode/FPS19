

# Nassington flooding 16 Aug 2020

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Station Road, Nassington, 16 August 2020<br>090\_North Northamptonshire/Flood investigation report - Station Road, Nassington.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Nassington
* Surface water flooding caused by intense, short-duration rainfall overwhelmed drainage, producing excess surface water that flowed to low points around properties in Nassington and led to internal flooding of at least one building on Station Road. (p5)
* Flooding at Station Road (within Nassington) caused internal flooding of at least one property on 16 August 2020; the affected property has experienced internal flooding on multiple occasions in the last five years and reported the incident to the Flood Toolkit. (p7)
* In Nassington one property was reported to have flooded internally when surface water (from nearby Church Lane) exceeded drainage capacity, overtopped a dropped kerb and flowed into and through an integral garage. (p15)
* Intense heavy rainfall over a short period caused widespread surface-water/flash flooding across this area of Nassington. The Local Lead Flood Authority (NCC Flood and Water Management Team) received multiple formal and anecdotal reports of flooding from the area and individual reports continued to be received in the weeks after the event. Emergency response was largely reactive; fluvial (main river) warnings were not issued because river level triggers were not reached. (p17) [PAG]
* Intense heavy rainfall on 16th August 2020 caused surface water flooding across the area. Surface water drainage systems across the catchment were unable to collect and convey the rainfall, so excess water flowed over ground following local levels and accumulated in low points around properties. There were no effective overland flow routes to safe areas, resulting in reliance on buried drainage systems whose design capacity was exceeded. (p18)
* Quant: 1 property reported via Flood Toolkit (p7)
* Quant: 1 property (p15)

### Station Road, Nassington
* Internal flooding of at least one building on Station Road during the event; repeated internal flooding of a building on more than one occasion in the last five years. Surface water drainage systems were overwhelmed, with runoff flowing to local low points around properties. (p5)
* Internal flooding of an occupied building occurred on 16 August 2020; the same building has experienced internal flooding on more than one occasion in the last five years. The affected property reported the flood via the Flood Toolkit. (p7)
* Floodwater entered properties on Station Road, recorded as 'flooding through garage'. (p10) [TAB]
* Surface water runoff flowed along the highway from south to north; drainage (road gullies/surface water sewer) was exceeded or ineffective, causing water to pool at the low point at the road edge and overtop the dropped kerb and flow onto the adjacent property and into an integral garage. (p16)
* Photographs taken as part of the flood investigation show a residential property on Station Road with a roadside gully adjacent and a footpath that falls towards the garage door; during the 16 August 2020 flood incident surface water flowed along the carriageway toward the garage entrance, with the gully and the footpath gradient directing runoff toward the property. A parked vehicle is shown partially over the gully, which could have affected drainage access. (p32) [IMG]
* Quant: 1 property reported via Flood Toolkit (p7)

### Northamptonshire
* The county (Nassington area) experienced surface water flooding on 16th August 2020, prompting county-level actions including asset planning, maintenance/cleansing of streets and open spaces, and coordination between highways, planning and the LLFA to manage flood risk. (p23)

### Church Street, Nassington
* Surface water runoff from the direction of Church Lane (sic) overwhelmed a road gully, forming a large puddle that overtopped a dropped kerb, flowed across the footpath and entered an integral garage, contributing to internal flooding of a property. The footpath slope directed overtopping water toward the property frontage. (p15)
* Quant: 1 property (p15)


## Topics without coordinates

### Highway Authority
* Surface water flooding affected highways in the Nassington catchment on 16th August 2020, overwhelming or threatening highway drainage. The authority was tasked with drainage cleansing, more frequent inspections, assessing asset capacity where flooding posed risk to properties, and considering overland flow routes to reduce reliance on drains. (p23)
