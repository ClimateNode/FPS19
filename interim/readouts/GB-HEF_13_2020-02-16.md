

# Hereford flooding 16 Feb 2020

## Place names with coordinates

### Roman Road, Hereford
* {general}: Roman Road runs through the centre of the investigation area and lies adjacent to the Yazor Brook flood corridor, with nearby properties and infrastructure shown in Flood Zones 2 and 3 and exposed to river-flood risk. (p1) [IMG]
* A4110 Roman Road (Roman Road) was affected during the event; it is a known location of surface water flooding and lies close to flood flow paths. The highway was implicated in the event (strategic highway network impact) and road-raising works previously included structures to convey floodwater beneath the carriageway. (p2) [TAB]
* Flood water is conveyed under the carriageway via a concrete weir and overflow channel constructed when Roman Road was raised in the 2000s; Herefordshire Council has identified a requirement to promote drainage improvements at Roman Road and scheduled a highway drainage project (2021/22) in response to recent flooding. (p3)
* Fluvial (river) flooding affected areas of Roman Road in Hereford, leading to a proposed flood risk study and consideration of local mitigation measures. (p4)
* Quant: 5 properties (in the Towtree Lane / Roman Road area); flood depths from less than 6 inches up to 4 ft (p2) [TAB]

### Hereford
* {general}: Areas of Hereford around Towtree Lane and Roman Road overlap Environment Agency Flood Map zones 2 and 3 along the Yazor Brook corridor, placing nearby properties and infrastructure at elevated river-flood risk. (p1) [IMG]
* Five properties in the Towtree Lane / Roman Road area of Hereford reported internal flooding during the February 2020 fluvial event; affected properties are within or adjacent to Flood Zone 3 and are close to the confluence of Yazor Brook and its unnamed tributary draining Crowmoor. (p2) [TAB]
* Areas of Hereford (notably Towtree Lane and Roman Road) experienced fluvial flooding, prompting pursuit of DEFRA grant funding for a flood risk study and consideration of conveyance improvements to the local brook (weir level adjustments and overflow channel optimisation) and bunding. (p4)
* Quant: 5 properties; flood depths from less than 6 inches up to 4 ft (p2) [TAB]

### Towtree Lane, Burghill
* {general}: Towtree Lane crosses Environment Agency Flood Map zones 2 and 3 adjacent to the Yazor Brook; properties and infrastructure along the road fall within the high/moderate river-flood risk corridor. Some sections benefit from flood defences but substantial portions remain at high risk. (p1) [IMG]
* A ditch on Towtree Lane has been culverted; the absence of a surface feature (pond/ditch) prevents easy discharge of floodwater from the flood zone, increasing local flood risk and reducing drainage capacity. (p3)
* Fluvial (river) flooding affected areas around Towtree Lane, prompting proposals to improve the land drain on Towtree Lane and consideration of bunding works as mitigation. (p4)

### Yazor Brook
* Identified as the main river (blue line) along which Flood Zones 2 and 3 are concentrated; the brook is the watercourse driving river flooding that exposes adjacent properties and infrastructure to inundation. (p1) [IMG]
* Overflowing Yazor Brook (and associated blocked bridge/culvert and overflowing ditch) contributed to fluvial flooding that caused internal flooding of nearby properties close to the confluence with an unnamed tributary draining Crowmoor. (p2)
* The Yazor Brook feeds a fishing lake via a weir and has a series of small weirs upstream of Roman Road; the diverted watercourse can break out of bank (towards the south-east) and flood lower-lying farmland, contributing to local flood risk around Roman Road. (p3)
* Quant: 5 properties; flood depths from less than 6 inches up to 4 ft (p2)


## Topics without coordinates

### Herefordshire Council
* As Herefordshire Council Land Drainage Authority, HC has regulatory supervision of the ordinary watercourse (Yazor Brook) that overflowed during the fluvial event on 16 February 2020. The event caused internal flooding to local properties in the Towtree Lane / Roman Road area which fall under HC’s drainage remit. (p2)
* Herefordshire Council (Land Drainage Authority) proposes to exercise its flood risk management function in response to the recent flooding; the Council has identified a requirement to promote drainage improvements at Roman Road and has scheduled a highway drainage project on the A4110 for 2021/22. (p3)
* Quant: 5 properties (p2)
* Quant: 1 property (p3)
