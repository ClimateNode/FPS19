

# West Northamptonshire flooding 01 Oct 2019

Reference document:<br>2019 Flood Investigations<br>090\_West Northamptonshire/2019\_west northamptonshire.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Manor Court, Brackley
* Internal flooding occurred within a residential property on Manor Court; flooding was reported on more than one occasion, prompting a Section 19 flood investigation. (p1)
* Internal flooding of a residential property caused by surface water runoff following saturated ground and heavy rainfall; floodwater followed surface water flow paths through the local catchment concentrating on Manor Court. (p2) [MUL]
* Surface water flowed east down Manor Court, collecting and puddling in the carriageway; flow was fast and deepened at low points, overtopped the kerb and ran down a private driveway to the garage threshold, overwhelming road gullies and the outfall and causing internal flooding of the property's garage. (p3)
* Flooding occurred at Manor Court after above‑average antecedent rainfall and heavy rainfall on the day; local drainage systems lacked capacity to collect and dispose of surface water and there is no overland flow route to direct surface water away, resulting in surface water affecting properties and highway infrastructure. (p4)
* Quant: 1 property (p1)
* Quant: 1 property (p2) [MUL]
* Quant: 200mm (depth of floodwater in the garage) (p3)

### Northamptonshire
* Surface water flooding occurred in Brackley (Manor Court) causing internal flooding at a residential property; the event is described as occurring within Northamptonshire. (p2) [PAG]

### Brackley
* Internal flooding affected a residential property on Manor Court in Brackley; the property experienced internal flooding on multiple occasions, leading to an investigation. (p1)
* Internal flooding was reported at a residential property in Manor Court, within Brackley; the internal flooding was caused by surface water runoff reaching the property. (p2)
* Quant: 1 property (p1)
* Quant: 1 residential property (p2)

### Upper Stowe
* Internal flooding has been reported at a residential property on Francis Row, Upper Stowe on more than one occasion in the last five years, prompting a Section 19 investigation. (p4)
* Surface water flooding occurred in the village during heavy rainfall on 1 October 2019; surface water flowed to the southwest bend of Francis Row and the event resulted in internal flooding at a residential property in the village. (p5) [PAG]

### Francis Row, Upper Stowe
* Surface water flowed to the southwest bend of Francis Row from the north, east and west, causing internal flooding at a residential property (converted farm building). (p5) [PAG]
* Surface water from the carriageway, roofs, driveways and nearby agricultural land flowed to the southwest bend, bypassed an offset road gully, ponded at the low point, overtopped the highway and flowed into the driveway of an affected property; drainage at the low point could not cope with the volume and intensity of rain. (p6)
* Quant: 1 residential property (p5) [PAG]

### Manor Road, Brackley
* During the event surface water runoff from higher ground was intercepted by Manor Road and flowed north; this routing of surface water contributed to runoff reaching Manor Court and the resulting internal flooding at a property. (p2)
