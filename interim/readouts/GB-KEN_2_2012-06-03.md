## Place names with coordinates

### Oak Road, High Brooms
* A flood from a section of open channel of a watercourse next to a private car park near Oak Road damaged properties and vehicles. (p1)
* At Oak Road the open channel section (approx. 30 m) and the upstream culvert inlet are part of the flooded watercourse. A trash screen at the culvert inlet was observed clear in one photo and heavily obstructed by large debris (wood, metal, rubbish) in another — the debris was lodged against the screen and blocking flow into the culvert, reducing drainage capacity and contributing to local hydraulic obstruction. (p3) [IMG]
* {01 Sep 2002 - 30 Sep 2002, 03 Jun 2012}: An ordinary watercourse (open channel and culvert opening with a trash screen) at Oak Road was implicated in flooding. The June 2012 incident prompted remedial actions to reduce future flood risk, including debris removal, replacement/improvement of the trash screen, erection of 'No Flytipping' signs and regular inspections/maintenance. (p4) [TAB, MUL]
* Quant: three properties (p1)
* Quant: 1 trash screen obstructed; ~30 m open channel; ~1 km of downstream culvert (p3) [IMG]
* {01 Sep 2002 - 30 Sep 2002, 03 Jun 2012}: Quant: two similar flood incidents; one incident resulted in internal damage to properties (p4) [TAB, MUL]

## Topics without coordinates

### Home Group LTD
* An open channel on land owned by Home Group LTD became obstructed with rubbish and debris which caused the downstream culvert inlet to block, the channel to back up and overtop its banks. This overtopping led to flooding that damaged ground-floor properties (Taylor Court) and inundated the residents' car parking area accessed from Brook Road. Home Group LTD were contacted by Kent County Council and were urged to clear the rubbish and debris; they cooperated and cleared it. (p2) [TAB]
* {01 Sep 2002 - 30 Sep 2002, 03 Jun 2012}: Home Group LTD is the riparian owner of the section of open channel, the culvert opening and the trash screen at Oak Road which were associated with flooding. Following the 3 June 2012 flood incident (and consideration of similar historic incidents in September 2002) the company agreed actions with residents: erect 'No Flytipping' signs, produce and deliver a newsletter to local residents, accept reports of debris for prompt removal, replace the existing trash screen with one easier to clear, consider higher fencing and carry out regular (monthly) inspections and maintenance of the watercourse. (p4) [TAB, MUL]
* As landowner/manager of the affected car park and nearby homes, several of their properties/tenants were flooded with dirty water and in some cases sewage; their partner contractor (Roalco) cleared the culvert after the flood and Home Group will monitor and pursue cost recovery where possible. (p8) [IMG]
* Quant: approximately 0.6 m flood depth in the residents' car park (p2) [TAB]
* {01 Sep 2002 - 30 Sep 2002, 03 Jun 2012}: Quant: two similar flood incidents; one incident resulted in internal damage to properties (p4) [TAB, MUL]

### Oak Road Car Park
* The investigation states the flood occurred next to a private car park near Oak Road (Oak) in Tunbridge Wells; the open channel at that location overtopped/caused flooding that resulted in damage to three properties and vehicles nearby. (p1) [IMG]
* The car park flooded during the June Bank Holiday weekend; cars were damaged; the culvert between two bin stores was blocked with dumped furniture, household waste and detritus and was subsequently cleared to allow flow to resume. (p8) [IMG]
* Quant: 3 properties (p1) [IMG]

### Brook Road Culvert
* The culvert running between two bin stores became blocked with dumped rubbish (broken furniture, household waste and detritus), which was identified as one of the root causes of the car-park flooding; blockage was cleared by a contractor after the event to restore flow. (p8)

### Harold House
* Tenants' homes were flooded with dirty water and, in some cases, sewage during the June bank holiday weekend flood; vehicles were also reported damaged by floodwater. (p8) [IMG]

### Laburnum Court
* Tenants' homes were flooded with dirty water and, in some cases, sewage during the June bank holiday weekend flood; some cars were reported damaged by the floodwater. (p8) [IMG]
