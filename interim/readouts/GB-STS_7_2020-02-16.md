### River Dove
* High flows on the River Dove at the confluence with Rolleston Brook are implicated in the mapped flood location; River Dove flows and its confluence contributed to flooding observed adjacent to Rolleston-on-Dove. (p19) [PAG, IMG]
* {general}: The River Dove is described as increasing the extent of Flood Zones 2 and 3 in the lower-lying parts of the floodplain downstream of the hotel at Brookside, contributing to larger flood extents in that area. (p22)
* The River Dove influenced the extent of flooding in the area and contributed to inundation of nearby residential areas; its proximity to Brookside is linked to properties there being flooded during the event on 16 February 2020. (p23) [IMG, PAG]
* {16 Feb 2020, 17 Feb 2020}: High flows in the River Dove prompted the operation/closure of nearby control gates during the flood response. Two control gates under the railway were closed during the weekend of 16–17 February 2020, and the Tutbury sluice gate was closed on the morning of 17 February 2020 to prevent River Dove flood flows moving through culverts or down the Tutbury Mill Fleam. The report concluded these gates do not have capacity to divert significant volumes and were unlikely to have caused the flooding experienced at Brookside. (p34) [MUL]
* Identified as influencing flooding of the Rolleston Brook; the modelling study will assess the River Dove's influence and joint probability of events with Rolleston Brook to understand flood mechanisms and impacts on properties. (p35)
