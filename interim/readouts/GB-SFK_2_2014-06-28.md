

# Sudbury flooding 28 Jun 2014

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: East Street and Elizabeth Court, Sudbury<br>124\_Suffolk County/124\_2014\_Suffolk County/section-19-report-east-street-reformatted-updated.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### East Street, Sudbury
* Subject of a Section 19 flood investigation carried out by Suffolk County Council into a flood event affecting the street. (p1)
* Internal flooding of properties during the flood event; part of the single incident that caused internal flooding of five properties across East Street and Elisabeth Court. (p2)
* Internal flooding of domestic properties — inundation of main living rooms, garages and gardens; residents report previous flooding occurrences in East Street. (p3) [PAG]
* Surface-water (pluvial) flooding of East Street when the surface water drainage system capacity was exceeded; runoff from properties discharged onto the pavement and highway drainage/gullies were implicated in the incident. (p4) [IMG, PAG]
* Highway experienced surface water flooding/insufficient highway drainage capacity and blocked road gullies; prompted actions to assess Anglian Water surface water capacity, clear road gullies, produce a map of highway drainage and review the maintenance schedule. (p5) [TAB]
* Quant: 5 properties (combined across East Street and Elisabeth Court) (p2)
* Quant: 14 domestic properties (p3) [PAG]

### Sudbury
* The town was the location of the flood event investigated by Suffolk County Council under Section 19 of the Flood and Water Management Act 2010. (p1)
* Internal flooding of properties at locations in the town (specifically East Street and Elisabeth Court) during the flood event on 28 June 2014. (p2)
* Localised internal flooding in East Street (a part of Sudbury) affecting domestic properties' main living areas, garages and gardens. (p3) [IMG]
* Surface water (pluvial) flooding affected parts of the town during the event; East Street is identified in the report and on pluvial flood maps as being at risk during 1:30 and 1:100 year rainfall events. (p4) [IMG]
* Quant: 5 properties (combined across East Street and Elisabeth Court) (p2)
* Quant: 14 domestic properties (p3) [IMG]

### Suffolk
* County-level location for the incident; internal flooding occurred in Sudbury (East Street) with domestic properties affected. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 14 domestic properties (p3) [IMG]
