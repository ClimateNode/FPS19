

# Costock flooding 02 Jan 2024

Reference document:<br>Section 19 Report – Costock – Storm Henk, January 2024<br>093\_Nottinghamshire County/costock-s19-storm-henk-jan-2024.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Costock
* The village suffered a flood event caused by sustained rainfall over a saturated catchment during Storm Henk. (p1)
* Surface water runoff from saturated agricultural fields and connecting drainage ditches flowed north into the village, causing flooding of residential streets and properties (flows entered via Algar Close and travelled onto Main Street, Church Lane and Chapel Lane). (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Surface water from saturated agricultural fields flowed north into the village during Storm Henk, resulting in flooding that affected properties on Main Street, Church Lane and Chapel Lane. (p3) [PAG]
* Local residents and businesses were flood-affected; meetings were held with residents to establish flooding mechanisms and impacts, and a community flood signage scheme is being developed for the village. (p4)
* Quant: 8 properties (2 on Main Street, 2 on Chapel Lane, 4 on Church Lane) (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: Main Street: 2 properties; Chapel Lane: 2 properties; Church Lane: 4 properties (p3) [PAG]

### Nottinghamshire
* Major incident with significant flooding across the county resulting in internal flooding of residential and business properties, delivery of sandbags, closure of roads to promote safety, and displacement of residents. (p4)
* Quant: 69 roads (p4)

### Main Street, Costock
* Surface water from saturated agricultural fields and drainage routes flowed onto Main Street (via Algar Close and overland flow). Flooding of properties was worsened by vehicles driving through floodwater creating bow waves that pushed water into adjacent properties. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Properties on Main Street were flooded by overland flows from Algar Close and adjacent agricultural land; flooding was exacerbated by vehicles driving through floodwater creating bow waves that entered properties adjacent to the highway. (p3) [PAG]
* Quant: 2 properties (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 2 properties (p3) [PAG]

### Chapel Lane, Costock
* Surface water from saturated agricultural land and drainage ditches flowed into Chapel Lane, causing flooding to properties on the street. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Overland flood flows reached Chapel Lane and affected properties there. (p3) [PAG]
* Quant: 2 properties (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 2 properties (p3) [PAG]

### Church Lane, Costock
* Surface water runoff from saturated fields and drainage ditches reached Church Lane, resulting in flooding of properties on that street. (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Floodwaters flowed through to Church Lane from the overland flow paths, affecting properties on the lane. (p3) [PAG]
* Quant: 4 properties (p2) [IMG, PAG]
* Quant: 4 properties (p3) [PAG]


## Topics without coordinates

### Algar Close
* Overland floodwater entered the village via Algar Close before flowing onto Main Street. (p3)

### Rushcliffe Borough Council
* Within the borough, domestic properties and businesses experienced internal flooding leading to displaced residents; the council provided housing to displaced residents, coordinated debris/bulky item collection and cleaned debris, and signposted affected businesses and households to grants and sandbag support. (p4)
