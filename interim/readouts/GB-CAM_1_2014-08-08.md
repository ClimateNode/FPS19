

# Bar Hill flooding 08 Aug 2014 - 09 Aug 2014

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Bar Hill<br>021\_Cambridgeshire County/021\_2014\_Cambridgeshire County/Bar\_Hill\_2014\_Flood\_investigation\_Report.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Bar Hill
* Flooding affected various locations within Bar Hill; residents reported flooding caused by multiple sources including surface water and drains. (p2) [TAB]
* Widespread flooding across the village caused by an extreme short-duration rainfall event that overwhelmed drainage networks, inundated watercourses and the road network. Properties and local infrastructure flooded internally and externally. (p3) [IMG]
* Experienced flooding during the August 2014 extreme rainfall event; Oakington Brook flowing through the village was identified by residents as a main source of flooding, hydraulic modelling was commissioned to investigate mechanisms, and local infrastructure (infiltration basin outfall and watercourses) was cleared by the parish council; Anglian Water inspected and maintained sewer/network sections that drain to the infiltration basin. (p4) [TAB]
* Quant: 79 properties (internally flooded); approximately 30 properties (external flooding) (p3) [IMG]

### Bar Hill Primary School
* The school was flooded during the same extreme rainfall event. The report states the school was flooded but does not specify the exact source of flooding for this asset (likely surface water and/or surcharging drains given the overall causes). (p3)
* Quant: 1 school (p3)

### Recreation Ground
* The incident map shows a cluster of flood-affected locations in the central area around the Recreation Ground. This indicates the Recreation Ground and its immediate surrounds were inundated by surface water / urban flooding during the extreme rainfall, causing standing water on the grounds and likely short‑term inundation and access disruption to the open space on 8th August 2014. (p3) [IMG]

### Oakington Brook
* Identified by residents as the main source of flooding through Bar Hill during the August 2014 event; hydraulic modelling showed flooding from the brook is sensitive to summer weed growth and culvert blockages and recommended a maintenance regime and reminders to riparian owners. (p4) [TAB]


## Topics without coordinates

### Cambridgeshire County Council
* Was reported to by residents about flooding at various locations in Bar Hill on 8th/9th August 2014; the incident related to surface water and drainage/sewer issues and was recorded against this Risk Management Authority. (p2) [TAB]
* Responded to the August 2014 extreme rainfall by arranging jetting and cleansing of main lines and gullies, inspecting bridges near Hollytrees, holding a post-flood drop-in for affected residents, commissioning hydraulic modelling of Oakington Brook and publishing subsequent newsletters to residents. (p4) [TAB]

### Anglian Water
* {August 2014}: Following the August 2014 flood Anglian Water inspected and carried out maintenance on the affected sections of their network at Bar Hill; their network discharges to the maintained infiltration basin. (p4) [TAB]

### Cambridgeshire County Council Highways
* Following the extreme rainfall on 8th/9th August 2014 Cambridgeshire County Council Highways carried out post‑flood highway works (jetting and cleansing of main lines), inspected bridges near Hollytrees, held a post‑flood drop‑in that gathered resident evidence and commissioned hydraulic modelling of Oakington Brook which found flooding within Bar Hill is sensitive to summer weed growth and culvert blockages and recommended a maintenance regime and reminders to riparian owners. (p4) [TAB]
