

# Lyde flooding 16 Feb 2020

Reference document:<br>Herefordshire 2019/20 Section 19 Report: Lyde<br>055\_Herefordshire County/14\_Lyde.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Lyde
* {general}: The Environment Agency 'Flood Map for Planning (Rivers and Sea)' shown for the area identifies flood risk zones covering the settlement (Flood Zone 2 and Flood Zone 3 are indicated on the map legend) and shows areas benefiting from flood defences. This indicates parts of Lyde are at risk of river flooding and that some locations are mapped as protected by defences for planning purposes. (p1) [IMG]
* Three properties reported internal flooding during the February 2020 event; respondents reported floodwater coming from Church Road and from a blocked ditch/culvert, and noted a culvert alongside Church Road filled to capacity and was considered a recurring capacity issue. (p2)
* Quant: 3 properties (p2)

### Church Road, Lyde
* Blocked drains and debris on Church Road and a culvert alongside Church Road filling to capacity were reported as sources/routes of floodwater that reached properties; culvert diameter changes (originally 450mm, extended as 375mm, downstream end 300mm) reduced capacity and may have contributed to flooding. (p2)
* {01 Jul 2021 - 31 Jul 2021}: A highway drainage project to improve the highway drainage on Church Road was delivered in July 2021 (project delivered as part of drainage/flooding response measures). (p3)
* {2007, 16 Feb 2020}: Quant: 3 properties (p2) [MUL]

### Lower Lyde
* The ordinary watercourse in Lower Lyde (a tributary of the River Lugg) is culverted beneath Church Road; inadequate culvert capacity and blockages are identified as possible causes of fluvial flooding affecting nearby properties. (p2)
* Quant: 3 properties (p2)


## Topics without coordinates

### Herefordshire Council
* Herefordshire Council Land Drainage Authority (LLFA) proposes to exercise its flood risk management function in response to the recent flooding, has identified a requirement to promote delivery of a project to upsize part of the riparian-owned culvert, and is seeking DEFRA grant funding to study and deliver works. (p3)
