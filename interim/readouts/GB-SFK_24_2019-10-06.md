

# Beccles flooding 06 Oct 2019

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Ingate and Fredericks Road, Beccles<br>124\_Suffolk County/124\_2020\_Suffolk County/section-19-report-ingate-beccles.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Ingate, Beccles
* Identified in the report title as subject of a Section 19 flood investigation by the Lead Local Flood Authority following a flood event. (p1)
* Properties on Ingate Road experienced internal flooding during the flood event; part of the total of seven properties reported as flooded internally. (p2)
* Ingate experienced substantial ponding in a local depression causing migration of exceedance volumes into properties; highway gullies were blocked, highway flooded to similar depths as properties, rear gardens flooded and sewage was reported in gardens. Surcharging of the foul sewer (including jets at manhole ref 5901) contributed to the flooding. (p3) [PAG]
* Localised ponding at front and rear of buildings in the valley of the town's open drain; highway drainage performance reduced (surcharged outfalls and possible reverse flows) due to especially high levels in the main open drain; blocked highway gullies prevented runoff escape leading to combined highway and foul flooding. (p4)
* Properties experienced localised ponding at the front and rear due to lack of safe exceedance routes; highway drainage was overwhelmed/ineffective (4 highway gullies were completely blocked) causing no escape for rainfall and contributing to foul flooding. High water levels in the town's main open drain likely reduced highway drainage performance via surcharged outfalls or reverse flows. (p5) [PAG]
* Quant: 7 properties (p2)
* Quant: 7 properties (total across Ingate and Fredericks Road) internally flooded; 4 highway gullies reported completely blocked; flood depths up to 400 mm (p3) [PAG]

### Fredericks Road, Beccles
* Identified in the report title as subject of a Section 19 flood investigation by the Lead Local Flood Authority following a flood event. (p1)
* Properties on Fredericks Road experienced internal flooding during the flood event; part of the total of seven properties reported as flooded internally. (p2)
* Properties on Fredericks Road experienced internal flooding from a mixture of stormwater and foul sewage; rear gardens and the adjacent highway were flooded to similar depths. (p3)
* Properties sit within the natural valley of the town's open drain and suffered localised ponding at front and rear of buildings; evidence (weep holes) indicates historical attempts to reduce floodwater levels. (p4)
* Sits within the natural valley of the town's open drain and properties suffer localised ponding at front and rear due to lack of safe exceedance routes; affected by high flows in the main open drain consistent with the surface water/fluvial event. (p5) [PAG]
* Quant: 7 properties (p2)
* Quant: 7 properties (total across Ingate and Fredericks Road) internally flooded; flood depths up to 400 mm (p3)

### Beccles
* The town (as location for Ingate and Fredericks Road) is identified in the report title and is the area investigated by the LLFA following the flood event. (p1)
* Residential properties in Beccles (specifically on Ingate Road and Fredericks Road) experienced internal flooding as part of the flood event. (p2)
* Internal flooding of properties in the town (notably Ingate and Fredericks Road). Properties experienced internal flooding (mixture of stormwater and foul sewage), rear gardens flooded, and the highway flooded to similar depths. Flooding resulted from capacity exceedance of surface water and foul drainage during an intense storm and accumulation of water in a local depression which migrated into properties. (p3) [IMG]
* Quant: 7 properties (p2)
* Quant: 7 properties; 0.2Ha (2000m2); flood depths up to 400mm; 4 highway gullies reported blocked (p3) [IMG]

### Blyburgate, Beccles
* Jets of water observed from the centre of the road were traced to a foul sewer chamber (Manhole ref 5901) that was surcharging; sewage reported in back gardens indicating the foul sewer was overloaded/surcharging (likely due to stormwater entering the foul system via illegal/private/highway connections). Post-event road and chamber repairs support these findings. (p4)
* ‘Jets of water’ at the Blyburgate junction were identified as a surcharging foul sewer chamber (Manhole ref 5901); sewage was reported in back gardens indicating foul sewer surcharging (private drainage connecting to the foul sewer and stormwater ingress into the foul system). Post-event repair works to the road and chamber were carried out. (p5) [PAG]

### Peddars Lane, Beccles
* Jets of water reported from the centre of the road at the Blyburgate Junction with Peddars Lane were identified as a surcharging foul sewer chamber (Manhole ref 5901). Sewage was reported in back gardens, indicating private drainage connected to the foul sewer and surcharging of the foul system; post-event repair works to the road and chamber were carried out. (p4)

### Grove Road, Beccles
* Foul sewers were reported to have flooded Grove Road during the event. (p4)

### Anglian Water
* The foul sewer system (Anglian Water network) exceeded capacity and surcharged, contributing foul sewage to internal property flooding and gardens, and was reported to have affected Grove Road during the same event. (p3)
* Quant: 7 properties internally flooded (affected by mixture of stormwater and foul sewage) (p3)
