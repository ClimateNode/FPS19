

# Stockton-On-Tees Unitary Authority flooding 05 Dec 2013

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Tees Tidal Flooding<br>122\_Stockton-on-Tees/122\_2013\_Stockton-on-Tees/Flood\_investigation\_report\_-\_Tees\_Tidal\_Flooding.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Stockton-On-Tees Unitary Authority
* The Borough of Stockton-on-Tees was subject to severe tidal flooding during the event, causing widespread damage and major transport disruption (multiple major highways were flooded and key interchanges/roads were closed or partially closed). (p6)
* Quant: three major highways; A178 closed for approximately 2 months (p6)

### Port Clarence
* Residential area in Port Clarence identified as the worst affected area (appendix: 'Plan showing worst affected residential area in Port Clarence'). (p5)
* Port Clarence experienced internal flooding of residential properties, evacuations of residents to a rest centre, and local business flooding as a result of the tidal surge and overtopping/breach of defences. (p6) [PAG]
* Quant: 32 residential properties internally flooded; approximately 250 residents evacuated; up to 20 residents stayed overnight at the rest centre; part of 20 businesses affected across Port Clarence, Billingham Reach Industrial Estate and Seal Sands (p6) [PAG]

### Greatham Creek
* A flood bank on the southern side of Greatham Creek failed and there was a major breach of the flood defence, which led to extensive local flooding and contributed to the closure of the A178. (p6) [PAG]
* Quant: A178 closed for approximately 2 months (p6) [PAG]

### Billingham Reach Industrial Estate
* Businesses at the industrial estate were inundated by floodwater, causing damage and operational disruption to commercial premises. (p6)
* Quant: 20 businesses (this figure of 20 businesses is reported across Port Clarence, Billingham Reach Industrial Estate and Seal Sands) (p6)

### Portrack Interchange
* The A19 Portrack interchange was flooded and had to be closed, causing major transport disruption. (p6)

### Teesside Park
* The A66 trunk road was partially closed at Teesside Park due to tidal flooding associated with the same tidal surge event and its impacts on local defences. (p6)
