### Highway Authority
* {general}: Northamptonshire Highways have responsibility for highway drainage; new systems are designed to drain surface water runoff from the highway catchment only, and historic highway drainage may be substandard, which can contribute to surface water flooding risk. (p16)
* {general}: Highway drainage assets may have insufficient capacity to drain runoff, creating flood risk to properties; requires regular highway drainage cleansing (including carriageway channel) and assessment of asset capacity and third-party receiving systems. (p22)

### Northamptonshire County Council
* {general}: As Lead Local Flood Authority, NCC investigates flood incidents under Section 19, maintains a register of assets affecting flooding (surface runoff, groundwater, ordinary watercourses) and keeps records of flooding hotspots across the county; it can suggest causes of flooding in Hellidon and make recommendations but lacks mandate/funding to force action. (p16)

### Anglian Water
* {general}: In extreme weather the sewer systems AWS manages can become overwhelmed and result in sewer flooding that can affect properties served by those sewers. (p17)

### Water Authority
* {general}: Public sewer assets are to be assessed for sources of water entering them and for insufficient capacity where this could lead to flood risk to properties; improvement works should be considered where capacity is inadequate. (p22)

### Environment Agency
* Worked with the NCC Emergency Planning Team and the LLFA to support the community and the community-based Flood Warden in response to the flooding incident. (p23)
