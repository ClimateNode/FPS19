### Lancashire
* Significant flooding hit Lancashire in December 2015, affecting local communities and exposing weaknesses in communication and coordination of flood risk management; this led to the development of district-level reports to inform and support affected homeowners and businesses. (p3) [PAG]
* {December 2015}: The December 2015 floods produced the highest river flow levels on record in the area and resulted in widespread impacts across the county, including over 2,500 properties flooded. (p8) [TAB]
* Extreme storms and rainfall in November–December 2015 caused flooding throughout December 2015 affecting Lancashire: over 2,500 properties were flooded and there was damage to private gardens, garages and out‑buildings, business premises, agricultural property and critical public infrastructure including roads, bridges, retaining walls, water treatment plants, power and communications installations, and community buildings such as schools and village/town halls. (p9)
* Multiple communities across the county experienced flooding as a result of two major storm events in December 2015; the LLFA recorded and verified data, established flood timelines, identified affected communities and initiated a countywide Section 19 investigation and follow-on (stage two) reporting to track recommended remedial actions. (p10) [PAG]
* {December 2015}: Quant: over 2,500 properties flooded (p8) [TAB]
* Quant: over 2,500 properties (p9)
