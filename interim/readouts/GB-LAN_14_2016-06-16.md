

# Lancashire flooding 16 Jun 2016

Reference document:<br>16 June 2016 Flooding In Lancashire: Section 19 Report<br>069\_Lancashire County/section-19-flood-investigation-report-16-june-2016-flooding.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### West Lancashire
* On 16 June 2016 intense rainfall in West Lancashire overwhelmed drainage networks, causing widespread surface water flooding of homes and roads; Skelmersdale (within the district) was especially badly affected. (p3)
* Prolonged heavy rain and flash flooding across western Lancashire impacted the district: a total of 159 properties were flooded internally and/or externally; roads were closed in places. (p12) [PAG]
* Identified as the area significantly affected during the event; residents reported properties flooded (water ingress up to 100mm) in Upholland and Skelmersdale; West Lancashire properties were visited and drying works began the following day. (p13)
* Particularly damaging flash flooding on 16 June 2016 affected a large portion of West Lancashire district: intense rainfall overwhelmed drainage, causing standing water on roads, abandoned vehicles that impeded emergency access, and water ingress at low-lying properties. (p16)
* Quant: over 60 residential properties (Skelmersdale) (p3)
* Quant: 159 properties (p12) [PAG]
* Quant: properties flooded up to 100mm (4 inches) in Upholland and Skelmersdale (p13)

### Lancashire
* Flooding occurred across parts of Lancashire during the event. (p1)
* On 16 June 2016 a heavy downpour caused surface water and urban flooding in parts of western Lancashire, inundating homes and roads as local drainage networks were overwhelmed. (p3) [PAG]
* The county experienced flooding on 16 June 2016. This report provides a commentary on those flooding events based on reports made to the Lead Local Flood Authority and by Risk Management Authorities and local representatives. (p10)
* On 16 June 2016 intense rainfall overwhelmed natural and man-made drainage networks across western Lancashire. Particularly damaging flash flooding occurred across a large portion of West Lancashire district, causing flooding of homes and of the public highway network. (p15) [PAG]
* On 16 June 2016 extreme rainfall overwhelmed natural and man-made drainage networks across parts of the county, producing flash and surface-water/urban flooding. Impacts described include traffic driving through standing water creating bow waves that washed onto private property, vehicles abandoned in flooded roads impeding access for emergency vehicles, and water ingress at low-lying properties. (p16) [PAG]
* Quant: over 60 residential properties (in Skelmersdale) (p3) [PAG]

### Skelmersdale
* On 16 June 2016 intense, rapid rainfall overwhelmed natural and constructed drainage networks in Skelmersdale, resulting in widespread surface water flooding that entered homes and inundated roads. (p3)
* Particularly severely affected by the downpour on 16 June 2016: over 60 homes estimated flooded; several roads closed; residents reported flood depths up to 100 mm (4 inches); emergency services received over 70 calls relating to around 30 incidents and Lancashire Fire and Rescue Service responded from 1600hrs. (p12)
* Properties flooded with up to 100mm of water reported by residents; fire crews pumped out floodwater that had entered or was about to enter properties; council surveyor visits and drying with dehumidifiers began the day after the flood. (p13) [PAG]
* Quant: over 60 residential properties (p3)
* Quant: over 60 homes; over 70 calls; around 30 incidents; 100 mm (4 inches) (p12)
* Quant: properties flooded up to 100mm (4 inches) in Upholland and Skelmersdale (p13) [PAG]

## Topics without coordinates

### Lancashire County Council
* LCC Highways staff responded to various surface water flooding events, arranged procurement of sandbags and organised surveyor visits to flooded properties to begin drying works the day after the flood. (p13)
* Quant: approximately 100 sandbags (p13)

### Lancashire Fire and Rescue Service
* Responded to and dealt with flash flooding incidents from about 1600hrs on 16 June 2016 across Skelmersdale and the West Lancashire area. (p12)
* Quant: around 30 incidents; over 70 calls (p12)

### Highways
* Highways staff responded to surface water flooding incidents; due to lack of depot stocks procured sandbags from external suppliers and made them available to local officers. (p13)
* Quant: approximately 100 sandbags (p13)

### United Utilities
* Manholes in South Ribble became overwhelmed and spilled sewage onto roads and driveways; firefighters liaised with United Utilities and arranged for storm water to be pumped into the public sewer system as part of the immediate response. (p13)

### West Lancashire Borough Council
* Acted as asset manager for households affected by the flooding; the Council responded to care for flooded households and to restore properties back to their former condition, but was unable to provide details of properties in its ownership or information on whether flood-resistant/resilient products were fitted. (p13)
