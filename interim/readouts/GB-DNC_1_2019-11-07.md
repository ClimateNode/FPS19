### Doncaster
* High flows on watercourses produced flooding along associated floodplains across the borough. River Don recorded record-high flows, riverside embankments overtopped at Bentley and downstream, the Bentley Flood Corridor storage area filled, extensive surface water flooding occurred near small watercourses and dykes. Consequences included almost 800 households flooded, many residents evacuated, extensive road closures and large numbers of businesses impacted. (p5) [MUL]
* River Don flows exceeded containment, overtopping the left Riverside Bank starting early on the morning of the 8th and then overtopping the secondary Barrier Bank; overtopping spread floodwater beyond normal banks to inundate low-lying agricultural land and communities. (p7)
* Widespread flooding from intense rainfall and river responses: households flooded, residents evacuated, extensive road closures and many businesses affected within the borough. (p11)
* Widespread flooding affected much of the Doncaster borough during the event; multiple communities across the borough were impacted and were grouped for sub-investigation. (p15) [PAG]
* Flooding occurred on floodplains and in areas of the town as high flows passed through Doncaster; the River Don at Doncaster recorded its highest flow on record during the event. (p17) [PAG]
* Flooding of land alongside the River Don and floodplain storage filling occurred across the borough. Large numbers of properties were flooded, residents evacuated and a major incident was declared. (p18) [PAG]
* Doncaster appears within the geographic scope of the Environment Agency flood map and the Humberhead Levels floodplain is shown to extend into parts of the Doncaster area, indicating parts of the borough were within the flood risk area and affected by flooding during the event. (p24) [IMG]
* {01 Jun 2007 - 30 Jun 2007}: Doncaster Council records of flooded properties from June 2007 indicate widespread flooding across Bentley including to the north, south and along the North Swaithe Dyke and Swaith Dike corridors. (p27) [PAG]
* Doncaster was at the centre of the intense rainfall band on 7 Nov 2019 and the River Don peak flow at Doncaster was reported as the highest on record for the November 2019 event, contributing to significant local flooding and surface-water issues where drainage capacity was challenged. (p30) [IMG]
* The Don at Doncaster flood produced overtopping of defences and resulted in residential flooding around Bentley (South) — including Riviera Parade, Hunt Lane, Yarborough Terrace, Frank Road and Conyers Road — and filling of the Bentley Flood Corridor; pumping operations (Environment Agency and Fire Service) were used to return flood water to the river. (p38)
* Doncaster Council recorded and managed the multi-agency flood emergency response for the 7–10 Nov 2019 event: maintaining an overview of weather/flood warnings, producing briefing notes, recording streets evacuated, maintaining a flood risk call log, operating the multi-agency flood plan, and using road closure and sandbag protocols; debrief feedback was produced afterwards. (p41) [PAG]
