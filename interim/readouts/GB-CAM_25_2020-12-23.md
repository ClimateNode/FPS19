## Place names with coordinates

### St Neots
* {1947, 1998}: Large areas are at significant risk from fluvial (river) flooding from the River Great Ouse and from ordinary watercourses; surface water flooding risk also significant. Flooding can be exacerbated when the River Great Ouse remains high and contributory watercourses, highways gullies and surface water sewers cannot discharge efficiently. The town experienced major flooding events in 1947 and 1998. (p4) [MUL]
* {1947, 1998}: Large areas of the town are at significant risk from fluvial flooding (from the River Great Ouse and its tributaries) and from surface water flooding; built-up areas and infrastructure are intersected by flood zones and are vulnerable when the River Great Ouse remains high and tributaries and sewers cannot discharge. (p5) [PAG, IMG, MUL]
* Flooding began in St Neots on 23 December 2020 from intense rainfall and saturated ground. A major incident was declared; large areas of the town—particularly low-lying areas along the River Great Ouse and its contributory brooks—were affected by fluvial and surface water flooding. (p6) [PAG, IMG]
* Significant surface water flooding began on 23 December 2020 (predominantly 23–24 Dec) followed by fluvial (river) flooding when local watercourses exceeded channel capacity. Flooding across St Neots was declared a major incident; the event involved rapid high runoff due to intense rainfall and saturated ground. (p7) [PAG, MUL]
* Very rapid high runoff from 23 December rainfall produced significant surface water flooding in and around St Neots, followed by fluvial (river) flooding on 24 December 2020 when watercourses in St Neots exceeded channel capacity. Flood Warnings were issued for the area and properties were reported as being affected; warning lead times were short in places and the issued Flood Warning was close to or after properties were first reported as affected. (p8) [PAG]
* Thirty-four flood reports were received across St Neots for 23–24 December 2020. The community reported 14 properties flooded internally and a further 20 properties with external flooding (gardens/outbuildings). Flooding durations were reported between 1 and 5 days. Five key risk areas were identified within the town. (p11)
* Community-reported flooding across the town: 34 flood reports received by the LLFA for 23–24 December 2020; reports indicate both internal and external property flooding across multiple neighbourhoods. (p12) [PAG, IMG]
* Fluvial flooding into St Neots from Wintringham Brook and Hen Brook caused internal flooding to residential properties (Hampden Way) and flooding of gardens/outbuildings. Residents were temporarily displaced in some cases. (p18)
* The town is identified as the area at flood risk; the Environment Agency is considering strategic projects and improved warning/triggering to reduce future flood risk to St Neots. (p24)
* Intense rainfall on 23 December produced very rapid high runoff that caused significant surface water flooding in St Neots, followed on 24 December by fluvial (river) flooding when watercourses across St Neots exceeded channel capacity. (p27)
* Quant: duration between 1 and 5 days; river flow upstream of St Neots recorded at 34.29 m3/s (200PCSYM of the Long Term Average) (p7) [PAG, MUL]
* Quant: 34.29 m3/s; 200PCSYM of the Long Term Average; river levels exceeded those of 1998 and reached the highest on record (p8) [PAG]

### Anglian Water

(p13)
