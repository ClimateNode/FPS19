

# Chilcompton flooding 17 Jun 2016

Reference document:<br>Flood Investigation Report: Parsonage Lane, The Street, Church Lane, Broadway and Greenditch Close, Chilcompton 17th June 2016<br>113\_Somerset County/Flood Investigation Report  Broadway and Chilcompton – 17 June 2016.pdf

## Place names with coordinates

### Chilcompton
* Multiple assets within the village were affected: three residential properties and St John the Baptist Church were internally flooded (up to 80 mm) and the highway was flooded (up to 600 mm). (p4)
* Two focal areas of flooding on 2016-06-17: (1) Broadway and Greenditch Close (south of the village) — highway inundated but no internal flooding to properties; (2) The Street (outside Shell House), The Pitching Path and Church Lane — internal flooding to three residential properties and St John the Baptist Church. (p9) [PAG, IMG, MUL]
* Highway flooding on Broadway and Greenditch Close (south of the village); only the highway flooded and there was no internal flooding to properties. (p10) [PAG, MUL]
* On 2016-06-17 parts of Chilcompton experienced surface run-off flooding that led to four properties being flooded internally (details as above). (p11) [PAG, MUL]
* Intense, short-duration rainfall (44.6 mm on 2016-06-17, and ~42 mm over 10–16 June) produced exceptional surface water runoff that caused internal flooding to properties in Chilcompton. Multiple properties reported internal flooding; affected housing lies within the Environment Agency's Flood Zone 3 (high risk). The event is described as exceptional and the area has a history of flooding. (p14)
* Residents and property owners in Chilcompton were affected by the 17 June 2016 flooding; SCC distributed flood incident report forms to affected property owners, inspected gullies (approximately 40 m north of The Street's junction with Parsonage Lane) and the culvert under The Street, and actions included arranging removal of a large obstruction in a local watercourse and providing advice about a collapsing wall adjacent to a watercourse. (p15)
* Large amounts of surface water on streets and around drains/gullies (including Fry's Well and under the old railway bridge); gullies reported blocked or not cleaned and highway drainage lacking capacity to accept runoff, contributing to surface-water flooding of roads and properties. (p17)

### St John the Baptist Church
* Internally flooded to a depth of up to 80 mm. (p4) [MUL]
* The Church of St John the Baptist suffered internal flooding during the 17 June 2016 event; water entered the building and caused internal flooding/damage to the church interior. (p10) [MUL]
* {general}: St John the Baptist Church has suffered past flooding caused by surface run-off from the hillside to the west of the church (internal impacts described generally as 'suffering flooding'). (p11)
* St John the Baptist Church was affected by surface water runoff from nearby fields which contributed to flooding during the event on 2016-06-17. (p15)

### The Street, Chilcompton
* On 2016-06-17 The Street (outside the property known as Shell House) experienced flooding that led to internal flooding of three residential properties and of St John the Baptist Church. (p9) [PAG]
* On 2016-06-17 three residential properties and St John the Baptist Church suffered internal flooding in the area of The Street. (p10) [PAG]
* On 2016-06-17 surface run-off from the catchment (including Lynch Hill) travelled via B3139, Parsonage Lane and The Street to the lowest point on The Street. Four properties flooded internally; one property had kitchen flooding to about 600 mm and the rest of that property flooded to about 300 mm. Occupiers were displaced and expected to be unable to return for up to seven months while drying and repairs were completed. (p11) [PAG]
* Surface water run-off collected and travelled to the lowest point on The Street. Highway drains approximately 40 m north of its junction with Parsonage Lane surcharged and began to flood The Street, inundating the carriageway and immediate street area on 2016-06-17. (p12)
* The Street area was implicated in the 17 June 2016 flooding: site visits focused on the watercourse downstream of the culvert flowing under The Street, and gullies near The Street (and its junction with Parsonage Lane) were discussed as drainage assets relevant to the flooding impacts on properties/roads. (p15)
* Large amounts of surface water entered the highway network during the event; gullies and drains were reported blocked or ineffective, leading to surface-water accumulation on streets and risk to adjacent properties. (p17)

### Ston Easton
* Properties in Ston Easton were impacted by the 17 June 2016 flooding; SCC carried out site visits (including Green Street), gathered information from residents (e.g. Mill Cottage owner reported surface runoff from nearby fields), and assessed surrounding agricultural land, finding no obvious agricultural practices that compounded the 17 June flooding. (p15)

### Broadway, Chilcompton
* On 2016-06-17 the highway at Broadmead (sic) flooded; reports indicate only the highway was affected and there was no internal flooding to properties. (p9) [PAG, IMG, MUL]
* Highway flooding occurred on Broadway on 2016-06-17; there was no internal flooding of properties. (p10)

### Church Lane, Chilcompton
* Internal flooding of properties associated with Church Lane during the 17 June 2016 event; three residential properties and St John the Baptist Church suffered internal flooding linked to the focal flooding at The Street / The Pitching Path / Church Lane. (p10) [PAG]
* Two culverts on Church Lane (School Culvert and Manor Farm Culvert) were identified for repair/shotcreting and localised headwall repairs as part of the flood response. Works may require temporary road closure/diversion at School Culvert and use of pumping equipment to overpump the River Somer, causing local disruption. (p17)

### Greenditch Close, Chilcompton
* Highway flooding occurred on Greenditch Close on 2016-06-17; there was no internal flooding of properties. (p10)
* Reported blocked gullies and large amounts of surface water contributing to localised flooding in the Greenditch area of Chilcompton. (p17)

### The Pitching, Chilcompton
* Internal flooding of St John the Baptist Church and nearby residential properties on The Pitching Path / Church Lane occurred on 2016-06-17. (p10) [PAG]
* The River Somer breached its banks adjacent to the public footpath known as The Pitching, resulting in inundation of the footpath and surrounding riverside area on 2016-06-17. (p12)

### Parsonage Lane, Chilcompton
* Surface run-off travelled along Parsonage Lane towards The Street; flows from the catchment contributed to downstream inundation and to surcharge of highway drains in the immediate area on 2016-06-17. (p12)
* The junction area of Parsonage Lane with The Street was discussed during the 20/07/2016 site visit because gullies approximately 40 m north of that junction were relevant to drainage performance during the 17 June 2016 flooding; community expectations of those gullies' function were recorded as part of the investigation. (p15)

### Lynch Hill, Chilcompton
* Surface run-off from the Lynch Hill catchment travelled downhill into the Chilcompton drainage network and contributed to the large volumes of surface water that led to downstream flooding on 2016-06-17. (p12)

### Chilcompton Road, Chilcompton
* Surface run-off travelled via the B3139 Emborough to Chilcompton Road during the event, causing flooding of the highway carriageway as water moved towards lower-lying streets on 2016-06-17. (p12)

### Green Street, Ston Easton
* A separate SCC site visit was carried out to Green Street, Ston Easton to assess and gather information on flood impacts following the 17‑06‑2016 event. (p15)

### Stockhill Road, Chilcompton
* Observers reported large amounts of surface water and blocked gullies on Stockhill Road, indicating surface-water flooding and drainage capacity issues affecting the road. (p17)

### River Somer
* High volumes of surface water run-off increased flows in the River Somer to the extent that it breached its banks adjacent to the public footpath known as The Pitching, causing fluvial inundation of the immediate riverside area on 2016-06-17. (p12)


## Topics without coordinates

### Somerset County Council
* Acted as the Lead Local Flood Authority (LLFA) in response to the 17 June 2016 flash flood: provided advice on planning consultations, explained statutory consultee role, and tasked its Civil Contingencies Unit to liaise with the Environment Agency and the Parish Council to develop a Flood Action Plan. (p18)

### Mendip
* SCC FRM liaised with Mendip Area Highways regarding the response to and maintenance issues arising from the 17‑06‑2016 flood (e.g. highway drains and gullies). (p15)

### School Culvert
* Identified for shotcreting of the masonry arch and localised headwall repairs (anticipated five days). Works require overpumping the River Somer to allow repairs and may necessitate a temporary road closure/diversion and pumping equipment sited locally, causing disruption to residents. (p17) [PAG]
* Culvert structures damaged/require repair and reinforcement following the flood. Localised repairs to head walls and shotcrete application planned; works will require pumping the River Somer (over-pumping) and are expected to cause a probable road closure and diversion during repairs. Each culvert repair is anticipated to take about five days; completion likely delayed to Jan/Feb. (p18)

### Mill Farm
* Agricultural land/fields at Mill Farm produced surface water runoff that flowed toward nearby properties and contributed to flooding during the event on 2016-06-17. (p15)

### Manor Farm Culvert
* Scheduled for shotcreting of the masonry arch and localised headwall repairs (anticipated five days) as part of the flood-response works to reinforce the structure and prevent obstruction to flow. (p17)

### Chilcompton Wood
* A culvert just outside the village at Chilcompton Wood (on The Street) is to be shotcreted and have localised headwall repairs to reinforce the masonry arch; works anticipated to take five days as part of the flood-response programme. (p17)
