## Place names with coordinates

### Bluebell Road, Southampton Unitary Authority
* {01 Dec 2007 - 31 Dec 2007, 11 Jun 2012}: Surface water flooding incidents were recorded at this location, indicating the street has experienced flood impacts potentially related to the local drainage issues. (p2) [IMG, MUL]

### Daisy Dip
* Surface water flooding during a heavy rainfall event produced standing water covering large parts of the grassy eastern section of the park. Flooding was driven by surface water runoff from surrounding areas and a damaged surface water sewer infrastructure was identified as the cause/pathway. (p1)


## Topics without coordinates

### Daisy Dip Recreation Ground
* {25 Apr 2012, 11 Jun 2012}: Surface water accumulates in the playing fields then flows southeast across the lower section of the park into the garden of a property backing onto the park, creating a flow path along the gardens of surrounding properties. Investigation photos show a suspected damaged surface water sewer and a damaged manhole within the vegetated area, indicating compromised drainage contributing to the flooding. (p2) [IMG, MUL]
* {25 Apr 2012, 11 Jun 2012}: Quant: 10 properties (p2) [IMG, MUL]

### Southern Water
* {01 Dec 2007 - 31 Dec 2007, 02 Jun 2012, 11 Jun 2012}: Responded to surface water flooding reports at Bluebell Road / Daisy Dip area: sewer crew attended site on 2 June 2012 and, after inspection, indicated the surface water sewer and manhole covers required civil intervention. Southern Water committed to further site visits to determine remedial works. (p3) [MUL]
