

# Bamber Bridge flooding 01 Jan 2022 - 31 Jan 2022

### South Ribble
* The South Ribble district experienced flooding during the January 2022 event; the report headings identify Bamber Bridge and Chorley Road within this district as affected locations. (p1)

### Bamber Bridge
* Bamber Bridge experienced flooding during the January 2022 event; Chorley Road in Bamber Bridge is specifically noted in the report headings. (p1)
* Heavy rainfall overnight into 1 January 2022 and again on 8 January 2022 led to localised flooding in Bamber Bridge. Flooding of homes on Chorley Road occurred when a culverted watercourse backing up (due to an obstruction from demolition works) overflowed into the highway. (p3)
* Sections of public highway (notably Chorley Road) in Bamber Bridge flooded after a culvert was blocked. Floodwater breached thresholds of nearby homes, causing extensive ground-floor damage and necessitating emergency out-of-hours response by the police and local highway authority. The responsible parties repaired the culvert and no repeat flooding occurred after repairs (completed by end of February 2022). (p6)

### Walton-Le-Dale
* Heavy rainfall overnight into 1 January 2022 and again on 8 January 2022 contributed to localised flooding in the Walton-le-Dale area; the obstruction of a culverted local watercourse caused backing up and overflow into the highway, resulting in internal flooding of nearby homes (notably on Chorley Road). (p3)

### Chorley Road, Walton-Le-Dale
* Chorley Road in Bamber Bridge was affected by flooding during the January 2022 event referenced in the document. (p1)
* A number of homes on Chorley Road suffered internal flooding when a culverted local watercourse, which had been accidentally obstructed during nearby demolition works, backed up and overflowed into the public highway. Floodwater in the highway rose above property thresholds. The damaged and obstructed lengths of the culvert were later replaced, which remedied the problem. (p3)
* Damage to a culverted watercourse during demolition/site clearance caused a significant blockage that prevented normal flow and local highway drainage. Under heavy rainfall the public highway flooded, water breached thresholds of nearby homes and caused extensive flood damage to ground floors. Emergency out-of-hours response was required from the police and the local highway authority. The culvert was repaired and there have been no further instances of flooding since repairs were completed by the end of February 2022. (p6)


## Topics without coordinates

### Lancashire County Council
* {general}: A damaged and blocked culverted watercourse on Chorley Road, Bamber Bridge (caused during demolition/site clearance) prevented normal flow and overwhelmed local highway drainage, producing continuous/localised flooding at the side of a house. Lancashire County Council received the report, coordinated emergency attendance, referred the enquiry to its Flood Risk Management team, liaised with landowners and planning officers, commissioned a culvert survey (14 Feb 2022) and issued letters to landowners (1 Feb 2022). Repair works were completed by the end of February 2022 and no further flooding has been reported at this location since. (p5)
